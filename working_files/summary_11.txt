Dyson V16 Piston Animal Submarine Review: Powerful but Pricey
============================================================

Dyson has launched the V16 Piston Animal, its most powerful stick vacuum to date, featuring 315 air-watts of suction and a versatile new cleaner head for various floor types.

### Key Points
- The V16 Piston Animal delivers 315 air-watts of suction, surpassing the 262 air-watts offered by the previous flagship, the Gen5Detect.
- A new All Floor Cones Sense cleaner head automatically adjusts settings within one second when transitioning between hard floors and carpets.
- The Submarine variant includes a specialized wet cleaner head for mopping, alongside the Fluffy Optic head designed for hard floor maintenance.
- The "Animal" designation indicates the inclusion of a specialized hair-screw attachment specifically engineered to remove pet hair from surfaces.
- The V16 succeeds the V15 Detect, marking the first major update to this specific product line in five years.

### Why it Matters
This release establishes a new performance benchmark for Dyson’s cordless vacuum lineup, though it forces consumers to weigh premium power against the value of older, more affordable models. The introduction of adaptive cleaning technology signals a shift toward more automated, multi-surface home maintenance tools in the competitive appliance market.
