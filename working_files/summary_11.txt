MarketBeat Week in Review – 08/17 - 08/21
============================================================

U.S. stock markets faced a volatile week as Treasury bond buyback announcements, rising oil prices, and cautious earnings reports from major companies pressured the primary market indexes.

### Key Points
- The U.S. Treasury Department announced it will double bond buybacks starting in September, contributing to market uncertainty.
- Bitcoin rallied following White House discussions aimed at advancing the Clarity Act, which boosted investor sentiment for risk-on assets.
- Moderna shares surged 177% after a successful late-stage clinical trial for a melanoma vaccine developed with Merck.
- RTX secured a $23 billion U.S. Navy contract, providing long-term revenue stability despite broader concerns over high valuations in the defense sector.
- SK hynix announced a record $28.6 billion share repurchase and cancellation program to highlight its strong balance sheet amid tech sector pressure.
- Investors are awaiting the Personal Consumption Expenditures (PCE) index release on August 26 to gauge the current trajectory of inflation.

### Why it Matters
Market participants are currently balancing macroeconomic policy shifts with sector-specific growth opportunities in artificial intelligence, commodities, and healthcare. Monitoring upcoming inflation data and corporate earnings remains critical for investors navigating this period of heightened volatility and shifting liquidity.
