Google Photos causes headaches: users say editing pictures makes the app 'unresponsive'
============================================================

Google Pixel users are reporting that the Google Photos app becomes unresponsive after editing images, rendering essential navigation buttons and features unusable without displaying any error messages.

### Key Points
- Pixel 9, 10, and 11 series users report the app freezes immediately after performing edits like cropping.
- Affected users cannot access core functions such as sharing, deleting, or further editing once the app becomes unresponsive.
- Google support has acknowledged the reports but suggested troubleshooting steps have failed to resolve the issue.
- The glitch emerged following a major app update that introduced Gemini Spark integration and new "Moods" photo styles.
- Users have been advised to submit official bug reports to help Google engineers identify the root cause of the software failure.

### Why it Matters
This technical issue disrupts a primary utility for Pixel owners, potentially impacting the reliability of Google's flagship photo management software. If the bug remains unpatched, it could hinder the adoption of recently launched AI-powered editing features and frustrate users who rely on the platform for daily image processing.
