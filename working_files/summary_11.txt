Tech Tips for Parents this Digital Parenting Week
============================================================

During Digital Parenting Week, the Electronic Frontier Foundation is encouraging families to co-create collaborative digital safety plans to protect children from online harms while maintaining their personal privacy.

### Key Points
- Establish open communication with children about their favorite apps, games, and social media platforms to build trust.
- Use secure messaging services like Signal, which offers end-to-end encryption and minimal data collection for family communications.
- Regularly review app permissions, location settings, and photo-sharing habits to prevent the accidental disclosure of personal information.
- Implement strong, unique passwords and two-factor authentication across all household devices to improve overall security hygiene.
- Install privacy-focused tools like the EFF’s Privacy Badger to automatically protect family members from online tracking.

### Why it Matters
Developing a proactive digital safety strategy helps families balance the benefits of internet connectivity with the necessity of protecting sensitive personal data. By fostering digital literacy rather than relying solely on restrictive monitoring, parents can empower children to navigate online environments safely and independently.
