American investors are touring China’s robot factories to size up the competition
============================================================

Silicon Valley venture capitalists are increasingly traveling to China to evaluate the country's advanced robotics and hardware capabilities, aiming to assess competitive threats to their own domestic portfolio companies.

### Key Points
- Chinese firms Unitree and AgiBot accounted for 71% of global humanoid robot shipments last year.
- China controls 63% of the global supply chain for essential humanoid-robot components.
- Silicon Valley firms including Eclipse, G2 Venture Partners, and Founders Fund have sent delegations to tour Chinese factories and tech hubs.
- Investors are paying up to $10,000 for curated tours that provide access to Chinese robotics manufacturers, EV makers, and AI developers.
- The Chinese government has established a national policy to deploy humanoid robots across its economy by 2027.

### Why it Matters
These excursions signal a shift in how American investors perceive China's technological maturity, moving from dismissal to urgent competitive analysis. By witnessing China's manufacturing scale and data-collection infrastructure firsthand, venture capitalists are now integrating geopolitical risk and supply chain dependency into their long-term investment strategies.
