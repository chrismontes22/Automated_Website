You Can Soon Get Pre-IPO Exposure on Binance Wallet, But There's A Catch
============================================================

Binance Wallet and PancakeSwap have launched Pre-Access campaigns allowing users to purchase tokenized exposure to private companies before they undergo an initial public offering on traditional stock exchanges.

### Key Points
- The new Pre-Access campaigns determine token allocations based on Alpha Points, bStocks on-chain tiers, and Trencher Badge ownership.
- Tokenized holders receive no voting rights, dividends, or formal shareholder protections, and Binance warns of significant risks regarding liquidity and valuation.
- This initiative follows Binance’s recent expansion into traditional assets, including the launch of US equities trading and pre-IPO perpetual futures.
- Data from RWA.xyz shows record demand for tokenized stocks, with 3.7 million holders and over $3 billion in distributed value as of September 19.
- The first private company featured in the Pre-Access program has not yet been publicly identified by the exchange.

### Why it Matters
This expansion reflects a growing market trend where crypto exchanges bridge the gap between decentralized finance and traditional private equity markets. While these products offer retail investors early access to high-profile companies, the lack of standard shareholder rights highlights the speculative nature of these digital assets.
