A.I. Is Now a Major Election Issue
============================================================

Growing public opposition to massive artificial intelligence data centers has prompted politicians across the political spectrum to enact moratoriums and distance themselves from the controversial infrastructure projects.

### Key Points
- Gallup polling indicates 70% of Americans oppose the construction of hyper-scale data centers in their local communities.
- New York Governor Kathy Hochul enacted the first statewide moratorium on new data center construction in July.
- Texas Governor Greg Abbott suspended all state data center projects pending an audit of their costs and community benefits.
- Data Center Watch reported that at least 75 projects, totaling approximately $130 billion, were halted during the first three months of 2026.
- Critics cite concerns over the high consumption of electricity and water, as well as the minimal long-term job creation provided by these facilities.

### Why it Matters
The widespread backlash against data centers highlights a growing disconnect between the tech industry's rapid expansion and the economic anxieties of local communities. This political shift forces candidates to address whether artificial intelligence infrastructure serves the public interest or primarily benefits a small group of technology billionaires.
