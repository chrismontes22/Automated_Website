Google’s new Pixel 11 offers an incredible camera feature that Apple should copy
============================================================

Google has introduced the Pixel 11 smartphone lineup featuring new Camera Looks, a suite of customizable processing tools designed to give users greater control over their photographic aesthetic.

### Key Points
- The Pixel 11 introduces Camera Looks, which allows users to adjust image processing settings before capturing a photo.
- New preset options include Editorial, Velvet, Classic, Digi, Black Tie, and Minimal.
- The Minimal setting reduces HDR processing to provide a more natural, less digitally altered image.
- The Digi preset mimics the high-contrast, noisy aesthetic of compact digital cameras from the 2010s.
- These features offer granular control over image output, moving away from the heavy post-processing typical of modern smartphone sensors.

### Why it Matters
This shift toward customizable, low-processed photography reflects a growing consumer preference for artistic expression over automated image enhancement. By adopting similar features, smartphone manufacturers can better cater to users who want to move beyond the standard, highly processed look of modern mobile cameras.
