Snapchat joins other popular platforms in fight against 'AI slop'
============================================================

Snapchat is restricting the promotion of entirely AI-generated videos in its Spotlight feed to prioritize authentic human content, joining a growing industry movement against low-quality AI slop.

### Key Points
- Snap will stop recommending wholly AI-generated videos in the Snapchat Spotlight feed to improve user experience.
- The policy distinguishes between low-quality AI content and AI-enhanced or edited media, which remains permitted.
- LinkedIn recently introduced a reporting feature for AI-generated posts and has blocked billions of automated comment attempts.
- YouTube updated its monetization policies to exclude generic, repetitive, or template-based videos often produced by generative AI tools.
- Substack launched a detection tool for AI-generated writing, citing research that up to 40% of social media content may be synthetic.

### Why it Matters
These coordinated efforts reflect a broader industry shift to preserve platform integrity and user trust as generative AI tools make low-quality content easier to produce at scale. By prioritizing human-made media, these companies aim to prevent a "race to the bottom" where automated content farms degrade the overall quality of social media feeds.
