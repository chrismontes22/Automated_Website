Gurman: iPhone Ultra Wows Early Testers, Except for Its Camera
============================================================

Apple is reportedly preparing to launch its first foldable smartphone, the iPhone Ultra, featuring a book-style design and advanced display technology, at an event this September.

### Key Points
- The device features a 5.5-inch outer display and a 7.8-inch inner screen, maintaining a slim 4.5mm profile when unfolded.
- Internal testing highlights praise for the durable hinge mechanism and optimized iPad-like app layouts for the larger display.
- The camera system includes a 48-megapixel main lens and an ultra-wide lens, though it notably lacks a telephoto option.
- Apple has opted for side-button integrated Touch ID rather than in-display Face ID to preserve the device's thin form factor.
- Expected to launch around September 9 alongside the iPhone 18 series, the device may carry a starting price between $2,000 and $2,500.

### Why it Matters
This release marks Apple’s official entry into the competitive foldable smartphone market, challenging established devices like the Samsung Z Fold series. The high price point and specific hardware compromises will test whether Apple’s brand loyalty can drive mass adoption of foldable technology.
