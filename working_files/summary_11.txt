Is MCP becoming the common foundation of smart, decentralised search?
============================================================

The Model Context Protocol (MCP) is transforming AI search by enabling assistants to query live data sources directly, ensuring verifiable, up-to-date information without relying on static training memory.

### Key Points
- Launched by Anthropic in 2024, the open-source MCP protocol was transferred to the Linux Foundation’s Agentic AI Foundation in December 2025.
- MCP allows AI assistants to fetch real-time data from external servers, providing users with clear citations, licensing information, and source attribution.
- The protocol decentralizes search by allowing publishers to maintain control over their data rather than relying on centralized search engine indexing.
- Current development focuses on improving citation standards, establishing content trust, and implementing privacy-friendly traffic measurement for publishers.

### Why it Matters
By shifting from centralized indexing to direct, real-time queries, MCP addresses critical issues regarding AI hallucinations and outdated information. This transition empowers content creators to maintain ownership of their data while ensuring that AI-generated answers remain transparent, accurate, and legally compliant.
