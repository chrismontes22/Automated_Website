2024 vs. 2026 Mac Mini Buyer's Guide
============================================================

Apple has launched its new Mac mini featuring M6 and M5 Pro chips, offering significant AI performance gains and improved display support with a starting price of $899.

### Key Points
- The new Mac mini models arrive in stores and to customers on September 22, with pre-orders currently open.
- Pricing starts at $899 for the M6 model and $1,699 for the M5 Pro, reflecting a $100 increase over previous final retail prices.
- Key hardware upgrades include Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6, Thread networking, and enhanced display support for high refresh rates up to 165Hz.
- Apple claims the M6 chip delivers up to 4x faster AI performance and 4.8x faster prompt processing compared to the previous M4 generation.
- The M5 Pro configuration offers up to 18 CPU cores and 307GB/s memory bandwidth, catering to users running large local AI models.

### Why it Matters
This release prioritizes AI-driven workflows by integrating dedicated neural accelerators into the GPU, marking a significant shift in Apple's desktop hardware strategy. While general users may see minimal daily performance gains, the increased memory bandwidth and display capabilities provide a necessary upgrade for professionals working with high-refresh monitors and local large language models.
