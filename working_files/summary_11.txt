Bitcoin slips under $63,000 despite Iran deal hopes as Coldcard losses rattle market
============================================================

Bitcoin prices fell to $62,800 on Monday as a significant security exploit involving Coldcard hardware wallets overshadowed positive macroeconomic developments regarding U.S.-Iran diplomatic talks and falling oil prices.

### Key Points
- Bitcoin dropped 1% to $62,800, while ether declined to $1,858 despite broader market gains in stocks and gold.
- A series of exploits targeting Coldcard hardware wallets has resulted in the theft of approximately 1,367 bitcoin, valued at nearly $89 million.
- The security breach has affected 4,585 individual addresses across three distinct waves of attacks.
- Brent crude futures fell 7.3% to $81.55 following reports of potential U.S.-Iran negotiations, easing global inflation concerns.
- The 10-year Treasury yield decreased by four basis points to 4.69%, reflecting improved investor sentiment outside of the cryptocurrency sector.

### Why it Matters
The decoupling of cryptocurrency prices from positive macroeconomic indicators suggests that internal security concerns are currently outweighing broader market trends. Investors are closely monitoring whether bitcoin can maintain the $62,000 support level as the market attempts to recover from the ongoing hardware wallet exploit.
