Transcript: Trump’s Angry New Tirades on AI Wreck GOP Midterm Strategy
============================================================

Donald Trump’s vocal opposition to AI regulation is creating a political rift, complicating GOP efforts to address voter concerns regarding data centers and the rapid expansion of artificial intelligence.

### Key Points
- Donald Trump dismissed AI safety warnings as a "hoax" on Truth Social, contradicting calls from industry leaders like Anthropic for a global development slowdown.
- An NBC poll indicates that 70% of Americans are more concerned than excited about AI, with data centers proving particularly unpopular across the political spectrum.
- House Republicans are currently attempting to pass legislation to limit data center costs, despite Trump’s public insistence that the industry should remain unregulated.
- A bipartisan coalition, including figures like Bernie Sanders and Steve Bannon, is emerging to demand stricter oversight of AI infrastructure and development.
- Analysts suggest Trump’s stance is influenced by his family’s financial investments in the tech sector and a desire to maintain an image of unchecked winning.

### Why it Matters
The disconnect between Trump’s rhetoric and voter sentiment threatens to undermine the Republican Party's midterm messaging by alienating moderates and independents. By failing to align with the growing bipartisan demand for AI oversight, the GOP risks appearing out of touch while providing Democrats a clear opportunity to frame the party as beholden to tech oligarchs.
