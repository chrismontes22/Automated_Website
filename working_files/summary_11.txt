The Elon Musk Funder Trying to Sway the Race for LA’s Top Lawyer
============================================================

Tech investors and billionaire donors are pouring millions into the Los Angeles city attorney race to oppose DSA-backed candidate Marissa Roy and support challenger John McKinney.

### Key Points
- Tech investor John Hering has contributed over $1.25 million to defeat Marissa Roy, a California Department of Justice attorney known for pursuing Big Tech settlements.
- Pro-McKinney committees have received more than $1.9 million from various tech founders and investors, plus $4.2 million from an Airbnb-backed group.
- Marissa Roy has pledged to use the city attorney’s office to regulate corporations, specifically targeting labor misclassification and AI-driven worker exploitation.
- John McKinney, a county prosecutor, is running on a law-and-order platform while receiving significant financial backing from real estate and tech interests.
- The spending reflects a broader national trend of tech industry influence in local and state elections to preemptively block restrictive AI and labor legislation.

### Why it Matters
This race highlights the growing trend of billionaire-backed political spending aimed at influencing local offices that hold significant regulatory power over corporate business models. Because California often serves as a legislative blueprint for the rest of the country, tech leaders view the outcome as a critical defense against future labor and AI regulations.
