Who is Lakshmi Venu and what is Venu Srinivasan's daughter's role in the TVS family succession row?
============================================================

TVS Motor Company leaders Venu Srinivasan and his family are currently navigating a private dispute regarding the equitable allocation of economic ownership and family wealth among members.

### Key Points
- The dispute involves Venu Srinivasan, his son Sudarshan Venu, his wife Mallika Srinivasan, and their daughter Lakshmi Venu.
- Lakshmi Venu and Mallika Srinivasan claim no transparent economic settlement or valuation has been reached regarding the family's internal wealth interests.
- The disagreement follows a broader 2020 TVS family partition and a March 2024 Memorandum of Understanding concerning brand usage and non-compete agreements.
- TVS Holdings maintains a 74.45% promoter stake, with Venu Srinivasan controlling 66.55% of the company through the VS Trust.
- TAFE, led by Mallika and Lakshmi Venu, remains a separate entity from the TVS Motor Company and TVS Holdings business structure.

### Why it Matters
This internal conflict highlights the complexities of succession and wealth distribution within one of India’s most prominent industrial dynasties. The resolution of these economic interests could influence the governance and stability of major entities like TVS Motor Company and TAFE.
