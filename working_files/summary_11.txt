Sam Altman-backed bitcoin insurer Meanwhile secures $37.5 million in Bain Capital Crypto-led round
============================================================

Bitcoin-focused life insurer Meanwhile, backed by Sam Altman, has raised $37.5 million in a funding round led by Bain Capital Crypto to support its international expansion efforts.

### Key Points
- The Bermuda-based company has secured a total of $180 million in funding to date.
- Meanwhile offers life insurance policies where both premiums and death benefits are denominated entirely in bitcoin.
- The firm recently launched its BTC Life 1-Pay product, which allows for a single bitcoin premium payment.
- The company holds an insurance license from the Bermuda Monetary Authority granted in 2024.
- Meanwhile has partnered with 15 brokers to serve high-net-worth clients in Switzerland, Singapore, Hong Kong, and the UAE.

### Why it Matters
- This funding highlights the growing integration of cryptocurrency into traditional wealth management and estate planning sectors. By offering bitcoin-denominated insurance, the company aims to transition digital assets from speculative investments into functional financial tools for long-term inheritance and protection.
