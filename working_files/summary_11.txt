Don't leave AI to tech bros and oligarchs, UK's AI minister says
============================================================

UK AI Minister Kanishka Narayan has warned that the government must assert democratic control over artificial intelligence to prevent the technology from being dominated by private tech interests.

### Key Points
- Kanishka Narayan, the UK's first cabinet minister for AI, stated that the nation must build domestic technological capacity rather than relying solely on foreign-developed systems.
- The minister compared the current AI shift to the Industrial Revolution, emphasizing the need to manage the transition to avoid widespread social and economic hardship.
- Government officials recently utilized frontier AI models to identify 500 novel cybersecurity vulnerabilities within the public sector at a cost of £13,000.
- Narayan highlighted the UK's influence in AI safety, noting that major US laboratories are seeking testing services from the British AI Security Institute.
- The comments follow Barclays' recent announcement of 5,000 job cuts driven by an AI-led efficiency strategy.

### Why it Matters
This policy shift signals a move toward greater state intervention in the development and deployment of emerging technologies to protect national security and labor markets. By prioritizing domestic capability, the government aims to reduce reliance on international tech giants and ensure that AI integration does not destabilize the broader economy.
