Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang Says AI Hype Is Fueling Fear: ‘We’re Scaring People’
============================================================

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang has urged industry leaders to stop spreading alarmist predictions about artificial intelligence, arguing that current narratives regarding job displacement and existential threats are largely unfounded.

### Key Points
- Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang currently oversees the world’s most valuable company, which holds a market capitalization of $5 trillion.
- Huang criticized peers for promoting "made up" stories about AI, such as the singularity or the possibility that humanity lives in a simulation.
- The CEO emphasized that widespread job losses have not materialized and encouraged a more enthusiastic, balanced approach to AI adoption.
- Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei remains a prominent cautionary voice, previously warning that AI could displace half of entry-level white-collar roles.
- Huang currently ranks as the seventh-richest person globally, with a personal fortune estimated at $173 billion.

### Why it Matters
This public disagreement highlights a growing ideological divide between industry leaders regarding the responsible communication of AI capabilities and risks. As Nvidia continues to dominate the hardware market, Huang’s push for grounded discourse aims to maintain public confidence and prevent regulatory hurdles driven by speculative fears.
