Hiding In Plain Sight: Top Execs On Unlocking The Value In Legacy IP
============================================================

Industry leaders at MIPCOM discussed how revitalizing legacy intellectual property serves as a strategic engine for global content hits, offering proven audience demand and significant revenue potential.

### Key Points
- Data from Parrot Analytics shows TV shows based on existing IP generate three-and-a-half times the demand of original content.
- Producers are increasingly using a "B2C2B" strategy, building massive audiences on platforms like YouTube before selling content to traditional broadcasters.
- Companies like Banijay and WildBrain focus on managing "superbrands" and evergreen franchises to ensure long-term relevance across merchandising and live events.
- Financial firms like Easttree Media are helping rights holders unlock immediate capital by purchasing passive profit participations in long-running film and TV catalogs.
- Versos AI is enabling studios to monetize dormant library assets by licensing raw video footage for the training of artificial intelligence models.

### Why it Matters
Legacy IP provides a safer investment for studios facing budget constraints by offering a built-in audience and established brand recognition. By leveraging data analytics and new monetization channels like AI training, rights holders can transform stagnant library assets into consistent, high-value revenue streams.
