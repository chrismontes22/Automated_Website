Berlin Is Being Blackmailed By Hackers
============================================================

Berlin Mayor Kai Wegner has refused to pay a ransom demand from the Rhysida hacking group after the cybercriminals stole 5.79 terabytes of data from city systems.

### Key Points
- The Rhysida ransomware group is demanding 30 bitcoin, approximately 2 million euros, to prevent the release of stolen municipal data.
- Hackers compromised city networks between August 7 and August 12, forcing the shutdown of housing benefit and payment application systems on August 14.
- The perpetrators have threatened to auction the stolen data publicly if their financial demands are not met within seven days.
- German state police, prosecutors, and federal security services are currently investigating the scope of the breach and the identity of the attackers.
- The Rhysida group is suspected of operating out of Russia and Eastern Europe and was previously linked to an attack on the British Museum.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the growing vulnerability of municipal infrastructure to sophisticated ransomware attacks that disrupt essential public services. The city's refusal to pay sets a firm precedent for handling digital extortion, though it leaves the sensitive data of residents at risk of being leaked.
