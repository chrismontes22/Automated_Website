US backs Elon Musk's bid to overturn €120m EU fine against X
============================================================

The United States Department of Justice has formally intervened in Elon Musk’s legal challenge against a €120 million European Union fine imposed on X for deceptive verification practices.

### Key Points
- The European Commission fined X €120 million in December 2025 for allegedly deceiving users through its paid blue tick verification system.
- The US Department of Justice filed an application with the EU’s General Court to support X, citing concerns over international territorial jurisdiction.
- US officials, including Secretary of State Marco Rubio, argue the fine represents an overreach by foreign regulators against American technology companies.
- The European Commission maintains the fine is a necessary enforcement of the Digital Services Act to protect digital standards and user rights.
- X faces additional EU investigations regarding its AI assistant, Grok, and concerns over transparency in advertising and data access for researchers.

### Why it Matters
This intervention signals a significant escalation in the regulatory conflict between the United States and the European Union regarding the oversight of American digital platforms. The outcome of this case could establish a critical legal precedent for how international law applies to the extraterritorial enforcement of the EU's Digital Services Act.
