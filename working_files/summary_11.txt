Holtec Nuclear Targets $10.2 Billion Valuation in Nasdaq IPO
============================================================

Holtec Nuclear Corporation plans to raise up to $900 million in a Nasdaq initial public offering, targeting a valuation of $10.2 billion to accelerate its nuclear technology development.

### Key Points
- Holtec is offering 50 million Class A shares priced between $15 and $18, with an option for underwriters to purchase an additional 7.5 million shares.
- Proceeds will fund the deployment of the SMR-300 small modular reactor and the expansion of manufacturing capacity for energy technologies.
- The company is working to restart the 800-MW Palisades nuclear plant in Michigan by 2026, supported by a $1.52 billion Department of Energy loan guarantee.
- The Palisades site is slated to host the first two SMR-300 units, Pioneer One and Pioneer Two, with commercial operations targeted for the early 2030s.
- The IPO follows a surge in investor interest in nuclear power driven by rising electricity demand from artificial intelligence and data center operations.

### Why it Matters
This public offering highlights the growing role of nuclear energy in meeting the massive power requirements of the modern technology sector. By securing capital for both reactor restarts and modular technology, Holtec is positioning itself as a central player in the transition toward carbon-free, high-capacity electricity generation.
