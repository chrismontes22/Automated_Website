5 Cool MagSafe Gadgets That Are Not Mounts, Wallets, Or Power Banks
============================================================

Beyond standard mounts and wallets, a variety of innovative MagSafe-compatible accessories now offer specialized functionality for smartphone users, ranging from professional-grade storage solutions to mobile gaming controllers.

### Key Points
- The Hagibis MC100 Pro SSD Enclosure supports up to 2TB of storage, enabling high-speed data transfers for iPhone ProRes video workflows.
- The ULANZI M10 Magnetic Selfie Light provides five brightness levels and 360-degree rotation, offering up to 4.5 hours of illumination on a single charge.
- The TELESIN Magnetic Phone Camera Grip improves ergonomics for photography and includes a Bluetooth remote shutter functional up to 32 feet away.
- The abxylute M4 Snap-On Mobile Gaming Controller offers cross-platform compatibility for iOS and Android, providing physical controls for mobile gaming.
- The Fosi Audio MD3 Headphone Amplifier utilizes an ESS ES9039Q2M DAC to enhance audio quality for wired headphones while supporting pass-through charging.

### Why it Matters
These accessories demonstrate the expanding utility of magnetic mounting systems beyond basic charging and storage. By integrating specialized hardware directly onto the phone chassis, these devices provide professional-grade features to mobile users without the need for cumbersome cables or bulky external mounts.
