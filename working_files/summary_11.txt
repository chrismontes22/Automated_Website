Closed foldables cause Android Auto calls to fail: Moto and Google are investigating
============================================================

Android Auto users report a persistent bug preventing phone calls when using foldable devices like the Motorola Razr while the phone remains in a closed state.

### Key Points
- Users report that Android Auto fails to process calls unless foldable devices are physically opened.
- The issue appears linked to the recent integration of Gemini, as reverting to the classic Google Assistant resolves the connectivity failure.
- Reports have surfaced across Reddit, Google’s official support forums, and Motorola’s community pages.
- Both Google and Motorola have acknowledged the bug and are currently gathering information from affected users to develop a fix.

### Why it Matters
This bug highlights the ongoing integration challenges between evolving AI assistants and specialized hardware form factors. For drivers, the issue creates a significant safety and convenience concern by forcing manual interaction with mobile devices while operating a vehicle.
