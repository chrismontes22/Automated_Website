Inside Jason Kelce’s Potty Humor Marketing Ploy Against AI Data Centers
============================================================

Former NFL player Jason Kelce is starring in a viral marketing campaign for Garage Beer that uses humor to protest the environmental impact of AI data centers.

### Key Points
- The campaign features a satirical ad encouraging viewers to send urine to AI data centers to highlight the massive water consumption required for server cooling.
- Large data centers can consume up to 5 million gallons of water daily, equivalent to the usage of a town with 50,000 residents.
- Energy demands are also surging, with projects like Meta’s Hyperion campus in Louisiana projected to use three times the daily power of New Orleans.
- A recent Gallup poll shows 71% of Americans oppose the construction of new AI data centers in their local communities.
- Environmental activist Erin Brockovich maintains a database tracking the nearly 4,000 AI data centers currently operating across the United States.

### Why it Matters
The campaign highlights growing public frustration over the resource-intensive nature of AI infrastructure and the lack of transparency regarding its local impact. As these facilities become a significant political issue ahead of midterm elections, companies face increasing pressure to address concerns about utility costs, water scarcity, and environmental degradation.
