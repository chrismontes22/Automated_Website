"This one is especially bad": Steam game about mistreating ragdolls suffers second bout of mod-based malware this year
============================================================

Developer Mestiez has disabled the Steam Workshop for the sandbox game People Playground following the discovery of a malicious mod capable of compromising user data and system files.

### Key Points
- The developer identified a severe malware threat distributed through a mod on September 21 between 6PM and 8PM CEST.
- Affected users are advised to run anti-virus scans, delete all local mods, and change passwords for important accounts.
- The malware can potentially wipe personal data, vandalize Steam configuration files, and leak Discord information.
- This incident marks the second time this year that People Playground has faced a security breach via the Steam Workshop.
- Mestiez has instructed players to keep the game closed until an official safety announcement is posted on Steam.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the ongoing security risks associated with user-generated content platforms and the potential for malicious actors to exploit game modding ecosystems. It serves as a critical reminder for players to exercise caution when downloading community mods and underscores the challenges developers face in maintaining platform safety.
