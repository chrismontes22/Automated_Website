The Samsung T9 is our favorite portable SSD ever — and it's down to $240 at Amazon
============================================================

The Samsung T9 portable SSD is currently discounted at Amazon, offering high-speed performance and durable hardware encryption for users requiring fast data transfers for professional or personal projects.

### Key Points
- The 1TB Samsung T9 is priced at $240, while the 2TB model is available for $405.
- The drive utilizes a USB 3.2 Gen 2x2 interface to reach sequential read and write speeds of up to 2,000MB/s.
- Security features include XTS-AES 256-bit hardware encryption and a five-year limited warranty.
- The device is drop-resistant up to 3 meters and includes both USB-C and USB-A connectivity cables.
- Performance benchmarks confirm the drive is competitive with similar high-speed storage options from Crucial and Kingston.

### Why it Matters
This discount improves the competitive value of the Samsung T9, which previously faced criticism for being overpriced compared to similar market alternatives. The drive remains a top-tier choice for users with compatible USB 3.2 Gen 2x2 hardware who need reliable, high-speed storage for large media files.
