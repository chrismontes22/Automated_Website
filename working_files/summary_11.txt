Here's What Apple's Apps Look Like on the iPhone Duo
============================================================

Apple is preparing for the October 23 launch of the iPhone Duo by updating App Store screenshots to showcase how built-in applications adapt to the device's foldable display.

### Key Points
- The iPhone Duo features a versatile interface that supports landscape, portrait, and laptop-style orientations for various apps.
- Built-in apps like Contacts and Preview utilize sidebars, split-screen views, and bottom tool palettes to maximize the expanded screen real estate.
- The device includes a vertical dock and navigation bars that maintain consistent placement across different folding positions.
- The inner display hides the FaceTime camera under the screen, while the Dynamic Island expands from the side for alerts and Live Activities.
- Users can transition between open and closed states, with the device supporting multitasking features like side-by-side app usage.

### Why it Matters
This launch represents a significant shift in Apple's mobile hardware strategy by introducing a foldable form factor that requires a redesigned software interface. The success of the iPhone Duo will likely influence future app development standards and set a new benchmark for how iOS adapts to non-traditional screen sizes.
