Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra vs. Motorola Razr Fold: Choosing the right book-style foldable
============================================================

The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra and Motorola Razr Fold have launched as premium book-style foldables, offering distinct hardware advantages in battery capacity, processing power, and pricing.

### Key Points
- The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra features the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 processor, a 200MP primary camera, and a 5,000mAh battery.
- Motorola’s Razr Fold enters the market with a 6,000mAh battery, 80W wired charging, and an 8.1-inch inner display.
- Samsung’s device starts at $2,099, while the Motorola Razr Fold is priced lower at $1,899.
- The Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra is thinner at 4.1mm unfolded, whereas the Razr Fold offers stylus support and a 165Hz cover display.
- Both devices were released in 2026, with Samsung focusing on refined hardware and Motorola emphasizing aggressive specifications.

### Why it Matters
The competition between these two devices highlights a shift in the foldable market as manufacturers prioritize battery life and charging speeds to differentiate their flagship offerings. Consumers now face a choice between Samsung’s established software ecosystem and Motorola’s aggressive hardware-to-price ratio.
