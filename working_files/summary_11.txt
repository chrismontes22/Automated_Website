Cyber insurance gap leaves UK property businesses dangerously exposed: GlobalData
============================================================

UK property and real estate businesses face growing cyber threats, yet insurance adoption remains significantly lower than the broader SME market, leaving many firms financially vulnerable to attacks.

### Key Points
- Cyberattacks against UK property services companies rose by 17% in 2025, reaching 208 reported incidents.
- Cyber insurance penetration in the real estate sector lags behind the wider SME market across all business sizes.
- Conveyancing fraud resulted in £11.7 million in losses between April 2024 and March 2025, with 143 recorded cases.
- Property firms are high-value targets due to the sensitive financial and personal data they store for transactions.
- Medium-sized property businesses report a 55% insurance uptake, while sole traders remain the least protected at 7.7%.

### Why it Matters
- The widening protection gap leaves many property firms exposed to significant financial losses from data breaches and payment redirection fraud. Insurers have a clear opportunity to capture this underserved market by simplifying coverage and better educating small business owners on digital risks.
