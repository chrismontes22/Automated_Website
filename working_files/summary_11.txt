Hugging Face built a $4.5 billion empire on free AI models. Now Nvidia is buying it for $12.9 billion
============================================================

Nvidia has agreed to acquire the open-source AI platform Hugging Face for $12.9 billion, marking a significant consolidation of the AI ecosystem and its underlying developer infrastructure.

### Key Points
- The $12.9 billion acquisition values Hugging Face at approximately 86 times its $150 million in annualized revenue.
- Hugging Face hosts over 4 million open-source models and serves as a primary hub for AI researchers and developers.
- The company has expanded into physical robotics, including the Reachy Mini and Microduck, and enterprise-grade blob storage solutions.
- Nvidia aims to secure a critical distribution layer for open-source AI while integrating Hugging Face’s managed compute and cloud services.
- Hugging Face maintains a team of 250 employees and has completed six talent-driven acquisitions to date.

### Why it Matters
This acquisition allows Nvidia to control the primary gateway for open-source AI development, effectively bridging the gap between its hardware dominance and the software ecosystem. By absorbing Hugging Face, Nvidia gains a direct channel to millions of developers and a strategic outlet for its excess cloud computing capacity.
