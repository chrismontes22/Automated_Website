HSBC, Ant Digital test AI-agent payments using tokenized deposits
============================================================

HSBC and Ant Digital Technologies have successfully completed a technical trial enabling AI agents to perform real-time micropayments using tokenized bank deposits on a blockchain testnet.

### Key Points
- The trial integrated HSBC’s Tokenised Deposit Service with Ant Digital’s Anvita Flow network and the Jovay Testnet.
- AI agents demonstrated the ability to autonomously discover digital services and execute payments typically valued at less than $2.
- HSBC provided the necessary settlement infrastructure and real-time risk assessment protocols for the automated transactions.
- The demonstration was limited to technical verification and does not currently represent a live commercial product for bank customers.
- Other major financial institutions, including Santander, Sygnum, and CaixaBank, are also actively testing AI-agent-initiated payment systems.

### Why it Matters
This collaboration highlights a growing industry shift toward integrating programmable financial infrastructure to support autonomous AI-driven commerce. As AI agents increasingly operate 24/7, traditional banking systems may require blockchain-based solutions to facilitate the high-frequency, automated transactions that legacy platforms were not originally designed to handle.
