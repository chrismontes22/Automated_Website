Tech Odyssey Series: From Portugal to China, a New Conversation Begins
============================================================

The Tech Odyssey web series, produced by AICEP, highlights Portugal’s growing technology sector to foster international business partnerships and investment opportunities between Portuguese companies and the Chinese market.

### Key Points
- AICEP, Portugal’s trade and investment agency, produced the Tech Odyssey series to showcase the country's innovation ecosystem to global audiences.
- The series highlights diverse sectors including health technology, biotechnology, spatial audio, and the blue economy.
- Luís Costa, director of AICEP Shanghai, emphasizes Portugal's appeal through its renewable energy, strategic location, and openness to foreign investment.
- The program aims to facilitate two-way growth by helping Portuguese startups expand into China while attracting Chinese companies to invest in Portugal.
- The series is available on platforms like YouTube, LinkedIn, and Bilibili to serve as a long-term resource for international business development.

### Why it Matters
This initiative demonstrates how digital media can effectively bridge geographical gaps to stimulate cross-border economic cooperation and foreign direct investment. By highlighting specific industrial strengths, AICEP provides a practical framework for companies to evaluate new markets and establish strategic international partnerships.
