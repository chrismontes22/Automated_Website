Florida Police Arrested a Self-Described Demonologist After Two Women Said He Accessed Their Private Information and Posted It on Tiktok
============================================================

Port St. Lucie resident Mark Stephen Asea was arrested on a felony charge of unauthorized computer access after allegedly exposing private information about two women in TikTok videos.

### Key Points
- Mark Stephen Asea, 36, faces a third-degree felony charge for unauthorized computer access following an investigation by Port St. Lucie Police.
- Victims reported that Asea accessed sensitive data, including banking details, passwords, and private photos, after providing him with technical assistance.
- Investigators allege Asea featured victims in over 100 TikTok videos and maintained a website to display unauthorized personal content.
- Police seized various electronic devices, including computers, hard drives, and networking equipment, during a search of Asea’s residence.
- Asea is currently held on a $35,000 bond, with his arraignment scheduled for November 19, 2026.

### Why it Matters
This case highlights the significant security risks associated with granting remote computer access to individuals for technical support. It serves as a reminder for users to exercise extreme caution when sharing passwords or allowing third parties to manage personal devices.
