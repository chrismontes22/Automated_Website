Smart Contact Lenses May Arrive as Soon as 2030. Here’s What They Do
============================================================

Dubai-based deep-tech company Xpanceo is developing AI-powered smart contact lenses capable of health monitoring and augmented reality, with a public prototype reveal scheduled for MWC Barcelona in 2027.

### Key Points
- Xpanceo has developed 28 working prototypes using over 50 patents to integrate optoelectronic sensors into biocompatible, breathable polymer lenses.
- The system utilizes an external companion device worn on the head to handle computing, wireless charging, and data connectivity.
- Integrated biosensors monitor tear fluid to track glucose levels, electrolytes, and intraocular pressure without requiring invasive blood draws.
- The company plans to launch initial B2B applications for professional athletes and medical patients before targeting a general consumer release by 2030.
- Future development goals include creating a non-invasive brain-computer interface that leverages the eye's direct connection to the brain.

### Why it Matters
This technology represents a significant shift in wearable computing by moving digital interfaces and health diagnostics directly onto the human cornea. If successful, these lenses could transform proactive healthcare and personal connectivity, potentially establishing a new category of consumer electronics that rivals the utility of modern smartphones.
