How AIPAC and AI Spent Their Money This Midterm Election
============================================================

The current election cycle has become the most expensive in history, with over $16 billion raised as special interest groups and mega-donors exert significant influence on political outcomes.

### Key Points
- Total campaign ad spending for House and Senate races has surpassed $4 billion, according to data from AdImpact.
- Mega-donors, including Elon Musk, have directed massive contributions toward Republican candidates and aligned super PACs.
- The American Israel Public Affairs Committee (AIPAC) and its United Democracy Project continue to spend heavily despite growing public scrutiny.
- AI-affiliated super PACs, including those linked to OpenAI and Anthropic, spent $50.7 million during primaries but scaled back operations for the general election.
- Tech industry leaders are increasingly funding local and federal races to influence future regulatory frameworks regarding AI, crypto, and data centers.

### Why it Matters
The influx of record-breaking capital from Big Tech and special interest groups suggests that corporate entities are prioritizing the long-term prevention of regulation over specific partisan outcomes. This trend highlights a growing disconnect between public sentiment and the financial forces shaping the legislative agenda in Congress.
