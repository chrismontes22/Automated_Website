Skydancing At Last: $110B Merger Of Paramount And Warner Bros. Discovery Officially Closes
============================================================

Skydance has officially finalized its merger with Paramount and Warner Bros. Discovery, creating a massive new media entity that will begin trading on the New York Stock Exchange.

### Key Points
- David Ellison will lead the combined company as chairman and CEO, backed by his father, Oracle co-founder Larry Ellison.
- The new entity inherits approximately $80 billion in debt while aiming to transition into a tech-oriented operation to compete with Netflix and Disney.
- Shares of the combined company will trade on the NYSE, replacing the former listings of Paramount and Warner Bros. Discovery on the Nasdaq.
- A settlement with 12 state attorneys general requires the company to adhere to a five-year consent decree, including behavioral remedies for pay-TV negotiations.
- The deal includes a vast portfolio of assets, including HBO, CNN, and various film studios, following a year-long acquisition process.

### Why it Matters
This merger represents a significant consolidation of legacy media assets that must now navigate a challenging financial landscape defined by heavy debt and shifting consumer habits. The company's success will serve as a bellwether for whether traditional studios can effectively pivot to tech-driven streaming models while maintaining their existing broadcast and cable footprints.
