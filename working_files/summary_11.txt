Quote of the day by Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella: 'Our industry does not respect tradition – it only respects innovation' — a communiqué stamping authority
============================================================

Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella has successfully transformed the company over the last decade by shifting focus from desktop software toward cloud computing and aggressive artificial intelligence integration.

### Key Points
- Satya Nadella prioritized cloud-based innovation and connected devices upon succeeding Steve Ballmer as Microsoft CEO.
- The company pivoted toward artificial intelligence by integrating ChatGPT technology into core products like Bing and Windows.
- Microsoft’s Azure cloud platform has achieved significant financial success, recently surpassing $100 billion in annual revenue.
- Infrastructure growth is currently constrained by a global shortage of physical data center space and the energy required for AI model training.

### Why it Matters
Microsoft’s strategic evolution demonstrates how legacy technology giants must continuously adapt their business models to remain competitive in the rapidly changing cloud and AI sectors. This transition highlights the critical infrastructure challenges that major tech firms face as they attempt to scale resource-intensive artificial intelligence services globally.
