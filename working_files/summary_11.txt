Samsung and SK Hynix are upgrading their NAND factories in China
============================================================

Chinese memory manufacturer YMTC has overtaken Samsung and SK Hynix to become the leading producer of NAND flash chips in China, prompting the Korean firms to upgrade facilities.

### Key Points
- YMTC captured 38.5% of the Chinese NAND market this year, surpassing Samsung at 33.5% and SK Hynix at 17%.
- Samsung plans to upgrade its Xian facility to produce 286-layer V9 NAND chips, targeting 40,000 wafers per month by 2027.
- SK Hynix received US authorization to upgrade its Dalian plant from 192-layer to 240-layer chip production.
- The US Bureau of Industry and Security plans to restrict licenses for expanding or upgrading foreign-owned semiconductor factories in China starting in 2025.
- SK Hynix is currently exploring potential NAND production partnerships in the United States to diversify its manufacturing footprint.

### Why it Matters
These production shifts highlight the intense competition in the global memory market as companies navigate tightening US export controls on semiconductor technology. The race to increase layer counts and storage density remains critical for maintaining market share against rising domestic Chinese manufacturers.
