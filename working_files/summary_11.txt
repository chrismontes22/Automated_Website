OpenShot 4.0: Record, Edit, and Color Like Never Before
============================================================

OpenShot 4.0 introduces significant creative upgrades, including native screen recording, advanced color grading tools, local AI-powered subject masking, and a modernized timeline for improved video editing performance.

### Key Points
- New Color View features professional-grade color wheels, curves, LUT support, and live video scopes for precise footage correction.
- Integrated recording tools allow users to capture screen, webcam, microphone, and system audio as separate, editable timeline tracks.
- Ten new effects include audio visualizations, film grain, and displacement maps to enhance creative motion and visual style.
- Local machine learning models enable subject isolation and object masking without requiring cloud subscriptions or internet-based processing.
- The transition to a native Qt 6 timeline provides smoother zooming, improved keyframe management, and better overall interface responsiveness.
- Performance optimizations deliver faster rendering for blur, sharpen, and color grading effects compared to previous versions.

### Why it Matters
This major update positions OpenShot as a more competitive, professional-grade alternative to subscription-based video editing software by prioritizing local privacy and hardware efficiency. By integrating advanced AI and color tools directly into the workflow, the platform significantly lowers the barrier for creators to produce high-quality content without recurring costs.
