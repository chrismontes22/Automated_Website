Be Quiet! Light Mount TKL review
============================================================

The Be Quiet! Light Mount TKL is a compact 80% mechanical keyboard featuring upgraded, ultra-quiet V2 switches and new SOCD gaming capabilities for a refined, noise-conscious typing experience.

### Key Points
- The keyboard utilizes new Silent V2 Linear or Tactile switches, which offer improved lubrication and quieter operation than previous versions.
- It introduces "Quick Tap" (SOCD) functionality, allowing users to prioritize recent directional inputs for more efficient strafing in competitive games.
- The 80% form factor includes a brushed aluminum top plate, double-shot PBT keycaps, and a dedicated volume control knob.
- Customization is managed via the IO Center software, which supports macro recording, RGB lighting adjustments, and Microsoft Dynamic Lighting.
- The device is priced at $140 and features three internal layers of sound-dampening foam to minimize noise during use.

### Why it Matters
The Light Mount TKL serves as a premium solution for users who prioritize a quiet workspace without sacrificing essential gaming features. While it lacks the advanced Hall effect switches found in some competitors, its focus on build quality and noise reduction makes it a strong contender for hybrid office and gaming setups.
