Tencent-backed chipmaker Enflame nearly triples in its Shanghai debut
============================================================

Tencent-backed AI chipmaker Shanghai Enflame Technology saw its shares surge 179% on its Shanghai Star Market debut, reaching a market valuation of approximately 170.9 billion yuan.

### Key Points
- Enflame raised 6.12 billion yuan in its IPO, with shares closing at 397 yuan against an initial offer price of 142.18 yuan.
- The company reported a 2025 net loss of 1.16 billion yuan on 990 million yuan in revenue, with Tencent accounting for 84% of total sales.
- Retail investor demand was exceptionally high, resulting in an allocation rate of just 0.025% for individual participants.
- Founded in 2018 by former AMD executives, the firm focuses on AI chips for training and inference that operate outside the Nvidia ecosystem.
- Proceeds from the listing are earmarked for the development of fifth- and sixth-generation AI chips to compete with international high-end products.

### Why it Matters
This successful debut highlights the intense investor appetite for Chinese firms developing domestic alternatives to Nvidia’s restricted AI hardware. The listing provides critical capital for Enflame to scale its operations and pursue profitability amid China's broader strategic push for semiconductor independence.
