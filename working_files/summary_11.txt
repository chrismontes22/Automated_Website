Google unveils the Pixel 11 Pro and 11 Pro XL, the new Pixel 11 Pro Fold joins them
============================================================

Google has officially unveiled the Pixel 11 series, featuring the new Tensor G6 chipset, advanced Gemini Intelligence capabilities, and a unique HiLight notification LED on the camera island.

### Key Points
- The Pixel 11 lineup includes the Pro, Pro XL, and Pro Fold models, all powered by the 2nm Tensor G6 chipset manufactured by TSMC.
- A new "HiLight" feature integrates a notification LED into the rear camera housing to signal incoming calls and Gemini AI interactions.
- The Pro models feature upgraded camera systems, including a 50MP main sensor and a 48MP periscope lens, while the Pro Fold offers an 8.0-inch inner display.
- RAM configurations now start at 12GB for base models, with higher storage tiers offering 16GB to support intensive AI processing.
- Pre-orders for the new devices are currently open, with general retail availability scheduled to begin on August 20.

### Why it Matters
Google is shifting its hardware strategy to prioritize AI-driven features and custom silicon efficiency over raw battery capacity increases. This transition highlights the company's attempt to differentiate its flagship smartphones in a competitive market by integrating deep software intelligence directly into the physical device design.
