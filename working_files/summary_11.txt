Apple Event on October 13?
============================================================

Apple is reportedly planning an October 13 event to unveil a new smart home hub, a second-generation HomePod mini, and an updated Apple TV 4K model.

### Key Points
- Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman reports the October 13 event will feature three new hardware products.
- The lineup includes an all-new smart home hub, a refreshed HomePod mini, and a new Apple TV 4K.
- Industry analysts expect the event to be an online-only keynote streamed via Apple’s website and YouTube.
- Apple may host an in-person media session following the keynote to provide hands-on demonstrations of the new devices.
- The event timing aligns with historical patterns for distributing review units to journalists and content creators.

### Why it Matters
This event signals a significant expansion of Apple’s smart home ecosystem as the company seeks to compete more aggressively in the connected home market. A successful launch of these devices could strengthen Apple's hardware integration and influence consumer adoption of its home automation interface.
