Jury finds Arm guilty in licensing breach, Qualcomm awarded just $2 in total
============================================================

A Delaware jury ruled that Arm breached its licensing agreement with Qualcomm following the 2021 Nuvia acquisition, awarding Qualcomm two dollars in damages for the contract violations.

### Key Points
- A jury found Arm guilty of breaching its technology license agreement and interfering with Qualcomm’s business relations.
- Qualcomm was awarded nominal damages of $1 for each of the two offenses cited in the verdict.
- The dispute originated from Qualcomm’s 2021 acquisition of Nuvia and allegations that Arm withheld essential chip-testing tools.
- Qualcomm claims Arm’s actions negatively impacted a potential chip deal with Meta, resulting in a reported $170 million loss.
- Judge Maryellen Noreika will preside over future proceedings to determine if Qualcomm can stop paying royalties to Arm for five years.

### Why it Matters
This legal battle highlights significant tensions regarding intellectual property control and royalty structures within the semiconductor industry. The outcome of future court rulings could fundamentally alter how chip designers and manufacturers negotiate licensing fees, potentially impacting the cost and development of future mobile hardware.
