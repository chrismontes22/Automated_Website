Hedge Funds Start To Bet On A Bitcoin Rally
============================================================

U.S. hedge funds are shifting their strategy toward Bitcoin as professional traders begin betting on a price rally following a period of significant market decline throughout this year.

### Key Points
- Data from CryptoQuant indicates hedge funds are moving from short positions to bullish wagers on Bitcoin futures via the Chicago Mercantile Exchange.
- Major firms including Citadel, Bridgewater Associates, and Renaissance Technologies have historically maintained negative stances on cryptocurrency assets.
- Bitcoin has remained rangebound between $60,000 and $65,000 since late June, following a yearly low of $58,000.
- Current futures market activity suggests that many institutional traders now view the $58,000 price point as a potential bottom for the asset.
- Bitcoin is currently trading at $64,800, significantly below its all-time high of over $126,000 recorded last October.

### Why it Matters
The pivot by institutional investors signals a potential change in market sentiment for the largest cryptocurrency by market capitalization. This shift could provide necessary momentum to break Bitcoin out of its current trading range and influence broader digital asset valuations.
