Microsoft got hacked, and somehow Clippy ended up in a crypto scheme
============================================================

Microsoft confirmed that its official X account was compromised this week by unauthorized actors who used the platform to promote a cryptocurrency token under the guise of nostalgia.

### Key Points
- Microsoft regained control of its official X account after unauthorized users posted content promoting a cryptocurrency token.
- The hackers used the account to solicit engagement regarding the return of the iconic office assistant, Clippy.
- Microsoft deleted the fraudulent posts and issued a formal apology, clarifying that it does not endorse any cryptocurrency projects.
- The company is currently investigating the security breach to determine how the unauthorized access occurred.
- Microsoft advised users to rely exclusively on official company channels for accurate information regarding its products and intellectual property.

### Why it Matters
This security breach highlights the ongoing vulnerability of high-profile corporate social media accounts to sophisticated hijacking attempts. The incident serves as a reminder for users to remain cautious of suspicious financial solicitations, even when they appear on verified accounts from trusted technology brands.
