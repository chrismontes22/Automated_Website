Broadcom Launches Three New VCAP Exams for VMware Cloud Foundation 9
============================================================

Broadcom has expanded its VMware Cloud Foundation 9 certification program by introducing three new role-based VCAP exams, bringing the total number of available professional certifications to eight.

### Key Points
- The new exams are VCAP-VCF Administrator (3V0-11.26), VCAP-VCF Architect (3V0-12.26), and VCAP-VCF Support (3V0-13.26).
- Each exam consists of 60 questions, lasts 135 minutes, and costs USD 250 via Pearson VUE.
- Role-based exams cover all five VCF pillars—Automation, Operations, Storage, Networking, and VKS—from a specific functional perspective.
- The exams utilize varied question formats, including multiple-choice, drag-and-drop, and simulations for troubleshooting tasks.
- Registration for the new certifications is currently open through the official Certmetrics portal.

### Why it Matters
These certifications allow IT professionals to validate their expertise in managing, designing, or supporting entire VCF private cloud environments rather than focusing on single product pillars. This shift provides a more comprehensive credential for specialists tasked with overseeing complex, multi-region infrastructure deployments.
