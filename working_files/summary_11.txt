Stocks plunge as Amazon, Intel and Alphabet fuel a broad tech sell-off
============================================================

US stocks suffered a sharp decline on Friday as disappointing earnings from major technology companies fueled investor concerns regarding the sustainability of massive artificial intelligence infrastructure spending.

### Key Points
- The Nasdaq Composite tumbled 2.8%, marking its worst monthly performance since March 2025 with a 5.2% decline for October.
- Amazon and Alphabet shares fell following earnings misses, while Intel stock dropped 29% due to a weak fourth-quarter outlook.
- Cryptocurrency exchange Binance suffered a record $6.3 billion hack, contributing to a 2% slide in Bitcoin prices.
- Oil prices rose, with Brent crude reaching $112 per barrel, as geopolitical tensions in the Middle East persisted.
- SpaceX market value dropped by over $400 billion in less than a month following a share lockup expiration and post-earnings volatility.

### Why it Matters
The market sell-off reflects a growing investor skepticism toward the high capital expenditures required for AI development among major tech firms. This shift in sentiment suggests a broader rotation as market participants re-evaluate valuations and prioritize companies with more immediate, tangible earnings growth.
