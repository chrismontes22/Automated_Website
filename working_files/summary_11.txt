Cloudflare is the only vendor named a Visionary in 2026 SASE and SSE reports
============================================================

Cloudflare has been recognized as the only vendor named a Visionary in both the 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for SASE Platforms and the 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Security Service Edge.

### Key Points
- Cloudflare One is a unified, composable SASE platform built on a single global network architecture.
- The platform provides native governance for AI agents and human users, including cost-capping for AI inference.
- Cloudflare has integrated post-quantum encryption across its network to mitigate "harvest-now, decrypt-later" security threats.
- The company aims to deliver a fully quantum-secure SASE platform by 2028, ahead of the 2030 NIST mandate.
- Integration with Cloudflare Workers allows organizations to program custom security workflows directly into the SASE fabric.

### Why it Matters
This recognition highlights a shift in the cybersecurity market toward unified, agile architectures that can handle modern challenges like AI sprawl and quantum computing threats. By moving away from fragmented, legacy-stitched platforms, enterprises can achieve faster deployment times and more consistent security governance across their entire digital infrastructure.
