Forget the iPhone 18 Pro Max — you can score $1,100 off the Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra with this T-Mobile deal (no trade-in required!)
============================================================

T-Mobile is offering a $1,100 discount on the Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra for new and existing customers who add a line to an Experience More or Beyond plan.

### Key Points
- T-Mobile customers can purchase the Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra for $5.56 per month over 36 months without requiring a device trade-in.
- The promotion applies to both the mid-tier Experience More and the premium Experience Beyond wireless plans.
- The Galaxy S26 Ultra features a 6.9-inch AMOLED display, Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset, 12GB of RAM, and seven years of software support.
- New hardware enhancements include improved charging speeds and a Privacy Display designed to restrict viewing angles from the side.
- The deal provides a higher relative value than current iPhone 18 Pro Max promotions, which require the most expensive plan to maximize savings.

### Why it Matters
This aggressive pricing strategy positions the Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra as a more accessible flagship alternative to the newly released iPhone 18 lineup. By decoupling the discount from the most expensive service tier, T-Mobile is effectively lowering the barrier to entry for premium Android hardware.
