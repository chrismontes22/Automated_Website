UGREEN's local AI NAS rolls out huge price drops ahead of its Kickstarter launch
============================================================

UGREEN has announced significant price reductions for its upcoming HomeAgent AI NAS product lineup and delayed the official Kickstarter launch date from October 27 to November 17.

### Key Points
- The MSRP for the HomeAgent HA100 is reduced by $200 to $1,599, while the HA100 Pro drops by $600 to $5,399.
- Super Early Bird and Early Bird pricing tiers have been adjusted downward to reflect the new, lower MSRP values.
- Customers who placed deposits for the HA100 or HA100 Pro are eligible to receive a complimentary ID500 Pro camera.
- Deposits made for the MasterAgent MA100 now include four 8TB digital hard drives as a promotional gift.
- The official Kickstarter campaign launch has been rescheduled from October 27 to November 17.

### Why it Matters
These price adjustments make UGREEN’s local AI storage solutions more competitive for consumers prioritizing data privacy and local control over cloud-based alternatives. By lowering entry costs and offering hardware incentives, the company aims to strengthen its market position ahead of the delayed crowdfunding launch.
