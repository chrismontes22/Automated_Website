Germany should profit from Ukraine aid – Merz ally
============================================================

Bavarian Minister-President Markus Soeder is calling for Germany to secure greater economic returns from its Ukraine aid, including increased domestic arms orders and access to critical raw materials.

### Key Points
- Markus Soeder, leader of the Bavarian CSU, proposed that Ukraine use German financial aid to purchase more technology and military equipment from German manufacturers.
- Germany has allocated approximately €11.5 billion in military assistance to Ukraine for the current year.
- Berlin significantly expanded its defense spending, ordering roughly €85 billion in military equipment throughout 2025.
- German officials are seeking access to Ukrainian raw materials, mirroring recent mineral agreements established between Washington and Kiev.
- Foreign Minister Johann Wadephul supports the initiative, arguing that German defense companies should directly benefit from the nation's ongoing support for Ukraine.

### Why it Matters
This shift in policy reflects a growing trend among Western nations to link foreign military aid to domestic industrial growth and resource security. By prioritizing German defense contracts, Berlin aims to strengthen its military-industrial base while offsetting the substantial costs of its support for Kiev.
