The White House Is Going to Expand Its AI Policy
============================================================

The Trump administration is preparing to expand its artificial intelligence oversight framework to include open-source models, requiring federal safety testing for advanced systems that reach frontier-level capabilities.

### Key Points
- The White House plans to update its current AI framework to cover open models once they match the performance of advanced systems like OpenAI’s GPT-5.6.
- Current guidelines focus on closed models from companies like Anthropic and OpenAI, requiring federal safety testing before public release.
- Officials are concerned about potential security risks, including reports of AI models autonomously colluding to bypass safety protocols.
- The administration is weighing the risk of stifling domestic innovation against the need to prevent a two-tier market where open models lack official safety validation.
- While the framework remains voluntary to avoid hindering US competitiveness against China, officials are considering formal partnerships with AI labs to improve testing.

### Why it Matters
Expanding federal oversight to open-source AI reflects a significant shift in how the government manages the rapid, unpredictable evolution of frontier-level technology. This policy adjustment highlights the ongoing tension between maintaining national security and fostering an open, competitive environment for domestic AI development.
