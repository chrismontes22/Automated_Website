New York Will Find Out If Elementary Schoolers Can Still Learn Without Using AI
============================================================

New York City has implemented a one-year moratorium on generative artificial intelligence in public schools for students from 2K through 8th grade to prioritize human-led instruction.

### Key Points
- The policy affects approximately 600,000 students across the nation's largest public school system.
- Teachers are prohibited from using AI for grading assignments or counseling students in crisis.
- Middle school students are restricted to a maximum of 45 minutes of screen time per day.
- The district will remove or modify over 40 educational apps that currently utilize AI components.
- Exceptions remain for centrally approved instructional programs, coding software, and tools for students with disabilities.
- High school students will receive AI literacy training to understand the risks of algorithmic hallucinations.

### Why it Matters
As the largest school district in the United States, New York City’s experiment serves as a significant bellwether for how public institutions manage the integration of emerging technology. This policy could pressure AI developers to address safety and developmental concerns if other major school systems adopt similar restrictive frameworks.
