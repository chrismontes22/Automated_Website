Tested: Is the Asus ROG Zephyrus Duo the year’s most expensive laptop?
============================================================

The ASUS ROG Zephyrus Duo 16 features dual OLED displays and a powerful Nvidia RTX 5090 GPU, offering a high-end, productivity-focused experience despite its premium price point.

### Key Points
- The laptop is equipped with two 16-inch 2880 x 1800 OLED touchscreens and an Intel Core Ultra 9 386H processor.
- It includes 64GB of LPDDR5X RAM and an Nvidia GeForce RTX 5090 GPU to handle demanding creative and AI-driven tasks.
- Connectivity options include Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6.0, Thunderbolt 4 ports, HDMI 2.1, and an integrated SD card reader.
- The device weighs approximately 2.8kg and features a magnetic, removable keyboard design.
- Pricing for the high-end model is set at $5,499.99, with entry-level configurations starting at $4,499.99.

### Why it Matters
The ROG Zephyrus Duo 16 represents a significant hardware upgrade for users who require dual-screen multitasking but previously lacked the graphical power for professional creative workflows. While it offers unique utility for designers, its high cost and lower benchmark performance compared to traditional laptops make it a niche investment for power users.
