A 9-in-1 Blu-ray Drive + USB Hub That Turns Cheap DVDs into 100GB Storage You Own Forever
============================================================

The new BluHub external 9-in-1 Blu-ray drive offers a physical storage solution for users seeking to maintain ownership of their media libraries amid rising digital subscription costs.

### Key Points
- The BluHub features an aluminum chassis with a flip-top lid that allows users to display a spare disc while the drive operates.
- The device supports reading and writing for Blu-ray, DVD, and CD formats, including 6x burn speeds for 100GB discs.
- Integrated hardware includes four USB 3.0 ports, SD and microSD card slots, and captive USB-C and USB-A cables.
- Compatibility extends across Windows, macOS, Linux, ChromeOS, iPadOS, and Android via OTG.
- Early-bird pricing is set at $59, with a planned retail price of $129 and estimated shipping in November 2026.

### Why it Matters
As major manufacturers phase out optical drives and physical media production, this device provides a hedge against the shift toward subscription-based digital content. It offers consumers a way to retain control over their personal data and media libraries without relying on cloud-based services that can be revoked or altered.
