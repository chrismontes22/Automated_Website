Canva To Cut Or Relocate 22% of Global Workforce
============================================================

Canva is laying off 1,271 employees, representing 22% of its global workforce, as the design platform restructures its operations to remain competitive against emerging artificial intelligence technologies.

### Key Points
- The job cuts affect 1,271 staff members across the company's global operations.
- CEO Melanie Perkins described the restructuring as the most difficult period in the company's 13-year history.
- Departing employees will receive at least six months of salary, 12 months of healthcare coverage, and the ability to keep their company laptops.
- Canva is offering $10,000 grants to laid-off workers to assist them in starting their own business ventures.
- The company plans to collapse management layers and consolidate teams into centralized office hubs.

### Why it Matters
This significant workforce reduction highlights the ongoing pressure tech companies face as they pivot to integrate artificial intelligence into their core product offerings. The move reflects a broader industry trend where firms are aggressively restructuring to streamline operations and maintain market dominance against new AI-driven competitors.
