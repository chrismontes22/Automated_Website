Apple Details How iOS 27 Adapts to iPhone Duo's Displays
============================================================

Apple has unveiled iOS 27, a redesigned operating system specifically engineered to support the unique folding mechanics and dual-display interface of the company's new iPhone Duo smartphone.

### Key Points
- The iPhone Duo features a flexible interface that reorients controls, the Dock, and the status bar based on the device's folding angle and orientation.
- New multitasking capabilities include Split View, allowing users to run two apps simultaneously or open two windows of the same application.
- The A20 Pro chip powers exclusive camera features like Smart Take, which uses on-device AI to automatically capture photos when subjects are posing.
- Developers can utilize new APIs to optimize apps for the dual-display design, with early support confirmed for Netflix, Zoom, and Slack.
- Pre-orders for the iPhone Duo begin at 5 a.m. Pacific on October 16, with general retail availability starting October 23.

### Why it Matters
This release marks Apple's entry into the foldable smartphone market, signaling a significant shift in how the company approaches mobile hardware and software integration. By prioritizing adaptive interface design and multitasking, Apple aims to set a new standard for productivity and user experience on foldable devices.
