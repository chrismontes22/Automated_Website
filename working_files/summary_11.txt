Nasdaq Bought an AI Due-Diligence Platform as ICE Built Private-Credit Intelligence. Which Data Moat Is Deeper?
============================================================

Nasdaq has acquired the AI-driven due-diligence platform Dasseti to bolster its eVestment division, while Intercontinental Exchange continues developing its own private-credit intelligence tools alongside partner Apollo Global Management.

### Key Points
- Nasdaq finalized its acquisition of Dasseti on September 2 to integrate AI-assisted monitoring into its existing eVestment platform.
- The Dasseti platform tracks over 17,000 investment managers overseeing approximately $34 trillion in assets.
- Intercontinental Exchange is building a competing private-credit intelligence product, utilizing Apollo as an anchor partner to analyze deal terms.
- Nasdaq’s strategy focuses on capturing institutional workflows, whereas Intercontinental Exchange aims to provide granular, security-level data for the private-credit market.
- Both companies face challenges regarding data accuracy, document provenance, and the successful monetization of their respective AI-integrated platforms.

### Why it Matters
These competing strategies highlight a broader industry shift toward using AI to automate complex financial due diligence and data extraction. The success of these platforms will depend on whether firms prioritize broad institutional workflow integration or specialized, asset-level intelligence to secure long-term market dominance.
