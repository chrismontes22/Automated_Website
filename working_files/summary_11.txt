Bitcoin tops $80,000: 'Crypto winter is close to being over'
============================================================

Bitcoin surged above $81,000 this week as easing Federal Reserve rate hike concerns and falling Treasury yields helped the cryptocurrency defy its historically weak September performance trends.

### Key Points
- Bitcoin prices rose over 5% on Thursday and continued climbing on Friday, reaching levels above $81,000.
- Analysts suggest the recent rally indicates the prolonged "crypto winter" may be nearing an end after a 25% gain in August.
- Federal Reserve Governor Christopher Waller signaled potential support for keeping interest rates unchanged if inflation data continues to ease.
- Bernstein analysts maintain a $150,000 year-end price target for bitcoin, citing ongoing Treasury interventions in the bond market.
- While September has historically been a negative month for the token, it has bucked that trend for the past three consecutive years.

### Why it Matters
The recent price action suggests that bitcoin is increasingly sensitive to macroeconomic shifts and central bank policy rather than historical seasonal patterns. If the Federal Reserve maintains current rates, the resulting stability could bolster investor confidence in digital assets as a hedge against broader market volatility.
