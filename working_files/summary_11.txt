Trump Accounts And The Two Economies
============================================================

The new Invest America initiative, branded as Trump Accounts, aims to bridge the national wealth gap by providing every American child with an investment account to foster capital ownership.

### Key Points
- The program allows all 70 million American children to open investment accounts, with federal seeding for some and private contributions for others.
- Altimeter Capital CEO Brad Gerstner, a primary architect of the initiative, projects over $100 billion in private funding within the first year.
- Major donors include Michael and Susan Dell, Ray Dalio, and SpaceX, with two million children already receiving shares in the latter.
- The initiative seeks to move citizens from a labor-based economy to a capital-based one, countering the wealth-concentrating effects of artificial intelligence.
- The model draws inspiration from the Alaska Permanent Fund and Norway’s sovereign wealth fund, which distribute dividends to residents based on resource ownership.

### Why it Matters
This initiative represents a shift from traditional welfare-based redistribution toward an ownership-focused model designed to mitigate the economic divide exacerbated by AI. By integrating citizens into capital markets, the program aims to ensure long-term economic participation and stability as automation increases the productivity of capital relative to labor.
