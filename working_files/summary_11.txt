Proton VPN 8.0 is coming to iPhone — and it could be the app's biggest upgrade in years
============================================================

Proton VPN has released version 8.0.0 of its iOS application on GitHub, introducing a new Rust-based VPN engine designed to improve connection stability and enhance censorship resistance for users.

### Key Points
- The update introduces "Proton protocols," a custom WireGuard implementation written in Rust to replace the previous wireguard-go code.
- A new advanced kill switch feature will block all internet traffic unless the VPN is active, persisting even after device restarts.
- Improved functionality allows users to access local network devices and use Apple CarPlay while the kill switch is enabled.
- The new architecture provides a foundation for future features, including post-quantum encryption and better battery efficiency.
- While the build is available on GitHub, the official App Store release is pending final review by Apple.

### Why it Matters
This update represents a significant technical shift for Proton VPN by moving to a proprietary, Rust-based engine that simplifies development and debugging on iOS. By overcoming long-standing platform limitations regarding kill switches and network connectivity, the company is positioning its service as a more robust privacy tool for mobile users.
