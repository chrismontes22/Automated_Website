The iPhone Duo Will Run iOS 27.1 at Launch on Friday
============================================================

Apple will launch its first foldable smartphone, the iPhone Duo, on October 23 with a specialized version of the operating system known as iOS 27.1.

### Key Points
- The iPhone Duo debuts on October 23 running iOS 27.1, a version specifically optimized for the device's foldable hardware.
- Apple skipped the standard iOS 27 release for the new foldable to prioritize features tailored to its unique design.
- iOS 27.1 is currently exclusive to the iPhone Duo, while the beta process for iOS 27.2 began on September 16.
- Apple released the iOS 27.2 release candidate on October 19, signaling a near-term update for all compatible iPhone models.
- The company plans to unify its software ecosystem by allowing all devices, including the Duo, to update to iOS 27.2 later this year.

### Why it Matters
This release marks Apple’s strategic entry into the niche foldable smartphone market with software designed to ensure a seamless user experience. By aligning hardware innovation with specialized operating system updates, the company aims to minimize technical friction for customers transitioning to new form factors.
