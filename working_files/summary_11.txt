Two-Tier Encryption in the UK – Identical Apple Devices, Different Protection
============================================================

Apple has restricted access to its Advanced Data Protection feature for new UK users following a secret government demand to maintain the capability to decrypt iCloud data.

### Key Points
- The UK government issued a secret Technical Capability Notice (TCN) in January 2025 under the Investigatory Powers Act 2016.
- Apple responded by disabling Advanced Data Protection (ADP) for new UK users to avoid building a "backdoor" into its encryption architecture.
- Existing UK users who enabled ADP before the February 2025 cutoff retain the feature, creating a two-tier security system for British customers.
- Apple is currently challenging the legal gag order regarding the TCN at the UK's Investigatory Powers Tribunal.
- U.S. lawmakers, including Senator Ron Wyden, have criticized the UK's use of secret non-disclosure orders to compel technology companies.

### Why it Matters
This dispute highlights the ongoing global tension between national security surveillance powers and the fundamental right to end-to-end encryption. By forcing a choice between weakening security or withdrawing features, such legal mandates threaten to undermine the privacy standards of global technology platforms.
