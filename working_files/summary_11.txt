Bridget Griswold, a former Meta product manager, discusses her transition to CEO of Lean In and how she balances technical leadership with global advocacy for women.

### Key Points
- Bridget Griswold became CEO of the nonprofit organization Lean In in March after previously working as a product manager at Meta.
- Lean In operates in over 180 countries and manages the "Circles" program, which supports over 170,000 members in their professional development.
- The organization is currently prioritizing initiatives to close gender gaps in AI usage and encourage women to adopt AI tools for workplace efficiency.
- Griswold leverages her computer science background to personally address technical bugs and streamline workflows for her internal product and tech teams.

### Why it Matters
Griswold’s leadership highlights the growing intersection of corporate tech experience and mission-driven advocacy for gender equity in the workplace. Her focus on AI upskilling reflects a broader industry effort to ensure women remain competitive as automation and artificial intelligence reshape professional roles.
