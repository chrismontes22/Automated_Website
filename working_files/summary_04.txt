‘Friday Night Baseball’ Will Start Broadcasting Games Live, in Apple Immersive, on Vision Pro
============================================================

Apple and Major League Baseball have announced the September “Friday Night Baseball” schedule, featuring the debut of live 8K immersive broadcasts exclusively for Apple Vision Pro users.

### Key Points
- The Apple Immersive experience launches August 28 with a Red Sox vs. Yankees game, utilizing 180-degree 8K video and Spatial Audio.
- September matchups include key games for the Cleveland Guardians, Milwaukee Brewers, San Diego Padres, Philadelphia Phillies, and Los Angeles Dodgers.
- Apple Vision Pro users in nine countries, including the U.S., UK, and Japan, can access the immersive broadcasts via the Apple TV app.
- Standard “Friday Night Baseball” doubleheaders continue to stream weekly on Apple TV for subscribers in 60 countries and regions.
- The immersive broadcasts feature dedicated commentary teams, floating graphics, and stadium-wide audio captured by ambisonic microphones.

### Why it Matters
This expansion into 8K immersive sports broadcasting represents a significant push by Apple to drive hardware adoption for the Vision Pro headset. By integrating high-profile MLB content with advanced spatial technology, the company aims to redefine the live viewing experience and increase the value proposition of its Apple TV subscription service.
