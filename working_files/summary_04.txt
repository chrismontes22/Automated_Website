Capcom and Square Enix Suffer Online Attacks As Hackers Access Player Data
============================================================

Capcom and Square Enix have reported significant cybersecurity breaches involving unauthorized account access and data theft, highlighting a growing trend of AI-powered attacks targeting major Japanese corporations.

### Key Points
- Capcom confirmed a database breach on October 19 exposing email addresses and system-identifying information for over 11.7 million users.
- The Capcom incident resulted in 196 accounts having passwords illegally changed and 43 users suffering the theft of personal details like birth dates and locations.
- Square Enix reported unauthorized access to Final Fantasy XIV accounts caused by credential stuffing, where attackers used login data stolen from other companies.
- Japanese government officials report that cybersecurity breaches in 2026 have already surpassed the total number of incidents recorded throughout the entirety of 2025.

### Why it Matters
These incidents underscore the critical vulnerability of cross-platform credential reuse in an era where AI-driven attacks are rapidly increasing in frequency and sophistication. Users are encouraged to adopt unique passwords and two-factor authentication to protect their personal data from being compromised across multiple services.
