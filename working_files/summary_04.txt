Is this the end of the GTA 6 leaks? Take-Two's legal crusade appears to shut down the hubs behind them
============================================================

Take-Two Interactive has successfully forced the CyberLeek website and associated Telegram channel offline as part of an ongoing legal effort to stop unauthorized leaks of Grand Theft Auto 6.

### Key Points
- Take-Two Interactive filed subpoenas against Microsoft and Discord to identify the individuals behind the CyberLeek persona.
- Despite the removal of primary leak hubs, a seventh clip of unreleased GTA 6 gameplay has reportedly surfaced on Reddit.
- The CyberLeek group allegedly used the leaks to promote a cryptocurrency scheme involving a $CYBERLEEK meme coin.
- Grand Theft Auto 6 remains scheduled for a November 19, 2026, release on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S.

### Why it Matters
These leaks highlight the significant security challenges major publishers face when protecting highly anticipated intellectual property from bad actors. While Take-Two maintains that the incident will not impact long-term sales, the situation underscores the growing intersection between gaming leaks and illicit financial schemes.
