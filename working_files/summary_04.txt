Anthropic plans to spend $518 billion on AI infrastructure. Pre-IPO perps barely blink.
============================================================

AI developer Anthropic plans to invest $518 billion in cloud and infrastructure as it prepares for a potential public listing that could value the company at $2 trillion.

### Key Points
- Anthropic reported a $42 billion net loss in 2025, though $34 billion of that figure stems from non-cash accounting charges.
- Annual revenue grew twelvefold to nearly $4.6 billion, supported by a cash reserve of $20.28 billion at the end of 2025.
- Pre-IPO perpetual futures on cryptocurrency exchanges currently value the company at approximately $2 trillion, showing minimal volatility despite the spending announcement.
- Twelve exchanges currently list synthetic derivatives tracking Anthropic’s implied valuation, with Binance accounting for over 30% of total trading activity.
- The company’s infrastructure investment strategy is predicated on the belief that artificial intelligence will have a greater economic impact than electricity or the internet.

### Why it Matters
This massive capital expenditure signals an aggressive push to scale AI capabilities despite significant operating losses and reliance on a small number of major clients. The market's muted reaction suggests that investors are currently prioritizing long-term growth potential and the company's $2 trillion valuation target over immediate profitability concerns.
