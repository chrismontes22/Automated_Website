Trump says calls for more control on AI are a ‘SICK conspiracy’
============================================================

US President Donald Trump has dismissed calls for artificial intelligence development slowdowns as a conspiracy, arguing that increased government oversight could allow China to gain a technological advantage.

### Key Points
- President Trump rejected industry warnings about AI risks, claiming that executive leadership is sufficient to manage the technology without additional regulatory guardrails.
- Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei recently proposed a framework to slow AI development, citing potential risks like cyber-espionage and the creation of bio-weapons.
- OpenAI has delayed its planned IPO, which was targeting a $1 trillion valuation, amid growing industry concerns regarding AI safety and human control.
- Global tech stocks experienced significant declines on Monday, with the Nasdaq 100 falling 1.7% and major chipmakers like Nvidia and AMD seeing sharp losses.
- Anthropic reported that it successfully blocked attempts by users in Yemen to utilize its Claude model for developing missile-guidance and flight-control software.

### Why it Matters
The public disagreement between the White House and major tech executives creates significant uncertainty for investors who have driven the recent AI-led market rally. If development slows or regulatory pressure increases, the resulting shift in capital expenditure could have long-term consequences for the semiconductor industry and the broader global economy.
