Enshittification Has a New Mascot, and He’s Teaming Up With Death in a Hilarious Ad Campaign
============================================================

The Norwegian Consumer Council has launched a satirical video campaign featuring the character Enshittificator to highlight how corporations deliberately degrade digital products and services to maximize short-term profits.

### Key Points
- The campaign, titled "Breaking Free," uses dark humor to critique planned obsolescence, subscription-based features, and the removal of essential product functionality.
- Author Cory Doctorow, who popularized the term "enshittification" in 2022, collaborated with the nonprofit to help expose corporate practices that prioritize revenue over consumer rights.
- The videos reference real-world examples, including Google Photos' storage caps, BMW’s subscription-based heated seats, and Apple’s "batterygate" settlement.
- The Norwegian Consumer Council advocates for policy reforms centered on interoperability, data portability, and the decentralization of social media to reduce Big Tech's market power.
- Legislative efforts like the EU’s Digital Markets Act are cited as necessary steps to force platforms to support third-party integration and open competition.

### Why it Matters
This campaign highlights a growing consumer movement against business models that prioritize corporate lock-in and planned obsolescence over product quality and longevity. By framing these practices as systemic issues rather than isolated incidents, the initiative pressures regulators to adopt policies that protect consumer autonomy in an increasingly digital economy.
