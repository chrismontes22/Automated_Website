These camera apps give you more control over the iPhone 18 Pro’s aperture
============================================================

Apple’s native iOS camera app restricts the iPhone 18 Pro’s variable aperture to four settings, but third-party applications now offer more granular control for professional photography enthusiasts.

### Key Points
- The native iOS camera app limits the iPhone 18 Pro’s variable aperture to f/1.48, f/1.8, f/2.8, and f/4.
- Blackmagic Camera version 3.5.1 provides 10 aperture presets, offering more precise control than the default Apple software.
- Moment Pro Camera II has updated its $12.99 app to support 1/3 stop aperture increments between f/1.48 and f/4.0.
- Third-party developers utilize Apple’s SDK to bypass hardware-level restrictions, mimicking the manual control found on traditional digital cameras.

### Why it Matters
These third-party updates empower photographers to utilize the full potential of the iPhone 18 Pro’s hardware beyond Apple's simplified software constraints. While the small sensor size may limit visible depth-of-field changes, this increased control allows for more precise exposure management in varied lighting conditions.
