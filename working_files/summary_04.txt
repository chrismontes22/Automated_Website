Trump reveals millions of dollars' worth of share deals in big tech and AI
============================================================

US President Donald Trump disclosed over 1,000 stock market trades from July, involving significant investments in major technology and artificial intelligence firms like Microsoft, Nvidia, and SpaceX.

### Key Points
- Official filings reveal Trump’s portfolio executed between $6.5 million and $31 million in Microsoft stock sales during July.
- The trades included active buying and selling of shares in AI-focused companies Nvidia, Palantir, and Elon Musk’s SpaceX and Tesla.
- A White House spokesperson stated the portfolio is managed by independent third parties with no input from the President or his family.
- Trump continues to advocate for American dominance in AI development, explicitly rejecting calls for government-imposed safety regulations or international guardrails.
- The administration is simultaneously pursuing an emergency AI communication "hotline" with China to mitigate potential cyber risks and rogue incidents.

### Why it Matters
These disclosures highlight the intersection of the President's personal financial interests and his aggressive policy stance on prioritizing AI development over international regulation. The active trading in major defense and tech contractors underscores the high-stakes nature of the global AI race as the administration prepares for critical diplomatic meetings with China.
