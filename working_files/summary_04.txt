Sony’s WH-1000XM5 headphones are back down to their lowest price
============================================================

Sony has discounted its WH-1000XM5 noise-canceling headphones to $198 at Amazon and Best Buy, matching the lowest price previously seen during the 2026 Prime Day sales event.

### Key Points
- Sony WH-1000XM5 headphones are currently $198, a significant reduction from the original $399.99 retail price.
- Nintendo Switch 2 consoles will increase in price to $499.99 starting September 1, up from the current $449.99 base model cost.
- The Matic robot vacuum will see a $250 price hike on September 9, moving from $1,245 to $1,495 due to rising component costs.
- Best Buy is offering Labor Day discounts on Sonos home theater equipment, including $200 off the Arc Ultra soundbar and $100 off the Sub Mini.
- Matic has extended its return policy from 60 days to six months to accompany the upcoming price adjustment.

### Why it Matters
These Labor Day sales provide a final opportunity for consumers to purchase high-end electronics before scheduled price increases take effect across several major tech categories. Tracking these fluctuations helps shoppers avoid paying premiums on hardware like gaming consoles and smart home appliances as manufacturers adjust to rising production costs.
