How to best talk about AI in job interviews — even if you're skeptical of it
============================================================

Job seekers are increasingly facing questions about artificial intelligence during interviews, prompting experts to advise candidates on how to discuss their practical experience and adaptability with the technology.

### Key Points
- Gallup data shows the share of U.S. employees using AI at work rose from 21% in 2023 to 50% by early 2026.
- Hiring managers at firms like Robert Half prioritize candidates who can demonstrate how AI tools have streamlined tasks or improved decision-making.
- Career experts suggest that candidates with limited AI experience should be honest and inquisitive rather than feigning enthusiasm or apologizing for their lack of exposure.
- Recruiters are shifting focus toward well-rounded candidates who possess strong human judgment, emotional intelligence, and problem-solving skills alongside technical proficiency.
- Job seekers can use interviews to ask how prospective employers integrate AI, which demonstrates curiosity and helps assess the company's technological maturity.

### Why it Matters
As AI integration becomes standard across industries, the ability to discuss the technology's practical application has become a critical component of the modern hiring process. Candidates who demonstrate adaptability and clear communication regarding AI tools are better positioned to succeed, even if they remain skeptical of the technology's broader implications.
