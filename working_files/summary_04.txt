Rivian spinoff Mind Robotics hires Doug Field as president
============================================================

Mind Robotics, a secretive startup founded by Rivian CEO RJ Scaringe, has appointed former Ford and Apple executive Doug Field as its new president to lead robotics development.

### Key Points
- Doug Field, formerly a top executive at Ford, Tesla, and Apple, will lead Mind Robotics as its new president.
- Founded by RJ Scaringe in 2025, Mind Robotics has secured over $1 billion in financing to develop universally capable industrial robots.
- The startup plans to utilize AI training data from Rivian and test its initial robotic hardware within Rivian’s high-volume manufacturing facilities.
- Mind Robotics is currently expanding its workforce with 32 open positions to accelerate the development of robots featuring advanced dexterity and physical reasoning.
- While competitors like Tesla and Hyundai are pursuing humanoid designs, Mind Robotics has not yet disclosed the specific form factor of its technology.

### Why it Matters
This appointment signals a significant push to integrate advanced physical AI into industrial manufacturing environments. By leveraging Rivian’s factory data and infrastructure, the company aims to overcome the scaling challenges that have historically hindered the widespread adoption of autonomous robotics.
