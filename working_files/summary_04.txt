Potato by air
============================================================

Wing’s drone delivery service, operated in partnership with Walmart, is gaining popularity among suburban residents for its rapid, low-cost transport of small household items and groceries.

### Key Points
- The Wing Hummingbird 7000W-B drone features 16 propellers, a 60 mph top speed, and a 2.7-pound carrying capacity.
- FAA-authorized for beyond-line-of-sight flights, the service currently delivers items from local Walmart Supercenters in under 30 minutes.
- Deliveries are currently highly subsidized, often costing less than one dollar with no delivery fees or minimum order requirements.
- Wing has completed over 1 million residential deliveries, requiring verified accounts and clear delivery zones for all drop-offs.
- The service allows users to send items directly to other addresses within eligible service areas, provided the recipient is in the delivery radius.

### Why it Matters
The expansion of subsidized drone delivery highlights a shift in last-mile logistics that prioritizes extreme convenience and speed over traditional retail models. While currently a novelty for consumers, the technology poses significant questions regarding long-term economic sustainability, labor market impacts, and the environmental efficiency of small-parcel aerial transport.
