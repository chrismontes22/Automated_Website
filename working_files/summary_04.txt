The ECB will invest in tokenised securities, and settle the trades on its own new rail
============================================================

The European Central Bank plans to invest its own funds into tokenized securities to gain practical experience with distributed ledger technology and modern wholesale settlement systems.

### Key Points
- The ECB will purchase euro-denominated debt issued by regional governments, agencies, and European supranational bodies.
- Trades will be settled using central bank money via the Eurosystem’s proprietary Pontes distributed ledger platform.
- The initiative uses the bank’s own funds portfolio rather than monetary policy instruments, meaning it does not directly impact the broader euro area economy.
- The program aims to help the ECB understand the full investment cycle of tokenized assets, from trade execution to final settlement.
- This project is distinct from the retail digital euro, which remains a separate development focused on consumer payment instruments.

### Why it Matters
By actively participating in tokenized markets, the ECB aims to ensure that central bank money remains a viable settlement asset in an increasingly digitized financial landscape. This hands-on approach allows the institution to gain technical expertise and regulatory foresight that would be impossible to achieve solely through external supervision.
