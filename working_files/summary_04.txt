The RealReal won’t let AI agents hog designer clothing
============================================================

Luxury consignment retailer The RealReal has updated its bot defense systems to prevent AI agents from exploiting its shopping cart reserve feature to block other customers from purchasing.

### Key Points
- The RealReal implemented new security measures after reports showed Meta’s Muse AI could indefinitely hold items by repeatedly adding them to carts.
- The retailer’s previous system placed items on hold for 20 minutes, a window that AI agents were programmed to exploit continuously.
- CTO Luke Friang confirmed the updated configuration restricts AI agents from logging in and checking out while still allowing them to surface product listings.
- Meta reported that shopping is the most common use case for its Muse AI, which reached 10 million U.S. downloads in 40 days.
- Major retailers are increasingly blocking AI agents to protect their direct customer relationships and advertising revenue models.

### Why it Matters
The rise of autonomous shopping agents threatens to disrupt traditional e-commerce by bypassing direct human interaction and exploiting site inventory systems. As these tools become more accessible, retailers must balance the convenience of AI-driven shopping with the need to maintain fair access and site stability for all users.
