What I track in a day
============================================================

Verge senior reviewer Victoria Song details her rigorous daily testing regimen for various health wearables, smart scales, and metabolic tracking devices to evaluate their accuracy and real-world utility.

### Key Points
- Victoria Song is currently testing a suite of devices including the Apple Watch Ultra 3, Oura Ring 5, Garmin Cirqa, and Google Fitbit Air.
- The testing process involves building a "metabolic digital twin" using the Twin Health system, which integrates continuous glucose monitors and blood tests.
- Daily routines include syncing multiple apps, logging meals, and performing comparative analysis across three different smart scales, including the Withings BodyScan 2.
- The reviewer uses the Polar H10 chest strap and Eight Sleep Pod 4 Ultra as control devices to verify heart rate and sleep metric accuracy.
- Song emphasizes that her intensive data collection is for professional product evaluation and warns against the unsustainable nature of extreme self-quantification for average users.

### Why it Matters
This deep dive into professional wearable testing highlights the significant gap between consumer-grade health tracking and the complex, often redundant data collection required for accurate product validation. It serves as a cautionary reminder that excessive data tracking can lead to fatigue and that users should prioritize intentional, goal-oriented monitoring over constant, device-heavy quantification.
