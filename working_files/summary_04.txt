Anthropic has 7 cofounders. Dario and Daniela Amodei say one trait is key to making that work.
============================================================

Anthropic cofounders Dario and Daniela Amodei credit their unique seven-person leadership structure and mutual trust for the company's success as they prepare for a record-breaking initial public offering.

### Key Points
- Anthropic’s seven cofounders maintain a unique governance structure, controlling 50.1% of all shareholder votes to ensure long-term strategic alignment.
- The company filed S-1 paperwork revealing $4.6 billion in revenue and a $42 billion net loss for the previous year.
- Each of the seven cofounders has pledged to donate 80% of their personal equity in the company to philanthropic causes.
- Anthropic is targeting a valuation between $1.5 trillion and $2 trillion for its upcoming Nasdaq debut, potentially becoming the largest IPO in history.
- The company’s regulatory filings warn that a catastrophic AI-related incident could pose significant risks to the global insurance industry.

### Why it Matters
This IPO represents a major test for the AI sector as investors evaluate whether the market can absorb a massive valuation for a company with significant net losses. The founders' unique voting control and philanthropic commitments highlight a distinct corporate governance model that prioritizes long-term safety and ethical development over traditional shareholder influence.
