Whisker’s AI-powered litter robot thinks my cats swapped bodies
============================================================

The $899 Litter-Robot 5 Pro from Whisker functions as an effective automated waste-management device, but its premium AI-powered health and facial recognition features currently fail to deliver reliable results.

### Key Points
- The Litter-Robot 5 Pro features a dual-camera system and sensors designed to track cat health, identify waste, and distinguish between multiple pets.
- AI-driven "PawPrint" facial recognition and "WasteID" systems frequently misidentified cats and failed to accurately categorize bathroom habits during testing.
- The device requires an $8-per-month Whisker Plus subscription for advanced features that have struggled to provide actionable health insights since the product's launch.
- While the AI components underperform, the hardware excels at its primary function of scooping waste and containing odors compared to manual litter boxes.
- Whisker offers multiple models, including the $699 Litter-Robot 4 and the $599 Litter-Robot Evo, which provide varying levels of functionality and build quality.

### Why it Matters
The Litter-Robot 5 Pro highlights the growing trend of manufacturers integrating unrefined AI into household appliances to justify premium price points. Consumers should prioritize core hardware performance over promised software capabilities, as these complex smart features often require significant post-launch updates to become functional.
