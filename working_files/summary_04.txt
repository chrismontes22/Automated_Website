Silicon Valley wealth managers say elite tech workers are making these 5 money moves as AI mania continues
============================================================

Employees at major AI firms like OpenAI and Anthropic are increasingly consulting wealth managers to diversify portfolios and mitigate risks amid growing concerns regarding a potential industry bubble.

### Key Points
- Wealth advisors report a surge in clients from firms like OpenAI, Anthropic, and Nvidia seeking to reduce concentration risk in tech-heavy portfolios.
- Common strategies include structured stock liquidation, tax-loss harvesting, and utilizing variable prepaid forwards to manage capital gains.
- Some employees are diversifying into real estate, municipal bonds, and AI-adjacent sectors like "neocloud" stocks to hedge against market volatility.
- Financial planners estimate that while most selling is measured, a small percentage of clients are aggressively liquidating assets to fund luxury real estate and lifestyle expenses.
- The Nasdaq 100 has risen 91% over the last five years, fueling concerns that current valuations in the AI sector may be unsustainable.

### Why it Matters
This trend highlights a growing sense of caution among tech insiders who are prioritizing wealth preservation over continued market exposure. By locking in gains and diversifying, these employees are signaling a shift toward financial stability as they prepare for potential corrections in the high-growth AI market.
