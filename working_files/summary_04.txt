Strategy (MSTR) Surges 16% on Growing Digital Asset Adoption — A New Catalyst Looms Next Week
============================================================

Strategy Inc. shares surged 16.39 percent to $153.92 on Friday as the SEC introduced an Innovation Exemption allowing 24/7 trading of tokenized stocks for qualified platforms.

### Key Points
- Strategy Inc. stock closed at $153.92 following a 16.39 percent rally driven by broader cryptocurrency market gains.
- The SEC’s new Innovation Exemption permits 24/7 trading of tokenized stocks, provided holders retain standard equity rights.
- Crypto-related firms including MARA Holdings, Riot Platforms, and Bitdeer Technologies also saw positive market movement.
- The U.S. Senate recently rejected the Clarity Act in a 49-50 vote, stalling legislative efforts to define digital asset regulation.
- Industry stakeholders will discuss future policy at the CoinDesk Policy and Regulation conference scheduled for September 22.

### Why it Matters
The SEC's regulatory shift signals a significant move toward integrating digital assets into traditional financial markets despite recent legislative setbacks in the Senate. This development could reshape trading accessibility and market structure for investors and institutions navigating the evolving digital asset landscape.
