US stock futures slide after tech sell-off as oil climbs to 5-month highs
============================================================

US stock index futures declined on Friday following a significant tech sector sell-off, while Brent crude prices reached five-month highs due to escalating geopolitical tensions in the Middle East.

### Key Points
- S&P 500 futures fell 0.2%, the Dow Jones Industrial Average dropped 0.6%, and the Nasdaq 100 remained nearly flat during early Friday trading.
- The Nasdaq 100 experienced its worst daily performance since June on Thursday, driven by investor concerns regarding artificial intelligence spending.
- Tesla reduced its annual capital-spending forecast from $25 billion to $20 billion, sparking uncertainty about the company's future AI and robotics investments.
- Brent crude prices climbed above $114 per barrel following drone attacks on Saudi Aramco infrastructure and maritime security incidents near Oman.
- SK Hynix canceled plans for a US listing of its memory-chip unit, citing unfavorable market conditions and recent volatility in the semiconductor sector.

### Why it Matters
The recent volatility highlights growing investor skepticism regarding the massive capital expenditures required to sustain the current artificial intelligence boom. Rising oil prices further complicate the economic outlook by potentially fueling inflationary pressures and impacting global market stability.
