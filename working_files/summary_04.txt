OpenAI and Anthropic are battling Big Tech for talent. We asked workers who's winning them over — and who's not.
============================================================

Tech workers are weighing the prestige and potential financial upside of frontier AI labs like OpenAI and Anthropic against the stability and infrastructure scale offered by established Big Tech firms.

### Key Points
- Tech professionals report varying priorities, with some favoring the mission-driven culture of Anthropic and others preferring the massive infrastructure and revenue stability of hyperscalers like Google and Meta.
- NVIDIA is frequently cited as a top choice due to its central role in providing the hardware and accelerated computing power necessary for the current AI boom.
- Concerns regarding job security, internal politics, and the impact of past layoffs influence many workers' decisions to stay at established companies rather than join high-profile AI startups.
- Some engineers prioritize technical ownership and remote-first environments over the prestige associated with working at the industry's most visible AI labs.
- Opinions on Big Tech employers remain polarized, with some workers valuing Apple’s product culture while others express skepticism toward Amazon’s work environment.

### Why it Matters
The competition for talent highlights a shift in how engineers evaluate career risks and rewards during a period of rapid technological change. Companies that successfully balance ethical values, financial stability, and meaningful technical challenges are currently best positioned to attract top-tier talent in a crowded market.
