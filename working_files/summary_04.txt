Sergey Brin is cooking up Google's AI comeback in a California microkitchen
============================================================

Google cofounder Sergey Brin has established an influential "microkitchen" command center at the company's Mountain View headquarters to accelerate the development of the Gemini artificial intelligence model.

### Key Points
- Sergey Brin is actively steering Gemini development by bypassing corporate bureaucracy and directly managing key technical resources and personnel.
- Koray Kavukcuoglu has been appointed SVP of Google DeepMind, working closely with Brin to align AI research with product-focused goals.
- The "microkitchen" serves as a startup-like hub where Brin and his team make rapid decisions on model architecture, release windows, and resource allocation.
- Recent leadership changes include Demis Hassabis transitioning to Alphabet’s chief scientist and the departure of longtime chief scientist Jeff Dean.
- Google is under significant pressure to improve Gemini’s performance to remain competitive against rivals like OpenAI in the rapidly evolving AI market.

### Why it Matters
This shift toward a founder-led, agile command structure highlights Google's urgent attempt to regain its competitive edge in the high-stakes artificial intelligence race. By centralizing power around Brin, the company aims to overcome internal friction and bureaucratic delays that have hindered its ability to match the rapid innovation of industry peers.
