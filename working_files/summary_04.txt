'Saturday Night Live' spoofs Dario Amodei: ‘AI is the devil and I its maker’
============================================================

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei was parodied on Saturday Night Live as the tech leader faces increasing public scrutiny regarding his shifting stance on artificial intelligence safety and regulation.

### Key Points
- Jane Wickline portrayed Dario Amodei on SNL, depicting the CEO as a conflicted figure struggling to reconcile his public image with the risks of his technology.
- Amodei recently published an essay advocating for government regulation and a slowdown in AI development, contrasting with his recent focus on medical research breakthroughs.
- Former Anthropic researcher Jacob Coxon recently resigned, publicly criticizing AI labs for allegedly gambling with human safety.
- President Donald Trump criticized Amodei on Truth Social while proposing the creation of an "AI Force" to monitor industry developments.
- Meta’s chief AI scientist Yann LeCun publicly endorsed the SNL parody, highlighting ongoing industry divisions regarding AI doomsday warnings.

### Why it Matters
The parody highlights the growing cultural and political tension surrounding the rapid development of frontier AI models. As industry leaders like Amodei navigate conflicting pressures from regulators and critics, the debate over whether to prioritize safety or innovation remains a central challenge for Silicon Valley.
