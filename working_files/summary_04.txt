Is AI the End of Cheap Tech?
============================================================

The rapid expansion of generative AI is driving a global memory chip shortage, forcing manufacturers to raise prices on consumer electronics like smartphones, laptops, and gaming consoles.

### Key Points
- Generative AI models require massive amounts of high-bandwidth memory, leading tech giants to consume roughly 70% of the world's high-end chip supply.
- Major manufacturers including Apple, Samsung, and various gaming console producers have already increased prices or are expected to do so soon.
- The memory crisis is impacting essential sectors, with schools and hospitals facing higher costs for necessary hardware like Chromebooks and MRI machines.
- Experts warn that the era of cheap silicon is ending, with manufacturing capacity unlikely to meet demand until at least 2030.
- Smaller hardware companies are struggling to secure components, potentially leading to a market dominated only by the largest, most powerful corporations.

### Why it Matters
The rising cost of memory threatens to end the decades-long trend of increasingly affordable and powerful consumer technology. This shift risks deepening the global digital divide by making essential tools inaccessible to lower-income consumers and smaller organizations.
