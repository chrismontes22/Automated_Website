AI researchers put out videos saying superintelligence is ‘exactly as dangerous as it sounds’
============================================================

Current and former researchers from OpenAI, Google DeepMind, and Anthropic have released a series of interviews warning that superintelligent AI systems could pose an existential threat to humanity.

### Key Points
- Palisade Research launched the "frominside.ai" platform featuring interviews with industry experts regarding AI safety and existential risks.
- Former OpenAI researcher Daniel Kokotajlo stated that superintelligent AI would be god-like and currently lacks any effective control mechanisms.
- Google DeepMind scientist Neel Nanda estimated a 10 percent probability that advanced AI could lead to human extinction.
- Geoffrey Irving, formerly of OpenAI and Google DeepMind, suggested the likelihood of human extinction from AI development is approximately 50 percent.
- Contributors acknowledged the conflict of interest in their warnings, noting they are employed by the very labs developing these powerful technologies.

### Why it Matters
These warnings from industry insiders highlight a significant internal divide regarding the safety and long-term trajectory of artificial intelligence development. The lack of a cohesive solution among experts underscores the difficulty of balancing rapid technological innovation with the mitigation of potential existential risks.
