'The Jobs Apocalypse Is Postponed. An AI Jobs Boom Is Here'
============================================================

Recent analysis from The Economist suggests that artificial intelligence is currently a net creator of jobs in the United States, offsetting workforce reductions in specific administrative sectors.

### Key Points
- The U.S. economy has seen approximately 1 million new AI-related jobs created since mid-2023, significantly outpacing the estimated 200,000 AI-attributed layoffs.
- Employment in customer service and administrative roles has declined by 10% to 15% since January 2023 due to automation.
- Engineering, software development, and data science professions have added roughly 730,000 jobs above historical trends since 2022.
- Data-center construction spending rose 60% in one year, driving demand for electricians, grid engineers, and HVAC specialists.
- Indeed reports that data-center maintenance and installation roles offer wages approximately 40% higher than comparable positions in other industries.

### Why it Matters
While AI is causing significant disruption in specific white-collar sectors, the broader labor market remains resilient with low unemployment rates. This data suggests that the technology is currently fueling a massive infrastructure and technical hiring boom that outweighs initial job losses.
