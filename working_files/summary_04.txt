Litecoin token has its moment as network activity booms
============================================================

Litecoin has surged nearly 8% to reach $66, marking its strongest monthly performance since November 2024 as network activity increases and anticipation builds for next year's halving.

### Key Points
- Litecoin’s price rose 37% this month, currently ranking as the 24th largest cryptocurrency by market capitalization.
- The Litecoin Foundation reported over $1 billion in adjusted economic volume moved across the network within a 24-hour period.
- The network's fourth halving event is scheduled for July, which will reduce per-block rewards from 6.25 to 3.125 LTC.
- Technical analysis shows a "golden cross" pattern on price charts, signaling a potential long-term bullish trend for the asset.
- Increased capital inflows into derivatives tied to the token are accompanying the recent price appreciation.

### Why it Matters
The recent rally highlights how network utility and cyclical supply adjustments continue to drive investor interest in established altcoins. This performance suggests that market participants are closely monitoring supply-side constraints and on-chain transaction volume as indicators for future price momentum.
