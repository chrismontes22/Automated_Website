US court halts AI nude-image ban challenged by Musk
============================================================

The 8th US Circuit Court of Appeals has issued an injunction blocking Minnesota’s law banning AI-generated non-consensual nude images following a First Amendment challenge filed by Elon Musk’s xAI.

### Key Points
- The Minnesota legislation, which took effect last month, imposed fines of up to $500,000 per image for creating non-consensual sexualized deepfakes.
- xAI argued the law is an overbroad, content-based restriction on free speech that could result in billions of dollars in potential penalties.
- Minnesota Attorney General Keith Ellison’s office stated it will continue to defend the law, citing the need to combat AI-generated sexual abuse material.
- xAI is currently facing a separate class-action lawsuit alleging its Grok Imagine tool was used to create and distribute child sexual abuse material.
- The court’s decision pauses enforcement of the state law while the legal challenge proceeds through the federal appeals process.

### Why it Matters
This ruling highlights the ongoing legal tension between state efforts to regulate AI-generated harm and the First Amendment protections claimed by technology companies. The outcome of this case could set a significant precedent for how governments balance individual privacy and safety against the rapid development of autonomous AI models.
