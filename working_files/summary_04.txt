iPhone Duo's A20 Pro Chip Benchmarked Ahead of Launch
============================================================

Early Geekbench 7 benchmarks reveal that the upcoming iPhone Duo delivers performance comparable to the iPhone 18 Pro, powered by Apple’s advanced 2nm A20 Pro processor.

### Key Points
- The iPhone Duo features the A20 Pro chip, which includes a 6-core CPU and a 7-core GPU.
- Benchmark testing on iOS 27.1 yielded a single-core score of 3986 and a multi-core score of 11067.
- The device is equipped with 12GB of RAM, matching the memory specifications found in the iPhone 18 Pro series.
- Apple claims the A20 Pro provides up to 20 percent faster CPU performance and 40 percent faster GPU speeds than the previous A19 Pro.
- Pre-orders for the iPhone Duo begin on October 16, with an official retail launch scheduled for October 23.

### Why it Matters
These results confirm that Apple is maintaining consistent high-end performance across its latest hardware lineup despite the unique dual-screen design of the new model. Consumers can expect flagship-level processing power, though real-world performance may still vary due to thermal management and the demands of the device's dual-display configuration.
