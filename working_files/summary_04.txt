Has Big Tech Captured America's Schools?
============================================================

A new book titled "Coding Kids" examines how major technology companies are increasingly influencing American public school curricula and shaping educational standards to align with industry interests.

### Key Points
- The book "Coding Kids: Big Tech's Battle to Remake Public Schools" argues that corporations use classroom materials to market products and sanitize industry practices.
- Silicon Valley firms frequently lobby for laws and committee seats to influence subjects like computer programming, Big Data analytics, and virtual reality.
- Past tech initiatives, including widespread laptop adoption and algorithm-driven learning apps, have often failed to deliver on promises of democratized education or improved career skills.
- Tech companies are currently applying similar pressure on school districts to rapidly integrate artificial intelligence tools into classrooms.

### Why it Matters
This trend raises significant concerns regarding corporate influence over public education and the potential for industry-led indoctrination of students. As schools adopt new technologies, the lack of critical oversight may leave administrators and parents unaware of the long-term risks associated with these corporate partnerships.
