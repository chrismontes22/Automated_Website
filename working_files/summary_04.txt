Live updates: Bitcoin clings to $82,500 as SpaceX results lift stock futures
============================================================

Bitcoin remains precariously positioned above the critical $82,500 support level as the cryptocurrency’s price continues to track closely with broader volatility in the technology sector and stock markets.

### Key Points
- Bitcoin is currently trading just above $82,500, a threshold analysts identify as a vital support level to prevent a potential slide toward $70,000.
- Market maker Wintermute warns that a sustained break below the current support could trigger a 15% decline in the asset's value.
- SpaceX shares rose 9% in pre-market trading following strong earnings results from its artificial intelligence and satellite internet divisions.
- Nasdaq 100 futures climbed 0.4% as reports of significant computing power investments by Anthropic helped stabilize investor sentiment regarding the AI sector.
- Brent crude oil prices surpassed $110 per barrel following reports of regional attacks near the Strait of Hormuz.

### Why it Matters
The strengthening correlation between Bitcoin and tech stocks challenges the narrative that the cryptocurrency functions as an independent safe haven during periods of market instability. Investors are closely monitoring these price levels to determine if Bitcoin will maintain its current valuation or face a deeper correction tied to broader macroeconomic trends.
