Bernie Sanders Proposes Artificial Superintelligence Ban Amid Rogue AI Hackings
============================================================

Senator Bernie Sanders and Representative Greg Casar have introduced the Ban Artificial Superintelligence Act to permanently prohibit superintelligent AI development and establish strict federal safety oversight and penalties.

### Key Points
- The proposed legislation seeks a permanent ban on AI technologies that surpass human intelligence capabilities.
- A temporary pause on advanced AI development would remain in effect until federal regulators finalize comprehensive safety standards.
- The bill mandates the creation of a new Cabinet-level federal agency dedicated to enforcing AI safety regulations.
- Violations could result in the "corporate death penalty," forcing companies to shut down, or up to 20 years in prison for individual developers.
- The U.S. would be required to pursue international agreements to prevent the global development of superintelligent systems.

### Why it Matters
This legislation represents a significant escalation in the legislative effort to regulate the rapid advancement of artificial intelligence by imposing severe criminal and corporate penalties. If passed, it would fundamentally alter the operational landscape for major technology firms and potentially slow the global trajectory of AI innovation.
