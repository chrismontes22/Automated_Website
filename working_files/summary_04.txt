Bill Gates says to prepare for 20 years of ‘tumultuous’ AI disruption
============================================================

Microsoft co-founder Bill Gates warns that society must prepare for two decades of tumultuous AI disruption, describing the technology as the most dangerous and profound invention in history.

### Key Points
- Gates emphasizes that AI poses immediate, severe risks including cyberattacks, bioterrorism, and massive labor market displacement.
- He argues that current industry safeguards are insufficient and that governments must establish mandatory technical standards for frontier models.
- The transition period could split society between those who embrace the technological frontier and those who reject it.
- Gates advocates for a global regulatory response, comparing the necessary oversight to the management of nuclear weapons or airline safety.
- Despite the risks, he remains optimistic about AI’s potential to solve global health crises, such as eradicating malaria and improving agricultural outcomes.

### Why it Matters
The rapid advancement of AI creates a critical tension between the potential for unprecedented human abundance and the risk of catastrophic societal instability. Gates’ call for government intervention highlights a growing consensus that market forces alone cannot mitigate the existential threats posed by powerful, unaligned artificial intelligence.
