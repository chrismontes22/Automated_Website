US Tech Stocks See Sharp Falls As AI Spending Nears $1 Trillion
============================================================

U.S. technology stocks experienced a sharp two-day decline as investors expressed growing skepticism regarding the long-term profitability of massive capital expenditures directed toward artificial intelligence infrastructure and development.

### Key Points
- The Nasdaq index dropped more than 3.5% on Friday following significant losses across the broader technology sector.
- Intel shares plummeted by 30% and Reddit stock fell 26% amid heightened investor scrutiny of quarterly earnings reports.
- Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, Microsoft, and Oracle have collectively committed to spending $800.6 billion on artificial intelligence initiatives by 2026.
- Market analysts attribute the volatility to "growing pains" as major tech firms aggressively scale their AI infrastructure investments.

### Why it Matters
This market downturn signals a shift in investor sentiment from prioritizing rapid AI expansion to demanding immediate financial returns on massive capital investments. If these major technology companies fail to demonstrate profitability, the broader market may move away from the AI-focused trade that has driven recent growth.
