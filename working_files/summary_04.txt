Amazon is buying billions in Nvidia AI systems it can't yet turn on
============================================================

Amazon is accelerating its acquisition of Nvidia GB300 server racks to secure AI hardware, despite power shortages at data centers causing equipment to sit idle in warehouses.

### Key Points
- Amazon has implemented an Early Rack Receipt process to take ownership of Nvidia hardware earlier in the supply chain.
- Internal emails indicate AWS planned to receive $5 billion in GB300 racks initially, followed by $3 billion in monthly shipments.
- Power capacity constraints at various data centers have forced the company to stage finished server equipment in intermediary warehouses.
- Amazon projects approximately $220 billion in capital expenditures for the year, the highest among major technology companies.
- The company’s free cash flow turned negative last quarter as it prioritizes securing scarce AI components over immediate deployment.

### Why it Matters
This strategy highlights the intense competition among cloud providers to secure limited AI hardware, even at the risk of tying up billions in capital. While the move ensures supply, it creates significant financial pressure and operational challenges as companies struggle to align hardware delivery with available data center power.
