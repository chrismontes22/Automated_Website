Trump Reportedly Flirting With A New Round Of Tariffs On Gaming Hardware
============================================================

The Trump administration is reportedly considering new import tariffs on hardware, including gaming consoles and data center servers, to pressure companies into increasing domestic semiconductor manufacturing investments.

### Key Points
- Proposed tariffs would expand to include consumer electronics like laptops and gaming consoles alongside critical data center hardware.
- The administration aims to use these import taxes as leverage to force technology companies to build domestic chip production facilities.
- The Supreme Court previously ruled in a 6-3 decision that unilateral presidential tariffs are unconstitutional, leading to widespread corporate refunds.
- Gaming companies like Sony and Nintendo currently face consumer lawsuits regarding price hikes implemented during the previous tariff regime.
- Industry analysts warn that hardware prices may rise further due to existing RAM shortages driven by high demand for AI infrastructure.

### Why it Matters
New tariffs could disrupt the global technology supply chain and lead to significant price increases for consumers and businesses alike. If implemented, these measures risk repeating past legal challenges while potentially destabilizing the market for essential hardware components.
