Bitcoin kicks off new quarter in the old $82,000-$85,000 price range
============================================================

Bitcoin remains range-bound between $82,000 and $85,000 as U.S.-listed spot ETFs record a $148.7 million net outflow, ending a nine-day streak of significant capital inflows into the cryptocurrency.

### Key Points
- Bitcoin prices briefly exceeded $85,000 on Wednesday following favorable U.S. inflation data before retreating due to cooling institutional demand.
- U.S.-listed spot ETFs saw a $148.7 million net outflow, halting a record-breaking nine-day inflow streak that totaled $3.08 billion.
- The Bitfinex Absorption-to-Emission Ratio has compressed significantly, indicating that higher daily inflows are required to clear overhead supply between $84,000 and $86,500.
- Bitcoin open interest declined to $20.9 billion, while the 24-hour call-to-put ratio for options rose to 83% in favor of bullish call positions.
- Altcoins showed mixed performance, with Stacks (STX) surging 25% following leadership changes at Stacks Labs, while other assets like Avalanche and ICP experienced declines.

### Why it Matters
The stagnation of Bitcoin prices highlights a critical dependency on consistent institutional inflows via spot ETFs to overcome existing market supply. Investors are now closely monitoring macroeconomic bond market volatility and ETF absorption rates to determine if the asset can break through its current resistance levels.
