Rockstar Games breaks silence on Grand Theft Auto 6 leaks
============================================================

Rockstar Games has officially addressed recent unauthorized leaks of Grand Theft Auto 6, expressing disappointment while confirming that development on the highly anticipated title remains on track.

### Key Points
- Rockstar Games described the unauthorized release of gameplay and cinematic clips as "heartbreaking" for the development team.
- Parent company Take-Two Interactive initiated legal proceedings in a New York court to address the security breach.
- The leaked footage was removed from social media platforms following a series of copyright strikes issued by the developer.
- Despite the leaks, the game is still scheduled for a November release on PlayStation 5 and Xbox consoles.
- Rockstar plans to debut an "extended look" at the game during a special preview event airing on Netflix this week.

### Why it Matters
The incident highlights the significant security challenges major studios face when managing high-profile intellectual property in an era of intense fan speculation. While the leaks have disrupted the developer's intended marketing rollout, industry analysts expect the game to remain a massive commercial success upon its release.
