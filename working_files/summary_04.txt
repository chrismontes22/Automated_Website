Trump vows retaliation over EU fines against Apple and other ‘GREAT’ US tech giants
============================================================

President Trump has threatened to impose substantial tariffs on the European Union in retaliation for antitrust fines and tax penalties levied against major American technology companies like Apple.

### Key Points
- President Trump announced plans to initiate a Section 301 investigation into European Union regulatory practices targeting U.S. tech firms.
- The threat follows significant EU penalties, including an €890 million fine against Google and a long-running €13 billion tax recovery order against Apple.
- Trump explicitly criticized fines totaling billions of dollars levied against Meta, Amazon, and Apple, labeling the actions as discriminatory and unethical.
- The administration intends to reverse these penalties and implement new tariffs to prevent the EU from treating the United States as a financial resource.

### Why it Matters
This escalation signals a potential shift toward aggressive trade protectionism that could disrupt transatlantic economic relations and increase costs for global technology companies. If implemented, these tariffs may trigger a broader trade war, impacting market stability and the regulatory environment for digital services operating within Europe.
