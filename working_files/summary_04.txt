It’s Donald Trump Versus MAGA on Data Centers
============================================================

Conservative voters and Republican strategists are increasingly clashing with Donald Trump over his vocal support for AI data center expansion, citing concerns over local environmental and property impacts.

### Key Points
- A recent New York Times poll shows over 60 percent of Americans, including 47 percent of Republicans, oppose new data center construction.
- GOP strategist Tony Fabrizio warned candidates that unqualified support for AI data centers is a losing political position for the upcoming midterms.
- Donald Trump continues to defend the industry, claiming on Truth Social that communities rejecting data centers risk remaining economically backwards and poor.
- Internal analysis of Trump’s social media platform reveals that negative comments regarding AI and data centers outnumber positive ones by a four-to-one margin.
- The Interior Department is currently reviewing at least 12 data center proposals covering 17,600 acres of public land.

### Why it Matters
The growing divide between the Republican base and party leadership over data center development highlights a significant political vulnerability regarding infrastructure and technology policy. As candidates weigh local voter concerns against national economic competition with China, the industry faces increasing regulatory uncertainty and public backlash.
