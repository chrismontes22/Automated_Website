Anthropic’s cheaper Opus arrives weeks before its IPO
============================================================

Anthropic has launched the Claude Opus 5.5 AI model, offering performance comparable to the premium Fable 5.1 at a significantly reduced price point for enterprise users.

### Key Points
- Claude Opus 5.5 is priced at $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output, marking the first price reduction for the Opus series.
- The model features a one-million-token context window and is designed specifically for complex agentic coding and knowledge-based tasks.
- Third-party organizations METR and Frontier Design conducted safety evaluations on the model prior to its public release.
- New safety features include a biology classifier and cybersecurity safeguards, aligning with Article 55 of the European Union's AI Act.
- Anthropic plans to release updated Sonnet and Haiku versions of the model in the coming weeks.

### Why it Matters
This release signals a strategic shift toward aggressive pricing to retain enterprise customers who are increasingly seeking cost-effective alternatives to premium AI models. By balancing lower costs with mandatory third-party safety testing, Anthropic is positioning itself to meet both market demand and evolving regulatory requirements ahead of its upcoming Nasdaq listing.
