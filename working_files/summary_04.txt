‘Plug-in batteries are the real game-changer’: I talked to Octopus Energy and Anker about the home energy revolution than could soon be 'bigger than solar' for millions
============================================================

The UK government has legalized plug-in solar kits starting August 27, while industry experts and companies push for the future legalization of plug-in home batteries to lower energy costs.

### Key Points
- Plug-in solar kits allow residents to generate clean electricity by connecting panels directly to standard wall sockets.
- Plug-in batteries, such as those from Anker, Octopus Energy, and Windfall Energy, are designed to store cheap off-peak electricity for use during expensive peak-rate periods.
- Industry estimates suggest plug-in batteries could save households between £100 and £400 annually through grid arbitrage.
- While plug-in batteries are not yet legal in the UK, companies hope for regulatory approval within the next year.
- Safety concerns regarding battery storage are being addressed by comparing the devices to common household appliances like washing machines.

### Why it Matters
Plug-in energy technology offers a significant opportunity for renters and flat owners to reduce their electricity bills without requiring permanent, professional home installations. By enabling consumers to participate in grid arbitrage, these devices could transform residential energy consumption and help stabilize the national electricity grid.
