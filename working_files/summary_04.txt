An Israeli firm is sending spyware through online ads, researchers say
============================================================

Security researchers from Citizen Lab and Confiant have identified an Israeli ad tech firm, Discover-Tech, using malicious online advertisements to deliver zero-click spyware to targeted mobile devices.

### Key Points
- Discover-Tech allegedly used its "Controlium" platform to profile users and deploy spyware via targeted advertisements without requiring any user interaction.
- The investigation linked Discover-Tech to the blacklisted spyware firm Candiru, which targeted at least 45 devices at Al Jazeera during 2024 and 2025.
- Google, Comcast-owned Beeswax, and IAB Europe have removed or suspended Discover-Tech from their advertising platforms following the discovery of these security vulnerabilities.
- Experts recommend that high-risk users mitigate these threats by utilizing ad blockers, disabling ad tracking, and regularly resetting their mobile advertising identifiers.

### Why it Matters
This discovery marks the first documented instance of zero-click hacking delivered through the global digital advertising ecosystem, exposing a significant vulnerability in online ad infrastructure. It highlights the urgent need for stricter security vetting of ad tech vendors to prevent malicious actors from weaponizing standard web advertising networks.
