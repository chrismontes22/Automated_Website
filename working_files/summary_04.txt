Should You Buy an iPhone 17 Now or Wait for iPhone 18?
============================================================

Apple will delay the release of the standard iPhone 18 until spring 2027, focusing its upcoming September launch exclusively on the iPhone 18 Pro, Pro Max, and Ultra models.

### Key Points
- Apple plans to launch only the iPhone 18 Pro, Pro Max, and foldable iPhone Ultra this September, delaying the standard iPhone 18 until spring 2027.
- Analysts expect price increases of $100 to $300 for the upcoming Pro models, potentially impacting the resale value and cost of existing iPhone 17 units.
- Rumors suggest the standard iPhone 18 may feature cost-cutting measures, including lower-quality A20 chips and potentially insufficient RAM to support advanced iOS 27 features.
- Trade-in values for older devices are expected to decline significantly once the new iPhone 18 series is officially released.
- The iPhone 17 remains the recommended choice for most users due to its current balance of premium features, performance, and established market pricing.

### Why it Matters
The shift in Apple's release strategy suggests a move toward higher-margin hardware, potentially forcing consumers to pay more for premium models or wait longer for entry-level options. This pricing uncertainty and the potential for hardware compromises in future base models make the current iPhone 17 a more stable value proposition for most buyers.
