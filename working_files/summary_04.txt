XCancel — An Unofficial Twitter/X Mirror
============================================================

XCancel serves as a privacy-focused instance of Nitter, an open-source Twitter front-end that allows users to browse the platform without JavaScript, tracking, or mandatory account requirements.

### Key Points
- Nitter provides a lightweight alternative to Twitter, loading pages up to 15 times faster while preventing IP-based tracking and browser fingerprinting.
- The platform operates without requiring JavaScript, bypassing the mandatory sign-up and data-sharing policies currently enforced by Twitter.
- Future updates aim to introduce an account system, enabling users to follow specific profiles and maintain a chronological timeline without a native Twitter account.
- The project is community-maintained and accepts various cryptocurrency and fiat donations to support server hosting and ongoing software development.

### Why it Matters
Nitter offers a critical tool for users seeking to maintain digital privacy and reduce data exposure while accessing social media content. By decoupling the browsing experience from corporate tracking mechanisms, it provides a more performant and secure alternative for navigating public information.
