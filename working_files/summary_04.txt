'What companies are facing today is an AI zoo' — NordLayer warns that dozens of unsanctioned chatbots are quietly widening every company's exposure to data leaks
============================================================

NordLayer has launched AI Scout, a new security tool designed to monitor employee usage of unauthorized artificial intelligence services and prevent the accidental exposure of sensitive corporate data.

### Key Points
- AI Scout tracks both sanctioned and unsanctioned AI platforms by monitoring browser and desktop interactions across an organization.
- The software features data loss prevention (DLP) controls that redact or block sensitive information before prompts are submitted to external AI models.
- Administrators can use the platform to audit token consumption and inspect the technical infrastructure of AI agents and programming tools.
- Research indicates that 70% of employees use unapproved AI models at work, creating significant security risks for enterprise data environments.
- IBM reports that security breaches involving shadow AI increase the average cost of a data incident by approximately $400,000.

### Why it Matters
The rise of shadow AI creates significant security vulnerabilities as employees frequently bypass corporate policies to use unapproved tools. By providing visibility and pre-submission controls, this technology helps businesses mitigate the risk of data leaks while maintaining governance over rapidly evolving AI workflows.
