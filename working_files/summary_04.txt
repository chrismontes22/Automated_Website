Trump imposes 15% tariff on key chip material to counter China
============================================================

President Donald Trump signed an executive order imposing a 15% tariff and minimum import prices on foreign polysilicon to bolster domestic semiconductor and solar panel manufacturing capabilities.

### Key Points
- The new tariffs and price floors on imported polysilicon are scheduled to take effect in December.
- US polysilicon production has plummeted from 50% of the global market in 2005 to less than 2% in 2024.
- The policy aims to support domestic producers like Hemlock Semiconductor and Wacker Chemie against Chinese market dominance.
- China’s embassy condemned the move as an abuse of state power and signaled that Beijing will implement retaliatory countermeasures.
- The order follows a national security investigation led by Secretary of Commerce Howard Lutnick regarding critical technology supply chains.

### Why it Matters
This policy marks a significant escalation in the ongoing trade conflict between the United States and China over essential materials for artificial intelligence and military hardware. By prioritizing domestic production, the administration seeks to reduce reliance on foreign supply chains, though the move risks further retaliatory trade barriers that could impact global technology costs.
