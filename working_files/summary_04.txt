Abnormal AI's new CMO says 'safe is boring' and is ditching the B2B marketing playbook
============================================================

Cybersecurity startup Abnormal AI has appointed former Torq executive Don Jeter as its new chief marketing officer to lead a strategy focused on unconventional, consumer-facing brand building.

### Key Points
- Abnormal AI appointed Don Jeter as CMO to transition the company from a niche email security provider to a recognizable household brand by 2027.
- The company, founded in 2018, reached a $5.1 billion valuation following a $250 million Series D funding round in 2024.
- Jeter plans to move away from traditional B2B marketing by utilizing out-of-home advertising and "cultural moments" to capture attention beyond the CISO audience.
- The strategy aims to differentiate Abnormal AI in a crowded market dominated by trillion-dollar tech giants and numerous cybersecurity competitors.
- Jeter previously gained industry attention at Torq by executing offbeat campaigns, including sponsoring Monster Jam and hosting a tattoo bus at a major conference.

### Why it Matters
This shift reflects a growing trend where B2B companies adopt consumer-style marketing to build brand awareness and trust before initiating sales conversations. By prioritizing memorable, real-world experiences, Abnormal AI hopes to cut through the noise of a saturated cybersecurity market and establish a distinct identity.
