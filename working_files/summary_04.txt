Stripe says the singularity has arrived. Here's what they told investors about it.
============================================================

Stripe executives informed investors that they believe the artificial intelligence singularity began on January 1, 2026, prompting the company to adjust its long-term business strategy accordingly.

### Key Points
- Stripe leadership identified January 1, 2026, as the start of the singularity, citing a significant inflection in new firm creation and economic trends.
- The company reported a 41% year-over-year revenue increase for the first half of the fiscal year, attributing growth to AI acceleration.
- Stripe announced the acquisition of OpenRouter, an AI model marketplace, alongside the investor letter.
- CEO Patrick Collison, president John Collison, and technology lead William Gaybrick authored the letter to shareholders.
- Stripe joins other tech leaders, including Sam Altman and Elon Musk, in publicly declaring that the singularity has arrived.

### Why it Matters
This declaration signals a shift in how major financial infrastructure providers are positioning their operations to navigate an increasingly unpredictable economic landscape. By embracing this framework, Stripe aims to accelerate AI adoption while leveraging its private company structure to maintain long-term strategic flexibility.
