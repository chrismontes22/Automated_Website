Exclusive-Eric Trump-backed defense technology company Space-Eyes to go public in $638 million SPAC deal, sources say
============================================================

Defense technology firm Space-Eyes will go public through a $638 million merger with McKinley Acquisition Corp, with Eric Trump joining the company as a strategic adviser.

### Key Points
- The Miami-based company specializes in AI-powered counter-drone and geospatial intelligence technologies for government and corporate clients.
- Eric Trump, the third-largest private investor in Space-Eyes, will advise the firm on security threats and help identify board candidates.
- The merger is expected to generate up to $251.7 million in gross proceeds to fund business expansion and manufacturing.
- Space-Eyes is currently negotiating $35 million in new contracts, a significant increase from its current annual revenue of approximately $1 million.
- The company plans to scale operations to target diverse sectors, including Caribbean drug interdiction, Middle Eastern defense, and U.S. prison security.

### Why it Matters
This deal highlights the surging investor appetite for defense technology as global demand for AI-driven drone detection and autonomous systems continues to rise. By leveraging high-profile advisory roles and fresh capital, Space-Eyes aims to transition from a research-focused entity into a major player in the government contracting market.
