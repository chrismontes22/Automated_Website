US stock market hits all-time high as investors bet big on AI
============================================================

The United States stock market reached record highs on Tuesday as sustained investor enthusiasm for artificial intelligence drove significant gains across the S&P 500 and Nasdaq Composite indexes.

### Key Points
- The S&P 500 rose 0.58 percent to reach a new all-time high, while the Nasdaq Composite climbed 0.45 percent.
- Amazon led the "Magnificent Seven" tech stocks with a 1.95 percent gain, followed by Microsoft and Tesla.
- Marvell Technology and Cisco saw significant growth, rising 5.81 percent and 4.54 percent, respectively.
- The S&P 500 has recorded a 14 percent increase so far in 2026, with the Nasdaq up 18.78 percent.
- Market analysts attribute the rally to massive capital investment in AI data centers despite rising interest rates and geopolitical tensions.

### Why it Matters
The current market rally highlights a heavy reliance on the artificial intelligence sector to drive global equity performance. While investors remain optimistic about long-term returns, the concentration of growth in a few tech companies creates significant vulnerability if the AI sector fails to meet high earnings expectations.
