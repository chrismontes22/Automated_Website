Zcash Hits Eight-Year High After Grayscale Advances U.S. ETF Push
============================================================

Zcash surged over 22% to reach a market capitalization of $13.8 billion as investors reacted to Grayscale’s latest regulatory filings regarding a potential Zcash exchange-traded fund conversion.

### Key Points
- Zcash (ZEC) climbed to approximately $819 per token, marking a significant recovery from its June lows following a patched security vulnerability.
- Grayscale filed an amended registration statement with the SEC to convert its Zcash Trust into an ETF listed on NYSE Arca under the ticker ZCSH.
- Trading volume was dominated by derivatives, with ZEC futures reaching $9.54 billion compared to $1.06 billion in spot market activity.
- Digital Currency Group is currently in nonbinding discussions to potentially contribute 200,000 ZEC tokens to the proposed trust.
- The asset now ranks as the 12th-largest cryptocurrency by market value, despite remaining 75% below its all-time high of $3,190.

### Why it Matters
The potential conversion of the Grayscale Zcash Trust into an ETF signals a significant step toward institutionalizing privacy-focused digital assets within traditional financial markets. If approved, this move could increase liquidity and accessibility for investors while validating the long-term viability of Zcash following its recent technical recovery.
