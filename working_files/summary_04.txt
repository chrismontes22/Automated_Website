Saber CEO Denies Replacing Rideshare Writer With ChatGPT But Says ‘I Would Frankly In Retrospect Have Been Happy To Replace Her With AI’
============================================================

Saber Interactive CEO Matthew Karch confirmed the use of generative AI in the upcoming game Rideshare after former lead writer Stella Sacco alleged she was replaced by technology.

### Key Points
- Former narrative designer Stella Sacco claimed Saber Interactive replaced her with ChatGPT to generate content for the game Rideshare.
- Saber Interactive initially denied replacing writers with AI but later confirmed its use for an experimental "free-ride" mode.
- CEO Matthew Karch stated he would have been happy to replace Sacco with AI in retrospect, citing performance and honesty concerns.
- The company plans to update the game's Steam page to disclose specific AI features once the development set is finalized.
- Sacco maintains that the company used AI as a marketing tactic and failed to disclose its involvement to potential players.

### Why it Matters
This dispute highlights the growing tension between game developers and publishers regarding the integration of generative AI in creative workflows. The public fallout underscores the reputational risks companies face when failing to transparently disclose AI usage to consumers on platforms like Steam.
