CNET Finds 75% of US Adults Couldn’t Care Less About a Foldable iPhone
============================================================

Apple may unveil its first foldable smartphone, the iPhone Ultra, at its September event, though consumer interest remains low due to high projected costs and current market trends.

### Key Points
- The rumored iPhone Ultra is expected to feature a book-style folding design similar to the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 and Google Pixel Fold.
- Analysts estimate the device will retail between $2,000 and $2,500, significantly higher than the average consumer's preferred price point of $781.
- A CNET survey of 2,605 US adults found that 75% of respondents have no interest in purchasing a foldable Apple device.
- A global RAM shortage is driving up hardware costs, causing 41% of US adults to reconsider or delay their planned smartphone upgrades.

### Why it Matters
The potential launch of a high-priced foldable iPhone highlights a growing disconnect between Apple’s premium hardware strategy and the financial constraints of the average consumer. As memory chip shortages drive up device prices, many users are opting to retain older phones longer, signaling a challenging environment for luxury tech adoption.
