France’s Pasqal is public, with $360M and a valuation of about 100 times revenue
============================================================

Quantum computing firm Pasqal began trading on the Nasdaq under the ticker PSQL after completing a merger with Bleichroeder Acquisition Corp. II that secured approximately $360 million in cash.

### Key Points
- Pasqal finalized its merger on August 27, securing $360 million in capital to fund manufacturing, software development, and international expansion.
- The company’s valuation is based on a $2 billion pre-money figure, despite reporting 2025 projected revenues of €16.5 million.
- Pasqal utilizes neutral-atom technology, which traps atoms with lasers to avoid the extreme cryogenic requirements of traditional superconducting qubits.
- Current commercial partners include Saudi Aramco, Crédit Agricole CIB, and LG Electronics, with seven quantum processing units already deployed.
- The company maintains strategic ties to the Nvidia and IBM Quantum Network ecosystems to facilitate hardware distribution.
- Nobel laureate Alain Aspect serves as non-executive chair, while the French government retains veto power over significant non-EU ownership stakes.

### Why it Matters
This listing represents a significant test for the European deeptech sector as it seeks to scale foundational science into industrial-grade commercial operations. By choosing the Nasdaq, Pasqal highlights the ongoing challenge European firms face in accessing sufficient capital depth compared to domestic markets.
