Fake ChatGPT and Claude Apps Abuse Signed Executables to Deploy Crypto Miners
============================================================

Cybercriminals are distributing the YobaLoader malware and cryptocurrency miners by masquerading as legitimate desktop applications for AI tools like OpenAI’s ChatGPT and Anthropic’s Claude on GitHub.

### Key Points
- Attackers use SEO poisoning to surface fake GitHub projects, such as "GPT-6 Astra" and "Claude Opus," in search results.
- The malware employs DLL sideloading by pairing malicious files with legitimate, signed executables from OpenAI and Anthropic to bypass security.
- YobaDropper includes anti-analysis features, such as long sleep timers and geographic filters that exclude several Commonwealth of Independent States countries.
- The infection chain uses a domain generation algorithm and Google’s DNS-over-HTTPS to fetch payloads while evading network monitoring.
- Final payloads typically inject an XMRig cryptocurrency miner into the system process "svchost.exe" to hijack victim computing resources.

### Why it Matters
This campaign highlights how threat actors exploit the high demand for AI software to distribute malware through trusted platforms like GitHub. By mimicking legitimate developer tools, attackers can effectively bypass standard security filters and deceive users into installing malicious software.
