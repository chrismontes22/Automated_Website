Bitcoin, Nasdaq futures decline as Trump won’t rule out more Iran strikes
============================================================

Bitcoin and Nasdaq futures declined as President Donald Trump refused to rule out further military strikes against Iran ahead of the upcoming U.S. midterm elections.

### Key Points
- Bitcoin fell 1.3% to $83,324, while WTI crude oil futures rose nearly 1% to $93.28 per barrel.
- President Trump stated that while the war could end soon, he remains open to additional military action against Iran.
- Iran’s Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi declared the nation is prepared for conflict after Trump rejected a proposal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz.
- The 10-year Treasury yield reached 5.20%, marking its highest level since 2007 due to ongoing inflation and geopolitical concerns.
- Markets are awaiting U.S. PCE inflation, ISM manufacturing, and nonfarm payroll data releases scheduled for later this week.

### Why it Matters
Geopolitical instability in the Middle East is heightening market volatility and fueling inflation concerns that impact both traditional equities and cryptocurrency assets. Investors are closely monitoring upcoming U.S. economic data to gauge how these tensions might influence Federal Reserve interest rate policies.
