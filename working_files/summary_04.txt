These TCL smartphones and tablets are designed to protect your eye health — and a bunch of them are heavily discounted at Amazon today
============================================================

TCL is offering significant discounts of up to 35% on its NXTPAPER smartphone and tablet lineup at Amazon ahead of the retailer's upcoming Big Deal Days event.

### Key Points
- The TCL NXTPAPER 11 Plus tablet is currently discounted by 35%, bringing the price down to $239.98.
- Amazon has reduced the price of the TCL NXTPAPER 70 Pro smartphone by 15%, now available for $279.98.
- NXTPAPER technology features matte display layers designed to reduce blue light exposure and minimize eye strain.
- The 70 Pro smartphone includes a 6.9-inch 120Hz display, a 5,200mAh battery, and IP68-rated durability.
- The NXTPAPER 11 Plus tablet features a MediaTek processor, quad speakers with DTS audio, and 256GB of storage.

### Why it Matters
These discounts provide a cost-effective entry point for consumers seeking mobile devices that prioritize eye health without sacrificing performance or modern features. By offering these price cuts before major sales events, TCL is positioning its specialized display technology as a competitive alternative to standard LCD and OLED mobile hardware.
