Bitcoin flat near $64,000 as Kospi's record 17% surge leaves crypto untouched
============================================================

Bitcoin prices remained stagnant near $64,300 on Friday, failing to mirror a record 17% surge in South Korea’s Kospi index and significant gains among major global semiconductor stocks.

### Key Points
- Bitcoin held steady at $64,300 while major equity markets experienced a sharp rebound following a two-week selloff.
- South Korea’s Kospi index jumped 17%, driven by gains exceeding 23% for both Samsung and SK Hynix.
- Taiwan Semiconductor shares rose 10% as global chip stocks recovered from recent losses.
- A security vulnerability in Coldcard hardware wallets resulted in the theft of 594 bitcoin, valued at approximately $38 million.
- Most major cryptocurrencies, including Ether, XRP, and Solana, showed little movement despite the broader rally in technology equities.

### Why it Matters
The decoupling of bitcoin from semiconductor stocks suggests a shift in investor sentiment as crypto assets fail to track the volatility of traditional tech markets. This divergence highlights a potential cooling in the historical correlation between digital assets and high-growth equity sectors.
