How 'Musk' director Alex Gibney became a critic
============================================================

Oscar-winning filmmaker Alex Gibney explores the rise and influence of Elon Musk in his new documentary, Musk, which examines the billionaire's business practices and controversial political involvement.

### Key Points
- The documentary *Musk* premieres in theaters this Friday and will be available to stream on HBO in the coming months.
- Director Alex Gibney spent four years researching the film, utilizing interviews, legal documents, and internal communications to analyze Musk’s companies.
- Musk’s attorney, Alex Spiro, threatened a defamation lawsuit regarding the film's depiction of text messages between Musk and influencer Ashley St. Clair.
- The film alleges that Musk consistently oversells the capabilities of Tesla’s self-driving technology and SpaceX’s long-term goals to inflate market value.
- Gibney previously investigated corporate scandals involving Enron and Elizabeth Holmes before turning his focus to the tech entrepreneur's vast influence.

### Why it Matters
The documentary highlights growing concerns regarding the intersection of private corporate power and government policy, particularly regarding Musk's xAI and SpaceX. By questioning the transparency of these companies, the film invites public scrutiny into whether Musk’s influence over critical infrastructure and political discourse is being managed responsibly.
