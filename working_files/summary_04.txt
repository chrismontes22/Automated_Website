New leak may reveal the iPhone Ultra’s two launch colors
============================================================

Leaked accessory images suggest Apple’s upcoming foldable iPhone Ultra may launch in silver and dark blue color options ahead of the company's official product announcement next month.

### Key Points
- Leaker Sonny Dickson shared images of third-party camera protectors designed for the rumored iPhone Ultra.
- The leaked accessories indicate the foldable device could be available in silver and dark blue finishes.
- Previous reports suggested the foldable model might feature dark gray or black color variations instead of blue.
- Apple is also expected to introduce new dark cherry and light blue color options for the upcoming iPhone 18 Pro.
- The official announcement for both the iPhone 18 Pro and the foldable iPhone Ultra is scheduled for next month.

### Why it Matters
These leaks provide early insight into Apple's design strategy for its first entry into the competitive foldable smartphone market. Establishing distinct color options is a key component of the company's branding efforts to differentiate its new hardware from existing flagship devices.
