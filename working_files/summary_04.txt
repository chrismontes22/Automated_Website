Zep AI (YC W24) Is Hiring a Head of Forward Deployed Engineering
============================================================

Enterprise AI memory platform Zep is hiring a solo B2B demand generation marketer to manage growth strategies and campaign automation using autonomous agents for technical enterprise products.

### Key Points
- Zep provides enterprise-scale agent memory and context graph management, serving clients including Samsung, Zscaler, and various Fortune 500 technology companies.
- The company is seeking a marketer to operate as a team of one, utilizing AI agents to handle content production, campaign execution, and funnel measurement.
- Candidates must possess experience in B2B demand generation for technical products and demonstrate proficiency in using coding agents like Claude Code or Cursor.
- The role reports directly to founder Daniel, a former head of machine learning at SparkPost, and involves managing the company's search, social, and email pipelines.
- The position offers a remote-friendly work environment with a base in San Francisco and includes comprehensive benefits such as platinum health insurance and 401K matching.

### Why it Matters
This role highlights a growing trend in the tech industry where companies leverage AI agents to replace traditional marketing headcount, allowing small teams to achieve enterprise-scale output. By prioritizing candidates who build with automation, Zep is positioning its marketing operations to mirror the efficiency and technical sophistication of its core AI memory product.
