Zcash miner Fortitude names former Hut 8 chief Jaime Leverton CEO ahead of Nasdaq deal
============================================================

Fortitude Mining Holdings has appointed former Hut 8 CEO Jaime Leverton as its new chief executive to lead the Zcash-focused company toward a public listing on the Nasdaq.

### Key Points
- Jaime Leverton will assume the CEO role on September 21, succeeding Andrea Childs, who transitions to chief operating officer.
- The company plans to go public through a merger with HeartSciences, targeting a combined valuation of at least $400 million.
- Fortitude, owned by Digital Currency Group, produced 28% of all Zcash mined during the first half of 2026.
- The firm reported $20.9 million in revenue for the second quarter and operates over 60 megawatts of power across seven U.S. states.
- Upon completion of the merger, the entity expects to trade on the Nasdaq under the ticker symbol "TUDE."

### Why it Matters
This leadership transition signals a strategic push by Digital Currency Group to solidify Fortitude’s position as a major player in the Zcash mining sector. The upcoming public listing provides the company with increased capital access and market visibility as it scales its infrastructure and operations.
