Apple adds nearly 45 hearing devices to its Made for iPhone compatibility list
============================================================

Apple has expanded its Made for iPhone hearing aid program to include 623 total models, adding 43 new devices across 22 brands to improve wireless audio streaming compatibility.

### Key Points
- The updated list now features 623 compatible hearing aid models from 104 different brands.
- New brands added to the program include Bellson, MicroSom, Panasonic, RION, and Yeasound.
- Many of the newly added devices support bidirectional streaming, enabling hands-free calling for users.
- Apple officially added the MacBook Neo to its system requirements for streaming, effective with macOS Tahoe 26.
- Existing models from Bernafon, Pro Akustik, and SoniTon were updated to support bidirectional audio functionality.

### Why it Matters
This expansion enhances accessibility by allowing more users to integrate hearing devices directly with Apple’s ecosystem, including the iPhone, iPad, and Apple Vision Pro. By increasing the variety of supported hardware, Apple strengthens its position in the health-tech market and provides users with greater flexibility for hands-free communication.
