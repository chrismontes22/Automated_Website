It’s time to find a new browser
============================================================

The Browser Company has announced that the Arc browser will be discontinued by Christmas, prompting users to explore alternatives like Dia, Zen, Orion, and other popular web browsers.

### Key Points
- The Browser Company is shifting focus to its AI-centric browser, Dia, leaving Arc in maintenance mode until its final shutdown.
- Recommended browser alternatives include Zen, Orion, Vivaldi, Helium, Firefox, and Brave, each offering different levels of customization and privacy.
- New product releases featured this week include the Blink Mini 4K camera, the Teenage Engineering EP-35 amp, and the Claude Duet AI assistant.
- Amazon has rebranded Kindle Unlimited to Kindle Plus, introducing new features such as AI-narrated audiobooks for a $12 monthly subscription.
- The stop-motion film *Wildwood* is highlighted for its reliance on traditional human craftsmanship rather than generative AI.

### Why it Matters
The discontinuation of Arc marks a significant shift for power users who favored its unique interface and aesthetic design. As the browser market becomes increasingly crowded with AI-integrated tools, users must navigate a fragmented landscape to find software that balances modern features with privacy and performance.
