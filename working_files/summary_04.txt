How to Pre-Order Your iPhone 18 Pro From Apple
============================================================

Apple has officially opened pre-orders for the new iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max, with the devices scheduled for a wide retail launch on September 18.

### Key Points
- Pre-orders for the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max officially began on Saturday, September 12, at 5:00 a.m. Pacific Time.
- Customers are encouraged to use the Apple Store app to finalize configurations and payment methods in advance to ensure a faster checkout process.
- Apple recommends verifying account details, including billing addresses and payment methods, to avoid delays during the high-traffic launch window.
- Trade-in valuations should be completed beforehand to streamline the purchase, with old devices required within 14 to 15 days of receiving the new phone.
- In-store pickup remains a viable alternative for securing a device if shipping estimates for home delivery begin to slip shortly after launch.

### Why it Matters
Securing a launch-day delivery for new iPhone models often requires rapid checkout due to high demand and limited initial supply. By preparing account details and payment methods early, consumers can significantly reduce the risk of facing extended shipping delays.
