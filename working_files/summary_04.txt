"AI is a technofascist, anti-human and anti-art project", says creator of lefty tech satire Future? No Thanks!, before explaining why he used it anyway
============================================================

Game developer Paulo Pedercini has disclosed the use of generative AI tools for debugging and voice acting in his ecosocialist satire game, Future? No Thanks!.

### Key Points
- Paulo Pedercini used ChatGPT and Claude to assist with minor code debugging during the final stages of development.
- The game features approximately two minutes of AI-generated voice acting via ElevenLabs for characters intended to parody artificial intelligence.
- Pedercini previously removed AI-generated music from the game due to concerns regarding the technology's association with copyright infringement.
- A generative AI disclosure has been added to the game's Steam store page following the developer's public admission.
- Development of the project began in 2021, with the developer stating that 99% of the codebase was written without AI assistance.

### Why it Matters
This disclosure highlights the growing tension between independent developers and the evolving ethical standards regarding AI usage in creative projects. It underscores the difficulty of maintaining a strictly "AI-free" development process while navigating the practical limitations of modern software tools and community expectations.
