Welcome to Opt Out October: It’s Time to Ditch Unwanted Subscriptions and AI Spies
============================================================

The Electronic Frontier Foundation has launched its 2026 Opt Out October guide to help users restrict data collection, avoid invasive AI training, and improve overall digital privacy.

### Key Points
- The guide provides step-by-step instructions for adjusting privacy settings on major platforms like Snapchat, Twitch, and Chrome.
- It offers recommendations for alternative software, including privacy-focused social media platforms and Linux operating systems.
- Users can learn how to disable AI training features and opt out of intrusive age verification requirements.
- The resource includes advice on securing smart home devices, such as Amazon Alexa and Ring security cameras.
- The Electronic Frontier Foundation plans to provide ongoing updates to the guide to reflect changes in platform policies.

### Why it Matters
This initiative provides a centralized resource for consumers to regain control over their personal information amid increasing data harvesting by major technology companies. By simplifying complex privacy settings, the guide empowers users to reduce their digital footprint and mitigate risks associated with AI model training.
