(PR) Corsair Releases the HS80 V2 Max Wireless Gaming Headset
============================================================

Corsair has officially launched the HS80 v2 MAX WIRELESS gaming headset, featuring upgraded non-Newtonian drivers, extended battery life, and dual-wireless connectivity for PC, console, and mobile platforms.

### Key Points
- The headset features 50 mm non-Newtonian laminate drivers designed to reduce diaphragm vibration and improve audio clarity during intense gameplay.
- It offers up to 110 hours of battery life on 2.4 GHz wireless and up to 130 hours via Bluetooth connectivity.
- The device includes a broadcast-grade 10 mm microphone with optional AI-powered NVIDIA Broadcast noise reduction for compatible PC users.
- Multi-platform compatibility extends to PC, Mac, PlayStation, Nintendo Switch, and mobile devices through simultaneous wireless connections.
- The hardware is constructed with a durable aluminum frame and memory foam ear cushions, weighing a total of 370 grams.
- Available starting October 8, 2026, the product is supported by a two-year warranty through Corsair’s global retail network.

### Why it Matters
This release strengthens Corsair's competitive position in the high-end gaming peripheral market by addressing common user demands for extended battery life and superior audio precision. By integrating advanced driver technology and AI-enhanced communication tools, the company aims to provide a professional-grade experience for both competitive gamers and content creators.
