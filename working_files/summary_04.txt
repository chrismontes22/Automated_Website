Cash App is paying up to $2,500 in a settlement, but most will get far less
============================================================

Cash App and its parent company, Block Inc., are currently distributing payments to approved claimants following a $15 million class action settlement regarding platform security and data breaches.

### Key Points
- The $15 million settlement compensates users for out-of-pocket losses and lost time resulting from security breaches occurring between August 2018 and August 2024.
- Approximately 40,380 claims were approved for payment, with individual payouts averaging roughly $139.
- A separate $175 million settlement, mandated by the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau in June, addressed allegations of inadequate fraud protection.
- Payments for the class action lawsuit are being issued throughout October 2025 via check, prepaid card, Venmo, or Zelle.
- The deadline to file claims for these settlements has passed, and any current offers to file late claims are likely fraudulent.

### Why it Matters
These settlements highlight ongoing regulatory and legal scrutiny regarding the security protocols and customer support standards of major peer-to-peer payment platforms. The payouts serve as a reminder for users to remain vigilant against potential scams that often emerge following high-profile legal resolutions.
