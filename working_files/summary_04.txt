Ben Affleck’s ‘Ethical AI’ Vehicle Failing To Impress Human Viewers
============================================================

Ben Affleck’s new Netflix thriller Animals has received poor critical reception as audiences scrutinize the director’s controversial decision to incorporate AI technology into the film’s production process.

### Key Points
- Animals stars Ben Affleck and Kerry Washington as parents navigating a political kidnapping plot during a mayoral campaign.
- The film is currently struggling with low critic and audience scores on Rotten Tomatoes due to complaints regarding dialogue and visual composition.
- Affleck has publicly advocated for the "ethical" use of AI in Hollywood to assist creators without displacing human labor or violating intellectual property.
- Despite his advocacy, the specific technical applications of AI used during the production of Animals remain undisclosed by the director.

### Why it Matters
The negative reception of Animals highlights a growing tension between Hollywood’s push for AI integration and audience demand for authentic, human-led storytelling. This project serves as a high-profile test case for whether industry leaders can successfully balance emerging technology with the creative standards expected by viewers.
