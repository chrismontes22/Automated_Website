Can John Ternus find Apple’s next big thing?
============================================================

Apple has entered a new leadership era under CEO John Ternus, who is tasked with navigating the company from the iPhone-centric period into a future defined by AI-native hardware.

### Key Points
- John Ternus officially succeeded Tim Cook as Apple CEO less than two weeks before the company's September 2026 hardware event.
- The event featured the launch of the iPhone Duo, Apple’s first foldable smartphone, marking a significant shift in the company's hardware strategy.
- Apple is reportedly developing approximately six AI-native devices, including a smart home hub with a robotic arm and smart glasses expected by 2027.
- The company is undergoing a massive executive overhaul, with long-time leaders like Phil Schiller and Johny Srouji transitioning out of their primary roles.
- Apple is currently relying on a partnership with Google for AI models while it works to establish a long-term strategy for in-house AI development.

### Why it Matters
This leadership transition represents the most significant executive restructuring in Apple's history as the company attempts to move beyond the "Hotel California" culture of long-tenured management. Ternus must now prove he can successfully launch a new generation of AI-integrated products to secure Apple's market relevance as the iPhone era matures.
