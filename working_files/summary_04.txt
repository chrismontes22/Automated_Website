Sam Altman says he got ‘addicted’ to TikTok, including a Saturday afternoon where he scrolled ‘for like 3 hours’
============================================================

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman recently deleted TikTok after intentionally using the platform to study its recommendation algorithms while developing the company's now-abandoned Sora video generation application.

### Key Points
- Sam Altman admitted to becoming addicted to TikTok's recommendation engine while researching user engagement for OpenAI's internal projects.
- OpenAI officially scrapped its Sora social media app in March after six months of development to prioritize core generative AI goals.
- The company cited high compute costs and intense competition from rivals like Anthropic as primary reasons for abandoning the video-focused venture.
- Altman is currently collaborating with former Apple designer Jony Ive to develop a new AI-powered device intended to reduce digital notification fatigue.
- OpenAI maintains that its product philosophy prioritizes task completion over maximizing user time spent on its platforms.

### Why it Matters
This shift highlights the tension between addictive engagement models and the utility-focused design philosophy OpenAI aims to implement in its upcoming hardware. By moving away from social media features, the company is positioning its future AI devices as tools for productivity rather than platforms designed to capture user attention.
