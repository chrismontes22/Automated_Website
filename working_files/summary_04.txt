Crypto rallies through the Fed's first rate increase since 2023
============================================================

Bitcoin rose to $76,621 and the broader crypto market rallied after the Federal Reserve signaled a limited interest rate hike cycle, boosting investor confidence in risk-on assets.

### Key Points
- The Federal Reserve raised interest rates by 25 basis points to a 3.75%-4% range, projecting only one additional hike through 2027.
- Bitcoin climbed 0.88% to $76,621, while the small-cap CoinDesk 80 index outperformed with a 4.7% gain.
- Zcash surged 23% to a record $1,369 following a disclosure that investment firm Paradigm holds a stake in the privacy token.
- U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs saw continued outflows, shedding $295.98 million on Wednesday despite the broader market recovery.
- Aggregate crypto futures open interest increased to $64.4 billion, signaling that traders are actively adding exposure to the current rally.

### Why it Matters
The Federal Reserve's dovish outlook on future rate hikes has provided a much-needed catalyst for risk assets, helping crypto markets decouple from recent selling pressure. This shift suggests that investors are regaining an appetite for speculative assets, though persistent outflows from spot bitcoin ETFs indicate that institutional caution remains high.
