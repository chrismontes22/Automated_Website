Automating the ‘future of work’
============================================================

Capria Ventures is shifting its investment strategy to prioritize enterprise artificial intelligence, manufacturing automation, and the future of work while managing approximately ₹2,000 crore in total assets.

### Key Points
- Capria Ventures plans to invest in roughly 25 companies, with initial checks ranging from ₹5 crore to ₹15 crore per startup.
- The firm’s existing job-tech portfolio currently supports 1.4 million gig workers, generating nearly ₹200 crore in monthly earnings.
- Investment criteria focus on founder expertise, proprietary intellectual property, and solutions that provide measurable value to paying customers.
- The fund identifies significant growth potential in supply-chain coordination, factory automation, and health-tech solutions that expand clinical access.
- Past successful exits for the firm include acquisitions of companies such as Awign, Lab in App, and SalesKen.

### Why it Matters
This strategic pivot reflects a broader trend of venture capital firms moving toward industrial automation and AI-driven productivity tools to capture India's evolving manufacturing sector. By focusing on these high-growth areas, Capria Ventures aims to capitalize on the China+1 supply chain shift and the increasing demand for scalable enterprise technology.
