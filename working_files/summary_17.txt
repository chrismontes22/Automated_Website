The Enterprise Case for Open-Weight Models on Databricks
============================================================

Enterprise AI strategies are shifting toward multi-model architectures as companies increasingly adopt open-weight models to improve cost efficiency, data governance, and operational flexibility over single-provider frontier solutions.

### Key Points
- Databricks reports that the average number of unique models used per customer on its Unity Gateway increased from 4.2 to 7.1 since January 2026.
- Open-weight models now account for approximately 40% of token usage on the Unity Gateway platform, driven by the release of high-performing models like GLM 5.2.
- Enterprises are moving away from single-provider dependencies to avoid issues with restrictive guardrails, evolving data retention policies, and fluctuating model rankings.
- Databricks raised $3 billion in July 2026, with CEO Ali Ghodsi citing the need to secure massive GPU capacity to host and serve open-weight models.
- Unity Gateway allows organizations to switch between frontier and open-weight models via configuration changes rather than code, maintaining consistent governance and auditing.

### Why it Matters
Adopting a multi-model approach allows businesses to optimize AI spending and performance by matching specific workloads to the most cost-effective and capable models. This architectural flexibility ensures that companies can maintain operational continuity and strict data governance even as the rapidly evolving AI landscape introduces new models and policy requirements.
