Great Deals on Apple Stuff Today
============================================================

Amazon is currently offering significant discounts on Apple products, including AirPods Pro and MacBook Air models, as part of a limited-time sale event for Prime members.

### Key Points
- AirPods Pro 3 are available for $179, reflecting a $70 discount from the standard $249 retail price.
- The 15-inch MacBook Air with the M5 chip is priced at $1,299, down from its usual $1,499 cost.
- The 13-inch M5 MacBook Air is discounted to $1,099, reduced from the regular $1,299 price point.
- These promotional offers are scheduled to expire within a few hours of the announcement.
- Competing retailers like Best Buy are running concurrent sales, while Bookshop.org is providing free shipping incentives.

### Why it Matters
These flash sales provide consumers with an opportunity to purchase premium hardware at reduced prices during a period of rising retail costs. The aggressive discounting strategy also forces competing electronics retailers to adjust their pricing to maintain market share during the promotional window.
