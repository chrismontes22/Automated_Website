Russia jails IT worker for 13 years for donating money to Ukraine
============================================================

A Russian military court sentenced IT specialist Yuri Belenkiy to 13 years in prison for financing terrorism after Binance provided his cryptocurrency transaction history to Russian investigators.

### Key Points
- Yuri Belenkiy, a 49-year-old former IT director, received a 13-year sentence for donating over 120,000 roubles to Ukrainian military causes.
- The 2nd Western District Military Court convicted Belenkiy for transfers made between August 2022 and March 2024 to groups including the Azov battalion.
- Court documents reveal that Binance provided the defendant's transaction history to Russian authorities, which served as primary evidence in the criminal case.
- Belenkiy, who holds a Bulgarian residence permit, was arrested in September 2023 upon arriving in Moscow and plans to appeal the verdict.
- The Russian financial monitoring agency currently lists Belenkiy as a terrorist and extremist, and he was fined 500,000 roubles.

### Why it Matters
This case highlights the significant risks associated with using centralized cryptocurrency exchanges that may comply with international law enforcement requests. It also underscores how Russian authorities are increasingly utilizing digital financial records to prosecute citizens for supporting Ukrainian organizations, even when those individuals reside abroad.
