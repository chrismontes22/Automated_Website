China says it agrees to a $30B tariff cut and trade council with the U.S.
============================================================

The United States and China have agreed to reduce tariffs on $30 billion worth of goods and establish a formal dialogue to address the risks of artificial intelligence.

### Key Points
- The tariff reductions cover $30 billion of non-sensitive goods in each direction, including agricultural products, cosmetics, toys, and small appliances.
- President Xi Jinping and President Donald Trump agreed to establish a new trade council to facilitate ongoing economic negotiations.
- Both nations will launch a formal dialogue on artificial intelligence in November to discuss technology risks and establish an incident communication channel.
- Officials agreed to adopt the term "super intelligence" to describe advanced AI technologies during future bilateral discussions.
- The two countries extended their existing trade truce by two months to allow additional time for negotiating a more comprehensive trade agreement.

### Why it Matters
This agreement signals a period of relative stability between the world's two largest economies as they attempt to de-escalate trade tensions. By formalizing communication channels for emerging technologies like AI, both nations are setting a precedent for managing global security risks associated with rapid digital innovation.
