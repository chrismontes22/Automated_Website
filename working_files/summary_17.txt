Wolverine developer Insomniac denies using generative AI after fans spot strange restroom sign
============================================================

Insomniac Games has officially denied using generative AI in Marvel’s Wolverine after players speculated that a distorted restroom sign icon was created using artificial intelligence tools.

### Key Points
- Community director James Stevenson attributed the distorted restroom sign to a technical UV map bug rather than generative AI.
- Insomniac confirmed that a patch is currently in development to resolve the visual error within the game.
- Stevenson addressed additional technical concerns, including game crashes linked to the Scent Trailers and Audio Sense systems.
- The developer emphasized that they are testing fixes carefully to avoid introducing new stability issues for players.
- Marvel’s Wolverine recently received a three-out-of-five-star review from Eurogamer, citing a mix of strong characterization and dated gameplay mechanics.

### Why it Matters
This clarification highlights the growing sensitivity among gamers regarding the integration of generative AI in modern software development. As studios face increased scrutiny, transparent communication about technical bugs remains essential to maintaining player trust and managing public perception.
