5 Highly Rated TP-Link Devices That Aren't Routers
============================================================

Beyond its core networking equipment, TP-Link offers a variety of highly-rated consumer electronics, including smart home devices, PC components, and connectivity adapters that maintain the brand's reputation for reliability.

### Key Points
- The TP-Link Archer TX3000E PCIe Wi-Fi card provides Wi-Fi 6 speeds and Bluetooth 5.3 for desktop computers, holding a 4.7-star rating from over 10,000 Amazon reviews.
- Kasa Smart Plug HS103P2 and the Kasa Smart Light Switch HS200 offer hub-free smart home control and integration with Alexa and Google Home.
- The TP-Link UE306 USB to Ethernet adapter enables wired internet connections for devices lacking Ethernet ports, including laptops and the Nintendo Switch.
- The Tapo C210 2K Pan Tilt Security Camera features motion detection, infrared night vision, and support for local microSD storage up to 512GB.
- All featured products were selected based on maintaining at least a 4.5-star rating with thousands of customer reviews on Amazon.

### Why it Matters
These products demonstrate TP-Link's successful expansion from a router manufacturer into a broader ecosystem of affordable, user-friendly consumer technology. By leveraging its networking expertise, the company provides accessible smart home and connectivity solutions that integrate seamlessly into existing digital environments.
