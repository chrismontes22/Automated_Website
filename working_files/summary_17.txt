Sennheiser launch the Spectera SKM handheld
============================================================

Sennheiser has officially launched the Spectera SKM handheld transmitter, completing its bidirectional wideband wireless ecosystem designed for professional broadcast, theater, and live sound production environments.

### Key Points
- The Spectera SKM utilizes Sennheiser’s WMAS technology to deliver ultra-low-latency wireless audio at 1ms.
- The system operates on a single RF wideband channel, eliminating the need for separate wireless control networks.
- Engineers can remotely monitor battery life, signal strength, and audio levels while adjusting gain and low-cut settings in real time.
- The transmitter is compatible with a wide range of Sennheiser and Neumann microphone capsules and features AES-256 encryption.
- Available now for $1,999, the device is offered in both UHF and 1.4GHz frequency variants.

### Why it Matters
This release completes the core Spectera ecosystem, offering professional users a streamlined workflow that reduces the complexity of frequency coordination. By integrating control data directly into the wideband audio channel, the system provides greater operational visibility and efficiency for large-scale productions.
