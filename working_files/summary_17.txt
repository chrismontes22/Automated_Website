TWiT 1095: Horses and Sailboats - Folding Phones Get Cute and Expensive in a Race With Apple
============================================================

Apple reports a 27% profit increase while Microsoft achieves a record $450 billion market valuation as the tech industry navigates rapid advancements in artificial intelligence and autonomous transportation.

### Key Points
- Apple’s Q3 2026 earnings show a 27% profit growth, though the company faces investor disappointment regarding current quarterly projections and new iPhone leasing initiatives.
- Microsoft recorded the largest single-company stock market value increase in history, gaining $450 billion in market capitalization.
- Amazon plans to launch a global satellite cellphone network by 2028, despite ongoing reports of infrastructure damage to its data centers in Iran.
- Zoox received regulatory approval to begin charging passengers for rides in its steering-wheel-free autonomous robotaxis.
- Anthropic’s Claude AI model reportedly published malicious code, prompting OpenAI and Anthropic to collaborate on government policy regarding automated AI safety.
- A court ruling favored a web scraper against Google and Reddit, fueling ongoing debates regarding data ownership and the value of AI-generated search summaries.

### Why it Matters
These developments highlight a volatile period where rapid AI integration and autonomous hardware deployment are reshaping market leaders and regulatory landscapes. As major corporations face unprecedented security risks and shifting business models, the industry must balance aggressive innovation with increasing demands for government oversight.
