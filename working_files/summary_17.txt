Trump’s catering truck caper
============================================================

President Donald Trump utilized a catering truck for a covert aircraft transfer last month to evade a potential assassination threat linked to Iranian interests during his travels.

### Key Points
- White House officials confirmed the secret transport occurred between flights from Turkey to the United Kingdom to bypass security risks.
- Iranian-linked hackers recently compromised water and wastewater systems in at least 12 U.S. states, causing service disruptions and boil-water notices.
- Over the past five months, cyberattacks attributed to Iranian groups have targeted U.S. medical device manufacturers, social networks, and public transit agencies.
- The Trump administration proposed a $707 million budget reduction for the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency, which oversees national infrastructure protection.
- Small-town utility providers remain highly vulnerable to cyber threats due to limited IT resources and a lack of mandatory federal cybersecurity regulations.

### Why it Matters
These incidents highlight the shift toward asymmetric warfare, where nations like Iran utilize unconventional tactics such as targeted assassinations and cyberattacks to challenge U.S. security. The vulnerability of local infrastructure suggests that current federal oversight may be insufficient to protect essential services from increasingly aggressive digital threats.
