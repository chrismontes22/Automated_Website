The AI Apocalypse Is Here
============================================================

American public skepticism toward artificial intelligence reflects a growing, intuitive realization that widespread adoption of the technology threatens to erode individual liberty, human agency, and the foundations of a free society.

### Key Points
- Industry leaders openly pursue artificial general intelligence (AGI) designed to outperform humans in most economically valuable work, risking widespread human obsolescence.
- Critics argue that generative AI models, which mimic human personality and thought, are already unsettling human psychology and degrading the social fabric.
- Proposed interventions range from data center moratoriums and national security restrictions to calls for an international treaty banning superintelligence.
- The rapid pace of AI development threatens to break the intergenerational transmission of wisdom, leaving society unable to orient itself toward a stable future.
- Reliance on AI for writing and intellectual tasks risks "moral surrender," as individuals outsource the essential human process of forming thoughts and developing character.

### Why it Matters
The rise of AI represents a moral and cosmological shift that transcends traditional economic or industrial revolutions by challenging the status of humans as the primary bearers of language and intelligence. If left unchecked, this technology threatens to replace the capitalist work ethic with a system of state-managed dependence, ultimately undermining the individual autonomy necessary for a functioning democracy.
