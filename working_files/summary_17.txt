Big Tech is betting $700 billion on AI. Healthcare will decide whether the bet pays off
============================================================

Major technology companies are investing over $700 billion in AI infrastructure, with the healthcare sector serving as a critical testing ground for achieving significant, real-world economic productivity gains.

### Key Points
- Microsoft, Alphabet, Meta, and Amazon are collectively spending over $700 billion on capital expenditures to expand AI infrastructure.
- U.S. healthcare spending reached $5.3 trillion in 2024, with nearly $1 trillion attributed to administrative costs.
- Microsoft is collaborating with the Mayo Clinic on a healthcare-specific AI model, while Google Cloud is partnering with CVS Health to power the Health100 platform.
- CMS has introduced a 2.5% efficiency adjustment for 2026 physician payments, signaling that regulators expect AI to lower operational costs.
- Providers must adopt AI early to capture savings before reimbursement rates are adjusted downward to reflect increased industry efficiency.

### Why it Matters
The massive capital investment by Big Tech requires tangible productivity improvements to justify long-term financial returns. Healthcare provides a unique opportunity to prove this value by reducing administrative friction and expanding patient access in a sector currently constrained by workforce shortages.
