I use AI (Oct 2026)
============================================================

A Salesforce research engineer details how agentic AI has transformed his workflow over the past year, shifting from manual coding to managing multiple autonomous AI agents for development.

### Key Points
- The author now uses agentic tools like Claude Code to handle over 90% of his programming tasks, including feature prototyping, bug fixes, and test creation.
- Managing multiple AI agents simultaneously has introduced new challenges, such as cognitive fatigue and the need for human oversight to prevent "over-architected" or low-quality output.
- Generative AI has become the default for research and search, though the author remains skeptical of its utility for creative writing and commercial art generation.
- The author has implemented a personal AI policy to establish ethical boundaries and maintain human agency as AI tools become increasingly integrated into daily life.
- Despite advancements in AI capabilities, the author emphasizes that human expertise remains essential for high-level architectural design and nuanced, original creative work.

### Why it Matters
This shift toward agentic workflows highlights a significant evolution in software engineering, where human roles are transitioning from manual coding to high-level system management and validation. As these tools become more autonomous, the challenge for professionals lies in balancing increased productivity with the need to maintain craft, critical thinking, and personal voice.
