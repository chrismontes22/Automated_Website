EIF’s incoming chief says Europe’s Tech Champions funds hold 15 unicorns
============================================================

Incoming European Investment Fund CEO Jean-Christophe Laloux is prioritizing the expansion of growth-stage capital to ensure European startups can scale domestically rather than relocating to foreign markets.

### Key Points
- Jean-Christophe Laloux will assume leadership of the European Investment Fund on January 1, focusing on closing the funding gap for Series B and C startups.
- The European Tech Champions Initiative, a €4bn fund of funds, has backed 12 venture funds and currently supports 15 European unicorns.
- A second version of the initiative launching in November will target smaller funds between €300m and €500m to broaden regional ecosystem support.
- The EIB Group has earmarked €3bn for AI gigafactories and continues to provide venture debt and equity structures for deep tech sectors like biotech and defense.
- European deep tech valuations have surged from €1tn to €4tn over the past five years, with significant growth in security, space, and climate technologies.

### Why it Matters
Strengthening Europe's capital markets is essential for maintaining technological sovereignty and preventing successful startups from moving abroad for growth funding. By providing larger investment vehicles, the EIB Group aims to foster a self-sustaining ecosystem that keeps high-value innovation and economic productivity within the continent.
