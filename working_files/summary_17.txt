★ The iPhones 18 Pro
============================================================

The Apple iPhone 18 Pro marks the 20th generation of the company's flagship smartphone, delivering incremental hardware refinements and improved thermal performance over the previous year's model.

### Key Points
- The iPhone 18 Pro features a new variable aperture main camera, offering four stops from ƒ/1.48 to ƒ/4.0 for enhanced artistic control.
- It is powered by the A20 Pro chip, which provides noticeable speed and efficiency gains alongside a redesigned vapor chamber for better sustained performance.
- Apple has moved the Face ID infrared camera under the display, reducing the Dynamic Island cutout size and increasing usable screen space for Live Activities.
- The device introduces Photographic Styles 3, which adds a "Texture" layer with film-like grain and halation effects to the camera software.
- Most models utilize Apple’s proprietary C2 cellular modem, though U.S. iPhone 18 Pro Max units continue to use Qualcomm hardware to satisfy existing contracts.

### Why it Matters
The iPhone 18 Pro represents the culmination of Apple’s strategy to prioritize consistent, year-over-year technical refinement over radical design shifts. While the device lacks the excitement of a major redesign, its cumulative improvements ensure that users upgrading every few years receive a significantly more capable and durable product.
