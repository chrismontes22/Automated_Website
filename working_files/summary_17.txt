9 best Labor Day laptop deals I've found this weekend — up to $900 off Dell, HP, Apple, Acer, Lenovo, and more
============================================================

Major retailers including Best Buy, Amazon, Dell, and HP are offering significant Labor Day discounts on a wide range of laptops, with some price reductions reaching up to $900.

### Key Points
- Best Buy is offering up to $900 off select laptops, including the Acer Aspire Go 15 for $499.99.
- Dell has discounted the XPS 13 to $699.99, with an additional $100 student discount available.
- HP is running promotions with savings of up to 65% off across its laptop lineup.
- Apple is providing up to $150 in gift cards, while Walmart has entry-level laptops starting at $219.
- Lenovo is featuring various laptops and tablets with starting prices of $599.99.

### Why it Matters
These holiday sales provide a strategic opportunity for students and professionals to acquire high-performance hardware at significantly reduced prices before the end-of-year shopping season. By leveraging these discounts and additional education incentives, consumers can secure premium devices for work or school at a fraction of their standard retail cost.
