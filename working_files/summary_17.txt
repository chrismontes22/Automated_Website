We're Missing a Key Reason Why Americans Hate AI
============================================================

Artificial intelligence is facing significant public backlash as the technology fails to deliver on early promises of economic abundance, instead contributing to rising costs and persistent inflation.

### Key Points
- Tech leaders like Sam Altman and Marc Andreessen previously predicted that AI would drive the cost of labor and goods toward zero.
- Contrary to forecasts of mass unemployment and deflation, the current economy is experiencing high employment rates alongside rising consumer prices.
- The Conference Board reports that U.S. consumer confidence has reached its lowest level since 2014 due to ongoing cost-of-living concerns.
- Massive investment in AI infrastructure is currently driving up prices across the economy rather than creating the anticipated post-material abundance.

### Why it Matters
The disconnect between the promised economic benefits of AI and the current inflationary reality is fueling widespread public distrust toward the technology and its architects. This trend highlights a significant irony where the pursuit of automated efficiency is currently exacerbating financial strain for the average consumer.
