Rare ‘Antennagate’ press conference Q&A video surfaces online
============================================================

A previously unreleased 47-minute video recording of the 2010 iPhone 4 "Antennagate" press conference Q&A session has surfaced online, offering a rare look at Apple's historical crisis management.

### Key Points
- The footage features Steve Jobs, Tim Cook, and Bob Mansfield addressing signal reception issues following the iPhone 4 launch.
- The video was published by the YouTube channel Sir Mix-A-Lot Rare Music, though the source of the recording remains unknown.
- During the event, Apple offered customers free protective cases or full refunds to resolve the antenna design controversy.
- The Q&A session includes the moment executives famously produced their own caseless iPhones to demonstrate the signal drop issue to the press.

### Why it Matters
This footage provides a rare, unfiltered look at how Apple leadership navigated one of the company's most significant public relations crises. It serves as a historical case study in corporate transparency and crisis communication that continues to influence how tech companies manage product defects today.
