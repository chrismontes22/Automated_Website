Dumber Mini aims to be the perfect candybar dumphone
============================================================

The Dumber Mini is a new candybar-style featurephone running a stripped-down version of Android that combines a physical T9 keypad with modern app compatibility for £149.

### Key Points
- The device features a 2.8-inch touchscreen, 4GB of RAM, 64GB of storage, and dual nano-SIM slots with 4G LTE connectivity.
- It runs DumberOS, a custom fork of LineageOS that includes the Aurora Store for installing smartphone applications.
- Hardware includes an 8-megapixel rear camera, a 2-megapixel front camera, and a MediaTek chipset in a 110-gram frame.
- Preorders are currently open for £149, with the first 1,000 units expected to ship in the United Kingdom by Christmas.
- The phone is designed to reduce digital distraction through its compact form factor and limited screen real estate.

### Why it Matters
This device represents a growing market trend toward minimalist hardware designed to curb smartphone addiction while maintaining essential connectivity. By offering a lower price point than other niche minimalist handsets, it tests whether consumers are willing to trade high-end performance for a more intentional, simplified mobile experience.
