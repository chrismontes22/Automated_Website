Why We're Banning AI
============================================================

The magazine Current Affairs has implemented a strict anti-AI policy, banning all AI-generated text and illustrations to preserve human creativity and combat the automation of media production.

### Key Points
- The new policy, established through a union contract, prohibits the use of AI-generated content and art in the magazine.
- Current Affairs aims to counter the industry-wide trend of replacing human writers and artists with low-cost, automated content.
- Reports indicate that creative industries have lost over 200,000 jobs in four years, partly due to the adoption of generative AI workflows.
- The publication distinguishes between using AI as a minor assistive tool and using it to replace human labor or deceive readers.
- The magazine operates as a unionized 501(c)(3) organization, prioritizing human-led editorial processes over profit-driven automation.

### Why it Matters
This policy highlights a growing ideological divide in the media industry between profit-focused corporations and organizations prioritizing human labor. By rejecting AI-generated content, the publication challenges the market trend of prioritizing high-volume, low-cost output over editorial quality and worker sustainability.
