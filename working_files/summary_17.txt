Bitcoin's 44% gain in third quarter teases full-blown crypto bull run
============================================================

Bitcoin has surged 44% recently, outperforming major stock indices like the S&P 500 and Nasdaq as the cryptocurrency market experiences a significant rally driven by regulatory and technical factors.

### Key Points
- Bitcoin prices climbed above $84,000, reaching their highest level since January and clearing a key resistance line.
- The cryptocurrency closed the week of September 20 above its 50-week moving average for the first time in 45 weeks.
- Altcoins including ETH, XRP, SOL, UNI, and NEAR recorded gains ranging between 40% and 150%.
- A September 17 U.S. SEC decision granting a five-year innovation exemption for tokenized stock trading has provided a significant regulatory tailwind.
- Despite the rally, bitcoin remains 48% below its October record high of $126,000.

### Why it Matters
This shift signals a potential transition into a new bull market, marking a departure from earlier trends where tech stocks dominated investor interest. While the momentum suggests further growth, analysts advise caution regarding potential pullbacks as the market navigates this period of renewed volatility.
