The Odyssey and trojans again: MovieReaper attacks users in multiple countries via compromised torrents
============================================================

Cybercriminals are distributing a modular, multi-stage malware framework called MovieReaper by compromising the itorrents.org repository, leading to widespread infections across multiple countries and various industry sectors.

### Key Points
- The MovieReaper malware is disguised as popular pirated media files and distributed through compromised torrent trackers.
- Attackers leveraged the Solana blockchain to store and retrieve secondary command-and-control (C2) server addresses, enhancing the campaign's resilience against takedowns.
- The infection chain uses advanced anti-sandbox techniques, including manual DLL parsing and in-memory execution, to evade security software detection.
- Once installed, the final modular implant provides operators with extensive filesystem access, including file exfiltration and remote management capabilities.
- Victims have been identified globally, spanning individual users and organizations in sectors such as government, IT, retail, and agriculture.

### Why it Matters
This campaign demonstrates a sophisticated evolution in malware distribution by weaponizing public torrent repositories to reach a massive, diverse user base without compromising individual sites. By integrating blockchain technology for infrastructure management, the attackers have created a resilient framework that is significantly harder for security teams to disrupt through traditional domain or IP blocking.
