Meet 10 finance leaders on Fortune’s Most Powerful Women Asia list
============================================================

DBS Group CEO Tan Su Shan leads Fortune’s third annual Most Powerful Women Asia list, which recognizes 100 influential female leaders across 14 markets in the Asia-Pacific region.

### Key Points
- DBS Group CEO Tan Su Shan retained the No. 1 position, marking her status as the first woman to lead Southeast Asia’s largest bank.
- The 2026 list features 24 newcomers and highlights finance leaders from major global companies including Huawei, Sony Group, and ByteDance.
- Fortune evaluated candidates based on organizational scale, career trajectory, innovation, and broader societal impact across 14 Asia-Pacific markets.
- Notable inclusions feature Sony’s first female CFO, Lin Tao, and Huawei Deputy Chairwoman Meng Wanzhou, who ranked fourth on the list.

### Why it Matters
This ranking highlights the growing influence of women in top-tier financial and executive roles across the Asia-Pacific economic landscape. By showcasing diverse leadership, the list provides a benchmark for corporate performance and gender representation in the region's most significant industries.
