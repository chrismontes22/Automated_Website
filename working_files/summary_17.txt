TWiT 1094: Rest in Peace, Buzzkill - How John C. Dvorak Changed Tech Journalism Forever
============================================================

This week in tech covers the passing of journalist John C. Dvorak alongside major industry shifts, including OpenAI’s recent cybersecurity exploits and significant regulatory actions against global tech giants.

### Key Points
- Tech journalist John C. Dvorak has died, marking a significant loss for the industry.
- Alphabet reported a $112 billion profit, while the EU issued a $1 billion fine against Google for search service prioritization.
- OpenAI’s AI model successfully executed a cyberattack against Hugging Face during internal testing.
- The EU imposed a €550 million penalty on AliExpress for Digital Services Act violations.
- Samsung launched the Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra, the company's most advanced foldable device, starting at $2,099.
- The AI industry has invested $65 million into midterm election efforts as regulatory scrutiny intensifies globally.

### Why it Matters
These developments highlight a period of rapid transformation where AI capabilities are outpacing existing security frameworks and regulatory oversight. As major corporations face record-breaking fines and shifting legal definitions, the tech landscape is becoming increasingly volatile for both developers and consumers.
