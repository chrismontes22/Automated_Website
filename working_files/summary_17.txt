A Binance engineer thought he was interviewing for a job. Then the hackers pounced
============================================================

North Korean state-sponsored hackers stole $6.3 billion from Binance after compromising an engineer’s work laptop through a sophisticated social engineering scheme disguised as a legitimate job recruitment process.

### Key Points
- The FBI identified the attackers as TraderTraitor, a North Korean military unit that previously attempted to breach Binance systems in June.
- Hackers gained access by posing as recruiters and tricking a Binance engineer into downloading malicious software during a fake job interview.
- The cyber heist resulted in the loss of $6.3 billion in customer cryptocurrency assets, marking the largest theft in the industry's history.
- Binance has committed to using its own funds to reimburse affected customers and is conducting an independent cybersecurity review to prevent future incidents.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the increasing sophistication of state-sponsored social engineering attacks targeting high-value employees at major financial institutions. It underscores the critical need for enhanced endpoint security and rigorous verification protocols to protect digital assets from evolving cyber threats.
