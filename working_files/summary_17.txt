How To Disable Gemini On Your Android Phone
============================================================

Android users can revert from the Gemini chatbot to the classic Google Assistant by adjusting the Digital assistants from Google settings within the main Google app menu.

### Key Points
- Navigate to Google app settings, select Google Assistant, and choose Digital assistants from Google to toggle back to the original assistant.
- Gemini is designed for complex generative tasks, while Google Assistant remains optimized for speed and simple, rules-based commands.
- Users can disable Gemini’s data collection by turning off Gemini Apps Activity in the app settings menu.
- The Gemini app can be removed from the app drawer via the phone's main Apps settings menu.
- Samsung Galaxy users can reconfigure the side button to trigger the power-off menu instead of the AI assistant.

### Why it Matters
This transition highlights a growing consumer preference for simple, low-latency tools over resource-heavy generative AI for routine smartphone tasks. Providing users with the ability to revert ensures that individuals can prioritize functional efficiency and privacy over the advanced capabilities of large language models.
