5 things Android should steal from the iPhone Duo and iPhone 18 Pro
============================================================

**Apple has officially entered the foldable market with the launch of the iPhone Duo, alongside the new iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max flagship smartphones.**

### Key Points
- The iPhone Duo features a matte, nano-texture display coating designed to reduce glare, fingerprints, and the visibility of the central screen crease.
- The device includes an under-display camera capable of 1080p video recording at 30 or 60 FPS for improved video calls.
- The iPhone 18 Pro models introduce a variable aperture primary camera, utilizing six laser-cut blades to adjust between f/1.8 and f/4.0.
- Apple introduced iPhone Handoff, a T-Mobile-exclusive service allowing users to share a single phone line across two different iPhones via eSIM.
- The iPhone Duo supports IP68 water resistance, Qi2 wireless charging, and Apple Pencil input, consolidating features previously scattered across various Android foldables.

### Why it Matters
The release of the iPhone Duo represents a significant shift in the foldable market by integrating high-end hardware features that were previously fragmented across competing Android devices. By setting a new standard for display durability and camera versatility, Apple is pressuring Android manufacturers to refine their own foldable offerings to remain competitive.
