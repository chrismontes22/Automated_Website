EXCLUSIVE: Small AI Model Matches GPT-4.1 at Forecasting as Olas Founding Member Says Frontier Models Face ‘Increasing Economic Pressure’
============================================================

Olas has released a new specialized AI model, Olas-Predict-R1-14B, which matches the performance of GPT-4.1 in forecasting real-world events while operating on significantly less computing power.

### Key Points
- Olas-Predict-R1-14B achieved 75.8% accuracy across 2,628 prediction markets, outperforming the 75.4% accuracy of GPT-4.1.
- The model was trained on 214,529 forecasts from 5,116 resolved markets to improve predictive probability and reduce confident errors.
- The system is designed to run on a single GPU, offering a cost-effective alternative to large-scale, closed-source general-purpose AI models.
- Weights for the new model are publicly available on Hugging Face, allowing developers to host the technology independently.
- Thomson Reuters is pursuing a similar strategy by investing $40 million into proprietary, specialized AI models to control costs and data privacy.

### Why it Matters
The emergence of high-performing, specialized models challenges the industry assumption that superior AI results require increasingly massive and expensive general-purpose systems. If smaller models can handle complex workloads efficiently, it may disrupt the current capital-intensive infrastructure boom driven by major cloud providers and hardware manufacturers.
