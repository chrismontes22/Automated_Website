Autonomous site reliability engineering startup Traversal bags $120M in funding at a $1B valuation
============================================================

Autonomous site reliability engineering startup Traversal has secured $120 million in new funding, reaching a $1 billion valuation to expand its AI-driven infrastructure management and incident resolution platform.

### Key Points
- The $120 million funding round was led by Kleiner Perkins, Redpoint Ventures, and Khosla Ventures, with participation from Sequoia Capital, Battery Ventures, and Amex Ventures.
- Traversal utilizes causal machine learning to diagnose and remediate IT infrastructure issues, aiming to reduce the high costs of enterprise downtime.
- The company reports nine-fold revenue growth and a 20-fold increase in monthly active users over the past year.
- Current enterprise customers include PepsiCo, DigitalOcean, Nubank, and Harvey AI.
- CEO Anish Agarwal stated the capital will support accelerated hiring across engineering, research, and go-to-market teams.

### Why it Matters
As AI-generated code increases the velocity of software development, infrastructure engineers face mounting pressure to maintain system stability. Traversal’s specialized approach addresses the limitations of general-purpose models, offering a scalable solution to manage the rising costs and complexity of global enterprise downtime.
