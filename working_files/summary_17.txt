Nothing Is On Fire At Google via @sejournal, @pedrodias
============================================================

Google’s dominance in the AI sector faces scrutiny as internal resource allocation and a focus on protecting search revenue potentially hinder the development of disruptive, autonomous assistant technologies.

### Key Points
- Google has delayed the release of Gemini 3.5 Pro, missing its original June target while prioritizing cloud revenue and existing search-based AI features.
- Internal competition for limited compute resources between Google Cloud and the Gemini development team has reportedly hampered progress in key areas like coding.
- Despite strong consumer adoption of Gemini, enterprise data shows competitors like Anthropic and OpenAI currently lead in professional API spending and coding tasks.
- High-profile talent departures, including co-lead Noam Shazeer, suggest a shift in researcher sentiment toward companies where AI innovation is the primary business focus.
- Google’s search revenue grew 17% in Q2, reinforcing a corporate strategy that favors incremental improvements over radical disruption of its core advertising business.

### Why it Matters
Google’s current strategy mirrors Microsoft’s historical approach of prioritizing existing, profitable business lines over the development of potentially cannibalistic, next-generation technologies. This cautious pace may leave the company vulnerable to competitors who are willing to build autonomous agents that bypass traditional search interfaces entirely.
