Security, Safety & Smarter WiFi: Comcast Launches Xfinity Shield With Built-In Home Protection
============================================================

Comcast has launched Xfinity Shield, a new network-level security platform that provides automated cybersecurity, home motion detection, and family management tools for Xfinity Internet customers nationwide.

### Key Points
- Xfinity Shield provides network-level protection against malware, phishing, and hacking for all connected devices without requiring individual software installations.
- The platform includes WiFi Motion technology to detect unexpected movement within the home without the use of cameras or image recording.
- Family Settings allow users to manage screen time, set device limits, and pause internet access through individual profiles in the Xfinity app.
- The service is available at no additional cost to customers with eligible Advanced Xfinity Gateways.
- An optional Shield Select upgrade is available for $15 per month, adding indoor cameras, door sensors, and 24/7 urgent response services.

### Why it Matters
This platform shifts the responsibility of cybersecurity from individual device management to the home network infrastructure, addressing the security risks of households with dozens of connected devices. By integrating physical monitoring and parental controls, Comcast is positioning its gateway as a centralized hub for comprehensive home safety and digital management.
