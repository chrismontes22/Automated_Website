Sportsbooks Face New York Scrutiny Over AI and At-Risk Bettors
============================================================

The New York State Gaming Commission has advanced new proposals to restrict how mobile sportsbooks use artificial intelligence for personalized marketing and to mandate interventions for at-risk bettors.

### Key Points
- The proposed rules would prohibit sportsbooks from using AI to tailor promotions, odds, or betting suggestions to individual customers.
- Operators would be required to identify customers exhibiting signs of problem gambling and assign personnel to engage them directly.
- Nine licensed mobile operators, including FanDuel, DraftKings, and BetMGM, would be subject to these new regulatory standards.
- Commission Chairman Brian O’Dwyer urged operators to share data to prevent at-risk gamblers from simply switching to different betting platforms.
- The measures align with Governor Kathy Hochul’s 2026 State of the State program, which prioritizes addressing gambling addiction and marketing practices.

### Why it Matters
These proposals represent a significant shift in how regulators oversee the intersection of data-driven marketing and consumer protection in the sports betting industry. By limiting algorithmic personalization, New York aims to curb aggressive engagement tactics that may exacerbate gambling addiction among vulnerable users.
