AI and the Brand You Didn't Build
============================================================

Artificial intelligence is fundamentally changing brand management by creating an inferred identity that often diverges from the deliberate image crafted by individuals, corporations, and professional organizations.

### Key Points
- The "inference gap" represents the distance between a brand's intended identity and the probabilistic portrait constructed by large language models.
- AI synthesizes diverse data points, including journalism, social commentary, and historical records, to form conclusions that may contradict a brand's official messaging.
- The INFER framework—Identity, Narrative, Fidelity, Evidence, and Resilience—provides a structured method for organizations to audit and manage their AI-generated reputations.
- Unlike traditional search engine optimization, generative engine optimization focuses on how AI interprets and summarizes information rather than simply surfacing links.
- The shift toward AI-generated answers means that machines are increasingly acting as the primary interpreters of professional and corporate reputations for the public.

### Why it Matters
As AI becomes the primary interface for information retrieval, the gap between an expressed brand and an inferred brand can significantly impact public perception and professional credibility. Organizations must now actively manage their digital footprint to ensure that machine-generated summaries accurately reflect their intended identity rather than outdated or incomplete data.
