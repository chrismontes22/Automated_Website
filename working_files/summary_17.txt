Chuwi Just Put a Real Performance Chip in a $469 Tote-Bag Laptop
============================================================

The Chuwi MiniBook X U300 upgrades the compact laptop category by integrating a 13th-generation Intel Raptor Lake processor into a portable, 360-degree convertible chassis priced at $469.

### Key Points
- The device features an Intel U300 processor with five cores, offering improved multitasking performance over previous N100 and N150-based models.
- It includes a 10.51-inch WUXGA touchscreen display with a 16:10 aspect ratio and 100% sRGB color coverage.
- Hardware specifications include 16GB of LPDDR4X RAM, a 512GB PCIe SSD, and an M.2 slot for future storage upgrades.
- The aluminum unibody design weighs 920 grams and supports four usage modes: laptop, tablet, tent, and stand.
- The laptop comes pre-installed with Windows 11 Pro, providing professional features like BitLocker encryption and remote desktop access.
- Connectivity options include two full-featured USB-C ports, Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.2, and a 3.5mm audio jack.

### Why it Matters
This release signals a shift for mini laptops from underpowered novelties to viable tools for professionals who require portability without sacrificing performance. By including Windows 11 Pro and a more capable processor, Chuwi is positioning this device as a legitimate, secure option for mobile workers and remote device management.
