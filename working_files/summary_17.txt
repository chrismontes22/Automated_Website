AI could help fossil fuel companies create more emissions
============================================================

New research published in the journal npj Climate Action suggests that AI-driven productivity gains in the fossil fuel industry could significantly increase global greenhouse gas emissions.

### Key Points
- AI-enabled fossil fuel production could increase global energy emissions by 1.2 to 4.8 percent annually.
- The projected emissions increase is comparable to the total annual output of countries like Mexico or Russia.
- Researchers argue these "enabled emissions" from AI tools outweigh the climate benefits of AI-driven clean energy advancements.
- Former Microsoft sustainability employees Will and Holly Alpine authored the study after leaving the company over its fossil fuel partnerships.
- The study highlights a "self-reinforcing" cycle where tech companies provide AI tools to oil firms while simultaneously relying on them for data center power.

### Why it Matters
This research challenges the narrative that AI will be a net positive for the climate by highlighting the overlooked environmental impact of industrial efficiency tools. It suggests that current corporate sustainability metrics fail to account for the massive emissions generated when technology accelerates fossil fuel extraction.
