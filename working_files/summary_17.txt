Elon Musk's power now extends far beyond Tesla, and Ukraine shows why
============================================================

Elon Musk has emerged as a uniquely powerful non-governmental actor, wielding significant influence over global communications, military operations, and space access through his leadership of Tesla and SpaceX.

### Key Points
- SpaceX currently manages approximately 90% of all mass launched into Earth's orbit, establishing Musk as a dominant force in global space infrastructure.
- The Pentagon serves as the primary customer for Starshield, a military-grade satellite communications network developed by SpaceX for national security applications.
- Recent disruptions to Starlink services in Ukraine highlighted the growing reliance of sovereign nations on private-sector technology for critical battlefield communications and drone operations.
- Countries including the Philippines, Argentina, and Indonesia utilize Starlink technology to support national communications and emergency service infrastructure.
- Experts from the Harvard Kennedy School and the Brookings Institution suggest Musk’s resources and control over essential technology exceed those of many sovereign governments.

### Why it Matters
The concentration of such critical infrastructure under a single private individual creates unprecedented challenges for international relations and national security. This shift forces governments to navigate a new reality where private corporate decisions can directly impact the success of military operations and global connectivity.
