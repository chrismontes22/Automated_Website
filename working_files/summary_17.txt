Windows has been quietly wearing out your SSD, and the fix takes one setting
============================================================

Windows 11 users can extend the lifespan of their NVMe SSDs and reclaim significant storage space by disabling the Fast Startup feature, which performs unnecessary daily drive writes.

### Key Points
- Fast Startup creates a large hiberfil.sys file on the boot drive every time a computer shuts down, consuming gigabytes of storage capacity.
- Frequent writing to the hiberfil.sys file contributes to cumulative wear on SSDs, which is increasingly concerning as replacement costs for 1TB drives have doubled.
- Users can disable Fast Startup via the Control Panel or permanently remove the hiberfil.sys file by running the "powercfg /h off" command in an Administrator Terminal.
- Disabling hibernation removes a safety net for laptop users, as the system will no longer save the current session if the battery completely depletes.
- SSDs with less free space experience higher write amplification, making the removal of large, unnecessary system files beneficial for drive health and performance.

### Why it Matters
Rising NAND flash prices have made SSD longevity a significant financial concern for PC owners looking to maximize their hardware's lifespan. By disabling unnecessary background processes like Fast Startup, users can reduce avoidable wear and reclaim valuable storage space on their primary drives.
