Cyberattacks are the ultimate business continuity tests that nearly half of organizations fail
============================================================

Modern cyber resilience now requires organizations to maintain operational continuity during an active attack, as research shows 47% of CISOs report operational shutdowns following a security breach.

### Key Points
- Research indicates 47% of CISOs experienced operational shutdowns after a cyber incident, surpassing data loss or revenue impact.
- Approximately 73% of security leaders lack confidence in their organization's ability to effectively manage a major cyber incident.
- AI-driven threats are accelerating reconnaissance and vulnerability identification, allowing attackers to execute large-scale, multi-path campaigns with fewer resources.
- Coordination remains a significant hurdle, with 90% of organizations struggling to align stakeholders and 75% citing legal and communication delays as recovery inhibitors.
- Experts recommend establishing a "Minimal Viable Business" objective to prioritize critical functions and streamline decision-making during active crises.

### Why it Matters
Cyber resilience has evolved from a technical recovery task into a fundamental business continuity discipline that directly impacts market share and customer trust. Organizations that fail to integrate security across all departments risk prolonged operational paralysis and financial instability in an era of increasingly automated cyber threats.
