Predictions 2027: Humans Reclaim Content Creation; Machines Dominate Content Consumption
============================================================

Forrester’s 2027 predictions report highlights a shift toward human-led content creation to combat AI-driven misinformation and meet growing consumer demands for authenticity, transparency, and verifiable brand expertise.

### Key Points
- Half of all brands will prioritize human-generated content by 2027 to build trust and provide verifiable expertise for AI-powered search engines.
- The effective lifespan of influence-based content will shrink to 30 days, forcing brands to transition from campaign-centric planning to continuous content operations.
- Only 18% of US online adults currently trust AI-provided information, driving a need for brands to prove content provenance and human accountability.
- Agentic AI adoption will trigger the consolidation of one-third of existing content point solutions as companies integrate agents into their current technology stacks.
- Nearly half of marketing executives report that boards are actively questioning how their brand is represented within generative AI tools and search results.

### Why it Matters
This shift signals a fundamental change in digital strategy where brands must balance the efficiency of machine-led distribution with the necessity of human-verified authority. Companies that fail to prioritize authentic, attributable content risk losing consumer trust and relevance in an increasingly automated information ecosystem.
