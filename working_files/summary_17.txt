iPhone Duo 2 Prototypes Include Under-Display Face ID
============================================================

Apple is reportedly testing prototypes of its second-generation iPhone Duo that incorporate under-display Face ID technology to replace the current side-mounted fingerprint sensor for improved biometric authentication.

### Key Points
- Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman reports that Apple is testing under-display Face ID hardware for a potential future iPhone Duo model.
- The current first-generation iPhone Duo utilizes a side-button fingerprint sensor due to space constraints within its thin chassis.
- Apple is also considering the addition of a dedicated Action button to future iterations of the foldable device.
- The initial iPhone Duo model is scheduled for release tomorrow with a starting retail price of $1,999.
- Future upgrades for the product line may eventually include advanced telephoto camera systems alongside improved facial recognition technology.

### Why it Matters
Integrating Face ID into a foldable form factor would align the iPhone Duo with the premium authentication standards found in Apple's flagship smartphone lineup. This development signals Apple's commitment to refining its foldable hardware to match the feature parity and user experience of its traditional devices.
