AI has made borrowing more expensive – an expert explains how bond markets are changing
============================================================

Global bond markets are experiencing increased instability as rising government debt and intense competition for capital from the AI sector drive borrowing costs to multi-decade highs.

### Key Points
- US ten-year government bond yields have reached 5%, marking the highest level observed in nearly 20 years.
- Borrowing costs in the UK and France have climbed to their highest points since the 2008 global financial crisis.
- Massive corporate investment in AI infrastructure is creating a supply-demand imbalance, forcing governments to compete for limited capital.
- US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent announced a US$6 billion bond buyback program to stabilize costs, though analysts view this as insufficient against US$40 trillion in total debt.
- Rising interest payments are increasingly diverting public funds away from essential services like healthcare and education.

### Why it Matters
Higher bond yields increase the cost of servicing national debt, which forces governments to choose between raising taxes, cutting public spending, or risking fiscal instability. This shift signals an end to the era of cheap government borrowing as private sector demand for capital continues to grow.
