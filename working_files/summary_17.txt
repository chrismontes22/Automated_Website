AI isn’t only for enterprises; it’s time for SMBs to cash in
============================================================

Small and medium-sized businesses are struggling to realize returns on artificial intelligence investments, often hindered by the misconception that the technology is primarily designed for large global enterprises.

### Key Points
- Research from Deloitte indicates that small businesses reaching full AI integration can experience profitability increases of up to 111%.
- An OECD survey found that 57% of non-adopting small businesses believe AI is unsuitable for their specific type of work.
- Managed Service Providers are evolving into Managed Intelligence Providers to help small firms implement AI governance and identify high-value use cases.
- Data shows 90% of European small businesses using AI report productivity improvements, while 75% note positive changes in customer interactions.
- Pax8 research reveals that 84% of small businesses would trust an outside technology advisor to guide their AI implementation strategy.

### Why it Matters
Overcoming the "applicability illusion" is essential for small businesses to remain competitive as AI shifts from experimental projects to a core operational requirement. By leveraging external expertise, smaller firms can bypass the need for internal research departments and achieve measurable bottom-line growth through targeted automation.
