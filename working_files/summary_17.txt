Cloud AI Means Someone Else Has Your Data, MINISFORUM Fixes That
============================================================

MINISFORUM has launched the N5 MAX-P495 NAS and MS-S1 MAX-P495 workstation, a compact hardware pairing designed to run high-performance AI models and data storage locally at the edge.

### Key Points
- The N5 MAX-P495 NAS supports up to 200TB of local storage for managing large datasets, RAG knowledge bases, and persistent AI agent workloads.
- The MS-S1 MAX-P495 workstation provides high-speed compute for real-time inference, generative AI, and computer vision tasks in a compact, ventilated tower.
- The combined system delivers up to 131 TOPS of AI compute performance, 192GB of memory at 8533 MT/s, and 160GB of graphics memory.
- Both devices are designed to function as a private, centralized backend for AI development, eliminating the need for third-party cloud server subscriptions.
- The hardware supports specialized AI tools including OpenClaw and Hermes, enabling continuous operation for small businesses and home labs.

### Why it Matters
This hardware pairing offers a viable alternative to cloud-based AI by allowing users to maintain full data privacy and control over their computational workflows. By bringing enterprise-grade storage and processing power to the desktop, it enables small teams to deploy sophisticated AI agents and real-time applications without relying on external infrastructure.
