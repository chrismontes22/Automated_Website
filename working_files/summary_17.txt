Hackers hit the FBI — ShinyHunters say they have stolen 2TB of employee data, but the attack isn't looking for money, just an apology
============================================================

The hacking group ShinyHunters defaced the FBI’s jobs website, claiming they exploited an Oracle PeopleSoft zero-day vulnerability to steal two terabytes of sensitive employee and applicant data.

### Key Points
- ShinyHunters claims the breach was a retaliatory act against an FBI public service announcement from May that accused the group of using harassment and swatting tactics.
- The hackers allege they accessed 2TB of data, including personal information of FBI employees, spouses, and applicants from HR, MedLink, and Criminal Justice Information Services systems.
- Security experts warn that the alleged PeopleSoft zero-day exploit is highly valuable and poses a significant risk to any organization currently running the software.
- The FBI has reclaimed its website, which is currently offline for maintenance, while an investigation into the validity of the breach claims remains ongoing.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the severe risks posed by zero-day vulnerabilities in widely used enterprise software like Oracle PeopleSoft. If confirmed, the theft of sensitive government personnel data could have long-term national security implications and demonstrates a shift in tactics for extortion groups moving beyond simple financial gain.
