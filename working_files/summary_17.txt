500 Reasons to Buy Einride Stock Today
============================================================

Swedish freight-technology company Einride plans to deploy 500 Tesla Semi trucks across North America over the next two years to support major clients including Amazon.

### Key Points
- The 24-month rollout will span major freight corridors in California, Texas, New Jersey, Illinois, and Georgia.
- Einride’s Saga AI platform will manage the fleet, optimizing charging, routing, and energy costs for the new electric vehicles.
- H1 2026 revenue grew 26% year-over-year to €27 million, with management projecting H2 growth to accelerate between 60% and 73%.
- Despite revenue growth, the company reported a negative adjusted EBITDA of €34.6 million as it continues heavy investment in infrastructure and R&D.
- Einride aims to reach cash-flow breakeven by the second half of 2028, requiring a fleet size of approximately 1,500 to 2,000 vehicles.

### Why it Matters
This large-scale deployment represents a critical test for Einride’s ability to convert its $800 million potential revenue pipeline into tangible, long-term growth. Successfully integrating the Tesla Semi fleet could validate the company's business model and provide a necessary catalyst for its stock following a significant post-IPO decline.
