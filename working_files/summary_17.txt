I've used Bitwarden for years and didn't know about these 6 hidden settings that boost your security
============================================================

Bitwarden users can enhance their account security by auditing specific settings, including master password re-prompts, clipboard behavior, and advanced encryption standards like Argon2id for better vault protection.

### Key Points
- Enable "Master password re-prompt" on sensitive items to require authentication before autofilling specific credentials.
- Disable "Copy TOTP automatically" in the Autofill settings to prevent authenticator codes from being stored on the system clipboard.
- Audit "Autofill on page load" to ensure it is disabled, preventing potential exploitation by compromised or untrusted websites.
- Adjust "Default URI match detection" to "Host" or "Exact" to restrict where credentials are offered, rather than relying on broad base-domain matching.
- Use the "Blocked domains" feature to prevent Bitwarden from offering autofill, passkey prompts, or save prompts on specific websites.
- Switch the Key Derivation Function (KDF) to Argon2id in web vault settings to make offline brute-force attacks significantly more computationally expensive.

### Why it Matters
Regularly auditing password manager configurations ensures that default settings align with a user's specific security requirements and risk tolerance. These adjustments provide additional layers of defense against unauthorized access and potential data exposure without sacrificing the core functionality of the service.
