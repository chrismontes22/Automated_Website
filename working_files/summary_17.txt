Stock Indices Fall from Early Highs and Settle Mixed
============================================================

US stock indices finished mixed on Wednesday as the Dow Jones Industrial Average reached a record high, while the S&P 500 and Nasdaq 100 retreated from recent peaks.

### Key Points
- The Dow Jones Industrial Average rose +0.49%, while the S&P 500 fell -0.17% and the Nasdaq 100 dropped -0.83%.
- Advanced Micro Devices shares declined -7% following an underwhelming third-quarter sales forecast, dragging down other major chipmakers.
- July ADP employment data showed a gain of 44,000 jobs, falling short of the 65,000 expected by analysts.
- Federal Reserve officials Jeff Schmid and Neel Kashkari signaled a need for tighter monetary policy to combat persistent inflation.
- Mining stocks surged as gold, silver, and copper prices reached multi-month highs, with companies like Anglogold Ashanti gaining over +8%.

### Why it Matters
The market's mixed performance reflects a tug-of-war between strong corporate earnings and growing concerns over cooling economic data and hawkish Federal Reserve rhetoric. Investors are closely monitoring these signals to gauge the likelihood of future interest rate hikes as the economy navigates persistent service price pressures.
