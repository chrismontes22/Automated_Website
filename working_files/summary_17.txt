Attackers Heat French Supermarket Fridges Up to 50°C by Hijacking Danfoss Controllers
============================================================

Cyberattackers gained remote control of refrigeration systems across hundreds of French supermarkets on October 11, forcing retailers to discard food after disabling temperature controls on Danfoss units.

### Key Points
- Major retailers including Intermarché, Carrefour, Coopérative U, Monoprix, and E.Leclerc reported unauthorized access to their refrigeration management systems.
- The breach targeted Danfoss AK-SM and AK-SC series controllers, many of which are older models that no longer receive security updates.
- France’s cybersecurity agency, ANSSI, and the response team CERT-FR issued urgent warnings to disconnect these controllers from the internet or restrict access.
- The Paris prosecutor’s office has launched a formal cybercrime investigation into the incident, which reportedly caused millions of euros in food waste.
- While the exact entry method remains under investigation, security experts noted that thousands of these management interfaces were previously found exposed to the public internet.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the significant security risks associated with connecting legacy industrial control systems to the internet without proper network segmentation or firewall protection. The resulting food spoilage and financial losses demonstrate how vulnerabilities in critical infrastructure can cause immediate, large-scale disruption to essential supply chains.
