Bad news — paying a ransomware demand might cause hackers to come back and ask for more
============================================================

The Proofpoint 2026 AI-Era Ransomware Report reveals that 54% of organizations paid ransoms to attackers, despite data showing that payments often fail to prevent repeat extortion or data loss.

### Key Points
- The Proofpoint report surveyed 1,000 security professionals across 12 global markets regarding ransomware trends.
- Data indicates 37% of victims who paid a ransom faced subsequent extortion demands from the same attackers.
- Approximately 2% of organizations paid the requested ransom but failed to regain access to their encrypted files.
- Cybersecurity experts recommend mitigating risks through phishing awareness training, offline data backups, and AI-powered endpoint protection.

### Why it Matters
Paying ransomware demands frequently fails to resolve security incidents and often incentivizes criminals to target the same organization again. This trend highlights the critical need for robust, proactive defense strategies rather than relying on ransom payments to restore business operations.
