Live updates: Bitcoin whipsaws between $81,000 and $84,000 as altcoins slide
============================================================

Federal Reserve Bank of New York President John Williams signaled that one final interest rate hike may be appropriate this year, with no further increases expected in 2027.

### Key Points
- John Williams stated that one additional rate hike is likely necessary to address ongoing economic conditions.
- The Federal Reserve aims to guide inflation back toward its long-term target of 2% by next year.
- Williams noted that while artificial intelligence may influence inflation, the broader U.S. economy is not currently overheating.
- Market expectations currently diverge from Williams' outlook, as investors have priced in 50 basis points of rate hikes for 2027.
- Following the comments, the U.S. two-year Treasury yield edged down to 4.797% while Bitcoin prices dipped below $83,000.

### Why it Matters
These comments from a key Federal Reserve official suggest a more dovish monetary policy stance than current market projections anticipate. This potential shift in interest rate strategy could influence investor sentiment and asset valuations across both traditional stock markets and digital currencies.
