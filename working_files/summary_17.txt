JPMorgan Chase, Accenture and others are teaming up on a new venture to standardize how AI token use is measured
============================================================

The Linux Foundation has launched the Tokenomics Foundation, a new industry group featuring 30 founding members dedicated to standardizing AI token measurement and connecting consumption to business value.

### Key Points
- Founding members include major corporations such as Accenture, IBM, JPMorgan Chase, SAP, Oracle, and ServiceNow.
- The foundation aims to provide vendor-neutral frameworks to help enterprises manage rising AI costs and quantify return on investment.
- Executive director J.R. Storment noted that the group will release new frameworks and value metric definitions on a near-monthly basis.
- Initial resources include the "Big-T Notation" framework, with further projects scheduled for presentation at the Tokenomicon event in Amsterdam this September.
- The organization plans to develop comprehensive education and certification tracks to improve AI literacy across corporate finance and technical teams.

### Why it Matters
As AI token costs become a significant line item in enterprise budgets, companies are struggling to align technical consumption with measurable business outcomes. This foundation provides a standardized, vendor-agnostic approach that allows CFOs and technical leaders to move beyond flat usage limits toward value-based budgeting.
