VRChat Devs Spark Outrage After Refusing to Include AI Label Despite 95% of Fans Asking for It
============================================================

VRChat developers have decided against implementing AI-generated content labels despite a recent survey revealing that 95% of the platform's user base requested such filtering features.

### Key Points
- VRChat officials confirmed on October 28 that they will not introduce AI-generated content labels or filters.
- A company survey indicated that 95% of respondents supported the implementation of some form of AI labeling.
- Developers cited a lack of consensus, noting that survey participants provided 3,141 different suggestions for how to categorize AI content.
- The platform expressed concerns that labeling could lead to user harassment and "sleuthing" behavior toward creators.
- Community members have publicly criticized the decision on social media, questioning the purpose of the survey if the results are not utilized.

### Why it Matters
This decision highlights the growing tension between platform operators and users regarding the transparency and regulation of generative AI tools. The move risks alienating a significant portion of the community and raises questions about how social platforms will balance creator autonomy with user demands for content moderation.
