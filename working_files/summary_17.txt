#StopRansomware: SafePay Ransomware
============================================================

SafePay ransomware has targeted over 600 businesses and critical infrastructure organizations globally since August 2024, utilizing credential theft, phishing, and system vulnerabilities to encrypt sensitive data for ransom.

### Key Points
- SafePay actors gain initial network access by exploiting VPN vulnerabilities, brute-forcing passwords, and using phished account profiles.
- The group employs "living off the land" techniques, using legitimate administrative tools like PsExec and WinRM to move laterally and disable security software.
- Data is exfiltrated using common utilities such as RClone, FileZilla, and cloud services like OneDrive before files are encrypted with a hybrid RSA/ChaCha20 scheme.
- Ransom demands are typically issued in cryptocurrency, with threats to publish stolen data on a dedicated leak site if payments are not met.
- Authoring agencies, including the FBI and CISA, have identified specific indicators of compromise, including malicious IP addresses and custom PowerShell scripts like ShareFinder.ps1.

### Why it Matters
This advisory highlights a significant threat to global critical infrastructure, emphasizing the need for robust identity management and network segmentation to prevent widespread operational disruption. Organizations must prioritize patching known vulnerabilities and maintaining immutable, offline backups to mitigate the severe financial and data-loss risks posed by this active ransomware group.
