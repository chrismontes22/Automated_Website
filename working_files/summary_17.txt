Strategy Climbs 3%, BitMine Surges 7%, SharpLink Rises 5% as Crypto Stocks Bounce Back Toward $65K Bitcoin
============================================================

Strategy, BitMine Immersion Technologies, and SharpLink Gaming shares rose Monday as a broader crypto market relief rally followed a pause in geopolitical tensions and increased Bitcoin and Ethereum prices.

### Key Points
- Strategy shares climbed 3% to $94.43, while BitMine Immersion Technologies and SharpLink Gaming rose 7% and 5% respectively.
- Bitcoin traded near $64,986, supported by a market-wide shift toward risk-on assets following a reported pause in Iran strikes.
- Strategy confirmed a five-week pause in Bitcoin purchases, maintaining its holdings at 843,775 BTC with $3.75 billion in cash reserves.
- BitMine reported fiscal 2025 net income of $328.16 million, while SharpLink saw institutional ownership increase from 6% to 46%.
- Despite Monday's gains, all three companies remain in significant one-year drawdowns ranging from 62% to 78%.

### Why it Matters
These stocks function as leveraged proxies for cryptocurrency prices, meaning their performance is highly sensitive to macro-economic shifts and volatility in Bitcoin and Ethereum. While the current rally offers a tactical bounce, investors remain cautious due to ongoing dilution concerns and the potential for continued mark-to-market earnings pressure.
