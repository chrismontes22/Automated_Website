Brave1 and the open source future of war
============================================================

Ukraine’s Brave1 defense innovation engine is revolutionizing military procurement by using a decentralized, software-first model to rapidly deploy and update battlefield technology through an open-source ecosystem.

### Key Points
- Brave1 connects over 3,200 companies and 4,500 products, bypassing traditional bureaucratic delays to deliver innovation directly to the front line.
- The platform utilizes a "Brave1 Market" app store model, allowing commanders to procure vetted equipment using digital ePoints earned through combat success.
- Developers leverage a secure "Dataroom" containing over 500,000 hours of battlefield footage to train AI models for real-world targeting and navigation.
- The ecosystem enables 72-hour software update cycles, allowing engineers to patch drone and electronic warfare systems in response to evolving battlefield threats.
- International partnerships, including Brave France and Brave Germany, have unlocked over €100 million in joint funding to scale co-developed defense technologies.

### Why it Matters
This shift toward software-defined warfare forces a fundamental change in global defense economics by prioritizing low-cost, adaptable technology over expensive, long-term hardware projects. By proving that decentralized, open-source development can outperform traditional procurement, Brave1 provides a new blueprint for how enterprise and defense sectors must evolve to maintain relevance in rapidly changing environments.
