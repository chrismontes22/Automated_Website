What Earnings Season Reveals About Travel Right Now
============================================================

**Fora’s $1 billion valuation and new AI-driven travel tools from Amazon and IHG highlight a shifting industry landscape where conversational technology is reshaping how consumers book and plan travel.**

### Key Points
- Fora achieved a $1 billion valuation after raising $60 million to expand its AI-powered platform for human travel advisors.
- Amazon launched a conversational booking pilot featuring partners including Priceline, Virgin Atlantic, Lyft, and Flixbus.
- IHG introduced a beta version of conversational AI search on its website and mobile app to move beyond traditional keyword-based booking.
- Hilton reported a strong quarter and announced a reduction in franchise fees for hotels that maintain high guest experience scores.
- JetBlue is pursuing a major turnaround plan involving new fare structures, route optimization, and terminal shifts to address ongoing financial losses.
- Royal Caribbean trimmed its growth forecast, citing rising fuel costs linked to geopolitical tensions in the Middle East.

### Why it Matters
The travel industry is moving away from a "single front door" model dominated by Google search, as companies experiment with AI to capture customer intent through diverse digital and human-led channels. While these technologies aim to simplify complex booking journeys, the long-term success of these tools remains uncertain as travel providers balance innovation with the persistent challenge of consumer decision paralysis.
