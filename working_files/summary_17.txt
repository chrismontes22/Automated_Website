Feds probe 'mention markets.' And, lawmakers call for investigation into USS Lincoln
============================================================

Federal regulators are investigating prediction market site Kalshi over "mention markets" that allow users to wager on specific words spoken by public figures during live events.

### Key Points
- The Commodity Futures Trading Commission is reviewing Kalshi's "mention markets" following reports of individuals profiting from insider knowledge of political speeches.
- Kalshi has removed specific mention-based wagers from its platform while the federal inquiry remains ongoing.
- The site continues to offer betting options related to general statements made during political events, earnings calls, and live television broadcasts.
- This probe follows revelations that a teleprompter operator for President Trump previously profited from betting on the president's exact word choices.

### Why it Matters
The investigation highlights growing regulatory scrutiny over the intersection of prediction markets and political influence. As these platforms gain popularity, federal agencies are increasingly concerned about the potential for market manipulation and the ethical implications of wagering on public discourse.
