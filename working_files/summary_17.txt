Reolink Home Hub 2 On-Device AI, 16MP Camera Support, and Subscription-Free Security Storage
============================================================

Reolink has launched the Home Hub 2, a centralized security management device featuring on-device AI video search and expandable local storage for up to eight compatible cameras.

### Key Points
- The Home Hub 2 supports up to eight Reolink cameras, including Wi-Fi and Power over Ethernet (PoE) models, with resolutions up to 16 megapixels.
- Local AI Video Search allows users to find specific footage using keywords without requiring a cloud subscription.
- Storage options include a SATA slot for 2.5-inch HDDs or SSDs and a microSD card slot supporting up to 1TB.
- The device features Cross-Camera Tracking to follow objects across multiple feeds and integrates with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant.
- The hub maintains local recording capabilities during internet outages and offers alert integrations for platforms like Discord, Telegram, and Slack.
- The product is currently available in the United States for $199.99.

### Why it Matters
This device offers a subscription-free alternative for homeowners who prioritize data privacy and local control over cloud-based surveillance services. By centralizing AI processing and storage, it provides a cost-effective way to manage complex multi-camera security systems without recurring monthly fees.
