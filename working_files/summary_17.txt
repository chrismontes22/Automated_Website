Photoshop's Remove Tool Doesn't Touch Your Generative Credits
============================================================

Adobe Photoshop users can optimize their monthly generative credit usage by leveraging non-AI tools and specific settings that perform complex editing tasks without consuming the platform's limited resources.

### Key Points
- Adobe’s generative credit system applies to features like Generative Fill, Generative Expand, and Generate Image, while standard tools remain free to use.
- The Remove tool can function without consuming credits if configured for device processing or specific layer settings.
- Users can adjust image properties like width, height, and opacity by clicking and dragging on labels to activate hidden sliders.
- The targeted adjustment hand tool allows for intuitive editing of Curves and Hue/Saturation by dragging directly on specific areas of a photo.
- Switching Select Subject and Remove Background to cloud processing improves edge quality for complex subjects like hair without necessarily impacting credit limits.
- Content-Aware Fill remains a cost-free alternative for tasks like filling empty corners during cropping or removing objects from cluttered backgrounds.

### Why it Matters
Understanding which Photoshop features consume generative credits allows creative professionals to manage their monthly allotments more effectively. By mastering built-in, non-AI workflows, users can maintain high-quality editing standards while reserving paid credits for only the most complex generative tasks.
