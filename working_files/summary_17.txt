I drop-tested the best Pixel cases (and drove over the toughest) to see what "military grade" really means
============================================================

A tech reviewer tested the durability of four popular Google Pixel 8 cases by subjecting them to rigorous drop tests, extreme heights, and even a vehicle impact.

### Key Points
- The test evaluated the Poetic Revolution, Dbrand Grip Case, Quad Lock MAG Case, and BURGA Tough Case.
- All four cases successfully protected the Pixel 8 during 5-foot drops onto hard tiled surfaces.
- The Poetic Revolution case survived a 20-foot drop from a second-story building with the phone remaining fully functional.
- Running over the Poetic Revolution case with a seven-seat MPV resulted in a cracked glass back, though the device remained operational.
- Testing was conducted in 45°C (113°F) heat to simulate extreme environmental conditions.

### Why it Matters
These results validate that reputable phone case brands provide genuine protection that exceeds standard marketing claims. Consumers can rely on these products to safeguard expensive hardware against significant accidental damage in real-world scenarios.
