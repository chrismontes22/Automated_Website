Search Console AI Reports Go Global, Mueller On Recovery – SEO Pulse via @sejournal, @MattGSouthern
============================================================

Google has expanded its Search Console AI reports to all websites worldwide, while search advocate John Mueller clarified best practices regarding ranking recovery, markdown usage, and sitemap management.

### Key Points
- Google’s Search Console AI reports and AI opt-out controls reached global availability for all websites on August 31.
- John Mueller stated that ranking recovery following broad search updates can take several months to manifest after technical fixes are implemented.
- Mueller noted that major AI bots do not currently request markdown, suggesting that only SEO tools are actively seeking this format.
- Using cache-busting parameters like timestamps in sitemap URLs is discouraged, as it creates unstable canonical signals for search engines.
- Site owners are advised to use the existing "lastmod" element in sitemaps to communicate content freshness rather than manipulating URL structures.

### Why it Matters
These updates provide site owners with greater visibility into how their content appears within Google’s generative AI features. By emphasizing standard protocols over technical workarounds, Google is signaling that long-term SEO success relies on stable site architecture rather than frequent, reactive adjustments.
