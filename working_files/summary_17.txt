SpaceX and Nvidia are taking their relationship exclusive — here's what Musk said about their new status
============================================================

SpaceX CEO Elon Musk announced that the company will exclusively use Nvidia graphics processing units for its AI infrastructure, citing the superior performance of the Vera Rubin architecture.

### Key Points
- SpaceX will rely solely on Nvidia hardware for AI model training and advanced computing, bypassing competitors like Intel, AMD, and Broadcom.
- The company reported a 92% year-over-year revenue increase to $7.8 billion in the second quarter, driven by Starlink and AI infrastructure growth.
- Despite strong revenue, SpaceX posted a $541 million quarterly loss, leading to a 7% decline in share price following the earnings call.
- Musk aims to reach a $100 billion annual revenue run rate by the end of the year, though investors remain concerned about high capital expenditures.

### Why it Matters
This exclusive partnership signals a significant consolidation of AI supply chains, prioritizing performance over the risk-mitigation strategies typically used by large tech firms. The move highlights the critical role of Nvidia’s hardware in scaling high-performance computing for space and satellite operations.
