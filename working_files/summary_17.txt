Apple trained its own AI model for China, and handed the brain to Alibaba
============================================================

Apple has secured regulatory approval to launch a proprietary AI model in China, utilizing Alibaba’s Qwen technology and Baidu search to power Apple Intelligence across its device ecosystem.

### Key Points
- Apple is the first foreign company authorized by Beijing to offer a proprietary generative AI model within mainland China.
- The system integrates Alibaba’s Qwen model for core assistant functions and Baidu’s technology for search capabilities.
- The Cyberspace Administration of China officially registered the service in July 2026 following a lengthy regulatory review process.
- Apple Intelligence features will roll out to iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Vision Pro users in China in the coming months.
- Alibaba shares rose approximately 4% following the regulatory approval, reflecting investor confidence in the partnership's commercial potential.

### Why it Matters
This partnership marks a significant strategic shift for Apple, which typically prioritizes total control over its hardware and software stack. By outsourcing core AI functions to local firms, Apple demonstrates the concessions necessary for Western technology companies to maintain market access within China’s strictly regulated digital environment.
