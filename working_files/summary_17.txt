Comcast Is Turning Millions of Its Routers Into Motion Detectors
============================================================

Comcast is rolling out a new Wi-Fi motion sensing feature for millions of Xfinity gateways, enabling users to monitor home activity without installing additional hardware or dedicated sensors.

### Key Points
- The feature utilizes signal disruptions between Xfinity gateways and connected devices to detect movement within a home.
- Users can manage the service through the Xfinity app using three distinct monitoring modes: Home Watch, Away Watch, and Dark Watch.
- This functionality is integrated into the existing Xfinity Shield suite and is provided to customers at no additional cost.
- The technology competes with previous market attempts, such as Linksys’s discontinued service and radio-frequency sensing solutions from Philips Hue and Wiz.

### Why it Matters
This update transforms standard internet routers into comprehensive home security tools, potentially reducing the need for expensive, standalone motion-detection hardware. By integrating these capabilities into existing infrastructure, Comcast is significantly lowering the barrier to entry for smart home monitoring and security services.
