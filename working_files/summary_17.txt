WHIPSHOT and SLAPSHOT: the tools behind an active Citrix NetScaler campaign
============================================================

Google and Mandiant have identified active exploitation of two critical zero-day vulnerabilities in Citrix NetScaler ADC and Gateway appliances, allowing attackers to gain root-level access to internal networks.

### Key Points
- CVE-2026-88772, a memory overflow vulnerability with a 9.5 CVSS score, enables remote code execution without requiring authentication.
- Attackers are deploying custom malware, specifically the WHIPSHOT web shell and SLAPSHOT proxy tool, to maintain persistence and move laterally.
- The exploit targets the DTLS handshake process, affecting government, financial, educational, and legal organizations across North America and Europe.
- Indicators of compromise include specific SSL handshake failure logs and unusual delays in server responses when requesting hidden files.
- Mandiant advises immediate patching and rotating all credentials handled by the appliances, as they must be assumed compromised.

### Why it Matters
These vulnerabilities highlight the persistent risk posed by internet-facing edge devices, which often bypass standard endpoint security protections. Because these appliances frequently store sensitive credentials, a successful breach provides attackers with a direct, high-privilege gateway into an organization's internal network.
