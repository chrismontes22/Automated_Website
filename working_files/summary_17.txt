‘Smart TV That Spies on You Even When It’s Off’ Is My #1 Christmas Gift Item
============================================================

A new investigation by Gamers Nexus reveals that certain LG smart TVs continue to collect user data and record ambient audio even when the devices are powered off.

### Key Points
- Researchers found that LG smart TVs use Automatic Content Recognition (ACR) to scan local Wi-Fi networks and identify connected devices like smartphones and watches.
- The investigation claims the TVs store audio data locally when disconnected from the internet, transmitting the information once a connection is restored.
- Security experts identified vulnerabilities in the TV software that could potentially allow hackers to exploit these data-gathering features.
- LG has not issued a formal response to the findings, which contradict the company's previous claims that it does not record ambient conversations.
- The report highlights that these data collection practices often remain active unless users manually navigate complex settings menus to disable them.

### Why it Matters
This investigation highlights the growing tension between consumer privacy and the aggressive data-harvesting practices embedded in modern internet-connected home appliances. The findings may trigger regulatory scrutiny similar to recent legal actions taken against other manufacturers regarding unauthorized data collection and advertising practices.
