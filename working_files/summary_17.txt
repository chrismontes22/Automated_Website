DoubleZero brings dedicated fiber market data to Hyperliquid traders
============================================================

DoubleZero has launched a dedicated fiber-optic market data feed for Hyperliquid, providing professional trading firms with faster, more consistent access to the decentralized exchange’s full order book.

### Key Points
- The new service provides a continuous, ordered stream of market data for perpetual futures and assets like gold, oil, and silver.
- Development partners for the feed include validator operators and ecosystem firms such as Hyperion DeFi, MAVAN, and Kinetiq.
- The feed replaces the need for firms to manually assemble data from public APIs or operate their own individual Hyperliquid nodes.
- DoubleZero’s Edge service now supports three venues, having previously integrated data feeds for the Solana blockchain and the prediction market Kalshi.
- The infrastructure is designed specifically for market makers and quantitative trading firms requiring high-speed, reliable order book updates.

### Why it Matters
This development signals a shift as onchain markets adopt the specialized data distribution models long utilized by traditional electronic exchanges like the CME or Nasdaq. While the service does not enable faster trade execution, it standardizes professional-grade data access to support the growing institutional presence within decentralized finance.
