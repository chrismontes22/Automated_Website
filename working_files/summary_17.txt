Bitcoin, gold trim early losses as yen gives up gains. Oil stays near $100
============================================================

Bitcoin and gold prices recovered during Friday’s Asian trading session as the Japanese yen retreated and regional stock markets rebounded despite ongoing concerns regarding high global oil prices.

### Key Points
- Bitcoin recovered to nearly $82,000 after testing its 50-day moving average, while gold prices bounced back to $4,020 per ounce.
- Cryptocurrency markets saw $435 million in liquidations over 24 hours, with $385 million of those positions consisting of bullish long bets.
- Spot bitcoin exchange-traded funds recorded $381 million in outflows on Thursday, signaling a potential lack of demand to sustain a broader market rally.
- The Japanese yen fell back toward 157 per U.S. dollar after briefly reaching a high of 156.07 following hotter-than-expected Japanese inflation data.
- WTI oil futures remained near $100 per barrel, maintaining pressure on global inflation risks despite U.S. government efforts to increase fuel production.

### Why it Matters
The volatility in these assets highlights how sensitive global markets remain to fluctuations in the Japanese yen and persistent energy costs. Investors are closely monitoring these trends to determine if the current recovery in risk assets can be sustained or if inflationary pressures will trigger further instability.
