The end of 'rip and replace'? New survey finds businesses are sticking it out with legacy tech
============================================================

A new report from Ensono reveals that 57% of UK organizations are choosing to upgrade legacy systems rather than replace them to accommodate rising AI investment costs.

### Key Points
- Ensono’s second annual State of IT Modernization Report found that 71% of modernization projects have exceeded their original budgets.
- Over two-thirds of businesses have been forced to pause, scale back, or abandon IT modernization efforts within the last two years.
- Approximately 54% of decision-makers now report that AI is actively accelerating their modernization strategies, up from 39% last year.
- Common roadblocks to achieving a return on investment for AI include workflow challenges and limitations within existing infrastructure.
- Organizations are increasingly prioritizing affordable hardware upgrades, such as increasing RAM and storage, to support AI integration.

### Why it Matters
This shift indicates that businesses are moving away from total system replacement in favor of augmenting reliable legacy infrastructure to manage tightening IT budgets. By balancing traditional systems with new AI capabilities, companies aim to maintain operational stability while navigating the high costs and complexities of digital transformation.
