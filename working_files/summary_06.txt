QLD Doubles the Nasdaq-100 Instead of Tripling It. That 2X Middle Ground Is Why It Survives Crashes TQQQ Doesn’t
============================================================

The ProShares Ultra QQQ (QLD) offers 2x daily leverage on the Nasdaq-100, providing a lower-risk alternative to the 3x leverage of the ProShares UltraPro QQQ (TQQQ) during market downturns.

### Key Points
- QLD and TQQQ both track the Nasdaq-100, which includes major technology companies like Nvidia, Apple, and Microsoft.
- QLD targets 2x the daily return of the index, while TQQQ targets 3x using derivative instruments like swaps and futures.
- During the 2022 market decline, QLD lost approximately 61% of its value, whereas TQQQ suffered a steeper 79% loss.
- TQQQ currently holds $35.6 billion in net assets with a 0.82% expense ratio, compared to QLD’s $14.1 billion and 0.95% expense ratio.
- Volatility drag can cause long-term performance to deviate significantly from the funds' stated daily leverage objectives.

### Why it Matters
Choosing between 2x and 3x leverage significantly impacts an investor's ability to recover from major market drawdowns. While higher leverage maximizes gains during bull markets, the mathematical difficulty of recovering from deeper losses makes lower-leverage options more resilient during prolonged selloffs.
