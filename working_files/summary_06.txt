Bloom Energy’s AI Surge Meets a Valuation Reality Check
============================================================

Bloom Energy has emerged as a critical provider for AI data centers, driving a 370% stock surge over the past year as demand for rapid, on-site power generation grows.

### Key Points
- Bloom Energy reported record second-quarter revenue of $1.065 billion, representing a 165.5% increase compared to the previous year.
- The company raised its 2026 full-year revenue guidance to a range between $3.9 billion and $4.2 billion.
- Major contracts include an agreement with Oracle for up to 2.8 gigawatts of fuel-cell systems and a $2.6 billion service deal with Nebius.
- Brookfield expanded its financing framework for Bloom-powered infrastructure projects to $25 billion in June.
- Analysts maintain a "Moderate Buy" consensus, though the stock faces competition from alternative power sources like natural-gas turbines and nuclear initiatives.

### Why it Matters
Bloom Energy has transitioned from a niche fuel-cell manufacturer into essential infrastructure for the rapidly expanding AI data center market. Its ability to bypass grid delays makes it a high-growth, albeit volatile, investment that is currently priced for sustained long-term success.
