Kindle Aluminum Review (2026): Gorgeous, but Expensive
============================================================

Amazon has released the 12th-generation Kindle Aluminum, a premium, compact e-reader featuring 32 GB of storage and new color options, though it lacks advanced lighting and accessory compatibility.

### Key Points
- The Kindle Aluminum is available in Seaglass Green and Alpine Sky, priced at $190.
- It features a 6-inch flush screen, a 5.7-ounce aluminum body, and 32 GB of internal storage.
- Performance upgrades include faster book loading times compared to the 2024 plastic model.
- The device supports AI features like "Story So Far" but lacks auto-adjusting or warm-toned front lighting.
- It is compatible with the Kindle Click remote but does not support the new Kindle Page Turn Cover.

### Why it Matters
The introduction of premium, color-focused hardware suggests Amazon is shifting its e-reader strategy toward aesthetic appeal to encourage frequent device upgrades among existing users. However, the high price point and lack of advanced features may lead consumers to consider more functional alternatives like the Paperwhite or Colorsoft Signature Editions.
