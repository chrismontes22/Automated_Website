Phone 3a, Phone 4a series—it's your turn to receive Nothing OS 5.0 (Android 17)
============================================================

Nothing has officially launched its OS 5.0 update, based on Android 17, for the Phone 3a and Phone 4a series, featuring significant camera enhancements and improved system performance.

### Key Points
- The OS 5.0 update began its staggered rollout on October 30 for the Nothing Phone 3a and Phone 4a series.
- Phone 3a devices receive enhanced video creation tools, including new filters, tuning options, and Dual-view Video support.
- Phone 4a models gain simultaneous front and rear camera recording capabilities and improved Essential Voice transcription latency.
- Both series feature a refreshed camera user interface with updated controls, animations, and feedback mechanisms.
- The update follows the mid-October release for the Phone 3, which introduced the Geist typeface and expanded Essential AI tools.

### Why it Matters
This update expands the reach of Nothing’s latest software features to a broader range of its mid-range hardware lineup. By prioritizing camera functionality and AI-driven transcription, the company aims to maintain competitive performance standards across its diverse device ecosystem.
