Microsoft president warns Trump's immigration crackdown could drive top AI talent abroad
============================================================

Microsoft President Brad Smith warns that the Trump administration’s recent immigration restrictions on tech companies could drive essential artificial intelligence talent to relocate to other countries.

### Key Points
- The Trump administration suspended Microsoft and Adobe from the Permanent Labor Certification (PERM) program, citing alleged abuse of foreign worker hiring.
- Brad Smith denied claims that Microsoft replaces American workers with lower-paid foreign staff, noting the company hired 9,200 U.S. employees last fiscal year.
- Federal data shows certified H-1B visa applications dropped 10% in the first nine months of fiscal year 2026.
- Demand for specialized AI talent remains high, with H-1B applications for data scientists increasing by 22.5% during the same period.
- Smith highlighted that five of the six recent recipients of the National Medal of Technology and Innovation are immigrants to the United States.

### Why it Matters
Restrictive immigration policies threaten the United States' competitive edge in the global artificial intelligence race by limiting access to top-tier international engineering talent. This dispute highlights a growing tension between federal labor protection efforts and the technology sector's reliance on a global workforce to drive market value and innovation.
