Einride is buying 500 Tesla Semis, and Amazon owns a piece of the buyer
============================================================

Swedish electric-trucking firm Einride has announced a deal to deploy 500 Tesla Semis for Amazon and other customers, marking the largest such deployment of the vehicles globally.

### Key Points
- Einride will deploy 500 Tesla Semis across California, Texas, New Jersey, Illinois, and Georgia over the next 24 months.
- Regulatory filings reveal Amazon holds warrants for 12 percent of Einride, which vest as the retailer purchases freight services.
- The deployment aims to convert approximately $800 million in potential long-term annual recurring revenue into active freight contracts.
- Einride currently operates a fleet of 200 electric trucks and provides logistics management through its proprietary Saga AI platform.
- While the Tesla Semis will initially require human drivers, Einride maintains separate operations for Level 4 autonomous trucking.

### Why it Matters
This partnership highlights Amazon’s strategy of taking equity stakes in logistics providers to secure sustainable shipping capacity while meeting its net-zero carbon goals. The deal also serves as a critical test for Tesla’s ability to scale Semi production to meet the demands of major commercial freight networks.
