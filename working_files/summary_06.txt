Metro by T-Mobile is giving away the Samsung Galaxy A37 for FREE — here's how to claim yours
============================================================

Metro by T-Mobile is offering a free Samsung Galaxy A37 5G to new customers who switch their phone number and sign up for a $40 monthly unlimited plan.

### Key Points
- The promotion provides a $450 value device at no cost with no required trade-in or activation fees.
- Customers must enroll in the $40 per month unlimited talk, text, and data plan on T-Mobile’s 5G network.
- The Samsung Galaxy A37 features a 6.7-inch AMOLED display, an Exynos 1480 chipset, and a 5,000mAh battery.
- Samsung guarantees six years of OS and security updates for the device.
- Data speeds may be deprioritized on the Metro network after a user exceeds 35GB of usage in a single billing cycle.

### Why it Matters
This promotion highlights an aggressive push by prepaid carriers to capture market share by bundling high-value hardware with low-cost wireless subscriptions. It offers budget-conscious consumers a significant hardware upgrade without the long-term financial commitments typically associated with major carrier contracts.
