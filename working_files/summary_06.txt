"An outright ban will not eliminate AI from the modding process," Nexus Mods say as they rule out barring generated creations from their site
============================================================

Nexus Mods has officially rejected an outright ban on AI-generated content, choosing instead to implement mandatory disclosure requirements and stricter moderation standards for all uploaded modding projects.

### Key Points
- Nexus Mods CEO Victor Folmann confirmed that AI-generated mods remain permitted provided they meet new transparency, permission, and presentation criteria.
- Uploaders must now provide a public explanation detailing how and where AI tools were utilized in their modding process.
- AI-related tags will be displayed more prominently on mod pages, appearing immediately after adult-content warnings for better visibility.
- The platform is investing in specialized security staff and tools to detect potential malicious code generated by AI.
- Mod authors have a 60-day grace period ending January 2, 2025, to update existing uploads to comply with the new disclosure rules.
- Nexus Mods explicitly stated it will not use user-submitted content to train AI models or license such data to third-party developers.

### Why it Matters
This policy shift highlights the ongoing challenge of balancing creative freedom with user transparency in the rapidly evolving landscape of generative AI. By prioritizing disclosure over prohibition, Nexus Mods aims to maintain platform control while allowing the community to make informed decisions about the content they download.
