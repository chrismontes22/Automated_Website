iOS 27: All the New Phone and FaceTime Features
============================================================

Apple is introducing new features to the Phone and FaceTime apps in iOS 27, including on-device Call Context intelligence and Dual Capture video streaming for supported iPhone models.

### Key Points
- The Phone app’s new Call Context feature uses on-device Apple Intelligence to display relevant reservation or flight details from Mail during business calls.
- Dual Capture allows users on iPhone 17 models and iPhone Air to stream video from both front and back cameras simultaneously during FaceTime calls.
- iOS 27 improves network stability by optimizing transitions between Wi-Fi and cellular connections and enhancing call quality during poor signal conditions.
- Communication Safety features now expand beyond nudity to automatically block images containing gore or violence in FaceTime and Messages.
- Live Captions support has been updated to include Traditional Chinese for improved accessibility.

### Why it Matters
These updates leverage on-device processing and hardware-specific capabilities to streamline communication and improve reliability for Apple users. By integrating Mail data with phone calls and enhancing video versatility, Apple aims to increase the utility of its core communication tools while maintaining privacy standards.
