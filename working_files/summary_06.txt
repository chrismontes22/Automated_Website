iPhone Duo Is Delightful, and Different
============================================================

The new Apple iPhone Duo, a foldable device featuring a 5.4-inch outer screen and 7.6-inch inner display, launches on October 23, 2026, with a starting price of $1,999.

### Key Points
- The device features a precision-engineered titanium hinge and dual-display architecture designed for seamless content scaling.
- It replaces Face ID with side-button Touch ID and requires users to adapt to new interface layouts and navigation controls.
- Camera capabilities include Duo Preview and Smart Take, though it lacks the dedicated telephoto lens found on the iPhone 18 Pro.
- Battery life is managed by custom silicon across two internal cells, supporting extended use without requiring mid-day charging.
- The phone is available in Night Sky and Star White colorways, starting at a 256GB storage capacity.

### Why it Matters
The iPhone Duo represents Apple’s first entry into the foldable market, signaling a shift in how the company balances hardware form factors with user behavior. By offering a device that functions as both a compact phone and a tablet, Apple is challenging the dominance of traditional slab-style smartphones while testing consumer willingness to accept specific trade-offs in exchange for increased screen versatility.
