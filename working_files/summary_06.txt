Prime Day returns next month — what you need to know about Amazon's Big Deal Days sale event
============================================================

Amazon has officially scheduled its 2026 Big Deal Days shopping event for October 6th and 7th, offering exclusive discounts on electronics and consumer goods for Prime members.

### Key Points
- The two-day sale event takes place on October 6th and 7th, 2026.
- Most promotional offers are restricted to Amazon Prime subscribers.
- Featured product categories include Android phones, smartwatches, laptops, tablets, and kitchen appliances.
- Potential discounts are expected on foldable devices from Samsung, Apple, and Motorola, alongside the Google Pixel 11 series.
- Competitors including Best Buy and Walmart are expected to launch rival sales during the same period.

### Why it Matters
This event serves as the unofficial start of the fall shopping season, allowing consumers to secure holiday discounts before the Black Friday and Cyber Monday rush. It provides a strategic opportunity for shoppers to mitigate recent price increases on high-end technology and mobile devices.
