The Credential Layer Is Expanding Faster Than Security Teams Can See It
============================================================

GitGuardian highlights the urgent need for enterprises to map their expanding "credential layer" as rapid software production and AI agent adoption increase the risk of exposed secrets.

### Key Points
- GitGuardian reports that 28.65 million new hardcoded secrets were detected in public GitHub commits in 2025, a 34% year-over-year increase.
- Research indicates that 22% of initial breach access stems from compromised credentials, often found on unmanaged developer endpoints or collaboration systems.
- Infostealer malware, such as Shai-Hulud and S1ingularity, frequently targets developer laptops, where researchers found an average of nearly five secrets per compromised machine.
- AI coding agents introduce new security vulnerabilities, with over 2,000 valid secrets identified in public Model Context Protocol configuration files during 2025.
- Effective security requires a "Detect, Remediate, and Prevent" strategy that inventories credentials across repositories, endpoints, and cloud environments to provide necessary context.

### Why it Matters
As software development scales toward a 30x increase in volume, traditional security perimeters are failing to contain the proliferation of plaintext credentials. Organizations must gain full visibility into their credential layer to prevent attackers from exploiting the gap between managed vaults and unmonitored developer environments.
