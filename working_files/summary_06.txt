Building a second-wave AI business
============================================================

Bootstrapped startups can achieve sustainable success by leveraging AI to solve high-value problems rather than focusing solely on cost reduction or competing in commoditized, price-sensitive markets.

### Key Points
- Successful AI ventures should prioritize generative value, such as growth and utility, over simple automation or speed improvements.
- B2B purchasing decisions are driven by the need to avoid blame, claim credit, and reduce uncertainty for internal champions.
- Startups should target existing "forcing functions," such as competitive or technology crises, to bypass long sales cycles.
- Durable businesses require a proprietary data stack, built-in network effects, and a focus on specific, expensive problems.
- The "sponsored model" allows startups to serve underserved populations by securing funding from institutions with aligned missions.
- Utilizing cheap, open-source inference models allows for improved margins as technology evolves without requiring massive infrastructure.

### Why it Matters
Focusing on value creation rather than cost-cutting allows startups to build defensible, premium-priced businesses that are less vulnerable to market saturation. By aligning products with the career incentives of organizational buyers and leveraging existing crises, founders can create sustainable growth without needing massive venture capital.
