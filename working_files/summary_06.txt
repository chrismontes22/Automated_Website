Stocks dented by inflation risk from rising oil, dicey geopolitics
============================================================

Global stock markets are trending lower as rising oil prices, escalating Middle East conflict, and political instability in Europe drive investor anxiety ahead of critical U.S. inflation data.

### Key Points
- Brent crude futures climbed 1.3% to $97.50 per barrel following military confrontations between U.S. forces and Iran near the Strait of Hormuz.
- Diesel prices have reached record highs, currently trading 90% above levels recorded before the start of the conflict.
- The European Central Bank is expected to raise interest rates to 2.5% this Thursday, with further hikes anticipated by December.
- Markets are pricing in a 58% probability of a Federal Reserve rate hike during the September 16 meeting following strong August payroll data.
- Germany’s far-right Alternative for Germany (AfD) party secured a first-place finish in Saxony-Anhalt state elections, raising concerns regarding the long-term stability of the euro.

### Why it Matters
The convergence of surging energy costs and aggressive central bank interest rate hikes creates a challenging environment for global economic growth and asset valuations. Investors are closely monitoring these developments to gauge the risk of a prolonged inflationary period and potential political instability within the eurozone.
