The Swiss military is actively testing a transition from Microsoft 365 to open-source software alternatives to reduce reliance on American cloud-based applications and enhance digital sovereignty.

### Key Points
- The Swiss Army is spearheading a strategic initiative to replace proprietary Microsoft 365 tools with free and open-source software (FOSS).
- This move aims to mitigate risks associated with foreign cloud dependencies and improve long-term data control for national defense operations.
- The project reflects a broader trend of European organizations seeking independent, locally managed digital infrastructure to ensure operational security.
- Switzerland’s shift follows ongoing global discussions regarding the privacy implications of hosting sensitive government data on US-based cloud platforms.

### Why it Matters
This transition highlights a growing movement among government entities to prioritize digital sovereignty over the convenience of established commercial ecosystems. If successful, the Swiss model could serve as a blueprint for other nations looking to reduce their dependence on major American technology providers.
