Infinix Note 70 Pro certification helps confirm its major upgrade
============================================================

The upcoming Infinix Note 70 Pro has appeared in the Eurasian Economic Commission database, signaling that the smartphone is nearing its official market release following recent certification filings.

### Key Points
- The Infinix Note 70 Pro, identified by model number X6766, was officially listed in the Eurasian Economic Commission database.
- Leaked hardware specifications include a Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 chipset, 8GB of RAM, and 256GB of internal storage.
- The device features a 6.78-inch display with a 144Hz refresh rate and a 50MP main camera equipped with optical image stabilization.
- A significant hardware upgrade over the previous Note 60 Pro is the inclusion of a large 7,000 mAh battery.
- EEC certification typically indicates that a product launch will occur within a few weeks to a couple of months.

### Why it Matters
This certification confirms that Infinix is preparing to refresh its mid-range lineup with a focus on increased battery capacity. The transition to new chipset hardware may reflect broader industry trends regarding component pricing and supply chain adjustments for mobile manufacturers.
