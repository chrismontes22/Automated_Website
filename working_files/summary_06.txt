Galaxy S27 Ultra's redesign looks even more iPhone-like in new renders
============================================================

Newly leaked renders of the Samsung Galaxy S27 Ultra reveal a significant design shift, featuring a horizontal camera plateau and potential integration of native Qi2 magnetic charging support.

### Key Points
- Leaked renders show a horizontal camera layout, departing from Samsung's traditional vertical design.
- The device dimensions are reported at approximately 163.76 x 78.25 x 7.97mm, maintaining a similar footprint to the previous model.
- Samsung is expected to replace the aging 10MP 3x telephoto lens with updated camera hardware.
- The new internal configuration may allow for a larger battery capacity and native Qi2 magnetic accessory compatibility.

### Why it Matters
This redesign signals a major aesthetic shift for Samsung's flagship line, potentially aligning its hardware ecosystem more closely with Apple's MagSafe accessory standard. If implemented, native Qi2 support would offer users greater convenience by eliminating the need for specialized magnetic cases.
