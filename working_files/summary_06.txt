Sam Altman's love of watches is getting memed 'Lord of the Rings' style
============================================================

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman commissioned seven custom-made, ultra-luxury watches from Swiss manufacturer Vanguart for himself and senior staff, sparking widespread social media comparisons to Lord of the Rings.

### Key Points
- Sam Altman commissioned seven custom timepieces from the Swiss watchmaker Vanguart for himself and select senior OpenAI leadership.
- The custom watches are based on the Vanguart Orb model, which carries a starting retail price of $180,000.
- Social media users on X drew parallels between the exclusive seven-watch set and the "Rings of Power" from J.R.R. Tolkien’s fantasy series.
- Altman previously gained attention for wearing a rare $480,000 Greubel Forsey Invention Piece 1 in 2023.
- Other tech companies, including Uber and Palantir, have recently issued limited-edition, company-branded Tudor watches to employees as status symbols.

### Why it Matters
High-end timepieces are increasingly serving as prominent status symbols and personal branding tools among Silicon Valley’s elite. This trend highlights the growing intersection of luxury consumer goods and corporate culture within the tech industry.
