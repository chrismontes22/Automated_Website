AI can help pinpoint a top CEO candidate. The harder part is persuading them to take the job.
============================================================

Executive search firms are increasingly integrating artificial intelligence to accelerate candidate identification and administrative tasks, while reserving complex judgment and relationship-building for human recruiters in high-stakes hiring.

### Key Points
- AI tools now map markets and suggest unconventional talent pools, such as identifying dental tech experts for jewelry manufacturing roles.
- Search firms like Korn Ferry use AI to mine years of internal interview notes and retrieve specific candidate observations.
- Automation of administrative tasks, such as organizing emails and tracking systems, has reportedly doubled or tripled daily productivity for sourcing teams.
- Proprietary models trained on decades of hiring data assist recruiters by generating interview questions and behavioral exercises for executive finalists.
- Human recruiters remain essential for verifying technical expertise, interpreting nonverbal cues, and persuading executives to accept life-changing career moves.

### Why it Matters
The integration of AI into executive search shifts the recruiter's role from manual data gathering to high-level strategic advising and relationship management. While technology significantly increases efficiency in the early stages of hiring, the reliance on human judgment for final placements ensures that trust and cultural fit remain central to corporate leadership transitions.
