'Samsung Sound' is no more: app prepares for shutdown and future integration
============================================================

Samsung will officially shut down its dedicated Sound app on March 31, 2027, as the company migrates speaker management features into the centralized SmartThings platform for users.

### Key Points
- Samsung Sound app users must transition their devices to the SmartThings app to maintain control over soundbars and speakers.
- The company is providing advance notice to ensure users can register their hardware and migrate existing settings before the March 31, 2027, deadline.
- This move follows a series of recent Samsung app closures in 2026, including Samsung Messages, Quick Measure, and AR Doodle.
- Samsung states the consolidation is intended to provide a more convenient and unified experience across its device ecosystem.

### Why it Matters
This transition reflects Samsung's broader strategy to streamline its software ecosystem by consolidating niche applications into the primary SmartThings hub. By centralizing device management, the company aims to reduce maintenance overhead while simplifying the user experience across its connected hardware portfolio.
