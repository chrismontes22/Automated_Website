Engram is a sampler that turns broken AI hallucinations into music
============================================================

Music startup Thoughtful Things has launched a Kickstarter campaign for Engram, an experimental hardware sampler that uses local, custom-trained AI models to manipulate audio and generate unique soundscapes.

### Key Points
- Engram functions as an offline groovebox designed to "circuit-bend" tiny AI models for experimental sound design rather than automated song generation.
- The company explicitly states that its audio models are trained exclusively on commercially licensed datasets to avoid copyright infringement.
- Founder Evan King describes the device as a "field recorder for latent space," allowing users to transform input audio into glitchy, uncanny textures.
- The device features open firmware, enabling users to modify the system or load their own custom AI models.
- Kickstarter early-bird pricing starts at $675, representing a 30 percent discount off the projected future retail price of $850 to $900.

### Why it Matters
This device represents a shift toward localized, ethical AI hardware that prioritizes creative experimentation over the automated content generation seen in cloud-based platforms. By offering open firmware and transparent training data, Thoughtful Things provides a unique alternative for musicians seeking to integrate AI into their workflows without relying on internet-connected services.
