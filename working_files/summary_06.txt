Made in America: How AI is keeping this U.S. factory competitive
============================================================

GE Appliances is integrating artificial intelligence and advanced robotics into its Lafayette, Georgia, manufacturing plant to improve production efficiency, reduce costly errors, and maintain competitiveness against overseas rivals.

### Key Points
- GE Appliances uses AI-powered cameras and sensors to detect assembly errors in real-time, triggering immediate alerts to minimize production downtime.
- The company’s "Brilliant Factory" data platform allows executives in Louisville, Kentucky, to monitor performance metrics across nine major manufacturing plants.
- Predictive AI maintenance identifies overheating equipment before failures occur, preventing assembly line stoppages that cost up to $500 per minute.
- Each percentage point of manufacturing improvement saves the company between $1.5 million and $2 million annually.
- The facility recently underwent a $180 million expansion, adding 600 jobs while utilizing AI to optimize staffing and forecast market demand.

### Why it Matters
By leveraging AI to achieve near-perfect production, GE Appliances is successfully lowering operational costs and neutralizing the competitive advantage of lower-wage overseas manufacturers. This data-driven approach demonstrates how legacy industrial firms can utilize automation to sustain domestic operations and expand their workforce.
