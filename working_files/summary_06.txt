Amazon’s Alexa Tablets are coming for the iPad
============================================================

Amazon is launching a new lineup of Alexa Tablets on October 14, marking a significant shift from its Fire brand by incorporating full Google Play-supported Android software.

### Key Points
- The lineup includes the Tablet 8 ($229.99), Tablet 11 ($329.99), and the flagship Alexa Tablet 12 Pro starting at $499.
- All devices run full Android with Google Play support, moving away from the restricted operating systems used in previous Fire tablets.
- The 12 Pro features a 12.5-inch, 120Hz display with an optional Nanomatte finish developed in partnership with Corning to reduce glare.
- Hardware is powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 8400 processor, with configurations offering up to 16GB of RAM and 256GB of storage.
- The tablets integrate "Alexa Plus," an LLM-powered assistant designed to analyze on-screen content and provide contextual recommendations.

### Why it Matters
This transition signals Amazon's intent to compete directly with the iPad by prioritizing premium hardware and a standard software experience over budget-focused utility. By integrating advanced AI and high-end display technology, the company aims to capture a larger share of the tablet market beyond its traditional ecosystem.
