My Favorite Budget Camera Brand Is Finally Getting 4K Plus and a Mile-Long Range
============================================================

Amazon-owned security brand Blink has announced five new camera models, including its first 4K resolution device and specialized long-range cameras designed to extend connectivity beyond standard Wi-Fi limits.

### Key Points
- The Blink Mini 4K Plus ($80) introduces 4K resolution to the brand's compact indoor and outdoor camera lineup.
- Two new XR Plus models, the Frontier XR Plus ($200) and Outdoor 2K XR Plus ($150), utilize a dedicated hub to reach distances up to 7,000 feet.
- The wireless Blink Spotlight Cam 2K Plus ($110) offers motion-activated lighting without requiring junction boxes or external power outlets.
- The Blink Mini Pan-Tilt 2K Plus ($50) provides 350-degree panning and 115-degree tilting with automatic motion tracking for versatile coverage.
- All new devices are currently available for preorder and maintain integration with Amazon Alexa services.

### Why it Matters
These product additions signal a strategic shift for Blink as it moves beyond budget-focused hardware into higher-performance categories like long-range monitoring and 4K imaging. By addressing connectivity gaps and expanding feature sets, the company is positioning itself to compete more directly with premium smart home security providers.
