I cut my phone's animation speed in half, and now it feels twice as fast
============================================================

Android users can improve the perceived speed of their devices by adjusting animation scale settings within the hidden Developer options menu to make interface transitions feel significantly snappier.

### Key Points
- Android defaults window, transition, and animator duration scales to 1x, which prioritizes visual fluidity over raw responsiveness.
- Users can access granular control by enabling Developer options through the Build number menu in device settings.
- Setting animation scales to 0.5x cuts transition times in half, providing a balance between interface polish and increased speed.
- The Accessibility "Reduce animations" toggle completely disables transitions, which can make the user interface feel jarring or unpolished.
- Adjusting these settings does not improve actual app loading times but significantly enhances the perceived performance of both new and aging hardware.

### Why it Matters
Adjusting animation scales allows users to customize their device's responsiveness to better match their personal preferences for speed and fluidity. This simple software tweak can extend the usability of older smartphones by making the interface feel more modern and reactive without requiring a hardware upgrade.
