Edifier’s $52 gaming soundbar lets you ditch your headset without losing voice chat
============================================================

Edifier has announced the Hecate G2000 Bar, an affordable, mecha-inspired gaming soundbar featuring spatial audio, customizable RGB lighting, and an integrated AI-powered microphone for improved voice communication.

### Key Points
- The Hecate G2000 Bar launches on 16 October with an introductory price of 349 yuan ($52).
- The device features two 56mm full-range drivers and two 60mm passive radiators to enhance low-frequency audio output.
- It supports THX 7.1.4 spatial audio processing and includes six genre-specific presets for optimized gaming sound.
- The integrated microphone utilizes AEC 2.0 echo cancellation and AI-assisted noise reduction to facilitate clear voice chat.
- Connectivity options include Bluetooth 6.0 and USB sound-card input for compatibility with PCs, consoles, and mobile devices.
- Users can customize the aesthetic via 12 RGB lighting effects and five interchangeable themed stickers.

### Why it Matters
This release highlights a growing market trend toward compact, all-in-one audio solutions that eliminate the need for bulky external subwoofers or dedicated headsets. By offering advanced features like spatial audio and AI-enhanced microphones at a low price point, Edifier is positioning itself to capture budget-conscious gamers looking to streamline their desktop setups.
