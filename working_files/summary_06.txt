Stripe 2.9% vs. Adyen 0.6% vs. Braintree 2.59%: 2026 Fees

Adyen, Stripe, and Braintree offer distinct advantages for enterprise payment processing in 2026, with selection depending on whether a business prioritizes cost efficiency, developer tooling, or specific consumer wallets.

### Key Points
- Adyen provides the lowest cost for high-volume global retailers through its transparent interchange-plus pricing model.
- Stripe processed $1.9 trillion in 2025, surpassing PayPal to lead the group in total payment volume.
- Stripe remains the preferred choice for SaaS platforms and marketplaces due to its superior API documentation and embedded finance features.
- Braintree serves as the primary gateway for merchants requiring native integration of PayPal and Venmo payment options.
- Adyen maintains a unified global platform, while Stripe and Braintree often utilize flat-rate pricing that can become expensive at scale.

### Why it Matters
Choosing the right payment processor is a critical financial decision that can result in millions of dollars in annual savings or losses for large enterprises. While sticker prices provide a baseline, businesses must weigh the trade-offs between lower transaction fees and the strategic value of advanced developer tools or integrated consumer wallets.
