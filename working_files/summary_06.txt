Amazon is teasing its Black Friday sale next month — see 20+ early Android deals ahead of Big Deal Days
============================================================

Amazon has officially scheduled its Big Deal Days shopping event for October 6th and 7th, offering Prime members early access to significant discounts ahead of the holiday season.

### Key Points
- Amazon’s Big Deal Days sale will take place globally on October 6th and 7th.
- Shoppers require an active Amazon Prime membership to access the exclusive event deals.
- Early discounts are currently available on tech products, including the Google Pixel 10a and Garmin Venu 3S.
- Non-members can utilize a 30-day free trial to access Prime-exclusive savings during the two-day event.
- Current pre-sale offers include a $75 discount on the Google Pixel 10a and 36% off the Garmin Venu 3S smartwatch.

### Why it Matters
This event signals the start of the competitive holiday retail season, forcing consumers and competitors to prepare for early discounting cycles. By pulling major sales into October, Amazon aims to capture consumer spending before traditional Black Friday and Cyber Monday windows begin.
