Tesla’s Door Handles Lead to Its Biggest Recall Yet
============================================================

China’s State Administration for Market Regulation has ordered a massive recall of over 2.9 million Tesla vehicles due to safety concerns regarding the functionality of retractable door handles.

### Key Points
- The recall affects Tesla Model 3 and Model Y vehicles manufactured in China between 2019 and 2026.
- Remedies include over-the-air software updates to lower windows after collisions and the installation of clearer warning stickers for mechanical door releases.
- Eight other Chinese automakers, including Xiaomi and Geely, are also recalling over 1 million vehicles for similar flush door handle safety issues.
- A separate recall impacts nearly 50,000 imported Tesla vehicles due to insufficient driver monitoring systems for assisted steering technology.
- China plans to implement new regulations that will eventually ban fully retractable door handles on vehicles sold within the country.

### Why it Matters
This widespread regulatory action highlights growing global scrutiny over the safety implications of modern automotive design trends like flush door handles. As China takes an aggressive stance on vehicle technology standards, automakers may face significant pressure to redesign features that prioritize aesthetic appeal over emergency accessibility.
