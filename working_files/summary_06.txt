HP's EliteBook X G2i has the best touchpad and display I've used, but one inherited flaw ruins it
============================================================

The HP EliteBook X G2i offers premium performance and a high-quality 3K OLED display in a lightweight chassis, though inconsistent pricing and limited configuration availability complicate the purchasing process.

### Key Points
- The laptop features an Intel Core Ultra 7 366H processor, 32GB of LPDDR5x RAM, and a 1TB PCIe NVMe SSD.
- It includes a 14-inch 3K OLED display with a 2880x1800 resolution and a 5MP IR webcam.
- The device weighs approximately 2.2 pounds and includes a haptic "waterfall" touchpad and a high-quality keyboard.
- Connectivity options include two Thunderbolt 4 ports, one USB-C 10Gbps port, one USB-A port, and HDMI 2.1.
- HP lists a "Smart Buy" price of $2,899 for this configuration, though availability remains inconsistent on the company's website.

### Why it Matters
The EliteBook X G2i demonstrates strong hardware capabilities for business users, but HP's confusing retail strategy and lack of configuration options may deter potential buyers. These logistical hurdles make it difficult for customers to secure specific high-end models at transparent price points.
