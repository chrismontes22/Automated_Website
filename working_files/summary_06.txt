The Tim Ferriss Show Transcripts: How to Spot 10-Year Trends and Build Billion-Dollar Companies — Kevin Ryan of DoubleClick, MongoDB, Business Insider, and Many More (#884)
============================================================

Kevin Ryan, a prominent New York-based entrepreneur and investor, shares his strategic approach to identifying long-term market trends and building successful, high-growth technology companies over his multi-decade career.

### Key Points
- Kevin Ryan founded AlleyCorp, a venture capital firm that incubates and invests in sectors including healthcare, AI, deep tech, and consumer software.
- His track record includes co-founding major companies such as MongoDB, Business Insider, Gilt Groupe, Zola, and Transcend Therapeutics.
- Ryan previously served as CEO of DoubleClick, scaling the startup from 20 employees to a publicly traded company before its $1.1 billion acquisition.
- His investment philosophy prioritizes 10-year trends, such as nuclear energy and psychedelics, over short-term market hype.
- AlleyCorp’s recent initiatives include a $335 million fund and a commitment to donate 10% of gains from Transcend Therapeutics to psychedelic research and mental health causes.

### Why it Matters
Ryan’s career offers a blueprint for how institutional-scale success is achieved through disciplined trend analysis and the systematic incubation of companies. His focus on long-term, defensible moats provides a practical framework for investors and founders navigating the rapidly evolving landscapes of AI, healthcare, and deep tech.
