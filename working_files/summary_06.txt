What to expect from Apple’s ‘Pro and tell’ event
============================================================

Apple has scheduled a "Pro and tell" event for October 28 in Los Angeles, where the company is expected to unveil its first touchscreen MacBook Pro and updated hardware.

### Key Points
- The event takes place on October 28 at 1:00 PM ET in Los Angeles.
- Apple is expected to introduce its first MacBook Pro featuring an OLED touchscreen and a Dynamic Island interface.
- Base model MacBook Pro and iMac units will likely receive performance upgrades to the M6 chip.
- A new iPad Mini featuring an OLED display and an A20 processor is anticipated to launch.
- New MacBook Pro models may face price increases due to ongoing component shortages.

### Why it Matters
These product updates represent a significant shift in Apple’s hardware strategy, particularly with the integration of touchscreen technology into the flagship laptop line. The transition to OLED displays across the MacBook and iPad Mini lineups signals a broader effort to standardize premium screen technology across the company's ecosystem.
