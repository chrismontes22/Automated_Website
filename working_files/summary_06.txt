We just got a look at the stock market's worst-case scenario
============================================================

The S&P 500 and Nasdaq 100 fell sharply on Thursday as investors reacted to rising crude oil prices from the Iran conflict and investor fatigue regarding massive AI infrastructure spending.

### Key Points
- The S&P 500 dropped 1.2% and the Nasdaq 100 fell nearly 2% during Thursday's trading session.
- Magnificent 7 stocks collectively declined 4% as markets reacted to disappointing earnings and capital expenditure forecasts.
- Alphabet shares tumbled 7% despite strong Google Cloud growth, while Tesla shares fell 15% after missing profit estimates.
- Crude oil prices surged above $100 per barrel due to renewed tensions between the United States and Iran.
- Rising inflation expectations from higher energy costs have led investors to anticipate potential interest rate hikes.

### Why it Matters
This market volatility highlights a growing vulnerability as investors simultaneously lose patience with high AI capital expenditures and fear the inflationary impact of geopolitical instability. The upcoming earnings reports from Meta, Microsoft, and Amazon will serve as a critical test for whether the broader tech sector can maintain its valuation amid these mounting economic pressures.
