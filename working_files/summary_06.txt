Samsung could double the phones in the Galaxy Z series: 'Galaxy Z Slide' rumored
============================================================

Samsung is reportedly planning to expand its foldable smartphone lineup to six models by 2027, potentially introducing new TriFold and slidable device designs to the consumer market.

### Key Points
- Rumors suggest Samsung will launch six foldable devices in 2027, including a Galaxy Z TriFold 2 and a Galaxy Z Fold 9 Pro.
- The company is allegedly developing a "Galaxy Z Slide" device, building on slidable display prototypes showcased at CES 2025.
- Samsung Display previously demonstrated Slidable Flex Duet and Solo concepts that expand screen sizes up to 17.3 inches.
- The proposed Galaxy Z TriFold 2 may feature a thinner, more durable design with an estimated retail price of $2,800.
- Current foldable offerings include the Galaxy Z Fold 8, Fold 8 Ultra, and Flip 8 models.

### Why it Matters
Expanding the foldable portfolio signals Samsung's intent to maintain its market dominance by diversifying form factors beyond traditional book-style and clamshell designs. This strategy could push mobile hardware boundaries, though the high price points and technical complexity of these devices may challenge mass-market adoption.
