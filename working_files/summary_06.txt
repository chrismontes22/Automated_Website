She Designed Meta’s New AI Logo. Then Came the Hate
============================================================

Lettering artist Jessica Hische faced significant backlash on Threads after designing the logo for Meta’s new AI assistant, Muse, to support her small businesses and employees.

### Key Points
- Jessica Hische was hired by Meta to design the logotype and icon for Muse, the company's new AI agent.
- The artist received $12,500 for the project, which she stated was necessary to cover payroll and business expenses for her Oakland-based shops.
- Critics on social media accused Hische of "selling out" and supporting unethical AI practices, leading to intense online harassment.
- Hische defended her decision by noting that Meta hired a human artist rather than using generative AI to create the branding.
- The designer clarified that she did not use AI tools in her creative process for the project.

### Why it Matters
The intense reaction highlights the growing hostility within the creative community toward companies developing generative AI. This incident underscores the difficult financial trade-offs independent contractors face when balancing personal ethics against the need to sustain small businesses in a volatile market.
