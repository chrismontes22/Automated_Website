PCI SSC calls for human approval of AI agent actions involving cardholder data
============================================================

The PCI Security Standards Council has released new guidance requiring human oversight for AI agents handling cardholder data to mitigate security risks and prevent unauthorized system access.

### Key Points
- The PCI SSC recommends that human operators provide explicit approval for any AI actions involving cleartext cardholder data.
- Organizations should implement a "least agency" approach, limiting AI system permissions to only the specific capabilities required for assigned tasks.
- The guidance advises maintaining an AI inventory and bill of materials to track models, data-use policies, and unauthorized "shadow AI" tools.
- Security teams should conduct adversarial testing to identify potential vulnerabilities and ensure AI systems cannot bypass established access controls.
- Sensitive credentials, such as cryptographic keys and passwords, must be managed through independent tools rather than being stored in AI prompts or logs.
- Third-party AI providers must be assessed for compliance, with contracts explicitly prohibiting the use of company data for model training.

### Why it Matters
This guidance provides a critical framework for businesses integrating AI into payment environments where data security is paramount. By mandating human accountability and strict access controls, the PCI SSC aims to reduce the risk of automated breaches and ensure that AI adoption does not compromise existing payment security standards.
