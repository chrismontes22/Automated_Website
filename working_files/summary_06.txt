Indie Dev Releases Tearful Message After Multiple Laptops Are Stolen At Gamescom
============================================================

Indie developer Ryan Laley and other exhibitors reported multiple laptops stolen from the secure business area at Gamescom in Cologne, Germany, on the morning of August 28, 2026.

### Key Points
- Multiple developers, including Ryan Laley and publisher iam8bit, reported equipment theft from the Gamescom business hall.
- Stolen items included laptops containing unfinished game builds for *Petal Runner* and *Escape Academy 2*.
- Laley estimates that at least seven or eight laptops were taken from the supposedly secure, 24/7 monitored facility.
- The incident has been reported to local police and Gamescom management, though no equipment has been recovered.
- Major industry players, including CD Projekt, have reached out to offer support to the affected developers.

### Why it Matters
The theft highlights significant security vulnerabilities at major industry trade shows where developers rely on promised protection for expensive, mission-critical hardware. This incident serves as a cautionary tale for indie studios regarding the risks of leaving proprietary assets at convention booths overnight.
