Google’s Pixel Phones Are Getting a Tap-to-Share Feature for Contact Info and More
============================================================

Google has introduced a new tap-to-share feature for its Quick Share service, allowing Android users to exchange files and contact information by physically touching two compatible devices together.

### Key Points
- The update enables file and contact sharing by tapping devices, mirroring the functionality of Apple’s AirDrop.
- Google announced the feature during its Made by Google event alongside the new Pixel 11 and Pixel Watch 5.
- The tool is currently available for Pixel 6 and newer models, with support coming soon to Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 and Flip 8 devices.
- Users can share contact cards directly from the home screen, while photos and videos require opening the phone's Share Sheet first.
- Google recommends removing thick cases or adjusting device alignment if the NFC-based connection fails to initiate.

### Why it Matters
This update bridges a significant usability gap between Android and iOS ecosystems by simplifying the file-sharing process for mainstream users. By expanding this functionality to older devices and third-party manufacturers, Google is standardizing a convenient wireless transfer method across the broader Android market.
