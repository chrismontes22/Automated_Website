Nothing’s New Headphone (1) Pro Are Made for the Studio
============================================================

Nothing has officially unveiled the Headphone (1) Pro, a premium over-ear headset featuring a unique triple-driver architecture and professional-grade audio tools, launching in stores on September 29.

### Key Points
- The Headphone (1) Pro utilizes three distinct drivers per ear to separate bass, midrange, and treble frequencies for improved audio clarity.
- The design incorporates xMEMS technology and 9H Panda glass panels, allowing users to view the internal hardware components.
- Priced at $399, the headphones include a dedicated flat EQ mode developed in collaboration with Metropolis Studio for professional audio production.
- The device features 10 microphones for active noise cancellation and offers an IP52 rating for dust and water resistance.
- Battery life is rated at 68 hours with active noise cancellation disabled, and the unit includes a 3.5-mm jack for wired connectivity.
- The product will be available starting September 29 through Nothing’s website, Amazon, and Best Buy retail locations.

### Why it Matters
By integrating studio-grade features and high-end materials, Nothing is attempting to transition from a lifestyle brand to a serious contender in the professional audio market. This strategic shift challenges established premium competitors by offering specialized tools that cater to both casual listeners and audio engineers.
