A Wave of New Regulations Is Reshaping Retail. The Businesses That Prepare Now Will Have an Advantage.
============================================================

Global consumer brands must adapt to a wave of new regulations covering customs, AI disclosure, and product transparency by prioritizing data accuracy to maintain essential customer trust.

### Key Points
- New international regulations require businesses to provide verifiable data regarding pricing, product origins, and the use of artificial intelligence.
- The U.S. and EU have tightened customs exemptions, while the UK now mandates that all unavoidable fees be included in upfront advertised prices.
- The EU AI Act and California’s chatbot laws require clear, deliberate disclosure whenever consumers interact with automated systems rather than human agents.
- New EU packaging and deforestation regulations demand rigorous documentation of supply chains, impacting how products are labeled and sourced globally.
- India’s upcoming data protection framework introduces a consent manager system, further formalizing how companies must handle consumer privacy and digital interactions.

### Why it Matters
These regulations represent a fundamental shift in market expectations where transparency is no longer optional but a core component of consumer trust. Companies that proactively integrate these standards into their product data records will gain a competitive advantage by meeting the needs of both regulators and modern, AI-driven shopping agents.
