Samsung could finally build Galaxy Buds with ear hooks for workouts
============================================================

Samsung is reportedly developing its first open-ear Galaxy Buds featuring a secure ear-hook design, marking a significant shift toward fitness-focused hardware to compete with established audio brands.

### Key Points
- Leaked renders reveal a new ear-hook design for the model codenamed "Buds Canal 5," departing from traditional stem or bean-shaped earbuds.
- The device is expected to include modern features such as USB-C charging, water and dust resistance, and potential active noise cancellation.
- Software references and the model number SM-U600 confirm the earbuds are in active development despite previous reports of project delays.
- Samsung is entering a competitive market currently dominated by established open-ear products from companies like Shokz, Bose, and Sony.
- Branding remains unconfirmed, with previous leaks suggesting potential product names such as Galaxy Buds Able or Galaxy Buds On.

### Why it Matters
This move signals Samsung's intent to capture the growing fitness-focused audio market by offering users a way to listen to media while maintaining environmental awareness. To succeed, the company must differentiate its product from existing competitors beyond mere brand recognition to justify its entry into the crowded open-ear segment.
