Anthropic’s Claude Duet wants to do your job while you’re offline
============================================================

Anthropic has launched a research preview of Claude Duet, a proactive AI agent powered by Claude Opus 5.5 that operates independently to manage professional tasks across multiple platforms.

### Key Points
- Claude Duet functions within a dedicated virtual machine, allowing it to perform background tasks without requiring an active chat window.
- The agent provides daily summaries and handles complex workflows, including scheduling meetings, building dashboards, and developing web applications.
- Access is currently limited to a US-based waitlist for users with active Claude Pro or Max subscriptions.
- Anthropic has implemented security safeguards to prevent the unauthorized handling of sensitive information like credit card numbers and government IDs.
- The tool is designed to compete with other autonomous AI assistants, such as Meta Muse and OpenAI Dots.

### Why it Matters
This launch signals a shift in the AI industry from passive chatbots toward autonomous agents capable of executing complex, multi-step workflows independently. By integrating directly into professional environments, these tools aim to increase productivity, though they also raise new challenges regarding data privacy and the management of sensitive corporate information.
