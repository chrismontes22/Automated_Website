Bumble reportedly in talks to go private at a valuation of about $1bn
============================================================

Ithaca Holdings is reportedly in advanced negotiations to acquire dating app Bumble in a potential deal that would take the company private at a $1 billion valuation.

### Key Points
- Bumble shares surged 50% to $3.80 on Monday following reports of the potential acquisition by investment firm Ithaca Holdings.
- The company’s paying user base dropped 16.4% to 3.2 million by the second quarter of 2026, reflecting broader industry stagnation.
- Annual revenue declined nearly 10% to $966 million in 2025, prompting the company to cut 30% of its workforce that same year.
- Bumble, founded by Whitney Wolfe Herd in 2014, previously reached a $14 billion valuation during its 2021 initial public offering.
- Recent strategic shifts include the launch of an AI-powered dating assistant named Bee and the removal of the app's signature women-first messaging requirement.

### Why it Matters
This potential acquisition signals a significant retreat for a once-dominant public tech company struggling to maintain relevance among younger users. If finalized, the deal would mark the end of Bumble's five-year tenure on the Nasdaq and highlight the ongoing consolidation challenges facing the online dating sector.
