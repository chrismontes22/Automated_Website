Exclusive: Neurable’s First Brain-Scanning Headphones Want to Fine-Tune Your Focus
============================================================

Neurable has launched the Neurable One, a $499 pair of brain-scanning headphones featuring 12 EEG sensors designed to monitor focus, stress, and cognitive load in real time.

### Key Points
- The Neurable One headphones feature 12 integrated EEG sensors that passively monitor brain activity without requiring manual app activation.
- The companion app tracks eight metrics, including brain age, focus, stress, and sleepiness, to provide personalized productivity and recovery recommendations.
- Hardware specifications include 40-mm beryllium drivers, active noise cancellation, and a battery life of up to 22 hours, or 12 hours with sensors active.
- The device will be available for purchase on the company's website starting in October for $499.
- Neurable states the product is not a medical device and commits to not selling user brain data or using it for advertising purposes.

### Why it Matters
This release marks a strategic shift for Neurable as it moves from licensing its brain-computer interface technology to third-party manufacturers to producing its own consumer hardware. By integrating passive monitoring into everyday audio gear, the company aims to normalize neuro-tracking as a tool for workplace productivity and personal wellness.
