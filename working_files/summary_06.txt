6 phones to buy instead of the Google Pixel 11 Pro
============================================================

While the Google Pixel 11 Pro XL offers strong AI features, performance concerns and hardware downgrades make several alternative smartphones more compelling options for power users and mobile enthusiasts.

### Key Points
- The Pixel 11 Pro XL faces criticism for reduced RAM, smaller battery capacity, and an underwhelming GPU compared to its predecessor.
- The Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra remains the top U.S. choice for those seeking a stable ecosystem, seven years of updates, and high-end performance.
- The Redmagic 11S Pro provides superior gaming performance and a 7,500mAh battery for $899, significantly undercutting the Pixel's price point.
- The POCO F8 Ultra features a unique denim finish, 100W fast charging, and Bose-tuned audio, making it a versatile all-rounder.
- Global models like the OPPO Find X9 Pro and Vivo X300 Pro offer superior camera systems and faster processors, though they are harder to acquire in the U.S.

### Why it Matters
Choosing a smartphone involves balancing software preferences with hardware longevity and regional availability. These alternatives highlight that consumers can often find better value, performance, or specialized features by looking beyond Google's flagship offerings.
