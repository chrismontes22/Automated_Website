An Interview with Palantir CTO Shyam Sankar About the AI Religious War
============================================================

Palantir CTO Shyam Sankar argues that the AI industry must shift its focus from developing frontier models to prioritizing the practical diffusion of intelligence into enterprise workflows.

### Key Points
- Sankar contends that companies achieve greater success by using AI to pursue top-line growth and market dominance rather than simple cost-cutting or headcount reduction.
- The interview highlights the necessity of a systems-engineering approach to AI safety, comparing the current lack of rigor to historical industrial safety standards.
- Sankar warns that relying solely on frontier models risks "beta" outcomes, where companies lose their unique competitive advantage by training on generalized, commoditized intelligence.
- Palantir advocates for "Sovereign AI," where businesses maintain control over their own data and post-training pipelines to ensure models remain tailored to their specific operational needs.
- The discussion critiques the "doomerism" prevalent in some AI labs, characterizing it as a secular religion that ignores practical, real-world problem-solving.

### Why it Matters
- This perspective challenges the current market trend of outsourcing intelligence to a few dominant model providers, suggesting that long-term corporate value depends on maintaining proprietary data loops. By framing AI adoption as a strategic choice between commoditized "beta" and unique "alpha," Sankar provides a roadmap for businesses to integrate AI without sacrificing their competitive edge.
