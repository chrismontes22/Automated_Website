Google is about to roll out a new AI model. Employees say they're testing another that's way better.
============================================================

Google is internally testing a new Gemini 4 model codenamed Carbon, aiming to improve its coding capabilities and compete more effectively with rivals like Anthropic and OpenAI.

### Key Points
- Google employees are testing an internal model called Carbon, which some staff compare to Anthropic’s high-performing Claude Opus 5.5 for coding tasks.
- Carbon is part of the broader Gemini 4 family, which includes other internal iterations such as Argon and Barium.
- The upcoming Gemini 4 "Argon" model is designed for frontier performance in legal, finance, and defensive cybersecurity sectors.
- Google plans to release Argon to partners in its Fairwind Program for vulnerability testing before a wider public rollout.
- The company is accelerating development to maintain its competitive standing in the rapidly evolving market for AI-powered coding agents.

### Why it Matters
This development highlights Google's aggressive push to close the performance gap with industry leaders in the specialized field of agentic coding assistants. Success in this area is critical for Google as it seeks to maintain its influence among developers and enterprise users in an increasingly crowded AI market.
