Samsung’s $700 Galaxy S26 FE Is Better, but It’s Getting Harder to Call It a Bargain
============================================================

Samsung has announced the Galaxy S26 FE, a new smartphone featuring advanced camera software and Android 17, launching on September 3 with a starting price of $700.

### Key Points
- The Galaxy S26 FE starts at $700 for 128GB and $800 for 256GB, reflecting a $50 price increase over the previous model.
- Key software additions include Super Steady Video, My FanCam, and Audio Eraser, powered by Android 17 and One UI 9.
- Hardware features include a 6.7-inch display, an Exynos 2500 processor, and a 4,900-mAh battery with 45-watt wired charging.
- The camera system retains a 50-megapixel main sensor but introduces a new selfie camera with a wider field of view.
- Samsung promises seven years of software and security updates for the device, which will be available in Pistachio, Blueberry, and Graphite colors.

### Why it Matters
The Galaxy S26 FE brings premium software capabilities to a more accessible price point, positioning it as a competitive alternative to flagship models despite rising industry memory costs. Its integration with iOS 26 transfer tools and long-term update support makes it a significant option for users looking to upgrade from older hardware.
