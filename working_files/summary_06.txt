Breville’s Wild New Toaster Swaps the Timer for Optic Sensors to Tell When Toast Is Done
============================================================

The new $300 Breville Eye Q 850 Auto Toaster utilizes an internal optical sensor to monitor bread color and achieve precise doneness levels for various types of sliced bread.

### Key Points
- The Eye Q 850 features an Optic Sensor in the left slot that monitors visual browning rather than relying solely on time or temperature.
- The unit includes a motorized lift, adjustable chime alerts, and a dedicated "A Bit More" button for additional toasting.
- Specialized modes accommodate sourdough, bagels, English muffins, and toaster pastries, with a manual timer mode for non-bread items.
- The design incorporates a removable crumb tray and a built-in power cord storage slot for improved kitchen organization.
- Performance testing showed high accuracy for white and sourdough breads, though darker breads required closer monitoring to prevent over-toasting.

### Why it Matters
This premium appliance represents a shift toward sensor-based automation in small kitchen electronics, aiming to solve the common issue of inconsistent toasting. While the high price point limits its appeal to luxury consumers, it offers a sophisticated alternative to traditional spring-loaded models for daily users.
