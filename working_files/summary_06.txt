The Credential Layer Is Expanding Faster Than Security Teams Can See It
============================================================

GitGuardian highlights the urgent need for enterprises to map their expanding credential layer as rapid software production and AI agent adoption increase the risk of secret exposure.

### Key Points
- GitGuardian reports that 28.65 million new hardcoded secrets were detected in public GitHub commits in 2025, a 34% year-over-year increase.
- Research indicates that 44% of compromised developer machines contain more than 10 unique secrets, creating significant entry points for supply-chain attacks.
- Modern security threats, such as infostealer malware, can extract credentials from browser data, local files, and application storage on developer endpoints.
- Effective credential management requires visibility across repositories, collaboration tools, and developer laptops to identify ownership, validity, and permission scopes.
- GitHub projects an infrastructure scale increase of 30x to accommodate the growth of agentic development and automated code production.

### Why it Matters
As software production accelerates, the "credential layer" has expanded beyond traditional security perimeters, leaving organizations vulnerable to automated attacks that exploit unmanaged secrets. Establishing a comprehensive, context-aware inventory is now essential for security teams to prevent breaches before attackers can leverage exposed credentials for lateral movement.
