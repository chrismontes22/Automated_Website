Keeping the world safe when no one is in charge
============================================================

The rise of global multipolarity and rapid advancements in artificial intelligence are creating unprecedented security risks that traditional military power alone can no longer effectively contain or manage.

### Key Points
- The international order is shifting toward multipolarity, where multiple regional blocs compete for influence without a single dominant hegemon.
- Artificial intelligence and inexpensive drone technology are decentralizing power, making it difficult for even major states to maintain control over security threats.
- Emerging risks, including potential pandemics, cyberattacks, and the proliferation of biological or nuclear weapons, transcend national borders and traditional military defenses.
- International bodies like the UN, WHO, and IAEA provide existing frameworks for global oversight, monitoring, and compliance in sensitive areas.
- Recent initiatives, such as the joint Call for Action on AI oversight by Norway and Finland, aim to build cross-regional coalitions for global governance.

### Why it Matters
The current reliance on zero-sum power dynamics and national military strength is increasingly inadequate for addressing borderless technological and biological threats. Establishing robust international institutions and cooperative legal frameworks is now essential to prevent catastrophic global risks that individual nations cannot mitigate alone.
