Roborock Qrevo 2 Review (2026): A New Budget Vacuum King?
============================================================

Roborock has officially launched the Qrevo 2 Pro, a sub-$1,000 robot vacuum featuring advanced edge-cleaning technology, a 20,000 Pa motor, and improved navigation for residential floor maintenance.

### Key Points
- The Qrevo 2 Pro features a 20,000 Pa digital motor and mop pads with 200 RPM rotation speed.
- Integrated FlexiArm technology and a reversible side brush are designed to improve debris removal in corners and along furniture edges.
- The device utilizes LiDar navigation and supports the Matter smart home standard for voice command integration.
- Its 3.8-inch height allows the unit to clean under low-profile kitchen toe kicks and furniture.
- While effective at navigating large obstacles, the vacuum struggles to identify smaller items like cords and cat toys.

### Why it Matters
This release signals Roborock's renewed focus on its affordable Qrevo line, providing high-end cleaning features at a more accessible price point for consumers. The model offers a competitive alternative to existing budget options, though users should remain aware of its limitations regarding small object detection.
