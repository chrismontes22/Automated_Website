Rank Tracker Data, ChatGPT Tags & AI Search Links – SEO Pulse via @sejournal, @MattGSouthern
============================================================

Recent fluctuations in search rankings and referral data highlight the importance of verifying analytics, as Google and OpenAI implement significant changes to AI-driven search and traffic tracking.

### Key Points
- Rank-tracking firm Nozzle reported that 8% of Google search results pages were missing usual sites between September 13 and October 9, likely due to AI scraping responses.
- A bug in ChatGPT’s link tracking caused a 45% drop in attributed sessions in Google Analytics 4, which OpenAI began fixing on October 15.
- Google is increasing the visibility of inline links in AI Overviews and testing new "Preferred Sources" carousels to highlight specific publishers.
- Microsoft introduced the "Topics Portfolio" beta in Bing Webmaster Tools, allowing site owners to identify citation gaps and opportunity queries in AI search results.
- Recent Google job listings indicate the company is actively hiring to develop AI-generated content and "proactive" recommendation features for the Discover feed.

### Why it Matters
These technical shifts demonstrate that search and referral data can be volatile due to platform-side bugs or algorithmic testing. Marketing teams should cross-reference third-party tracking tools with Google Search Console to ensure reporting remains accurate and reflects actual user behavior.
