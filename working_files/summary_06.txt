Stoke Space raises $1bn for a rocket that has never flown
============================================================

Washington-based aerospace startup Stoke Space has secured $1 billion in Series E funding to develop its fully reusable Nova rocket, bringing its total capital raised to $2.3 billion.

### Key Points
- The funding round was co-led by Point72 Ventures and Spark Capital, with participation from Y Combinator and several other investment firms.
- Stoke Space is developing the Nova launch vehicle, which aims for full reusability of both stages to reduce costs and increase flight frequency.
- The company plans to launch its Nova Pathfinder vehicle in early 2027, followed by a larger Nova Block 2 vehicle in 2029.
- Despite having no orbital flight history, the company has received development support from NASA, the U.S. Space Force, and the Defense Innovation Unit.
- The capital will be used to build necessary infrastructure, including factories, test stands, and recovery facilities for high-cadence operations.

### Why it Matters
This massive investment highlights the intense market demand for reliable, low-cost launch capacity as an alternative to SpaceX’s dominance. By backing unproven technology, investors are betting that the aerospace industry requires multiple providers to ensure national security and commercial access to orbit.
