OPPO Find 10 Pro Max review: the best Android phone ever made
============================================================

The Oppo Find X10 Pro Max launches globally as a premium Android flagship, featuring a versatile triple 200MP camera system, massive 8,000 mAh battery, and high-end MediaTek performance.

### Key Points
- The device features three 200MP sensors for the main, ultrawide, and telephoto cameras, supported by a Hasselblad-partnered imaging engine.
- It is powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 9600 Pro processor with up to 16GB of RAM and 1TB of storage.
- The global model includes an 8,000 mAh battery with 80W charging, though EU variants feature a smaller 7,150 mAh capacity.
- The 6.78-inch QHD AMOLED display supports a 144Hz refresh rate and features ultra-thin 1.15mm bezels.
- Software runs on ColorOS 17, which is guaranteed to receive five major Android version updates and six years of security patches.
- Pricing starts at £1,499 in the UK and €1,799 in Europe, positioning it as a high-end competitor to the latest Samsung and Apple flagships.

### Why it Matters
The Find X10 Pro Max represents a significant push by Oppo to capture the global premium smartphone market by offering a "no-compromise" hardware experience. Its success challenges established market leaders like Samsung and Apple by proving that a comprehensive, feature-rich device can effectively compete at the highest price points.
