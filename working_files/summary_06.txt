The Plot to Delete Democracy
============================================================

Gil Durán’s book *The Nerd Reich* examines how a network of Silicon Valley billionaires is leveraging their wealth and political influence to dismantle democratic institutions and regulatory oversight.

### Key Points
- Tech elites, including Elon Musk and Peter Thiel, donated nearly $400 million to support Donald Trump’s reelection and influence federal policy.
- The Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE), led by Musk and Vivek Ramaswamy, aimed to slash federal agencies and budgets despite failing to meet its $2 trillion savings goal.
- Russell Vought, a key architect of Project 2025, has utilized the Office of Management and Budget to implement autocratic restructuring and purge federal staff.
- Silicon Valley figures are promoting "network states" and private tech cities, seeking to bypass traditional government authority through cryptocurrency and corporate-led governance.
- Companies like Palantir and various crypto ventures have secured significant government contracts and regulatory leniency through their proximity to the Trump administration.

### Why it Matters
The concentration of political power among a small group of tech oligarchs threatens to replace democratic governance with a system prioritizing corporate profit and deregulation. Understanding these alliances is essential for voters to recognize how private financial interests are actively reshaping the federal government and public policy.
