iFixit Shares iPhone 18 Pro Teardown With Inside Look at New Features
============================================================

iFixit’s latest teardown of the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max reveals increased battery capacities, a new variable aperture camera system, and significant challenges for internal hardware repairs.

### Key Points
- The iPhone 18 Pro features a 4,288 mAh battery, while the Pro Max includes a 5,567 mAh capacity, marking a 9.4% increase for the larger model.
- U.S. and several international markets receive eSIM-only versions, while other regions retain a physical SIM card tray.
- Internal cooling is improved through the integration of a larger vapor chamber across both Pro models.
- NAND storage is now embedded within the dual-layer logic board, complicating potential storage-related repairs for technicians.
- iFixit reported that the screen's plastic frame frequently cracked during the disassembly process, suggesting potential durability concerns for future repairs.

### Why it Matters
These findings highlight a growing trend where internal design choices prioritize performance and cooling at the expense of long-term repairability. As components like NAND storage become more integrated, consumers may face higher costs or increased difficulty when attempting to service or upgrade their devices.
