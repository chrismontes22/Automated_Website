Data governance culture gives companies competitive edge — Taxtech boss
============================================================

Taxaide Technologies Limited CEO Bidemi Olumide explains how Nigerian enterprises are leveraging advanced financial technology and data automation to improve compliance, operational efficiency, and long-term competitive market positioning.

### Key Points
- Automation allows businesses to replace manual data processing with real-time analytics, significantly increasing speed and accuracy in tax and compliance reporting.
- Leading Nigerian firms are integrating Enterprise Resource Planning systems with banking and regulatory portals to enable seamless, automated financial data flows.
- AI-assisted anomaly detection is being deployed to identify compliance risks and potential tax exposure before they result in regulatory penalties.
- The shift toward mandatory electronic filing by the Nigeria Revenue Service and Lagos State Internal Revenue Service has accelerated local adoption of tax technology.
- Digital security is increasingly viewed as a strategic board-level asset rather than a technical cost, essential for protecting intellectual property and client trust.

### Why it Matters
Adopting robust data governance and automation tools is now critical for Nigerian firms to maintain regulatory compliance and operational agility in a digital-first economy. Companies that prioritize these technologies gain a significant competitive advantage by transforming reactive risk management into proactive, data-driven strategic decision-making.
