Ford explains why it chose Apple Maps over Google for its new EVs
============================================================

Ford has selected Apple Maps as the native navigation provider for its upcoming Fathom electric vehicle, prioritizing deep integration for range estimation and charging data over CarPlay Ultra.

### Key Points
- Ford will integrate Apple Maps directly into the native software of its new Universal Electric Vehicle (UEV) platform.
- The partnership focuses on leveraging Apple’s data for real-time range estimation, weather conditions, elevation, and charger availability.
- Ford executive Alan Clarke cited Apple’s improved mapping quality and collaborative development team as primary reasons for the selection.
- The Fathom vehicle will continue to support standard Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, allowing users to choose alternative navigation apps like Waze or Google Maps.
- Ford explicitly confirmed it is not adopting the next-generation CarPlay Ultra interface for this specific vehicle platform.

### Why it Matters
This decision highlights a strategic shift where automakers are prioritizing native, deeply integrated software experiences to manage complex electric vehicle functions like battery preconditioning. By choosing a native Apple Maps implementation, Ford aims to provide a seamless, high-performance interface that bridges the gap between third-party smartphone projection and proprietary vehicle systems.
