A college in Illinois tried to cancel its Flock contact and it refused
============================================================

Elgin Community College engaged in a year-long dispute with Flock Safety after the school attempted to terminate its contract over concerns regarding data privacy and unauthorized surveillance access.

### Key Points
- Elgin Community College installed six Flock Safety cameras in April 2025 to improve campus security.
- School administrators sought to cancel the contract by October 2025, citing concerns over data sharing with Customs and Border Patrol.
- Flock Safety refused the removal request, claiming no contractual violations occurred despite the college offering to pay for the remaining term.
- College staff repeatedly covered the cameras with trash bags, but workers in unmarked vehicles consistently removed the coverings to restore functionality.
- The dispute concluded in April 2026 when the contract expired and the company finally removed the surveillance equipment.

### Why it Matters
This conflict highlights growing tensions between educational institutions and private surveillance providers regarding data governance and the right to terminate security agreements. It underscores the potential legal and operational challenges schools face when attempting to regain control over campus monitoring systems.
