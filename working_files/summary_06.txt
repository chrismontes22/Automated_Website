"That's so AI" is the new insult, and it means bullshit
============================================================

Generation Alpha is increasingly using the phrase "That's so AI" as a derogatory slang term to describe anything perceived as cheap, inauthentic, or lacking genuine human value.

### Key Points
- Gen Alpha uses "That's so AI" to label knock-off merchandise, exaggerated claims, or insincere behavior as suspect.
- The term functions as social commentary, contrasting with previous nonsense-based slang like "skibidi" or "6-7."
- The phrase reflects a growing cultural skepticism toward the quality and reliability of artificial intelligence products.
- Linguistic experts note this shift as a modern evolution of slang that serves as a substitute for profanity.

### Why it Matters
This linguistic trend signals a significant branding challenge for technology companies investing billions into generative AI tools. When a product name becomes synonymous with low-quality "slop," it highlights a disconnect between corporate marketing and public perception among younger demographics.
