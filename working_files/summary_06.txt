The Best Early Prime Day Deals Ahead of Amazon’s Second Sale (2026)
============================================================

Amazon has announced its Prime Big Deal Days event for October 6 and 7, featuring early discounts on various consumer electronics, home appliances, and tested tech products.

### Key Points
- Prime Big Deal Days officially runs from October 6 through October 7, with early discounts currently available.
- Featured tech deals include the Google Pixel 10a for $424 and Google Pixel Buds Pro 2 for $190.
- Home appliance discounts include the Roborock Qrevo 2 Pro robot vacuum priced at $550 and the FlexiSpot EN1 Standing Desk for $170.
- Additional discounted brands include JBL, Shark, Levoit, and TP-Link.
- The sale serves as a precursor to the broader holiday shopping season, including Black Friday and Cyber Monday.

### Why it Matters
This event signals the start of the competitive holiday retail season, offering consumers early opportunities to purchase tested electronics at reduced prices. For Amazon, these sales events are critical for driving Prime subscriptions and maintaining market dominance against other major retailers during the final quarter of the year.
