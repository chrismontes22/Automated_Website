If you bought a Pixel 11 Pro, you should activate this free perk immediately
============================================================

Google Pixel 11 Pro and Pro XL owners can now claim a complimentary six-month subscription to Google AI Pro, providing access to advanced artificial intelligence tools and premium services.

### Key Points
- The Google AI Pro subscription, formerly known as Gemini Advanced, offers a retail value of approximately $120 over the six-month trial period.
- Included benefits feature 5TB of cloud storage, YouTube Premium Lite, and 30 days of event history for Nest devices via Google Home Premium.
- Users gain access to advanced tools like the Jules coding agent, Google Antigravity development platform, and increased usage limits for Gemini in Google apps.
- Activation requires navigating to the settings menu within the Gemini app to manually initiate the trial offer.
- The subscription is managed through Google Play, requiring a payment method on file that will only be charged after the six-month promotional period expires.

### Why it Matters
This promotion highlights Google's strategy to integrate its advanced AI ecosystem directly into the hardware experience to drive user adoption of its subscription services. By bundling high-value features like cloud storage and premium media access, Google aims to demonstrate the long-term utility of its AI platform to new smartphone customers.
