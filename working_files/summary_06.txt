Nasdaq (NDAQ) Just Made It Harder For Crypto Criminals To Hide
============================================================

Nasdaq’s Verafin unit has partnered with Stablecore to integrate digital asset activity with traditional banking data, aiming to improve financial crime detection for banks offering stablecoin services.

### Key Points
- Nasdaq Verafin and Stablecore are merging traditional banking records with digital asset transaction data to identify money laundering across both financial systems.
- The integration allows banks to offer stablecoins and tokenized deposits while maintaining unified compliance monitoring for all customer activity.
- Amarillo National Bank is currently testing the platform, with a broader rollout for mutual customers scheduled between late 2026 and early 2027.
- The digital asset market has grown to approximately $2.4 trillion, increasing the regulatory pressure on banks to monitor crypto-related transactions.
- Nasdaq’s Financial Technology segment reported $539 million in revenue for the second quarter of 2026, reflecting a 16% year-over-year increase.

### Why it Matters
This partnership addresses a critical visibility gap for financial institutions as they increasingly adopt blockchain technology and stablecoin products. By centralizing compliance data, Nasdaq aims to capture a larger share of the growing fintech market while helping banks mitigate the rising risks associated with digital asset fraud.
