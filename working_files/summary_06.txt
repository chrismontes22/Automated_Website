You don't need a TV app to stream 10,000 free movies on Google’s Freeplay right now
============================================================

Google TV Freeplay has expanded its streaming service to include over 10,000 on-demand movies and television shows alongside more than 300 live channels for users in the United States.

### Key Points
- The service now offers a library of 10,000 on-demand titles, including content from studios like A24 and Lionsgate.
- Google TV Freeplay has surpassed 300 live channels, featuring networks such as Bloomberg TV+, Yahoo! Sports Network, and The Martha Stewart Channel.
- The update is available automatically on Google TV Streamer boxes and smart TVs from manufacturers including Sony, TCL, and Hisense.
- Content is ad-supported and requires a Google account and a high-speed internet connection to access.
- The platform has grown by approximately 50 channels since December 2025 to provide a broader range of news, sports, and entertainment.

### Why it Matters
This expansion positions Google TV Freeplay as a direct competitor to established ad-supported platforms like Tubi and Pluto TV. By integrating on-demand content directly into the operating system, Google provides a cost-effective alternative for consumers facing widespread subscription fatigue.
