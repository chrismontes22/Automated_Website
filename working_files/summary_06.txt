My Guaranteed Steps to Tell if a New Device Is a Flock Camera
============================================================

Flock Safety’s widespread use of AI-powered license plate readers has created public confusion, prompting experts to provide methods for identifying these surveillance cameras and verifying local government contracts.

### Key Points
- Flock Safety cameras are typically identified by a distinct oval, hooded shape and a circular lens, often featuring a small, shiny logo on the front.
- Large, dark-colored solar panels mounted near the camera are a primary indicator of Flock’s automatic license plate reader (ALPR) hardware.
- The Atlas of Surveillance and Street-Level Surveillance hub are recommended tools for verifying which surveillance technologies local law enforcement agencies have purchased.
- Private entities, including retail chains like Home Depot and Lowe’s, may independently contract with Flock, leading to cameras on private property despite municipal cancellations.
- Public records requests and direct inquiries at city council meetings serve as formal methods to confirm the presence and usage policies of surveillance equipment.

### Why it Matters
Understanding the prevalence and placement of surveillance technology is essential for citizens concerned about privacy and data collection in their communities. Distinguishing between municipal and private camera systems helps residents hold local officials accountable for the technologies deployed in public spaces.
