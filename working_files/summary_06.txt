The 4 Seasons of an Entrepreneur’s Journey — and How Each One Changes Your Priorities
============================================================

Roof Maxx founder explains how entrepreneurs can achieve long-term success by aligning their business strategies and professional goals with the four distinct seasonal stages of a career.

### Key Points
- The career lifecycle is divided into four stages: spring for ideation, summer for investment, autumn for harvesting results, and winter for legacy planning.
- Roof Maxx, a company valued at over eight figures, provides a cost-effective roof restoration solution that disrupts traditional full-replacement industry models.
- The company’s flagship product underwent rigorous testing at Ohio State University to ensure efficacy before expanding to a national dealer network.
- Strategic pivots, such as selling a previous contracting business to fund new ventures, allowed the founders to scale operations across all 50 states.

### Why it Matters
Recognizing the specific stage of one's professional journey allows business leaders to allocate time and capital more effectively. This framework helps entrepreneurs transition from early-stage conceptual dreaming to sustainable growth and eventual long-term legacy planning.
