Binance founder Changpeng Zhao on crypto hack: 'There's no 100% security'
============================================================

Binance has restored full platform operations and guaranteed user fund restitution following a major October 29 cyberattack that resulted in the theft of $6.3 billion in digital assets.

### Key Points
- Hackers drained approximately $6.3 billion from Binance during a 20-minute security breach on October 29.
- The FBI and blockchain intelligence firms attributed the attack to TraderTraitor, a cyber unit linked to North Korea.
- Attackers bypassed cryptographic keys by compromising an internal employee laptop to access automated transaction workflows.
- Binance utilized corporate treasury reserves, insurance funds, and the Secure Asset Fund for Users (SAFU) to cover all customer losses.
- Binance currently manages roughly $171 billion in total assets and is led by co-CEOs Richard Teng and Yi Le.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the persistent vulnerability of even the world's largest cryptocurrency exchanges to sophisticated, state-sponsored cyber threats. The successful recovery of user funds demonstrates the importance of robust insurance reserves in maintaining market confidence and platform stability during high-magnitude security crises.
