Dow jumps over 1,000 points in response to Democrats' control of Congress
============================================================

The Dow Jones Industrial Average surged over 1,500 points as U.S. markets rallied following a Democratic sweep of Congress in the 2026 midterm elections.

### Key Points
- The Dow Jones Industrial Average rose 3%, while the S&P 500 and Nasdaq Composite climbed 1.6% and 1.4% respectively.
- Brent crude oil futures dropped 8% to $95 per barrel amid investor optimism regarding potential resolutions to conflicts in the Middle East.
- Tech sector performance was mixed, with Apple and Microsoft hitting record highs while chipmakers like Nvidia and Micron declined.
- Bitcoin recovered to trade above $80,000, and Ethereum gained 7% to surpass $2,400 following the election results.
- Duolingo shares jumped over 30% on strong user growth, while McDonald's reported slowing same-store sales.

### Why it Matters
The market's positive reaction to a divided government highlights investor relief regarding the conclusion of midterm uncertainty. While historical data suggests these election-driven rallies are often short-lived, the shift in legislative control will likely influence future policy decisions on housing, pharmaceuticals, and data center regulation.
