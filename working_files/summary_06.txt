This Former Google Employee Created an ‘AI’ Chatbot That Lets You Speak to An Actual Human
============================================================

Former Google project manager Tucker Bryant has paused his viral ChatTJB project, a chatbot that replaces artificial intelligence with human volunteers to answer user queries manually.

### Key Points
- Tucker Bryant launched ChatTJB in April, initially answering all user questions himself before expanding to a volunteer network.
- The project gained significant traction after a $6,000 billboard advertisement in San Francisco prompted over 100,000 user inquiries.
- ChatTJB currently relies on a growing list of volunteers, with sign-ups increasing by approximately 1,000 people per day.
- Bryant created the platform to highlight "cognitive surrender," a term describing the tendency to rely excessively on AI for simple decision-making.
- The service is currently offline as Bryant manages the surge in traffic and explores potential future partnerships for the project.

### Why it Matters
The project highlights a growing societal debate regarding the risks of outsourcing human critical thinking to automated systems. By demonstrating a genuine user demand for human interaction, ChatTJB serves as a cultural critique of the rapid integration of AI into daily life.
