Xiaomi's Redmi Note 17 Pro Max has the best battery life of any phone — I still wouldn't use it
============================================================

The Xiaomi Redmi Note 17 Pro Max offers industry-leading battery life but suffers from significant hardware downgrades and outdated software, making it a poor value at its $499 price.

### Key Points
- The device features a massive 9,210mAh silicon-carbon battery that provides up to two and a half days of usage.
- Hardware performance is hampered by the aging Snapdragon 6 Gen 5 chipset and a base configuration of only 8GB of RAM.
- The phone includes a 6.83-inch 120Hz OLED display and offers IP68 and IP69K ingress protection for enhanced durability.
- Despite its 50MP main camera, the device lacks 4K60 video recording and features a middling wide-angle lens.
- The handset launched with Android 16, despite the stable release of Android 17 being available for over two months.

### Why it Matters
The Redmi Note 17 Pro Max highlights a growing trend of hardware regression in the mid-range smartphone market as manufacturers prioritize battery capacity over processing power. Consumers seeking a balanced device may find better performance and longevity in competing models or previous-generation handsets.
