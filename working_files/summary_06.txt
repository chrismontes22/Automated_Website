FTC hits Samsung with a $15 million fine over Galaxy S21, S22's 'false' gaming performance
============================================================

South Korea’s Fair Trade Commission has fined Samsung 20 billion KRW, approximately $15 million, for misleading consumers regarding the gaming performance of its Galaxy S21, S22, and Tab S8 devices.

### Key Points
- The South Korean Fair Trade Commission (FTC) issued a 20 billion KRW ($15 million) fine against Samsung.
- Regulators found that the Game Optimization Service (GOS) restricted CPU and GPU performance regardless of device temperature.
- Affected models include the Galaxy S21, Galaxy S22, and Galaxy Tab S8 series.
- The GOS caused significant lag, freezing, and reduced graphical quality during gameplay.
- Samsung maintains that the GOS was implemented to ensure stable, long-term gaming performance and plans to contest the fine.

### Why it Matters
This ruling highlights the growing regulatory scrutiny over how manufacturers advertise performance capabilities versus real-world user experiences. It sets a significant precedent for transparency in mobile marketing, forcing companies to be more explicit about software-based performance limitations that impact consumer hardware.
