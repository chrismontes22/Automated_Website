One year with the Google Pixel 10 Pro Fold: Sometimes it's good to be boring
============================================================

The Google Pixel 10 Pro Fold offers a refined software experience and durable hardware, but it struggles to compete with rivals due to underwhelming cameras and connectivity issues.

### Key Points
- The device features a 6.4-inch cover screen and an 8-inch inner panel, both protected by Gorilla Glass Victus 2 and IP68 water and dust resistance.
- Software highlights include the Material You interface, the exclusive Magic Cue contextual assistant, and Android 17’s new Bubbles multitasking feature.
- Hardware performance is hampered by the Tensor G5 chip, which lacks the power for high-end gaming and suffers from occasional overheating.
- The foldable weighs 258g and faces consistent criticism for its subpar camera system compared to competitors like the Vivo X Fold 5.
- Cellular connectivity remains a notable weakness due to the performance of the integrated Exynos modem.

### Why it Matters
At a premium price point of $1,799, the Pixel 10 Pro Fold highlights the trade-offs between Google's superior software ecosystem and its lagging hardware performance. Consumers seeking a high-end foldable may find better value in competing devices that offer more reliable imaging and connectivity capabilities.
