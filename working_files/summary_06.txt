The OG reading app just got a big update
============================================================

The latest edition of The Verge’s Installer newsletter highlights the major redesign of the read-later app Instapaper 10 alongside curated recommendations for technology, gaming, and entertainment media.

### Key Points
- Instapaper 10 features a significant redesign, an improved web application, and new organization tools.
- The newsletter recommends the SwitchBot Battery Circulator Fan 2 Pro, a $120 USB-C rechargeable device.
- Featured media includes Josh Johnson’s comedy special "Elon’s Epic Ego Explained" and the cozy management game Leafy Corner.
- Brian Donohue, owner of Instapaper, shared his personal app workflow, including his upcoming Markdown editor, Emerald.
- Community recommendations include the logic game Murdoku and the movie-tracking app Paradiso.

### Why it Matters
These updates reflect a broader trend of legacy productivity tools undergoing modern refreshes to compete with newer, AI-integrated software. For users, these recommendations highlight how niche, well-designed applications continue to provide value in an increasingly crowded digital ecosystem.
