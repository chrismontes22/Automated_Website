Yearn for the Tern: Apple Has a New Daddy
============================================================

John Ternus has officially succeeded Tim Cook as Apple CEO, taking leadership of the technology giant just days before its highly anticipated annual iPhone launch event on September 9.

### Key Points
- John Ternus, formerly Apple’s senior vice president of hardware engineering, assumed the CEO role on Tuesday after 25 years with the company.
- Tim Cook has transitioned to the role of executive chair, maintaining a senior influence within the organization.
- The company faces significant market challenges, including a global RAM shortage, rising component costs, and intense competition in the artificial intelligence sector.
- Apple is expected to unveil new hardware next week, with industry analysts projecting potential price increases of $200 to $250 for the upcoming iPhone 18 lineup.
- Reports suggest Apple may introduce its first foldable smartphone, a move intended to capture market share from competitors like Samsung and Google.

### Why it Matters
The leadership transition marks a strategic pivot toward a product-focused approach as Apple navigates a volatile market defined by rising "chipflation" and the rapid evolution of artificial intelligence. Ternus must balance the company's hardware legacy with the urgent need to remain competitive against rivals who have gained significant ground in AI integration.
