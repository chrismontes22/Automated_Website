Apple Faces $5.7 Billion Patent Infringement Verdict Over iPhone And Apple Watch Haptics
============================================================

A federal jury in San Diego ordered Apple to pay $5.7 billion in damages after finding the company infringed on two patents related to haptic vibration technology.

### Key Points
- Taction Technology sued Apple in 2021, alleging unauthorized use of its patented vibration innovations in the Taptic Engine.
- The jury verdict concerns haptic technology integrated into Apple’s iPhone and Apple Watch product lines.
- Jurors determined that Apple’s patent infringement was not willful, potentially impacting future penalty calculations.
- Apple confirmed plans to appeal the decision following the conclusion of the trial in the Southern District of California.

### Why it Matters
This massive damages award highlights the significant legal and financial risks major technology firms face regarding intellectual property rights in hardware components. The outcome of the pending appeal will be closely watched by the industry as it could set a precedent for how patent infringement is valued in consumer electronics.
