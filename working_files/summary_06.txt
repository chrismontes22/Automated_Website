Boox’s tiny Picco e-reader should land in November
============================================================

Boox has unveiled new details about its upcoming Picco e-reader, a compact 3.97-inch device featuring a touchscreen, physical page-turn buttons, and a Linux-based operating system launching this November.

### Key Points
- The Picco features a 3.97-inch touchscreen display and customizable physical buttons for navigation.
- Unlike previous Boox products, the device runs on a stripped-down, Linux-based operating system rather than Android.
- Storage relies entirely on a microSD card, with file transfers supported via USB-C or a companion WiFi app.
- The device lacks the magnetic phone-attachment system found on competing Xteink X4 models.
- Additional built-in software includes a timer, a to-do list, and a reading statistics tracker.

### Why it Matters
The Picco represents a strategic shift for Boox into the niche market for ultra-portable, distraction-free reading devices. By opting for a closed Linux system over Android, the company is prioritizing a simplified user experience over the extensive customization typically found in its larger e-ink tablets.
