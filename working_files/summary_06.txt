China targets 2030 for its national blockchain network buildout
============================================================

China has established a 2030 deadline to complete a national blockchain network, aiming to integrate the technology into trade, manufacturing, and agriculture under its latest digital development plan.

### Key Points
- The Central Cyberspace Affairs Commission released the 15th Five-Year Plan for National Informatization to guide digital infrastructure growth.
- Strategic implementation will focus on trade and finance in the Yangtze River Delta and industrial applications in the Chengdu–Chongqing region.
- The initiative mandates the development of stronger national technical standards to facilitate secure data circulation and trading.
- While promoting blockchain as a core technological priority, the Chinese government maintains a strict ban on all cryptocurrency-related business activities.

### Why it Matters
This initiative signals China's commitment to establishing blockchain as a foundational layer for its domestic digital economy and industrial modernization. By standardizing this technology, the government aims to enhance operational efficiency across key sectors while maintaining centralized control over digital infrastructure.
