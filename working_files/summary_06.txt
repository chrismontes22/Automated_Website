These are the smallest 65W GaN chargers I've used yet
============================================================

The UGREEN Nexode Air 65W and Rolling Square 65W chargers offer compact, high-performance GaN technology, providing efficient power delivery for smartphones, tablets, and laptops in a portable form factor.

### Key Points
- The UGREEN Nexode Air 65W is currently priced at $24, significantly lower than the $49 Rolling Square 65W alternative.
- Both chargers utilize Gallium Nitride (GaN) technology to achieve a size roughly half that of traditional chargers while maintaining 65W power output.
- The Rolling Square model features a textured side design for improved grip, while the Nexode Air is available in four distinct color options.
- Both devices support USB PD 3.0 power profiles and PPS, ensuring compatibility with a wide range of devices including Samsung phones and iPhones.
- The Nexode Air includes a trickle-charging feature for overnight use, while the Rolling Square emphasizes a cooling-focused design.

### Why it Matters
These compact GaN chargers represent a shift toward highly portable, universal power solutions that replace bulky traditional power bricks for mobile professionals. Choosing between these models allows consumers to balance budget-friendly pricing against specific design preferences like tactile grip or aesthetic color matching.
