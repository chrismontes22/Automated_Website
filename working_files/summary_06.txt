AppleCare One launching in the UK as theft and loss coverage expands
============================================================

Apple is launching AppleCare One in the UK, a subscription service allowing users to bundle protection for multiple devices under a single monthly payment starting at £16.99.

### Key Points
- AppleCare One covers three devices for £16.99 monthly, with additional products added for £4.99 each.
- Theft and Loss protection is expanding in the UK to include iPad and Apple Watch models.
- Existing AppleCare+ plans can be transitioned to the new bundle, with automatic refunds issued for unused annual coverage.
- Devices must be under four years old, or one year for headphones, to qualify for enrollment.
- AppleCare One limits users to a total of three claims per year across all registered devices.
- Enrollment can be completed online or via device diagnostics, typically avoiding the need for an Apple Store visit.

### Why it Matters
This expansion simplifies device management for households with multiple Apple products by consolidating insurance costs into a single, more affordable monthly subscription. It also provides UK users with broader access to theft and loss coverage, potentially increasing long-term hardware retention and service revenue for the company.
