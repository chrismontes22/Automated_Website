Kraken Users Can Register For Ōura IPO Access Through xStocks
============================================================

Payward Services has opened non-binding indications of interest for the upcoming Ōura IPO, allowing eligible Kraken and xStocks users across 110 countries to prepare for potential tokenized exposure.

### Key Points
- Eligible users can register non-binding interest in the Ōura IPO through the Kraken and xStocks ecosystem.
- Upon the company's Nasdaq listing, Payward plans to offer Ōura exposure via the tokenized ŌURAx product.
- This initiative marks the fourth IPO access project for Payward this year, following offerings for SpaceX, Bending Spoons, and Jersey Mike’s.
- The tokenized model aims to facilitate round-the-clock blockchain-based trading for newly public equities.
- Participation is subject to strict regional eligibility requirements and regulatory compliance standards.

### Why it Matters
This development highlights the ongoing convergence between traditional brokerage infrastructure and cryptocurrency exchange platforms. By integrating pre-IPO access with tokenized secondary markets, companies like Kraken are expanding the utility of blockchain-based assets for retail investors.
