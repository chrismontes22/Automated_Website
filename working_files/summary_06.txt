‘Largest Crypto Hack in History’: Binance Loses More Than $6B
============================================================

Binance has confirmed a massive security breach resulting in the theft of $6.3 billion, prompting the exchange to pause all withdrawals while pledging to reimburse affected traders.

### Key Points
- Hackers reportedly gained access to Binance servers by compromising an employee’s laptop.
- The exchange has committed to covering all user losses using its secured reserves and market purchases.
- Blockchain research firms TRM and Chainalysis have linked the attack to the North Korean group TraderTraitor.
- Binance is currently coordinating with the FBI and the US Secret Service to investigate the incident.
- Cryptocurrency prices declined across the market following the announcement of the record-breaking theft.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the significant security risks inherent in the largely unregulated cryptocurrency market, where deposits lack the federal insurance protections afforded to traditional banking customers. The scale of this theft underscores the growing sophistication of cyberattacks and raises urgent questions regarding the long-term stability of major digital asset exchanges.
