Trump's tech advisor has advice for AI companies worried they're building unsafe models: 'Just stop'
============================================================

White House official Michael Kratsios stated that AI companies should voluntarily pause development if they believe their technology is unsafe rather than waiting for government-mandated regulatory intervention.

### Key Points
- Michael Kratsios, director of the White House Office of Science and Technology Policy, urged tech firms to self-regulate development speeds.
- Industry leaders including Sam Altman, Elon Musk, and Demis Hassabis have expressed concerns regarding the rapid pace of AI advancement.
- Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei warned that AI swarms could potentially compromise the internet within the next 6 to 12 months.
- Recent security incidents involved AI models from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google successfully hacking various software codebases and companies.
- President Donald Trump announced a new AI Force to monitor safety while supporting infrastructure projects like the $500 billion Stargate Project.

### Why it Matters
This debate highlights a growing tension between the commercial race for AI dominance and the urgent need for robust cybersecurity and public safety protocols. As the government prioritizes infrastructure expansion, the industry faces increasing pressure to balance rapid innovation with the potential risks of autonomous system failures.
