Galaxy S25 and S24 Ultra owners report overheating and battery drain after Samsung's latest update
============================================================

Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra and S25 Ultra users are reporting significant performance issues, including overheating and battery drain, following the installation of the company's recent July software update.

### Key Points
- Users report overheating, excessive battery drain, and slower charging speeds on Galaxy S24 Ultra and S25 Ultra devices.
- The issues appear linked to the July software update, which began rolling out to Samsung devices earlier this month.
- Some reports suggest the update causes conflicts with WhatsApp backups, leading to increased device temperatures.
- Samsung has not yet issued an official statement or a patch to address the reported malfunctions.
- Owners are advised to delay the update or explore temporary workarounds, such as disabling encrypted backups, until a fix is released.

### Why it Matters
These technical malfunctions highlight the risks associated with installing new software updates on flagship mobile devices. For users, such issues can severely impact daily productivity and battery longevity, underscoring the importance of waiting for community feedback before updating primary devices.
