Tesla-SpaceX Merger Being Considered
============================================================

Elon Musk has confirmed that special committees at Tesla and SpaceX are actively evaluating a potential merger that could combine two companies with a combined $3.5 trillion valuation.

### Key Points
- Elon Musk stated that special committees at both companies are currently examining the feasibility of a potential merger.
- A successful deal would require approval from at least 50% of Tesla shareholders, a threshold Musk described as a significant hurdle.
- SpaceX reported $7.5 billion in revenue from its cloud computing division and $5.2 billion from Starlink, despite an overall year-to-date loss of $1.1 billion.
- Musk cited potential synergies in artificial intelligence, software development, and chip technology as primary motivations for combining the two entities.
- No formal agreement on transaction terms has been reached, and Musk emphasized that there is no guarantee a merger will proceed.

### Why it Matters
A merger between these two industry giants would create a massive, vertically integrated conglomerate spanning automotive, aerospace, and artificial intelligence sectors. This consolidation could fundamentally reshape the tech and energy markets, though it faces significant regulatory scrutiny and shareholder approval challenges.
