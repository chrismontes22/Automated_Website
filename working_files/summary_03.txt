The Only Thing Capable of Uniting Bernie and Bannon
============================================================

A bipartisan coalition of progressive and conservative figures, including Bernie Sanders and Steve Bannon, has united to demand stricter federal regulation and oversight of artificial intelligence development.

### Key Points
- Senator Bernie Sanders and strategist Steve Bannon appeared at the "Pro-Human Assembly" to advocate for public control over AI technology.
- Bipartisan legislative efforts include an AI-safety bill from Senators Ted Cruz, John Thune, and Amy Klobuchar, alongside a Department of Energy oversight proposal.
- Former President Donald Trump opposes these regulatory efforts, labeling AI safety concerns as a "hoax" and emphasizing the technology's economic potential.
- The Future of Life Institute, a nonprofit backed by a $650 million donation from Vitalik Buterin, organized the event to promote human-centric AI development.
- Participants included labor officials, faith leaders, and economists concerned about potential mass workforce displacement and existential risks.

### Why it Matters
This unusual political alignment signals a significant shift in the legislative landscape as lawmakers attempt to address the rapid advancement of artificial intelligence. The emergence of this cross-party coalition could complicate industry efforts to avoid regulation, potentially forcing a major confrontation between federal policymakers and major technology companies.
