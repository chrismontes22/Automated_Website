Quote of the day by Netscape co-founder Marc Andreessen: 'Software is eating the world' — a pithy assessment of the modern tech landscape
============================================================

Venture capitalist Marc Andreessen’s influential essay on software’s economic dominance remains a defining perspective on how digital transformation continues to reshape traditional industries and global business landscapes today.

### Key Points
- Marc Andreessen, co-founder of Netscape and Andreessen Horowitz, famously argued that software companies are increasingly disrupting and replacing traditional industry sectors.
- The 2011 essay cited the collapse of Borders as a primary example of a legacy retailer failing to prioritize its online business strategy against Amazon.
- Andreessen noted that software-driven business models allow companies like Amazon to scale rapidly by leveraging core digital engines to sell diverse products.
- Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang has recently expanded on this thesis, suggesting that the rise of artificial intelligence will eventually supersede traditional software development.
- The technology sector continues to face cyclical market skepticism, with current AI advancements threatening to render existing software giants redundant.

### Why it Matters
This perspective highlights the ongoing necessity for legacy businesses to adapt to digital-first models to avoid obsolescence in a rapidly evolving economy. As AI begins to reshape the software landscape, companies must navigate these technological shifts to maintain their market relevance and competitive advantage.
