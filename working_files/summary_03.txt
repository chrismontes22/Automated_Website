AI and Data Centers Will Impact Midterm Voting, According to Experts
============================================================

A new ABC News, Washington Post, and Ipsos poll reveals that 57% of Americans view artificial intelligence negatively, potentially impacting upcoming midterm election results for political candidates.

### Key Points
- A majority of Americans (57%) believe AI is harmful to society, while 67% feel the government is failing to regulate the technology effectively.
- Public opposition to the construction of new data centers is high, with 66% of respondents opposing them nationally and 72% opposing them in their own communities.
- Concerns regarding AI include potential job losses, economic inequality, psychological dependency, and the risk of election interference.
- Lawmakers are increasingly linking data centers to rising consumer energy costs, with mentions of electricity bills in congressional newsletters surging significantly between 2025 and 2026.
- Political strategists warn that candidates who supported data center construction without transparency may face severe electoral consequences in key races.

### Why it Matters
The growing public backlash against AI and data centers has transformed these technologies into significant bipartisan political liabilities ahead of the midterm elections. Candidates must now navigate voter concerns over resource consumption and economic stability, signaling that AI regulation will remain a central issue in future election cycles.
