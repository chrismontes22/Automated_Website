AI Models Built From Rat Brains Just Got Closer to Reality
============================================================

Amazon Web Services has launched a limited preview of The Biological Computing Company’s AI model, which utilizes neural patterns from rat brain cells to enhance video generation efficiency.

### Key Points
- The Biological Computing Company (TBC) uses rat brain cells and human stem cells on 3Brain silicon arrays to derive computational patterns for software.
- TBC claims its technology can generate video up to five times faster than standard open-source models while reducing inference costs.
- Founded in Baltimore, the startup has raised over $50 million in total funding, including a recent $25 million round.
- Select AWS enterprise customers can now access the technology, which aims to improve the efficiency of existing generative AI video models.
- The company maintains research and development laboratories in San Francisco, employing approximately 35 staff members.

### Why it Matters
This integration signals a growing interest in biological computing as a viable method to improve the efficiency and performance of resource-heavy generative AI models. If successful at scale, this approach could provide a sustainable alternative to traditional math-based algorithms by leveraging the natural processing capabilities of living neural networks.
