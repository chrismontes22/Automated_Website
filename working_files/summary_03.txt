Look Out for This iPhone Duo Preorder Scam That Can Steal Your Data
============================================================

Cybersecurity researchers at Malwarebytes have identified a sophisticated phishing scam targeting iPhone users with fake iPhone Duo preorder advertisements that deploy the dangerous DarkSword exploit chain.

### Key Points
- The scam uses a deceptive website to trick users into providing personal information under the guise of a $500 discount on the upcoming iPhone Duo.
- The DarkSword exploit targets iPhones running iOS 18.4 through 18.6.2, allowing attackers to steal sensitive data simply by loading a malicious webpage.
- Stolen data includes passwords, messages, call history, photos, and cryptocurrency wallet information.
- Apple has released security updates to patch the vulnerability, and users are urged to install the latest iOS version immediately.
- Victims who interacted with the site should reboot their devices and change passwords for all financial, email, and Apple accounts.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the critical importance of maintaining updated software to defend against "zero-click" exploits that bypass traditional user interaction. Failing to apply security patches leaves devices vulnerable to comprehensive data theft, including the loss of sensitive financial and cryptocurrency assets.
