CFTC staffing cuts test regulator as crypto markets expand
============================================================

The Commodity Futures Trading Commission faces significant oversight challenges as staffing levels drop and enforcement actions decline despite the rapid expansion of cryptocurrency and prediction markets.

### Key Points
- CFTC staffing levels fell 21% by the end of 2025 compared to the previous decade's average, with a 22% decline occurring between January 2024 and January 2025.
- Enforcement actions in 2025 dropped nearly 80% below the annual average recorded over the prior decade.
- The agency has recently rolled back or closed cases against crypto entities including Gemini, Celsius, FTX, and WorldWideMarkets Inc.
- Chairman Michael Selig is currently the sole member of the five-person commission, complicating the development of new rules for digital assets.
- The Government Accountability Office plans to launch an investigation into the agency's workforce reductions and enforcement capacity in December 2026.
- The CFTC aims to hire approximately 100 employees in mission-critical roles by the end of 2026 to address its growing regulatory responsibilities.

### Why it Matters
Reduced regulatory oversight in the rapidly growing crypto and prediction-market sectors creates uncertainty regarding market integrity and consumer protection. The agency's ability to effectively police these high-volume financial environments remains a critical concern for lawmakers and market participants alike.
