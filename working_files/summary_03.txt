Is It Even Worth Buying Tech During Early Holiday Sales? Here’s What We Recommend
============================================================

A new CNET survey reveals that average US holiday tech spending has dropped to $808 this year, down $123 from last year due to inflation and rising costs.

### Key Points
- Average holiday tech budgets for US adults fell from $931 last year to $808 this year.
- Rising costs for everyday essentials like gas and utilities are limiting discretionary spending on new electronics.
- Global RAM shortages are driving up hardware and service prices, reducing the frequency of deep holiday discounts.
- Consumers are increasingly choosing to keep existing devices longer due to a lack of significant innovation in new product releases.
- Experts suggest prioritizing essential replacements, such as broken TVs or laptops, over nonessential upgrades during the holiday season.

### Why it Matters
The shift in consumer spending reflects a broader trend where high living costs and stagnant innovation are turning premium tech into luxury items. This change forces retailers to adjust their holiday strategies, as shoppers become more selective and less likely to pursue annual device upgrades.
