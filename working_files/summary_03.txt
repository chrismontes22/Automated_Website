PHP2Shell Exploitation Hits Government Entity Amid Global Scanning and Signs of AI-Assisted Hacking
============================================================

Palo Alto Networks Unit 42 has retracted its claim linking a European government agency breach to PHP2Shell vulnerabilities, even as global exploitation of the flaws continues.

### Key Points
- Palo Alto Networks Unit 42 clarified that while a government agency was compromised via a vulnerable PHP application, they cannot confirm PHP2Shell (CVE-2026-109618/109619) involvement.
- CYFIRMA identified a suspected North Korea-linked campaign using PHP2Shell to probe 216 hosts across global financial, government, and aviation sectors.
- Researchers observed attackers using agentic AI to automate post-exploitation tasks, including Active Directory reconnaissance and credential harvesting.
- Security firms, including ReliaQuest and Datadog, have provided detection methods for PHP2Shell, which can cause memory exhaustion and Apache worker crashes.
- Bitsight reports that approximately 37 million internet-facing systems remain potentially exposed, necessitating direct PHP updates to mitigate the remote code execution risks.

### Why it Matters
The widespread nature of these vulnerabilities poses a significant threat to global infrastructure, as PHP powers a vast portion of the modern web. The emergence of agentic AI in these attacks highlights a shift toward more sophisticated, automated exploitation that can rapidly escalate from initial access to domain-wide compromise.
