Kraken Parent Company Payward Delays IPO
============================================================

Payward, the parent company of the cryptocurrency exchange Kraken, has postponed its highly anticipated initial public offering until at least the second quarter of 2027 due to market volatility.

### Key Points
- Payward previously filed confidentially with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission in November 2025.
- The company reported $508 million in revenue for the second quarter, representing a 17% year-over-year increase.
- Kraken’s total number of funded accounts grew by 42% to reach 6.6 million users.
- Payward previously secured $800 million in funding, achieving a total company valuation of $20 billion.
- Other industry firms, including Grayscale, Consensys, and Ledger, have also delayed their respective plans for public market debuts.

### Why it Matters
This delay reflects ongoing caution among cryptocurrency firms regarding the stability of public equity markets despite recent growth in digital asset prices. The trend suggests that major crypto companies are prioritizing internal growth and market timing over immediate public listing amid broader economic uncertainty.
