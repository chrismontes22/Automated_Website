IMF says AI investment is keeping global growth near 3%
============================================================

IMF Managing Director Kristalina Georgieva reports that AI infrastructure investment is driving 3% annual global economic growth, with Asian hardware manufacturers seeing the most significant regional gains.

### Key Points
- Global tech companies are projected to spend between $660 billion and $690 billion on AI infrastructure throughout 2024.
- AI-related hardware and technology products now account for more than 10% of total global trade in goods.
- Advanced Asian economies could see growth increase by up to 1 percentage point due to their role in chip and robotics production.
- The IMF estimates that AI will eventually impact 40% of global jobs, with 60% of roles in wealthier nations facing potential transformation.
- The World Bank is currently funding smaller, specialized AI tools to assist farmers, educators, and healthcare systems in emerging markets.

### Why it Matters
The rapid expansion of AI infrastructure is reshaping global trade dynamics and creating a significant economic divide between tech-exporting nations and those reliant on energy imports. While the technology boosts productivity, the displacement of entry-level roles threatens to widen the employment gap between wealthy and developing countries.
