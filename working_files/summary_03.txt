My brief romance with an AI bird feeder
============================================================

The Kiwibit Bird Feeder 2 Pro offers high-quality 4K AI-powered bird identification, but users may face unexpected challenges when invasive pests like pigeons and mice disrupt the experience.

### Key Points
- The Kiwibit Bird Feeder 2 Pro features a 4K HDR camera and AI-driven species identification software.
- The device retails for approximately $269 and includes a solar panel for continuous power.
- Integrated features include mobile alerts, educational species information, and an audible Nuisance Animal Alarm.
- Persistent visits from larger birds, such as wood pigeons, can obstruct the camera lens and attract secondary pests like mice.
- The reviewer suggests starting with simpler, non-AI feeders before investing in premium smart technology to avoid potential maintenance and pest issues.

### Why it Matters
Smart bird feeders represent a growing niche in consumer home technology, blending wildlife observation with advanced computer vision. However, this experience highlights that hardware capabilities cannot always overcome the practical realities of managing local wildlife and pest control in a residential garden.
