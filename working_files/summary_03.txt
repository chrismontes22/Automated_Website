10 of the US' youngest billionaires in 2026 — and how they got their fortunes
============================================================

**Forbes' latest list of America's 400 richest billionaires reveals a surge in young wealth, with seven of the ten youngest members earning their fortunes through the artificial intelligence boom.**

### Key Points
- The number of billionaires under 40 on the Forbes 400 list increased to 10 this year, up from four in 2023.
- Seven of these young billionaires are founders of AI companies, including four cofounders from Anthropic, which is valued at $1.5 trillion.
- Lukas Walton remains the only billionaire on the list to inherit his fortune, while the other nine are self-made entrepreneurs.
- Companies represented by these young billionaires include Anthropic, OpenAI, Surge AI, Cognition, Robinhood, and the defense-tech firm Anduril.
- The minimum net worth required to qualify for the 2024 Forbes 400 list reached a record high of $4.4 billion.

### Why it Matters
The dominance of AI founders among the youngest billionaires signals a massive shift in wealth creation driven by rapid technological innovation rather than traditional inheritance. This trend highlights the immense market valuation of AI infrastructure and its growing influence on the global economy.
