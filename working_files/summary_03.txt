Swaptopus inches towards reality as CATL reveals new semi truck battery
============================================================

CATL and Octopus Energy have launched a joint venture called Swaptopus to develop a European battery-swapping network for commercial trucks using the new TECTRANS II battery platform.

### Key Points
- The TECTRANS II battery system features a 170 Wh/kg energy density, allowing for 0.6 tonnes of additional payload capacity.
- The platform supports up to 1,000 km of range and achieves an 80% charge in 25 minutes using megawatt-level fast charging.
- The system utilizes a full-tab low-impedance design to reach 96% round-trip efficiency, reducing energy losses during operations.
- A Memorandum of Understanding with DHL Group aims to accelerate the decarbonization of European road freight through this collaborative ecosystem.
- The proposed network intends to support over 300,000 electric trucks and unlock more than £30 billion in private investment.

### Why it Matters
This partnership addresses critical barriers to commercial fleet electrification by combining modular battery technology with a scalable swapping infrastructure. By integrating logistics demand with energy storage, the initiative provides a practical pathway for major shipping firms to meet net-zero emission targets.
