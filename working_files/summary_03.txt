Google confirms Pixel 11 price increase, reveals ‘dedicated’ effort to make Android more RAM-efficient
============================================================

Google is set to increase prices across its Pixel hardware lineup, including the upcoming Pixel 11 series, due to a severe, supplier-driven surge in global RAM memory costs.

### Key Points
- Google Vice President Shakil Barkat confirmed that rising memory costs, which have increased sixfold since 2025, will force dynamic price adjustments across the Pixel portfolio.
- The Pixel 11 and Pixel 11 Pro are rumored to see a $100 price increase, with some models potentially featuring reduced RAM capacity to manage supply constraints.
- Google plans to mitigate the impact on consumers through trade-in offers, bundled perks, and increased storage options for new devices.
- The company is actively engineering software and Android optimizations to reduce memory requirements, aiming to maintain performance on devices with less RAM.
- Existing devices, including the mid-range Pixel 10a, may also face price changes as the company adjusts to current market realities.

### Why it Matters
These price adjustments signal a shift in Google's hardware strategy as the company struggles to balance premium performance with the rising costs of essential components. Consumers should anticipate higher entry prices for upcoming smartphones and potential cost increases for current models already on the market.
