Agnikul sees global appetite grow as satellite firms line up for dedicated launches
============================================================

India is positioning itself as a global hub for satellite launches as Chennai-based start-up Agnikul Cosmos reports rising international demand for dedicated, precision-orbit small satellite deployment services.

### Key Points
- Agnikul Cosmos receives one to two launch enquiries weekly, with a significant portion originating from international satellite operators.
- The company aims to scale operations to 100 missions annually by utilizing reusable launch vehicles capable of 10–12 flights each.
- Dedicated launches provide economic value by placing payloads directly into specific orbits, reducing the need for onboard propulsion hardware and fuel.
- Agnikul is expanding manufacturing and testing infrastructure in Tamil Nadu to support operations from launch sites in Sriharikota and Kulasekarapattinam.
- The Indian space sector is expected to see a surge in commercial constellation deployments between 2026 and 2027 following recent policy reforms.

### Why it Matters
The shift toward dedicated, high-frequency launch services allows small-satellite operators to optimize mission life and reduce hardware complexity. By leveraging reusability and domestic infrastructure, Indian private firms are challenging traditional cost-per-kilogram metrics to capture a larger share of the global commercial space market.
