Cloud startup Volta claims $10B AI lab deal for Norway bit barn
============================================================

AMD has acquired AI chip startup Taalas to enhance inference performance by etching machine learning models directly into silicon, achieving speeds of up to 17,000 tokens per second.

### Key Points
- AMD purchased Taalas to integrate AI models into custom silicon, significantly boosting inference efficiency.
- Early technical demonstrations show the hardware processing up to 17,000 tokens per second.
- The acquisition aims to optimize model-specific integrated circuits for high-performance AI workloads.
- Taalas specializes in hardware-level acceleration that bypasses traditional software-based model execution.

### Why it Matters
This acquisition signals a strategic shift toward hardware-level AI optimization to overcome the latency and power limitations of general-purpose processors. By embedding models directly into silicon, AMD could gain a significant competitive advantage in the rapidly expanding market for high-speed, efficient AI inference hardware.
