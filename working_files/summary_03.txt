Smart rings are looking like my kind of AI gadget
============================================================

Sandbar is developing the Stream, a new smart ring designed to simplify AI interaction by allowing users to dictate text and take notes discreetly without using their smartphones.

### Key Points
- The Stream smart ring features a microphone, battery, and Bluetooth connectivity to facilitate hands-free dictation and AI assistant access.
- CEO Mina Fahmi demonstrated the device’s ability to seamlessly dictate text into both Mac and iPhone platforms using a proprietary firmware update.
- The device includes a "whisper mode" designed to capture audio accurately in public settings without requiring the user to speak loudly.
- Sandbar plans to ship the Stream later this year, joining other emerging wearable competitors like the Index 01 and Vocci.
- The technology aims to reduce reliance on smartphone interfaces by offloading complex processing to connected devices while keeping the hardware unobtrusive.

### Why it Matters
Smart rings represent a shift toward more subtle, wearable AI hardware that prioritizes efficient input over replacing the smartphone entirely. By streamlining how users capture thoughts and interact with digital assistants, these devices could significantly reduce the friction associated with mobile text entry and voice commands.
