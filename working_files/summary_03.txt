Midterm elections 2026: all the news
============================================================

The 2026 midterm elections are highlighting significant voter opposition to data center expansion and AI development, shaping key congressional races and influencing future federal technology regulation.

### Key Points
- Approximately 76 percent of voters polled by NBC News expressed opposition to the presence of data centers within their local communities.
- Democratic candidates have significantly increased their focus on Big Tech, with campaign ads mentioning the industry rising from two in 2020 to 154 between 2025 and 2026.
- Tech billionaires have contributed over $340 million to oppose California’s Proposition 40, a ballot measure proposing a 5 percent tax on billionaire wealth.
- Elon Musk has donated at least $276 million to Republican groups, including $158 million to the Senate Leadership Fund, to support the party's midterm efforts.
- OpenAI established an Election Operations Center to monitor misinformation and platform abuse throughout the voting period.

### Why it Matters
The outcome of these elections will determine the legislative environment for AI and data center infrastructure, potentially leading to stricter federal oversight and new tax policies. As tech companies face increased scrutiny from both voters and lawmakers, the industry must navigate a shifting political landscape that could fundamentally alter its operational and financial future.
