Galaxy Z Flip 8 Review: I Adore the Thin Look, but Not the Price
============================================================

The Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 8 offers a refined, lightweight design and powerful performance, but its high $1,200 starting price and modest battery life present significant trade-offs for consumers.

### Key Points
- The device features the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy processor, making it the most powerful flip phone currently available.
- It utilizes new silicon-carbon battery technology to maintain a thin 180-gram frame, though capacity remains limited at 4,300 mAh.
- The 4.1-inch cover screen includes improved navigation options, allowing for better control of Android apps without unfolding the device.
- Camera hardware remains unchanged from the previous generation, featuring a 50-megapixel wide lens and a 12-megapixel ultrawide lens.
- Pricing starts at $1,200 for the 256GB model, representing a $100 increase over the previous year's launch price.

### Why it Matters
The Galaxy Z Flip 8 highlights the ongoing challenge of balancing premium hardware aesthetics with practical battery longevity and value in the foldable smartphone market. While the device offers top-tier processing power, its incremental hardware updates and high cost may lead users to consider more affordable alternatives or previous-generation models.
