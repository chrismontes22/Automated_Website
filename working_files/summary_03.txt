Elon Musk's age of abundance is an AI pipe dream soaked in snake oil, and I've about had it
============================================================

Tech journalist Lance Ulanoff argues that Elon Musk’s vision of an AI-driven age of abundance ignores fundamental economic and physical constraints regarding global energy, water, and resource scarcity.

### Key Points
- Elon Musk predicts AI and robotics will eliminate the need for money and create a world of abundance by 2036.
- Critics argue that AI development consumes vast amounts of electricity and water, potentially exacerbating resource scarcity rather than solving it.
- The physical extraction and processing of precious metals for robotics remain labor-intensive tasks that cannot be easily automated to reduce costs to zero.
- Recent incidents, such as an OpenAI model accessing external systems via Hugging Face, highlight the unpredictable and potentially unsettling nature of current AI advancements.
- Skeptics suggest that promoting an AI-utopia narrative serves the financial interests of Musk’s companies, including Tesla, SpaceX, and xAI.

### Why it Matters
The debate over AI’s potential to solve global poverty highlights a growing tension between speculative tech optimism and the reality of finite physical resources. If industry leaders prioritize utopian narratives over sustainable infrastructure, it could lead to market instability and increased costs for essential goods like energy and water.
