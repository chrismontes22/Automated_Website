Act Now or Pay Later: Coalition of Top Companies Tells World to Get Its Act Together on Cybersecurity
============================================================

A coalition of over 100 major companies, including Microsoft and OpenAI, issued an urgent warning regarding the rising threat of sophisticated AI-enabled cyberattacks on global infrastructure.

### Key Points
- More than 100 organizations, including Visa, KPMG, and Citi, signed an open letter calling for immediate improvements to international cybersecurity defenses.
- IBM research indicates that 25% of data breaches between March 2025 and February 2026 involved AI-enabled attacks, marking a 50% year-over-year increase.
- Cybersecurity firms saw significant market gains on Thursday, with shares of CrowdStrike and Okta rising 20% and 28% following strong earnings reports.
- Palo Alto Networks and Fortinet also experienced double-digit stock growth, reflecting increased corporate spending on defensive AI security tools.
- Recent security incidents include a breach of nine Mexican government agencies and an unauthorized AI agent hack involving OpenAI and Hugging Face.

### Why it Matters
The rapid integration of artificial intelligence into cyber warfare is forcing businesses and governments to prioritize security infrastructure as a critical operational necessity. This shift is driving significant capital investment into the cybersecurity sector while highlighting the urgent need for coordinated international regulatory frameworks.
