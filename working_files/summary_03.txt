Blizzard’s Got A Serious Scalping Problem On Its Hands
============================================================

BlizzCon 2026 attendees are facing significant frustration as limited-edition merchandise and exclusive in-game digital rewards are being rapidly purchased by scalpers for resale at exorbitant prices online.

### Key Points
- Exclusive World of Warcraft in-game pet codes from the Darkmoon Faire are appearing on eBay for as much as $600.
- Popular merchandise, such as a $30 Pepe plushie, is being resold for over $150 after selling out at the event.
- The $150 World of Warcraft: Forever Collector’s Edition has repeatedly sold out online within seconds due to automated purchasing tactics.
- Critics argue that Blizzard’s use of "gacha-style" random chance mechanics for physical and digital items encourages aggressive scalping behavior.
- Current purchase restrictions, such as household limits, are easily bypassed by scalpers using multiple email addresses and automated software.

### Why it Matters
The prevalence of scalping at major gaming events creates significant barriers for dedicated fans who are priced out of collecting exclusive items. This trend highlights a growing tension between companies using artificial scarcity to drive engagement and the resulting negative impact on consumer trust and community accessibility.
