Musk opens the door to a SpaceX-Tesla merger as shareholders weigh a deal
============================================================

Elon Musk has confirmed that independent committees are evaluating a potential merger between SpaceX and Tesla following the rocket company's first profitable quarter as a public entity.

### Key Points
- SpaceX reported a $4.4 billion net profit for the third quarter, driven by $7.6 billion in revenue from its "Super Intelligence" AI division.
- The company's total quarterly revenue reached $14.6 billion, a 161 percent increase compared to the previous year.
- SpaceX shares rose nearly five percent in after-hours trading following Musk's comments regarding the potential corporate combination.
- Independent directors at both companies are currently studying the merger proposal, though no formal terms have been established.
- Anthropic remains a major client, having committed $45 billion over three years for computing power at SpaceX’s Colossus data centers.

### Why it Matters
A merger between these two industry giants would create a massive conglomerate focused on AI, robotics, and space-based computing. Investors face significant uncertainty, however, as the deal requires approval from a majority of all Tesla shareholders and must navigate complex conflicts of interest.
