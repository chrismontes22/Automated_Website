Not what I wanted to hear: rumors now say Galaxy S27 Ultra could miss a huge upgrade
============================================================

New reports suggest Samsung may exclude LPDDR6 RAM and UFS 5.0 storage upgrades from the upcoming Galaxy S27 Ultra due to concerns regarding rising production costs and pricing.

### Key Points
- Tipster Lanzuk claims LPDDR6 RAM and UFS 5.0 storage may be reserved for limited edition flagship devices from various manufacturers rather than the Galaxy S27 Ultra.
- The potential exclusion of these memory and storage upgrades contradicts earlier rumors from April that suggested these features would be included in the next flagship.
- Samsung is reportedly concerned that integrating these advanced components would significantly increase mass production costs and the final retail price of the device.
- Additional rumors indicate that Samsung may freeze other hardware improvements, including camera and display technology, for the standard Galaxy S27 lineup.
- UFS 5.0 technology was previously expected to boost sequential performance to 10.8GB per second to better support intensive AI processing tasks.

### Why it Matters
This shift highlights the ongoing challenge manufacturers face in balancing cutting-edge hardware innovation with the need to keep consumer device prices competitive. If Samsung opts to forgo these upgrades, it could signal a broader industry trend of prioritizing cost management over incremental performance gains in flagship smartphones.
