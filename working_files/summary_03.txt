Big Red gets cozy with Google to offer TPUs to AI customers
============================================================

GitHub is expanding its infrastructure by four million cores to maintain service stability as the platform re-architects its systems to handle the surge in AI-generated content and traffic.

### Key Points
- GitHub is adding four million compute cores to prevent service outages caused by the rapid increase in AI-driven platform activity.
- The infrastructure overhaul aims to manage the "AI slop-pocalypse," referring to the massive influx of automated code and content submissions.
- This architectural shift reflects the broader industry trend of scaling backend resources to support the growing computational demands of generative AI tools.

### Why it Matters
This expansion highlights the immense strain that AI-driven automation is placing on global software development platforms. As companies like GitHub scale their infrastructure to accommodate these workloads, the move underscores the critical need for robust, high-capacity systems to maintain the integrity of the open-source ecosystem.
