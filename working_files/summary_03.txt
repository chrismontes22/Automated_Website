58% of ad execs expect agentic buying to hit scale within a year, according to IAB Europe research
============================================================

European advertising executives are increasingly adopting agentic ad buying, with 58% of surveyed leaders expecting these AI-driven systems to reach operational scale within the next twelve months.

### Key Points
- A survey of 50 European ad executives found that 86% of large organizations with over 500 employees already utilize advanced agentic systems.
- Adoption remains uneven, as only 48% of companies with 500 or fewer staff have integrated similar collaborative AI-human workflows.
- Major industry players including WPP, Omnicom, CNN, and News U.K. are actively developing proprietary buyer and seller agents.
- Current AI implementation focuses on low-risk tasks, with 76% of respondents prioritizing reporting, analysis, and dashboard management.
- Security and privacy concerns remain the primary barriers to wider adoption, leading many firms to maintain strict human oversight of automated processes.
- Investment in AI marketing technology is rising, with 59% of organizations planning to increase their budgets in 2026 compared to 2025.

### Why it Matters
The shift toward agentic advertising signals a fundamental change in how media is bought and sold, moving from manual execution to automated, data-driven workflows. As firms increase investment to improve efficiency and measurement, the industry faces a critical transition period where proving return on investment will determine the long-term viability of these AI tools.
