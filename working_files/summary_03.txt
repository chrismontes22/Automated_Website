Your dusty Nest and Home speakers just got a surprise firmware lifeline
============================================================

Google is rolling out firmware update 3.79.550760 to older Nest speakers, including the original Google Home, Home Mini, Nest Mini, and Nest Audio, via its Home Preview Program.

### Key Points
- Firmware version 3.79.550760 is currently available for testing through the Google Home Preview Program.
- Eligible devices include the original 2016 Google Home, Home Mini, Nest Mini, and Nest Audio.
- The Google Home Max is excluded from this specific round of firmware testing.
- Users can enroll in the update by navigating to the Preview program section within the Google Home app settings.
- A broader public release for the firmware is expected to occur over the coming weeks.

### Why it Matters
This update extends the functional lifespan of older smart speakers, allowing users to maintain their existing hardware rather than purchasing newer models. By refining software performance, Google ensures that legacy devices remain reliable tools for managing basic smart home tasks and voice commands.
