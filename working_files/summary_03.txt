‘Godfather of AI’ says billionaires like Elon Musk are right about the future of work—but he predicts mass unemployment is on its way
============================================================

AI pioneer Geoffrey Hinton and Senator Bernie Sanders warn that rapid advancements in artificial intelligence could lead to massive workforce displacement and significant long-term economic instability for millions.

### Key Points
- Geoffrey Hinton warns that tech companies are prioritizing short-term profits by developing AI systems specifically designed to replace human workers.
- Senator Bernie Sanders estimates that automation could displace nearly 100 million U.S. jobs across sectors ranging from manual labor to white-collar professions.
- HSBC projections suggest OpenAI may require over $207 billion in funding to sustain operations, with profitability not expected until at least 2030.
- Senator Mark Warner cautioned that AI-driven disruption could push unemployment rates for recent college graduates as high as 25% within three years.
- While tech leaders like Jensen Huang predict a transition to a four-day workweek, experts remain divided on whether new job creation will offset losses.

### Why it Matters
The potential for widespread job displacement poses a significant challenge to global economic structures and the social value of human labor. Policymakers and industry leaders must now determine if regulatory guardrails can mitigate these risks before the technology fundamentally alters the modern workforce.
