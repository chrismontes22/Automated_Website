Will the iPhone Duo Fit in Your Pocket? Here’s How It Compares to Other iPhone Sizes
============================================================

Apple’s new $1,999 iPhone Duo foldable smartphone offers a unique hybrid design, featuring a 5.4-inch cover screen and a 7.6-inch inner display for enhanced multitasking and media consumption.

### Key Points
- The iPhone Duo measures 11.3mm thick when folded and weighs 254 grams, making it heavier than the 249-gram iPhone 18 Pro Max.
- The device utilizes TouchID for security rather than FaceID and features a matte inner display designed to reduce glare.
- It is significantly heavier and thicker than the iPhone Air, which weighs 165 grams and measures 5.6mm in thickness.
- The phone-tablet hybrid is currently priced at $1,999, positioning it as a premium, first-generation device compared to the standard iPhone 17 and 18 lineups.

### Why it Matters
The iPhone Duo represents Apple's entry into the foldable market, signaling a shift toward hardware that prioritizes screen real estate for mobile productivity. While its high price and weight may limit mass appeal, it serves as a critical test for how Apple integrates foldable technology into its existing ecosystem.
