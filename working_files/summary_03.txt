AI Off-Balance-Sheet Obligations Hit a Subprime-Like Reset Wall in 2027 and 2028
============================================================

**AI industry analysts warn that massive, unsustainable compute commitments could trigger a financial crisis similar to the 2007 subprime mortgage collapse as payment obligations reset in 2027.**

### Key Points
- Frontier AI labs and cloud providers have signed over $2.3 trillion in "take-or-pay" compute contracts that remain largely off-balance sheet.
- These contracts function like subprime "teaser" rates, where payments are deferred until data centers are completed, creating a massive future financial cliff.
- Current AI business models rely on the assumption that companies can refinance these obligations through future fundraising rounds rather than operational revenue.
- Total economic obligations for AI infrastructure are estimated at $2.1 trillion, a figure comparable to the scale of the 2007 subprime mortgage market.
- Analysts note that AI spending currently accounts for nearly half of U.S. GDP growth, making the sector highly vulnerable to a sudden contraction in capital.

### Why it Matters
The reliance on deferred, high-stakes debt to fund AI infrastructure creates a systemic risk that could destabilize the broader economy if capital markets tighten. If these companies cannot grow their revenue fast enough to meet their "reset" payment schedules, the resulting defaults could trigger a significant market downturn.
