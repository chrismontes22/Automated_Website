Binance hackers stole $6.3 billion in record-breaking crypto heist
============================================================

Binance has confirmed a record-breaking $6.3 billion cryptocurrency heist, marking the largest theft in industry history, while the exchange works with law enforcement to recover the stolen assets.

### Key Points
- Hackers stole $6.3 billion in assets, with $3 billion already frozen by the exchange.
- Binance has pledged to cover all user losses using a $6.34 billion company reserve fund.
- Blockchain analysis firms TRM and Chainalysis attribute the complex attack to the North Korean group TraderTraitor.
- The breach involved unauthorized withdrawal transactions initiated through compromised DevOps infrastructure.
- Deposits and withdrawals remain suspended as the company coordinates with law enforcement to investigate the incident.

### Why it Matters
This incident represents the largest cryptocurrency theft on record, significantly exceeding the previous $1.46 billion benchmark set by Bybit. The scale of the attack highlights critical vulnerabilities in exchange security and underscores the growing sophistication of state-sponsored cybercrime groups targeting digital financial infrastructure.
