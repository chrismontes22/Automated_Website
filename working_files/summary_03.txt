Trump’s Truth Social API Is a Blatantly Corrupt Favor to Wall Street
============================================================

Trump Media & Technology Group has launched a $100,000-per-month API service providing Wall Street firms with expedited, automated access to official government statements posted on the Truth Social platform.

### Key Points
- The Truth API allows financial institutions to ingest presidential communications into trading terminals faster than the general public can access them.
- Donald Trump maintains an 18% stake in Trump Media & Technology Group, which reported a net loss exceeding $700 million in the previous year.
- Sens. Adam Schiff and Elizabeth Warren have formally requested an SEC investigation, citing concerns over market integrity and potential presidential corruption.
- The service bypasses traditional press office channels, effectively monetizing official government communications as a private corporate product.

### Why it Matters
This arrangement raises significant ethical and legal questions regarding the privatization of public records and the potential for insider trading based on government announcements. By selling a speed advantage to wealthy investors, the administration risks undermining fair market access and blurring the lines between official duties and personal financial gain.
