The Trump Administration Is Trying to Get Musk and X out of a $137 Million EU Fine
============================================================

The United States government has filed a legal application to support Elon Musk’s challenge against a €120 million fine imposed on X by the European Commission.

### Key Points
- The U.S. Department of Justice and Department of State are seeking to intervene in the European General Court to contest the Digital Services Act penalty.
- The European Commission issued the fine in December, citing deceptive verification practices and failures in X’s advertising repository and researcher data access.
- X and Elon Musk filed appeals in February, characterizing the EU investigation as superficial and biased while disputing the interpretation of transparency obligations.
- The U.S. government argues the fine represents regulatory overreach that threatens American innovation and economic interests.
- The Digital Services Act mandates that large online platforms implement stricter measures to mitigate illegal content and improve transparency.

### Why it Matters
This intervention signals a significant escalation in geopolitical tension regarding the regulation of American technology companies operating within the European Union. The outcome of this case could establish a legal precedent for how international authorities balance national sovereignty against the enforcement of foreign digital safety laws.
