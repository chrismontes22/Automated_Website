ShieldFont fights AI scraping by handing crawlers the wrong words
============================================================

ShieldFont is a new web typography tool that protects online content from AI scrapers by displaying readable text to humans while serving decoy words to automated crawlers.

### Key Points
- Developed by Isaque Seneda and Gabriel Abrucio with support from Playtype, the tool uses font-based substitution to swap words in HTML source code.
- The technology forces AI scrapers to ingest incorrect data while browsers render the intended text for human readers.
- ShieldFont aims to increase the financial cost of large-scale data scraping by forcing attackers to use more expensive OCR or vision-based processing methods.
- The project is available on GitHub, though it requires users to accept trade-offs in SEO performance and increased browser-side processing requirements.
- Research indicates that swapping approximately 25% of a page's words can significantly degrade the meaning of text for automated language models.

### Why it Matters
This tool represents a growing trend of "creative resistance" where publishers use technical friction to combat unauthorized AI training on their intellectual property. By shifting the cost of data extraction from the creator to the scraper, it provides a potential mechanism for writers and artists to regain control over their digital content.
