AI investors or casino gamblers? What Broadcom CEO Hock Tan said that sparked sharp global AI selloff
============================================================

Broadcom CEO Hock Tan warned that excessive AI spending and speculative investor behavior could create a market bubble, triggering a significant sell-off in global technology and chip stocks.

### Key Points
- Broadcom CEO Hock Tan criticized businesses for wasting capital on experimental AI initiatives that lack clear long-term profitability.
- OpenAI reached $50 billion in annualized revenue as of September, but Tan noted the company remains unprofitable due to high training costs.
- Global AI stocks declined following the comments, with South Korea’s Kospi index falling 6% and chipmakers Samsung and SK Hynix dropping up to 9%.
- Japan’s Nikkei index fell nearly 2%, while Indian IT stocks rallied up to 5% as investors shifted away from AI-heavy portfolios.
- Tan cautioned that insufficient capital could become a major constraint if developers fail to secure customers capable of generating actual returns.

### Why it Matters
These comments highlight growing investor anxiety regarding the sustainability of the massive capital expenditures currently fueling the global AI infrastructure buildout. The market reaction underscores a potential shift in sentiment as stakeholders begin to prioritize concrete financial returns over speculative growth in generative AI models.
