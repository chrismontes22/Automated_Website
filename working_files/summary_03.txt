Coffee Break: AI Credit and Credibility Collapsing or Will the AI Booster/Doomers Power Through?
============================================================

Oracle is invoking rare force majeure clauses to delay data center payments as financial analysts warn that the AI infrastructure boom faces significant systemic risks and revenue shortfalls.

### Key Points
- Oracle CEO Larry Ellison is attempting to shield the company from expenses on the "Project Jupiter" data center in New Mexico by citing force majeure.
- A Columbia Business School report warns that AI infrastructure financing is shifting toward opaque, off-balance-sheet structures that mask correlated systemic risks.
- Moody’s projects capital expenditures for major tech firms will reach $1 trillion in 2027, threatening credit quality as companies transition to asset-heavy models.
- Satellite imagery analysis suggests that over 2 gigawatts of U.S. data center projects scheduled for 2026 completion have not yet begun land clearance.
- Anthropic is reportedly developing a predictive surveillance system to monitor activists and report potential threats to law enforcement.

### Why it Matters
The aggressive expansion of AI infrastructure is increasingly reliant on massive debt and optimistic revenue projections that may not materialize. These financial vulnerabilities, combined with the controversial ideological influence of "effective altruism" within frontier AI labs, suggest a growing disconnect between corporate promises and economic reality.
