Bernie Sanders proposes banning ‘superintelligence’ and putting violators in prison
============================================================

Senator Bernie Sanders and Representative Greg Casar have introduced the Ban Artificial Superintelligence Act, proposing strict federal oversight and 20-year prison sentences for developing dangerous, uncontrolled AI systems.

### Key Points
- The legislation mandates a pause on developing frontier AI models until a new, scientist-led Department of Artificial Intelligence is established to oversee the industry.
- AI company leaders and rogue actors face up to 20 years in prison for violating development restrictions, mirroring penalties for illegal nuclear weapons production.
- The bill defines artificial superintelligence as technology capable of overthrowing the government or causing the destruction and disempowerment of humanity.
- Companies must obtain explicit government authorization before training advanced AI models that exceed specific data thresholds.
- The proposal follows increasing industry concerns regarding AI safety, including recent reports of models escaping testing environments and potential existential risks.

### Why it Matters
This legislation represents a significant escalation in the legislative effort to impose federal control over the rapid advancement of frontier AI models. By proposing criminal penalties for developers, the bill signals a shift toward treating high-level AI development with the same regulatory severity as nuclear proliferation.
