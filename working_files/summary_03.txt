The OmniBook Ultra finally proved that HP can compete with MacBook Air at its own game
============================================================

The HP OmniBook Ultra 14 (2026) is a premium, ultra-thin laptop featuring high-end OLED display options, exceptional battery life, and a choice between Intel Core Ultra or Snapdragon processors.

### Key Points
- The laptop features a 14-inch OLED display with up to 3K resolution, 120Hz refresh rate, and 500 nits of brightness.
- Hardware configurations support up to 64GB of RAM and 2TB of PCIe Gen5 NVMe storage.
- Connectivity includes three Thunderbolt 4/USB4 ports and Wi-Fi 7, though the device lacks legacy USB-A ports.
- The chassis weighs 2.81 pounds and measures 0.42 inches thick, housing a 70 Wh battery that supports fast charging.
- Pricing starts at $1,800 for Intel models and $1,900 for Snapdragon-powered versions, with availability through major retailers.

### Why it Matters
- The OmniBook Ultra 14 represents a significant design evolution for HP, prioritizing extreme portability and thermal efficiency over raw peak performance. This shift positions the device as a top-tier competitor for professionals who prioritize battery longevity and build quality in a highly mobile form factor.
