Who are we Now? – Jessitron
============================================================

Software developers can maintain deep technical understanding and accountability in the age of AI by adopting the "Verum Factum" philosophy, which emphasizes learning through active participation and experimentation.

### Key Points
- The "Verum Factum" principle posits that true understanding comes from creating and participating in a system, rather than observing it from the outside.
- Developers should treat software as a "symmathesy," a learning system where humans, code, and tools evolve together through continuous feedback loops.
- AI agents lack the capacity for true accountability or consciousness, meaning developers must remain responsible for defining goals and verifying agent-generated code.
- To ensure reliability, developers must shift from manual coding to "artful vexations," such as rigorous property-based testing, observability, and automated verification.
- Human-centric skills like curiosity, collaboration, and building real-world community (Gemeinschaft) are essential to balance the transactional nature of modern tech work.

### Why it Matters
As AI accelerates the pace of software development, developers risk losing the deep, intuitive connection to their code that ensures system stability and long-term maintainability. By consciously acting as "Symmathecists" who guide AI tools rather than passively relying on them, professionals can retain their agency and ensure that technology serves human-centered goals.
