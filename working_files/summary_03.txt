These Sleep Headphones Are My Best Redeye Flight Hack. They Just Got a Nifty Upgrade
============================================================

Anker unveiled a new lineup of audio products at IFA in Berlin, including upgraded sleep earbuds, AI-powered note-taking devices, and clinical-grade hearing aids featuring proprietary chip technology.

### Key Points
- Anker announced the Sleep Earbuds 4 and 4 Pro, featuring a smaller ergonomic design and a touchscreen case for the Pro model.
- The SleepLab Pro speaker uses radar technology to track sleep, heart rate, and breathing without requiring wearable devices.
- The Soundcore AeroClip 2 Pro, priced at $230, utilizes an AI audio chip to transcribe and summarize meetings and calls.
- New audio hardware, including the $200 Space 2 Pro and $130 Liberty Buds 2, launches on September 22 with advanced noise cancellation.
- The new RIC Hearing Aids use the proprietary Thus chip to perform 40 billion operations per second for real-time voice isolation.

### Why it Matters
Anker is aggressively expanding its audio ecosystem by integrating proprietary AI chips into both consumer electronics and specialized accessibility hardware. This strategy signals a shift toward multifunctional devices that prioritize health tracking and productivity alongside traditional audio performance.
