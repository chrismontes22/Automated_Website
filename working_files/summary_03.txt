He Built Products at Beats, Apple and LVMH. Here’s How He Ended Up at a $1.5 Billion Startup That’s Making Your Data Safer.
============================================================

Former Apple Music executive Ian Rogers is now driving security and AI strategy at Ledger, a $1.5 billion startup focused on protecting digital assets and personal identity.

### Key Points
- Ian Rogers, who previously led Apple Music and served as LVMH’s chief digital officer, joined Ledger in 2021.
- Paris-based Ledger produces hardware crypto wallets that store private keys offline to prevent unauthorized access to digital assets.
- The company has sold over eight million devices, including its latest $179 NanoGen 5 hardware wallet.
- Rogers currently serves as chief human agency officer, focusing on the intersection of human control and AI security.
- Ledger was last valued at $1.5 billion in 2021 and utilizes secure chip technology similar to that found in credit cards.

### Why it Matters
As digital threats evolve, Ledger’s focus on hardware-based security highlights the growing need for personal sovereignty over digital assets and identity. Rogers’ transition from consumer entertainment to security infrastructure underscores a broader industry shift toward protecting users in an increasingly automated, AI-driven landscape.
