Storage infrastructure will underpin post-quantum security
============================================================

Western Digital is integrating post-quantum cryptography into enterprise hard drives to protect long-term data assets against future quantum computing threats and evolving cyber security risks in AI workflows.

### Key Points
- Enterprise data growth driven by AI requires long-term storage solutions that prioritize both massive capacity and advanced cyber resilience.
- The "harvest now, decrypt later" threat model involves attackers collecting encrypted data today to decrypt it once quantum computing capabilities mature.
- Western Digital is embedding post-quantum cryptography (PQC) into hardware to secure firmware authentication, key establishment, and diagnostic tools.
- New PQC implementations align with NIST standards, utilizing hybrid approaches that combine classical encryption with quantum-resistant algorithms.
- Hard drives are being engineered to maintain trust architectures throughout their five-year operational lifecycles, protecting data against both current and future threats.

### Why it Matters
As AI transforms data into a long-term strategic asset, organizations must ensure their storage infrastructure remains secure against future technological shifts. Integrating quantum-resistant defenses at the hardware level prevents sensitive intellectual property from becoming a long-term regulatory or operational liability.
