Cheaper, open and intelligent: Chinese AI models gain ground, as they make inroads in the US
============================================================

Chinese artificial intelligence startups like Moonshot and Zhipu are gaining significant traction among American users and companies due to their high performance, cost-effectiveness, and increasing operational efficiency.

### Key Points
- Mozilla CTO Raffi Krikorian and companies like Coinbase are adopting Chinese AI models, including Moonshot’s Kimi K3 and Zhipu’s GLM-5.2, for daily tasks.
- Chinese models are increasingly viewed as competitive alternatives to U.S.-based frontier models from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google.
- The Trump administration has accused Moonshot of using "covert" methods to build its K3 model by distilling technology from Anthropic’s Fable.
- Beijing has rejected allegations of illicit model distillation as groundless, despite ongoing U.S. efforts to restrict China's access to advanced AI chips.
- Other notable Chinese releases include Alibaba’s Qwen3.8 Max and DeepSeek’s V4, which continue to challenge established American AI products.

### Why it Matters
The adoption of Chinese AI models by U.S. firms highlights a growing market preference for affordable, high-performing alternatives that challenge the dominance of American tech giants. This trend complicates U.S. efforts to maintain a technological edge and raises significant concerns regarding intellectual property security and international trade policy.
