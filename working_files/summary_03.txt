Logitech Launches ‘Chain Reaction’ to Accelerate Supply Chain Decarbonization towards 2030 Climate Goals
============================================================

Logitech has launched Chain Reaction, a global supply chain decarbonization program designed to help 17 initial suppliers reduce carbon emissions and optimize factory operations through renewable energy aggregation.

### Key Points
- Logitech aims to halve its Scope 3 greenhouse gas emissions by 2030 through this new supplier-focused initiative.
- The program aggregates supplier purchasing power to secure affordable, high-standard renewable energy instruments.
- Participating suppliers receive technical workshops, carbon accounting tools, and factory evaluations to identify energy and mechanical efficiencies.
- Logitech will integrate carbon transparency metrics into its commercial business reviews and new business award processes.
- The company reported a 33% reduction in its total Scope 3 carbon footprint in fiscal year 2026 compared to its 2021 baseline.

### Why it Matters
This initiative shifts the focus of corporate sustainability from internal operations to the broader manufacturing supply chain where the majority of emissions often occur. By providing suppliers with technical and financial tools, Logitech aims to lower operational costs and mitigate energy price volatility while meeting its own climate targets.
