From Zuck to Musk: Big Tech’s Scandals and Scammers Are Coming to the Big Screen
============================================================

Hollywood is releasing a wave of films this fall that examine the controversies and societal impacts surrounding prominent tech figures like Mark Zuckerberg, Elon Musk, and Sam Altman.

### Key Points
- *The Social Reckoning*, directed by Aaron Sorkin and starring Jeremy Strong, premieres October 9 and explores the societal damage caused by Facebook.
- Alex Gibney’s four-hour documentary *Musk* debuts October 16, focusing on the life and business practices of the Tesla and SpaceX CEO.
- *You Can See Everything*, a documentary by Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim, examines the private life of Theranos founder Elizabeth Holmes.
- Luca Guadagnino’s biopic *Artificial*, starring as OpenAI CEO Sam Altman, is scheduled for a Christmas Day theatrical release.
- Several streaming platforms are simultaneously releasing tech-focused content, including Netflix’s series on the collapse of the FTX cryptocurrency exchange.

### Why it Matters
These films reflect a growing public appetite for critical examinations of Big Tech’s influence and the accountability of its most powerful leaders. By shifting from science fiction to real-world scandals, the industry is highlighting the tangible consequences that modern technology and corporate leadership have on daily life.
