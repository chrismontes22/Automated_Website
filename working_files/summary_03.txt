Hunter Biden launches $LAPTOP memecoin, trolls Trump by promising that 20% will be reserved for traders who lost money on president’s token
============================================================

Hunter Biden is set to launch a new memecoin titled $LAPTOP on the Base network this September, aiming to reclaim the narrative surrounding his controversial 2020 laptop scandal.

### Key Points
- The $LAPTOP token is scheduled to launch on September 9 with a total supply of 1 billion coins.
- Founders will retain 30% of the supply, which is subject to a six-month lock-up period.
- A 20% allocation is reserved for subscribers and individuals who lost money on the TRUMP memecoin.
- The project is built on Base, a blockchain network developed by the cryptocurrency exchange Coinbase.
- Public reception on social media has been largely critical, leading some exchanges to retract promotional posts.

### Why it Matters
The launch highlights the growing trend of high-profile figures leveraging political controversy to drive speculative activity in the volatile memecoin market. This development raises significant questions regarding the risks of celebrity-backed digital assets and the potential for market manipulation in the cryptocurrency sector.
