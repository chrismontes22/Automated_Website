Consulting's race to become AI native
============================================================

Major consulting firms are rapidly integrating artificial intelligence into their service models, forcing a strategic shift that increasingly blurs the traditional lines between professional advisory and technology companies.

### Key Points
- Firms like KPMG, PwC, and McKinsey are embedding AI across their operations, with some reporting that tech-focused services now account for over 40% of global revenue.
- Major consultancies are aggressively hiring thousands of data and AI professionals while introducing new technical career tracks to meet client demand for implementation.
- Partnerships with AI leaders including OpenAI, Nvidia, and Microsoft are enabling firms to automate junior-level tasks and offer clients direct access to AI-enhanced platforms.
- Despite the "AI-native" branding, industry experts note that most firms remain focused on organizational change rather than becoming pure-play technology developers.
- Research from Source Global indicates that while clients expect AI to impact service delivery, many remain skeptical about the extent of this transformation.

### Why it Matters
The shift toward AI-driven services allows consulting firms to capture higher-growth revenue streams while remaining essential intermediaries for Fortune 500 companies navigating digital transformation. However, firms must carefully balance their new technical identities with their traditional premium pricing models to avoid being commoditized as low-cost software providers.
