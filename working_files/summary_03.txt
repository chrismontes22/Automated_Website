Potato by air
============================================================

Wing’s Hummingbird 7000W-B drone, operating in partnership with Walmart, is currently providing low-cost, rapid residential delivery services that allow users to send small items to neighbors’ homes.

### Key Points
- The Wing Hummingbird 7000W-B drone features 16 propellers, a 60 mph top speed, and a 2.7-pound carrying capacity.
- FAA-authorized beyond-line-of-sight operations enable Wing to deliver over 2,000 household items from local Walmart Supercenters.
- Current service models include no delivery fees or order minimums, with deliveries often arriving in under 30 minutes.
- Wing has completed over 1 million residential deliveries, requiring verified accounts and clear delivery zones for safety.
- The service allows users to send items to third-party addresses, provided the recipient is within an eligible service area.

### Why it Matters
This service represents a significant shift in last-mile logistics by offering highly subsidized, rapid delivery that bypasses traditional gig-economy labor costs. While the convenience is high, the model raises questions regarding long-term economic sustainability and the environmental impact of using multiple drones for small, individual item shipments.
