Asha Sharma Insists There's 'More Demand Than Consoles Available' as Xbox Hardware Sales Continue to Plummet
============================================================

Microsoft reports a 2% decline in overall Xbox revenue for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027, citing ongoing hardware supply constraints and shifting content performance.

### Key Points
- Total Xbox revenue reached $5.379 billion for the quarter ending September 30, 2026, marking a 2% year-over-year decrease.
- Hardware sales fell 14% as Microsoft continues to struggle with console supply shortages and rising component costs.
- CEO Asha Sharma noted that the company is prioritizing an improved "accountability margin" through recent restructuring and studio consolidation.
- Revenue from Xbox content and services declined 2%, though Microsoft expects growth in the second quarter driven by major title releases.
- High demand for RAM from AI companies like OpenAI and Google has contributed to increased console prices and manufacturing difficulties.

### Why it Matters
The ongoing hardware supply issues and declining console sales highlight the challenges Microsoft faces in maintaining its gaming market share against rising production costs. As the industry prepares for the high-profile launch of Grand Theft Auto 6, the company's ability to meet consumer demand will be critical to its financial performance in the coming quarter.
