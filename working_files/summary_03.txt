Velaura AI raises $110M to develop power-efficient AI chips
============================================================

Velaura AI has secured $110 million in Series A funding, reaching a $1 billion valuation to advance its energy-efficient chip design technology for artificial intelligence applications.

### Key Points
- Seligman Ventures led the $110 million funding round, which included participation from the Samsung Catalyst Fund and Mayfield.
- Formerly known as Auradine, the company rebranded in March to shift its focus from bitcoin mining hardware to AI processor development.
- The company’s Titan Core suite provides pre-packaged cell libraries designed to reduce energy consumption for matrix multiplication operations by two to four times.
- Velaura is currently collaborating with multiple hyperscalers to implement its technology on advanced three- and two-nanometer manufacturing nodes.
- The new capital will be used to accelerate engineering initiatives and expand customer-facing teams for both data center and edge device markets.

### Why it Matters
As artificial intelligence models demand increasing computational power, energy efficiency has become a critical factor in both operational costs and environmental sustainability. By providing specialized building blocks that lower power consumption, Velaura aims to improve the economics of AI hardware for large-scale data centers and edge computing.
