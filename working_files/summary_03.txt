Ben Affleck Doesn’t “Worry About SkyNet” When It Comes To AI; “It’s Another Form Of Computing”
============================================================

Filmmaker Ben Affleck recently discussed the practical applications of artificial intelligence in Hollywood, emphasizing that the technology serves as a useful tool rather than an existential threat.

### Key Points
- Ben Affleck argued that AI is a form of advanced computing rather than a "Skynet" style threat to the entertainment industry.
- Netflix acquired Affleck’s AI startup for $587 million earlier this year, integrating its small team into the streaming platform.
- Affleck expressed concerns regarding AI's impact on education and "learned helplessness" rather than its potential to replace human creativity in film.
- His production company, Artist Equity, is developing new business models to ensure fair profit participation for workers amid evolving industry technologies.
- Affleck serves as an advisor for Netflix following the acquisition of his firm, which focused on developing AI tools for filmmakers.

### Why it Matters
Affleck’s perspective offers a pragmatic counter-narrative to the widespread industry anxiety surrounding AI’s potential to disrupt creative professions. His successful exit and ongoing work with Artist Equity highlight how established creators are actively shaping the integration of new technology into traditional business models.
