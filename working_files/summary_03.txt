Residents call for moratorium on AI data centres at meeting in Darwin
============================================================

Darwin residents are calling for a moratorium on new data centre developments in the Northern Territory, citing significant concerns regarding water consumption, noise pollution, and gas-powered energy usage.

### Key Points
- Twelve data centre developers are currently targeting the Northern Territory for potential projects.
- Residents and local representatives are demanding a moratorium until stricter environmental and community regulations are established.
- The Northern Territory government aims to become a global data centre hub, potentially utilizing gas from the Beetaloo Basin for power.
- A proposed $40 billion hyper-scale AI data centre at Weddell has become a focal point for community opposition.
- Critics argue that these facilities provide minimal local employment while placing excessive strain on groundwater and local infrastructure.

### Why it Matters
The conflict highlights a growing global tension between the rapid expansion of AI infrastructure and the environmental concerns of local communities. Establishing clear regulatory guardrails is essential to determine whether these projects can provide tangible economic benefits without compromising regional water security and climate goals.
