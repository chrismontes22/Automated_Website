12 celebrities who support AI
============================================================

A growing number of prominent Hollywood figures, including directors and actors, are actively investing in, founding, and integrating generative artificial intelligence tools into their creative and business ventures.

### Key Points
- Ben Affleck’s company InterPositive was acquired by Netflix for $587 million to develop AI-driven post-production tools for filmmakers.
- James Cameron joined the board of directors at Stability AI, while Martin Scorsese serves as an advisor for the AI firm Black Forest Labs.
- Ashton Kutcher and Serena Williams have launched venture capital initiatives specifically focused on funding startups within the artificial intelligence sector.
- "South Park" creators Matt Stone and Trey Parker operate Deep Voodoo, a studio specializing in deepfake technology for creative media projects.
- Natasha Lyonne and Darren Aronofsky are utilizing AI production studios to develop new films and animated series that explore the boundaries of storytelling.

### Why it Matters
The active participation of high-profile celebrities in the AI industry signals a significant shift in how Hollywood approaches emerging technology, moving from skepticism to direct integration. By positioning themselves as founders and investors, these figures are shaping the future of content production and influencing how AI tools will be standardized across the entertainment landscape.
