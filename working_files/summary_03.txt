MapQuest now shows you Flock and other license plate reader cameras on your route
============================================================

MapQuest has introduced a new mobile app feature that allows users to identify the locations of automated license plate readers along their planned driving routes for increased privacy.

### Key Points
- The new "surveillance layer" identifies cameras manufactured by companies including Flock Safety, Axon Enterprise, Motorola Solutions, and PlateSmart.
- Automated license plate readers capture vehicle data such as license plate numbers, make, model, and color using machine learning technology.
- MapQuest added the feature in response to user demand, citing it as one of the top three requests from their driver base.
- Public backlash against surveillance technology has led some municipalities, such as Tempe, Arizona, to terminate contracts with camera providers like Flock Safety.
- Flock Safety has responded to privacy concerns by reducing data retention periods from 30 days to 7 days and clarifying that their systems do not use facial recognition.

### Why it Matters
This feature highlights the growing public tension regarding the widespread use of surveillance technology in public and private spaces. By providing transparency into camera locations, MapQuest is positioning itself as a privacy-conscious alternative in a competitive mapping market.
