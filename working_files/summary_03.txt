Nvidia is playing many parts in the AI gold rush, a top business guru says
============================================================

Nvidia has solidified its position as the central pillar of the artificial intelligence industry by simultaneously supplying essential hardware, financing major startups, and funding large-scale infrastructure projects.

### Key Points
- Nvidia has invested billions into prominent AI firms including OpenAI, Anthropic, and xAI to secure its position within the industry.
- The company holds equity stakes in data center specialists like CoreWeave and Nebius that rely on its high-performance processors.
- Nvidia is collaborating with financial giants Apollo and KKR to raise $500 billion for AI infrastructure development.
- Revenue grew 85% year-on-year to $82 billion in the quarter ending April 26, with net income surging 211% to $58 billion.
- Critics, including Mark Cuban and Michael Burry, have warned that the company’s expansive influence creates systemic risks similar to the dot-com bubble.

### Why it Matters
Nvidia’s strategy of financing its own customers creates a circular economic ecosystem that makes the global AI boom heavily dependent on the company's continued success. This concentration of power raises concerns about market stability, as any disruption to Nvidia’s operations could potentially trigger a widespread collapse across the technology sector.
