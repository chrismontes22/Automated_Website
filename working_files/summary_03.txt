Usage Intelligence: The Monetization Signal Most AI Companies Are Ignoring
============================================================

AI companies are increasingly adopting usage intelligence to replace guesswork with data-driven strategies for pricing, product development, and churn prevention in a rapidly evolving software market.

### Key Points
- AI companies face unique economic risks because compute and model licensing costs can outpace revenue if pricing is not aligned with actual consumption.
- IDC research indicates that 48% of software buyers demand better visibility into their usage, while 70% view rapid ecosystem changes as a top security risk.
- Usage intelligence involves collecting telemetry data, such as activation events and feature-level engagement, to create actionable insights for sales and product teams.
- Tools like Thales Sentinel enable vendors to track usage across diverse environments, including on-premises and offline systems, to support flexible monetization models.
- Data-informed pricing adjustments and early churn detection allow companies to move from static models to agile, evidence-based business strategies.

### Why it Matters
Usage intelligence serves as the critical feedback loop that allows AI firms to align their business models with real-world product consumption. By grounding pricing and development in concrete data, companies can mitigate financial risks and improve long-term customer retention in a highly competitive market.
