These social media posts promise big online discounts on Lego, Calvin Klein and more — but really they're just phishing scams
============================================================

The Milk Dragon threat group is using sophisticated phishing kits and fake ecommerce websites on social media to steal payment credentials and bypass multi-factor authentication in 66 countries.

### Key Points
- Security firm Group-IB identified the Milk Dragon campaign, which uses the BytePress malware to capture keystrokes in real-time before forms are even submitted.
- The scam targets users on Facebook and TikTok by promoting fraudulent discounts for 21 major brands, including Lego and Calvin Klein.
- Attackers successfully intercept multi-factor authentication codes by relaying requests between the victim and legitimate banking or retail websites.
- The campaign has impacted victims in 66 countries, with the highest concentrations of activity reported in Malaysia, Singapore, and Thailand.
- The Milk Dragon phishing kit is available for purchase on Telegram as a service, with subscription plans starting at 300 USDT per month.

### Why it Matters
This campaign highlights a significant evolution in phishing tactics by shifting from email-based lures to social media platforms to bypass traditional spam filters. The ability to capture data in real-time and defeat multi-factor authentication poses a severe risk to both individual consumers and the security protocols of financial institutions.
