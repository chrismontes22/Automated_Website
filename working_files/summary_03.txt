Trump accounts were closed in 2021 due to concerns over possible money laundering, Capital One says
============================================================

Capital One disclosed in a court filing that it closed over 300 bank accounts held by President Donald Trump in 2021 due to flagged anti-money laundering concerns.

### Key Points
- Capital One stated the account closures followed months of internal analysis by its anti-money laundering team in accordance with regulatory guidance.
- President Trump filed a lawsuit alleging the bank closed his accounts for political reasons following the January 6 Capitol attacks.
- The bank maintains it provided the president’s businesses several months and multiple extensions to secure alternative banking services.
- Trump has also initiated a separate $5 billion lawsuit against JPMorgan Chase, alleging similar politically motivated debanking practices.
- In August 2025, President Trump signed an executive order aimed at preventing federal regulators from penalizing banks based on their business relationships.

### Why it Matters
This legal battle highlights the ongoing tension between major financial institutions and political figures regarding the criteria used for account termination. The outcome could influence future regulatory oversight and the standards banks must meet when citing risk management as a justification for closing customer accounts.
