Crypto billionaire Michael Saylor says he made $15 billion last year with ChatGPT—and has one rule: ‘Don’t try to outwork the robots’
============================================================

MicroStrategy founder Michael Saylor credits ChatGPT with helping his company raise $15 billion by designing innovative Bitcoin-based financial products that traditional banking experts initially viewed with significant skepticism.

### Key Points
- Michael Saylor utilized ChatGPT to develop a novel Bitcoin-based preferred stock offering for his company, MicroStrategy.
- The firm successfully raised approximately $15 billion through this AI-assisted financial strategy and related public offerings.
- Saylor advises professionals to focus on using AI to solve unprecedented problems rather than competing with automation.
- MicroStrategy remains the first publicly traded company to integrate Bitcoin as a primary component of its corporate treasury.
- Financial analysts have expressed concerns regarding the company's high leverage and heavy reliance on Bitcoin's market performance.

### Why it Matters
This strategy highlights a shift in corporate finance where AI tools are used to engineer complex, non-traditional capital structures. By leveraging automation to navigate regulatory and financial hurdles, companies can potentially unlock massive funding opportunities that were previously considered impossible or too risky.
