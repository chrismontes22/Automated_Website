The Internet Is Currently On Fire
============================================================

A surge in critical remote code execution vulnerabilities, including the widespread PHP2Shell flaw, has triggered urgent security warnings for global web infrastructure and major software platforms.

### Key Points
- PHP2Shell (CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619) threatens approximately 70 percent of websites, prompting warnings from the NSA and the UK’s National Cyber Security Centre.
- GitLab reported CVE-2026-110264, a critical RCE vulnerability with a maximum 10/10 severity score, affecting self-managed installations prior to version 7.0.
- Plex addressed four vulnerabilities (CVE-2026-96652 through CVE-2026-96656) that attackers used to hijack servers for unauthorized cryptocurrency mining and proxy traffic.
- Security researchers attribute the rapid increase in exploit attempts to AI tools, which significantly lower the barrier for discovering and scaling complex cyberattacks.
- Major entities including Square Enix and various Pokémon applications have reported unauthorized access or service disruptions linked to these recent security flaws.

### Why it Matters
The integration of AI into the exploitation process has fundamentally altered the cybersecurity landscape by making complex attacks faster and more accessible to malicious actors. This shift forces organizations to accelerate patching cycles to defend against automated threats that can scale from thousands to millions of attempts in mere hours.
