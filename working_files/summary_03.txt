The fight for tech accountability starts now
============================================================

Major technology companies face increased political risk and potential congressional scrutiny following historic Democratic gains in the midterm elections, which threaten their close alliance with the Trump administration.

### Key Points
- Democrats secured control of both the House and Senate, empowering them to launch investigations into tech industry practices and regulatory favors.
- Silicon Valley firms, including Google, Meta, and OpenAI, previously donated millions to the Trump inauguration and received favorable regulatory treatment.
- Public and political backlash is mounting against hyperscale data centers and the rapid, unregulated expansion of artificial intelligence.
- Lawmakers are expected to scrutinize potential conflicts of interest involving cryptocurrency, federal contracts, and the administration's hands-off approach to tech oversight.
- Legislative efforts to override local data center regulations and impose AI moratoriums face significant new hurdles under the shifted congressional balance.

### Why it Matters
The shift in congressional power signals a potential end to the era of "gangster tech regulation" that characterized the recent alignment between Silicon Valley and the White House. This transition forces tech companies to navigate a more hostile regulatory environment while risking their previous influence over federal policy and industry standards.
