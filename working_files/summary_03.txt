Residents In Michigan Are Suing A Data Center Over Noise Pollution
============================================================

Residents in Dowagiac, Michigan, have filed a lawsuit against Alliance Cloud Services, a subsidiary of Hyperscale Data, over persistent noise pollution caused by its local data center facility.

### Key Points
- Residents report a constant, high-pitched whining noise originating from the facility's cooling system since it transitioned to cryptocurrency mining in 2021.
- Dowagiac officials enacted an industrial noise ordinance in March 2024, setting limits of 65 dB during the day and 55 dB at night.
- Hyperscale Data has faced fines for exceeding local noise limits, though the company is currently disputing these findings.
- CEO William Horne stated the company plans to transition the site to AI and robotics, which includes sealing the vented wall causing the noise.
- The company has offered to purchase properties from affected residents, though many homeowners prefer to remain in their long-term residences.

### Why it Matters
This legal action highlights the growing tension between the rapid expansion of AI-focused data centers and the quality of life for nearby residential communities. As these facilities proliferate, companies face increasing pressure to balance operational requirements with local noise ordinances and environmental concerns to avoid costly litigation.
