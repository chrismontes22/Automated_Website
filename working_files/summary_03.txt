The software engineering hiring rebound has a catch: It's leaving early-career engineers behind
============================================================

Software engineering job postings are rebounding, but hiring demand is increasingly concentrated on senior-level roles as companies prioritize experienced workers capable of managing and reviewing AI-generated code.

### Key Points
- Nearly 70% of US software development job postings on Indeed in early 2025 were for senior-level roles, compared to 55% in 2019.
- Software development job postings rose approximately 15% in June 2025 compared to early 2024, though they remain below pre-pandemic levels.
- AI tools are increasingly automating routine coding tasks, leading companies to favor engineers who can direct AI output and perform complex quality control.
- The decline in entry-level and mid-level job postings may create a long-term talent gap by limiting the pipeline of workers gaining necessary experience.

### Why it Matters
The shift toward senior-only hiring suggests that AI is fundamentally altering the career trajectory for software engineers by automating entry-level responsibilities. If companies fail to invest in junior talent, they risk a future shortage of experienced professionals capable of overseeing increasingly complex AI-driven development workflows.
