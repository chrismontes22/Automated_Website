The Alexa Tablet 12 Pro is almost the Android tablet we needed
============================================================

The new Alexa Tablet 12 Pro features a high-quality 12-inch Nanomatte display and full Android support, offering a compelling alternative to the iPad despite heavy pre-installed Amazon software.

### Key Points
- The 12-inch display uses Corning-partnered Nanomatte technology to significantly reduce glare while maintaining sharpness.
- Pricing for the new lineup starts at $229.99 for the 8-inch model, with the 12 Pro model retailing for $549.99.
- The device runs full Android with Google Play Store access, marking a departure from Amazon’s traditional Fire OS.
- Hardware includes a MediaTek Dimensity 8400 processor, a 120Hz refresh rate, and a 3:2 aspect ratio screen.
- The tablet comes pre-loaded with non-removable Amazon advertising widgets and the Alexa Plus assistant, which users can replace with Google Gemini.

### Why it Matters
The Alexa Tablet 12 Pro represents a significant shift for Amazon by adopting a standard Android interface, making it a more versatile competitor in the premium tablet market. While the hardware and display quality offer a strong value proposition, the aggressive integration of Amazon’s ecosystem and advertising may require users to customize the software to achieve a clean experience.
