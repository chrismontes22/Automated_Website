Trump Family’s Crypto Bank Gains OCC Approval with Sheikh al Nahyan as Largest Shareholder
============================================================

The Office of the Comptroller of the Currency has conditionally approved a new bank to manage the USD1 stablecoin, featuring significant ownership stakes from UAE and Trump-affiliated entities.

### Key Points
- The new bank, WLTC Holdings, will directly manage reserve assets for the $4 billion USD1 stablecoin under the federal Genius Act framework.
- Sheikh Tahnoon bin Zayed al Nahyan holds a 49% stake in the venture, while a Trump family-affiliated entity maintains a 38% ownership share.
- The OCC required major shareholders to sign formal passivity commitments, pledging not to influence the bank's daily management or operations.
- Binance has signed a contract to provide marketing support for the USD1 stablecoin, which is currently backed by Treasuries and cash equivalents.
- The approval remains conditional, requiring a final examination by the OCC before the institution can officially commence operations.

### Why it Matters
This development highlights growing concerns regarding potential conflicts of interest between the Trump administration and foreign government officials. The integration of private stablecoins into the U.S. banking system also signals a shift in federal policy toward private digital assets over government-backed alternatives.
