Italian hosting collective Autistici/Inventati is shutting down after a US terrorist designation
============================================================

The Italian nonprofit Autistici/Inventati is shutting down all digital services after the United States government designated the collective as a Specially Designated Global Terrorist entity in August.

### Key Points
- The U.S. State Department and Treasury added Autistici/Inventati to the Specially Designated Nationals list on 26 August under Executive Order 13224.
- Washington alleges the collective provided digital infrastructure to extremist groups, including Rose City Antifa, the Informal Anarchist Federation, and the PKK.
- The nonprofit, which hosted 16,000 mailboxes and 10,000 blogs, denies the allegations and maintains its mission was providing digital self-defense tools for activists.
- Following the designation, the organization faced service disruptions from its domain registrar, PayPal, and its bank, Banca Etica, due to secondary sanctions risks.
- The collective is currently facilitating data retrieval for users before the wind-down period concludes on 25 September.

### Why it Matters
This closure highlights the significant reach of U.S. secondary sanctions, which can compel foreign banks and service providers to cut ties with entities even without local legal mandates. It demonstrates how American financial and administrative leverage can effectively dismantle digital infrastructure globally, raising critical questions about tech sovereignty and the vulnerability of non-commercial internet services.
