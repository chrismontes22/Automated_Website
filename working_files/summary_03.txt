Genki’s new customizable controller has a big screen and adjustable buttons
============================================================

Genki has launched the Manta, a highly customizable gaming controller featuring a 2.9-inch integrated LCD screen and advanced hardware, currently available through a Kickstarter crowdfunding campaign.

### Key Points
- The Manta features a Raspberry Pi RP2350 microcontroller that allows users to adjust thumbstick dead zones, haptic intensity, and button sensitivity.
- The controller introduces "tristage triggers" with three distinct travel distances and includes analog face buttons with adjustable actuation points.
- Hardware specifications include drift-resistant TMR thumbsticks, linear magnetic haptic motors, and a 2,600mAh user-replaceable battery.
- Connectivity options include Bluetooth, a 2.4GHz wireless dongle, and a wired USB-C connection for reduced latency.
- Early Kickstarter backers can purchase the device for $189, with a planned full retail price of $279.

### Why it Matters
The Manta represents a significant shift toward hardware-level personalization, allowing players to fine-tune physical inputs to match specific gaming genres. By integrating a dedicated screen and advanced microcontrollers, Genki is challenging standard controller designs and offering professional-grade customization to the broader consumer market.
