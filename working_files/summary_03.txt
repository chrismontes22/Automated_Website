Mexico (Taiwan) fact of the day
============================================================

Mexico has emerged as a critical hub for artificial intelligence infrastructure, now supplying 40 percent of United States server imports as Taiwanese manufacturers rapidly expand regional production facilities.

### Key Points
- Mexico’s server exports reached $46.9 billion this year, briefly surpassing Taiwan as the top US supplier in May.
- Computer servers have overtaken automobiles to become Mexico’s leading export category to the United States.
- Taiwanese firms have invested over $1.6 billion in Mexican manufacturing since 2020 to leverage geographic proximity and tariff-free trade.
- Server-related hardware accounted for nearly 20 percent of Mexico’s $317 billion in total exports between January and May.
- Taiwan has risen to become Mexico’s third-largest trading partner, climbing from eighth place in 2022.

### Why it Matters
This shift highlights a significant restructuring of global supply chains as tech giants prioritize nearshoring to meet the massive hardware demands of the AI boom. By integrating deeply into the US data center ecosystem, Mexico is securing a vital role in the future of the global digital economy.
