Payments firm OpenPayd targets year-end Nasdaq listing to fund U.S. expansion and acquisitions
============================================================

London-based payments infrastructure firm OpenPayd expects to complete its Nasdaq listing via a merger with Titan Acquisition Corp. by year-end to fund its planned 2027 U.S. market expansion.

### Key Points
- OpenPayd aims to finalize its merger with Titan Acquisition Corp. by the end of the year, pending regulatory and shareholder approvals.
- The company plans to launch U.S. operations by April 2027, supported by 43 state money transmitter licenses recently acquired through MSB USA Inc.
- Financial reports show the firm generated $73 million in revenue for the fiscal year ending April 30, 2026.
- The proposed business combination implies a pro forma equity value of approximately $1.1 billion for the payments provider.
- OpenPayd intends to use capital from the public listing to pursue strategic acquisitions of technology and additional licensing.

### Why it Matters
The move highlights the growing interest among global fintech firms in capturing the U.S. market as domestic digital asset regulations begin to take shape. By securing a public listing, OpenPayd gains the necessary capital and currency to compete with established players in the evolving intersection of traditional finance and blockchain.
