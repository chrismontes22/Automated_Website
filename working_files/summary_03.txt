Geekom IT13 Max review: Max by name, but restricted by nature
============================================================

The Geekom IT13 Max is a compact mini PC featuring an Intel Core Ultra 9 processor, offering strong connectivity and office performance despite limitations in graphics and memory capacity.

### Key Points
- Powered by an Intel Core Ultra 9 185H processor with integrated Intel Arc graphics.
- Features 24GB of LPDDR5 RAM and a 500GB SSD with an additional M.2 expansion slot.
- Includes extensive connectivity options, such as two USB4 ports, Wi-Fi 7, and dual 2.5GbE networking.
- Designed for space-constrained environments with a compact 132 x 132 x 50mm footprint and VESA mount compatibility.
- Priced at $1,399, positioning it as a direct competitor to the Apple Mac Mini for office and light creative tasks.

### Why it Matters
The IT13 Max serves as a capable, space-saving solution for office workers and 2D designers who prioritize port variety and desk real estate. However, its reliance on integrated graphics and capped RAM makes it less suitable for users requiring intensive video editing or advanced AI-driven workflows.
