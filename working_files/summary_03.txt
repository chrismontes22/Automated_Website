Trump-linked crypto venture World Liberty Trust granted bank status
============================================================

The Office of the Comptroller of the Currency has granted conditional bank charter approval to World Liberty Trust, a crypto venture partially owned by the Trump family.

### Key Points
- The OCC granted conditional approval for World Liberty Trust to issue U.S. dollar-pegged stablecoins directly to clients.
- World Liberty Financial is 38% owned by an entity affiliated with Donald J. Trump and his family members.
- The approval allows the company to bypass third-party providers like BitGo to manage its own digital currency transactions.
- Abu Dhabi investment firm MGX invested $2 billion in the venture in May 2025, with plans to utilize the stablecoin on the Binance exchange.
- Senator Elizabeth Warren and other Democratic lawmakers have criticized the decision, citing unprecedented conflicts of interest and potential self-dealing.
- Final charter approval remains contingent on the company meeting specific requirements, including an increase in capital reserves.

### Why it Matters
This decision marks the first time a sitting president's family business has received federal bank status, raising significant questions regarding executive oversight and potential conflicts of interest. The move could reshape the regulatory landscape for digital assets while intensifying political debate over the intersection of presidential authority and private financial gain.
