Transcript: Omar Aguilar, CEO and CIO of Schwab Asset Management
============================================================

Schwab Asset Management CEO and CIO Omar Aguilar discusses how his background in quantitative research and behavioral finance shapes the firm’s long-term investment strategies and client-focused decision-making tools.

### Key Points
- Schwab Asset Management oversees nearly $2 trillion in assets, contributing to the firm's total $13 trillion in client assets.
- Aguilar emphasizes a "core" investment philosophy centered on transparency, low-cost ETFs, and accessibility for retail investors.
- The firm utilizes "Biagnostics" tools to help financial advisors identify and mitigate common investor biases like herding and recency bias.
- Aguilar advocates for a disciplined, long-term approach, noting that market timing is often counterproductive for retirement planning.
- The firm is actively integrating AI to improve operational efficiency and provide better analytical support for both advisors and clients.

### Why it Matters
By merging quantitative rigor with behavioral science, Schwab aims to help investors avoid emotional pitfalls that often lead to poor financial outcomes. This approach highlights the industry's broader shift toward using technology to provide personalized, scalable advice while maintaining low costs for mass-affluent and retail clients.
