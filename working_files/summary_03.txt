EBO Max review: The AI-powered family robot that gets smarter over time (and my cat adores)
============================================================

The Enabot EBO Max is an AI-powered home monitoring robot featuring 4K video, V-SLAM navigation, and adaptive learning capabilities designed to assist with family and pet companionship.

### Key Points
- Launched March 31, 2026, the EBO Max is priced at $599.99 and features an 8MP camera with night vision.
- The robot utilizes V-SLAM navigation to map homes and includes fall detection, activity monitoring, and two-way communication.
- AI-driven features allow the device to recognize family members, learn household patterns, and respond to natural language voice commands.
- Hardware includes a 2,500mAh battery, 64GB of local storage, and a modular design that allows for easy cleaning of the wheel tracks.
- Notable limitations include a lack of Alexa or Google Home integration and occasional performance glitches during complex AI tasks.

### Why it Matters
The EBO Max serves as a versatile, mobile security and companionship solution that bridges the gap between traditional home surveillance and interactive smart home robotics. By offering advanced AI learning at a lower price point than premium models, it provides a practical, albeit unrefined, option for users seeking automated home monitoring and pet engagement.
