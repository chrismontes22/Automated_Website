OpenAI's Altman Says the Use of AI is 'Non-Negotiable'
============================================================

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman argued that national AI adoption is essential for economic growth, comparing the technology's transformative potential to the historical integration of electricity into modern society.

### Key Points
- Sam Altman discussed AI integration during a fireside chat with U.S. Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick at the G20 Innovation Ministerial in North Carolina.
- Altman stated that rejecting AI adoption would be as detrimental to a nation's progress as refusing to implement electricity over a century ago.
- The CEO predicted that AI will become an invisible, ubiquitous utility integrated into every product and service within the next decade.
- Cybersecurity was identified as a critical risk, with Altman warning that urgent action is required to prevent significant setbacks for the technology.

### Why it Matters
This perspective highlights the growing consensus among industry leaders that AI is a foundational economic driver rather than a temporary trend. By framing AI as a mandatory utility, Altman signals that global competitiveness will increasingly depend on a nation's ability to integrate and secure these advanced systems.
