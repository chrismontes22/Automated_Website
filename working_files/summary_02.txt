Google Workspace Weekly Recap - October 2, 2026
============================================================

Google has announced several updates to its Workspace platform, including the integration of Gemini 3.8 Flash Lite for voiceovers and new programmatic API support for document comments.

### Key Points
- Google Vids now features Gemini 3.8 Flash Lite for improved AI voiceovers and offers fully customizable caption fonts, positioning, and animations.
- The Gemini app is introducing "skills" to replace Gems, providing reusable prompts for task-specific guidance across Workspace applications.
- Developers can now programmatically manage comments in Docs, Sheets, and Slides, with added support for suggested edits in Google Docs.
- Google Workspace for Education users now have access to a dedicated student hub within the Gemini app for centralized learning tools.
- Interoperability between Google Meet and Microsoft Teams is now generally available for Android-based video conferencing devices.
- Administrators can utilize a new, simplified setup process for Google Workspace Client-side encryption to improve deployment speed.

### Why it Matters
These updates streamline content creation and administrative workflows while enhancing cross-platform compatibility for enterprise and educational users. By integrating advanced AI tools and improving API functionality, Google is positioning its ecosystem to better support automated document management and collaborative video conferencing.
