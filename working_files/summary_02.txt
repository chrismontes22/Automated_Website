Global financial markets face significant volatility as South Korean stocks plummet nearly 11 percent, driven by a sharp downturn in the AI semiconductor sector and broader economic instability.

### Key Points
- The KOSPI index experienced its third-largest loss in history, heavily impacting major tech firms Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix.
- Global markets are reacting to concerns over the AI bubble, with analysts questioning the sustainability of current semiconductor valuations and corporate spending.
- NYSE margin debt has reached historic levels, signaling increased leverage and potential risk within the broader financial system.
- Rising jet fuel costs and geopolitical tensions, particularly regarding the ongoing conflict in Iran, continue to pressure global supply chains and airline operations.

### Why it Matters
The sudden market contraction highlights growing investor anxiety regarding the reliance on AI-driven growth and the stability of major tech-sector financing. This downturn could signal a broader correction as high debt levels and geopolitical instability threaten to undermine global economic confidence.
