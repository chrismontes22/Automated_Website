Bethesda Is Taking Legal Action Against Doom-Themed Memecoin Promoted By Former Doom Designer
============================================================

Original Doom designer Sandy Petersen is facing legal scrutiny from Bethesda after promoting a $DOOM memecoin and a browser-based game called Doomsol that utilized unauthorized game assets.

### Key Points
- Sandy Petersen promoted the $DOOM memecoin and Doomsol game on X, investing over $1,187 of his own funds into the project.
- The Doomsol project falsely used assets from the fan-made Freedoom project, which publicly disavowed any connection to the cryptocurrency scheme.
- Following a "rug pull" where the token's value collapsed, the project websites for Doomsol, Quakesol, and Wolfensteinsol were taken offline.
- Bethesda is currently pursuing legal action against the creators of Doomsol for the unauthorized commercial use of Doom intellectual property and assets.
- Bethesda clarified that while they support creative community projects, they intervene when unauthorized ventures risk confusing consumers regarding official product affiliation.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the growing tension between legacy gaming intellectual property and the volatile, often predatory world of memecoin promotion. By taking legal action, Bethesda is setting a precedent that it will aggressively protect its brand from being leveraged to facilitate potential financial scams.
