I tested Eufy's E50, and it has everything you need in a local security camera system
============================================================

The Eufy Security NVR System E50 offers a comprehensive, subscription-free home surveillance solution featuring four PoE cameras, a 2TB hard drive, and advanced motion-tracking capabilities for local storage.

### Key Points
- The E50 bundle includes an NVR with a 2TB hard drive, four PTZ PoE cameras, and necessary mounting accessories for $1,199, or $839 with a promotional code.
- Each camera features dual lenses, including a 2K fixed lens and a 4K movable lens with 360-degree rotation, 5x zoom, and 65-foot range.
- The NVR supports up to 16TB of storage and includes eight PoE ports, allowing users to expand the system with additional cameras.
- On-device AI enables features like OmniTrack motion tracking, familiar face recognition, and customizable exclusion zones without requiring cloud subscriptions.
- The system utilizes end-to-end encryption for secure video transmission and is slated to receive RTSP protocol support in the coming months.

### Why it Matters
This all-in-one system provides a long-term cost advantage over subscription-based security cameras by keeping all video data stored locally on a physical drive. It offers a robust, privacy-focused alternative for homeowners who prefer hardwired reliability and ownership of their surveillance footage.
