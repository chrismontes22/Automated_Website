Warning over fake social media shopping deals
============================================================

Derbyshire Police have issued a formal warning regarding organized crime groups using social media to lure shoppers into fake discount scams involving major UK retail brands.

### Key Points
- Scammers are advertising fraudulent discounts of 40% to 70% on social media platforms for retailers including Morrisons, Currys, and Very.co.uk.
- Derbyshire Police have recorded 30 instances of victims losing money to these scams so far this year.
- Criminals often gain access to victims' existing shopping accounts to place unauthorized orders after stealing initial payment details.
- The operation involves a sophisticated network of technical specialists and money mules to process stolen funds and goods.
- Authorities advise against clicking social media links and warn that payments via bank transfer or cryptocurrency are major red flags.

### Why it Matters
These scams represent a growing threat to consumer financial security by exploiting trust in well-known high-street brands. The organized nature of these attacks highlights the increasing risk of identity theft and unauthorized account access for online shoppers.
