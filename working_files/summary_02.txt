AI Server Stocks Rally as the Hardware Bid Broadens: Hewlett Packard Enterprise Jumps 9%, Super Micro Climbs 6%, Dell Rises 3%
============================================================

Hewlett Packard Enterprise, Super Micro Computer, and Dell Technologies shares surged on Thursday as investor interest in artificial intelligence hardware expanded from chipmakers to server assembly companies.

### Key Points
- Hewlett Packard Enterprise led the sector with a 9.02% gain, followed by Super Micro Computer at 6.08% and Dell Technologies at 3.36%.
- The rally occurred without company-specific news, signaling a broader rotation into AI hardware assemblers rather than individual stock-driven growth.
- Market performance for these server makers significantly outpaced the iShares U.S. Technology ETF, which rose 1.83% during the same period.
- Rising memory costs, which benefit chip suppliers, create potential margin pressure for server assemblers like Super Micro Computer.
- Investors are monitoring whether server companies can successfully pass increased component costs on to their customers.

### Why it Matters
This rally highlights a shift in investor focus toward the "picks-and-shovels" infrastructure companies supporting the AI buildout. While the sector is benefiting from strong demand, the rising cost of memory components creates a complex environment where stock prices are climbing despite potential margin compression for hardware assemblers.
