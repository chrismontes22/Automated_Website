GPT-6 Astra Solves a WWI German Radio Cipher
============================================================

The AI model GPT-6 Astra has successfully decoded a previously unsolved World War I German ADFGVX radio message transmitted on November 27, 1918, regarding naval movements in Sevastopol.

### Key Points
- The decrypted message confirms an English cruiser arrived in Sevastopol on November 24, 1918, followed by an Allied squadron on November 26.
- The cipher was solved using the keyword "TRUPPENVERSCHIEBUNG," a key previously thought to have been implemented by the German military starting in December 1918.
- The ADFGVX method utilizes a complex grid-based substitution and transposition system to secure military communications.
- This specific message was part of a collection of 50 unsolved ciphers hosted on the German science portal Scienceblogs.de.
- Historical logs from the HMS Canterbury corroborate the intelligence contained within the decoded transmission.

### Why it Matters
This breakthrough demonstrates the growing capability of large language models to perform complex cryptanalysis on historical documents that have long eluded human experts. By successfully applying specific military encryption keys to archival data, AI tools provide new methods for historians to verify and contextualize events from the First World War.
