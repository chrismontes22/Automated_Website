NextBlock backs Soda Labs with $3M to bring privacy to public blockchains
============================================================

Luxembourg-based venture capital firm NextBlock has invested $3 million in Soda Labs to develop privacy infrastructure that allows financial institutions to conduct secure transactions on public blockchains.

### Key Points
- Soda Labs secured $3 million in seed funding led by NextBlock to scale its programmable privacy infrastructure for financial institutions.
- The company utilizes a cryptographic system combining garbled circuits and multiparty computation to enable private data processing on public networks.
- Soda’s technology has already processed over 100 million transactions on the COTI network, supporting platforms like Zoniqx and PriveX.
- The new "Soda Bubble" coprocessor is expanding across major ecosystems, including Ethereum, Polygon, Arbitrum, Base, and eventually Solana.
- Internal testing indicates the architecture offers 10 to 100 times greater throughput and significantly lower costs compared to existing privacy alternatives.
- Founders Avishay Yanai and Meital Levy plan to use the capital to transition current pilot programs with financial institutions into full production deployments.

### Why it Matters
This investment addresses a critical barrier to institutional adoption by enabling regulated entities to utilize the liquidity of public blockchains without exposing sensitive financial data. By providing a scalable, chain-agnostic privacy solution, Soda Labs could bridge the gap between traditional finance requirements and decentralized infrastructure.
