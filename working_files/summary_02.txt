AI Isn’t The Hard Part
============================================================

**Claude Corps launches a 12-month fellowship program this October to help nonprofits build sustainable AI workflows, addressing the critical gap between technological access and actual organizational capability.**

### Key Points
- Anthropic is funding the $150 million Claude Corps initiative to embed early-career technologists into nonprofits focused on public health, housing, and food security.
- The program aims to place 1,000 fellows across 400 host organizations by 2027 to help institutions integrate AI into daily operations.
- CodePath manages recruitment and training, while Social Finance oversees project evaluation and the development of long-term financial scaling models.
- Research from the MIT NANDA initiative indicates that 95% of enterprise generative AI pilots fail to deliver measurable profit or performance improvements due to poor integration.
- Participating nonprofits receive a $10,000 implementation grant and access to discounted Claude software licenses and API credits.

### Why it Matters
The success of AI adoption depends less on the intelligence of the models themselves and more on an organization's ability to reorganize workflows and establish clear governance. By focusing on institutional capability rather than just software access, this experiment seeks to prevent a widening inequality gap where only well-funded corporations can effectively leverage new technology.
