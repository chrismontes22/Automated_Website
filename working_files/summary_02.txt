Ad Tech Briefing: Can flipping the ad auction wrest control of ad tech from Google?
============================================================

Prebid is proposing a new programmatic advertising architecture that would reverse auction flows, potentially reducing publisher reliance on Google’s ad server by moving the final competition outside its control.

### Key Points
- Prebid aims to formalize a new auction model by early 2027 where ad servers bid into its platform rather than the current reverse process.
- The proposal seeks to separate Google Ad Manager (GAM) from Google’s AdX exchange without requiring a formal divestiture of assets.
- Google has not committed to the new architecture and reportedly faces scrutiny over attempts to limit court-ordered integration provisions.
- Google recently introduced optional budget allocation controls for advertisers across Search, Shopping, and Performance Max to provide more manual oversight.
- WARC analysis indicates that while advertising expenditure for major brands has increased by 56% since 2019, the advertising-to-sales ratio has declined to 5.5%.

### Why it Matters
This shift represents a significant attempt by independent ad tech entities to reclaim control over programmatic auctions from dominant platforms like Google. If successful, the change could fundamentally alter how digital advertising is traded and reduce the influence of major tech companies over publisher revenue streams.
