Google’s new Tensor G6 brings performance, efficiency, and post-quantum security to the Pixel 11 series
============================================================

Google’s new Pixel 11 lineup features the Tensor G6 processor and a Titan M3 security chip designed to meet NIST post-quantum cryptography standards for enhanced device protection.

### Key Points
- The Tensor G6 chip offers 25% faster browsing, 20% better power efficiency, and a TPU with 50% more compute capacity.
- The Titan M3 coprocessor is Google’s first mobile security chip to adopt NIST post-quantum cryptography standards against future decryption threats.
- Gemini Nano now runs background tasks 3.5 times faster while consuming 3.5 times less energy on the new hardware.
- The Pixel 11 series includes four models starting at $899, with the entry-level 128GB storage tier removed from the lineup.
- Advanced camera features include 120x Pro Zoom, 4K portrait video, and significantly faster Instant Night Sight processing.

### Why it Matters
The integration of post-quantum cryptography addresses the "harvest now, decrypt later" threat, where encrypted data is stolen today to be unlocked by future quantum computers. By adopting these standards, Google is aligning its hardware with federal mandates and setting a new security benchmark for the broader smartphone industry.
