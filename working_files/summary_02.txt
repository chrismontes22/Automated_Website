WhatsApp expands parental controls to teen accounts with group alerts, Meta AI restrictions
============================================================

WhatsApp has expanded its parental control features to include teen accounts, offering parents increased visibility into group activity and new restrictions for interactions with the Meta AI chatbot.

### Key Points
- Parents can now receive notifications when teens join or leave groups or when group sizes increase significantly.
- New settings allow parents to manage teen interactions with Channels, Status updates, and contact privacy.
- A "Limited Content" option for Meta AI restricts discussion topics and disables Private Processing features for younger users.
- All parental controls are managed via a PIN, though parents cannot access the content of encrypted messages or calls.
- These updates build upon previous parental control tools introduced earlier this year for pre-teen accounts on the platform.

### Why it Matters
These updates reflect Meta's ongoing effort to balance user privacy with safety concerns regarding AI and social interaction for younger demographics. By providing granular control without compromising end-to-end encryption, the company aims to address regulatory pressures and parental demands for safer digital environments.
