As Midterms Approach, Top American Fears Include AI Interference in the Political Process
============================================================

Chapman University’s 12th annual survey reveals that Americans now rank artificial intelligence’s impact on politics and the economy among their top ten most significant national fears for 2026.

### Key Points
- Corrupt government officials remain the top American fear, cited by 61.1% of the 1,221 adults surveyed by NORC.
- Concerns regarding the U.S. entering another world war rose to the second position, affecting 58% of respondents.
- AI harming the political process debuted at number three, while AI hurting the economy entered the list at number seven.
- Personal fears, including the illness or death of loved ones, continue to rank within the top five concerns for the public.
- The survey, conducted in April, highlights that 42% of Americans fear AI will replace human workers.

### Why it Matters
The inclusion of artificial intelligence in the top ten list reflects growing public anxiety regarding the rapid integration of new technology into daily life and governance. This shift suggests that policymakers and businesses must address these societal concerns to maintain public trust as AI tools become more prevalent in the workforce and political landscape.
