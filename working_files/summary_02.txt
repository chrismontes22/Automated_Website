Presented by Stripe
============================================================

Stratechery founder Ben Thompson has announced a new exclusive sponsorship deal with Stripe, marking a shift toward a hybrid business model combining paid subscriptions with integrated advertising.

### Key Points
- Stratechery will feature "Presented by Stripe" branding across its website, email newsletters, and podcasts starting in October 2026.
- Thompson has updated his ethics policy to mandate disclosure of the Stripe relationship in any content where a conflict of interest may be relevant.
- The agreement guarantees total editorial independence, ensuring Stripe has no control over the publication's content or editorial direction.
- Revenue from the sponsorship will fund new product development, including a transition to in-person, video-based episodes for the *Sharp Tech* podcast.
- Existing subscribers will continue to receive all content, though the ad-supported model will apply to all readers regardless of subscription status.

### Why it Matters
This partnership signals a strategic pivot for independent media creators who are increasingly adopting hybrid revenue models to scale operations. By formalizing a long-standing professional relationship, the move highlights the growing trend of niche publishers leveraging high-value audiences to secure long-term corporate sponsorships.
