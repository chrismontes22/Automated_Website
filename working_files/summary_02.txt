Tech Bros Run the Marxist Playbook
============================================================

Critics argue that major technology companies are utilizing Marxist-style critiques of capital to highlight how AI firms extract intellectual property, public resources, and labor for private gain.

### Key Points
- AI companies including Google, Apple, Microsoft, OpenAI, and Meta face criticism for training large language models on copyrighted works without providing compensation or licensing fees.
- The rapid expansion of hyperscale data centers is straining local power grids, potentially leading to increased electricity costs for residents and businesses.
- AI integration into consumer devices often occurs by default, leading to concerns regarding unauthorized data collection and the forced obsolescence of older hardware.
- Tech firms benefit from significant government subsidies, tax incentives, and regulatory support, shifting the operational costs of AI development onto the general public.
- Emerging competition from Chinese models like DeepSeek and Moonshot relies on leveraging existing U.S. technical advancements, further complicating the global AI landscape.

### Why it Matters
The current AI business model relies on an extractive economy that prioritizes trillion-dollar valuations while externalizing significant social and infrastructure costs onto everyday citizens. This dynamic creates long-term market instability and growing public fatigue, which may eventually trigger a consumer-led shift away from pervasive AI technologies.
