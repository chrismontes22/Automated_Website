Abundantia Entertainment To Turn AI Creative Arm AiOn Into Standalone Studio: “Truly A Confluence Of Technology And Art”
============================================================

India’s Abundantia Entertainment is transitioning its internal AI division, AiOn, into a standalone native studio to develop hybrid content and harness ideas from non-traditional creative sources.

### Key Points
- Abundantia Entertainment founder Vikram Malhotra announced that AiOn will launch as a fully independent AI studio by 2027.
- The studio will produce both fully generated and hybrid content, managing the entire production ecosystem.
- AiOn is currently collaborating with AI video technology company InVideo on a slate of five upcoming films.
- The initiative follows a significant increase in story pitches from creators outside the traditional filmmaking industry.
- Current projects in development include *Chiranjeevi Hanuman* and *Jai Santoshi Mata: Sukh Sampatti Daata*.

### Why it Matters
This move signals a shift toward integrating AI as a core production pillar rather than a mere cost-saving tool in the Indian entertainment market. By institutionalizing AI-driven workflows, Abundantia aims to mitigate financial risk and tap into a new generation of creators who are leveraging technology to bypass conventional industry gatekeepers.
