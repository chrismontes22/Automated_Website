AI IR Overlay – Incident Response Specification for AI Agents
============================================================

The AI IR Overlay framework provides a standardized specification for managing incident response in autonomous agents by focusing on containment, evidence collection, and mapping potential system blast radii.

### Key Points
- The framework includes 24 playbooks, three JSON schemas, and two reference implementations to address risks from authorized but unintended agent actions.
- It introduces an AI Bill of Materials (AI-BOM) to document service identities, tool scopes, and write targets for production agents.
- A containment ladder (M0–M5) allows security teams to restrict agent capabilities, such as disabling write tools, without fully terminating the session.
- The specification maps directly to NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 3, NIST CSF 2.0, and the OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications.
- The validator tool performs schema checks to ensure security controls are declared, though it does not verify the functional performance of those controls.

### Why it Matters
This framework addresses the unique challenge of securing autonomous agents that operate using valid credentials, where traditional intrusion detection fails to identify malicious activity. By providing a structured approach to containment and evidence gathering, it helps organizations identify critical security gaps in their agentic workflows before an incident occurs.
