Using Device Linking to Eavesdrop on WhatsApp and Signal
============================================================

German law enforcement agencies are bypassing end-to-end encryption on WhatsApp and Signal by linking suspect accounts to police-controlled desktop computers through physical access or intercepted verification codes.

### Key Points
- German Customs officials exploit the "device linking" feature in messaging apps to mirror account activity on police-controlled hardware.
- This method avoids the need to break encryption protocols because messages are delivered directly to the linked device.
- Authorities gain access by physically handling a suspect's phone or intercepting SMS verification codes through phishing or telecommunications surveillance.
- Security experts advocate for improved user interfaces that clearly display all active linked devices to help users detect unauthorized account access.

### Why it Matters
This surveillance technique highlights a significant vulnerability in how messaging apps manage multi-device synchronization. It demonstrates that even robust end-to-end encryption can be circumvented if an attacker gains the ability to authorize a new, trusted device on an existing account.
