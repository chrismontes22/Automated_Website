So Many New Meta Glasses: Muse AI Everywhere, and Ray-Bans Without a Camera
============================================================

Meta is expanding its wearable AI lineup by integrating the Muse agent into its smart glasses while introducing new camera-free models and FDA-cleared hearing enhancement features.

### Key Points
- Meta’s Muse AI agent will soon be available on smart glasses to assist with tasks like scheduling and personal data management.
- New camera-free Meta Ray-Ban Audio glasses offer up to 12 hours of battery life and retail between $379 and $479.
- An FDA-cleared hearing enhancement mode is now available for $149 or via a Meta One subscription.
- Third-generation Meta Ray-Ban glasses feature minor battery improvements and new frame designs, with prices ranging from $449 to $529.
- Meta introduced "Holograms," a hyper-realistic avatar system for video chats, currently in limited beta for WhatsApp.

### Why it Matters
Meta is aggressively positioning its hardware to compete with upcoming AI-enabled wearables from Google, Samsung, and Apple. However, the lack of significant privacy safeguards and the introduction of subscription-based health features raise questions about user trust and the long-term viability of agentic AI in public spaces.
