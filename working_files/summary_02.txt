SanDisk’s Extreme Portable SSD (V3) hits 2,000MB/s as the V3 pair rolls out
============================================================

SanDisk has launched its V3 series of portable solid-state drives, featuring the high-performance Extreme model with 2,000MB/s read speeds and a more affordable, lightweight standard Portable version.

### Key Points
- The SanDisk Extreme Portable SSD (V3) offers read speeds up to 2,000MB/s and write speeds up to 1,700MB/s using a USB 3.2 Gen 2x2 connection.
- The Extreme (V3) features an IP65 rating for dust and water resistance, 3-meter drop protection, and 256-bit AES hardware encryption.
- The standard SanDisk Portable SSD (V3) reaches read speeds of 1,000MB/s and includes a carabiner loop for portability.
- The Extreme (V3) is available in capacities up to 4TB with a five-year warranty, while the Portable (V3) ranges from 500GB to 2TB with a three-year warranty.
- Both drives are currently available for purchase through major US retailers, with the 1TB Extreme priced at $239.50 and the 1TB Portable at $159.99.

### Why it Matters
These new drives provide significant performance upgrades for users needing faster data transfers for professional media workflows or reliable mobile backups. Choosing between the two models allows consumers to balance the need for rugged, high-speed hardware against the convenience of a lightweight, budget-friendly storage solution.
