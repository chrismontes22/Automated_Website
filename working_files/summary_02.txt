From tunnel to connectivity layer: What GnuVPN says about where VPNs are heading
============================================================

Consumer VPN providers are evolving beyond basic encryption by integrating protocol flexibility, cross-platform support, and travel-focused eSIM services to maintain stable connectivity across increasingly fragmented network environments.

### Key Points
- Modern VPNs are shifting focus from simple server counts to network resilience, utilizing multiple protocols like WireGuard, OpenVPN, and AmneziaWG to maintain stable connections.
- Services like GnuVPN are expanding cross-platform compatibility to include Windows, macOS, Linux, and mobile app stores like Huawei AppGallery and Xiaomi GetApps.
- The integration of eSIM technology allows users to combine mobile data access with privacy tools, streamlining the experience for international travelers.
- According to the GSMA Intelligence 2025 Global Consumer Survey, 12% of international travelers utilized eSIMs, highlighting a growing market for integrated connectivity solutions.
- The industry is moving toward "invisible" security, where software automatically adapts to changing network conditions without requiring manual user intervention.

### Why it Matters
The convergence of connectivity and privacy tools reflects a fundamental shift in how consumers expect digital security to function in a mobile-first world. By bundling services like eSIMs with VPNs, providers are positioning themselves as essential infrastructure for seamless, secure internet access rather than just standalone privacy utilities.
