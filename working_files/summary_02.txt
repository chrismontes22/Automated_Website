Future Apple wearables, iOS 27 beta 5, iPhone 20 details
============================================================

Apple is testing new fitness wearable concepts and exploring expanded sports broadcasting rights for Apple TV, including potential golf coverage, alongside the latest iOS 18 beta updates.

### Key Points
- Apple released the fifth beta version of iOS 18, introducing several system refinements and feature updates.
- The company is reportedly evaluating new hardware designs for future fitness-focused wearable devices.
- Rumors suggest Apple is actively pursuing additional sports broadcasting rights, specifically targeting professional golf.
- Apple TV has launched a new promotional campaign offering a curated collection of free movies to subscribers.
- Discussions include early speculation regarding the design and material composition of the future iPhone 20.

### Why it Matters
These developments highlight Apple's ongoing strategy to integrate health-tracking hardware with a robust ecosystem of exclusive sports and entertainment content. By diversifying its media rights and wearable portfolio, the company aims to increase long-term user engagement across its services and hardware platforms.
