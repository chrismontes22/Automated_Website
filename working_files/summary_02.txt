The new ESP32 put Wi-Fi 6 and gigabit ethernet on a chip that used to blink LEDs
============================================================

Espressif Systems has unveiled the ESP32-S31, a powerful new microcontroller featuring dual 320 MHz RISC-V cores, Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.4, and gigabit Ethernet support for advanced IoT applications.

### Key Points
- The chip utilizes two 320 MHz RISC-V high-performance cores and a dedicated 40 MHz low-power core for background tasks.
- Wireless capabilities include Wi-Fi 6 (2.4 GHz), Bluetooth 5.4, and 802.15.4 support for Thread, Zigbee, and Matter protocols.
- Integrated hardware includes a gigabit Ethernet MAC, USB 2.0 High-Speed OTG, and a hardware JPEG codec for imaging and display tasks.
- The S31 provides 60 programmable GPIOs and supports up to 32 MB of external octal PSRAM for memory-intensive projects.
- Enhanced security features include secure boot, flash encryption, and hardware accelerators for AES, RSA, and ECC cryptographic operations.

### Why it Matters
The ESP32-S31 significantly expands the capabilities of the popular ESP32 platform, allowing developers to consolidate complex networking, multimedia, and industrial tasks onto a single, cost-effective chip. This shift enables more sophisticated smart-home hubs and edge-AI devices while maintaining the accessibility that made the original series a staple for hobbyists and engineers alike.
