The School Behind the Billionaires and the Billionaire Tax
============================================================

UC Berkeley faculty and prominent tech alumni are clashing over California’s Proposition 40, a proposed wealth tax that has sparked a multimillion-dollar political battle ahead of the November election.

### Key Points
- Proposition 40, dubbed the "Billionaire Tax," aims to increase taxes on the ultrarich to fund state programs like Medicaid.
- UC Berkeley economics professor Emmanuel Saez and law professor Brian Galle are the primary architects behind the measure.
- Former Google CEO Eric Schmidt, along with Larry Page and Sergey Brin, have contributed $85 million to oppose the initiative.
- The SEIU United Healthcare Workers West union initiated the proposal to address revenue shortfalls in the state healthcare system.
- Polling from the Berkeley Institute of Government Studies indicates that voters aged 18 to 29 are the strongest supporters of the tax.

### Why it Matters
The conflict highlights a deep ideological divide within the California tech ecosystem, pitting academic proponents of wealth redistribution against the billionaire class that drives the regional economy. The outcome of this ballot measure could set a significant national precedent for how states approach progressive taxation and wealth inequality.
