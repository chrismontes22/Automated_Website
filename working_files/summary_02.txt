Ledger confirms hidden chip in crypto wallet amid $92M theft claims
============================================================

Ledger has confirmed the discovery of an unauthorized hardware implant in a customer’s cryptocurrency wallet following an investigation into significant reported thefts linked to reseller CryptoBilis.

### Key Points
- Ledger identified an unauthorized electronic component inside a wallet during an investigation into reported cryptocurrency losses involving the Southeast Asian reseller CryptoBilis.
- CryptoBilis has suspended all hardware wallet sales while Ledger and law enforcement investigate the potential security breach.
- Security researcher Mark Karpelès reported finding suspicious circuitry behind the screen of a Ledger Nano X device obtained from Malaysia.
- Blockchain analysis firm Bitquery estimated total losses across 311 affected wallets at approximately $92.9 million, though Ledger has not independently verified this figure.
- Ledger advises customers who purchased devices from CryptoBilis to avoid setup and recommends that existing users transfer assets to new wallets with fresh recovery phrases.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights a critical supply chain vulnerability where physical tampering can compromise hardware wallets before they reach the end user. It underscores the importance of purchasing security devices exclusively through authorized channels to mitigate the risk of sophisticated hardware-level attacks.
