I was there for the dot-com burst. Here's how the AI bubble will pop.
============================================================

The current artificial intelligence investment boom mirrors historical market cycles, combining rapid technological growth and significant financial leverage that could eventually lead to a major economic correction.

### Key Points
- Anthropic has seen explosive growth, reaching an annualized revenue of $65 billion as of July, while OpenAI reports $40 billion in annualized revenue.
- Tech giants including Alphabet, Meta, and Amazon have shifted from generating free cash flow to burning cash to fund massive AI infrastructure projects.
- Alphabet’s off-balance-sheet commitments increased by nearly $500 billion in the last three months, exceeding its annual free cash flow generation.
- Nvidia is utilizing vendor financing, including a $105 billion credit agreement for a data center, to stimulate demand for its AI chips.
- Historical parallels suggest that while AI demand is currently high, the eventual alignment of supply and demand could trigger a market bust.

### Why it Matters
The AI sector is currently a primary driver of U.S. economic growth, meaning a potential market correction could trigger a broader recession. Investors and businesses must balance the pursuit of transformative technological opportunities with the reality that speculative bubbles often result in significant capital losses.
