Lexar's SL500 Might Become Part of Your Everyday Kit
============================================================

The Lexar SL500 Portable SSD offers high-speed external storage designed to help smartphone content creators manage large 4K video files and prevent storage limitations during professional production workflows.

### Key Points
- The Lexar SL500 supports read speeds up to 2,000 MB/s and write speeds up to 1,800 MB/s via a USB 3.2 Gen 2x2 interface.
- It enables direct recording of Apple ProRes 422 HQ video on iPhone 15 Pro models and later, bypassing internal storage constraints.
- The drive features a slim 4.8 mm aluminum unibody design with IP54 dust and water resistance and drop protection up to 6.6 feet.
- Available in 1 TB, 2 TB, and 4 TB capacities, the device is compatible with PC, Mac, Android, and iPhone platforms.
- The optional Mag Set includes a magnetic sleeve for secure attachment to smartphones, facilitating one-handed operation during mobile shoots.

### Why it Matters
As smartphone cameras generate increasingly large data files, external SSDs have become essential tools for maintaining professional production efficiency. Using dedicated hardware allows creators to bypass internal storage bottlenecks and keep professional projects organized separately from personal media.
