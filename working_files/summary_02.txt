You Can Now Link More Devices to a Signal Messaging Account
============================================================

Signal has updated its messaging app to allow users to link multiple Android phones, tablets, and iPhones to a single account using a secure QR code system.

### Key Points
- Users can link multiple devices by scanning a QR code found in the Signal settings menu.
- The update supports message history transfers and 45 days of media, protected by end-to-end encryption.
- Signal v8.22 for iOS and v8.20 for Android are now available in their respective app stores.
- The interface now automatically adjusts its layout to fit tablets, foldable phones, and various screen aspect ratios.
- Chromebooks are supported for linking, though the interface remains in a preliminary state.

### Why it Matters
This update addresses the growing consumer demand for multi-device support as foldable phones and tablets become more common. By improving cross-platform usability while maintaining end-to-end encryption, Signal aims to remain competitive against larger messaging rivals like WhatsApp and Telegram.
