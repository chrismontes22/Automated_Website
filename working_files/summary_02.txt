Skip the new computer and upgrade your existing PC to Windows 11 Pro for less than $10
============================================================

Microsoft is offering a discounted Windows 11 Pro license for $9.97 through September 27, providing a cost-effective way for users to upgrade their compatible PC operating systems.

### Key Points
- The Windows 11 Pro license is available for $9.97, a significant reduction from the $199 MSRP.
- This promotional offer expires on September 27 at 11:59 p.m. Pacific Time.
- Professional features included are BitLocker encryption, Hyper-V for virtual machines, and Windows Sandbox.
- General system enhancements include Copilot integration, Snap Layouts, and DirectX 12 Ultimate support.
- Users must verify hardware compatibility using the Microsoft PC Health Check app before purchasing the license.

### Why it Matters
This deal offers a low-cost alternative for users looking to upgrade older hardware without committing to ongoing subscription fees. It provides an accessible entry point for individuals and professionals to access advanced security and productivity features integrated into the latest Windows environment.
