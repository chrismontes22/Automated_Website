I tried OpenAI's new AI agent at work. Cue the corporate IT headaches.
============================================================

OpenAI has introduced Dots, a new AI agent feature for ChatGPT Enterprise users designed to automate complex workplace tasks and streamline digital workflows through secure, integrated software access.

### Key Points
- Dots allows users to create AI agents capable of performing specific tasks, such as building websites or managing newsletter publishing tools.
- The feature requires integration with existing workplace applications and often necessitates IT department approval for external sharing or password access.
- During testing, an AI agent successfully automated the transfer of content from a Google Doc into a newsletter tool, achieving 95% accuracy.
- OpenAI product lead Alexander Embiricos stated that the company is intentionally launching Dots within restrained corporate environments to ensure safety and control.
- While consumer-facing AI tools may offer more immediate "magical" experiences, Dots is built to navigate the complex security and authentication requirements of enterprise IT systems.

### Why it Matters
The rollout of Dots highlights the significant friction between rapid AI innovation and the cautious, security-focused nature of corporate IT departments. While these agents offer clear productivity gains by automating repetitive tasks, their widespread adoption will likely be slowed by the necessity of navigating complex enterprise guardrails and administrative approvals.
