A data centre that does not hog land or water
============================================================

Bengaluru-based startup TakeMe2Space is developing India’s first orbital data-center network to process satellite imagery and store critical information directly in low-earth orbit for various industrial clients.

### Key Points
- TakeMe2Space aims to reduce data transmission costs by processing earth-observation imagery in orbit rather than sending raw data to terrestrial servers.
- The company utilizes a modular architecture based on 1.3-kilowatt "hexes" and is developing radiation shielding to lower hardware costs.
- Current compute pricing is $2 per minute, with a long-term goal to reduce operational costs from $10 million to $100,000 per kilowatt by 2029.
- The startup plans to launch two satellites this year, followed by additional deployments in 2027 and 2028, culminating in a 1,500-kg satellite launch in 2029.
- TakeMe2Space reported ₹5 crore in revenue for FY26 and is currently seeking Series A funding to secure necessary launch capacity.

### Why it Matters
This initiative represents a shift toward edge computing in space, potentially transforming how industries like mining, agriculture, and defense manage massive satellite data volumes. If the company successfully lowers its operational costs, it could establish a viable commercial alternative to traditional terrestrial cloud infrastructure for space-based assets.
