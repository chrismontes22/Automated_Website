Apple Explains What Happens to Conversations Siri Recap Hears
============================================================

Apple is introducing new Audio Intelligence features, including the Siri Recap note-taking tool, for the upcoming Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4 models launching later this year.

### Key Points
- Siri Recap uses Apple Intelligence to generate conversation summaries, omitting sensitive data like financial information and government identifiers.
- The feature processes audio locally on the device's Secure Exclave and sends condensed, encrypted text to Private Cloud Compute for analysis.
- Apple confirms that no full audio recordings are stored, speaker attribution is disabled, and summaries automatically delete after seven days.
- Users must manually opt in to the feature and can manage scheduling or toggle recording via the Apple Watch app and Control Center.
- The functionality requires an Apple Watch Series 12 or Ultra 4 paired with an iPhone 16 or later, excluding the iPhone 16e model.

### Why it Matters
This update represents a significant shift in wearable technology by integrating continuous ambient listening to assist with memory and productivity. While Apple emphasizes privacy through encryption and local processing, the feature raises new social and ethical considerations regarding consent in public or private spaces.
