Is Your Echo Show Display Filling With AI Slop? I Found How to Turn It Off
============================================================

Amazon is introducing the new Echo Show 16 while users report frustration over intrusive, AI-generated advertisements appearing on their existing smart display devices across the Echo lineup.

### Key Points
- Amazon recently announced the Echo Show 16 to compete with Apple’s upcoming HomeView smart display.
- Users report that the "Created For You" feature is displaying irrelevant, AI-generated content and images on Echo Show devices.
- Owners can provide feedback by tapping the three-dot menu on a slide and selecting the thumbs-down icon.
- Content categories can be managed by navigating to Settings, then Display & Appearance, and finally Content Categories on the device.
- Users can limit unwanted slides by enabling the "Rotate Once" setting or by configuring the display to show only personal photo albums.

### Why it Matters
The rise of AI-generated content on smart home devices highlights a growing tension between platform monetization and user experience. As companies integrate generative tools into hardware, providing clear opt-out mechanisms is essential to maintaining consumer trust and device utility.
