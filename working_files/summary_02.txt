GTA 6 Leaker Promises Escalating Mayhem As Meme Coin Crashes
============================================================

An anonymous leaker known as Cyberleek is attempting to ransom alleged Grand Theft Auto 6 gameplay footage by tying its release to the market performance of a meme coin.

### Key Points
- The leaker claims to possess a 200GB playable build of Grand Theft Auto 6 and various gameplay clips.
- Access to the alleged footage is contingent on the $Cyberleek cryptocurrency reaching specific market capitalization milestones up to $30 million.
- The $Cyberleek token value dropped 50% in 24 hours, currently holding a market cap of approximately $2.2 million.
- The account operator claims the scheme protests anti-consumer practices, specifically demanding a physical disc version of the game from publisher Take-Two.
- Official release of Grand Theft Auto 6 is scheduled for late 2025, with no confirmation regarding the authenticity of the leaked materials.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the growing trend of using high-profile intellectual property leaks to manipulate volatile cryptocurrency markets. It underscores the ongoing security challenges faced by major game publishers like Rockstar Games as they approach highly anticipated product launches.
