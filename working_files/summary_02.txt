AI-native patent firm Fearn launches to take on the billable hour
============================================================

Fearn has launched an AI-native patent firm that utilizes proprietary software to reduce legal drafting time and costs for startups operating in competitive, high-tech industry sectors.

### Key Points
- Fearn raised $5.5 million from investors including Kindred Ventures and a16z Speedrun to disrupt the $14 billion global patent market.
- The firm’s proprietary FearnOS platform reduces patent drafting time from 40 hours to as little as 30 minutes using AI-assisted workflows.
- Services include provisional filings for $2,500 and non-provisional applications for $9,000, with a guarantee to refund drafting fees if no claims are allowed.
- Every application is reviewed by former Big Law patent experts to ensure quality and legal accuracy alongside AI-generated drafts.
- The firm serves startups across diverse sectors, including robotics, semiconductors, defense, biotech, and software development.

### Why it Matters
By integrating AI directly into the legal business model, Fearn lowers the financial and temporal barriers that often prevent early-stage startups from securing essential intellectual property. This shift challenges the traditional, high-cost Big Law model and allows smaller companies to protect their innovations at the speed of modern technological development.
