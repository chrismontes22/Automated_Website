Slovakia finds Russian backdoor in traffic speed cameras
============================================================

Slovakia’s national security agency has issued an alert regarding NERO R-ONE traffic cameras, which contain a Russian-linked backdoor allowing unauthorized network access via hardcoded SMS commands.

### Key Points
- The NBU identified the cameras as rebranded CORDON PRO.M devices manufactured by the Russian firm Semicon.
- Security analysis revealed a backdoor triggered by SMS messages from hardcoded Russian phone numbers.
- Technical flaws include disabled SecureBoot, vulnerable web management portals, and unencrypted live video streams.
- The Interior Ministry paused the deployment of 279 cameras purchased for a €30 million EU-funded traffic monitoring project.
- Similar devices are reportedly installed in Croatia and other Eastern European nations.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the significant national security risks associated with integrating foreign-manufactured hardware into critical infrastructure. The discovery of backdoors in government-procured equipment underscores the urgent need for rigorous supply chain vetting and independent security audits for public sector technology projects.
