4 AI Decisions Tech Leaders Will Tackle In New York This November
============================================================

The Technology & Innovation Forum East, held November 4–5 in New York City, will guide tech leaders in making critical strategic decisions regarding enterprise-wide artificial intelligence implementation and governance.

### Key Points
- The event focuses on four core pillars: prioritizing AI investments, scaling infrastructure, evolving IT operating models, and preparing for autonomous agentic systems.
- Forrester analysts will lead sessions on managing AI costs, reducing technical debt, and establishing accountability frameworks for increasingly autonomous technology.
- Practical workshops will address workforce transformation, including strategies for AI literacy, organizational change management, and redefining human-machine decision rights.
- Keynotes will explore the shift from experimental AI projects to sustainable business value, emphasizing the integration of data quality and robust enterprise architecture.

### Why it Matters
As organizations move past the initial experimentation phase, leaders must transition from launching isolated pilots to building cohesive, scalable AI ecosystems. This forum provides the necessary framework to align infrastructure, governance, and human capital to ensure long-term competitive advantage.
