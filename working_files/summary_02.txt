This Harry Potter Edition Phone From Realme Is the Coolest Collab of 2026
============================================================

Realme has launched a limited-edition Harry Potter version of its 16 Pro 5G smartphone, featuring extensive thematic design elements, custom software, and exclusive collectibles for franchise fans.

### Key Points
- The device features a leather-like brown rear panel with gold accents, a Hogwarts crest, and light-sensing color-changing technology.
- Hardware includes a 6.78-inch 144Hz AMOLED display, MediaTek Dimensity 7300-Max processor, 12GB of RAM, and a 7,000-mAh battery.
- The phone is equipped with a 200-megapixel primary camera and supports 80-watt fast charging.
- Software customizations include a Harry Potter boot animation, themed icons, and a Hogwarts-inspired user interface.
- The package includes a trunk-shaped box, house-themed bookmarks, postcards, and a Hogwarts acceptance letter.
- Limited to 5,000 units, the device is priced at INR 62,999 and is currently exclusive to the Indian market.

### Why it Matters
This collaboration highlights the growing trend of using niche pop-culture branding to differentiate hardware in a saturated smartphone market. By integrating deep software and accessory customization, Realme aims to drive collector interest and brand loyalty among dedicated fan bases.
