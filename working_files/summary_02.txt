North Korea rejects U.S. allegation over $6 billion Binance crypto heist
============================================================

Binance co-CEO Richard Teng reports that asset outflows have normalized following a $6.3 billion heist, while North Korea dismisses U.S. allegations of involvement in the massive cyberattack.

### Key Points
- Binance experienced $4.72 billion in net outflows following a major security breach, but the exchange claims 95% of affected tokens have been replenished.
- The FBI alleges North Korea is responsible for the $6.3 billion theft, a claim Pyongyang official Kim Yo Jong labeled as "absurd rhetoric."
- Blockchain analytics firm Elliptic estimates North Korea has stolen $7.6 billion in cryptocurrency this year to fund its nuclear and ballistic missile programs.
- Binance has launched a "community appreciation campaign" offering fee rebates and yield incentives to stabilize its user base through November 21.
- Chainalysis reports that North Korea was linked to 89% of all crypto hack losses in 2026, up significantly from 15% in 2021.

### Why it Matters
The incident highlights the growing vulnerability of centralized cryptocurrency exchanges to state-sponsored cyberattacks aimed at bypassing international sanctions. If the U.S. implements stricter regulations on crypto wallets and exchanges, it could significantly disrupt a primary revenue stream used to finance North Korea's weapons development.
