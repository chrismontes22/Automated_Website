Trump’s media company reports $238m loss
============================================================

Trump Media & Technology Group reported a $238 million net loss for the second quarter of 2026, driven largely by unrealized losses on digital assets and equity securities.

### Key Points
- TMTG recorded $1.7 million in revenue for the quarter, an 89 percent increase compared to the same period in 2025.
- The company’s total net losses for the first half of 2026 reached $644 million against $2.5 million in total revenue.
- Unrealized losses on digital assets and equity securities accounted for $190.4 million of the quarterly shortfall.
- Shares of the company, trading under the ticker "DJT," fell 8 percent following the Monday filing with the SEC.
- The firm’s new Truth API service, which provides early access to market-moving posts, has secured 10 corporate clients paying monthly fees up to $100,000.
- Similarweb data indicates that visitor traffic to the Truth Social platform declined by more than one-third in July compared to the previous year.

### Why it Matters
The widening gap between TMTG’s operational costs and revenue highlights the financial volatility inherent in the company's business model. Investors remain focused on whether the firm can successfully monetize its influence through new ventures like Truth API while navigating declining user engagement on its core social platform.
