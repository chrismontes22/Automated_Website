GTA 6 Leaker Drops New Nudity-Filled Gameplay, Polls People on Whether to Release Story Spoilers
============================================================

New footage from the upcoming Grand Theft Auto 6 has leaked online, featuring explicit content and threats from the leaker to release further story spoilers and character details.

### Key Points
- The leaked footage depicts the protagonist Jason exploring a nudist town, featuring explicit character models and violent gameplay sequences.
- The leaker, known as "CyberLeek," is soliciting cryptocurrency payments from fans to determine which game assets to release next.
- Protesters are being encouraged to gather outside Rockstar Games and Take-Two Interactive offices while dressed as leeks to protest digital-only game releases.
- Rockstar Games and parent company Take-Two Interactive have issued subpoenas to platforms including Discord, Microsoft, and X to identify the source of the leaks.
- Netflix is scheduled to host an exclusive reveal of GTA 6 gameplay footage on August 27, ahead of the game's November 19, 2026, release date.

### Why it Matters
These persistent security breaches create significant internal pressure at Rockstar Games as the studio prepares for its highly anticipated title launch. The situation highlights growing concerns regarding intellectual property protection and the potential for bad actors to exploit fan anticipation for financial gain.
