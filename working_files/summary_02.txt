Critical PHP vulnerabilities force government websites offline
============================================================

Government agencies worldwide have taken websites offline following the discovery of critical PHP vulnerabilities, dubbed PHP2Shell, which could potentially impact up to 70% of the internet.

### Key Points
- OpenAI’s GPT 5.6-Sol model identified the flaws, which allow unauthorized remote command execution on servers running outdated PHP code.
- The PHP Foundation released emergency patches after the vulnerabilities were prematurely leaked by the cybersecurity firm Chainguard.
- Global agencies, including the U.S. NSA and FBI, issued urgent warnings advising organizations to take systems offline if immediate patching is not possible.
- Cloudflare reported a massive surge in exploitation attempts, escalating from thousands to millions of attacks within hours of the public disclosure.
- Security firm Wiz categorized these vulnerabilities as among the most severe in software history, noting they were previously considered too complex for human discovery.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights a dangerous new era where AI accelerates both the discovery of critical infrastructure flaws and the speed of global cyberattacks. It forces organizations to re-evaluate the security of foundational web technologies that have remained largely unchanged for decades.
