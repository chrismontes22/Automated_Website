I captured 72 hours of idle Android packets behind pfSense
============================================================

A new study by PraveenTechWorld reveals that a factory-default Google Pixel 8 smartphone transmits data to Alphabet servers over 8,000 times daily, even while locked and completely idle.

### Key Points
- Researchers used an external hardware wiretap to monitor three Google Pixel 8 devices over 72 hours of continuous, undisturbed idle time.
- Stock Android devices averaged 348 background telemetry pings per hour, totaling approximately 8,300 transmissions every 24 hours.
- Data sent includes nearby Wi-Fi router MAC addresses for location triangulation, hardware serial numbers, and search query prefetching.
- Installing GrapheneOS reduced background connections by 96.5%, while a fully de-Googled installation resulted in zero background data transmissions.
- The study provides a comprehensive dataset and a 42-endpoint blocklist for users to mitigate tracking via tools like Pi-hole or AdGuard.

### Why it Matters
This research highlights the significant volume of passive data collection occurring on modern mobile devices without direct user interaction. These findings provide critical transparency for privacy-conscious consumers and system administrators looking to audit or restrict background telemetry on their personal networks.
