Polymarket Halted International Trading After Hackers Drained Accounts
============================================================

Polymarket has resumed operations on its international prediction platform following a security breach at a third-party vendor that resulted in approximately $2.7 million in stolen user funds.

### Key Points
- A UK-based third-party vendor breach allowed unauthorized access to some trader wallets on the international Polymarket platform.
- Blockchain analytics firm Bubblemaps estimates that roughly 700 users were affected by the incident.
- Polymarket has committed to refunding all traders impacted by the unauthorized withdrawals.
- The company’s US-based platform, which operates under federal financial regulations, remained unaffected by the security event.
- Polymarket previously experienced a similar security breach in June 2026 that resulted in over $2 million in losses.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the persistent security vulnerabilities facing decentralized prediction markets that rely on third-party integrations. As the platform continues to scale following a recent $1 billion funding round, these recurring security issues may impact user trust and regulatory scrutiny.
