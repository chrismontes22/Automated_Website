Turns out Brits would quite like their private messages to stay private
============================================================

An Oxford judge has dismissed a three-year legal pursuit by AICompany against energy firm SSE, ruling that the debt claim originated from a non-existent business address.

### Key Points
- AICompany attempted to collect alleged debts from SSE through prolonged litigation.
- The presiding judge threw out the case after confirming the claimant’s address did not exist.
- This ruling concludes a three-year legal battle between the two entities.
- The case highlights the risks of automated or poorly vetted debt collection practices in the energy sector.

### Why it Matters
This case serves as a cautionary example of the legal and reputational risks associated with aggressive, automated debt recovery strategies. It underscores the necessity for companies to verify the legitimacy of claims before initiating formal court proceedings to avoid wasting judicial resources.
