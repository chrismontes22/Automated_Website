Crypto thieves attack man in home and threaten to kill pregnant wife's baby in 'horrific' robbery
============================================================

Masked intruders violently assaulted a Solihull businessman and his pregnant wife with hammers, forcing a transfer of hundreds of thousands of pounds in cryptocurrency during a home invasion.

### Key Points
- Three masked men broke into a Solihull home in December, assaulting the victim with hammers for 45 minutes while threatening his pregnant wife.
- The attackers followed instructions from an accomplice via a live video call to identify and drain the victim's cryptocurrency wallet.
- In addition to the digital assets, the criminals stole several luxury Rolex watches before fleeing the scene in a getaway vehicle.
- Crimestoppers is offering a £10,000 reward for information leading to the identification of the suspects, who remain at large.
- Security experts report a global surge in "wrench attacks," where criminals use physical violence to bypass the lack of institutional safeguards in self-custody crypto wallets.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the growing physical security risks faced by cryptocurrency investors who lack the protection of traditional banking institutions. As digital assets become more valuable, the rise of violent home invasions underscores the urgent need for better personal security measures for those holding significant wealth in private wallets.
