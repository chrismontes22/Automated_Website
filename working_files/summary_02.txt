A simple explainer of the massive $6.3 billion crypto heist at Binance
============================================================

North Korean military hackers stole $6.3 billion from the Binance cryptocurrency exchange in a sophisticated cyberattack, prompting significant concerns regarding the security and regulation of digital asset platforms.

### Key Points
- Hackers breached Binance accounts at 4:05 p.m. ET on Thursday, completing the theft in approximately 20 minutes.
- The FBI confirmed the attack was orchestrated by the North Korean military hacking unit known as TraderTraitor.
- Binance successfully froze $3 billion of the stolen assets and has reimbursed nearly 100% of customer losses using its own holdings.
- Critics highlight that crypto exchanges lack the strict regulatory oversight, insurance requirements, and auditing standards mandated for traditional Wall Street brokerage firms.

### Why it Matters
This incident underscores the extreme vulnerability of centralized cryptocurrency exchanges to state-sponsored cyber warfare. It intensifies the debate over whether the crypto industry requires more rigorous federal regulation to protect investors from catastrophic financial losses.
