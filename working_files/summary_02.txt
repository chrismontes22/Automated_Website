Community banks sue OCC over trust bank charters of crypto firms
============================================================

The Independent Community Bankers of America has filed a lawsuit against the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency, challenging its authority to grant national trust charters to cryptocurrency firms.

### Key Points
- The ICBA filed the lawsuit in the U.S. District Court for the District of Columbia on Friday.
- The trade group alleges the OCC bypassed congressional intent by allowing crypto companies to obtain federal bank charters without meeting standard regulatory requirements.
- Plaintiffs argue these firms avoid obligations like the Community Reinvestment Act, FDIC insurance, and strict capital liquidity standards.
- The OCC began approving or conditionally approving these trust charters for crypto companies under the Trump administration.
- National trust charters currently restrict firms from accepting traditional deposits or issuing commercial loans.

### Why it Matters
This legal challenge highlights a growing tension between traditional financial institutions and the expanding regulatory footprint of digital asset companies. A court ruling could force federal regulators to tighten oversight of crypto firms or potentially revoke the legitimacy granted by these specialized banking charters.
