Recent developments in artificial intelligence highlight growing concerns regarding data center energy usage, the spread of AI-generated misinformation, and the rapid automation of professional and creative industries.

- New York City venues have begun banning smart glasses to protect privacy, reflecting rising public anxiety over pervasive surveillance technology.

### Why it Matters
The rapid integration of AI into infrastructure, media, and professional workflows is creating significant ethical and regulatory challenges. These incidents demonstrate how the unchecked expansion of AI technology can outpace existing oversight, leading to environmental concerns, misinformation, and the erosion of traditional labor roles.
