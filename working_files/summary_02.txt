Stop Chasing AI Search Rankings. Start Defining the Category Instead.
============================================================

"Machine Relations" creator explains why establishing consistent industry definitions is more effective for long-term AI search visibility than competing for volatile, daily-changing rankings on AI recommendation lists.

### Key Points
- AI search engines frequently rotate cited websites, with Gemini and ChatGPT dropping over 35% of previously cited sources daily.
- "Machine Relations" is defined as the practice of making a brand legible and credible to AI by establishing clear problem definitions and evaluation criteria.
- Research from the publication Paralax shows that AI engines are four times more likely to cite neutral, explanatory sources when asked how to evaluate a category.
- Category leaders capture approximately 76% of market value, a trend that is amplified when AI models adopt a specific company's framework to explain a market.
- To dominate AI discovery, companies should publish a single, dated definition of their category and ensure consistent usage across all public-facing materials.

### Why it Matters
Establishing a proprietary framework allows a company to set the criteria by which all competitors are judged, effectively controlling the narrative before a buyer even sees a list of vendors. Because AI models prioritize consistent, authoritative explanations, owning the definition ensures a brand remains the primary reference point regardless of how often search rankings fluctuate.
