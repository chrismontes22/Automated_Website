Hacksaw Targets Operators With Peer-to-Peer Prediction Markets
============================================================

Hacksaw Group has launched a B2B peer-to-peer prediction-market platform, allowing iGaming operators to integrate event-based trading technology while maintaining their own brand identity and customer relationships.

### Key Points
- The Malta Gaming Authority granted Hacksaw an extension to its B2B Critical Gaming Supply License to include Type 3 Gaming Services.
- The platform utilizes a connected volume framework to pool participation across the company’s existing network of iGaming operators.
- Hacksaw developed the new infrastructure within its current cost base without disclosing specific rollout schedules or initial commercial partners.
- The company cites Bernstein estimates suggesting the global prediction-market sector could reach $1 trillion in annual trading volume by 2030.
- Hacksaw Group remains a B2B technology provider and will not operate as a consumer-facing exchange for these prediction markets.

### Why it Matters
This expansion allows Hacksaw to diversify its revenue streams by entering the high-growth prediction-market sector without the overhead of managing direct consumer relationships. By providing the underlying infrastructure to established operators, the company positions itself to capture market share in an emerging category while leveraging its existing regulatory approvals and distribution network.
