Meta has acquired the AI agent orchestration startup Conductor to enhance its internal model training capabilities and streamline the development of automated workflows across its platform.

### Key Points
- Meta finalized the acquisition of Conductor to integrate its specialized agent orchestration technology into existing AI development pipelines.
- Conductor provides tools designed to manage and coordinate complex AI agent tasks, which Meta aims to leverage for improved model performance.
- The deal marks a strategic move by Meta to bolster its infrastructure as competition in the generative AI and automation sector intensifies.
- Financial terms of the acquisition were not disclosed by either company following the announcement.

### Why it Matters
This acquisition highlights Meta's ongoing commitment to scaling its AI infrastructure through targeted talent and technology purchases. By integrating Conductor’s orchestration capabilities, Meta can improve the efficiency of its AI agents, potentially setting a new standard for how large-scale models are managed and deployed in production environments.
