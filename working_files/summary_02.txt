Nscale closed a $3.36B pre-IPO round led by Third Point, with $1B from Nvidia still to come
============================================================

London-based Nscale has secured $3.36 billion in a pre-IPO convertible note round led by Third Point, with significant backing from Nvidia ahead of its New York Stock Exchange listing.

### Key Points
- The funding round includes a $2.36 billion tranche and a $1 billion commitment from Nvidia expected in mid-November.
- Investors in the round include Apollo, Citadel, Hudson Bay Capital, the Abu Dhabi Investment Council, and 8090 Industries.
- Nscale reported $140.6 million in revenue for the first half of 2024, alongside a net loss of $1.02 billion.
- The company’s contracted backlog has grown to $103.4 billion, up from $38 billion at the end of 2023.
- The firm, which features Sheryl Sandberg and Nick Clegg on its board, filed to list on the NYSE under the ticker NSCL.

### Why it Matters
This massive capital injection highlights the intense investor demand for infrastructure companies supporting the artificial intelligence sector. By securing Nvidia as a major stakeholder, Nscale strengthens its market position while providing the chipmaker with further influence over its primary hardware customers.
