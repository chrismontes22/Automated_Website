⚡ Weekly Recap: This2Shell, That2Shell, Everything's an RCE? Rogue AI, Supply Chain Chaos and More
============================================================

A surge of critical remote code execution vulnerabilities, including the widespread PHP2Shell flaws, has triggered global security alerts and forced organizations to prioritize urgent patching and system hardening.

### Key Points
- The PHP Foundation released patches for two critical PHP2Shell vulnerabilities (CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619) that saw millions of exploitation attempts.
- Google’s Brotli decoder library and several dependent tools were patched against a severe remote code execution flaw dubbed Node2Shell.
- eMudhra exited the TLS certificate market after major browsers blocked its certificates due to the use of weak private keys.
- LiteLLM suffered a supply chain attack after a leaked master key allowed attackers to inject malicious code into a popular Python package.
- Plex Media Server vulnerabilities are being actively exploited in the wild to deploy cryptocurrency miners and proxy software.
- Rapid7 reports that the average time between a high-severity vulnerability disclosure and active exploitation has shrunk to just three days.

### Why it Matters
The rapid weaponization of newly disclosed vulnerabilities and the compromise of trusted software supply chains demonstrate that traditional patching cycles are no longer sufficient. Organizations must adopt proactive threat intelligence and rigorous exposure management to defend against attackers who are increasingly leveraging AI to accelerate their exploitation efforts.
