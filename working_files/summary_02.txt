Disney+ Turns To TikTok In Search Of Daily Engagement
============================================================

Disney and TikTok have launched a U.S. pilot program featuring fan-made short videos within the Disney+ vertical feed, aiming to increase user engagement and app retention.

### Key Points
- Selected TikTok videos featuring Disney, Marvel, Star Wars, and Pixar characters will appear in the Disney+ "Verts" feed.
- Participating creators gain access to official Disney assets through a new, tiered Disney Creator Ambassador Program.
- The pilot is currently limited to the United States, with plans to expand into additional global markets in the future.
- Financial terms, creator compensation, and specific content moderation policies remain undisclosed by both companies.
- Disney aims to transform its streaming service from a destination for deliberate viewing into a habitual, daily engagement platform.

### Why it Matters
This partnership represents a strategic shift for Disney+ as it attempts to adopt the high-frequency, habitual consumption patterns popularized by social media platforms. By integrating external creator content, Disney hopes to increase the time subscribers spend in the app and bridge the gap between official releases.
