Notorious Hacker Steals 250,000 User Discord Server For Infamous Terraria Mod Calamity, Immediately Uses It To Pump And Dump A Crypto Coin
============================================================

The popular Terraria mod Calamity saw its official Discord server compromised by a notorious hacker, leading to a cryptocurrency pump-and-dump scheme targeting over 250,000 community members.

### Key Points
- The Calamity Discord server was breached on October 9, 2026, after the owner was tricked into granting access to individuals posing as former moderators.
- A hacker known as "Kata" utilized the compromised server to promote a fraudulent cryptocurrency, resulting in a classic pump-and-dump scam.
- The Calamity mod had previously ceased all development in August following multiple allegations of child endangerment involving the project's staff.
- A former moderator successfully "nuked" the server's contents to stop the spread of malicious links, though the hackers retained control of the remaining announcement channel.
- Tens of thousands of users have exited the server as the r/CalamityMod subreddit issued emergency warnings to avoid engaging with the scammers.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the ongoing security vulnerabilities facing large community Discord servers when administrators are targeted by social engineering attacks. The breach serves as a stark reminder for users to remain vigilant against unauthorized financial solicitations, even within long-standing and trusted gaming communities.
