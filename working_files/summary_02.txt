Trump gives 'national treasure' Musk prestigious medal after political falling-out
============================================================

President Donald Trump awarded the National Medal of Science to SpaceX and Tesla CEO Elon Musk during a ceremony honoring six prominent American technology industry leaders in Washington.

### Key Points
- Elon Musk received the National Medal of Science alongside Google co-founder Sergei Brin, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, and AMD chief Lisa Su.
- Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella and Michael Dell were awarded the National Medal of Technology and Innovation at the "Science: A New Golden Age" summit.
- The event marked a public reconciliation between Trump and Musk following their highly publicized fallout over the Department of Government Efficiency last year.
- Trump also accepted a posthumous National Medal of Science on behalf of his late uncle, MIT professor John Trump.
- During the summit, industry leaders including Musk and Huang committed to self-regulating artificial intelligence, which Trump refers to as "super intelligence."

### Why it Matters
This ceremony signals a strategic realignment between the White House and key technology titans who are essential to U.S. defense and economic infrastructure. By mending ties with Musk and engaging with industry leaders, the administration aims to secure private-sector cooperation for its economic agenda while bypassing traditional government oversight of emerging technologies.
