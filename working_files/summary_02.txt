Tim Cook’s legacy isn’t just phones, it’s politics
============================================================

Tim Cook’s fifteen-year tenure as Apple CEO is defined by his strategic navigation of complex geopolitical tensions between the United States and China to protect the company's supply chain.

### Key Points
- Cook maintained Apple’s manufacturing dominance in China by balancing relations with President Xi Jinping while managing political scrutiny in the United States.
- The company faced criticism for storing Chinese user data on mainland servers and removing apps to comply with local government censorship requirements.
- Cook successfully lobbied the Trump administration for tariff exemptions on key products like the Apple Watch during the US-China trade war.
- Apple’s leadership navigated domestic political pressure by offering symbolic gestures, such as manufacturing tours and donations, to appease US policymakers.
- Following his transition to executive chairman, Cook will continue to serve as Apple’s primary negotiator with global policymakers.

### Why it Matters
Cook’s ability to maintain Apple’s massive manufacturing infrastructure amidst escalating superpower conflicts was essential to the company’s multi-trillion-dollar valuation. This legacy highlights the extreme geopolitical dependencies of modern tech giants and the complex ethical compromises required to sustain global supply chains.
