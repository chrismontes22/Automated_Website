U.S. Seizes NightmareStresser Domains Linked to Hundreds of Thousands of DDoS Attacks
============================================================

The U.S. Department of Justice and international law enforcement agencies have seized domains associated with NightmareStresser, a prominent DDoS-for-hire service used to launch hundreds of thousands of cyberattacks.

### Key Points
- The FBI and Royal Canadian Mounted Police seized domains nightmare-stresser.com and nightmarestresser.org under a warrant from the U.S. District Court for the District of Alaska.
- NightmareStresser reportedly hosted over 566,000 registered users and 52 servers, facilitating attacks against government agencies, gaming platforms, and educational institutions since 2022.
- The platform offered advanced Layer 4 and Layer 7 attack methods, including features to bypass CAPTCHAs, geoblocks, and rate limits for paying cryptocurrency customers.
- This action is part of Operation PowerOFF, a global law enforcement initiative that has now seized over 100 domains and charged twelve defendants related to booter services.

### Why it Matters
The dismantling of NightmareStresser disrupts a major source of malicious traffic that frequently degrades internet services and targets critical infrastructure. This operation highlights the ongoing success of international cooperation in targeting the commercialized cybercrime ecosystem that enables widespread digital disruption.
