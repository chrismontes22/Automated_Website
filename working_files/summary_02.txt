EU authorities include HTX exchange in Russian sanctions
============================================================

The European Union has officially sanctioned the cryptocurrency exchange HTX, formerly known as Huobi Global, for allegedly facilitating financial services that undermine restrictive measures imposed against Russia.

### Key Points
- The European Council added HTX to a list of 18 entities accused of providing crypto-asset services that frustrate EU sanctions against Russia.
- EU officials claim these entities provide a financial lifeline for Russia’s ongoing military operations in Ukraine.
- The UK government previously sanctioned HTX in May 2024 over suspicions that the platform supported the Russian government.
- The EU simultaneously announced new restrictions prohibiting Belarusian nationals from managing or controlling crypto-asset service providers within the bloc.
- HTX has stated that regulatory compliance remains its absolute priority and that it monitors frameworks in all jurisdictions.

### Why it Matters
These sanctions signal an intensifying effort by Western regulators to close loopholes that allow digital assets to bypass international financial restrictions. By targeting crypto exchanges, the EU aims to prevent the circumvention of measures designed to limit Russia's economic capacity during the war in Ukraine.
