ChatGPT Can Now Add Your Mac Activity to Its Memories
============================================================

OpenAI has launched a new Computer History feature for macOS that tracks user activity across websites and applications to provide personalized context for its ChatGPT and Codex models.

### Key Points
- The feature is currently available to ChatGPT Pro, Business, and Enterprise subscribers on macOS, excluding users in the UK, Switzerland, and the European Economic Area.
- It utilizes the macOS accessibility API to log clicks, typing, keyboard shortcuts, and app switches rather than recording screen visuals or audio.
- Users maintain control over the feature through manual opt-in, the ability to pause tracking, and the option to delete specific history logs at any time.
- OpenAI states that activity summaries are generated in the cloud but stored locally, and the data is not used for training its AI models.
- The company warns that Computer History files are not encrypted by the application and could potentially be accessed by other programs running on the user's system.

### Why it Matters
This feature represents a significant shift toward highly personalized AI agents that anticipate user needs by monitoring cross-application behavior. While it offers increased productivity, the lack of native file encryption raises potential security concerns regarding how sensitive user data is handled and stored on local devices.
