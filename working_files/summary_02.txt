A Prediction Market About the Past? Sure, Why Not!
============================================================

The new startup Verifact Markets has launched a prediction platform that allows users to wager on the truthfulness of disputed historical events and controversial past news stories.

### Key Points
- Verifact Markets allows users to trade "true" or "false" contracts on past events like the origins of Covid-19 and the death of Jeffrey Epstein.
- The platform is funded by Positron Capital Management, the family office of tech investor Peter Wokwicz.
- Users can submit evidence to support their positions, which the company claims it will analyze using a proprietary, secretive mathematical system.
- The service is currently restricted to users outside the United States as the company seeks a viable legal path for domestic operation.
- Trades are conducted on-chain using stablecoins, and the platform does not currently charge transaction fees.

### Why it Matters
This platform represents a novel, albeit controversial, attempt to gamify the adjudication of historical facts and public debates. By incentivizing users to provide evidence for disputed claims, it challenges traditional methods of information verification and raises significant questions about the reliability of crowd-sourced truth.
