Luxury smartphone manufacturer Vertu has unveiled the Agent Q, a high-end AI device featuring a unique falcon-wing design and premium materials like alligator skin.

### Key Points
- The device is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset.
- It runs on a proprietary operating system known as VAOS.
- The phone includes a dedicated concierge button, offering one year of free butler services.
- Design elements prioritize luxury materials and craftsmanship over standard mobile gaming performance.
- The product is specifically targeted at ultra-high-net-worth individuals in markets like China and the Middle East.

### Why it Matters
The Vertu Agent Q highlights a niche segment of the mobile market where status and exclusive services take precedence over technical specifications. This release underscores how luxury brands differentiate themselves from mass-market tech giants by offering bespoke concierge support and exotic materials to affluent consumers.
