DLSS 5 proves that Nvidia has lost the plot
============================================================

Nvidia’s upcoming DLSS 5 technology faces significant criticism for using generative AI to alter game aesthetics, causing performance drops of up to 50% on high-end Blackwell GPUs.

### Key Points
- DLSS 5 functions as a photorealism filter rather than a traditional upscaler, often overriding the original artistic intent of game developers.
- Early testing shows inconsistent visual quality, with reports of distorted character models and graphical artifacts in titles like Final Fantasy VII Remake.
- The technology is currently restricted to Nvidia’s RTX 5000-series graphics cards, with future support planned for the RTX 4000-series.
- Enabling the feature can reduce frame rates by as much as 50%, necessitating the simultaneous use of traditional upscaling and frame generation.
- Critics argue the tech acts as a performance-heavy crutch that may pressure developers to design games specifically for AI-enhanced visual outputs.

### Why it Matters
This technology represents a shift toward AI-driven visual modification that could fundamentally change how games are developed and experienced. If widely adopted, it risks creating a hardware-gated standard that prioritizes synthetic photorealism over the original creative vision of game designers.
