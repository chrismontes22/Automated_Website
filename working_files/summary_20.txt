Strange Times
============================================================

The United States economy currently faces a complex landscape defined by rising interest rates, persistent inflation, and strong GDP growth despite historically low consumer sentiment and market volatility.

### Key Points
- Mortgage rates have climbed above 7%, while the Federal Reserve continues to hike interest rates to combat a 3.4% inflation rate.
- The Atlanta Fed forecasts 5% real GDP growth for the third quarter, supported by a resilient labor market where unemployment claims have dropped significantly.
- Treasury yields have surged across all maturities, with the 10-year yield rising from 0.5% in March 2020 to approximately 5.2% today.
- Despite rising rates, the S&P 500 has delivered an 18.8% annualized return since March 2020, largely driven by massive capital expenditure in artificial intelligence.
- Long-term bonds have experienced significant losses, contrasting sharply with the strong performance of equity markets during the same period.

### Why it Matters
The current economic environment challenges traditional financial theories that suggest rising interest rates should consistently suppress stock market performance. Understanding these conflicting indicators is essential for investors navigating a period where AI-driven growth and government spending continue to decouple market returns from standard monetary policy expectations.
