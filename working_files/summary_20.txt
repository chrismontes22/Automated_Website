iPhone Duo's inner display protector is replaceable, here's how much it costs
============================================================

Apple has confirmed that the top protective layer of the iPhone Duo’s foldable screen can be replaced for $19.99 for customers with an active AppleCare subscription.

### Key Points
- AppleCare subscribers can replace the iPhone Duo’s matte screen cover for a $19.99 service fee at authorized service providers.
- The company offers additional repair tiers, including $29 for cover display or back glass repairs and $99 for inner display replacements.
- Accidental damage claims are priced at $99, while theft and loss protection coverage costs $149.
- Apple’s hardware engineering team designed the top layer to be replaceable to address natural wear and tear over the device's lifespan.
- Competitor Samsung currently provides the first screen protector replacement for free within the first year, charging $19.99 for subsequent services.

### Why it Matters
This repair policy highlights Apple's strategy to manage the long-term durability concerns associated with foldable display technology. By offering an affordable replacement path for the outer layer, the company aims to reduce the total cost of ownership and improve consumer confidence in foldable hardware.
