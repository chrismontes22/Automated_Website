10 Friday AM Reads
============================================================

Rising interest rates and inflation are pressuring market valuations, while global concerns grow regarding U.S. economic stability and the long-term dominance of the dollar in international finance.

### Key Points
- Mortgage rates have climbed to 7% and the 10-year Treasury yield reached 5%, contributing to falling valuations despite a bull market.
- Municipal bonds are currently yielding 5%, offering a tax-advantaged alternative that competes with equity returns for conservative investors.
- Bloomberg reports that Donald Trump has traded more securities than all members of Congress combined since returning to office.
- Global investors are increasingly wary of U.S. Treasuries as foreign governments move gold reserves out of American vaults.
- BlackRock recently liquidated eight additional ETFs, highlighting the ongoing volatility and turnover within the exchange-traded fund market.

### Why it Matters
These shifts suggest a potential turning point for the U.S. economy as debt levels rise and international confidence in the dollar begins to waver. Investors must navigate a complex environment where traditional assets like bonds are becoming more competitive against stocks while geopolitical risks increase.
