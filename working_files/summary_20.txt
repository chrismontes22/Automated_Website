Natural gas is cheap, so why is your bill so high?
============================================================

Rising natural gas delivery charges in Pennsylvania are expected to climb further as utilities expand infrastructure to support the massive energy demands of new regional data centers.

### Key Points
- Pennsylvania residents face rising gas bills driven by fixed delivery and capacity charges rather than the cost of the fuel itself.
- Utilities recover infrastructure investment costs from all ratepayers, a model that analysts expect will be impacted by the surge in data center construction.
- PJM, the regional grid operator, projects that data centers could account for 38% of grid demand by 2029, requiring significant new power plant development.
- While tech companies have pledged to protect consumers from energy price hikes, experts note that current regulatory frameworks make it difficult to isolate these costs.
- Local municipalities are implementing zoning restrictions to mitigate environmental impacts, but these measures do not address the rising utility rates for residents.

### Why it Matters
The shift toward AI-driven data centers is creating a significant financial burden for residential ratepayers who must subsidize the necessary grid and pipeline upgrades. Because utility companies are guaranteed a return on infrastructure investments, consumers often pay for capacity expansions regardless of whether the projected energy demand fully materializes.
