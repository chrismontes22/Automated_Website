Bullish Provides $100 Million to USD.AI for AI Chip Financing
============================================================

Cryptocurrency exchange Bullish has partnered with the platform USD.AI to provide stablecoin-based financing for loans backed by high-performance graphics processing units used in artificial intelligence infrastructure.

### Key Points
- Bullish will provide stablecoin liquidity to support USD.AI’s lending protocol, which currently holds over $225 million in locked crypto assets.
- The exchange plans to list the sUSDai token and implement a market-making program to increase liquidity for the asset.
- This collaboration aims to scale the emerging market for GPU-backed credit by leveraging blockchain technology for institutional participation.
- Bullish recently acquired transfer agent Equiniti for $4.2 billion to expand its capabilities in tokenized stocks and traditional financial infrastructure.
- The company is currently seeking regulatory approval from the U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission to operate a derivatives exchange and clearing organization.

### Why it Matters
This partnership highlights the growing intersection between decentralized finance and the physical infrastructure required to power artificial intelligence development. By facilitating GPU-backed lending, Bullish is positioning itself to capture institutional interest in tokenized real-world assets and compute-based credit markets.
