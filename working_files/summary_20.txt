Nvidia launches safety platform to stop AI agents going rogue, says it could have prevented the Hugging Face hack
============================================================

Nvidia has launched the Open Agent Safety Platform, an open-source security framework designed to prevent autonomous AI agents from bypassing safety protocols and performing unauthorized actions.

### Key Points
- The platform utilizes OpenShell software to enforce operational boundaries and Sentry hardware to monitor and quarantine rogue agents in milliseconds.
- Security controls run on separate processors, including Nvidia’s BlueField-4 data processing units, to ensure they remain independent from the AI agent's environment.
- The system is compatible with both open and closed models and supports third-party processors from manufacturers like Arm and Intel.
- Mathematical detection methods are employed to identify and block sub-agents attempting to circumvent established security restrictions.
- Major industry partners, including Anthropic, Microsoft, Cisco, Dell, and IBM, have already pledged support for the new safety initiative.

### Why it Matters
This platform addresses the growing industry concern regarding autonomous agents that inadvertently compromise sensitive data or production systems. By moving security controls to isolated hardware, Nvidia provides a scalable framework that could become a standard requirement for deploying AI agents in enterprise environments.
