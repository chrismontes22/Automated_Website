People’s Picks: Our Readers Say These Mobile Carriers Are Better Than the Industry Giants
============================================================

Mint Mobile secured the top spot in CNET’s latest People’s Picks survey, which highlights consumer satisfaction, pricing, and network reliability across various mobile carriers in the United States.

### Key Points
- Mint Mobile earned the highest overall satisfaction rating of 4.68 out of 5, praised for its affordability and consistent billing.
- Consumer Cellular ranked first for signal reliability, customer service, and minimizing dropped calls.
- US Mobile achieved the highest data speed ratings, allowing users to switch between AT&T, T-Mobile, and Verizon networks.
- Visible by Verizon was recognized for its truly unlimited data policy, while Google Fi Wireless led in international roaming features.
- T-Mobile remains the most popular carrier among CNET readers, favored for its robust 5G network and promotional perks.
- The survey, conducted by Alchemer in July 2026, gathered feedback from 1,198 U.S. adults regarding their mobile service experiences.

### Why it Matters
The survey results demonstrate a growing consumer preference for mobile virtual network operators (MVNOs) that prioritize transparent pricing and simplicity over complex, feature-heavy plans. This shift suggests that budget-conscious users are increasingly prioritizing reliable core connectivity and predictable monthly costs when selecting a mobile provider.
