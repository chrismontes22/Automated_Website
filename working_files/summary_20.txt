U.S. Futures Steady as Investors Monitor Oil Prices, Fed Data and Bond Yields: Dow Jones, S&P, Nasdaq, Wall Street
============================================================

U.S. stock futures remained flat on Wednesday as investors weighed rising global government bond yields, escalating tensions between the United States and Iran, and upcoming economic data releases.

### Key Points
- S&P 500 and Nasdaq 100 futures held steady, while Dow Jones Industrial Average futures saw a marginal gain of 0.17%.
- The U.S. 10-year Treasury yield approached 5%, reaching a three-year high, while Japanese and Australian bond yields hit multi-decade peaks.
- U.S. military strikes against Iran’s Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps have heightened concerns regarding oil prices and regional stability in the Strait of Hormuz.
- Market participants are awaiting the Federal Reserve’s Beige Book and further economic reports to gauge the future trajectory of interest rates and inflation.
- The Bank of Canada’s recent policy communications are under scrutiny as global central banks navigate the balance between economic growth and elevated borrowing costs.

### Why it Matters
Rising bond yields and geopolitical instability create a challenging environment for equity valuations by increasing financing costs and inflationary pressures. Investors are closely monitoring these variables to determine if central banks will maintain high interest rates or pivot toward more accommodative monetary policies.
