From design to peel-ready sticker in a single go, the Liene PixCut S1 all-in-one printer and cutter is the ideal back-to-school gadget
============================================================

The Liene PixCut S1 is an all-in-one printer and cutter designed to streamline the creation of custom stickers and labels for students and home users through automated processing.

### Key Points
- The device uses dye-sublimation technology with a 300 x 300 dpi thermal print head to produce smudge-resistant, high-detail prints.
- Users design projects via the Liene Photo app, which features AI-powered background removal and automatic cut-path generation.
- The printer eliminates manual alignment by keeping paper inside the chassis from the initial print pass through the final contour cut.
- Measuring 281 x 196 x 104.5mm, the unit is approximately 70% smaller than traditional multi-device home printing and cutting setups.
- The PixCut S1 Starter Kit includes 18 sheets each of 4 x 7-inch sticker paper and 4 x 6-inch photo paper.
- A promotional price of $237.99 is available from August 24 through August 30, 2026, offering a $42 discount off the standard retail price.

### Why it Matters
This device simplifies the sticker-making process by removing the need for multiple machines and manual intervention, making custom labeling accessible for home and school projects. By integrating printing and cutting into a compact, user-friendly unit, it offers a practical solution for users who previously faced time-consuming, multi-step workflows.
