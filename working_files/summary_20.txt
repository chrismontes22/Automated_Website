Onfolio Holdings Announces 1-for-50 Reverse Stock Split to Regain Nasdaq Compliance
============================================================

Onfolio Holdings will execute a 1-for-50 reverse stock split on August 10, 2026, to regain compliance with Nasdaq’s minimum $1.00 bid price requirement for continued exchange listing.

### Key Points
- Onfolio Holdings (NASDAQ:ONFO) is implementing a 1-for-50 reverse stock split effective August 10, 2026.
- The action aims to satisfy Nasdaq Listing Rule 5550(a)(2), which mandates a minimum $1.00 closing bid price.
- Outstanding common shares will decrease from approximately 42 million to roughly 850,000 following the transaction.
- Fractional shares resulting from the split will be rounded up to the nearest whole share.
- The company will continue trading under the existing ONFO ticker on a split-adjusted basis.

### Why it Matters
Maintaining a Nasdaq listing is essential for Onfolio to ensure continued access to capital markets and a broader investor base. While the split does not change the company's underlying business value, the resulting reduction in public float may lead to increased share price volatility and wider trading spreads.
