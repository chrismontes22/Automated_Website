Parliamentary panel may recommend stricter monitoring of crypto transactions in India
============================================================

The Parliamentary Standing Committee on Finance is finalizing a report recommending enhanced regulatory oversight and advanced monitoring technologies for virtual digital assets, including cryptocurrencies and non-fungible tokens.

### Key Points
- The committee, led by BJP MP Bhartruhari Mahtab, held over six meetings to evaluate the legal framework for digital assets.
- The Financial Intelligence Unit-India recently issued non-compliance notices to 15 service providers under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act.
- Virtual Digital Asset Service Providers were officially integrated into India’s anti-money laundering and counter-financing of terrorism framework in March 2023.
- The Finance Ministry continues to warn investors that digital assets remain unregulated and carry significant financial risks without formal regulatory recourse.

### Why it Matters
These recommendations signal a shift toward stricter government control over the domestic cryptocurrency market to mitigate financial crime and investor risk. Establishing a formal legal framework will likely impact how service providers operate and how individual investors interact with digital assets in India.
