Amazon MSK now supports TLS 1.3 for data in transit
============================================================

Amazon MSK now supports the TLS 1.3 protocol for Provisioned clusters, enabling faster, more secure data encryption and helping organizations meet evolving enterprise compliance and post-quantum security standards.

### Key Points
- Amazon MSK Provisioned clusters now support TLS 1.3 through configurable encryption policies.
- Customers can select named security policies to enforce specific TLS versions and cipher suites.
- The update allows for a phased migration by supporting both TLS 1.2 and 1.3 simultaneously.
- Stricter policies can be applied to restrict connections exclusively to TLS 1.3 for enhanced security.
- This feature is available at no additional cost across all AWS Regions where Amazon MSK Provisioned is offered.

### Why it Matters
Adopting TLS 1.3 provides stronger protection against modern cyber threats and ensures data remains secure against future decryption attempts by quantum computers. This update simplifies compliance management for enterprises by allowing them to align their streaming infrastructure with rigorous security standards at their own pace.
