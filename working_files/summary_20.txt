President Trump has announced plans for new 'AI Force' and 'AI Czar' amid growing AI safety concerns — new unit will 'cherish' AI and not 'stifle' it, Trump clarifies, while dismissing safety warnings as hoaxes
============================================================

President Donald Trump has announced plans to establish an “AI force” and appoint an “AI Czar” to promote industry growth while maintaining U.S. technological dominance over China.

### Key Points
- President Trump announced the initiative on Truth Social, comparing the proposed AI unit to the existing U.S. Space Force.
- The administration stated it will not restrict or stifle the AI industry, aiming instead to support its expansion and global competitiveness.
- No specific implementation details or official candidates for the “AI Czar” position were provided, though venture capitalist David Sacks is rumored.
- The announcement contrasts with warnings from industry leaders like Anthropic’s Dario Amodei, who have urged Washington to implement stricter AI safety guardrails.
- High-level discussions regarding AI regulation are expected during upcoming events, including a UN gathering and a White House dinner with tech executives.

### Why it Matters
This initiative signals a shift toward a deregulatory, growth-focused federal approach to artificial intelligence that prioritizes economic and geopolitical dominance over safety-based restrictions. The move highlights a growing divide between the administration's stance and the concerns of researchers who fear that rapid, unchecked AI development poses existential risks.
