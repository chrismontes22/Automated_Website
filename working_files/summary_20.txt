The first thing Apple’s new CEO did was rename a Canadian lake
============================================================

Apple has updated its Maps application to label Lake Ontario as "Lake America" for U.S. users following a direct request from President Donald Trump amid ongoing trade tensions.

### Key Points
- Apple implemented the name change on the first day of John Ternus’s tenure as CEO, though the request was handled by executive chairman Tim Cook.
- The change follows an August 27 executive order from President Trump requiring federal agencies to update the Geographic Names Information System.
- Google previously updated its maps to reflect the name change on August 29, citing its policy of following official U.S. government data.
- MapQuest refused to adopt the new name, resulting in a surge of over 180,000 downloads and reaching the top of the App Store charts.
- The name "Ontario" is of Indigenous origin, predating the formation of both the United States and Canada.

### Why it Matters
This move highlights the complex intersection of corporate governance, geopolitical pressure, and the influence of government mandates on private technology platforms. The resulting consumer backlash and the sudden resurgence of a legacy competitor demonstrate the potential reputational risks companies face when aligning digital products with controversial political directives.
