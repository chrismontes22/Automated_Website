(PR) Sandisk Reports Fiscal First Quarter 2027 Financial Results
============================================================

Sandisk Corporation reported strong fiscal first-quarter 2027 financial results driven by robust data center demand and announced an eight-for-one forward stock split to increase share accessibility.

### Key Points
- Sandisk reported strong fiscal first-quarter 2027 results, highlighted by significant growth within its data center business segment.
- The company announced an eight-for-one forward stock split to make shares more accessible to employees and individual investors.
- Stockholders of record as of November 9, 2026, will receive seven additional shares for every single share held.
- The additional shares will be issued after market close on November 19, 2026, with post-split trading beginning November 20, 2026.
- CEO David Goeckeler attributed the performance to technology leadership, strategic customer partnerships, and disciplined financial management.

### Why it Matters
The stock split aims to lower the per-share price, potentially increasing liquidity and making the company's equity more attractive to a broader base of retail investors. This move reflects management's confidence in the company's long-term growth trajectory and its commitment to maintaining durable free cash flow.
