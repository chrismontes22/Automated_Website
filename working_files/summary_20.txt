Alta nos rendimentos dos Treasuries supera ganhos do setor de software
============================================================

Wall Street indices faced downward pressure as surging Treasury yields reached their highest levels since 2002, offsetting gains in the technology and software sectors during Thursday's trading session.

### Key Points
- The 10-year Treasury yield climbed to 5.3445%, marking its highest point in over two decades.
- The Dow Jones Industrial Average fell 0.46%, while the S&P 500 and Nasdaq Composite declined 0.22% and 0.07%, respectively.
- Interest-rate-sensitive sectors struggled, with real estate dropping 1.4% and banking stocks falling 2.2%.
- Accenture shares surged 22% following a positive annual revenue forecast, boosting the broader S&P 500 software index by 1.7%.
- The Cboe VIX volatility index rose to 17.23 points, reaching its highest level in two weeks.

### Why it Matters
Rising government bond yields force investors to reevaluate the valuation of equities, as safer assets now offer more competitive returns. This shift places increased pressure on upcoming third-quarter corporate earnings reports to justify current stock prices amid ongoing interest rate concerns.
