Here’s what people who have used the iPhone Ultra like most
============================================================

Apple is reportedly preparing to launch the foldable iPhone Ultra, a new device featuring a 7.8-inch inner display and a compact design that has impressed early internal testers.

### Key Points
- The iPhone Ultra is expected to feature a 5.5-inch outer display and a 7.8-inch foldable inner screen with iPad-like app layouts.
- Early reports highlight the device's pocket-friendly form factor, durable hinge mechanism, and utility as a camera viewfinder.
- The device will reportedly utilize Touch ID for biometric authentication rather than the Face ID technology found on standard iPhone models.
- Hardware specifications include a main camera and an ultra-wide lens, with an anticipated retail price starting at approximately $2,000.

### Why it Matters
This device represents Apple’s first major entry into the competitive foldable smartphone market currently dominated by competitors like Samsung. If successful, the iPhone Ultra could establish a new premium category for the company and influence future mobile hardware design standards.
