Align Technology Sees Invisalign Growth Runway Despite Consumer Headwinds
============================================================

Align Technology CFO John Morici highlighted growth strategies for Invisalign, focusing on converting traditional wire-and-bracket patients to clear aligners despite ongoing economic headwinds and inflationary pressures.

### Key Points
- Align Technology aims to capture the 75% of North American orthodontic cases currently treated with traditional wires and brackets.
- The company is introducing lower-cost, no-refinement products to help dental practices compete more effectively on price.
- International markets, particularly in India, Southeast Asia, and Brazil, are currently driving double-digit growth for the company.
- Align is expanding consumer financing options through partnerships to help patients manage treatment costs amid a challenging economic environment.
- Management expects 100 basis points of operating-margin improvement annually through productivity initiatives and localized manufacturing.

### Why it Matters
Align’s strategy to prioritize market conversion over traditional orthodontics is critical for sustaining long-term growth in a saturated dental sector. By lowering entry costs and expanding financing, the company aims to maintain its competitive edge against traditional methods while navigating global economic volatility.
