Apple’s AI glasses to be unveiled at WWDC27: report
============================================================

Apple plans to unveil its upcoming AI-powered smart glasses at the Worldwide Developers Conference in 2027, with a commercial release expected to follow later that same year.

### Key Points
- Apple is targeting a public reveal of the smart glasses at WWDC27 to allow developers time to build third-party app integrations.
- The device will not feature a display but will support Siri, music playback, phone calls, and photo capture capabilities.
- Development was delayed from 2026 to address privacy concerns regarding integrated cameras and data collection.
- Apple is considering disabling video recording features to focus exclusively on AI-driven visual intelligence and object identification.
- The company is currently prototyping new hardware and software privacy features to differentiate the product from existing competitor devices.

### Why it Matters
This launch strategy mirrors the Apple Vision Pro rollout, prioritizing developer ecosystem growth before the final hardware hits the market. By focusing on privacy-centric AI features rather than displays, Apple aims to navigate the regulatory and social challenges that have hindered other wearable camera products.
