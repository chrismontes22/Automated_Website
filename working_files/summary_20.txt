PSA: T-Mobile is down, iPhones showing ‘SOS’ mode
============================================================

T-Mobile has fully restored cellular service across the United States following a widespread network outage that left many iPhone users unable to place calls or access mobile data.

### Key Points
- The outage began around 4 p.m. ET on the affected date, causing widespread connectivity issues for subscribers nationwide.
- Impacted iPhone users reported seeing an "SOS" icon in their status bars, indicating a total loss of cellular network connectivity.
- Downdetector and social media platforms, including Reddit, recorded thousands of user complaints regarding the inability to send texts or use data.
- T-Mobile confirmed the resolution of the technical challenges and issued a formal apology to customers for the service disruption.
- Competitor networks, including Verizon and AT&T, were not reported to be affected by the technical issues impacting T-Mobile.

### Why it Matters
Reliable cellular connectivity is essential for modern communication, and widespread outages highlight the vulnerability of mobile infrastructure to technical failures. This incident underscores the critical dependence consumers have on carrier stability for both personal communication and emergency services.
