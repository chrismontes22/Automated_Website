This 13-in-1 USB-C docking station with built-in GaN+ charger changed my charging habits for the better — a review
============================================================

The MOKiN 13-in-1 USB-C docking station offers a versatile, budget-friendly solution for expanding laptop or handheld gaming PC connectivity with high-speed charging and dual 4K display support.

### Key Points
- The device features 13 ports, including dual HDMI 2.0 outputs, multiple USB-C and USB-A ports, and a Gigabit Ethernet connection.
- It provides a combined 160 W of power output, capable of fast-charging multiple peripherals simultaneously.
- An integrated LCD screen displays real-time power draw, helping users monitor the charging status of connected devices.
- The unit lacks a 3.5 mm headphone jack and an SD card reader, which may limit its utility for some professional workflows.
- Pricing fluctuates significantly, with frequent direct discounts to $83.99 compared to its $129.99 Amazon listing and $169.99 MSRP.

### Why it Matters
This docking station serves as a cost-effective alternative to premium brands for users looking to transform thin-and-light laptops or handheld consoles into full desktop workstations. Its high-wattage charging capabilities and real-time power monitoring provide a practical tool for managing battery health across multiple wireless peripherals.
