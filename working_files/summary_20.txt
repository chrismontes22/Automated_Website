Nvidia earnings keep Wall Street futures subdued ahead of key results: Dow Jones, S&P, Nasdaq
============================================================

U.S. stock futures remain flat as investors await second-quarter earnings from Nvidia, which are expected to significantly influence market sentiment regarding artificial intelligence spending and broader technology sector performance.

### Key Points
- Nvidia is scheduled to release its second-quarter financial results after the market closes on Wednesday.
- The Commerce Department reported that the headline PCE price index rose 0.2% in July, slightly exceeding economist expectations.
- Core PCE inflation remained steady at 3.3% annually, aligning with market forecasts.
- The Conference Board’s consumer confidence index fell to 89.4 in August, signaling ongoing household caution regarding the economic outlook.
- U.S. crude oil prices dropped over 3% on Tuesday following the announcement of new U.S. sanctions targeting Iranian economic interests.

### Why it Matters
Nvidia’s upcoming earnings report serves as a critical barometer for investor confidence in the sustainability of the current artificial intelligence boom. Market participants are closely monitoring these results alongside inflation data to gauge the future direction of U.S. monetary policy and broader economic stability.
