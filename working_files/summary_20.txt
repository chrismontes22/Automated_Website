Cantor Fitzgerald Offers Kalshi Prediction Market To Institutional Clients
============================================================

Cantor Fitzgerald has partnered with the prediction market platform Kalshi to provide its 3,000 institutional clients with direct access to trade event contracts on a CFTC-regulated exchange.

### Key Points
- Cantor Fitzgerald will act as the broker, facilitating block trades for institutional clients on Kalshi’s prediction market platform.
- Kalshi provides the necessary pricing and liquidity for event contracts covering real-world outcomes like political elections and economic indicators.
- This collaboration marks one of the first instances of a major Wall Street firm integrating regulated prediction markets into its institutional service offerings.
- The partnership supports Kalshi’s strategic goal of expanding its user base and diversifying offerings ahead of a potential initial public offering.
- Kalshi previously established a similar partnership with Interactive Brokers earlier this year to increase institutional participation.

### Why it Matters
This partnership signals a growing institutional interest in using prediction markets as legitimate tools for hedging risk and speculating on real-world events. By bridging the gap between traditional finance and event-based trading, the deal could accelerate the mainstream adoption of prediction markets within professional investment portfolios.
