Reclinker raises £10m to turn demolition waste back into cement
============================================================

Construction startup Reclinker has secured £10 million in Series A funding to scale its innovative process for recycling demolition waste into low-carbon cement at commercial production facilities.

### Key Points
- Reclinker raised £10 million in a round co-led by Clean Growth Fund and AP Ventures to expand operations from Cardiff to Europe and the US.
- The company’s technology, developed at the University of Cambridge, uses electric arc furnaces to convert demolition waste into cement without requiring traditional kilns.
- The process aims to reduce cement-related carbon emissions by 50% while maintaining cost parity with conventional manufacturing methods.
- Reclinker has already produced thousands of tonnes of material at a Celsa Steel UK plant and secured its first commercial sales contracts.
- The funding round included participation from the Development Bank of Wales, Kibo Invest, Zero Carbon Capital, and Cambridge Enterprise.

### Why it Matters
Cement production accounts for approximately 7% of global CO2 emissions, making it a critical target for industrial decarbonization efforts. By repurposing demolition waste, Reclinker offers a scalable solution to replace dwindling supplies of blast-furnace slag and helps the construction industry meet rising demand for sustainable building materials.
