SafePay Has Compromised 600 Critical Infrastructure Victims Worldwide, CISA Warns
============================================================

A joint international advisory warns that the SafePay ransomware group has compromised over 600 critical infrastructure organizations across multiple continents since emerging in August 2024.

### Key Points
- Government agencies from the US, UK, and Germany identified SafePay as a top-tier threat, noting 70 attacks in May 2026 alone.
- The group likely represents a rebrand of established ransomware actors, including LockBit, ALPHV/BlackCat, and INC Ransomware.
- Attackers utilize "living off the land" techniques, exploiting VPNs and edge device vulnerabilities to gain administrative network access.
- SafePay employs a double extortion model, demanding cryptocurrency payments and pressuring victims via direct phone calls.
- Conduent settled a class-action lawsuit for $80 million in October 2026 following a 2024 data breach attributed to the group.

### Why it Matters
The rapid rise of SafePay highlights a significant escalation in the sophistication and frequency of global ransomware operations targeting critical infrastructure. Organizations must prioritize robust identity management and network segmentation to mitigate the severe financial and data-privacy risks posed by these evolving extortion tactics.
