Time Magazine Profiles John Ternus
============================================================

New Apple CEO John Ternus is navigating the company’s transition into the artificial intelligence era while launching the Duo, a high-end foldable smartphone designed to maintain Apple's hardware dominance.

### Key Points
- John Ternus officially became Apple CEO on September 1, succeeding Tim Cook after a 25-year career at the company.
- The newly released Duo foldable phone features a custom polymer surface and a precision hinge, marking Apple's most significant design shift since 2007.
- Apple is integrating Google’s Gemini technology into its new Siri to bolster AI capabilities while prioritizing on-device privacy and user data security.
- The company faces increased competition from AI-focused hardware startups, including a venture led by former Apple design chief Jony Ive.
- Apple currently maintains a massive ecosystem with 2.5 billion active devices, generating nearly $500 billion in annual revenue.

### Why it Matters
Apple is attempting to balance its traditional focus on premium hardware with the urgent need to remain relevant in a rapidly evolving artificial intelligence market. By emphasizing privacy-centric, personalized AI tools over general-purpose super-intelligence, the company aims to leverage its massive user base to maintain its competitive moat against tech rivals.
