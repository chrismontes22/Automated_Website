Fortnite-maker Epic Games confirms 'huge pivot' towards using AI
============================================================

Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney has announced a major strategic shift toward integrating artificial intelligence into development workflows to accelerate content creation and improve game production efficiency.

### Key Points
- CEO Tim Sweeney confirmed Epic Games is retooling its development processes to prioritize AI-driven code and art generation.
- Reports indicate the company requested developers use AI tools to complete an upcoming collaboration project featuring Disney, Marvel, and Star Wars characters.
- Management reportedly instructed staff to move away from hand-written code in favor of full AI-generated art workflows.
- The pivot follows a period of significant organizational change, including the layoff of over 1,000 employees earlier this year.
- The initiative has sparked industry debate regarding job security and the creative quality of AI-generated assets in professional game development.

### Why it Matters
This shift signals a broader industry trend where major developers are aggressively adopting generative AI to reduce production timelines and costs. While the move aims to empower creators, it highlights ongoing tensions between technological efficiency and the preservation of traditional roles within the creative arts sector.
