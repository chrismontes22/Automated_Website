Crypto Stocks Fall as Bitcoin Drops Ahead of Clarity Act Vote and Fed Decision
============================================================

Cryptocurrency stocks and major digital assets declined in premarket trading as investors braced for a pivotal congressional vote on the Clarity Act and an upcoming Federal Reserve decision.

### Key Points
- Bitcoin dropped 2.9% to $76,833.83, while Ether fell 3.8% to $2,471.74 during Tuesday's premarket session.
- Coinbase Global shares decreased by 4.7% amid the broader market downturn for digital asset-related companies.
- Mining firms including Riot Platforms, MARA Holdings, Hut 8, and Bit Digital saw share prices slide between 1.6% and 2.2%.
- The Clarity Act aims to establish a formal regulatory framework for the cryptocurrency industry after previously stalling in Congress.

### Why it Matters
The market is reacting to uncertainty surrounding both future federal oversight of digital assets and potential shifts in national monetary policy. These developments could significantly alter the operating environment for crypto exchanges and mining companies, influencing investor sentiment across the sector.
