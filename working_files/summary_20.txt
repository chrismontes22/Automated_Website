IPhone Duo Preorders Sell Out in Minutes: Backordered Until January
============================================================

Apple has officially opened preorders for the iPhone Duo, the company’s first foldable smartphone, which features a 7.6-inch display and starts at a retail price of $1,999.

### Key Points
- The iPhone Duo features a 7.6-inch internal display, a 5.4-inch outer screen, and dual 48-megapixel cameras.
- Preorders began Friday, but shipping through Apple is currently delayed until the final week of January.
- The device supports the Apple Pencil and includes multitasking features like drag-and-drop functionality.
- Retail availability in physical Apple Stores and at carriers like AT&T, Verizon, and T-Mobile begins October 23.
- The foldable phone enters a competitive market currently dominated by Samsung’s Galaxy series and Google’s Pixel Fold.

### Why it Matters
This launch marks Apple’s long-awaited entry into the foldable smartphone market, testing whether the company can convert its loyal ecosystem users to a new form factor. The device’s success will determine if Apple can effectively challenge established Android competitors like Samsung and Google in the premium high-end mobile segment.
