Slow Ventures Backs 3 More Creators in Its $64 Million Bet on Niche ‘Cults’
============================================================

Slow Ventures has invested in three niche creators as part of its $64 million fund, prioritizing established audience trust over traditional tech startups to build sustainable, creator-led businesses.

### Key Points
- Slow Ventures invested between $1 million and $3 million each in creators Erin McGoff, Will Lasry, and Kyle Lee.
- The firm targets "niche creators" who serve as authorities in specific industries rather than mass-market entertainment figures.
- Investments are structured as equity stakes in creator-led holding companies, typically ranging from 15% to 20%.
- The firm plans to back approximately 20 creators total using its $64 million Creator Fund launched in February 2025.
- Due diligence includes analyzing audience engagement and community trust, but the firm maintains a hands-off approach post-investment.

### Why it Matters
- This strategy reflects a shift in institutional capital toward creators who treat their audiences as a foundation for durable, non-media businesses. By betting on trusted authorities, investors aim to mitigate the risks associated with traditional tech startups while capitalizing on the growing value of community-driven distribution.
