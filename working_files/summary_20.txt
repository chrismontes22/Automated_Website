Brood War Bench
============================================================

A new benchmark study evaluating AI performance in the real-time strategy game StarCraft: Brood War reveals that current models struggle to progress beyond a beginner level of play.

### Key Points
- Codex Astra emerged as the top-performing model, achieving an undefeated 100% win rate in the round-robin tournament.
- Claude Fable demonstrated the most sophisticated gameplay by successfully climbing the tech tree and managing complex unit production.
- Grok 4.6 models consistently failed to execute basic combat loops, often spending excessive time reasoning without issuing game commands.
- Most AI agents struggled with macro-management, frequently creating disconnected subagents that failed to coordinate army production and economic growth.
- All tested models were unable to defend against simple strategies, such as a basic photon rush, that a human beginner could easily execute.

### Why it Matters
This benchmark highlights significant limitations in current AI reasoning capabilities when applied to complex, high-speed environments requiring long-term planning and real-time execution. The results suggest that while models are improving, they still lack the strategic cohesion and tactical awareness necessary to master dynamic, multi-tasking digital environments.
