Teradek Ace Micro TX First Look at IBC 2026
============================================================

Teradek unveiled the Ace Micro TX at IBC 2026, a compact wireless transmitter that delivers zero-delay 4K video to iPhones and professional cameras via a single USB-C connection.

### Key Points
- The device supports 4K 30p uncompressed video transmission at ranges up to 500 feet with less than 1 millisecond of latency.
- It utilizes a BB3 chipset to operate across 5GHz and 6GHz frequencies with automatic channel selection to minimize signal interference.
- Compatibility extends to the entire Teradek Bolt and Ace ecosystem, allowing integration with Bolt 4K, Bolt 6, Bolt 7, and Ace 750 systems.
- The transmitter features internal antennas, M3 and M2.5 mounting points, and dual USB-C ports for video input and pass-through power.
- It is currently available for purchase at a retail price of $599 USD.

### Why it Matters
This release simplifies professional wireless monitoring by allowing users to integrate smartphones and cinema cameras into a unified, low-latency video ecosystem. By removing the reliance on standard WiFi, the hardware provides a reliable, broadcast-quality solution for filmmakers requiring high-speed mobile monitoring workflows.
