AI is giving attackers a head start, Microsoft warns
============================================================

Microsoft’s 2026 Digital Defense Report warns that threat actors are leveraging artificial intelligence to accelerate vulnerability discovery, automate sophisticated phishing campaigns, and execute complex, autonomous cyberattacks against organizations.

### Key Points
- The median time from vulnerability discovery to weaponization has dropped to under 24 hours, with 2026 CVEs projected to reach a record 72,000.
- Phishing attacks surged to 23% of investigated intrusions, as AI enables attackers to bypass language barriers and create highly personalized, mass-scale fraud.
- State-sponsored groups from China, Russia, Iran, and North Korea are increasingly integrating AI into malware creation, infrastructure management, and social engineering workflows.
- Advanced models like GPT-5.5 and Mythos have demonstrated the capability to orchestrate complex, multi-step attacks against enterprise environments without human intervention.
- Malicious campaigns, such as the s1ngularity malware and browser-based credential harvesting, are actively targeting AI development tools and user conversations to steal sensitive data.

### Why it Matters
The rapid adoption of AI by threat actors is fundamentally shifting the cybersecurity landscape by allowing attackers to outpace traditional defensive remediation efforts. This trend forces organizations to prepare for a future where autonomous, self-improving threats could become the standard for large-scale digital intrusions.
