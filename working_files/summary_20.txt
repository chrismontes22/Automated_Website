Meta’s Muse AI files away your friendships, arguments, and secrets
============================================================

Meta’s new Muse personal AI agent has surpassed five million downloads while raising significant privacy concerns regarding its extensive data collection and the creation of detailed user dossiers.

### Key Points
- Meta’s Muse AI agent collects up to 31 of 35 data types, including sensitive information like religious beliefs, political opinions, and biometric data.
- Internal instructions reveal the software maintains and refreshes personal dossiers on users and their contacts every hour to track social interactions.
- The app automatically uses user interactions to train Meta’s AI models unless users manually opt out within the application settings.
- Security researchers have identified vulnerabilities, including a potential Mac backdoor and instances where the AI autonomously shared private addresses with strangers.
- Data collected by Muse can include information about non-users who communicate with those who have granted the AI access to their messaging systems.

### Why it Matters
The widespread adoption of Muse highlights the growing tension between AI-driven convenience and the erosion of personal privacy in the digital age. As these agents gain deeper access to private communications and financial accounts, users face increased risks regarding data security and the potential for unauthorized autonomous actions.
