Rethinking secure file transfer for a cross-domain world
============================================================

The UK's National Cyber Security Centre warns that traditional file transfer systems are increasingly vulnerable, urging organizations to abandon perimeter-based trust in favor of proactive, continuous file verification.

### Key Points
- The National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) warns that modern interconnected systems rely on outdated protocols that cannot withstand sophisticated cyberattacks.
- Traditional Managed File Transfer (MFT) models prioritize delivery speed over security, leaving organizations exposed to malware and supply chain vulnerabilities.
- High-profile incidents like the 2023 MoveIT breach demonstrate how attackers exploit the assumption that routine file transfer platforms are inherently safe.
- Security experts recommend deep content inspection, sandboxing, and Content Disarm and Reconstruction (CDR) to sanitize files before they enter internal networks.
- Organizations must shift from reactive patching to a security-first model where every file is validated at every stage of its journey.

### Why it Matters
Relying on perimeter-based security is no longer sufficient as attackers now exploit vulnerabilities within 48 hours of disclosure. By treating every file as a potential threat, businesses can prevent lateral movement and mitigate the risk of catastrophic data breaches in increasingly complex digital environments.
