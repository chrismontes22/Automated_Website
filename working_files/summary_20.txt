US markets rise as Democrats sweep midterm elections
============================================================

US markets trended upward on Wednesday following a Democratic sweep of the midterm elections, as investors reacted to shifting political control and potential changes in federal policy.

### Key Points
- The Dow Jones Industrial Average rose 1.8 percent, while the S&P 500 and Nasdaq gained 1.1 percent and 1 percent, respectively.
- Retailers Walmart and Target saw shares climb 1.3 percent and 3.3 percent amid expectations of potential shifts in national tariff policy.
- Healthcare stocks showed mixed results, with CVS Health dropping 12 percent and UnitedHealthcare falling 2.9 percent, while Cigna rose 1.6 percent.
- Tech companies faced volatility, as Oracle and Nvidia shares declined while Alphabet stock increased by 3.5 percent at the opening bell.
- Global oil prices dropped significantly, with Brent crude falling 3.09 percent to $100.40 per barrel and West Texas Intermediate declining 3.13 percent to $93.08.

### Why it Matters
The shift in legislative control creates significant uncertainty for the artificial intelligence sector, with analysts predicting potential delays in data center development due to increased regulatory scrutiny. Investors are now balancing these domestic political changes against cooling energy prices and ongoing geopolitical developments in Ukraine and Iran.
