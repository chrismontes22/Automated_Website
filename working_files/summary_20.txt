Before you buy another USB-C charger, make sure it has this critical (and hidden) spec
============================================================

Consumers should look beyond total wattage when buying USB-C chargers, as specific voltage and current profiles are required to achieve optimal fast-charging speeds for modern smartphones and devices.

### Key Points
- USB Power Delivery (USB PD) with Programmable Power Supply (PPS) is essential for fast charging on most modern Android devices, including Samsung Galaxy and Google Pixel phones.
- Total wattage is often insufficient for determining speed; chargers must support specific voltage and current combinations, such as the 21V profiles required by newer handsets.
- PPS support is typically indicated by a voltage range, such as 3.3V–21V, rather than the fixed voltage outputs found on standard USB PD chargers.
- Multi-port chargers often split total power between connections, and high-speed charging may require a cable specifically rated for 5A current.
- Manufacturers often omit detailed charging specifications, requiring consumers to check product labels or technical documentation for broad voltage coverage.

### Why it Matters
Understanding these technical specifications prevents consumers from purchasing chargers that provide sluggish performance despite high wattage claims. Investing in a charger with broad PPS voltage support ensures compatibility with future devices and reduces the accumulation of electronic waste.
