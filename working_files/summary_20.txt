UAE “Spy Sheikh” Behind Trump Crypto Bank: 49% Ownership, $500M and Questions MAGA Can’t Avoid
============================================================

Investors linked to UAE national security adviser Sheikh Tahnoon bin Zayed al Nahyan hold a 49% stake in the holding company for Donald Trump’s planned crypto banking venture.

### Key Points
- Sheikh Tahnoon bin Zayed al Nahyan and his co-investors are the largest shareholders in the holding company behind World Liberty Financial’s proposed national trust bank.
- The Office of the Comptroller of the Currency granted preliminary conditional approval for the bank to issue, redeem, and safeguard the USD1 stablecoin.
- This ownership stake follows a previous $500 million investment by Tahnoon-linked entities into the broader World Liberty Financial business.
- Sheikh Tahnoon oversees a financial empire managing over $1.3 trillion in personal and state-controlled assets.
- Specific details regarding the bank's board composition, governance rights, and potential veto powers remain undisclosed.

### Why it Matters
This partnership highlights a significant financial intersection between a Trump-backed enterprise and a senior foreign government official. The involvement of a major sovereign wealth figure in a federally chartered U.S. banking project raises questions regarding transparency and the influence of foreign capital in domestic financial infrastructure.
