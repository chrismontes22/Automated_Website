The iPhone Duo Is Solving A Big Comfort Problem With Folding Phones - Here's How
============================================================

Apple has addressed thermal management in its new $1,999 iPhone Duo foldable by utilizing a specialized graphite pipe within the hinge to dissipate heat from the A20 Pro chip.

### Key Points
- The iPhone Duo features a unique thermal design that routes heat through the device's hinge to prevent accumulation when the phone is closed.
- Apple integrated a thin vapor chamber alongside a flexible graphite pipe to manage temperatures generated by the A20 Pro processor.
- Thermal product design manager Banafsheh Barabadi noted that the hinge mechanism was engineered to maintain heat transfer while enduring repeated folding.
- Apple claims the new cooling system provides up to 35% better sustained performance compared to the previous iPhone 17 Pro model.

### Why it Matters
Effective thermal management is critical for foldable devices, which often struggle with heat dissipation due to their compact, multi-layered construction. If successful, this engineering solution could set a new industry standard for performance stability in high-end foldable smartphones.
