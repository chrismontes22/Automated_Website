Actor Turned Anti-Crypto Activist Points To White House For Crypto Crimes
============================================================

Actor and director Ben McKenzie testified before Congress to warn against cryptocurrency fraud, highlighting industry risks and the alleged financial ties between government officials and the operator Tether.

### Key Points
- Ben McKenzie directed the documentary "Everyone Is Lying to You for Money," which premiered at SXSW London in 2025.
- The actor co-authored the 2023 New York Times bestseller "Easy Money" with journalist Jacob Silverman to expose crypto industry practices.
- McKenzie cited a Pew Research Center report noting that 42% of men aged 18-29 have engaged in cryptocurrency trading or usage.
- Senator Richard Blumenthal publicly supported McKenzie’s testimony regarding potential financial conflicts involving the Trump administration and Tether.
- McKenzie previously interviewed FTX founder Sam Bankman-Fried in 2022, months before the company's collapse.

### Why it Matters
This testimony highlights growing regulatory scrutiny regarding the intersection of political influence and the volatile cryptocurrency market. By focusing on how the industry targets young demographics, McKenzie’s advocacy underscores the urgent need for consumer protection and transparency in digital asset investments.
