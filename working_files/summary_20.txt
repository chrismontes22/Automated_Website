Lenovo’s budget brand just dropped a 27-inch all-in-one PC for under $700
============================================================

Lenovo has expanded its Lecoo sub-brand by launching the N27 Pro, an affordable 27-inch all-in-one desktop computer currently available in China with a starting price of approximately $671.

### Key Points
- The N27 Pro features a 27-inch 1440p display with a 100Hz refresh rate and 99% sRGB color coverage.
- It is powered by an Intel Core i5-13420H processor, 16GB of RAM, and up to 1TB of storage.
- Connectivity options include five USB-A ports, one USB-C port, HDMI, and Gigabit Ethernet.
- The device is available in Cloud Feather Black and Snow Feather White, including a wireless keyboard and mouse.
- Pricing ranges from ¥4,499 to ¥4,999, with no official announcement regarding a release in the United States.

### Why it Matters
The N27 Pro demonstrates Lenovo's strategy of using the Lecoo brand to offer high-specification hardware at aggressive price points that undercut premium competitors. If these models eventually reach Western markets, they could disrupt the entry-level all-in-one segment by providing larger, higher-resolution displays than the 1080p panels typically found at similar price tiers.
