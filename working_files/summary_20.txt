Top 5 Branded Videos of the Week: Best buyin’
============================================================

Zhong’s FIFA-sponsored video leads this week’s most-watched branded YouTube content, as creators like Airrack and Gohar Khan leverage high-traffic partnerships with major brands like DraftKings and Best Buy.

### Key Points
- Creator Zhong secured the top spot with 24.1 million views for a FIFA-sponsored soccer competition video.
- Airrack’s arcade-themed video for DraftKings reached 19.3 million views, highlighting the brand's aggressive sports betting promotion strategy.
- Best Buy dominated the third and fourth positions with tech-focused content from Andrew Bond and Gohar Khan, totaling over 34 million combined views.
- Best Buy’s campaign specifically targeted back-to-school and dorm-living demographics through product showcases of TVs and smart home devices.
- Financial creator Marissa Van partnered with the FSA Store to address economic concerns, reflecting a growing trend of viewers seeking financial advice on YouTube.

### Why it Matters
These rankings illustrate how major brands are increasingly utilizing creator-led Shorts to drive engagement during specific seasonal retail pushes like back-to-school shopping. By aligning with niche influencers, companies can effectively integrate product demonstrations into viral entertainment, significantly increasing their reach compared to traditional advertising methods.
