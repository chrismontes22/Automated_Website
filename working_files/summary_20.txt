Christine Lagarde: Hearing of the Committee on Economic and Monetary Affairs of the European Parliament
============================================================

European Central Bank President Christine Lagarde addressed the European Parliament, highlighting how artificial intelligence integration and current economic conditions are shaping the euro area's monetary policy and productivity.

### Key Points
- The ECB projects euro area GDP growth of 0.9% in 2026, 1.4% in 2027, and 1.5% in 2028.
- Headline inflation reached 3.2% in August 2026, driven largely by rising energy costs and refining margins.
- ECB interest rates were increased by 25 basis points this month to maintain price stability amid persistent inflation risks.
- Approximately 38% of euro area firms reported at least moderate AI use by late 2025, though only 7% reported significant adoption.
- Lagarde emphasized the need for increased European investment in AI infrastructure, including a €30 billion initiative for AI "Gigafactories."

### Why it Matters
The ECB is closely monitoring AI adoption because it could significantly influence long-term productivity, labor market dynamics, and inflationary pressures. By balancing technological investment with a measured monetary policy, the central bank aims to foster economic growth while ensuring that energy-related price shocks do not become permanently embedded in the economy.
