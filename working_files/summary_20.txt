Google Maps for Android Auto could soon offer new colors that won’t burn your retinas
============================================================

Google is testing a new, darker color scheme for Google Maps within Android Auto to improve visibility and reduce screen glare for drivers during daily navigation.

### Key Points
- The Android Auto beta version 17.6.663424 introduces a darker map surface and a dark-gray turn indicator.
- Current versions feature a bright light-mode background and a teal turn indicator that can be distracting on large infotainment screens.
- The update aims to improve nighttime visibility by darkening the map surface and replacing the high-contrast teal elements in dark mode.
- These UI adjustments appear specific to the Android Auto interface rather than the standalone Google Maps mobile application.

### Why it Matters
These interface refinements address common driver complaints regarding screen brightness and visual comfort during nighttime or high-glare conditions. By optimizing the color palette, Google aims to minimize distractions and improve the overall safety and usability of the Android Auto navigation experience.
