Paying The Past To Buy The Future; Will Microsoft Finally Do In-Game Ad Insertion?
============================================================

AI companies like Perplexity and OpenAI are increasingly leveraging influencer marketing to humanize their products, while Xbox explores new in-game advertising strategies and Nielsen accelerates streaming data reporting.

### Key Points
- Perplexity, OpenAI, and Anthropic are actively recruiting influencers to promote AI tools, mirroring historical advertising cycles where platforms court their future competitors.
- Microsoft-owned Xbox filed a patent for inserting advertisements during natural gameplay breaks, building on existing tests of reward-based advertising models.
- Nielsen is shifting from weekly to daily streaming viewership updates for paying clients to provide faster competitive insights for studios and platforms.
- WealthStage, formerly known as 33Across, has rebranded to focus specifically on digital advertising solutions for the financial services sector.
- DoorDash announced a $425 million investment in Wonder, a ghost kitchen operator that recently acquired the food delivery platforms Grubhub and Seamless.

### Why it Matters
These developments highlight a broader industry shift toward aggressive monetization and data-driven marketing across AI, gaming, and streaming sectors. As companies seek to humanize complex technologies and capture faster audience insights, they risk alienating users who remain sensitive to intrusive advertising and platform-driven content strategies.
