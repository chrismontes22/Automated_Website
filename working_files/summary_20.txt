Dalio Says We're Nearing Point Where AI Bubble May Burst...
============================================================

Bridgewater founder Ray Dalio warns that the current artificial intelligence boom is a classic market bubble nearing a collapse due to rising interest rates and debt-fueled infrastructure spending.

### Key Points
- Ray Dalio identified the AI sector as a bubble during the Forbes Global CEO Conference in Singapore.
- High interest rates are increasing the cost of debt used by technology giants to finance massive AI infrastructure projects.
- Market gains remain concentrated in a small number of stocks despite record highs for the S&P 500 and Nasdaq 100.
- Potential triggers for a market correction include wealth taxes and the practical difficulty of converting unrealized asset gains into cash.

### Why it Matters
This warning highlights the growing tension between record-breaking equity valuations and the rising cost of capital in the global economy. If the AI bubble bursts, it could significantly impact the technology sector and broader market stability as companies struggle to service debt-funded investments.
