Dear Microsoft, where is the Surface to showcase all these improvements to Windows 11?
============================================================

Microsoft is preparing to release the major Windows 11 version 26H2 update to the public, yet the company currently lacks a clear marketing strategy to showcase these new features.

### Key Points
- Microsoft has released Windows 11 version 26H2 to the Release Preview Channel, with a general rollout scheduled for the coming weeks.
- The update introduces significant UI modifications, including the ability to reposition the Taskbar, alongside various under-the-hood performance improvements.
- Despite the scale of these changes, Microsoft has not announced a dedicated hardware event or a new Surface device to highlight the operating system's evolution.
- The company faces a marketing challenge in communicating technical OS improvements to consumers compared to previous hardware-focused campaigns involving Snapdragon processors.

### Why it Matters
The lack of a unified marketing push for this update risks leaving consumers unaware of significant improvements to the Windows 11 ecosystem. Without a flagship device to demonstrate these changes, Microsoft may struggle to differentiate this release from previous versions of the operating system.
