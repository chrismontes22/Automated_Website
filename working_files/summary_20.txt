The Download: a “God-driven” cryptocurrency and a solar engineering roadmap
============================================================

Eli and Kaitlyn Regalado face legal scrutiny after their cryptocurrency project, INDXcoin, collapsed, resulting in over 500 investors losing more than $3 million in a religiously marketed fraud scheme.

### Key Points
- Eli and Kaitlyn Regalado promoted INDXcoin to evangelical Christian communities, claiming divine guidance for the project.
- The cryptocurrency venture collapsed within one year, leaving investors with total financial losses exceeding $3 million.
- The Regalados testified in court that they believed they were directed by God to initiate a "wealth transfer" through the digital coin.
- The case highlights the risks of affinity fraud, where perpetrators exploit shared religious or social beliefs to gain trust and solicit investments.

### Why it Matters
This case underscores the growing vulnerability of niche communities to sophisticated financial scams disguised as faith-based opportunities. It serves as a cautionary example of how digital asset platforms can be weaponized to exploit personal trust for significant illicit gain.
