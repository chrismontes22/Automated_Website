I ditched my travel laptop for a mini PC with a 100-inch screen
============================================================

A modular travel workstation combining a Khadas Mind mini PC and an Xgimi Mogo 3 Pro projector offers a high-performance, large-screen alternative to traditional bulky laptop computers.

### Key Points
- The setup features a Khadas Mind mini PC equipped with a 13th-gen Intel Core i7 processor and 32GB of RAM.
- An Xgimi Mogo 3 Pro portable projector provides a 1080p display ranging from 40 to 100 inches with automatic keystone and focus.
- A Satechi 4-port GaN travel charger powers both the mini PC and projector from a single wall outlet.
- The system utilizes a Logitech wireless keyboard and mouse to maintain a compact, cable-managed workspace.
- This configuration is designed for destination-based work, requiring a flat, light-colored surface for projection.

### Why it Matters
This modular approach challenges the necessity of traditional laptops for travelers who prioritize screen real estate and desktop-grade performance over on-the-go mobility. While the setup involves higher costs and increased component complexity, it provides a viable, ergonomic alternative for professionals seeking a home-office experience in hotel environments.
