Scott Bessent endorses Meta’s Muse Glimmer as win for innovation
============================================================

US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent has endorsed Meta’s new Muse Glimmer open-source AI model, framing the release as a strategic victory for American innovation and national security interests.

### Key Points
- Meta released Muse Glimmer on August 10, 2026, as a 30-billion-parameter multimodal model under the permissive Apache 2.0 license.
- The model is designed for local execution on consumer-grade NVIDIA, AMD, and Apple hardware, eliminating the need for expensive cloud-based data center infrastructure.
- Muse Glimmer features a 120,000-token context window and is optimized for agentic workloads that require autonomous decision-making over extended periods.
- Secretary Bessent argues that open-source development helps protect American intellectual property by establishing domestic standards that counter foreign AI competition.
- The model serves as a distilled, efficient version of Meta’s proprietary Muse Spark 1.2 system, prioritizing accessibility for developers and businesses.

### Why it Matters
This endorsement signals a shift in government policy by framing open-source AI as a vital tool for maintaining technological sovereignty against international competitors. By supporting local-first models, the administration aims to reduce reliance on cloud-based APIs and protect sensitive user data from third-party oversight.
