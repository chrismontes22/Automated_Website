MusiMap Relaunches Music Intelligence Platform After Utopia Music Collapse
============================================================

Music intelligence firm MusiMap has relaunched as an independent company, introducing a suite of four audio analysis tools backed by nearly four decades of human-curated musicological research.

### Key Points
- The new product suite includes MusiTag, MusiSearch, MusiProfile, and MusiTrace, launched in September 2026.
- The company’s technology relies on a database of over one million manually analyzed recordings dating back to 1986.
- Gracenote co-founder and former Universal Music Group CTO Ty Roberts has joined the firm as Chief Commercial Officer.
- MusiMap returned to independent ownership in 2025 following its separation from the collapsed parent company Utopia Music.
- Early commercial adopters of the platform include the music strategy agency Light On and the Red Hot Organization.

### Why it Matters
MusiMap’s return to independence highlights the ongoing industry demand for high-quality, context-aware music metadata in an era dominated by automated AI solutions. By leveraging human-annotated data, the company aims to provide more nuanced search and discovery capabilities for labels, streaming platforms, and automotive entertainment systems.
