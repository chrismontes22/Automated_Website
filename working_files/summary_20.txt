Irritating Google Home bug refuses to answer your basic questions
============================================================

Google Home users are reporting a persistent technical glitch that prevents smart speakers and displays from answering basic informational queries while still performing tasks like setting timers.

### Key Points
- Users report that Google Home devices fail to answer simple questions, such as inquiries about federal holidays or cooking recipes.
- Basic device functions, including setting timers and retrieving sports scores, remain operational despite the error.
- The issue appears primarily limited to devices running the older Google Assistant experience rather than the newer Gemini for Home platform.
- Multiple reports of the malfunction have surfaced on Reddit, prompting users to seek clarification from Google.

### Why it Matters
This glitch highlights the ongoing reliability challenges facing Google’s legacy smart home ecosystem as the company transitions toward its newer AI-powered Gemini interface. If left unaddressed, these inconsistencies may frustrate long-term users and accelerate the pressure to migrate toward newer, potentially more stable hardware and software integrations.
