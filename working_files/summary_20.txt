Fairlife restarts production after cyberattack, Coca-Cola says
============================================================

Fairlife has resumed the majority of production at its four United States factories following a mid-July cyberattack that prompted unauthorized access to the company's internal network systems.

### Key Points
- The Coca-Cola Co. confirmed that Fairlife production has restarted after a security breach was first detected on 16 July.
- Retail availability of Fairlife products remains stable due to sufficient existing inventory levels maintained throughout the disruption.
- The Anubis ransomware group claimed responsibility for the attack, alleging they stole one terabyte of data and encrypted Nutanix systems.
- Coca-Cola stated that the incident has not compromised product quality or safety and is not expected to have a material financial impact.
- The company continues to work on restoring all impacted systems while preparing to release its second-quarter financial results.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the ongoing vulnerability of major food and beverage supply chains to sophisticated ransomware attacks targeting industrial production systems. While Coca-Cola reports minimal operational disruption, the threat of data exposure underscores the increasing pressure corporations face regarding cybersecurity and data protection protocols.
