Apple Event Logo Hints at Two iPhone 18 Pro Features
============================================================

Apple’s upcoming September 9 event invite suggests the iPhone 18 Pro series may feature a new mechanical aperture camera system and a fresh sky blue color option.

### Key Points
- Apple’s "Surprise and shine" event graphic hints at a variable aperture camera system for the iPhone 18 Pro Max.
- The rumored mechanical aperture would allow users to manually adjust depth of field, similar to professional DSLR camera functionality.
- Mark Gurman suggests the event's sky blue color scheme indicates a new aesthetic option for the upcoming iPhone 18 Pro lineup.
- Additional rumored color variants for the new Pro models include Dark Cherry, Dark Gray, and Silver.
- The official product reveal is scheduled for Apple's September 9 event, which is less than two weeks away.

### Why it Matters
The introduction of a variable aperture would mark a significant hardware upgrade for iPhone photography, offering users greater control over focus and lighting conditions. This potential shift signals Apple's continued focus on competing with professional-grade camera equipment to maintain its premium market position.
