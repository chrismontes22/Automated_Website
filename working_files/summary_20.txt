HomeKit Weekly: ULTRALOQ releases its first UWB smart lock that works with Apple Home
============================================================

ULTRALOQ has introduced the Bolt Mission smart lock, which utilizes ultra-wideband technology to provide precise, hands-free door unlocking without relying on GPS or cloud-based location tracking.

### Key Points
- The Bolt Mission uses ultra-wideband, Bluetooth Low Energy, and radar-based sensing to detect a user's precise distance and direction.
- Intent detection logic runs locally between the lock and an iPhone, eliminating the need for a home hub or cloud connection.
- ULTRALOQ also launched the Aliro-certified Bolt Pro and Latch 5 Pro, which support tap-to-unlock via Apple Wallet and Samsung Wallet.
- All three new lock models are Matter-certified for compatibility with Apple Home, Google Home, and Amazon Alexa.
- The Bolt Pro and Latch 5 Pro offer multiple entry methods, including fingerprint scanning, PIN codes, app control, and physical keys.

### Why it Matters
This release represents a significant shift toward more reliable, localized smart home security by moving away from inconsistent GPS-based auto-unlocking. By adopting ultra-wideband and the cross-platform Aliro standard, these locks offer consumers more versatile and accurate entry options that function independently of specific ecosystem limitations.
