Apple Maps to Help Power Autonomous Driving in Ford's 2027 EV
============================================================

Ford will integrate Apple Maps directly into its upcoming Universal Electric Vehicle platform, launching in 2027 with a target price point of approximately $30,000 for mainstream consumers.

### Key Points
- Ford’s new Universal Electric Vehicle (UEV) platform will feature native Apple Maps integration via the new MapKit for Automotive SDK.
- The navigation system includes intelligent EV routing, battery preconditioning, and support for Ford’s hands-free BlueCruise driving technology.
- Apple’s SDK allows automakers to embed mapping data directly into vehicle displays without requiring a connected iPhone.
- Apple confirmed that it will not collect individual user location data or activity tied to identities through this automotive integration.
- The UEV platform is scheduled for a 2027 release, aiming to provide affordable electric vehicle options for the mass market.

### Why it Matters
This partnership marks a significant shift in Apple’s automotive strategy by moving beyond simple phone mirroring to providing native, embedded software for major manufacturers. By targeting a $30,000 price point, Ford and Apple are positioning advanced navigation and EV-specific features to reach a broader, mainstream consumer base.
