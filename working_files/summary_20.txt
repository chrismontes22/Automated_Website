Matt Haughey: ‘The Car Industry a/B Tested Selling a Car With and Without CarPlay and the Results Are Not Shocking’
============================================================

General Motors has faced significant consumer backlash after removing Apple CarPlay and Android Auto from its vehicles, while the identical Honda Prologue continues to outsell the Chevrolet Blazer EV.

### Key Points
- General Motors transitioned its new vehicle lineup to the proprietary Android Automotive OS, citing privacy concerns as the primary motivation for the change.
- The Honda Prologue is built on the same platform as the Chevrolet Blazer EV but retains full support for Apple CarPlay and Android Auto.
- Sales data from 2024 through mid-2026 shows the Honda Prologue consistently outselling the Chevrolet Blazer EV by margins ranging from 43% to 166%.
- Despite the shared mechanical underpinnings, consumers are increasingly choosing the Honda-branded vehicle over the Chevrolet model due to the inclusion of phone integration features.

### Why it Matters
The sales disparity suggests that smartphone integration is a critical deciding factor for modern car buyers, potentially impacting the long-term market share of automakers who restrict these features. This real-world comparison forces General Motors to evaluate whether its proprietary software strategy is alienating customers who prioritize seamless mobile connectivity.
