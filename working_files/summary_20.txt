More Nothing smart glasses rumors suggest we might see two variations
============================================================

Nothing is reportedly developing two variations of AI-powered smart glasses, including one model featuring a camera and another focused on audio, with a potential market release in 2027.

### Key Points
- Tipster Roland Quandt reports that Nothing is developing two smart glasses models, one featuring a camera and one focused on audio capabilities.
- The audio-only version is expected to include auto-tinting photochromic lenses, call functionality, and deep integration with smartphone AI assistants.
- The camera-equipped model may offer advanced features such as real-time translation and integration with ChatGPT.
- Development efforts are reportedly led by CEO Carl Pei, with previous reports suggesting the hardware will rely on smartphones for cloud-based AI processing.
- The company is targeting a potential product launch in 2027, marking a significant expansion beyond its current portfolio of Android phones and audio accessories.

### Why it Matters
Expanding into smart eyewear allows Nothing to compete in the emerging wearable AI market by leveraging its distinct design language and software ecosystem. However, the inclusion of a camera model may present significant privacy challenges and regulatory hurdles similar to those faced by other tech companies in the smart glasses space.
