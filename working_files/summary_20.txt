5 Best GEO Agencies for AI Search Competitive Analysis
============================================================

Brands are increasingly turning to specialized generative engine optimization (GEO) agencies to analyze competitive visibility and citation performance across AI-driven search platforms like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity.

### Key Points
- Profound offers detailed competitive intelligence by tracking visibility, sentiment, and citation share across major generative AI platforms.
- iPullRank focuses on technical and content strategy to resolve visibility gaps caused by complex information architecture or entity relationships.
- NP Digital translates competitive research into actionable marketing plans, including SEO, digital PR, and brand positioning adjustments.
- Omnius provides targeted GEO analysis specifically for SaaS, fintech, and technology companies monitoring recommendation and alternative-search prompts.
- Reboot Online addresses authority gaps by leveraging digital PR expertise to increase brand mentions across credible third-party sources.

### Why it Matters
As AI search platforms change how users discover information, traditional SEO metrics are no longer sufficient for maintaining market share. Companies must now adopt GEO strategies to understand why competitors are winning citations and how to adjust their own content and authority to remain visible in generative results.
