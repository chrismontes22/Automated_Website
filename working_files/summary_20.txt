How Excelland Robotics Puts Robots to Work
============================================================

Shenzhen-based Excelland Robotics is preparing for a Hong Kong IPO as it scales its portfolio of commercial service robots and AI vision solutions for global markets.

### Key Points
- Excelland has deployed over 114,600 robots across 5,100 global customers as of March 2026.
- The company’s product lineup includes specialized machines for indoor delivery, outdoor cleaning, and unmanned retail.
- Its Yomie MX model features an intelligent robotic arm capable of pressing elevator buttons without requiring building infrastructure upgrades.
- The firm generated RMB 317.7 million in 2025 revenue, with 37.5% coming from its Yoware AI vision model platform.
- Expansion plans target international markets including Japan, South Korea, and Thailand to address global labor shortages.

### Why it Matters
Excelland’s transition from a hardware manufacturer to a provider of cloud-based visual intelligence and service-based models highlights the evolving business requirements for commercial automation. By focusing on reliability and task-specific utility rather than humanoid aesthetics, the company aims to prove that robotics can become a sustainable, profitable fixture in the global workplace.
