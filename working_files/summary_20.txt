Market sell-off gains steam as concerns about Iran war and high AI spending collide
============================================================

U.S. equity markets experienced a sharp decline on Thursday as rising oil prices and disappointing tech earnings fueled investor anxiety regarding geopolitical tensions and future economic growth.

### Key Points
- The S&P 500 fell 1.2% and the Nasdaq dropped 2.6% as investors moved capital into safe-haven Treasury bonds.
- Brent crude prices climbed nearly 4% to $113 per barrel amid reports of potential U.S. and Israeli military operations targeting Iranian infrastructure.
- Meta shares declined after the company announced plans to increase AI infrastructure spending, while Tesla fell following a reduction in its capital expansion plans.
- Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang warned that capital for AI investment is becoming scarce due to high interest rates and existing spending commitments.
- The yield on the 10-year Treasury note dropped below 5% for the first time since late September as market volatility increased.

### Why it Matters
A sustained market downturn could undermine the economic narrative central to the Republican platform ahead of the November 3 elections. This volatility highlights the growing tension between aggressive corporate AI investment strategies and the macroeconomic pressures of rising interest rates and geopolitical instability.
