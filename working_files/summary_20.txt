Billionaires love Trump’s AI rebrand
============================================================

The evolution of video game non-player characters into a political slur reflects growing concerns over how tech leaders are rebranding artificial intelligence to diminish human value.

### Key Points
- The "NPC" meme, originating from the 1987 game "Zelda II," has evolved into a political insult used to dehumanize ideological opponents.
- Tech figures including Donald Trump, Elon Musk, and Jeff Bezos are pushing to rebrand "Artificial Intelligence" as "Super Intelligence" to imply superiority.
- Critics argue that elevating AI status risks granting computer programs rights while simultaneously framing regular humans as disposable, mindless entities.
- Despite industry efforts to suggest AI consciousness, experts maintain that large language models remain sophisticated mathematical programs lacking genuine human sentience.
- Pope Francis has publicly rejected claims of AI consciousness, warning that algorithms lack the essential "spark of humanity."

### Why it Matters
The normalization of the "NPC" label and the rebranding of AI as "Super Intelligence" threaten to erode the perceived value of human consciousness in public discourse. By framing technology as superior to people, industry leaders risk justifying the disenfranchisement of individuals while prioritizing the rights of non-sentient algorithms.
