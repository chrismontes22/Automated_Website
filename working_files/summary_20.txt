October ends with a thud for Intel, Reddit, AI stalwarts
============================================================

The Nasdaq composite experienced its largest single-day decline since June on Friday as investors retreated from major technology stocks amid concerns over massive artificial intelligence capital expenditures.

### Key Points
- The Nasdaq composite fell significantly, leaving the index down 7.8% from its recent closing high.
- Intel shares dropped 29%, Reddit fell 29%, and Sandisk declined 13% during the broad market selloff.
- Major hyperscalers including Microsoft, Meta Platforms, Amazon, and Alphabet saw share prices slide between 1% and 2%.
- Financial institutions faced pressure, with Goldman Sachs shares falling 4% and Jefferies dropping 6%.
- Commodities saw sharp declines, as silver fell 6% and gold dropped 3.5% while oil prices rose 2%.
- Bitcoin prices fell 3% following reports of a $6 billion hack involving the cryptocurrency exchange Binance.

### Why it Matters
The recent market volatility reflects growing investor anxiety regarding the sustainability of massive capital spending on artificial intelligence infrastructure by major technology firms. As these companies increasingly rely on external financing, market participants are scrutinizing the long-term profitability and monetization strategies of the current AI-driven economic boom.
