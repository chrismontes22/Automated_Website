Crypto Gurus Concerned AI Is Poised to Crack Blockchain Math, Allowing Hackers to Steal Funds With Impunity
============================================================

Ethereum researchers and industry leaders are debating whether rapid advancements in artificial intelligence could eventually threaten the cryptographic security protecting blockchain wallets and digital assets.

### Key Points
- Ethereum researcher Justin Drake has urged the blockchain industry to prepare for potential AI-driven threats by moving assets to addresses hidden behind cryptographic hashes.
- Ethereum creator Vitalik Buterin acknowledged that AI-accelerated mathematics could pose a long-term risk to current encryption, though he cautioned against immediate, panicked wallet migrations.
- The Association for Human Mathematics and other experts have expressed skepticism regarding OpenAI’s recent claims of revolutionary breakthroughs in mathematical problem-solving.
- While AI has already been used to exploit firmware bugs in crypto wallets, there is currently no evidence that AI can bypass standard public key encryption.
- Buterin warned that the process of migrating funds between wallets carries its own significant risks, noting that he has lost more capital through botched transfers than through security hacks.

### Why it Matters
The potential for AI to compromise cryptographic security could fundamentally alter the risk profile of decentralized finance and long-term digital asset storage. While the immediate threat remains speculative, the industry must balance the need for proactive security upgrades against the dangers of hasty, error-prone migration strategies.
