Here’s everything new for Apple Calendar in iOS 27
============================================================

Apple has introduced significant updates to the Calendar app in iOS 27, featuring AI-powered natural language input, redesigned event interfaces, and expanded widget support for iPhone users.

### Key Points
- Smart Event Details uses Apple Intelligence to automatically populate event titles, times, and locations from natural language input.
- The Calendar app features a refreshed design with reorganized event details and new icons for improved navigation.
- iCloud calendars now support the "Busy/Free" status option, aligning functionality with Google and other third-party providers.
- Users can now utilize new extra-large Home Screen widgets for "List" and "Up Next" views to display more information.
- Siri gains enhanced onscreen awareness and a visual intelligence feature that creates calendar events by scanning flyers via the Camera app.

### Why it Matters
These updates streamline personal scheduling by reducing manual data entry and improving the integration between Apple’s ecosystem and third-party calendar services. By leveraging AI and expanded interface options, Apple is positioning its native app as a more competitive and efficient tool for professional and personal time management.
