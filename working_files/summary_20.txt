Anthropic’s New AI Model Can Identify More Software Bugs Than Ever. Microsoft Is Struggling To Fix Them Fast Enough.
============================================================

Anthropic’s new AI model, Mythos, is uncovering software vulnerabilities in Microsoft products at a record pace, forcing the company to struggle with a massive backlog of critical security patches.

### Key Points
- Anthropic’s Mythos AI model identified hundreds of critical and important bugs in Microsoft software, including SharePoint, Microsoft 365, and Teams.
- Microsoft engineers are currently in a "mad dash" to patch these vulnerabilities before similar AI tools become widely available to adversarial hackers.
- In July, Microsoft released a record-breaking 600 software patches, highlighting the unprecedented volume of security flaws being discovered.
- Security experts warn that current triage strategies are insufficient because AI can "chain" multiple low-severity bugs together to execute devastating cyberattacks.
- Internal documents suggest Microsoft’s security response teams are understaffed, struggling to balance the high cost of patching legacy code against new product development.

### Why it Matters
The rapid discovery of vulnerabilities by AI models like Mythos signals a new era of cybersecurity where the volume of exploitable flaws threatens to overwhelm traditional software maintenance practices. If companies fail to adapt their triage strategies to account for AI-driven bug chaining, they risk leaving critical infrastructure exposed to sophisticated, automated attacks.
