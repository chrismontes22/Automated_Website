EY to add “agent economics” role to scrutinise AI spending- report
============================================================

EY is hiring a head of agent economics to oversee its AI-powered workforce and ensure that the firm’s technology investments deliver measurable business value across all global divisions.

### Key Points
- The new executive will lead EY’s AI value realisation office, reporting to global managing partner Anthony Caterino.
- The role aims to centralize the management of AI agents to prevent fragmented deployments across different business functions.
- EY is advising clients to adopt similar oversight structures to track the tangible impact of their own artificial intelligence initiatives.
- The firm previously introduced AI token budgets and routing systems to optimize model selection and control operational spending.

### Why it Matters
This move reflects a growing corporate trend of shifting from experimental AI adoption to rigorous financial accountability and centralized governance. By establishing a dedicated oversight office, EY is setting a precedent for how large enterprises can quantify the return on investment for complex, agentic AI systems.
