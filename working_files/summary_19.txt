The Post-training Process OpenAI Used for ChatGPT
============================================================

Post-training processes like reinforcement learning from human feedback transform raw, pretrained language models into conversational, safe, and highly capable assistants through structured, multi-stage alignment and optimization pipelines.

### Key Points
- Post-training enables essential capabilities including conversational fluency, safety alignment, tool use, and complex multistep reasoning.
- The foundational InstructGPT pipeline consists of three stages: supervised fine-tuning, reward model training, and reinforcement learning with human feedback (RLHF).
- Reward models are trained on human preference rankings to grade model outputs, which are then used to optimize the policy model via the PPO algorithm.
- KL divergence penalties are applied during training to prevent "reward hacking" and ensure the model does not drift too far from its original behavior.
- Reasoning models, such as OpenAI’s o1, utilize significant inference-time compute to generate internal chains of thought, drastically improving performance on complex logic tasks.
- RLHF is highly efficient, allowing smaller models to outperform significantly larger ones by prioritizing high-quality, human-aligned training data.

### Why it Matters
These post-training techniques are the primary drivers behind the practical utility and safety of modern frontier AI models. By refining how models interact with users and external tools, these methods allow developers to balance helpfulness with strict safety constraints, ultimately determining how effectively AI can be integrated into professional and daily workflows.
