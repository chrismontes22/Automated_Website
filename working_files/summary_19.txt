Old iPad Keyboard Makes For a Modern Netbook
============================================================

Maker bob-foss has revived the portable netbook concept by combining a high-end Lenovo gaming tablet with a modified iPad Mini keyboard case using a custom 3D-printed mount.

### Key Points
- Enthusiast bob-foss created a compact laptop by repurposing a hinged keyboard case originally designed for an iPad Mini.
- The project utilizes a 3D-printed adapter to securely integrate a high-performance Lenovo gaming tablet into the legacy hardware.
- This build avoids the performance limitations and poor specifications that historically plagued the netbook category during its peak popularity.
- The resulting device offers a functional, professional-grade form factor suitable for mobile productivity without the clutter of traditional DIY cyberdeck projects.

### Why it Matters
This project demonstrates how modern high-performance tablets can be paired with legacy accessories to create functional, ultra-portable computing solutions. It highlights a continued consumer interest in small form factor devices that prioritize portability and build quality over the compromised hardware typical of the original netbook era.
