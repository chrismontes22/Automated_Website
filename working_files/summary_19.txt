Bitcoin Hits 3-Week Low As Strategy Plans $5 Billion Sale
============================================================

Bitcoin prices dropped to their lowest level since July 9 after Strategy announced plans to sell up to $5 billion in cryptocurrency following a disappointing quarterly earnings report.

### Key Points
- Bitcoin fell 3.1% to approximately $62,702 on Friday following the company's announcement.
- Strategy reported quarterly revenue of $122.4 million, missing analyst expectations of $122.9 million.
- CEO Phong Le stated the potential sale aims to bolster cash reserves and fund future stock repurchases.
- The firm currently holds 843,775 bitcoin, which had an aggregate market value of $63.8 billion as of July 6.
- Shares of Strategy declined 7.3% on Friday, contributing to a 42% loss for the company so far this year.

### Why it Matters
This move signals a potential shift in strategy for the world’s largest institutional bitcoin holder as it seeks to improve liquidity and address investor concerns regarding its capital structure. The decision highlights the ongoing tension between aggressive cryptocurrency accumulation and the need for traditional financial stability in volatile market conditions.
