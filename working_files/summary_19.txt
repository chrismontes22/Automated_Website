Zuck rekindles open weights Llama drama with Muse Glimmer
============================================================

Meta has released Muse Glimmer, a 30-billion parameter large language model, marking the company's return to the open weights arena after a year-long hiatus from such releases.

### Key Points
- Muse Glimmer is a 30-billion parameter large language model developed by Meta.
- This release signals Meta's strategic shift back toward the open weights development model.
- An open version of the related Muse Spark model is expected to follow this release.
- CEO Mark Zuckerberg recently emphasized the importance of open weights in the future of artificial intelligence.

### Why it Matters
This release reignites the industry debate regarding the accessibility of powerful AI models versus proprietary "superintelligence" controlled by a few corporations. By providing open weights, Meta influences the competitive landscape for developers and researchers who rely on accessible, high-performance models for their own applications.
