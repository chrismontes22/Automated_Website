Fed chair warns AI spending could fuel inflation and pledges to contain prices
============================================================

Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh warned that rapid artificial intelligence investment could trigger economic shocks, pledging to prioritize price stability amid rising inflation and global geopolitical instability.

### Key Points
- Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh identified AI spending as a potential economic shock that increases demand before boosting supply-side productivity.
- U.S. inflation reached a four-month high of 3.7% in September, exceeding the Federal Reserve's 2% target rate.
- The Federal Reserve implemented a quarter-percentage-point interest rate hike in September to combat rising consumer prices.
- Ongoing Middle East conflict and the Iran war have contributed to global oil supply shocks and increased gasoline prices.
- CME Group’s FedWatch Tool indicates an 86% market probability that the Federal Reserve will maintain current interest rates at the October 28 meeting.

### Why it Matters
The Federal Reserve faces a delicate balancing act as it attempts to curb inflation without stifling the economic growth driven by emerging artificial intelligence technologies. Investors are closely monitoring these policy signals to gauge whether the central bank will prioritize aggressive rate hikes or maintain stability in the coming months.
