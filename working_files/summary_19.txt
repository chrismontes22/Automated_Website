Oh no, here come the AI Tamagotchis
============================================================

Meta unveiled a new lineup of AI-powered wearables and hardware at its Connect event, including next-generation VR glasses and the Muse Charm AI agent device.

### Key Points
- Meta introduced third-generation Ray-Ban Meta camera glasses and a camera-free audio-only version.
- The company announced new $1,299 VR glasses scheduled for a spring 2027 release.
- The Muse Charm is a portable AI agent device featuring a 2-inch OLED display and 5G connectivity.
- New flagship smartphones from Oppo, Xiaomi, and Motorola were announced featuring the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 chip.
- Disney+ increased its ad-free Premium plan price to $21.49, marking its fourth price hike in four years.

### Why it Matters
Meta’s pivot toward integrated AI hardware signals a shift in the tech industry from failed early-stage AI gadgets to more capable, agent-driven consumer devices. This aggressive expansion into wearables and AI agents sets the stage for increased competition with upcoming hardware from companies like OpenAI.
