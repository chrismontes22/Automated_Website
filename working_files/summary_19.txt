Paradex facilitates record $16M ETH options trade at lower cost than Deribit
============================================================

Decentralized exchange Paradex has executed a record $15.76 million Ethereum options trade, demonstrating that on-chain platforms can offer more competitive pricing than centralized incumbents like Deribit.

### Key Points
- Paradex processed the $15.76 million ETH options trade using Paradigm’s request-for-quote (RFQ) liquidity network.
- The trade incurred a premium of $45,220, which the platform reports is cheaper than equivalent execution costs on Deribit.
- Following the RFQ integration on September 15, 2026, Paradex saw open interest surge 2.6 times to reach $202 million.
- The platform utilizes Starknet’s ZK-based encryption to ensure trade privacy and offers zero fees for retail participants.
- New European-style ETH options on the exchange feature cash settlement via time-weighted average price to mitigate potential market manipulation.

### Why it Matters
This trade serves as a proof of concept that institutional investors will utilize decentralized infrastructure if execution quality and costs remain competitive with centralized alternatives. By leveraging established off-chain liquidity networks, Paradex effectively bypasses the liquidity hurdles that have historically hindered the growth of decentralized derivatives protocols.
