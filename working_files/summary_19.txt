Satechi’s Dual-Monitor Adapter Gives Its USB-C Plug a Parking Spot
============================================================

Satechi has launched the ExtendView USB4 Dual HDMI Multiport Adapter, a compact docking solution designed to support dual 4K displays and multiple peripherals through a single laptop connection.

### Key Points
- The adapter features two HDMI ports supporting 4K resolution at 60Hz for extended display setups.
- It includes two USB-A and two USB-C data ports capable of transfer speeds up to 10Gbps.
- The device supports up to 125W of pass-through charging for laptops when paired with a 140W power supply.
- A built-in storage slot for the integrated braided nylon cable prevents the connector from dangling during transport.
- Compatibility varies by model, with base M1 and M2 MacBooks limited to a single external display.
- The unit is currently available on the Satechi US store for $119.99.

### Why it Matters
This adapter offers a portable alternative to bulky desktop docks for professionals who frequently transition between mobile and stationary workspaces. By streamlining cable management and display connectivity, it simplifies hardware integration for users with limited desk space or high-performance peripheral needs.
