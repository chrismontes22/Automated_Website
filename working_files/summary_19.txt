Coffee Break: Dolly Parton, the Dead Zone, Junk Food Addiction, and Algorithmic Intelligence
============================================================

This news summary covers the legacy of Dolly Parton, the environmental impact of industrial agriculture, the debate over ultra-processed food addiction, and the limitations of artificial intelligence in biology.

### Key Points
- Country music icon Dolly Parton is remembered for her cultural influence, philanthropic Imagination Library, and extensive songwriting catalog.
- The Gulf of Mexico’s "dead zone" continues to expand due to nutrient runoff from industrial farms entering the Mississippi River.
- Researchers are debating whether ultra-processed foods should be classified as addictive due to their formulation for hyper-palatability.
- A study on the bacteriophage ΦX174 reveals that current AI models struggle to predict the biological consequences of genetic mutations.
- Computer scientists warn that widespread reliance on generative AI may lead to cultural homogenization and the silencing of diverse perspectives.

### Why it Matters
These topics highlight the tension between technological advancement and the complex, often overlooked realities of biological and social systems. Understanding these challenges is essential for evaluating how industrial policies and emerging tools like AI impact both public health and the natural environment.
