ASOS Confirms Data Breach Linked to Stolen Employee Credentials
============================================================

ASOS has confirmed a data breach occurring on October 6 after attackers compromised an employee account to access third-party platforms and expose sensitive customer personal information.

### Key Points
- Attackers gained unauthorized access by impersonating a trusted contact to steal employee login credentials.
- The breach involved third-party platforms, including a Simon AI instance, rather than direct access to ASOS internal systems.
- Stolen data includes customer names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, and specific website search history.
- ASOS confirmed that payment information remains secure and the company's core operations were not disrupted by the incident.
- The threat actor, identified as the "Xuanye group," utilized a newly created Telegram channel to communicate the breach.
- Snowflake, a cloud data platform, is investigating the incident following claims that its infrastructure was involved in the unauthorized access.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the significant security risks posed by third-party integrations and the increasing sophistication of social engineering attacks against corporate employees. Customers should remain vigilant against potential phishing attempts, as the exposure of detailed search history and contact information increases the risk of targeted fraud.
