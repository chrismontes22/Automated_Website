Blue Yonder redesigns supply chain operations around AI agents
============================================================

Blue Yonder is redesigning its supply chain software architecture to replace fragmented manual workflows with autonomous AI agents that operate at machine speed under human supervision.

### Key Points
- Blue Yonder CEO Duncan Angove stated that traditional forecast-based supply chain models are failing due to permanent global market disruptions.
- The company is shifting from dashboard-based interfaces to a "frictionless outcomes" model where operators set objectives rather than executing individual tasks.
- Blue Yonder is collaborating with Nvidia Corp. to train specialized open-weight AI models for supply chain orchestration.
- The new operating model aims to dissolve departmental silos, creating a single, organic, and adaptive system for managing global goods.
- Adoption is primarily driven by persistent labor shortages rather than a goal of reducing total headcount.

### Why it Matters
This transition represents a fundamental shift in enterprise software, moving from tools that assist human planners to systems that autonomously manage complex logistics. By automating routine decision-making, companies can better navigate geopolitical volatility and supply chain instability while allowing human staff to focus on high-level strategic objectives.
