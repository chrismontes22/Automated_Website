While Hollywood Fears an AI Future, China's Film Industry is Embracing It
============================================================

Chinese actors are increasingly licensing their digital likenesses to production studios as AI-driven micro dramas transform the entertainment industry by drastically reducing traditional film production costs.

### Key Points
- Over 128,000 AI-generated micro dramas were produced in China during the first quarter of 2024, accounting for more than 95% of the market.
- Tech giants including ByteDance and Kuaishou Technology provide the video-generation software fueling this rapid industry shift.
- Shenzhen-based studio Feixiang Universe produces 90-minute films for $500,000 by utilizing AI models like ChatGPT, Kimi, and DeepSeek.
- Studios are licensing real human faces to combat the homogenous, overly symmetrical appearance often produced by current AI training data.
- Industry experts suggest these Chinese production trends serve as a potential preview for future shifts within the Hollywood film industry.

### Why it Matters
The rapid adoption of AI in Chinese media demonstrates how automation can slash production budgets while fundamentally altering the role of human performers. As these technologies expand globally, they pose significant questions regarding the future of creative labor and the aesthetic standards of digital storytelling in Hollywood.
