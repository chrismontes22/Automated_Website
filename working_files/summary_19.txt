The Brand-New Apple Watch Ultra 4 Is Already $99 Off, A New Low for This Model
============================================================

The Apple Watch Ultra 4 is currently available for a record-low price of $700, marking a $99 discount on the premium wearable just weeks after its initial market release.

### Key Points
- The $99 discount applies to the black titanium model featuring a burgundy trail loop.
- The 49mm device offers an extended battery life exceeding two days on a single charge.
- New hardware features include upgraded heart rate sensors and integrated Siri AI capabilities.
- The watch includes built-in cellular connectivity for independent use without an iPhone.
- The previous-generation Apple Watch Ultra 3 is also discounted to $649 for budget-conscious consumers.

### Why it Matters
This significant early price reduction makes Apple’s latest high-end wearable more accessible to consumers seeking advanced health tracking and extended battery performance. The aggressive discounting of both the current and previous models suggests a competitive push to capture market share in the premium smartwatch segment.
