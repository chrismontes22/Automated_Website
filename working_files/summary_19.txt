Samsung’s Galaxy Tab S12 Ultra Costs $1,399.99, Can It Replace Your Laptop?
============================================================

Samsung is launching the Galaxy Tab S12 Ultra and S12+ on October 7, offering high-end AMOLED displays and MediaTek processors starting at $1,199.99 for premium mobile productivity.

### Key Points
- The 14.6-inch Galaxy Tab S12 Ultra starts at $1,399.99, while the 12.6-inch S12+ begins at $1,199.99.
- Both tablets feature the 3nm MediaTek Dimensity 9500 processor, which Samsung claims offers significant CPU, GPU, and NPU performance gains.
- Standard features include an included S Pen, IP68 water and dust resistance, and support for microSD cards up to 2TB.
- The devices run Android 17 with One UI 9, featuring Samsung DeX for desktop-style multitasking and integrated AI tools.
- Keyboards and 45W fast chargers are sold separately, though Samsung is offering a limited-time 30% discount on keyboards in the U.S.

### Why it Matters
These tablets represent an iterative update aimed at users who want to bridge the gap between mobile devices and traditional laptops. Whether these devices can serve as true laptop replacements depends heavily on whether a user's essential professional software is compatible with the Android-based DeX environment.
