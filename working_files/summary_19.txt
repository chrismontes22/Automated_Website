SWI raises $894 million to repurpose former FTX bitcoin miner Genesis Digital to AI
============================================================

SWI Group has raised $894 million to transform the former FTX-backed bitcoin miner Genesis Digital Assets into a specialized data center focused on artificial intelligence and computing workloads.

### Key Points
- SWI Group secured 800 million euros ($894 million) from institutional investors, bringing the company's total valuation to 6.1 billion euros.
- The firm acquired a 70% stake in Genesis Digital Assets, rebranding the unit as SWI Digital to pivot away from cryptocurrency mining.
- Operations will shift to AI-focused data centers in the United States and Sweden, while the company plans to divest existing sites in Kazakhstan.
- CEO Max-Hervé George confirmed the company is abandoning bitcoin mining to prioritize high-performance computing and AI infrastructure.
- The move follows a broader industry trend where miners leverage existing power connections and data center capacity to secure more stable revenue streams.

### Why it Matters
This transition highlights a significant shift in the digital infrastructure sector as companies prioritize AI hosting over the volatile returns of bitcoin mining. By repurposing power-heavy mining sites, firms like SWI Group are capitalizing on the surging demand for high-performance computing capacity.
