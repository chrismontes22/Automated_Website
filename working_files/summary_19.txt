Here Are Tuesday’s Top Wall Street Analyst Research Calls: Advanced Micro Devices, Dynatrace, Five Below, Fortune Brands, Klarna Group, Jersey Mike’s Subs, Moderna, Sherwin-Williams, and More

U.S. stock futures are trading higher as investors await Federal Reserve commentary and key inflation data while monitoring significant market movements in Bitcoin, gold, and energy prices.

### Key Points
- Futures are rising following a mixed Monday session where the Dow Jones Industrials gained 0.26% while the Nasdaq and S&P 500 declined.
- Investors are focused on Wednesday’s Core PCE Price Index and upcoming comments from Federal Reserve Chairman Warsh at the Jackson Hole summit.
- Bitcoin continues a strong rally, trading between $77,000 and $80,000, while gold prices climbed 1.11% to reach $4,653.
- Treasury Secretary Bessent announced plans to utilize the Treasury General Account to help lower interest rates, impacting 10-year and 30-year bond yields.
- Energy prices fell as the Treasury expanded economic sanctions against Iran, with Brent Crude dropping 2.47% to $92.06.
- Wall Street analysts issued several rating changes, including a Strong Buy upgrade for Advanced Micro Devices with a new $641 price target.

### Why it Matters
The upcoming inflation data and Federal Reserve commentary are critical for determining the future trajectory of interest rates and broader market stability. These economic indicators, combined with shifting geopolitical tensions and commodity price volatility, are currently driving significant investor caution and asset reallocation.
