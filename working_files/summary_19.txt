Anti-Circumvention Laws Raise the Cost of Knowledge
============================================================

Reddit is suing SerpAPI and Perplexity under the Digital Millennium Copyright Act, a move that threatens to restrict public access to online data and hinder independent digital research.

### Key Points
- Reddit filed a lawsuit claiming that SerpAPI and Perplexity violated Section 1201 of the Digital Millennium Copyright Act by bypassing access controls.
- The legal action targets tools that crawl and index public Reddit content for use in AI-driven search summaries.
- A federal district judge ruled in July that the lawsuit can proceed, establishing a legal precedent that could limit automated web research.
- Critics argue that Reddit posts are publicly accessible and that indexing them constitutes fair use rather than copyright infringement.
- The case highlights a growing trend of platforms restricting data access to monetize information through exclusive partnerships with major tech companies like Google.

### Why it Matters
This ruling creates significant legal uncertainty for researchers and journalists who rely on large-scale data analysis to study public sentiment and historical trends. If platforms successfully use anti-circumvention laws to gatekeep public information, the cost of accessing knowledge will rise and independent research may become restricted to only the wealthiest institutions.
