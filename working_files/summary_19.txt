Tom Lee Sees Ethereum at $6K if Bitcoin Reaches $150K
============================================================

Fundstrat’s Tom Lee projects Ethereum could reach $6,000 as the cryptocurrency undergoes a fundamental course correction and gains utility through tokenized assets and automated financial software agents.

### Key Points
- Tom Lee identifies Ethereum’s recent price increase as a fundamental recovery rather than a short squeeze.
- The analyst suggests a potential $6,000 valuation for Ethereum if the ETH/BTC ratio reaches 0.04 and Bitcoin hits $150,000.
- Ethereum is positioned to benefit from the growth of stablecoins, tokenized Wall Street assets, and programmable payment rails for AI agents.
- Public blockchains are increasingly viewed as superior infrastructure for automated, high-speed financial transactions compared to traditional banking systems.
- Ethereum is currently trading at approximately $2,432, reflecting a need for significant relative strength to reach Lee’s long-term price targets.

### Why it Matters
This analysis highlights the growing institutional belief that Ethereum’s value is tied to its role as a foundational layer for future automated financial infrastructure. If public blockchains successfully capture the demand for machine-to-machine transactions, it could fundamentally shift how capital moves across global markets.
