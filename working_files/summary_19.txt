Nvidia is investing $1 billion to bolster American scientific computing, while TSMC partners with GlobalFoundries in a $2 billion deal to expand domestic semiconductor packaging capabilities.

### Key Points
- Nvidia is funding the development of at least seven AI-optimized supercomputers to strengthen United States scientific computing infrastructure.
- TSMC and GlobalFoundries have entered a $2 billion agreement to increase domestic production of silicon interposers for high-performance AI datacenters.
- Microsoft is transitioning its Surface Laptop Ultra line to Nvidia SoCs, moving away from Intel processors for its high-end hardware.
- EQT has acquired a majority stake in the Swiss cybersecurity firm Acronis, valuing the company at more than $3.5 billion.
- Shopify has acquired the CSS framework Tailwind to ensure the long-term stability of the open-source project.

### Why it Matters
These investments reflect a broader industry push to secure domestic semiconductor supply chains and high-performance computing power amid rising global demand for AI infrastructure. Strengthening these foundational technologies is critical for maintaining technological sovereignty and supporting the next generation of enterprise-grade artificial intelligence applications.
