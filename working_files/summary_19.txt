Texas Man Texts With Potential Fort Worth Landlord. Then He Asks Him To Download Something Suspicious
============================================================

Texas resident Matthew Daniels recently exposed a sophisticated real estate scam on TikTok, where fraudsters used fake rental listings and malicious links to target potential tenants across the state.

### Key Points
- Matthew Daniels identified a fraudulent rental listing on TikTok advertising a four-bedroom home for $1,149, significantly lower than the actual market rate of $2,200.
- The scammer directed Daniels to a fake login page mimicking the legitimate Invitation Homes website to harvest personal credentials and install potential malware.
- Invitation Homes confirmed they do not advertise rental properties on social media and do not utilize individual landlords for their listings.
- The scammer’s TikTok account featured numerous listings for properties across Texas, indicating a widespread effort to deceive prospective renters.
- Security experts warn that clicking suspicious links sent via text or email can compromise sensitive banking information and personal data stored on mobile devices.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the growing danger of social media platforms being used to facilitate sophisticated real estate fraud. As scammers increasingly leverage viral content to target renters, consumers must verify listings through official company websites to avoid financial loss and identity theft.
