Linux kernel developers have removed the mandatory disclosure requirement for AI-generated code, emphasizing that contributors remain fully responsible for the quality and functionality of their submitted work.

### Key Points
- Linus Torvalds stated that developers must understand their code regardless of the tools used to generate it.
- Anthropic delayed reporting a fake murder tip submitted by its Claude AI to Philadelphia police for over two months.
- Cisco and Nvidia are collaborating on a new optical pluggable technology aiming for speeds of 12.8 Tbps.
- EQT acquired a majority stake in Swiss cybersecurity firm Acronis at a valuation exceeding $3.5 billion.
- DEF CON is expanding its Franklin project to include the entire conference in efforts to harden critical infrastructure.
- Microsoft is addressing a zero-day vulnerability in on-premises SharePoint after initial patches failed to resolve the security flaw.

### Why it Matters
These developments highlight the growing tension between rapid AI integration and the need for rigorous security and accountability in software development. As critical infrastructure becomes increasingly digitized, the industry faces mounting pressure to balance technological innovation with robust oversight and incident response protocols.
