US futures edge higher ahead of PMI data as Iran sanctions and oil remain in focus: Dow Jones, S&P, Nasdaq, Wall Street
============================================================

US stock futures rose slightly on Friday as investors balanced positive earnings from Ross Stores against ongoing concerns regarding rising bond yields and impending sanctions against Iran.

### Key Points
- Dow, S&P 500, and Nasdaq 100 futures posted modest gains of 0.1% to 0.4% during early morning trading.
- Ross Stores shares climbed over 8% after the retailer raised its annual earnings guidance following a strong second-quarter performance.
- US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent announced plans for severe new sanctions against Iran, contributing to volatility in global energy markets.
- Investors are awaiting preliminary August US business activity data, with S&P Global’s services PMI expected to reach 53.9.
- Brent crude prices retreated slightly to $93.41 per barrel but remain on track for a weekly gain of over 5%.

### Why it Matters
The market is currently navigating a complex environment defined by geopolitical tensions in the Middle East and persistent inflationary pressures. These factors, combined with fluctuating bond yields, are forcing investors to closely monitor consumer spending health and the resilience of the broader US economy.
