The Man Who Built China’s Screen Giant Is Betting on RISC-V
============================================================

Beijing-based chip designer ESWIN Computing, founded by BOE creator Wang Dongsheng, is preparing for a Hong Kong initial public offering to scale its RISC-V-based semiconductor and platform business.

### Key Points
- ESWIN is offering 1.57 billion H shares at a price range of HK$1.48 to HK$1.59, with trading scheduled to begin on October 9.
- The company specializes in RISC-V architecture, a royalty-free, open-standard instruction set used in robotics, automotive systems, and artificial intelligence.
- Despite its focus on advanced computing, over 80% of current revenue is derived from human-machine interaction chips used in displays and smartphones.
- The firm remains heavily loss-making, reporting a loss of RMB1.52 billion in 2025, largely due to high research and development expenditures.
- ESWIN’s customer base is highly concentrated, with its top five clients accounting for 89.5% of total revenue in 2025.

### Why it Matters
The company’s public listing serves as a critical test of whether a firm can successfully transition from a niche display-component supplier to a broad-based computing platform provider. By leveraging the open RISC-V architecture, ESWIN aims to challenge established proprietary chip ecosystems, though it must overcome significant financial losses and heavy reliance on a small group of customers to achieve long-term viability.
