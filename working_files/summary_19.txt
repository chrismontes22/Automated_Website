AI Labs Look To Space For Their Data Center Needs
============================================================

As public opposition to land-based data centers grows, major tech companies and startups are seeking regulatory approval to launch massive satellite constellations for orbital artificial intelligence computing.

### Key Points
- SpaceX, in partnership with Nvidia, plans to launch space-optimized AI chips into orbit starting in late 2027.
- The FCC is currently reviewing applications for hundreds of thousands of data center satellites from companies including Amazon, Starcloud, and Cowboy Space.
- Google’s Project Suncatcher aims to deploy solar-powered satellite constellations, with prototype launches scheduled for early 2027.
- China has already begun deploying its own 2,800-satellite network, prompting concerns from the European Space Policy Institute regarding global competitiveness.
- Technical challenges for orbital infrastructure include managing extreme heat in a vacuum, protecting hardware from space radiation, and addressing limited power capacity.

### Why it Matters
Moving data centers to orbit offers a potential solution to local zoning resistance and land-use conflicts in the United States. However, the transition faces significant engineering hurdles and economic questions regarding the long-term viability of deploying expensive, short-lived hardware in space.
