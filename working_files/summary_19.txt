Lenovo's tiny AI PC ditches the box, and it's the smartest form factor I've seen in years
============================================================

The Lenovo Yoga Mini PC features a unique, puck-shaped design and powerful Intel Core Ultra processors, offering a stylish, compact Windows alternative for users prioritizing desk aesthetics and performance.

### Key Points
- Available with Intel Core Ultra 5 325 or Ultra X7 358H processors and up to 32GB of soldered LPDDR5X RAM.
- Features a distinctive circular chassis measuring 130 x 130 x 48.6 mm with an integrated fingerprint scanner and dual microphones.
- Includes a robust port selection featuring two Thunderbolt 4 ports, HDMI 2.1, 2.5 GbE Ethernet, and multiple USB-C and USB-A connections.
- Pricing starts at $700 for the base model, with higher-end configurations reaching $1,712.
- Offers limited internal upgradeability, requiring a suction cup to access the SSD, while RAM remains non-upgradable.

### Why it Matters
The Lenovo Yoga Mini PC challenges the traditional "box" aesthetic of small-form-factor computers by prioritizing a premium, display-worthy design. While its price point competes with high-end laptops, it provides a unique, space-saving solution for Windows users who value both desk ergonomics and capable processing power.
