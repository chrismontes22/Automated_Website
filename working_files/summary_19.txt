Happy 25th Anniversary, iPod
============================================================

Apple celebrates the 25th anniversary of the original iPod, the iconic portable music player that transformed the company and revolutionized the digital music industry starting in 2001.

### Key Points
- Steve Jobs unveiled the first iPod on October 23, 2001, featuring a 5GB hard drive and a mechanical scroll wheel for $399.
- The device promised "1,000 songs in your pocket" and utilized FireWire for rapid music synchronization with iTunes.
- Apple expanded the lineup over two decades to include the iPod mini, nano, shuffle, and the touchscreen-based iPod touch.
- By 2011, Apple had sold more than 304 million iPod units worldwide, cementing its status as a cultural phenomenon.
- Apple officially discontinued the final remaining model, the iPod touch, in May 2022, citing the integration of music features into other devices.

### Why it Matters
The iPod served as the primary catalyst for Apple's transition from a computer manufacturer into a dominant force in consumer electronics and digital media. Its success laid the essential groundwork for the iPhone and the modern ecosystem of integrated Apple services.
