Transcript: Glen Kacher, CIO of Light Street Capital
============================================================

**Light Street Capital founder Glen Kacher discusses the long-term investment potential of artificial intelligence and the critical role of semiconductor infrastructure in the evolving technology landscape.**

### Key Points
- Glen Kacher, CIO of Light Street Capital, emphasizes that AI is in the early stages of a 10-to-15-year infrastructure and application development cycle.
- The firm’s portfolio maintains a heavy concentration in key semiconductor players, including Nvidia, AMD, Broadcom, and TSMC, to capture growth in AI compute.
- Kacher identifies "agentic AI"—autonomous systems capable of executing complex tasks—as the next major driver of productivity and market demand.
- Despite concerns regarding energy consumption and data center bottlenecks, Kacher views these as manageable education and infrastructure challenges rather than existential threats to the industry.
- The firm utilizes a "long the disruptor, short the incumbent" strategy, leveraging insights from private venture investments to inform public market positions.

### Why it Matters
Understanding the shift toward agentic AI and the massive capital expenditure in hardware provides a clearer picture of how the technology sector will evolve over the next decade. By focusing on the foundational "picks and shovels" of the AI revolution, investors can better navigate the volatility inherent in disruptive innovation cycles.
