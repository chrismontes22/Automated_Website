Fiserv Targets Turnaround With Project Elevate, AI and Clover Growth
============================================================

Fiserv CEO Takis Georgakopoulos is implementing a strategic turnaround plan focused on platform stability, product simplification, and operational cohesion to drive long-term growth across its financial and merchant segments.

### Key Points
- CEO Takis Georgakopoulos is reorganizing products by customer segment to better leverage synergies across the company’s financial and merchant technology platforms.
- Project Elevate aims to generate $500 million in savings and 200 basis points of margin expansion by consolidating redundant technology stacks and products.
- Fiserv is investing an incremental $100 million into technology infrastructure to accelerate security remediation and integrate advanced artificial intelligence capabilities.
- The company reported a significant year-to-date decline in client departures, attributing the improvement to enhanced service levels and platform stability.
- Clover remains a primary growth driver, with management expanding its capabilities for larger businesses through partnerships with Tabit and Rectangle.
- Fiserv is prioritizing debt reduction to reach a leverage target of 2.5 to 3 times by 2029 before focusing on share repurchases.

### Why it Matters
These operational changes signal a shift toward greater efficiency and internal coordination as Fiserv seeks to modernize its legacy systems and compete more effectively in the fintech sector. By streamlining its product portfolio and investing in AI, the company aims to improve long-term margins and strengthen its value proposition for banking and merchant clients.
