Finland launches criminal probe of suspected environmental damage by Google
============================================================

Finnish police have opened a criminal investigation into Google’s subsidiary, Tuike Finland, over suspected environmental law breaches during site preparation for a new data center in Muhos.

### Key Points
- Authorities are investigating potential violations of the Water Act regarding unauthorized forest clearing and drainage activities.
- The site is part of a $15 billion investment by Alphabet’s Google to expand artificial intelligence infrastructure across Finland.
- Finnish officials ordered a work stoppage at the construction site in early October following public outcry over environmental damage.
- A guilty verdict could result in corporate fines for the company, which has pledged to cooperate with the ongoing police inquiry.

### Why it Matters
This investigation highlights the growing tension between rapid AI infrastructure expansion and strict environmental regulations in European markets. The incident serves as a critical test for how global tech giants manage local legal compliance while navigating increasing public scrutiny over the ecological footprint of large-scale data centers.
