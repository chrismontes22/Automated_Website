Hedge funds quietly placing short bets as midterms loom — here’s who they’re targeting
============================================================

Hedge funds are shorting companies perceived as having close ties to the Trump administration in anticipation of potential Democratic gains during the upcoming midterm elections.

### Key Points
- Hedge funds are betting against stocks of companies that have aligned themselves with the Trump administration, according to the Bear Traps Report.
- Short sellers are targeting firms that contributed to the White House ballroom renovation and those represented at recent administration-led AI summits.
- The strategy anticipates that a Democratic-controlled Congress would launch aggressive investigations into companies with strong ties to the current White House.
- Analysts note that the cryptocurrency sector is also under scrutiny due to the Trump family's recent expansion into digital assets.
- Larry McDonald, who runs the Bear Traps Report, suggests the market has not yet fully priced in the potential for significant political shifts in Congress.

### Why it Matters
This trend highlights how political volatility can directly influence market strategies and stock valuations as investors hedge against potential legislative changes. It underscores a growing intersection between partisan politics and financial risk management, where corporate associations with government officials become a factor in investment performance.
