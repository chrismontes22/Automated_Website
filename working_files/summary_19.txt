Inside Kazakhstan’s push to become a regional AI centre
============================================================

Kazakhstan has appointed an artificial intelligence program named SKAI to its sovereign wealth fund board as part of a national strategy to modernize the economy through advanced technology.

### Key Points
- The sovereign wealth fund Samruk-Kazyna appointed SKAI, an AI program with voting rights, to its board of directors in late 2025.
- President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev designated 2026 as the "Year of Digitalisation and Artificial Intelligence" to drive economic diversification.
- Kazakhstan established a Ministry of Artificial Intelligence and Digital Development and became the second jurisdiction globally to pass comprehensive AI governance legislation.
- The Astana Hub innovation cluster has incubated the country's first unicorn, the video-generation startup Higgsfield, with plans to reach five unicorns by 2030.
- Researchers at Nazarbayev University launched Oylan, a locally developed large language model designed to preserve Kazakh culture and ensure data sovereignty.
- Critics have raised concerns regarding the environmental impact of energy-intensive data centers and the potential for human rights risks in biometric surveillance.

### Why it Matters
This initiative positions Kazakhstan as a regional leader in AI adoption, aiming to reduce economic reliance on raw materials through digital transformation and local tech development. However, the rapid integration of these technologies faces significant scrutiny regarding infrastructure sustainability and the transparency of government oversight.
