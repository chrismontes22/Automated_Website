XRP treasury Evernorth’s shares rise 5% at Nasdaq opening print
============================================================

Evernorth Holdings shares rose 5% during their Nasdaq debut on Monday, marking the launch of the first publicly traded company focused on managing a large-scale XRP digital treasury.

### Key Points
- Evernorth Holdings (XRPN) shares climbed 5% following the company's official listing on the Nasdaq stock exchange.
- The firm appointed former Coinbase executive Hugo Jeandesboz as chief investment officer to oversee treasury strategy and liquidity management.
- Evernorth plans to hold approximately 473 million XRP, valued at roughly $657 million, to become the largest publicly traded XRP treasury.
- The company generates additional income by deploying its XRP holdings through various decentralized finance (DeFi) yield strategies.
- The listing follows an October 2 shareholder approval to merge Evernorth with the blank-check firm Armada Acquisition Corp. II.

### Why it Matters
This listing provides institutional and retail investors with a regulated vehicle to gain exposure to XRP assets through traditional stock markets. By integrating DeFi yield strategies into a public treasury, the company represents a significant bridge between cryptocurrency holdings and conventional financial infrastructure.
