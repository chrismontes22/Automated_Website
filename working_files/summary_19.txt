Trump Promised To Drain The Swamp. Now He’s Building An AI Empire
============================================================

President Trump recently endorsed the rapid expansion of artificial intelligence data centers, sparking debate over the administration's close ties to major tech companies and federal land usage policies.

### Key Points
- President Trump publicly criticized opposition to data centers, arguing they are essential for economic growth and job creation.
- The administration plans to repurpose federal land for AI infrastructure, partnering with companies like NextEra Energy.
- Palantir Technologies secured a $10 billion U.S. Army contract and additional ICE funding in 2025.
- The Pentagon previously blacklisted Anthropic from government contracts after the company refused to allow its AI to be used for autonomous weapons.
- Critics argue these partnerships represent government cronyism rather than a free-market approach to the artificial intelligence industry.

### Why it Matters
The administration's support for data center expansion highlights a shift in policy that prioritizes strategic AI development over the populist skepticism of corporate-government alliances. This trend raises significant questions about the influence of major tech firms on federal land use and the future of government contracting.
