Lucky PC scavenger discovers 12 RTX 3070 GPUs from the crypto mining era — cards survived years of basement storage with only minor signs of wear
============================================================

A Reddit user discovered twelve abandoned Nvidia RTX 3070 graphics cards in a basement, sparking discussions about the viability of repurposing hardware previously used for cryptocurrency mining operations.

### Key Points
- The haul includes 12 Zotac-branded Nvidia RTX 3070 GPUs, MSI MPG Z390 motherboards, and 1000W Rosewill power supplies.
- The hardware was previously utilized for Ethereum mining during the COVID-19 pandemic before being stored unused.
- Inspection revealed minor silicone bleeding from thermal pads, a common issue that typically requires cleaning and re-pasting to restore functionality.
- The RTX 3070 features 8GB of GDDR6 memory and 5,888 CUDA cores, remaining a capable card for 1440p gaming today.
- The user intends to test each unit individually and sell the majority of the cards while retaining at least one for personal use.

### Why it Matters
This discovery highlights the potential for secondary market value in hardware retired from cryptocurrency mining rigs. It also serves as a reminder that mining cards, often run at optimized power levels, can remain functional for gaming if properly cleaned and maintained.
