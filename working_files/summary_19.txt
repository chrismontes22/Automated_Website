BTC price risks $80K loss into FOMC, earnings: Three things to know in Bitcoin this week
============================================================

**Bitcoin struggles to maintain the $80,000 support level as investors brace for a critical week of US Federal Reserve interest rate decisions and major corporate earnings reports.**

### Key Points
- Bitcoin is currently down 4% for October, failing to meet historical "Uptober" performance trends as it tests the $80,000 psychological support level.
- The Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) meets Wednesday, with markets pricing a 90% probability that interest rates will remain in the 3.75-4% range.
- Major US tech companies, including Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon, and Intel, are scheduled to release their third-quarter earnings reports later this week.
- South Korea’s KOSPI index fell 1.5% on Monday, reflecting broader investor anxiety regarding the sustainability of capital expenditure in the artificial intelligence sector.
- The US will release its preferred inflation gauge, the Personal Consumption Expenditures (PCE) index, and third-quarter GDP data on Thursday.

### Why it Matters
The convergence of macroeconomic policy decisions and high-stakes corporate earnings creates significant volatility for both digital assets and traditional equity markets. Investors are closely monitoring these indicators to determine if current market valuations, particularly in the tech sector, can be sustained amid tightening financial conditions.
