Crazy Taxi: World Tour Is Fun Enough, But Some Fans Are Going To Have To Make A Hard Choice
============================================================

Sega is reviving the classic arcade franchise with Crazy Taxi: World Tour, a nostalgic driving game that faces controversy due to the company's use of generative AI tools.

### Key Points
- Crazy Taxi: World Tour maintains the series' signature arcade-style gameplay, featuring chaotic driving and a soundtrack headlined by The Offspring.
- Sega confirmed the use of generative AI during the game's concepting phase, though the company claims all final in-game assets are human-made.
- The title aims to provide a manageable, throwback experience rather than a modern, large-scale open world.
- The integration of AI in development has sparked industry-wide debate regarding ethical standards and the future role of technology in game design.

### Why it Matters
The inclusion of generative AI in a beloved legacy franchise highlights the growing tension between corporate efficiency and player sentiment regarding creative labor. This development forces consumers to weigh their nostalgic attachment to classic series against broader concerns about the ethical implications of AI in the gaming industry.
