Schneider Electric has announced a $22.6 billion acquisition of industrial software firm PTC to bolster its infrastructure capabilities amid rising demand for data center power management solutions.

### Key Points
- Schneider Electric is acquiring PTC for $22.6 billion to expand its portfolio of smart power infrastructure and industrial software.
- The deal reflects a broader market trend where infrastructure companies are capitalizing on the rapid expansion of global data centers.
- Microsoft is shifting its hardware strategy by replacing Intel processors with Nvidia SoCs in the new Surface Laptop Ultra, starting at $2,599.
- Google is partnering with a nuclear power provider to upgrade reactors, aiming to add 890MW of capacity through improved turbines and digital controls.
- EQT has acquired a majority stake in Swiss cybersecurity firm Acronis, valuing the company at more than $3.5 billion.
- Shopify has acquired the CSS framework Tailwind to ensure the long-term stability of the project as web development trends shift toward AI-assisted coding.

### Why it Matters
This wave of high-value acquisitions and infrastructure investments highlights how major corporations are aggressively positioning themselves to support the energy and software demands of the AI era. These strategic moves signal a long-term commitment to scaling the physical and digital foundations required for modern computing and data center operations.
