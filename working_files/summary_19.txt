The iPhone Duo’s $1,999 price looks almost reasonable once you see what Apple charges elsewhere
============================================================

Apple has launched its first foldable smartphone, the iPhone Duo, which features a $1,999 starting price in the United States that remains the lowest among all global markets.

### Key Points
- The iPhone Duo features a 7.6-inch internal OLED display, an A20 Pro chip, and a quad-camera system.
- While the US starting price is $1,999, international costs vary significantly due to regional pricing strategies and local taxes.
- Consumers in Türkiye and Brazil face the highest costs, with prices exceeding $4,700 and $4,200 respectively when converted to US dollars.
- European markets, including the UK, France, and Germany, see prices ranging from approximately $2,600 to $2,665.
- The device is available in storage configurations ranging from 256GB to 2TB and includes IP68 water and dust resistance.

### Why it Matters
The significant price disparity highlights how Apple adjusts its global strategy to account for local economic factors and import costs. This pricing structure positions the US market as the most affordable entry point for the company's new foldable technology.
