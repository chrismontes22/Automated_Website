Samsung Galaxy S26 FE leaks in unboxing video
============================================================

Samsung is set to officially unveil the Galaxy S26 FE on August 27, featuring a 6.7-inch display, the Exynos 2500 chipset, and a triple-lens rear camera system.

### Key Points
- The device features a 6.7-inch FHD+ Super AMOLED screen with a 120Hz refresh rate.
- Internal hardware includes the Exynos 2500 chipset, 8GB of RAM, and up to 256GB of storage.
- The camera setup consists of a 50MP main sensor, a 12MP ultrawide lens, and an 8MP telephoto lens.
- Power is supplied by a 4,900 mAh battery supporting 45W wired charging.
- Samsung plans to provide seven years of OS upgrades for the IP68-rated handset.
- Available color options include Blueberry, Graphite, and Pistachio.

### Why it Matters
This release represents Samsung's latest effort to capture the mid-range market by offering premium features like long-term software support at a lower price point than flagship models. However, the hardware specifications have drawn criticism from some analysts who question the device's value proposition compared to competing smartphones in the same category.
