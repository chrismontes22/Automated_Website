Wall St Week Ahead: Earnings flood, Fed meeting will test rickety AI market rally
============================================================

Technology stocks face increased volatility as investors scrutinize the sustainability of massive artificial intelligence spending ahead of a critical week of quarterly earnings and Federal Reserve policy updates.

### Key Points
- The Nasdaq Composite declined following disappointing quarterly reports from Meta and semiconductor firm Lam Research.
- Alphabet, Microsoft, and Amazon are scheduled to release earnings this week, representing a pivotal test for the AI-driven market rally.
- Hyperscalers are projected to spend approximately $800 billion on capital investments this year, raising concerns about long-term returns and financing costs.
- S&P 500 earnings are currently on track to grow by more than 32% compared to the previous year.
- The Federal Reserve is expected to hold interest rates steady during Wednesday's meeting, though markets are pricing in an 80% chance of a hike in December.

### Why it Matters
The market is shifting from blind optimism toward a more discerning evaluation of whether massive AI infrastructure investments are generating tangible financial returns. These upcoming earnings reports will determine if the recent tech pullback is a temporary correction or the beginning of a broader shift in investor confidence.
