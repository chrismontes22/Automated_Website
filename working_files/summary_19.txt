Guillermo Del Toro Assures “No Godd*mn AI” Used In ‘Pan’s Labyrinth’ 3D Re-Release — Comic-Con
============================================================

Director Guillermo Del Toro announced that his 20th-anniversary 3D re-release of Pan’s Labyrinth will feature no artificial intelligence, prioritizing traditional restoration techniques to preserve the film's artistic integrity.

### Key Points
- Guillermo Del Toro confirmed the 3D re-release of Pan’s Labyrinth will premiere in theaters on October 9.
- The director explicitly rejected the use of generative AI in the restoration process, citing a commitment to protecting the redeeming power of art.
- The film will be presented in 4K, with specific versions available in 3D and HDR by Barco.
- Cineverse and Fathom are partnering to handle the theatrical distribution for this milestone anniversary event.
- During a San Diego Comic-Con panel, Del Toro reflected on the production challenges of the 2006 dark fantasy set during the Spanish Civil War.

### Why it Matters
This decision highlights the ongoing tension between traditional filmmaking craftsmanship and the increasing integration of generative technology in Hollywood post-production. By choosing more expensive manual restoration methods, Del Toro reinforces his position as a prominent advocate for human-led artistry in the modern film industry.
