Antino Backdoor Lets China-Linked UAT-11587 Turn Microsoft 365 Into a C2 Channel
============================================================

Cisco Talos researchers have identified a China-nexus espionage group, UAT-11587, using a novel Rust-based backdoor called Antino to target government and policy organizations across eight Asian countries.

### Key Points
- The UAT-11587 campaign has compromised at least 350 endpoints across 16 organizations since September 2025.
- The Antino backdoor uses Microsoft 365, specifically Outlook and OneDrive via Microsoft Graph, to execute commands and exfiltrate stolen data.
- Attackers utilize highly targeted phishing emails featuring spoofed domains and pixel-perfect recreations of Gmail attachment previews to deceive victims.
- The malware delivery chain leverages legitimate cloud services like Cloudflare Pages and Amazon CloudFront to blend malicious traffic with standard HTTPS activity.
- Forensic evidence, including Simplified Chinese metadata and mainland-specific Rust package mirrors, links the activity to China-based threat actors.

### Why it Matters
This campaign demonstrates how sophisticated actors can bypass traditional security filters by abusing trusted enterprise platforms and legitimate diagnostic binaries. By operating entirely within the Microsoft 365 ecosystem, the Antino backdoor effectively evades detection, highlighting a critical need for organizations to monitor internal cloud service traffic for anomalous behavior.
