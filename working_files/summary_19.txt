Is Thermo Fisher Scientific Stock Outperforming the Nasdaq?
============================================================

Thermo Fisher Scientific, a $230 billion life sciences leader, continues to show long-term growth despite recent short-term stock volatility and underperformance compared to the broader Nasdaq Composite index.

### Key Points
- Thermo Fisher Scientific maintains a $230 billion market cap, solidifying its status as a mega-cap stock within the life sciences and diagnostics industry.
- Shares have risen 27% over the past 52 weeks, outperforming the Nasdaq Composite’s 21.6% return during the same period.
- The stock has declined 27.7% over the last three months, currently trading 3.8% below its 52-week high of $647.
- Analysts maintain a "Strong Buy" consensus rating with a mean price target of $636.56, representing a 2.3% upside from current levels.
- The company’s performance is bolstered by recovering biotech research spending and an optimistic financial outlook through 2026.

### Why it Matters
Thermo Fisher Scientific’s ability to outpace competitors like Danaher Corporation highlights its resilience in the volatile life sciences sector. Sustained investor confidence suggests that the company's strategic focus on biopharmaceutical services and laboratory technology remains a key driver for long-term market growth.
