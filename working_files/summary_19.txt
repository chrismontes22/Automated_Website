Power Operators Must Plan Now for Four Quantum Attacks
============================================================

The White House is urging critical infrastructure sectors to prepare for quantum computing threats that could compromise essential power grid security and data integrity by as early as 2029.

### Key Points
- The June executive order, "Securing the Nation Against Advanced Cryptographic Attacks," mandates proactive defense measures for energy and utility infrastructure.
- Adversaries are currently employing "harvest now, decrypt later" tactics, intercepting and storing encrypted utility data for future decryption by quantum machines.
- Quantum-capable attackers could manipulate SCADA telemetry and control commands, potentially causing physical damage to turbines, generators, and power grid equipment.
- "Islanded" AI data centers with dedicated power supplies face heightened risks, as they lack the backup support of the broader electrical grid.
- Executives are advised to prioritize cryptographic inventory, implement crypto-agility in new hardware, and mandate NIST-standard compliance in all future procurement contracts.

### Why it Matters
Quantum computing poses an existential threat to the public key cryptography that currently secures global energy infrastructure and industrial control systems. If these defenses fail, adversaries could gain unauthorized control over critical power assets, leading to widespread outages and permanent physical damage to essential equipment.
