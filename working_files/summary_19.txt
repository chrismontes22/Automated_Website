The bloodletting in stocks is stanched as investors buy the dip and rotate out of tech
============================================================

Global stock markets rebounded as investors bought the dip following recent tech sector volatility, while expectations for an October interest rate hike by the Federal Reserve significantly declined.

### Key Points
- The S&P 500 rose 0.33% yesterday, with the equal-weight index climbing 1.36% as market gains broadened beyond technology stocks.
- CME FedWatch data indicates an 85% probability that the Federal Reserve will maintain current interest rates during the upcoming October meeting.
- Energy sector stocks in the S&P 500 have surged 50% year-to-date, driven by rising oil prices and increased power demand from AI data centers.
- Asian markets saw broad gains, with South Korea’s Kospi rising 2.05% and Japan’s Nikkei 225 increasing 1.39% in recent trading.
- Bitcoin reached a price of $82,101, while Brent crude oil traded at $105 per barrel.

### Why it Matters
The shift away from tech-heavy concentration toward broader market participation suggests a healthier, more sustainable rally for investors. Stable interest rate expectations provide a favorable environment for borrowing costs, potentially supporting continued growth across diverse economic sectors.
