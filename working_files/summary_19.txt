Mobile ad giants AppLovin and Unity locked in legal dispute over data collection
============================================================

AppLovin has filed a lawsuit against Unity in a California court, seeking to block the company from allegedly using proprietary advertising data to train its competing machine-learning models.

### Key Points
- AppLovin filed for a temporary restraining order in the Superior Court of California to stop Unity from collecting data via its Ad Quality SDK.
- The lawsuit alleges Unity improperly intercepted information regarding ad creatives, user engagement, and auction clearing prices to gain a competitive advantage.
- AppLovin is simultaneously pursuing JAMS arbitration for claims including trade-secret misappropriation, breach of contract, and unfair competition.
- Unity denies the allegations, characterizing the lawsuit as an attempt by a dominant incumbent to stifle competition and hinder a smaller product.
- A court hearing regarding the request for a temporary restraining order is scheduled for September 30 at 11 a.m. PT.

### Why it Matters
This legal battle highlights the intense competition and data sensitivity within the mobile advertising industry as platforms leverage AI to optimize ad auctions. The outcome could set significant precedents for how advertising technology companies are permitted to collect and utilize data from rival mediation platforms.
