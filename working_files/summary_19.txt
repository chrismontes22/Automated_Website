I ditched flagship everything for niche tech that actually solves real problems
============================================================

Niche technology projects like E Ink monitors, modular hardware, and self-hosted services are increasingly appealing to users who prioritize repairability, privacy, and long-term sustainability over mainstream flagship specifications.

### Key Points
- E Ink monitors offer a power-efficient, eye-friendly alternative to high-refresh-rate OLED and LCD screens for productivity tasks.
- The Fairphone 5 features a modular design, allowing users to easily replace components like batteries and camera sensors using a standard screwdriver.
- Framework laptops provide a modular architecture that enables users to upgrade individual parts, including mainboards and processors, over several years.
- Self-hosting services like Immich, Nextcloud, and Jellyfin allow users to manage personal data and media independently of subscription-based cloud platforms.
- These niche alternatives emphasize user control and ethical manufacturing over the rapid upgrade cycles typical of mainstream consumer electronics.

### Why it Matters
The shift toward modular and self-hosted technology reflects a growing consumer desire for hardware longevity and digital sovereignty. By prioritizing repairability and open-source software, these companies and projects challenge the industry-standard model of planned obsolescence and corporate data control.
