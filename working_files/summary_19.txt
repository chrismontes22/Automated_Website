MediaTek Dimensity 9600M is also official
============================================================

MediaTek has unveiled the Dimensity 9600M, a new flagship chipset featuring advanced generative and agentic AI capabilities, designed to bring high-end performance to a broader range of smartphones.

### Key Points
- The Dimensity 9600M is built on TSMC’s 3nm N3P process and features an all-large-core CPU architecture reaching 4.21GHz.
- Key hardware includes the Arm Mali-G1 Ultra MC12 GPU, support for LPDDR5X RAM, and UFS 4.1 storage.
- The chipset integrates the NPU 990 to support advanced generative and agentic AI tasks.
- Connectivity features include Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6.0, and comprehensive 5G support via the 3GPP-R17 modem.
- The processor supports high-end camera sensors up to 320MP and display refresh rates of 180Hz for WQHD+ screens.
- Initial devices featuring the chip, including the vivo X500 and Oppo Find X10, are expected to launch later this quarter.

### Why it Matters
This release allows MediaTek to democratize premium AI features by integrating high-end software capabilities into a more accessible hardware tier. By expanding its flagship-level AI engine to more devices, the company strengthens its competitive position against rivals in the rapidly evolving smartphone processor market.
