Bakare Mubarak unveils initiative at UN forum
============================================================

Cultural ambassador Bakare Mubarak has launched the Sino-African Tech-Creative Corridor to integrate artificial intelligence and digital infrastructure between Chinese innovation hubs and Nigeria’s expanding creative economy.

### Key Points
- Bakare Mubarak introduced the initiative at the United Nations High-Level Political Forum in New York.
- The project aims to connect technology infrastructure in Shenzhen, China, with the creative industry in Lagos, Nigeria.
- The framework focuses on using artificial intelligence and cryptographic provenance to protect African digital intellectual property.
- The initiative seeks to move beyond traditional sponsorship by fostering long-term institutional partnerships with global investors.
- The presentation was supported by Global Youth Philanthropy and the Peaceland Foundation.

### Why it Matters
This initiative represents a strategic shift toward positioning African youth as active architects of industrial technology rather than passive consumers. By bridging the gap between Chinese innovation hubs and African creative markets, the project could significantly enhance the economic viability and legal protection of digital assets across the continent.
