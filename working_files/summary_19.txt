Prolo Ring Lets You Click and Scroll Without a Mouse
============================================================

The Prolo Ring is a new Bluetooth-enabled wearable input controller designed to replace traditional mice by allowing users to navigate multiple devices using finger-based gestures and touch surfaces.

### Key Points
- The Prolo Ring features an 18 × 30 mm capacitive trackpad and a "Modstrip" for switching between four distinct control modes.
- The Pro Edition supports over 40 customizable air gestures, macros, and shortcuts via the Prolo Studio desktop application.
- It connects via standard Bluetooth HID to Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS, and XR devices without requiring specific drivers.
- The device weighs between 4.1 and 5.2 grams and offers approximately 10 hours of battery life on a single charge.
- Users must select from US sizes 7–18 using a provided sizing kit, as the 23 mm wide band requires a precise fit.

### Why it Matters
This wearable technology offers a space-saving alternative for professionals managing multiple devices in cramped workspaces by eliminating the need for a dedicated mouse surface. By consolidating input controls into a single finger-worn device, it provides a versatile, cross-platform solution for users who frequently switch between laptops, tablets, and smart TVs.
