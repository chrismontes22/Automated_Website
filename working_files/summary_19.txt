CartographAI Launched To Help Advertisers Pick The Right Tech Vendors. Now, It’s Helping Vendors Market Themselves, Too
============================================================

Startup CartographAI, founded by industry veterans Jay Friedman and Danilo Tauro, provides advertisers with objective vendor assessments and an accelerator program to streamline ad tech procurement processes.

### Key Points
- CartographAI offers free, criteria-based rankings for approximately 1,000 ad tech vendors, categorizing them as emerging, capable, or advanced.
- The company launched an accelerator program for vendors like The Trade Desk and Adelaide, charging $1,000 to $3,000 monthly for market insights and co-marketing.
- Founders Jay Friedman and Danilo Tauro utilize their backgrounds from Goodway Group and Procter & Gamble to provide expert-led, buy-side focused consulting services.
- A proprietary AI analyst tool is scheduled for release later this year to help brands automate vendor discovery and RFP evaluations.
- The platform aims to reduce the typical six-month vendor evaluation cycle by translating complex technical offerings into actionable, buyer-centric language.

### Why it Matters
This platform addresses the fragmentation of the ad tech market by providing a transparent, data-driven alternative to traditional industry analyst reports. By aligning vendor capabilities with specific advertiser needs, CartographAI helps brands reduce procurement friction and make more informed technology investments.
