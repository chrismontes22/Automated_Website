MarketBeat Week in Review – 08/31 - 09/04
============================================================

Stock markets face downward pressure as a strong jobs report fuels interest rate hike concerns, while investors continue to monitor corporate earnings and emerging artificial intelligence infrastructure trends.

### Key Points
- Markets drifted lower heading into the holiday weekend due to a robust jobs report that may influence Federal Reserve interest rate decisions.
- Cybersecurity remains a top-performing sector, with Palo Alto Networks seeing analyst price target upgrades despite high valuations.
- Broadcom reported strong earnings but provided weak guidance, citing supply issues rather than a decline in artificial intelligence demand.
- Eli Lilly announced the acquisition of Merida Biosciences to expand its portfolio beyond the obesity market into autoimmune and allergic diseases.
- AeroVironment secured a landmark U.S. Army contract, potentially serving as a catalyst ahead of its September 9 earnings report.
- NVIDIA is investing $3.5 billion in MediaTek to strengthen its accelerated computing position against emerging custom silicon competition.

### Why it Matters
The current market environment reflects a transition period where investors are balancing macroeconomic uncertainty with the sustained growth potential of the artificial intelligence sector. Monitoring these specific corporate developments and sector shifts is essential for navigating potential volatility as the market reacts to upcoming inflation data.
