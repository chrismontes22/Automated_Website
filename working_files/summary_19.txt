‘The economy is increasingly reliant on AI gains’: U.S. GDP grew 2.2% amid ‘sudden reversal of optimism’ on AI tech
============================================================

The U.S. economy expanded at a solid 2.2% annual pace during the second quarter, driven by robust consumer spending and significant business investment in artificial intelligence technologies.

### Key Points
- The Commerce Department reported a 2.2% GDP growth rate, surpassing the initial estimate of 1.5%.
- Consumer spending rose by 3.8%, a substantial increase from the 0.7% growth recorded in the first quarter.
- Business investment, excluding housing, grew by 9% as companies prioritized spending on artificial intelligence infrastructure.
- A 12.6% surge in imports, largely due to computer chip shipments, reduced overall GDP growth by 1.7 percentage points.
- Underlying economic strength, excluding volatile trade and government spending, reached a 4.6% growth rate.

### Why it Matters
The U.S. economy is increasingly dependent on the artificial intelligence sector to drive both business investment and consumer wealth. This reliance creates potential market vulnerability should investor optimism regarding AI technology suddenly decline.
