The FBI Says North Korean Hackers Stole $6.3 Billion from Binance, and Kim Jong Un’s Sister Calls It a ‘Grave Political Provocation’
============================================================

The FBI has officially attributed the $6.3 billion cryptocurrency theft from Binance to the North Korean hacking group TraderTraitor, despite formal denials from the nation’s leadership.

### Key Points
- The FBI and U.S. Secret Service identified the Lazarus Group, also known as TraderTraitor, as the perpetrator behind the Binance breach.
- Binance reported that the theft occurred after an engineer’s laptop was compromised, allowing unauthorized access to platform funds.
- The exchange has pledged to cover all losses and has already replaced approximately 95% of the stolen assets.
- Following the resumption of transactions on November 1, Binance experienced $4.72 billion in net withdrawals.
- North Korean official Kim Yo Jong denied the allegations, claiming the accusations are a political provocation intended to damage the country's reputation.
- This incident surpasses the $1.5 billion Bybit theft in 2025, which previously held the record for the largest cryptocurrency heist.

### Why it Matters
This massive security breach highlights the growing vulnerability of major cryptocurrency exchanges to state-sponsored cyber operations aimed at bypassing international economic sanctions. The incident underscores the significant financial risks facing digital asset platforms and the ongoing challenge of securing global financial infrastructure against sophisticated, government-backed hacking units.
