Apple’s $250 Million AI iPhone Settlement Is Coming Sooner Than Expected. Learn Who’s Eligible for a Payout
============================================================

A federal court has accelerated the timeline for Apple’s $250 million false-advertising settlement, which compensates customers who purchased specific iPhone models marketed with unavailable artificial intelligence features.

### Key Points
- Apple agreed to a $250 million settlement for customers who bought iPhone 15 Pro, 15 Pro Max, 16, 16E, 16 Plus, 16 Pro, or 16 Pro Max models.
- The lawsuit alleges Apple misled consumers by advertising advanced Siri AI capabilities that were not available when the devices launched.
- US District Court Judge Noël Wise moved the final approval date to February 24, 2027, potentially accelerating payouts by seven months.
- Approximately 36 million customers are eligible for individual payments estimated to range between $25 and $95 per device.
- The settlement administrator, Verita, will contact eligible customers directly once the official claim website is established.

### Why it Matters
This settlement highlights the growing legal risks tech companies face when marketing AI features that are not fully functional at the time of product release. It sets a significant precedent for consumer protection in the rapidly evolving AI hardware market.
