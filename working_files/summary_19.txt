Choose One of These 3 College Majors If You Want to Avoid Being Replaced By AI, According to Experts
============================================================

Experts recommend that college students pursue degrees in anthropology, mathematics, or philosophy to remain competitive and uniquely valuable in a workforce increasingly dominated by artificial intelligence technologies.

### Key Points
- Experts suggest anthropology, mathematics, and philosophy as superior alternatives to computer science for long-term career success.
- Federal Reserve Bank data shows recent computer science graduates face a 6.1% unemployment rate, exceeding the 5.7% average for all recent graduates.
- Industry leaders argue that majoring specifically in AI is too narrow, comparing it to specializing in a single software tool like Excel.
- Mathematics majors are valued for their manual problem-solving skills, while anthropology and philosophy provide critical insights into human behavior and ethics.
- The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics projects a 15% growth in software developer jobs through 2034, indicating strong long-term demand despite current entry-level challenges.

### Why it Matters
These recommendations highlight a shift in the labor market toward valuing uniquely human cognitive skills over narrow technical training. By focusing on foundational disciplines, students may better adapt to an evolving professional landscape where AI handles routine technical tasks.
