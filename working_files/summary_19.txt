Norway Should Buy OpenAI
============================================================

The Norwegian Government Pension Fund Global should acquire OpenAI to prevent the concentration of wealth and power, ensuring transformative artificial intelligence benefits humanity rather than private shareholders.

### Key Points
- The proposal suggests the $2 trillion Norwegian Government Pension Fund Global purchase OpenAI, which currently holds an estimated valuation of $800 billion.
- Critics argue that AI models are built on public data and infrastructure, yet current ownership structures threaten to create a permanent underclass through technological unemployment.
- OpenAI transitioned from a non-profit to a for-profit corporation, a move that critics claim expropriated collective human effort for private gain.
- Norway is proposed as a steward due to its stable democratic track record, commitment to international aid, and experience managing sovereign wealth.
- The plan envisions transferring OpenAI to an international multilateral institution to ensure democratic oversight and global benefit.

### Why it Matters
This proposal highlights the growing tension between rapid technological advancement and the current concentration of AI development within private, profit-driven corporations. It challenges policymakers to consider radical ownership models to prevent systemic inequality and ensure that the benefits of automation are shared globally.
