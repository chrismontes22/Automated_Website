The $150 Charger That Explains Why Your Laptop Is Still at 60 Percent
============================================================

Satechi has launched the ChargeView 240W Desktop Charger, a six-port USB-C device featuring a real-time LCD screen that displays individual wattage and thermal data for connected electronics.

### Key Points
- The charger features six USB-C ports with a 240W total power budget, including two 140W ports capable of charging high-performance laptops.
- An integrated LCD screen provides live wattage readings for each port and monitors internal temperatures to prevent overheating.
- The device utilizes GaN technology to maintain a compact, fanless design measuring 4.3 by 3.6 by 1.5 inches.
- It supports PD 3.2 AVS and PPS protocols to optimize charging speeds and protect long-term battery health.
- The unit retails for $149.99 and includes a two-year warranty, a precision stand, and an auto-dimming display.

### Why it Matters
This charger addresses a common consumer frustration by providing transparency regarding power distribution across multiple devices. By offering real-time data, it allows users to verify charging speeds and ensure their hardware is receiving the necessary power for efficient operation.
