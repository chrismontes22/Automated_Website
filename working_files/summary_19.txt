Director Danny Boyle Says He Used AI to Animate Photos in Latest Film ‘Ink’
============================================================

Director Danny Boyle has confirmed the use of generative AI in his latest film, Ink, to animate historical photographs and create realistic digital mice for the production.

### Key Points
- Danny Boyle disclosed that approximately 30 seconds of his film Ink utilizes generative AI technology.
- The AI was used to animate archival photographs of figures including Margaret Thatcher and the Kray brothers.
- Digital mice were generated using AI to avoid the ethical concerns associated with using live animals on set.
- The film, which chronicles Rupert Murdoch’s acquisition of The Sun, includes these disclosures within its official credits.
- Boyle emphasized that the technology should be used respectfully and must include compensation for actors if their likenesses are replaced.

### Why it Matters
This admission highlights a growing transparency trend in Hollywood regarding the integration of generative AI in high-profile feature films. By openly discussing his methodology, Boyle challenges industry norms and underscores the evolving ethical debate surrounding the use of synthetic media in professional filmmaking.
