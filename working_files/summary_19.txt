10 Monday AM Reads
============================================================

Consumer prices have surged over 30 percent since 2019, while rising Treasury yields and shifting venture capital trends signal significant ongoing adjustments across the broader United States economy.

### Key Points
- Overall consumer prices have increased by more than 30 percent since 2019, with raw steak prices doubling from $6 to $13 per pound.
- The 10-year Treasury yield has climbed to 4.943%, creating higher borrowing costs that threaten to disrupt stock market stability and economic growth.
- Venture capital firms are shifting toward capital-intensive "moonshot" investments, such as nuclear fusion, following a period of repricing in the enterprise software sector.
- Internal documents reveal that the recent failure of the Lincoln Memorial reflecting pool’s liner was caused by human oversight and chemical incompatibility rather than vandalism.
- Apple has officially entered the foldable smartphone market with the launch of the iPhone Duo, marking a significant shift in the company's hardware strategy.

### Why it Matters
These economic indicators reflect a period of sustained financial pressure that is forcing both individual consumers and large-scale investors to reevaluate their long-term strategies. Understanding these shifts is essential for navigating a landscape defined by high inflation, volatile borrowing costs, and evolving technological priorities.
