Cisco and Nvidia have announced a strategic partnership to develop high-speed optical pluggable technology capable of reaching 12.8 Tbps to support next-generation data center infrastructure requirements.

### Key Points
- Cisco and Nvidia are collaborating to advance pluggable optical modules beyond current 1.6 Tbps industry standards.
- The new USFP (Universal Small Form-factor Pluggable) technology aims to achieve data transfer speeds of 12.8 Tbps.
- This initiative addresses the growing demand for high-bandwidth connectivity in modern AI-driven data centers.
- The partnership leverages Cisco’s networking expertise alongside Nvidia’s leadership in accelerated computing hardware.

### Why it Matters
This collaboration signals a significant shift in networking hardware as data centers struggle to keep pace with the massive bandwidth requirements of modern artificial intelligence workloads. By pushing the limits of optical connectivity, the companies aim to eliminate bottlenecks that currently constrain the performance of large-scale computing clusters.
