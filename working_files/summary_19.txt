Kraken parent adds 24/7 dollar settlement with Singapore Gulf Bank
============================================================

Kraken parent company Payward has partnered with Singapore Gulf Bank to provide institutional clients in Asia and the Gulf region with 24/7 instant US dollar transaction settlement services.

### Key Points
- Payward integrated the SGB Net clearing network to facilitate real-time US dollar settlements for select institutional partners.
- Singapore Gulf Bank will utilize Kraken Prime to access digital asset liquidity and price customer trades.
- The companies intend to expand these settlement services to include additional currencies and a broader client base in the future.
- This initiative aligns with a global trend among financial institutions to modernize payment infrastructure beyond traditional banking hours.

### Why it Matters
This partnership highlights the growing integration between traditional banking infrastructure and digital asset platforms to improve global liquidity and transaction speed. By enabling 24/7 settlement, these firms are addressing long-standing inefficiencies in cross-border finance that currently rely on limited banking hours.
