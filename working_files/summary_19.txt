Two new Macs might skip M5 chips and go straight to M6
============================================================

Apple is reportedly planning to bypass the base M5 processor for upcoming Mac mini and iMac models, opting instead to transition these desktop computers directly to the M6 chip.

### Key Points
- Bloomberg reporter Mark Gurman indicates that Apple is testing new Mac mini models equipped with M6 and M5 Pro chips.
- The refreshed iMac and entry-level 14-inch MacBook Pro are expected to be among the first devices to feature the M6 processor.
- Development for the new iMac and entry-level MacBook Pro is reportedly complete, with a potential market release scheduled for this fall.
- The shift in chip strategy is linked to a reported memory supply crisis that has delayed the release of new Mac mini and Mac Studio hardware.

### Why it Matters
This strategic shift suggests Apple is adjusting its hardware roadmap to prioritize newer silicon architecture for its desktop lineup despite ongoing supply chain constraints. By skipping the base M5 generation, the company may be aiming to streamline its product performance tiers and maintain a competitive edge in the desktop market.
