California's Gig Drivers Just Secured Collective Bargaining Power with Newly Certified Union
============================================================

The California Public Employment Relations Board has officially certified the California Gig Workers Union, granting ride-hailing drivers the legal right to collectively bargain with Uber and Lyft.

### Key Points
- The California Gig Workers Union received certification after securing support from at least 30% of active drivers in the state.
- Assembly Bill 1340 provided the legal framework necessary for independent contractors to engage in collective bargaining.
- The union aims to negotiate for improved health insurance, gas price subsidies, and increased pay transparency.
- The organization was established with support from the Service Employees International Union (SEIU).
- California is now the third state to permit ride-hailing unionization, following Washington and Massachusetts.

### Why it Matters
This development marks a significant shift in the gig economy by granting independent contractors formal leverage to influence their working conditions and compensation. It sets a major precedent for how labor organizations may address the integration of autonomous vehicle technology and long-term job security in the transportation sector.
