The Asus Googlebook is reportedly going to cost ~$1,400, spelling the end for its affordable range
============================================================

A leaked retail listing for the upcoming Asus Googlebook suggests a premium price point of approximately $1,400, signaling Google's strategic shift toward high-performance, AI-capable hardware over budget devices.

### Key Points
- A PC-Canada listing identifies the Asus Googlebook model CX9406 with a price tag of roughly CA$1,900, or US$1,400.
- The hardware specifications include an Intel Core Ultra 5 Processor 325, 16GB of RAM, and a 512GB SSD.
- Google is pivoting its laptop strategy away from affordable, entry-level Chromebooks to focus on devices capable of advanced on-board AI processing.
- Industry analysts note that retail listings often use placeholder pricing, though the high-end specifications align with a premium market positioning.

### Why it Matters
This pricing strategy confirms Google's intent to compete in the premium laptop market rather than maintaining its traditional dominance in the budget-friendly education and entry-level sectors. By prioritizing high-performance hardware, the company aims to support intensive on-board AI tasks that standard, low-cost Chromebooks cannot handle.
