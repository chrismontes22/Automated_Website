Origin Energy hack traced to Accenture's Manila office
============================================================

A cyberattack on an Origin Energy call center in Manila has compromised the personal data of approximately 900,000 current and former Australian customers, triggering an ongoing police investigation.

### Key Points
- The breach involved a former employee of Accenture, which operates offshore call centers for Origin Energy in the Philippines.
- Stolen data includes customer names, addresses, email addresses, dates of birth, phone numbers, and detailed billing histories.
- The perpetrator reportedly attempted to extort the energy provider for money in exchange for the return of the sensitive information.
- The Australian Federal Police are currently working with Origin Energy to gather evidence and disrupt the associated criminal activity.
- Origin Energy has offered specialist identity and cyber support services to affected customers while urging them to remain vigilant against potential scams.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the significant security risks associated with outsourcing customer data management to third-party providers in offshore locations. It serves as a reminder of the growing threat of extortion-based cyberattacks targeting major Australian corporations and their sensitive consumer databases.
