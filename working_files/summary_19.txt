Your Amazon Prime Subscription Comes With An Alexa Upgrade You Should Use More
============================================================

Amazon Prime members now receive complimentary access to Alexa+, an advanced AI assistant that offers enhanced smart home control, task management, and conversational capabilities across compatible devices.

### Key Points
- Alexa+ is included at no extra cost for active Amazon Prime subscribers who sign in using their primary account credentials.
- The service features an upgraded AI model capable of remembering previous requests, managing smart home devices, and assisting with shopping.
- Users without a Prime membership can access the service via an Alexa+ Standard plan, which costs approximately $20 per month.
- Prime-integrated Alexa+ includes 5GB of video storage, while the standalone Standard plan offers 1TB of photo and video storage.
- Compatible hardware includes Amazon Echo devices, Fire TV, the Alexa mobile app, and the official Alexa web browser interface.

### Why it Matters
Integrating advanced AI into the Prime ecosystem increases the overall value proposition for subscribers by centralizing home automation and digital assistance. This strategy encourages deeper user engagement with Amazon’s hardware and software services while competing against other premium AI-driven platforms.
