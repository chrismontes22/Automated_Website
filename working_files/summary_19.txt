Equinix turns the network into the control plane for enterprise AI inference
============================================================

Equinix has introduced Fabric One and Inference Exchange to transform its global network into an adaptive control plane for managing distributed enterprise artificial intelligence infrastructure and inference workloads.

### Key Points
- Equinix Fabric One is an intent-driven, managed service designed to automate complex connectivity across cloud, edge, and AI environments.
- Equinix Inference Exchange provides pre-staged, Nvidia-powered AI infrastructure to help enterprises deploy models closer to their data and users.
- The new offerings were developed in collaboration with partners including Nvidia Corp., Together AI Inc., Amazon Web Services, and Google Cloud.
- Fabric One is scheduled for beta release later this year, with general availability for both services expected by 2027.
- The platform aims to address operational challenges like data sovereignty, latency, and the need for flexible, multi-model AI architectures.

### Why it Matters
These services represent a strategic shift for Equinix, positioning its neutral interconnection platform as the essential foundation for managing complex, distributed AI workflows. By simplifying the orchestration of compute and connectivity, the company aims to reduce the operational friction enterprises face when scaling AI from experimental pilots to production-ready applications.
