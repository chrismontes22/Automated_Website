Poll: Majority of Americans Distrust AI in Workplace, Cite Job Security Concerns
============================================================

Widespread public distrust regarding artificial intelligence persists as reports from the International Monetary Fund and Goldman Sachs highlight significant risks of job displacement and rising economic inequality.

### Key Points
- Goldman Sachs estimates that approximately one-quarter of all white-collar jobs, including legal and journalism roles, are at risk of replacement by AI.
- U.S. employers announced 153,074 job cuts in October 2025, with the technology sector contributing over 33,000 layoffs driven by AI adoption and cost-cutting.
- The International Monetary Fund warns that AI-driven labor disruptions could increase inequality and recommends that governments expand unemployment insurance and worker retraining programs.
- Major corporations, including Amazon and Nestlé, have already begun cutting thousands of positions as they transition toward automated and cognitive-based AI systems.
- Experts like Mike Adams suggest that AI models could potentially replace up to 50% of desk jobs, fueling concerns about algorithmic bias and data privacy.

### Why it Matters
The rapid integration of AI into the workforce is creating a significant disconnect between corporate efficiency goals and public job security. Addressing these concerns through policy, such as expanded social safety nets and workforce retraining, is essential to mitigate the potential for widespread economic instability.
