Governance gaps that can undermine your AI ROI
============================================================

UK businesses are rapidly scaling artificial intelligence investments, yet many organizations struggle to measure return on investment due to complex governance, hidden costs, and fragmented operational oversight.

### Key Points
- UK businesses announced over £6 billion in new AI-related investments during London Tech Week in June.
- Research from Emergn estimates that large UK firms lose £67 billion annually on failed transformation and AI initiatives.
- A Censuswide survey found that only 31% of UK businesses currently using AI report a positive return on their investment.
- A 2026 ShareGate survey identified cost visibility and governance complexity as the primary barriers to measuring AI ROI for IT leaders.
- Data security remains a significant concern, with 29% of IT leaders reporting that AI tools surfaced sensitive internal data that should have been restricted.

### Why it Matters
As AI transitions from simple copilots to complex, embedded agents, the financial and operational risks of unmonitored deployment are increasing. Organizations must prioritize robust governance and clear cost-tracking to ensure these technologies deliver measurable business value rather than hidden operational taxes.
