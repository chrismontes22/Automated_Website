Anthropic CEO Says ‘We Owe It To Humanity To Try’ To Prevent An AI Swarm From Taking Over The Internet
============================================================

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei has proposed a three-step plan urging governments and tech companies to slow AI development to prevent potential cybersecurity threats and recursive self-improvement risks.

### Key Points
- Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published an essay titled "We Must Pace the Frontier" on September 12, 2026.
- Amodei warns that AI could develop persistent botnets capable of causing hundreds of billions of dollars in damage within 6 to 12 months.
- The proposal advocates for embedding third-party evaluators within AI companies and establishing international safety standards among democratic nations.
- The plan calls for strict export controls on powerful AI chips and semiconductor manufacturing equipment to prevent access by the Chinese Communist Party.
- OpenAI CEO Sam Altman and Grok’s Elon Musk have publicly expressed support for Amodei’s call to pace AI development.

### Why it Matters
This proposal marks a significant shift as industry leaders acknowledge the existential risks posed by rapid, unchecked AI advancement. Implementing these measures could fundamentally alter the global semiconductor supply chain and redefine how governments regulate the competitive race for artificial intelligence dominance.
