Take Five: Six months of war
============================================================

Central bank leaders convene at the Jackson Hole symposium this week as investors seek clarity on monetary policy amid rising global inflation and volatile bond market conditions.

### Key Points
- Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh will deliver a keynote address addressing potential central bank reforms and current interest rate trajectories.
- Global energy prices remain elevated, with European diesel and U.S. gasoline costs rising significantly due to the ongoing conflict in the Middle East.
- U.S. core PCE data, expected Wednesday, is projected to remain above the Federal Reserve’s 2% target for the 65th consecutive month.
- Nvidia is scheduled to report second-quarter earnings on Wednesday, providing a critical update on the sustainability of the artificial intelligence spending boom.
- International inflation reports are expected from Australia, France, Spain, and Tokyo throughout the week.

### Why it Matters
These developments highlight the growing tension between long-term structural economic concerns and the immediate pressures of energy-driven inflation. Market participants are closely monitoring these indicators to gauge whether current equity valuations and central bank policies can withstand ongoing geopolitical instability.
