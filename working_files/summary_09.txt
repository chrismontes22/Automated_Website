macOS Tahoe 26.7 is Full of References to Unreleased Apple Products
============================================================

The macOS Tahoe 26.7 release candidate reveals codenames for several unreleased Apple products, including new home hubs, iPhone 18 models, updated MacBook Pros, and potential camera-equipped AirPods.

### Key Points
- Apple is developing two home hub variants, J490 and J491, alongside a tabletop robot codenamed J595.
- Code references indicate a new "B790" AirPods project featuring camera integration and image streaming capabilities.
- Future iPhone hardware includes the iPhone 18 series and a foldable iPhone Ultra, with expected releases starting this September.
- Upcoming Mac updates feature M6-powered 14-inch MacBook Pros and new iMac models, while the iPad mini is slated for an OLED display upgrade.
- Software strings confirm that Apple Intelligence Writing Tools are expanding to China, subject to specific content safety and regulatory restrictions.

### Why it Matters
These findings provide a roadmap for Apple’s hardware strategy, signaling a significant expansion into smart home robotics and advanced wearable technology. The inclusion of these codenames in official software suggests that several of these devices are nearing production and could be unveiled at upcoming fall events.
