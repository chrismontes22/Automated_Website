RTX 5090 Buyer Asked To Sign A Resale Loyalty Pledge: ‘NO EXPORT – NO EXCEPTIONS’
============================================================

Micro Center is requiring customers to sign loyalty pledges and provide government identification when purchasing Nvidia RTX 5090 graphics cards to prevent unauthorized resale and international export.

### Key Points
- Customers at a Tustin, California Micro Center must sign a declaration confirming the hardware is for personal or business use within the United States.
- The mandatory form requires buyers to provide a home address and a scan of a government-issued ID to track the device.
- Retailers are implementing these measures to comply with federal policies restricting the export of high-performance consumer GPUs to specific foreign markets.
- Reports indicate that some store managers are further vetting customers to ensure hardware is intended for gaming rather than commercial resale.
- The surge in demand for AI-capable hardware has significantly inflated prices and tightened supply for high-performance computer components.

### Why it Matters
These restrictive purchasing requirements highlight the growing tension between consumer hardware availability and federal efforts to control the global flow of AI-capable technology. As geopolitical export controls tighten, retailers are increasingly forced to act as gatekeepers to ensure compliance with national security interests.
