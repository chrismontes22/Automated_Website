McDonald’s AI pricing push is widening the divide with some franchisees
============================================================

McDonald's is deploying machine-learning algorithms across its global restaurant network to dynamically adjust menu prices based on local customer demand, competitor data, and individual store transaction patterns.

### Key Points
- The AI-driven pricing engine analyzes millions of daily transactions to determine optimal price points for menu items at nearly 14,000 U.S. locations.
- Internal software tracks "customer willingness to pay" and monitors public pricing data from competitors like Wendy's and Burger King.
- Franchisees report significant price variations for identical products between neighboring stores, with some locations charging up to 21% more than others.
- McDonald's updated its business standards in January to require franchisees to engage with the company's approved pricing tools and consultants.
- Internal documents show the corporation monitors and records instances where franchisees deviate from the AI-generated pricing recommendations.

### Why it Matters
This shift toward AI-managed pricing signals a broader trend of corporations using granular data to maximize profit margins at the expense of uniform consumer pricing. While the strategy aims to boost corporate earnings, it risks damaging brand loyalty and inviting regulatory scrutiny regarding potential antitrust concerns and franchisee autonomy.
