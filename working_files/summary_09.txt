ASML’s CEO is putting his paycheck on the line for employee morale. It could help retain talent that’s nearly impossible to replace
============================================================

ASML CEO Christophe Fouquet will link executive compensation to a new internal Workplace Experience Index following a significant decline in employee engagement scores across the semiconductor company.

### Key Points
- ASML’s employee engagement index dropped from 78% in 2025 to 73.5% this year.
- The new index measures six factors, including leadership trust, psychological safety, and team effectiveness.
- This policy change applies to the Board of Management and senior leadership to address morale after recent restructuring.
- ASML previously announced plans to cut 1,700 positions, leading to negotiations with four Dutch labor unions.
- The company relies on highly specialized talent, making the retention of its 44,000 employees critical to maintaining its EUV lithography market dominance.

### Why it Matters
Linking executive pay to staff sentiment serves as a strategic effort to stabilize a workforce essential for producing the world's most advanced AI-enabling hardware. By prioritizing internal culture, ASML aims to mitigate the risks of turnover and resentment that often follow large-scale corporate restructuring.
