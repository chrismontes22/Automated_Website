Inaugural Retreat Highlights: Exploring Unprecedented Futures
============================================================

The Hoover Institution’s Tech Futures Lab convened experts on June 23-24, 2026, to analyze potential technological breakthroughs and mitigate risks associated with emerging innovations in global governance and economics.

### Key Points
- The inaugural retreat gathered leaders from academia, industry, government, and nonprofits to evaluate strategic technological surprises.
- Participants conducted interconnected exercises to trace the long-term impacts of emerging technologies on geopolitics, health, and humanity.
- Discussions focused on exploring plausible future trajectories for AI and national power rather than generating specific, narrow forecasts.
- Insights from the two-day event will establish the research agenda and wargaming topics for the Tech Futures Lab throughout the coming year.

### Why it Matters
This initiative helps policymakers and industry leaders proactively address the systemic risks posed by rapid technological advancement. By mapping potential disruptions, the Lab provides a framework for navigating the complex intersection of innovation, national security, and global economic stability.
