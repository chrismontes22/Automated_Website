I Tried Breville’s $300 Slot Toaster That Watches Bread So It Doesn’t Burn
============================================================

The new $300 Breville Eye Q 850 Auto Toaster utilizes an internal optical sensor to monitor bread color and automatically stop the toasting cycle once perfection is reached.

### Key Points
- The Eye Q features an Optic Sensor in the left slot that monitors visual doneness rather than relying solely on traditional time and temperature settings.
- The unit includes a motorized lift, adjustable chimes, and dedicated modes for sourdough, bagels, English muffins, and toaster pastries.
- A built-in safety feature prevents the toaster from operating unless bread is detected inside the slot.
- The device includes a removable crumb tray and a convenient power cord storage compartment located on the bottom of the unit.
- Performance testing showed consistent results for white and sourdough breads, though darker breads required manual monitoring to prevent over-toasting.

### Why it Matters
This premium appliance represents a shift toward sensor-based automation in small kitchen electronics, aiming to eliminate the guesswork of manual toasting. While the high price point limits its appeal to luxury consumers, it offers a sophisticated alternative to standard models for those who prioritize consistent results in daily use.
