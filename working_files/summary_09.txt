Thin friction and thicker walls
============================================================

The proliferation of AI agents and automated bots is eroding the social norms and interpersonal friction that have historically supported human connection, cultural exchange, and public discourse.

### Key Points
- Automated bots and AI agents are increasingly capable of generating high volumes of content without regard for social reputation or community standards.
- The decline of traditional social friction threatens the open, permeable interactions that have long served as foundations for scientific and cultural progress.
- Digital environments are shifting toward restricted access models where users must be pre-verified or invited to participate in online spaces.
- The rise of interchangeable digital identities makes it difficult to distinguish between authentic human communication and synthetic, automated interactions.

### Why it Matters
The erosion of these social norms suggests a future where digital spaces become increasingly siloed and inaccessible to the general public. This shift could fundamentally limit the collaborative potential of the internet by replacing open discourse with gated, filtered environments.
