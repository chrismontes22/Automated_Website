'I Really Want Game Pass to Work' — After Navigating the Xbox 'Reset,' Double Fine Boss Tim Schafer Is Indie Once Again
============================================================

Double Fine Productions has returned to independent development following its departure from Microsoft, focusing on community-driven projects and experimental game jams to sustain its future operations.

### Key Points
- Studio head Tim Schafer confirmed Double Fine is operating independently after seven years under the Microsoft/Xbox banner.
- The studio launched a new Kickstarter campaign for "Amnesia Fortnight," a series of developer-run game jams, to generate funding and creative momentum.
- Double Fine established "Double Fine Action Labs" to release smaller, experimental titles like *Thank You Bus Driver*.
- Schafer expressed interest in developing *Brutal Legend 2* if the studio can secure $100 million in funding.
- The studio is prioritizing smaller, AA-scale projects to maintain creative flexibility and avoid the risks associated with massive, high-budget productions.

### Why it Matters
This transition highlights the ongoing challenges and strategic shifts facing mid-sized "AA" studios in a volatile gaming market. By returning to independent roots and community crowdfunding, Double Fine is testing a sustainable model that prioritizes creative experimentation over the high-stakes requirements of major corporate ownership.
