Bots Used to Be a Threat. Now They’re Your Customers.
============================================================

Digital marketers must shift from blocking automated traffic to optimizing websites for AI agents, as restrictive security measures now risk excluding brands from modern consumer discovery and purchasing.

### Key Points
- Bots currently account for 53% of all web traffic, necessitating a shift in how brands manage automated interactions.
- Overly restrictive security measures like CAPTCHAs and login walls can prevent AI tools like ChatGPT and Claude from indexing essential product information.
- Brands should prioritize structured data for pricing, inventory, and shipping to ensure AI agents can accurately interpret and present their offerings.
- Traditional web analytics may no longer reflect true organic discovery, as consumer interactions increasingly occur within AI-driven search interfaces rather than on brand websites.
- Companies must re-engineer site architectures to treat "good bots" as a primary audience to avoid being bypassed by marketplaces and resellers.

### Why it Matters
As AI agents increasingly act on behalf of consumers to research and purchase products, websites that block these tools risk losing visibility and sales. Brands that fail to adapt their digital infrastructure may find themselves excluded from the consideration set, effectively ceding their market presence to competitors with more accessible data.
