Weekly poll results: smart glasses are a solution looking for a problem
============================================================

A recent reader poll reveals that less than 5% of consumers actively use smart glasses, highlighting significant challenges for tech companies attempting to drive mainstream adoption of wearables.

### Key Points
- Fewer than 5% of survey respondents report using smart glasses on a regular basis.
- Consumers cite a lack of compelling use cases as the primary barrier to purchasing these devices.
- Privacy concerns regarding integrated cameras remain a major deterrent, with many users labeling the technology as intrusive.
- Regional availability issues continue to limit the accessibility of various smart eyewear products currently on the market.
- Public perception remains negative, with some potential users actively avoiding those who wear smart glasses in public.

### Why it Matters
The widespread consumer skepticism suggests that tech companies face a difficult path toward normalizing head-mounted displays. Until manufacturers can address privacy concerns and provide clear utility, these devices will likely remain a niche product rather than a mass-market success.
