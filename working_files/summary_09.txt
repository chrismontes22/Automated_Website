Iran-Linked Handala Hack Tied to HEAVYGRAM Telegram Backdoor That Can Steal Passwords
============================================================

The Iran-linked threat actor Void Manticore is utilizing the HEAVYGRAM backdoor and CRUDEEXCLUDE utility to conduct surveillance and data exfiltration against journalists and political dissidents via Telegram.

### Key Points
- Void Manticore, an actor affiliated with Iran's Ministry of Intelligence and Security, operates the Handala Hack persona to target opposition groups and journalists.
- The HEAVYGRAM malware uses Telegram as a command-and-control channel to execute system commands, capture screenshots, and exfiltrate sensitive browser and messaging data.
- CRUDEEXCLUDE is a Delphi-based utility that masks malicious activity by configuring Microsoft Defender exclusions and preparing environments for secondary malware deployment.
- Attackers employ social engineering on platforms like WhatsApp and Instagram, masquerading malicious installers as legitimate software such as KeePass or Pictory.
- The U.K. National Cyber Security Center tracks this flexible malware family under the name CHOSEN BRICK, noting its capability for diverse operational outcomes.

### Why it Matters
These findings highlight a sophisticated, multi-stage infection chain that leverages encrypted messaging platforms to bypass traditional security monitoring and maintain persistent access. The reliance on legitimate-looking applications and automated defense evasion poses a significant risk to high-profile targets and organizations vulnerable to state-sponsored espionage.
