Samsung may finally fix the Galaxy S Ultra’s biggest weakness
============================================================

Samsung is reportedly planning to upgrade its flagship Galaxy S27 Ultra and S27 Pro smartphones with larger battery capacities starting in 2027 to improve overall device runtime.

### Key Points
- The Galaxy S27 Ultra is expected to feature a 5,534mAh rated battery, likely marketed as 5,700mAh or 5,800mAh.
- Samsung will reportedly introduce a new Galaxy S27 Pro model equipped with a 5,087mAh rated battery.
- The company is expected to utilize silicon-carbon anode technology to increase capacity without significantly increasing device thickness or weight.
- These upgrades mark a departure from the 5,000mAh standard Samsung has maintained for its flagship Ultra models since 2020.

### Why it Matters
This shift addresses long-standing consumer demand for better battery life in Samsung's premium smartphone lineup. By adopting advanced battery materials, the company aims to remain competitive against rival Android devices that have already begun integrating higher-capacity power cells.
