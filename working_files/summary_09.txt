A Conversation With Ari Emanuel About Entertainment and AI
============================================================

Hollywood super-agent Ari Emanuel discusses the future of entertainment, the impact of AI on creative industries, and his strategic focus on live events in a wide-ranging conversation.

### Key Points
- Ari Emanuel serves as the Executive Chairman of WME and CEO of TKO Group Holdings, which owns the UFC and WWE.
- The discussion highlights the "MARI" initiative, a live events and experiences company focused on scaling properties like Frieze and Barrett-Jackson.
- Emanuel argues that AI will act as a creative unlock for high-end talent while emphasizing that human connection remains essential.
- The conversation explores the "two-towers" advertising model and the potential for AI to disrupt traditional search-based ad revenue.
- Emanuel advocates for direct communication and "rolling the calls" as a method to cut through ambiguity and force resolution in business.

### Why it Matters
This dialogue underscores a shift in the media landscape where live, human-centric experiences are becoming increasingly valuable as digital content becomes commoditized by AI. By betting on live sports and events, Emanuel is positioning his companies to capture premium demand in an economy where personalized, real-world engagement is becoming a scarce and highly monetized commodity.
