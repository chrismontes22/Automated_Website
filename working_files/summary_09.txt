Brave browser adds email aliases to help users evade tracking
============================================================

The Brave browser has launched a new Email Aliases feature in version 1.94, allowing users to generate disposable addresses to protect their primary identity from data breaches and spam.

### Key Points
- Brave version 1.94 introduces disposable email aliases to prevent cross-site identity tracking and reduce exposure to phishing attacks.
- Users must register a free Brave Account to enable message forwarding, which is currently limited to five aliases per user.
- The system utilizes the OPAQUE protocol to authenticate users without transmitting passwords or hashes to Brave’s servers.
- Forwarded emails undergo automated spam and malware filtering before being deleted from Brave’s servers within seconds of delivery.
- Brave plans to launch a paid Premium version in the future to remove the current five-alias restriction.

### Why it Matters
This feature addresses a significant privacy gap by preventing websites from linking user identities through email addresses stored in databases. By masking primary contact information, users can better mitigate the long-term risks associated with corporate data breaches and targeted phishing campaigns.
