The weird and wonderful headphones of CanJam 2026
============================================================

CanJam SoCal 2026 showcased innovative, high-end audio gear from boutique manufacturers, highlighting unique engineering feats like multi-driver in-ear monitors and vacuum tube-integrated headphones for dedicated audiophile enthusiasts.

### Key Points
- CanJam SoCal 2026 took place on August 29 at the Irvine Marriott, featuring exhibitors like Sennheiser, Focal, HiFiMan, and Noble Audio.
- The Campfire Audio Chimera in-ear monitors debuted with a complex nine-driver "quad-brid" design and a premium $7,500 price tag.
- The Écoute TH2 headphones gained attention for incorporating a built-in vacuum tube preamp and dual-mono amplification into a portable form factor.
- Accessible options were also present, including Fosi Audio’s sub-$150 wired earbuds and the $900 Dan Clark Audio Aeon Core planar magnetic headphones.
- Exhibitors utilized high-resolution streaming services like Qobuz to demonstrate the capabilities of specialized DACs, amplifiers, and cables.

### Why it Matters
These niche audio shows provide a vital platform for experimental hardware that mainstream consumer electronics brands often overlook. By fostering innovation in driver technology and materials, events like CanJam help push the boundaries of high-fidelity sound for the broader audio market.
