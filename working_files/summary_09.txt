TourBox NEO review: Customisable and comprehensive without breaking the bank
============================================================

The TourBox NEO editing console offers photographers and videographers extensive customization and workflow efficiency for $169, providing a wired, high-performance alternative to more expensive wireless editing hardware.

### Key Points
- The TourBox NEO features over 150 button combinations and 400 assignable functions compatible with software like Photoshop, Premiere, and DaVinci Resolve.
- Priced at $169, the device utilizes a wired-only connection and lacks both Bluetooth connectivity and haptic feedback found in premium models.
- The console is constructed with a tactile, rubberized finish and includes a variety of physical dials, knobs, and wheels for precise control.
- It is designed exclusively for laptop and desktop workstations and is not compatible with tablets.
- The unit competes directly with the Logitech MX Creative Console and XPPen Pilot Pro, offering superior functional depth at a similar price point.

### Why it Matters
The TourBox NEO provides a cost-effective solution for creative professionals seeking to optimize their editing speed without paying for premium wireless features. By prioritizing extensive control mapping over connectivity, it serves as a powerful tool for stationary workstation users looking to streamline complex software workflows.
