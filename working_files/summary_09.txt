The best cards in my Google Wallet don't even support Google Wallet
============================================================

Google Wallet’s "Everything else" feature allows users to digitize physical cards and documents by creating custom passes, effectively reducing the need to carry a traditional leather wallet.

### Key Points
- Users can create custom passes by scanning photos, documents, or screenshots of items not natively supported by Google Wallet.
- The feature automatically extracts information from barcodes and QR codes, making them immediately scannable within the app.
- Custom passes are secured behind the user's phone passcode and biometric authentication, providing a private way to store sensitive data.
- The tool is ideal for digitizing insurance cards, loyalty programs, and membership IDs that lack official digital integration.
- Dynamic QR codes and NFC-based cards are not compatible with the custom pass creator, as they require specific hardware or real-time verification.

### Why it Matters
This feature bridges the gap between official digital wallet support and the reality of everyday items that lack native integration. By allowing users to centralize their personal documents securely, Google is significantly increasing the utility and adoption of digital wallets for daily tasks.
