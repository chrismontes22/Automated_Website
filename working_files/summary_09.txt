Android 17 QPR2 Beta 3 is pretty neat, especially if you hate fraud and scams
============================================================

Google has released Android 17 QPR2 Beta 3 for eligible Pixel devices, introducing new security measures against call-forwarding fraud alongside interface customizations and critical system stability bug fixes.

### Key Points
- The update introduces OS-level confirmation dialogs for manual call-forwarding codes to prevent social engineering scams.
- New Quick Settings layout options allow users to rearrange media cards, brightness bars, and tiles for improved personalization.
- Foldable devices receive a new app handle feature to simplify switching between full-screen and split-screen viewing modes.
- Google resolved bugs causing visual corruption in the notification shade and false battery capacity degradation warnings.
- The Pixel 6 and 6 Pro are officially excluded from the beta program as they have reached their end-of-life support cycle.
- Eligible devices include the Pixel 6a, 7 series, Pixel Fold, and Pixel Tablet, with the final release expected in the December 2026 Feature Drop.

### Why it Matters
These updates demonstrate Google's ongoing commitment to mitigating mobile security threats while refining the user experience through interface flexibility. By addressing both high-level fraud prevention and device stability, the company ensures its ecosystem remains competitive and secure for long-term users.
