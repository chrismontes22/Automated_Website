Android 17 could soon fix your phone's Wi-Fi and mobile data problems for you
============================================================

Google is expanding its Pixel diagnostic suite in the upcoming Android 17 update to include automated troubleshooting tools for mobile data, Wi-Fi, and cellular calling connectivity issues.

### Key Points
- New diagnostic tools were discovered in the Android 17 QPR3 Beta 1 and the latest Google Play Services beta.
- The features are located within the Device Health & Support section of the Pixel settings menu.
- Users can utilize these tools to identify network errors and receive automated fixes or guided troubleshooting steps.
- These additions aim to resolve common connectivity problems, such as dropped calls and intermittent mobile data, without manual configuration.
- A stable release for these diagnostic features is expected to arrive for Pixel devices early next year.

### Why it Matters
These tools address long-standing user complaints regarding Pixel connectivity by reducing the need for complex manual network troubleshooting. By automating the diagnostic process, Google aims to improve device reliability and decrease the burden on customer support services.
