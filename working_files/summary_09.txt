Water Utilities Group Partners With DEF CON Offshoot For Water Watch Center
============================================================

The National Rural Water Association and DEF CON Franklin have launched the Water Watch Center to provide essential cybersecurity services to small U.S. water utilities facing rising threats.

### Key Points
- The Water Watch Center supports community water systems serving fewer than 10,000 people, which account for 91% of all U.S. water utilities.
- Five cybersecurity firms—Rapid7, Defendify, Legato Security, L1 Secure, and Sentinel Technologies—will provide managed detection and response services.
- The initiative aims to protect critical infrastructure from state-sponsored cyber adversaries, including groups linked to the Iranian Red Guard and the Chinese military.
- The National Rural Water Association will serve as a central hub for sharing threat intelligence and vulnerability patch data among participating utilities.

### Why it Matters
This partnership addresses a critical security gap for thousands of small water systems that often lack the resources to defend against sophisticated nation-state cyberattacks. By centralizing threat intelligence, the program provides a scalable model to protect essential public infrastructure from potential service disruptions.
