The Aosu R1 Ultra is a great subscription-free video, albeit with some minor flaws
============================================================

The Aosu R1 Ultra video doorbell offers a subscription-free security solution by utilizing an included base station for 60 days of local loop recording and advanced AI motion detection.

### Key Points
- The AosuBase Mini provides 8GB of local storage, eliminating the need for mandatory monthly cloud subscription fees.
- The device features a 5MP sensor with radar and AI human recognition to minimize false alerts and improve detection accuracy.
- Installation supports both battery-powered and hardwired configurations, though the battery requires removing the entire unit for a six-hour recharge.
- Night vision is limited to infrared monochrome, lacking the color low-light modes or integrated spotlights found on some competitors.
- The doorbell includes unique privacy features like voice-disguise options and anti-tamper alerts that trigger a chime if the unit is removed.

### Why it Matters
This device challenges the industry-standard model of locking essential security features behind recurring monthly subscription fees. By prioritizing local storage and robust hardware, it provides a cost-effective alternative for homeowners seeking privacy and long-term value without ongoing service costs.
