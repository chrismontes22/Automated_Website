iPhone 20 Pro and Pro Max Might Get Bigger Screens in 2027
============================================================

Apple is reportedly planning to increase the display sizes of its iPhone 20 Pro and Pro Max models to 6.4 and 7 inches for a 2027 release.

### Key Points
- The iPhone 20 Pro and Pro Max are expected to launch in September 2027, marking the 20th anniversary of the original iPhone.
- Leaks from Digital Chat Station suggest the devices may feature a quad-curved, bezel-less design with glass wrapping around all four edges.
- Potential hardware upgrades include solid-state buttons, under-display Face ID sensors, and a more efficient 2nm processor.
- The new models aim to improve display technology with thinner, brighter OLED panels and advanced silicon-anode battery components.

### Why it Matters
Increasing screen real estate and removing bezels reflects the growing consumer demand for immersive mobile content consumption. These design shifts could establish a new aesthetic standard for Apple’s hardware, potentially defining the company's design language for the next decade.
