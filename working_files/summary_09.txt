AI music maker Suno now generates spoken words
============================================================

AI music platform Suno has launched a public beta for its new Speech feature, allowing users to generate synthetic voiceovers alongside custom background music tracks on its platform.

### Key Points
- The Speech feature is now available on Suno’s web and mobile platforms for creating voiceovers up to eight minutes long.
- Users can choose between a Simple mode for prompted descriptions or an Advanced mode for custom scripts with adjustable voice settings.
- The tool allows for the simultaneous generation of spoken word and background music, though the music can be toggled off for clean audio.
- Suno’s expansion into text-to-speech competes with established providers like ElevenLabs and Adobe.
- The company acknowledges the feature is in early beta and expects performance inconsistencies as it gathers user feedback.

### Why it Matters
This expansion allows Suno to diversify its generative audio offerings beyond music, potentially mitigating risks associated with its primary music-focused business model. By integrating voice and music into a single workflow, the company aims to capture a broader market of content creators seeking cohesive, all-in-one audio production tools.
