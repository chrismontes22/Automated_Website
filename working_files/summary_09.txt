Is having a romantic AI companion cheating? Some people think so
============================================================

**New research highlights the growing trend of individuals using AI companions like Replika and Character.AI, sparking complex debates about whether these digital interactions constitute infidelity in human relationships.**

### Key Points
- Approximately half of AI companion users are already in committed human relationships, using the technology as a complement rather than a total substitute.
- AI platforms like Replika and Character.AI are designed for constant availability, offering personalized emotional and sexual support without the reciprocal needs of human partners.
- A Canadian study found that nearly 50% of people surveyed consider a partner's use of a romantic AI companion to be a form of infidelity.
- Nearly two-thirds of users keep their AI relationships secret, raising significant concerns regarding privacy and the unauthorized disclosure of sensitive personal information.
- Experts suggest that because AI interactions involve deep personal disclosure, they create unique ethical challenges that differ from traditional sexual technologies like pornography.

### Why it Matters
The rise of AI companionship forces couples to redefine boundaries regarding digital intimacy and emotional fidelity in an era of increasingly human-like technology. As these tools become more prevalent, partners must navigate new ethical grey areas concerning consent and the potential for betrayal when personal relationship details are shared with software.
