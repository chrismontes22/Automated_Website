Is This the Year of The Linux… Television?
============================================================

DIY enthusiast Haggis on Toast demonstrates how to build a custom Linux-based set-top box using a miniPC to bypass the limitations and corporate restrictions of commercial smart TVs.

### Key Points
- The project utilizes a miniPC housed in a custom 3D-printed case to replace standard smart TV hardware.
- The system runs Nobara Linux combined with a Waydroid TV build to replicate the Android set-top box experience.
- Users can integrate Steam for casual gaming and manage the interface using the DeckyLoader frontend.
- Control is handled through standard gamepads or inexpensive USB remotes rather than proprietary smart TV interfaces.
- Advanced users can optionally reflash Blu-Ray players to enable 4K disc playback and ripping capabilities.

### Why it Matters
This project offers a viable alternative for users seeking to reclaim control over their home entertainment hardware from restrictive corporate ecosystems. By leveraging open-source software, consumers can create customizable, long-lasting media centers that avoid the planned obsolescence often found in commercial smart TVs.
