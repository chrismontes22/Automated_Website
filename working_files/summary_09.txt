Pink-Slipped Exec Chris Aronson Sues Paramount For Millions Over “Broken Promise”
============================================================

**Former Paramount executive Chris Aronson has filed a lawsuit against the studio alleging age discrimination and breach of contract following his termination after the Skydance media merger.**

### Key Points
- Chris Aronson, the former president of Domestic Theatrical Distribution, is seeking over $4 million in damages after being terminated in October 2025.
- The lawsuit alleges Paramount attempted to shorten Aronson’s contract to reduce compensation by $1.3 million and cut his family’s healthcare coverage.
- Aronson claims the studio engaged in age-based targeting, citing data that employees aged 55 and older were disproportionately affected by recent layoffs.
- Paramount replaced Aronson with Shaun Barber, a former Lionsgate executive, in November 2025.
- The complaint, filed in Los Angeles Superior Court, includes four legal claims and requests a jury trial to address the alleged discriminatory practices.

### Why it Matters
This litigation highlights the internal friction and legal challenges facing Paramount as it undergoes a major corporate restructuring under new owner David Ellison. The case could set a precedent for how the studio manages executive transitions and potential age discrimination claims during high-profile industry mergers.
