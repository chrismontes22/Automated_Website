Live updates: Bitcoin slips under $86,000 as money rotates into BCH and ZEC
============================================================

Bitcoin Cash surged 28% to reach $349 following an announcement from CME Group that it will launch regulated futures contracts for the token starting on October 19.

### Key Points
- Bitcoin Cash experienced the largest daily gain among major tokens after CME Group confirmed the upcoming listing of its futures contracts.
- The new futures products allow institutional investors to gain exposure to Bitcoin Cash and Uniswap without the need for direct asset custody.
- Bitcoin traded slightly lower at $85,800, while other altcoins like ZEC and XRP saw gains of 9% and 3%, respectively.
- Analysts suggest that speculative capital is currently shifting from Bitcoin into altcoins as investors seek higher profit opportunities in the broader market.
- Bitcoin Cash remains well below its January peak of $660, with technical analysts identifying $450 as the next potential resistance level for the asset.

### Why it Matters
The introduction of regulated futures on the CME Group exchange provides a critical hedging venue that typically improves market liquidity and tightens spot pricing. This development enables institutional participation by bypassing traditional custody barriers, potentially signaling a broader shift in capital allocation across the cryptocurrency sector.
