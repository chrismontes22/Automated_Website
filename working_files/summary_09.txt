Character.AI files for bankruptcy
============================================================

Generative AI startup Character.AI has filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection and initiated a sale process, with Disney emerging as the lead bidder for its assets and talent.

### Key Points
- Character.AI listed estimated liabilities between $50 million and $100 million in its Chapter 11 bankruptcy filing.
- Disney has offered $20 million for specific assets and a nonexclusive license to the company's intellectual property.
- The deal includes an "acquihire" strategy to bring former Character.AI technical staff and former CEO Karandeep Anand to Disney.
- Character.AI plans to continue operating its services and paid subscriptions while seeking a final sale by late November or early December.
- The company previously faced significant legal and regulatory scrutiny regarding chatbot safety and unauthorized use of intellectual property.

### Why it Matters
This acquisition highlights a growing trend of major corporations absorbing struggling AI startups to secure specialized technical talent and proprietary technology. For Disney, the move strengthens its internal AI capabilities while resolving long-standing disputes over the unauthorized use of its intellectual property on the platform.
