Google Photos fixes a stupidly annoying step for AI edits
============================================================

Google Photos has introduced a streamlined camera workflow that allows users to capture images and immediately access generative AI editing tools without navigating through their device gallery.

### Key Points
- The update removes the need to switch between a phone's native camera app and the Google Photos gallery.
- Users can now transition directly from taking a photo to using AI features like Remix and Photo to video.
- This change reduces the multi-step process previously required to edit fresh snapshots within the application.
- Google Photos has evolved into an AI-first platform featuring tools such as Magic Editor, Reimagine, Auto Frame, and Ask Photos.

### Why it Matters
This update reduces user friction by integrating capture and editing into a single, cohesive workflow. By prioritizing speed, Google is encouraging more frequent use of its generative AI suite for casual photography and social media sharing.
