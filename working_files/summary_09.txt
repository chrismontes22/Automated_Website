These AI Barons Are Ready to Give Away Their Fortunes
============================================================

**Ineffable Intelligence founder David Silver has pledged to donate all proceeds from his company’s future sale to charity, joining a growing movement of AI entrepreneurs committing to philanthropy.**

### Key Points
- David Silver, a former Google DeepMind researcher and AlphaGo developer, founded Ineffable Intelligence with a record-breaking $1.1 billion seed round.
- Silver formalized his commitment through a binding contract with Founders Pledge, a nonprofit that has seen its total pledge value reach $4 billion this year.
- The AI industry currently accounts for one-third of all lifetime pledges recorded by Founders Pledge.
- Silver intends to prioritize near-term humanitarian causes, such as the Malaria Foundation, rather than focusing on long-term speculative goals.
- Other prominent AI figures, including Mustafa Suleyman and Anton Osika, have also pledged significant portions of their equity to charitable causes.

### Why it Matters
This trend highlights a shift in Silicon Valley where AI founders are increasingly using personal wealth pledges to address global issues while navigating the ethical complexities of extreme capital concentration. Critics argue that such philanthropy may allow donors to bypass systemic economic reforms or justify reckless industry acceleration, raising questions about the accountability of unelected megadonors.
