Android 17 QPR2 Beta 6 is rolling out: unexpected reboots and kernel crash fixes lead the way
============================================================

Google has released Android 17 QPR2 Beta 6 for Pixel devices, delivering critical stability fixes for camera-related reboots and system kernel crashes ahead of the final software launch.

### Key Points
- The update, build vCP41.260831.007, is available for Pixel models ranging from the 6a through the 10 series.
- A primary fix addresses unexpected reboots on the Pixel 9 Pro Fold triggered by third-party camera viewfinder initialization.
- Developers resolved a system kernel crash occurring when apps requested simultaneous camera and microphone access during biometric verification.
- The release includes patches for visual regressions in the status bar, screensaver blanking issues, and gesture navigation failures in the Recents menu.
- Google temporarily removed the lock screen blur effect to address underlying software bugs identified during the beta testing phase.

### Why it Matters
This update marks a significant step toward the final release of Android 17 QPR2, ensuring core system stability for a wide range of Pixel hardware. By addressing critical kernel and camera-related crashes, Google is refining the user experience for beta testers before the software reaches the general public.
