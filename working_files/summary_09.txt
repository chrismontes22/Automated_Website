The 4 Elements of Exposure Prioritization and How to Operationalize Them
============================================================

Gartner reports that organizations can reduce breach likelihood by 70% by shifting from CVSS-based vulnerability prioritization to a risk-based framework incorporating threat intelligence, asset context, and control validation.

### Key Points
- Gartner research indicates that fewer than 10% of all identified vulnerabilities are actually exploited by attackers.
- Effective exposure management requires integrating threat intelligence, asset context, exploitability modeling, and security control validation.
- Relying solely on CVSS severity scores often leads to remediation paralysis and the inefficient allocation of security resources.
- The Picus Platform automates this four-pillar framework to help security teams identify and prioritize only the most critical, exploitable risks.
- Organizations using evidence-based validation report up to a 92% reduction in missed remediation deadlines for high-risk vulnerabilities.

### Why it Matters
Transitioning to a risk-based prioritization model allows security teams to move away from reactive, volume-based patching toward a proactive strategy that addresses actual business impact. By focusing resources on validated threats rather than hypothetical risks, organizations can significantly decrease their attack surface while reducing operational fatigue.
