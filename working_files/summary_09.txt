Digital sovereignty has become Europe’s top priority — here's how open source is helping pave the way
============================================================

The European Commission is prioritizing open source software as a strategic tool to achieve digital sovereignty and secure critical supply chains against global technological dependence.

### Key Points
- The EU aims to reduce reliance on non-EU digital infrastructure, which currently accounts for over 80% of the bloc's technology products and services.
- A new EU Open Source Strategy requires approximately €2 billion in public and private investment over the next seven years to support development and procurement.
- The initiative introduces "Sovereignty Effectiveness Assurance Levels" (SEAL) to categorize technology based on the degree of European control and independence from foreign jurisdictions.
- Experts advise businesses to move beyond passive software consumption by actively contributing to upstream projects to reduce technical debt and improve security.
- The strategy aligns with broader regulatory frameworks, including the Cyber Resilience Act, to address vulnerabilities in the software supply chain.

### Why it Matters
This shift forces European organizations to rethink their procurement strategies by prioritizing transparency and active maintenance of open-source dependencies. By fostering a more self-reliant digital ecosystem, the EU hopes to mitigate the risks of being sidelined in the ongoing global technology race between the United States and China.
