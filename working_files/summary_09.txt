Fuck it, make it anyway
============================================================

Software engineers and creative professionals are grappling with a crisis of purpose as generative AI tools increasingly automate tasks and diminish the personal fulfillment found in traditional craftsmanship.

### Key Points
- Software developers report significant professional anxiety regarding the rapid integration of Large Language Models into coding workflows.
- The author highlights a loss of personal satisfaction and pride when using AI assistants to generate code or creative tools.
- Developer Shad is currently building Uncamera, an iOS photography app that avoids generative AI to prioritize manual design and authentic film-like output.
- The author reaffirms a commitment to manual development, such as building a custom game engine in C++, to preserve the joy of learning and creation.

### Why it Matters
This shift reflects a broader existential debate within the tech industry regarding whether efficiency or the human creative process should define professional success. As AI tools become standard, creators must decide if they will prioritize market competitiveness or the intrinsic value of their craft.
