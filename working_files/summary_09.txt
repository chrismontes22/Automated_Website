Photo and Video Editing in ChatGPT Just Got an Adobe-Size Update
============================================================

Adobe has integrated over 70 creative and productivity tools into ChatGPT, allowing users to edit photos, resize content, and generate PDFs directly through the chatbot interface.

### Key Points
- Users can access Adobe’s suite by typing "@Adobe" in ChatGPT to perform batch edits, resize images, and utilize Adobe Stock photography.
- The update introduces interactive features like customizable Express templates and color-picking tools for more precise text-based editing.
- Cross-functional workflows now allow users to convert spreadsheet data into professional PDF documents using Adobe Acrobat integration.
- Access is available to both free and paid Adobe account holders, with usage limits determined by the user's specific ChatGPT subscription plan.
- Adobe recommends that professional users adjust data control settings within ChatGPT to prevent their project content from being used for AI model training.

### Why it Matters
This integration streamlines creative workflows by allowing users to perform professional design tasks without switching between multiple software applications. It signals a broader industry shift toward embedding specialized enterprise tools directly into conversational AI platforms to increase user productivity.
