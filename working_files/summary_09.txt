Trump Made Big Stock Trades In SpaceX, Palantir And Tech Companies In June
============================================================

President Donald Trump executed over 1,000 stock trades in June, including significant transactions in major tech, defense, and financial companies, according to a recent Office of Government Ethics filing.

### Key Points
- The Office of Government Ethics reported over 1,000 individual stock purchases and sales by President Trump during the month of June.
- Major transactions included a $5 million to $25 million sale of a Vanguard ETF and purchases of Berkshire Hathaway, Visa, and Mastercard.
- Trump acquired SpaceX shares valued between $15,001 and $50,000 on June 23, roughly 11 days after the company's initial public offering.
- Trading activity involved frequent buying and selling of tech firms like Meta, Apple, Nvidia, and the defense contractor Palantir.
- The White House maintains that these trades are executed via automated, computer-based model portfolios rather than direct personal intervention.

### Why it Matters
The high volume of trading raises ongoing questions regarding potential conflicts of interest between the president's personal financial portfolio and his official government duties. Lawmakers continue to scrutinize the transparency of these investments, particularly given the significant influence federal policy can exert on the companies involved.
