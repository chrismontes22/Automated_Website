Poland lost $230m in cryptocurrency trying to buy Venezuelan oil in 2023 — USB drives with crypto handed directly to scammers
============================================================

Poland’s state-backed energy group Orlen lost approximately $424 million in a failed 2023 attempt to purchase Venezuelan oil using USDT cryptocurrency amid complex international sanctions and fraud.

### Key Points
- Orlen enlisted the firm Hannon to facilitate a $600 million oil purchase from Venezuela, utilizing USDT stablecoins to bypass U.S.-led sanctions.
- Hannon lost $190 million in USDT to fraudulent brokers posing as representatives of the Venezuelan state oil company, PDVSA.
- Three Polish oil tankers incurred $72 million in demurrage and port fees while waiting for oil deliveries that largely never materialized.
- The total financial loss, including shipping costs and failed transactions, reached $424 million according to a Polish government investigation.
- Investigators found that the failure stemmed from a lack of identity verification (KYC) for the crypto wallets rather than inherent flaws in blockchain technology.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the extreme risks and regulatory hurdles businesses face when attempting to use cryptocurrency to circumvent international sanctions. It serves as a cautionary tale regarding the necessity of rigorous identity verification and legal oversight when conducting high-value transactions in volatile, unregulated markets.
