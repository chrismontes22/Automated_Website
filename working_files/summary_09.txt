The Weakest Leg
============================================================

Life insurance provider TruStage continues to face significant operational disruptions following a July 2026 cybersecurity incident that has left major business functions only partially available for months.

### Key Points
- TruStage reported a cybersecurity incident on July 10, 2026, which continues to impact core business operations and payment processing capabilities as of September 11.
- The outage has caused some life insurance policies to lapse due to the company's inability to process premium payments, triggering financial chargebacks for downstream partners.
- Credit union services remain operational as they utilize separate systems, preventing a total collapse of the company's business functions.
- The incident has sparked multiple lawsuits and public friction between industry partners, including a notable confrontation involving Family First Life President Shawn Meaike and Ethos representatives.
- TruStage is currently migrating services to a new, secure environment, though the company has not yet confirmed the extent of potential data compromises.

### Why it Matters
The prolonged outage highlights the fragility of complex, multi-layered insurance distribution networks where a single failure at the carrier level causes cascading financial and operational damage. This event underscores the critical need for robust disaster recovery protocols in the insurance sector to protect policyholders from losing coverage due to administrative system failures.
