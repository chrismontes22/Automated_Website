How BOOX Rewrote Books
============================================================

Onyx International, the Guangzhou-based maker of BOOX electronic-paper devices, has filed for a Hong Kong IPO as it seeks to expand its global footprint in productivity hardware.

### Key Points
- Onyx International, founded in 2008, specializes in E Ink-based tablets, monitors, and e-readers that run on a customized version of Android.
- The company’s BOOX OS supports over 1.2 million monthly active users, positioning the brand as a "knowledge-focused productivity" tool rather than a traditional e-reader.
- International markets accounted for over 54% of the company's revenue in the first half of 2026, with significant sales in the United States and Europe.
- Financial filings show 2025 revenue reached RMB 1.13 billion, though net profit declined by 24% in the first half of 2026 amid rising inventory and shifting product demand.
- The company plans to use IPO proceeds to fund research and development, including AI-driven features like document summarization and handwriting recognition.

### Why it Matters
The IPO highlights the challenges of scaling a niche hardware business that relies on rapid product innovation to maintain growth in a fragmented market. By transitioning from simple e-readers to multifunctional productivity tools, Onyx aims to prove that its software-centric ecosystem can provide long-term value beyond one-time hardware sales.
