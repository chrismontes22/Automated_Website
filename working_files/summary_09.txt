Crypto giant Binance attempting to recover $6.3bn stolen in hack linked to North Korea
============================================================

Binance has frozen $3 billion of the $6.3 billion stolen in a massive cyberattack, as the exchange works with federal authorities to investigate the suspected North Korean heist.

### Key Points
- Binance confirmed a $6.3 billion theft, with $3 billion in digital assets successfully frozen by the exchange.
- The breach involved a sophisticated attack that bypassed initial security measures, utilizing self-destructing tools to evade detection.
- Analytics firms, including Chainalysis and TRM Labs, have linked the incident to North Korean hacking units.
- The US Secret Service and FBI are collaborating with Binance to trace stolen funds and identify the perpetrators.
- Binance has pledged to cover all user losses, setting aside over $6.34 billion in company funds to maintain liquidity.
- Cryptocurrency markets reacted negatively to the news, with Bitcoin prices falling 5% and shares of Coinbase and Robinhood declining.

### Why it Matters
This record-breaking heist highlights the persistent vulnerability of major cryptocurrency exchanges to state-sponsored cyber warfare. The incident has triggered widespread market volatility and renewed concerns regarding the long-term stability of centralized crypto platforms and their ability to guarantee user assets during systemic crises.
