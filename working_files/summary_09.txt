This CD Player Doesn’t Hide Its Mechanism, It Puts It on Display
============================================================

The ClearFrame Speaker CD Player combines nostalgic physical media playback with modern design, allowing users to display album art while listening through a built-in speaker or Bluetooth connectivity.

### Key Points
- The device features a transparent polycarbonate body and a slot to display physical album artwork as a rotating piece of home decor.
- It includes a built-in 40mm speaker, a 3.5mm headphone jack, and Bluetooth 5.3 support for versatile audio output.
- The unit weighs 454 grams and is powered by an 1,800mAh battery, providing up to eight hours of playback per charge via USB-C.
- Integrated hardware includes a retro-style LED Nixie tube display and a 2MB SDRAM anti-shock buffer to prevent playback skipping.
- Optional accessories include a wall mount, book case, and display stand to accommodate various home interior configurations.

### Why it Matters
This device addresses the growing demand for physical media among collectors by transforming outdated CD collections into functional, aesthetic home decor. By integrating modern wireless features into a portable design, it bridges the gap between vintage music formats and contemporary lifestyle preferences.
