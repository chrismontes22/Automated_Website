Not a test: Amazon will give you a FREE Pixel Watch 5 when you preorder the Google Pixel 11 Pro Fold
============================================================

Google is offering a free Pixel Watch 5 to customers who preorder the new Pixel 11 Pro Fold foldable smartphone through Amazon before its August 20 release date.

### Key Points
- Amazon is bundling a free 41mm Wi-Fi Pixel Watch 5, valued at $400, with preorders of the $1,899.99 Pixel 11 Pro Fold.
- The Pixel 11 Pro Fold features a Tensor G6 processor, 16GB of RAM, and dual Super Actua OLED displays with an IP68 durability rating.
- The included Pixel Watch 5 offers 64GB of storage, a Snapdragon W5 Gen 2 chip, and up to 48 hours of battery life.
- The promotion is available for unlocked versions of the foldable device ahead of the official August 20, 2026, launch.

### Why it Matters
This promotion provides significant added value for consumers planning to purchase Google's latest flagship foldable hardware at launch. By bundling high-end wearables with premium smartphones, Google aims to incentivize early adoption and increase the integration of its ecosystem among power users.
