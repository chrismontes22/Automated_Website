Brighter isn't better and more is less. The AI slowdown is nigh, no matter who says what
============================================================

Schneider Electric has announced a $22.6 billion acquisition of PTC to bolster its power infrastructure capabilities as the global datacenter boom drives increased demand for smarter energy management.

### Key Points
- Schneider Electric is acquiring PTC for $22.6 billion to integrate advanced industrial software into its power infrastructure portfolio.
- The deal reflects a strategic shift toward digitizing energy management to support the rapid expansion of AI-driven datacenters.
- PTC provides critical product lifecycle management and industrial IoT software that complements Schneider’s existing hardware offerings.
- This acquisition follows a broader industry trend of infrastructure companies consolidating software assets to capture value from the current datacenter construction surge.

### Why it Matters
This acquisition signals a major consolidation in the industrial technology sector as companies race to provide the integrated software and hardware required for modern, energy-intensive datacenters. By combining power management with industrial software, Schneider Electric aims to secure a dominant position in the infrastructure market supporting the global AI transition.
