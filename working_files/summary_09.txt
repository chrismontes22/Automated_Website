Bitcoin falls below $82,000 as investors retreat from risk assets
============================================================

Bitcoin dropped below $82,000 on Thursday as a broader market sell-off, driven by rising geopolitical tensions and declining tech stocks, impacted major digital assets and investor sentiment.

### Key Points
- Bitcoin fell over 3% to approximately $81,600, contributing to a 1.4% decline in the total cryptocurrency market capitalization.
- Data from CoinGlass indicates that traders experienced roughly $400 million in liquidations during the 24-hour period.
- Rising Middle East tensions pushed Brent crude oil prices above $112 per barrel, the highest level recorded since May.
- Major US stock indexes trended lower following a mixed reception to recent earnings reports from prominent technology companies.
- Fundstrat strategist Sean Farrell noted that volatility in US Treasury markets continues to constrain liquidity and weigh on risk appetite for digital assets.

### Why it Matters
The decline highlights the ongoing sensitivity of the cryptocurrency market to macroeconomic instability and geopolitical conflicts. As investors retreat from riskier assets, the volatility underscores the challenges bitcoin faces in maintaining a sustained recovery despite improving credit conditions.
