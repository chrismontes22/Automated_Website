Microsoft Says Workers Are Now Using Copilot AI as Much as Teams and Outlook
============================================================

Microsoft reported significant growth in its fiscal year 2026 fourth-quarter earnings, driven by widespread adoption of Copilot AI and the rapid deployment of nearly 40 million autonomous agents.

### Key Points
- Microsoft reported 18% year-over-year revenue growth, with Azure surpassing $100 billion in annual revenue for the first time.
- Copilot engagement has reached parity with Outlook and Teams, with over 30 million paid Microsoft 365 seats currently active.
- Business customers have deployed nearly 40 million AI agents across more than 10,000 companies since the launch of Agent 365.
- Copilot revenue increased 60% quarter-over-quarter, while the company added 31 new data centers to support its expanding AI infrastructure.
- One in three pull requests on GitHub now involves an AI agent, signaling a shift toward agentic workflows in software development.

### Why it Matters
Microsoft’s transition from simple automation to autonomous agentic AI represents a fundamental shift in how corporations measure the return on investment for expensive technology deployments. By moving beyond basic time-saving metrics toward complex cognitive task completion, the company aims to justify the massive capital expenditures required to sustain the current AI boom.
