98% of fraudulent hires have company credentials by the time they’re caught
============================================================

A new report from HYPR reveals that 98% of fraudulent hires successfully obtain corporate network credentials before being detected, highlighting a critical security gap in enterprise onboarding processes.

### Key Points
- HYPR’s State of HR Identity Fraud Detection report identifies a 90-day blind spot between hiring and onboarding that attackers exploit to gain internal access.
- While 98% of HR leaders have encountered candidate fraud, third-party security tools currently detect only 53% of these identity-based threats.
- Organizations rely on an average of 2.2 disconnected checkpoints to identify fraud, often depending on manual discovery rather than automated verification.
- IT and telecommunications sectors, despite being technically advanced, rely more heavily on manual observation to catch fraudulent workers than other industries.
- Resolving a single hiring fraud incident typically requires one to three weeks, resulting in significant operational costs and security risks for the affected company.

### Why it Matters
The lack of unified ownership between HR and IT departments during the hiring transition creates a dangerous vulnerability that allows bad actors to infiltrate corporate networks. As AI-generated candidates become more sophisticated, companies must shift from reactive, manual detection methods to integrated identity verification to prevent unauthorized access.
