AI Referrals Are Recreating The Oldest Mistake In Conversion Optimization via @sejournal, @slobodanmanic
============================================================

Generative AI tools like ChatGPT frequently cite deep-link content but direct users to generic homepages or internal search pages, creating a significant conversion mismatch for high-intent visitors.

### Key Points
- Similarweb reports that 65% of ChatGPT citations point to deep pages, yet 58.8% of referral traffic lands on homepages.
- Previsible data indicates that 28.8% of AI-referred traffic is directed to internal search results pages, which are often poorly optimized.
- Ahrefs observed that over 80% of its AI search traffic lands on homepages or product pages rather than the specific editorial content cited.
- AI-referred visitors are typically further along the sales funnel, arriving pre-convinced and ready to act rather than needing a generic brand introduction.
- Despite representing a small percentage of total traffic, AI referrals often drive a disproportionately high share of signups and conversions.

### Why it Matters
This disconnect forces high-intent users to restart their research journey on generic pages, leading to lost conversion opportunities and poor user experiences. Optimizing landing pages and internal search functionality ensures that these pre-informed visitors can immediately transition from research to action.
