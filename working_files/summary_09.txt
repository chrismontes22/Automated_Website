Oracle co-founder Larry Ellison thinks AI could make us all rich through a universal high income – but governments will need to change
============================================================

Oracle co-founder Larry Ellison envisions a future where AI and robotics drive significant productivity gains, potentially enabling a "universal high income" model through new taxation on automated value.

### Key Points
- Larry Ellison proposed shifting government taxation from human labor to the value generated by robots and AI systems.
- The Oracle co-founder argued that AI will increase productivity and lower the cost of goods rather than simply replacing human workers.
- Ellison suggested adopting a "universal high income" model, inspired by economist Milton Friedman’s negative income tax, to distribute AI-generated wealth.
- The executive acknowledged that while automation will displace some jobs, humans will transition into supervisory roles, similar to pilots using autopilot.
- Oracle has faced significant workforce reductions, with reports indicating approximately 21,000 layoffs between June 2025 and June 2026.

### Why it Matters
This vision highlights a growing debate among tech leaders regarding the long-term economic consequences of widespread automation and job displacement. Implementing such a system would require a fundamental restructuring of global tax policies and social safety nets over the coming decade.
