Mesh Network Telephony Over Bluetooth on Android Phones
============================================================

The MeshLive project introduces a decentralized Android application that enables end-to-end encrypted voice and video communication by utilizing smartphone Bluetooth hardware to create a peer-to-peer mesh network.

### Key Points
- MeshLive functions as an Android application that facilitates voice and video calls without requiring dedicated external hardware.
- The system uses Bluetooth to relay signals between devices, with each node extending the network range by approximately 20 meters.
- Security features include end-to-end encryption and rotating random identifiers that change every 15 minutes for non-contact users.
- The platform supports one-way video broadcasting, while two-way video calling remains in an experimental development phase.

### Why it Matters
This project offers a privacy-focused alternative to centralized telephony by removing reliance on traditional cellular infrastructure. It demonstrates the potential for software-only mesh networking to provide communication in environments where standard networks are unavailable or compromised.
