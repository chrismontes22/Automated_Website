The FAA wants to reboot the nation's airspace. This airline shows how it might work
============================================================

The Federal Aviation Administration has awarded an $875 million contract to Air Space Intelligence to modernize U.S. airspace management using predictive artificial intelligence and data analytics technology.

### Key Points
- Alaska Airlines reported saving approximately one million gallons of fuel annually by using the Flyways AI software to optimize flight routes.
- The FAA’s new SMART system aims to provide a unified, real-time view of national airspace to prevent bottlenecks and congestion before they occur.
- Air Space Intelligence has previously partnered with Delta Air Lines, United Airlines, the U.S. Army, and the U.S. Air Force.
- The technology functions as a decision-making aid for human dispatchers and controllers rather than an autonomous replacement for human oversight.

### Why it Matters
This initiative represents a significant shift toward integrating predictive analytics into national infrastructure to improve operational efficiency and reduce environmental impact. If successful, the transition could fundamentally transform how air traffic controllers manage the world's busiest and most complex airspace.
