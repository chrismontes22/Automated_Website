Apolosign's portable 32-inch smart TV is more than just a TV
============================================================

The Apolosign 32-inch Portable Smart TV functions as a large-format Android tablet on wheels, offering versatile mobility for home entertainment, professional presentations, and interactive educational use cases.

### Key Points
- The device features a 32-inch touchscreen, available in 4K UHD ($819) or 1080p ($719) resolutions, running on an Android operating system.
- Hardware includes a Rockchip RK3576 octa-core processor, 16 GB of RAM (8 GB physical plus 8 GB virtual), and up to 256 GB of storage.
- The unit is mounted on a height-adjustable, swiveling stand with wheels, weighing approximately 48 pounds (22 kg).
- Battery life is rated for up to six hours at 50% brightness, with a full recharge time of approximately 2.5 to 3 hours.
- Connectivity options include two USB-A ports, one USB-C port, one HDMI port, and an included 8-MP detachable webcam.

### Why it Matters
This device represents a growing market segment of mobile, all-in-one smart displays that bridge the gap between traditional televisions and large-format tablets. Its portability and flexible mounting options offer a practical solution for users needing a shared, interactive screen that can easily transition between home, office, and classroom environments.
