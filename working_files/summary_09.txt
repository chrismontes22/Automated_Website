Google has quietly been rebuilding Chrome on Android for years, and it's now nearly 50% smoother because of it
============================================================

Google has significantly improved the performance of Chrome on Android, achieving a 48% reduction in scroll stutters compared to 2023 through extensive architectural updates to its rendering pipeline.

### Key Points
- Google reduced "scroll jank" in Chrome for Android by 48% since late 2023.
- The performance gains resulted from three years of engineering work on the browser's rendering pipeline.
- Engineers rerouted touch inputs, reduced inter-process communication delays, and offloaded more tasks to the GPU.
- Collaboration with the Android operating system helped prioritize input threads for more reliable frame delivery.

### Why it Matters
These technical refinements provide a smoother, more responsive browsing experience for millions of Android users by eliminating micro-stutters during page navigation. This update demonstrates Google's ongoing commitment to optimizing core browser stability alongside its recent focus on integrating generative AI features.
