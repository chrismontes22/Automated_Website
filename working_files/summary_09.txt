Chinese AI shakes up global markets
============================================================

Chinese AI developers like DeepSeek, Z.ai, and Moonshot AI are challenging American dominance by offering high-performance, cost-effective models that are increasingly adopted by global businesses to reduce operational expenses.

### Key Points
- Chinese firms are narrowing the technology gap, with analysts noting their infrastructure now rivals top-tier US frontier models.
- Businesses are shifting toward "model routing," using cheaper, self-hosted Chinese open-weight models for routine tasks to cut rising token costs.
- Startups like Relevance AI report that usage of open-weight models has surged from roughly 5% to 25% this year.
- Security experts warn that locally hosted models, regardless of origin, require robust cybersecurity protocols to mitigate risks like malicious backdoors.
- Concerns persist regarding potential bias in Chinese-developed models and regulatory uncertainty, particularly in sensitive sectors like finance and healthcare.

### Why it Matters
The rise of affordable, high-quality Chinese AI models is pressuring the profit margins of US tech giants and forcing investors to re-evaluate the massive capital expenditures currently fueling the AI boom. This shift toward cost-efficient, self-hosted alternatives is fundamentally changing the economics of AI deployment for global enterprises.
