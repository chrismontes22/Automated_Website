Google confirms Pixel price hikes across the board
============================================================

Google has confirmed that the upcoming Pixel 11 smartphone lineup and existing models will face price increases due to rising memory costs and shifting global supply chain economics.

### Key Points
- Google VP Shakil Barkat announced that price adjustments will apply to the Pixel 11 series and the Pixel 10a.
- The company cited unprecedented surges in memory component costs as the primary driver for the upcoming price hikes.
- Leaks suggest the Pixel 11 Pro XL and Pixel 11 Pro Fold may see a $100 price increase.
- Base storage options for the Pixel 11 and Pixel 11 Pro are expected to start at 256GB, effectively raising the entry-level price.
- Google plans to offer promotional discounts and trade-in programs to help mitigate the impact of these price changes for consumers.

### Why it Matters
These price adjustments reflect broader inflationary pressures and supply chain volatility currently impacting the global consumer electronics market. As hardware costs rise, consumers may see a shift toward higher base-model pricing and reduced hardware specifications across the smartphone industry.
