AI Doesn’t Have an ROI Problem, Companies Have a Work Architecture Problem.
============================================================

Businesses must move beyond simple task automation and redesign their organizational architecture to fully capture the economic potential of the projected $10.3 trillion investment in AI infrastructure.

### Key Points
- Global investment in AI data centers and infrastructure is projected to reach $10.3 trillion between 2025 and 2032.
- Five major hyperscalers—Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, Microsoft, and Oracle—are expected to spend $4.2 trillion on AI infrastructure through 2029.
- Research indicates that AI productivity gains are often lost due to outdated organizational structures, bureaucratic handoffs, and rigid management hierarchies.
- A 2026 study of 515 startups showed that firms which reorganized their workflows around AI generated 1.9 times more revenue than those that did not.
- Experts advocate for shifting from "Org Charts" based on reporting lines to "Work Charts" that organize humans and AI agents around specific business outcomes.

### Why it Matters
Applying AI to inefficient, legacy processes merely accelerates existing bottlenecks rather than creating new value. To achieve a true return on intelligence, leaders must fundamentally rethink how work is structured and move away from departmental silos toward outcome-based workflows.
