SpaceX joins forces with Qualcomm to build Starlink Mobile 5G network
============================================================

SpaceX has partnered with Qualcomm to utilize Dragonwing Cellular Infrastructure platforms for its upcoming Starlink Mobile terrestrial network, marking a significant step toward launching a nationwide US carrier.

### Key Points
- Qualcomm’s Dragonwing technology will support tens of thousands of Starlink Mobile base stations starting in 2027.
- The agreement is described by Qualcomm as its largest 5G deal to date, aiming to enhance network coverage, capacity, and spectral efficiency.
- Analysts emphasize that securing spectrum rights and tower leases remains a critical hurdle for Starlink to compete with established carriers.
- Industry experts suggest Starlink faces significant challenges in matching the performance and reliability of existing terrestrial mobile networks.

### Why it Matters
This partnership signals SpaceX's serious intent to challenge major US mobile carriers like Verizon, AT&T, and T-Mobile. Successfully building this infrastructure could fundamentally disrupt the economics of the wireless industry, though the company still faces substantial regulatory and logistical obstacles.
