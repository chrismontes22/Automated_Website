DraftKings gets four-month Louisiana license extension amid AI review
============================================================

The Louisiana Gaming Control Board has granted DraftKings a four-month conditional license extension while regulators investigate the company’s use of artificial intelligence and shareholder suitability requirements.

### Key Points
- The Louisiana Gaming Control Board issued a conditional license extension for DraftKings that remains effective through February 19, 2027.
- Regulators are investigating allegations that DraftKings used artificial intelligence to target promotions toward high-betting customers, a practice internally referred to as "elasticity."
- The board is reviewing whether shareholders with stakes of 5% or more have completed mandatory suitability reviews required by state law.
- DraftKings must submit monthly compliance reports and certify that all marketing materials and digital advertisements adhere to responsible-gambling standards.
- A formal renewal hearing is scheduled for February 18, 2027, to determine the company's long-term licensing status in Louisiana.

### Why it Matters
This regulatory scrutiny highlights the growing tension between sportsbook operators and state authorities regarding the ethical use of predictive algorithms in gambling. The outcome of this investigation could set a significant precedent for how AI-driven marketing practices are monitored and regulated across the broader U.S. sports betting industry.
