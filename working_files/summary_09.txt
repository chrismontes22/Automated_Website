Bitcoin Crosses $80,000 For The First Time Since May Amid Crypto Bounce Back
============================================================

Bitcoin prices surpassed $80,000 for the first time since May, leading a broader cryptocurrency market rally fueled by investor interest in the debasement trade and recent political developments.

### Key Points
- Bitcoin reached $80,453 on Tuesday, marking a 25% increase over the past week.
- Ether rose to $2,503, while major tokens including Solana, Binance Coin, XRP, and Dogecoin saw gains between 18% and 52%.
- Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent announced increased bond buybacks to address rising yields and national debt concerns.
- President Donald Trump recently met with crypto executives to advocate for the passage of the CLARITY Act to establish digital asset regulations.
- Hedge fund manager Ray Dalio suggested investors shift capital from debt assets toward gold and bitcoin to hedge against potential dollar weakening.

### Why it Matters
This market shift reflects growing investor anxiety regarding U.S. national debt and the long-term stability of fiat currency. By moving capital into alternative assets, market participants are signaling a strategic hedge against potential inflation and sovereign debt volatility.
