Google just set the date for its big Googlebook event in New York
============================================================

Google has officially clarified that it will not hold a Googlebook announcement event on September 15, contradicting previous reports regarding the launch of its new laptop platform.

### Key Points
- Google confirmed to Android Central that no Googlebook-related announcements are scheduled for September 15.
- The company previously teased the Googlebook platform as a premium laptop line built for Gemini Intelligence.
- Planned features for the platform include custom Gemini-powered widgets, Magic Pointer, and native Android codebase integration.
- Hardware partners for the project include Lenovo, ASUS, and HP, with leaked designs featuring a signature HiLight LED bar.

### Why it Matters
The cancellation of the anticipated September event leaves the timeline for Google’s entry into the premium laptop market uncertain. This delay impacts hardware partners and consumers waiting to see how Google integrates its Gemini AI and Android ecosystem into a dedicated computing platform.
