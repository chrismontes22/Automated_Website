Nevermind the RAM crisis, these are the 5 things you can do for the best PC gaming experience without overpaying for parts
============================================================

As high demand for data center components drives up PC hardware prices through 2030, gamers can optimize their existing setups using strategic upgrades and software-based performance enhancements.

### Key Points
- Global supply shortages for RAM and graphics cards have caused significant price inflation, making new high-end PC builds prohibitively expensive for many consumers.
- Gamers can boost performance on existing hardware by enabling AMD EXPO or Intel XMP in the BIOS to unlock higher RAM speeds.
- AI-powered upscaling technologies like Nvidia DLSS, AMD FSR, and Intel XeSS allow older GPUs to maintain playable framerates in modern, demanding titles.
- Peripherals like mice and keyboards have seen significant innovation, with new magnetic sensor technology offering improved durability and responsiveness.
- Affordable monitor upgrades, including 1440p panels and OLED displays, provide a cost-effective way to enhance visual quality without replacing core internal components.

### Why it Matters
These strategies allow PC gamers to maintain a high-quality experience despite the current market volatility and inflated component costs. By focusing on peripherals, software optimization, and existing game libraries, users can avoid overpaying for hardware while waiting for supply chains to stabilize.
