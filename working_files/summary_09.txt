Can Reddit fend off a new wave of AI SEO spam?
============================================================

Reddit is facing an influx of AI-driven marketing spam as brands attempt to manipulate generative search results by planting fake, product-focused recommendations within popular community discussions.

### Key Points
- Reddit was the most-cited domain in May by major AI tools including ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google’s Gemini.
- Marketers are using "astroturfing" tactics, such as fake user accounts and scripted comments, to influence AI-generated search summaries.
- Reddit now blocks approximately 23 million spam views daily and is deploying new AI-based detection tools to identify coordinated manipulation.
- Subreddit moderators are implementing stricter verification processes and keyword filters to combat covert promotional content from brands and marketing agencies.
- Approximately 40 percent of all discussions on the platform are estimated to be commercial in nature.

### Why it Matters
As AI chatbots increasingly rely on Reddit to provide human-like answers, the platform has become a primary target for companies seeking to influence consumer purchasing decisions. This trend threatens the perceived authenticity of Reddit, potentially undermining the trust that makes the site a critical resource for users seeking unbiased information.
