Judge says Pentagon's measures against Anthropic were 'illegal and baseless'
============================================================

U.S. District Judge Rita Lin ruled that the Pentagon acted illegally by designating AI company Anthropic a national security risk in retaliation for its criticism of military technology.

### Key Points
- Judge Rita Lin determined the Pentagon’s supply chain risk designation was a retaliatory measure rather than a legitimate security concern.
- The dispute originated in February when President Donald Trump and Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth targeted Anthropic over its refusal to support unrestricted military AI use.
- Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei previously raised concerns regarding the potential use of company products for mass surveillance and autonomous armed drones.
- The ruling blocks the enforcement of a directive that ordered federal agencies to cease using Anthropic’s Claude chatbot.
- OpenAI, a primary competitor, secured a partnership with the Department of Defense shortly after the government initiated its action against Anthropic.

### Why it Matters
This ruling establishes a significant legal precedent regarding the limits of government authority when penalizing private technology firms for their public policy stances. It highlights the ongoing tension between national security interests and the ethical concerns raised by AI developers as these companies prepare for major public offerings.
