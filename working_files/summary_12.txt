Fenix Flexin isn’t even denying using AI to make ‘Rubberz’ anymore
============================================================

Los Angeles rapper Fenix Flexin has acknowledged using artificial intelligence in his song "Rubberz" after previously denying the claims despite evidence from producer Medasin and AI detection tools.

### Key Points
- Fenix Flexin admitted to using AI in his track "Rubberz" after initially claiming the song was recorded manually in a home studio.
- Producer Medasin identified the song as a product of the AI tool Treblo, formerly known as Sonauto.
- The artist previously told Hot 97’s "Mornings With Mero" that there was "no AI" involved in the production of the track.
- Fenix Flexin defended the use of new technology, stating that artists must adapt to modern tools to remain relevant in the music industry.

### Why it Matters
This admission highlights the growing tension between traditional music production and the integration of generative AI tools in mainstream hip-hop. As artists increasingly adopt these technologies, the industry faces ongoing debates regarding creative authenticity and the transparency of production processes.
