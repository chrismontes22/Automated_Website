Crypto Exchange Luno Cuts 20% Of Staff
============================================================

London-based cryptocurrency exchange Luno is laying off 20% of its global workforce as the company restructures its operations to navigate ongoing market volatility and declining digital asset prices.

### Key Points
- CEO James Lanigan announced the 20% staff reduction, citing challenging global market conditions for the digital asset sector.
- Luno serves approximately 16 million customers worldwide with operations spanning South Africa, London, and various Asian markets.
- The company plans to pivot its business model toward institutional investors, core infrastructure development, and new product launches.
- Strategic goals include providing non-US stablecoins to emerging markets and utilizing institutional-settlement services to streamline cross-border financial transactions.
- The firm joins a growing list of cryptocurrency companies downsizing during the prolonged industry downturn often referred to as the "crypto winter."

### Why it Matters
This restructuring reflects a broader industry trend where crypto firms are shifting focus from retail growth toward more stable revenue streams like institutional services and financial infrastructure. By prioritizing institutional-settlement business, Luno aims to reduce the complexity of cross-border payments while insulating itself from the volatility of retail digital asset trading.
