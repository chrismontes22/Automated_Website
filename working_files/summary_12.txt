Colleges Are Automating Admissions, but Their Most Time-Consuming Work Is Still Manual — And Costly
============================================================

The State of Automation in Admissions Report 2026 reveals that U.S. colleges struggle to scale enrollment because labor-intensive tasks like transcript evaluation remain largely manual despite digital investments.

### Key Points
- While 80% of institutions automated CRM systems, only 12.5% have automated transcript and credit evaluations.
- Nearly 65% of surveyed leaders reported an increase in application or financial aid fraud over the last two years.
- Approximately 70% of admissions leaders identified staffing and heavy workloads as their primary operational challenges.
- Over 72% of institutions lack 24/7 applicant support, forcing staff to spend significant time on routine student inquiries.
- 65% of respondents emphasize that human oversight remains essential for reviewing AI-generated outcomes in admissions decisions.

### Why it Matters
Many institutions are prioritizing enrollment growth without first addressing the operational bottlenecks that prevent efficient application processing. By failing to automate complex tasks, colleges risk overwhelming their staff and hindering their ability to manage increased student volume effectively.
