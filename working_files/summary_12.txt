WhatsApp Ends Support For Older Phones: Requirements And Security Impact
============================================================

WhatsApp has officially restricted access for smartphones running outdated operating systems, requiring users to upgrade to Android 6.0 or iOS 15.5 to maintain connectivity on the messaging platform.

### Key Points
- WhatsApp implemented a server-side block that prevents users on unsupported legacy software from accessing the application.
- The platform now requires a minimum of Android 6.0 (Marshmallow) for Android devices and iOS 15.5 for Apple hardware.
- Older devices, including aging Samsung Galaxy models and early iPhone generations, are no longer compatible with the service.
- The enforcement is permanent, displaying a full-screen warning that cannot be bypassed until the device software is updated or replaced.
- WhatsApp cited cybersecurity risks, noting that legacy operating systems lack the security patches necessary to protect against modern malware and data interception.

### Why it Matters
This policy shift highlights the growing tension between maintaining legacy hardware and ensuring robust end-to-end encryption security. Users must now prioritize software lifecycle management to avoid sudden service disruptions and potential vulnerabilities associated with unpatched operating systems.
