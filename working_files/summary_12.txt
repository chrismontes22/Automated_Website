MarketBeat Week in Review – 07/27- 07/31
============================================================

Investors are navigating a volatile market landscape defined by Federal Reserve interest rate policies, ongoing geopolitical tensions, and a critical focus on free cash flow in recent earnings reports.

### Key Points
- The Pacer US Cash Cows 100 ETF (COWZ) has gained 10.5% in 2026 as investors rotate into defensive, cash-rich assets.
- Major tech companies including Microsoft and Amazon are being rewarded by markets for delivering strong earnings and robust free cash flow.
- Semiconductor stocks like Advanced Micro Devices and SK hynix face short-term volatility but remain central to long-term AI infrastructure growth.
- Defense stocks such as RTX and Lockheed Martin are gaining attention amid escalating hostilities between the United States and Iran.
- Investors are closely monitoring upcoming employment data, with the July jobs report scheduled for release on August 7.

### Why it Matters
Market participants are increasingly prioritizing companies with strong balance sheets and tangible cash flow to hedge against inflation and economic uncertainty. This shift highlights a broader transition where investors are moving away from speculative growth toward defensive and value-oriented strategies.
