Qualcomm claims its Snapdragon C chip beats Intel in battery power for $300 laptops
============================================================

Qualcomm has unveiled the Snapdragon C, an 8-core processor designed for budget-friendly laptops that promises significant battery efficiency improvements over competing Intel N250 chips in everyday tasks.

### Key Points
- The Snapdragon C features an 8-core Kryo CPU with a 3GHz single-core and 2GHz multi-core maximum frequency.
- Qualcomm claims the chip offers 106% better Netflix playback efficiency and 99% better Microsoft Teams performance than the Intel N250.
- Major manufacturers including Acer, Asus, HP, and Lenovo are currently developing devices powered by the new processor.
- The hardware is specifically engineered for the entry-level laptop market, targeting a price point around $300.
- Official pricing and specific retail release dates for the new Snapdragon C-powered laptops have not yet been announced.

### Why it Matters
This development signals a shift in the budget laptop market as Qualcomm challenges Intel’s dominance in the entry-level segment. If these efficiency gains hold in real-world usage, consumers could see a substantial increase in battery life for affordable devices.
