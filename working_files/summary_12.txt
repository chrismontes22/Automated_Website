Gen Alpha won’t write emails when they join the workforce in 2030—research reveals they’ll sent voice notes to their boss instead
============================================================

A new report from the London School of Economics and Jabra predicts that voice-based AI will become the primary method for workplace communication by the year 2028.

### Key Points
- Researchers suggest that workers will increasingly use voice commands to draft content, relegating traditional typing to a secondary role for editing and refinement.
- The shift is expected to prioritize conversational speed and efficiency, potentially allowing employees to capture ideas more effectively while away from their desks.
- Experts note that voice-to-text AI tools will likely transcribe spoken inputs into written documents to maintain the searchability and accountability of traditional emails.
- Academics warn that relying on raw audio messages could hinder productivity, complicate record-keeping, and create communication overload within professional organizations.
- The transition is viewed as a significant accessibility improvement for workers who need to manage tasks while multitasking or on the move.

### Why it Matters
This shift signals a fundamental change in how employees interact with digital tools, moving from manual input to AI-assisted verbal direction. While it promises increased creative efficiency, businesses must balance these gains against the potential loss of searchable, written documentation and the risks of workplace communication fatigue.
