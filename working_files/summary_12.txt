You only need one fast charger for your iPhone, Samsung, and Pixel, but it needs this feature
============================================================

To achieve peak charging speeds for modern smartphones like the Google Pixel 11 and Samsung Galaxy S26, users must select USB-C chargers that support specific power delivery standards.

### Key Points
- Achieving maximum charging speeds on Pixel and Galaxy devices requires a USB Power Delivery (PD) charger with Programmable Power Supply (PPS) support.
- PPS technology allows chargers to adjust voltage and current in precise 20mV and 50mA increments to match a device's specific power requirements.
- iPhones utilize a similar standard called Adjustable Voltage Supply (AVS), which is natively supported by most high-quality USB PD 3.2 chargers.
- Users should look for chargers rated at 27W or higher that explicitly list compatibility with USB PD 3.2, PPS, and AVS standards.
- The Google Pixel Flex Dual 67W charger is an example of a versatile adapter that supports these protocols to charge multiple devices at peak performance.

### Why it Matters
Understanding these technical requirements prevents users from inadvertently settling for slower charging speeds due to incompatible hardware. Investing in a certified USB PD 3.2 charger with PPS and AVS support ensures a single, efficient power solution for diverse smartphones and laptops.
