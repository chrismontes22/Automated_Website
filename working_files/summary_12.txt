Dario Amodei spent 2,000 words laying out to a critic on X why Anthropic must persist in the AI ‘race to the top’
============================================================

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei defended the company’s safety record against public criticism from Encode AI’s Nathan Calvin regarding the firm's rapid development of advanced artificial intelligence models.

### Key Points
- Nathan Calvin, chief strategy officer of Encode AI, accused Anthropic of prioritizing a race to superintelligence over essential safety commitments.
- Amodei argued that maintaining a leading position at the industry frontier allows Anthropic to set higher safety standards for all developers.
- Anthropic cited specific safety-focused actions, including restricting model access for Chinese state-controlled firms and limiting the release of its Mythos model.
- Amodei acknowledged potential shortcomings regarding a recent draft bill circulated to Congress that faced criticism for its impact on federal oversight.
- The exchange occurs as Anthropic prepares for a highly anticipated IPO in November, with the company currently reporting $16.8 billion in third-quarter revenue.

### Why it Matters
This public debate highlights the growing tension between rapid commercial AI development and the industry-wide push for rigorous safety guardrails. As Anthropic approaches a significant market valuation, its ability to balance competitive growth with ethical oversight remains a critical concern for regulators and investors alike.
