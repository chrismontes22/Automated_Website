BitMart founder says security team ‘severely understaffed’ after new hack
============================================================

BitMart founder Sheldon Xia confirmed the cryptocurrency exchange is operating with a severely understaffed security team following a new hack linked to the recent Mercado Bitcoin breach.

### Key Points
- Blockchain analytics firm Bitquery identified approximately $391,000 in suspicious transfers from BitMart wallets across three blockchains.
- The stolen funds were traced to a wallet previously implicated in the $46 million hack of Brazil-based exchange Mercado Bitcoin.
- BitMart is currently undergoing a restructuring process, with plans to cease all operations by January 2027.
- Founder Sheldon Xia cited employee threats and internal retaliation as primary factors complicating the retention of essential technical security staff.
- The exchange has proposed recovery options for users, including liquid asset distributions, pending court approval expected between December and January.

### Why it Matters
This security breach highlights the operational instability of an exchange already facing a planned shutdown and ongoing user withdrawal delays. The incident underscores the broader risks to customer assets when financial platforms struggle with internal staffing shortages and organizational restructuring during periods of high cyber threat activity.
