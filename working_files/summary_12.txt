Franklin Moves Bitcoin DRIP ETFs Toward Nasdaq With Dividend Reinvestment Plan
============================================================

Franklin Templeton has filed for two new ETFs that automatically reinvest equity dividends into Bitcoin, providing investors with a hybrid portfolio strategy managed through Nasdaq-listed funds.

### Key Points
- Franklin Templeton filed Form 8-A for the Franklin US Equity Bitcoin DRIP Index ETF (FBEQ) and the Franklin US Innovation Bitcoin DRIP Index ETF (FIBT).
- The funds utilize a 95% equity and 5% Bitcoin starting allocation, with management fees set at 0.09% and 0.19% respectively.
- Dividends are automatically reinvested into Bitcoin, with quarterly rebalancing triggered if the crypto allocation exceeds 5% or breaches a 20% threshold.
- The ETFs are designed to provide exposure through derivatives and Bitcoin-linked products rather than direct ownership of digital coins.
- The filings propose an effective date of October 27, though the funds have not yet begun trading on the Nasdaq exchange.

### Why it Matters
These products simplify crypto-equity investing by automating the reinvestment process, removing the need for investors to manually manage separate asset allocations. This strategy shifts the focus from pure Bitcoin speculation toward a diversified portfolio where stock performance remains the primary driver of returns.
