I'll build a house inside of you
============================================================

Professional hockey player Ilya Rozanov faces an indefinite suspension and contract termination after a massive data breach exposed his private communications, illicit activities, and intimate videos.

### Key Points
- The Boston-based athlete was suspended by his team following the leak of sensitive emails, records of bribery, and evidence of doping.
- The scandal involves explicit footage of Rozanov and Montreal center Shane Hollander, which was circulated publicly after being stolen from a compromised device.
- Rozanov faces potential mandatory military conscription in Russia, as his immigration status and legal standing have collapsed in the wake of the investigation.
- The league’s legal team confirmed that the termination of Rozanov’s contract is pending, effectively ending his professional career in the United States.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the severe professional and personal consequences for athletes when private digital data is weaponized in the age of social media. The fallout serves as a cautionary tale regarding the intersection of high-stakes sports, personal conduct, and the permanent nature of digital footprints.
