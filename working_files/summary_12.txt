Crypto bro faces 280 years in prison for defrauding investors with promises of an 'AI supercomputer' for mining — jury convicts businessman of running $24-million Ponzi scheme, claimed up to 30% APR and a 100% money-back guarantee
============================================================

Las Vegas businessman Brent Kovar was convicted of 15 federal charges for operating a $24 million Ponzi scheme that falsely promised high returns through cryptocurrency mining and artificial intelligence.

### Key Points
- Brent Kovar was found guilty of 11 counts of wire fraud, two counts of mail fraud, and two counts of money laundering.
- The Profit Connect scheme defrauded approximately 400 investors of $24 million between 2017 and 2021.
- Kovar falsely claimed the company utilized AI supercomputers to generate 15% to 30% annual returns for investors.
- Evidence revealed the company held zero cryptocurrency reserves and used new investor funds to pay earlier participants and personal expenses.
- Kovar faces a maximum potential sentence of 280 years in federal prison for his role in the fraudulent operation.

### Why it Matters
This case highlights the ongoing risks associated with investment schemes that leverage buzzwords like artificial intelligence and cryptocurrency to exploit retail investors. It serves as a stark reminder of the importance of due diligence, as victims of such Ponzi schemes often face significant challenges in recovering lost capital.
