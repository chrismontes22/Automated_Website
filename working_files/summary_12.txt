Microsoft is finally letting Windows 11 users take back the keyboard keys Copilot stole
============================================================

Microsoft is introducing a new Windows 11 feature that allows users to remap the dedicated Copilot keyboard key to the Right Ctrl or Context Menu functions.

### Key Points
- Users can reconfigure the Copilot key via Settings under the Bluetooth & Devices menu.
- The update is currently available in Windows 11 Insider and preview builds for testing.
- Certain keyboard shortcuts involving the Right Ctrl key may remain incompatible after remapping.
- Microsoft is gradually reducing deep Copilot integration as part of its broader K2 operating system improvements.
- The change provides more flexibility for laptop users who prefer traditional keyboard layouts over the dedicated AI button.

### Why it Matters
This update signals a shift in Microsoft's strategy to prioritize user customization over mandatory AI hardware integration. By allowing users to reclaim keyboard space, the company is addressing long-standing feedback regarding the forced placement of the Copilot key on modern laptops.
