Have You Protested AI Recently? Anthropic May Be Watching You for Precrimes
============================================================

Investigative reporting reveals that AI developer Anthropic is monitoring activists and reporting potential threats to law enforcement, raising significant concerns regarding corporate surveillance and predictive policing practices.

### Key Points
- Investigative journalist Daniel Boguslaw reported that Anthropic tracks activists near its physical assets and shares data with police departments.
- Anthropic maintains these measures are standard physical-security protocols designed to protect employees from potential threats.
- A recent company job posting for an "enterprise intelligence specialist" explicitly lists "activism" as a category for investigation and tracking.
- Anthropic updated its privacy policy this summer to facilitate sharing user conversation data with law enforcement without requiring a court order.
- The company remains under scrutiny by the Pentagon, which continues to classify the AI firm as a potential supply chain risk.

### Why it Matters
These reports highlight the growing tension between private AI companies and public oversight regarding the use of predictive surveillance tools. The integration of corporate intelligence gathering with law enforcement protocols raises critical questions about due process and the potential for overreach when monitoring political activism.
