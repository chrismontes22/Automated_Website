ICYMI: the 7 biggest tech stories of the week, from the best gadgets of IFA 2026 to GPT-6 Astra and 'the AGI era'
============================================================

TechRadar’s weekly roundup highlights major industry shifts, including OpenAI’s launch of the GPT-6 Astra model, new product reveals at IFA 2026, and Dyson’s entry into the smart toothbrush market.

### Key Points
- OpenAI introduced GPT-6 Astra, marking a significant milestone toward Artificial General Intelligence.
- IFA 2026 in Berlin showcased diverse innovations, including new robotics, laptops, and modular solar technology.
- Dyson launched the CameraJet, a high-end smart toothbrush featuring integrated liquid flossing and an internal camera.
- Google and Apple updated their mapping services to rename Lake Ontario as "Lake America" following a U.S. government directive.
- IKEA expanded its smart home portfolio with the Liljebagge, a radiator thermostat featuring Matter and Thread support.
- Sonos CEO Tom Conrad discussed the company's new Ultra hardware line and an upcoming AI-infused operating system.

### Why it Matters
These developments signal a rapid acceleration in AI capabilities and the continued integration of smart technology into everyday household appliances. As major tech firms push toward AGI and connected ecosystems, consumers face both increased convenience and new questions regarding data privacy and corporate influence over public information.
