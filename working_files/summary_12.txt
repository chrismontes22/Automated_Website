After ditching Nvidia for AMD, here are 4 things that actually surprised me about the switch
============================================================

Switching from Nvidia to AMD graphics cards involves significant trade-offs in software integration, upscaling technology, and ray-tracing performance that impact the overall 4K gaming experience for desktop users.

### Key Points
- AMD’s Linux support is highly integrated, utilizing open-source kernel drivers like amdgpu and Mesa, whereas Nvidia relies on proprietary components.
- Nvidia’s DLSS remains a more mature suite of tools compared to AMD’s FSR, offering superior neural-rendering and ray-reconstruction capabilities.
- Lower reported GPU temperatures on high-wattage cards like the RX 9070 XT often reflect superior cooling designs rather than lower total power consumption.
- Nvidia maintains a competitive advantage in ray tracing and path tracing, which remains a critical factor for users playing graphically demanding titles like Cyberpunk 2077.
- Community-developed tools like LACT are essential for Linux users to manage fan curves and power settings in the absence of official AMD software.

### Why it Matters
Understanding these differences helps consumers make informed hardware decisions based on their specific operating system and gaming priorities. While AMD offers competitive value and open-source flexibility, Nvidia’s ecosystem remains the industry standard for advanced rendering features and ray-tracing performance.
