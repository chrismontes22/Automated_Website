Reviewers say RTX Spark laptops deliver the AI power creators want
============================================================

The first wave of NVIDIA RTX Spark-powered laptops from manufacturers including Microsoft, ASUS, and HP has officially launched, targeting high-end creative professionals and AI development workflows.

### Key Points
- Seven major laptop models from ASUS, Dell, HP, Lenovo, Microsoft, and MSI now feature the NVIDIA RTX Spark platform and N1X processor.
- The Microsoft Surface Laptop Ultra, starting at $2,599.99, is positioned as a premium MacBook Pro competitor for Windows users.
- ASUS introduced the ProArt P14 and P16, emphasizing portability and creator-focused OLED displays with shipping beginning October 16, 2026.
- Pricing for the new hardware ranges from $2,599.99 to $3,799.99, with most units shipping throughout October and early November 2026.
- Reviews highlight the platform's strong performance for local AI tasks and content creation, though battery life remains a point of contention for some models.

### Why it Matters
These devices represent a significant attempt by NVIDIA and its partners to establish a high-performance Windows alternative to the MacBook Pro for professional creators. By focusing on local AI capabilities and unified memory, the RTX Spark platform aims to redefine the standard for premium Windows laptops in the enterprise and creative markets.
