Tim Cook scores $47 million pay deal to stay on as Apple's chairman
============================================================

Tim Cook has transitioned from his role as Apple CEO to executive chair, passing leadership to John Ternus while maintaining a significant compensation package and advisory position.

### Key Points
- John Ternus has officially succeeded Tim Cook as Apple CEO after Cook’s 15-year tenure.
- Cook will receive a $2 million annual salary and a $45 million target equity award in his new role as executive chair.
- Incoming CEO John Ternus will earn a $3 million salary with a $55 million target equity award for his first year.
- Apple’s stock price increased by approximately 2,300% during Cook’s leadership, rising from $13 to $317 per share.
- Cook’s compensation package includes performance-based restricted stock units tied to Apple’s total shareholder return relative to the S&P 500.

### Why it Matters
Retaining Cook as executive chair ensures continuity for Apple as the company navigates complex geopolitical relationships and develops its long-term artificial intelligence strategy. This transition mirrors the succession model used by Warren Buffett at Berkshire Hathaway, signaling that Cook will remain a vital strategic advisor to the new leadership.
