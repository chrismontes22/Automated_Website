Anthropic advocates for an $800 billion investment in artificial intelligence infrastructure, while industry developments highlight shifts in model pricing, cybersecurity threats, and emerging hardware memory technologies.

### Key Points
- Anthropic argues that global AI infrastructure spending must reach $800 billion to surpass the economic impact of electricity and the internet.
- OpenAI released a budget-friendly GPT-6.1 Terra model, offering performance competitive with premium alternatives at one-tenth the API cost of Astra.
- Apollo Global Management reports that AI financial gains are currently concentrated within the "Magnificent Seven" tech companies rather than the broader S&P 500.
- Former Intel CEO Pat Gelsinger has joined Nagare Labs to develop Z2RAM, a new memory technology aiming to combine high bandwidth with dense storage.
- EQT acquired a majority stake in Swiss cybersecurity firm Acronis, valuing the company at approximately $3.5 billion.
- DEF CON founder Jeff Moss announced that the conference will expand its critical infrastructure hardening project following successful voting security initiatives.

### Why it Matters
These developments underscore a growing divide between massive capital expenditure in AI and the practical challenges of achieving widespread profitability across the broader technology sector. As security threats evolve and hardware limitations persist, the industry is increasingly focused on balancing rapid innovation with sustainable infrastructure and robust defensive measures.
