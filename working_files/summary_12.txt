Dutch Police Arrest 24-Year-Old Amsterdam Man in ShinyHunters Investigation
============================================================

Dutch authorities have arrested a 24-year-old Amsterdam man, identified as Pepijn van der Stap, for his alleged leadership role within the notorious cybercriminal hacking group known as ShinyHunters.

### Key Points
- Dutch police arrested the suspect on September 15, 2026, with a court appearance scheduled for September 29, 2026.
- The FBI and Dutch National Police allege the suspect is a leader of ShinyHunters, a group linked to over 140 organizational breaches.
- Authorities claim the suspect is also involved in an unrelated investigation regarding the attempted incitement of two murders.
- ShinyHunters publicly denied any association with the arrested individual, claiming the police are seeking positive publicity.
- The group recently claimed to have stolen sensitive data from the FBI’s job application portal by exploiting a vulnerability in Oracle PeopleSoft.
- FBI officials report that ShinyHunters has extorted at least $70 million from victims by targeting third-party cloud vendors.

### Why it Matters
This arrest marks a significant escalation in the international effort to dismantle ShinyHunters, a group responsible for massive data breaches and millions of dollars in extortion. The case highlights the ongoing challenge of tracking sophisticated cybercriminals who often operate across borders and maintain professional personas within the cybersecurity industry.
