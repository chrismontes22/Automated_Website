Nasdaq rally hasn't stopped these tech stocks from flashing warning signs
============================================================

Despite record highs in the Nasdaq Composite, market analysts warn that underlying weakness in the semiconductor sector and broad index dispersion suggest potential risks for investors.

### Key Points
- The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index remains 14% below its June highs, with the average member down 26% from 52-week peaks.
- Only Nvidia, AMD, and Taiwan Semiconductor are currently trading closer to their highs than the broader semiconductor index.
- GlobalFoundries, KLA, and On Semiconductor are significantly underperforming, each down more than 40% from their 52-week highs.
- AMD recently surpassed a $1 trillion market valuation, contributing to the tech-heavy rally alongside Meta’s new Muse AI agent.
- BTIG strategist Jonathan Krinsky noted that current market dispersion mirrors patterns observed during the 2000 market correction.

### Why it Matters
The divergence between record-breaking index levels and struggling individual stocks suggests that current market momentum may be concentrated in a few select companies. This internal weakness could signal increased volatility and potential medium-term risks for investors despite the recent surge in AI-related valuations.
