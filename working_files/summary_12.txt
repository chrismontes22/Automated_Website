Bitcoin tops $86,000 ahead of U.S. jobs report
============================================================

Bitcoin reached a record high of $86,885 on Friday as investors closely monitor upcoming U.S. labor market data and navigate broader volatility in global bond and currency markets.

### Key Points
- Bitcoin briefly climbed to $86,885 before stabilizing near $86,000, marking a 3% gain for the month of October.
- Economists forecast September nonfarm payrolls to increase by 90,000, with the national unemployment rate expected to remain steady at 4.1%.
- The U.S. 10-year Treasury yield reached 5.34%, while the U.S. Dollar Index hit an 18-month high, increasing pressure on global risk assets.
- France’s borrowing premium over German bonds widened to a 14-year high amid growing concerns regarding the nation's significant fiscal deficit.

### Why it Matters
The resilience of bitcoin despite rising bond yields and a strengthening dollar suggests a shift in how investors perceive the cryptocurrency as a risk asset. These market movements highlight broader economic instability, particularly in Europe, where rising sovereign debt costs are impacting investor confidence in major economies.
