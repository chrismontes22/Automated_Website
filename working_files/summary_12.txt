Oppo's Find X10 Pro Max goes all out with three 200MP cameras and a massive battery
============================================================

Oppo has officially launched the Find X10 and Find X10 Pro Max, featuring advanced triple 200MP camera systems, massive 8,000mAh batteries, and powerful MediaTek Dimensity 9600 series chipsets.

### Key Points
- The Find X10 Pro Max features a unique triple 200MP rear camera array and the new LUMO True Image Engine.
- Both models are equipped with 8,000mAh batteries, supporting 80W wired and 50W wireless charging.
- The Pro Max utilizes the MediaTek Dimensity 9600 Pro chipset, while the base Find X10 uses the 9600M.
- Displays on both devices support a 144Hz refresh rate and reach a peak brightness of 3,600 nits.
- Pricing starts at €1,399 for the Find X10 and €1,799 for the Find X10 Pro Max.

### Why it Matters
- These flagship releases represent an aggressive attempt by Oppo to capture market share during a period of record-low global smartphone sales. By prioritizing high-end camera hardware and battery capacity, the company is positioning its new lineup to compete directly with premium devices from Samsung, Google, and Apple.
