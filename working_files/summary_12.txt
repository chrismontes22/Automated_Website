This lifetime VPN adds an AI threat scanner and costs just $50
============================================================

GhostShield is offering a lifetime subscription to its AI-powered VPN service for $49.99, providing users with real-time threat detection and enhanced privacy features at a significant discount.

### Key Points
- The service features an AI Threat Analyzer that identifies malicious domains and suspicious traffic patterns in real time.
- Security protocols include WireGuard encryption, RAM-only servers, a strict no-logs policy, and an automatic internet kill switch.
- A single subscription supports up to 10 simultaneous devices across Windows, macOS, iOS, and Android platforms.
- Additional tools include an ad and tracker blocker, split tunneling, and a Family Mode for content filtering.
- Users can access 20 server locations across 16 countries with no bandwidth limitations.

### Why it Matters
This offer provides a cost-effective security solution for users seeking to combine standard VPN privacy with proactive AI-driven threat monitoring. By moving beyond static blocklists, the service offers a more dynamic approach to identifying emerging online risks for personal devices.
