OPPO is putting three 200MP cameras on its next flagship phone
============================================================

OPPO is set to launch the Find X10 Pro Max in China this month, featuring the world’s first smartphone camera system equipped with three 200MP rear sensors.

### Key Points
- The device features a triple 200MP camera array developed in partnership with Hasselblad, including a main sensor, ultrawide lens, and periscope telephoto.
- The main camera utilizes a 1/1.3-inch Samsung ISOCELL HPC sensor with an f/1.5 aperture and CIPA 6.5-level stabilization.
- The 200MP periscope telephoto lens offers a 70mm focal length and CIPA 7.5 stabilization, supporting 10cm macro photography.
- All three lenses support 8K video recording, with the device enabling full-focal-length 8K Log capture.
- Expected hardware includes a Dimensity 9600 Pro processor, a 6.78-inch 144Hz LTPO OLED display, and an 8,000mAh battery.
- The smartphone is scheduled to launch around September 17, running ColorOS 17 based on Android 17.

### Why it Matters
This triple 200MP configuration represents a significant escalation in mobile photography hardware, potentially setting a new industry standard for high-resolution imaging across all focal lengths. By prioritizing advanced sensor technology and stabilization, OPPO is positioning the Find X10 Pro Max as a premium competitor for professional-grade mobile content creation.
