Which do you plan to buy: iPhone 18 Pro or foldable iPhone Ultra? [Poll]
============================================================

Apple is reportedly shifting its upcoming September event strategy to feature three premium devices, including the standard iPhone 18 Pro, the Pro Max, and a new foldable iPhone Ultra.

### Key Points
- The iPhone 18 Pro is expected to feature a 2nm A20 Pro chip, an Apple C2 modem, and a refined, smaller Dynamic Island.
- The iPhone 18 Pro Max will likely retain a traditional design while focusing on significant battery life improvements and a triple-camera system.
- The rumored iPhone Ultra will debut as a book-style foldable with a 5.5-inch outer screen and a 7.8-inch internal display.
- Hardware specifications for the iPhone Ultra include a titanium chassis, Touch ID integration, and a dual-camera setup.
- Industry reports suggest the foldable iPhone Ultra could carry a premium starting price of $2,000 or higher.

### Why it Matters
This shift toward an all-premium lineup signals Apple's intent to capture the high-end smartphone market by diversifying its flagship offerings. The introduction of a foldable device represents a major strategic pivot that could redefine the company's hardware ecosystem and competitive standing against existing foldable rivals.
