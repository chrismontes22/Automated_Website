Trump's Crypto Comments Cheered: Robinhood CEO Stresses 'Broad Ownership' At White House Event, Binance Founder Says, 'We're So Back'
============================================================

President Donald Trump hosted a White House summit with cryptocurrency industry leaders to reaffirm his administration's commitment to establishing U.S. leadership in digital assets and emerging technologies.

### Key Points
- Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong expressed optimism that the administration and regulators will successfully pass the CLARITY Act.
- Robinhood CEO Vlad Tenev emphasized the company's mission to promote broad financial ownership as a pillar of a stable, prosperous society.
- Ripple CEO Brad Garlinghouse noted that 67 million Americans now hold cryptocurrency, highlighting the sector's transition from a fringe industry to a mainstream political force.
- SEC Chairman Paul Atkins and CFTC Chairman Michael Selig are reportedly aligned with the administration's goal of creating consistent regulatory rules.
- Binance founder Changpeng Zhao publicly celebrated the administration's supportive stance toward the digital asset sector.

### Why it Matters
This summit signals a significant shift toward formalizing cryptocurrency regulation, potentially providing the legal certainty that industry participants have long sought. By aligning federal regulators with legislative goals, the administration aims to solidify the United States as a global hub for digital asset innovation and investment.
