Exaggerating to your boss about how much you're using AI at work? You're not alone.
============================================================

Nearly half of U.S. employees are exaggerating their artificial intelligence expertise or usage to meet workplace expectations, according to a new survey from workforce intelligence firm Visier.

### Key Points
- A Visier survey found 45% of workers feel pressured to use AI tools even when they lack confidence in their proficiency.
- Only 28% of employees believe their leadership team fully understands how AI is being utilized within their organization.
- Approximately 59% of workers report uncertainty regarding their employer's long-term strategy for integrating AI into the workplace.
- About one-third of employees are requesting additional AI training and upskilling opportunities to improve their actual technical capabilities.
- Data from Gallup indicates that regular AI users are significantly more likely to report increased productivity and higher-quality work than irregular users.

### Why it Matters
The prevalence of "performative AI" creates a transparency gap that prevents organizations from accurately assessing where technology adds genuine value versus where it creates inefficiencies. This disconnect between leadership expectations and employee reality can hinder effective digital transformation and undermine trust within the workforce.
