Radar Trends to Watch: November 2026
============================================================

The rapid evolution of AI models and agentic tools is reshaping software development and cybersecurity, introducing both advanced capabilities and significant new vulnerabilities for organizations to manage.

### Key Points
- New open-weight models like Kimi 3.1 Pro, Mistral Large 4, and Meta’s Muse series are achieving performance parity with frontier-class AI systems.
- Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google are expanding AI agent standards and interfaces, including the Agent Skills standard and ChatGPT’s Intelligent UI feature.
- Security threats are escalating, highlighted by the PHP2Shell vulnerabilities, compromised eMudhra certificate authority keys, and malicious AI agent activity.
- Google has expanded its SynthID watermarking technology to include protein structures, while OpenAI prepares to implement text watermarking to comply with EU regulations.
- The US Department of Homeland Security has banned the use of DeepSeek models across all federal agencies due to emerging security and data privacy concerns.

### Why it Matters
The integration of AI into critical infrastructure and development workflows is creating a complex security landscape where traditional defenses are increasingly insufficient. Organizations must balance the rapid adoption of powerful new models and agents with rigorous security protocols to mitigate risks like prompt injection, data leakage, and supply chain attacks.
