Gamescom Says Stolen Laptops ‘Make Us Very Sad,’ But Says Exhibitors Have To Protect Against Theft: ‘This Is Noted In The Terms And Conditions’
============================================================

Gamescom organizers are facing backlash after multiple indie developers reported equipment thefts at the 2026 event in Cologne, prompting a controversial response regarding exhibitor security responsibilities.

### Key Points
- Indie developers, including Ryan Laley, iam8bit, and Tessera Studios, reported stolen laptops and hardware from their booths during the August 2026 event.
- Gamescom organizers issued a statement citing terms and conditions that advise exhibitors to purchase individual stand security and theft insurance.
- The event organizers noted that while uniformed guards patrol the venue, they cannot prevent all theft at an event of this scale.
- Exhibitors pay significant fees to participate, with basic booth space starting at approximately €2,100 before additional costs for security or travel.
- Reports of theft have surfaced from previous years, leading to increased criticism regarding the adequacy of current security measures for smaller studios.

### Why it Matters
The incident highlights a growing tension between large-scale trade show organizers and the financial risks faced by independent developers who invest heavily to showcase their work. This PR crisis may force event organizers to reevaluate their security protocols and liability policies to maintain trust within the gaming industry.
