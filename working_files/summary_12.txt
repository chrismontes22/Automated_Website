Banks Struggle to Digest the Mounting Debt Driving the AI Boom
============================================================

Despite a high-profile rebranding of artificial intelligence to "super intelligence" by the White House, major tech companies face mounting financial pressure due to massive capital requirements for infrastructure.

### Key Points
- Tech giants including Microsoft, Alphabet, and Meta have collectively raised over $159 billion in bonds through mid-2026, a 47% increase over the previous year.
- Hyperscalers have accrued over $1.1 trillion in off-balance-sheet commitments for future data center leases and $1.7 trillion in purchase obligations for hardware.
- Industry leaders like Broadcom CEO Hock Tan and Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan identified capital availability and rising borrowing costs as the primary bottlenecks for AI expansion.
- Private credit firms, including Blackstone and Apollo, are increasingly financing AI infrastructure, though rising default rates and withdrawal caps signal growing market strain.
- Goldman Sachs estimates that 2026 capital expenditures for major hyperscalers will reach $750 billion, nearly exhausting their combined operating cash flow.

### Why it Matters
The reliance on massive debt and off-balance-sheet financing to fund AI infrastructure creates significant systemic risk if the expected returns on these investments fail to materialize. As borrowing costs rise and investor demand for tech debt cools, the industry's aggressive expansion strategy faces a potential liquidity crunch that could impact both tech balance sheets and the broader financial markets.
