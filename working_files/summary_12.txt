Russia’s largest bank Sberbank secures approval to trade crypto
============================================================

Sberbank, Russia’s largest financial institution, has received regulatory approval to facilitate cryptocurrency trading and custody services, with full client access scheduled to launch on December 1.

### Key Points
- The Bank of Russia officially added Sberbank to its registry of cryptocurrency exchange providers and digital asset depositories.
- Sberbank is currently testing trading capabilities for Bitcoin, Ethereum, and USDT within its existing mobile banking application.
- New regulations effective September 1 establish a formal framework for banks and brokers to manage digital assets under central bank oversight.
- While trading is permitted, the Russian government maintains a strict ban on using cryptocurrencies for direct payments.
- Starting July 2027, all domestic crypto transactions must be processed through registered intermediaries, effectively prohibiting peer-to-peer transfers.

### Why it Matters
This development signals a significant shift in Russia's financial policy, moving from a proposed total ban on crypto to a state-regulated market model. By integrating digital assets into major banking infrastructure, the move provides a controlled environment for institutional and retail investors while tightening government oversight of the sector.
