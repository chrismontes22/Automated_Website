Iran’s Strait of Hormuz toll booth ran through a bitcoin exchange, U.S. says
============================================================

The U.S. Treasury has sanctioned Tehran-based cryptocurrency exchange BitBank for allegedly laundering hundreds of millions of dollars in Bitcoin to support the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps.

### Key Points
- The Treasury sanctioned BitBank and its software developer, Pishtaz Simorgh Electronic Trade Company, for facilitating illicit financial transfers.
- BitBank reportedly processed payments for the Hormuz Safe Marine Services Authority, which charges tankers up to $2 million for passage through the Strait of Hormuz.
- Sanctions freeze all BitBank property under U.S. jurisdiction and prohibit Americans from conducting business with the exchange.
- Foreign financial institutions face potential exclusion from the U.S. banking system if they process transactions linked to the designated entities.
- The U.S. government alleges these funds directly support the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps, a branch of the Iranian military.

### Why it Matters
These sanctions demonstrate the U.S. government's increasing focus on tracking and disrupting the use of digital assets to bypass international financial restrictions. By targeting the infrastructure supporting maritime transit fees, regulators are attempting to close loopholes that allow sanctioned regimes to monetize critical global shipping chokepoints.
