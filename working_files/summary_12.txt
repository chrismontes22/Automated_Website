I Went From AI Prompt to 3D-Printed Armor in 18 Hours
============================================================

Google’s new Gemini 4 Argon model successfully enabled a user to develop a custom web app for designing and 3D printing functional cosplay armor in under 18 hours.

### Key Points
- The user developed "KeriForge," a web application capable of generating 3D-printable armor components like vambraces.
- Gemini 4 Argon demonstrated improved speed and context awareness compared to previous iterations like Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite.
- The final design was exported to Nomad Sculpt and Layerling for refinement before being printed using flexible TPU 75A material.
- Despite minor printing wobbles due to material softness, the resulting 3D model was fully manifold and fit the user's forearm dimensions perfectly.
- The project required approximately 11 hours of iterative development and 7 hours of printing time.

### Why it Matters
This experiment demonstrates that advanced generative AI models can now bridge the gap between conceptual software development and physical manufacturing for non-expert users. By simplifying complex coding and design tasks, these tools lower the barrier to entry for custom fabrication and rapid prototyping.
