A market veteran says there are 3 reasons to stay calm amid earnings angst and AI jitters
============================================================

Market veteran Ed Yardeni argues that the artificial intelligence investment boom remains durable despite recent investor anxiety regarding Alphabet’s earnings and fluctuating capital expenditure forecasts from major tech firms.

### Key Points
- Ed Yardeni cites a collective $235 billion in recent AI financing, including capital initiatives from Meta, Anthropic, and SpaceX, as evidence of robust sector growth.
- Anthropic reported a significant revenue surge to $16.3 billion year-over-year, achieving its first quarterly operating profit in the second quarter.
- The number of Anthropic customers spending over $100,000 annually grew to 11,900, reflecting a substantial increase in enterprise demand.
- South Korean chipmaker SK Hynix reported strong third-quarter earnings and expects memory shortages to persist through 2030 due to high demand.

### Why it Matters
These indicators suggest that the underlying economics of AI infrastructure remain strong despite short-term market volatility and concerns over capital spending efficiency. Sustained demand for high-end chips and improved profitability at AI labs provide a positive outlook for the long-term viability of the sector.
