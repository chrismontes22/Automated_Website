Xbox hijacks your custom background for Call of Duty Modern Warfare 4 ads
============================================================

Xbox users are expressing frustration after a mandatory Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 promotional background replaced custom dashboard themes on Xbox Series X|S consoles this week.

### Key Points
- Microsoft automatically updated Xbox Series X|S dashboards with a full-screen Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 promotional background.
- The advertisement overrides user-selected custom backgrounds, though it can be disabled via the "Show event-themed backgrounds" setting in the Personalization menu.
- Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 is currently a top-selling title on both Xbox and PlayStation platforms.
- Critics argue the forced takeover highlights a growing trend of tech companies exerting control over hardware that consumers have already purchased.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the ongoing tension between digital platform holders and consumers regarding the limits of hardware ownership and user customization. It reflects a broader industry shift where companies increasingly prioritize promotional content over user control, raising concerns about the future of digital rights and consumer autonomy.
