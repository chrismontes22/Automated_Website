Salesforce after Dreamforce: How the CRM giant can grow beyond its own interface
============================================================

Salesforce is shifting its growth strategy by enabling AI agents to perform tasks outside its traditional interface, allowing customers to interact with business data through preferred external platforms.

### Key Points
- Salesforce is promoting "Headless 360" and "Agentforce" to allow AI agents to generate custom interfaces within tools like Claude, Slack, and other client surfaces.
- Research from Qualitate indicates that 75% of customers who have modeled headless access expect their Salesforce spending to increase rather than decline.
- The company is positioning its "enterprise AI harness"—comprising Data 360, Application 360, and Agent Fabric—to provide the business context necessary for agents to act.
- Salesforce recently acquired the customer-agent platform Fin for $3.6 billion to offer a faster, lower-friction entry point for organizations not yet ready for full platform implementation.
- While 90% of surveyed customers are interested in headless access, many remain in the pilot or proof-of-concept phase due to concerns regarding security, pricing, and enterprise readiness.

### Why it Matters
By decoupling its functionality from its own user interface, Salesforce aims to remain the essential "system of intelligence" that powers business workflows across an organization's entire software stack. This transition allows the company to capture value from AI consumption even when users interact with Salesforce data through third-party environments, though it requires proving that these new agentic workflows deliver measurable financial outcomes.
