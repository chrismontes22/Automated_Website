AI and regulation are reshaping the future of building security
============================================================

Modern security strategies now require the integration of physical and digital defenses to protect critical infrastructure against sophisticated threats like AI-driven cyberattacks and traditional mechanical tampering attempts.

### Key Points
- Security professionals are adopting a "hacker's mindset" to test product resilience against both physical lock-picking and digital exploits like phishing and malware.
- Modern access systems now incorporate advanced features such as biometric authentication, mobile credentials, and energy-harvesting technology.
- Artificial intelligence is being deployed to monitor real-time entry data for anomalies while simultaneously posing new risks through autonomous threat chains.
- The European Union’s NIS2 Directive and Cyber Resilience Act mandate stricter security standards for both digital networks and their physical operating environments.
- Interconnectivity in modern hardware and software increases the risk that a single compromised element could lead to a total network breach.

### Why it Matters
The convergence of physical and digital security is essential for protecting modern infrastructure as threats become increasingly complex and automated. Businesses must now prioritize holistic resilience to comply with evolving regulations and defend against sophisticated, multi-vector attacks.
