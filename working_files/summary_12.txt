Nippon Ichi, NIS America Head Says Company Is ‘Researching’ AI But Personally Doesn’t Think It Will Make Development Faster
============================================================

Nippon Ichi Software CEO Kenzo Saruhashi confirmed the company is currently researching generative AI for localization and development but remains focused on prioritizing product quality over production speed.

### Key Points
- CEO Kenzo Saruhashi stated that Nippon Ichi Software is not currently using generative AI for game localization or development.
- The company is researching AI technology to determine if it can genuinely improve the quality of its final products.
- Saruhashi emphasized that the studio’s primary goal is maintaining high standards for fans rather than using AI solely for efficiency.
- The publisher aims to unify its Japanese and Western branches to facilitate simultaneous worldwide game releases.
- Leadership expressed caution regarding AI, noting the industry-wide pressure to adopt the technology while acknowledging potential risks to creative output.

### Why it Matters
This cautious approach highlights a growing divide in the gaming industry between companies prioritizing rapid AI-driven production and those concerned with maintaining creative quality. By focusing on quality over speed, Nippon Ichi Software’s stance provides insight into how traditional developers are navigating the pressure to integrate emerging technologies.
