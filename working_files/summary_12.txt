Demand for cybersecurity pros with AI skills is reaching a 'fever pitch'
============================================================

Box CEO Aaron Levie and industry experts predict a significant surge in cybersecurity job demand as AI-driven threats necessitate advanced automated defense systems and specialized human oversight.

### Key Points
- Box CEO Aaron Levie expects massive investment in cybersecurity, labeling it one of the fastest-growing professional categories due to escalating AI-powered risks.
- The Bureau of Labor Statistics projects a 21% growth in information security analyst roles between 2025 and 2035, ranking it among the top 20 fastest-growing occupations.
- Cloudflare has deployed over 250 AI agents in nine months to automate alert triage and data aggregation, shifting staff focus toward proactive risk mitigation.
- Security professionals must now secure complex model pipelines and data flows against emerging threats like prompt injection and data poisoning.
- Experts suggest that while AI may automate entry-level tasks, it simultaneously raises the bar for new hires to possess advanced judgment and specialized technical expertise.

### Why it Matters
The integration of AI into cybersecurity is fundamentally transforming the industry from manual, reactive firefighting to automated, machine-speed defense. This shift creates a high-stakes environment where companies must balance massive investments in new technology with the recruitment of highly skilled experts capable of architecting resilient, AI-native systems.
