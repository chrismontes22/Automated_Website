Kamala Harris asks Congress for a new AI regulator and a treaty with China
============================================================

Vice President Kamala Harris, Barack Obama, and Pete Buttigieg are calling for federal AI oversight and international treaties, signaling a shift in Democratic policy ahead of the 2028 election.

### Key Points
- Kamala Harris proposed creating a new federal entity to conduct independent safety testing on frontier AI models.
- Harris and other Democratic leaders are advocating for international treaties to regulate AI development speeds and global safety standards.
- Former President Barack Obama urged Democrats to prioritize concrete AI legislation, distancing himself from both accelerationist and doomer perspectives.
- Donald Trump dismissed calls for AI regulation as a conspiracy, favoring a deregulatory approach and rescinding previous executive orders on oversight.
- Senate negotiators John Thune and Amy Klobuchar are currently drafting legislation that includes developer duties of care and potential model-blocking powers.

### Why it Matters
These coordinated statements from prominent Democrats elevate artificial intelligence from a niche technical debate to a central platform issue for the 2028 presidential cycle. This shift forces a clear partisan divide, as the party moves toward advocating for federal regulatory bodies while facing opposition from Donald Trump’s deregulatory stance.
