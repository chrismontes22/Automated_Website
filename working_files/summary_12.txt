Alleged Rockstar Games Hacker Arrested In Connection With Data Breach That Revealed GTA Online Profits
============================================================

Dutch authorities have arrested 24-year-old Amsterdam resident Pepijn van der Stap, an alleged leader of the hacking group ShinyHunters linked to major breaches at Rockstar Games and Ticketmaster.

### Key Points
- Dutch National Police arrested the suspect, identified by Reuters as Neo Security employee Pepijn van der Stap, on September 28.
- The FBI alleges Van der Stap has served as a leader of the ShinyHunters hacking collective since 2025.
- ShinyHunters is accused of compromising over 100 organizations, including a high-profile extortion attempt against Rockstar Games.
- Leaked data from the Rockstar breach revealed that GTA Online generates over $1 million in daily revenue.
- FBI Assistant Director Brett Leatherman issued a public warning urging remaining ShinyHunters members to surrender following the arrest.

### Why it Matters
This arrest highlights the ongoing challenge of tracking cybercriminals who transition between illicit activities and professional cybersecurity roles. The case underscores the significant financial and operational risks that data extortion groups pose to major corporations and their proprietary business intelligence.
