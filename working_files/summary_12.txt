LG’s 7 New Apple Home Accessories Include a Hands-Free Smart Lock
============================================================

LG Electronics and Apple have announced a new lineup of seven smart home accessories, including security cameras, a video doorbell, a smart lock, and a thermostat, launching December 2026.

### Key Points
- The collection features three security cameras, a dual-lens video doorbell, a smart deadbolt, a thermostat, and a temperature sensor.
- Products support Apple Home and Siri, with prices ranging from $39.95 for sensors to $399.95 for the floodlight camera.
- The smart deadbolt utilizes Ultra Wideband technology for hands-free entry via iPhone or Apple Watch.
- Cameras offer 2.5K resolution and activity recognition, with encrypted video history available through iCloud+ plans.
- The thermostat integrates with Apple’s Adaptive Temperature and Clean Energy Guidance to optimize climate control and energy usage.
- Initial availability begins in December 2026, with specific regional rollouts across the United States, Canada, and the United Kingdom.

### Why it Matters
This partnership simplifies the smart home experience by offering a unified ecosystem that eliminates the need for multiple third-party apps. By integrating directly with Apple Home, these devices provide consumers with a more secure, streamlined, and privacy-focused way to manage home automation.
