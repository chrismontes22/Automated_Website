'Profoundly corrupt': Trump sued over selling early access to Truth Social posts
============================================================

A federal lawsuit filed against Donald Trump alleges that selling early access to official presidential statements via Truth Social violates the First Amendment and constitutes government corruption.

### Key Points
- The Freedom of the Press Foundation and The Intercept filed the lawsuit in the Southern District of New York.
- Trump Media & Technology Group’s "Truth API" service charges up to $100,000 monthly for advance access to presidential posts.
- At least 10 customers have reportedly signed up for the service, which provides market-moving information before public release.
- President Trump retains a 41% stake in the parent company of Truth Social, which is currently held in a trust.
- Plaintiffs argue the scheme violates constitutional requirements for equal access to government information and federal ethics rules regarding personal profit.

### Why it Matters
This legal challenge highlights significant concerns regarding the intersection of presidential authority, private financial gain, and the public's right to transparent government communication. If successful, the case could set a major precedent for how officials manage digital platforms and prevent the monetization of official government statements.
