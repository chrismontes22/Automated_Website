Wall Street Skittish on Skydance as Stock Drops Further Post-Merger
============================================================

Skydance Corp. shares fell nearly 10% over two days following the completion of David Ellison’s $111 billion merger with Paramount and Warner Bros. Discovery amid investor skepticism.

### Key Points
- Skydance shares, trading under the ticker SKYD, closed at $8.89 on Wednesday after a two-day decline.
- The newly merged entity carries approximately $80 billion in debt, raising significant concerns regarding financial viability.
- Fitch Ratings downgraded the company’s credit rating, citing high leverage and risks associated with integrating the massive media assets.
- TD Cowen analyst Doug Creutz advised investors to hold shares, warning of potential execution failures common in large media mergers.
- The company will distribute 471.3 million warrants on October 13, allowing shareholders to purchase stock at a $12 strike price.

### Why it Matters
The market's negative reaction reflects deep-seated concerns about the company's ability to manage its massive debt load while navigating a struggling linear television landscape. Investors remain wary of whether management can successfully integrate these complex assets to create long-term value.
