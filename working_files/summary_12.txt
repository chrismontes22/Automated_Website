AT&T on iPhone 18 Pro Max Issues: 'We Are Working With Apple'
============================================================

AT&T and Apple are working to resolve persistent cellular connectivity issues affecting the iPhone 18 Pro Max, which frequently forces devices into SOS mode for many users.

### Key Points
- Affected iPhone 18 Pro Max users report intermittent or total loss of cellular data, text messaging, and voice calling capabilities.
- The connectivity problems have persisted since the device's September 18 launch, impacting thousands of customers across various online support forums.
- AT&T has advised customers to install the latest software updates, though users report that iOS 27.0.1 does not currently resolve the network connection failures.
- The U.S. version of the iPhone 18 Pro Max is unique in using a Qualcomm cellular modem, distinguishing it from other models using Apple's C2 modem.
- Customers experiencing ongoing issues are encouraged to visit Apple or AT&T retail locations for further support or potential device replacements.

### Why it Matters
This connectivity failure poses a significant risk to user safety by potentially preventing access to emergency 911 services. The situation highlights the challenges of integrating specific hardware components like Qualcomm modems into new flagship devices and underscores the importance of timely software patches for critical infrastructure.
