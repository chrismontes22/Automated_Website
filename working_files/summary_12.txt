GLO@23: The Bull still reigns supreme
============================================================

Billionaire entrepreneur Otunba Mike Adenuga Jr. celebrates 23 years of Globacom, the Nigerian telecommunications giant he maintains as a wholly indigenous, privately owned company since its 2003 launch.

### Key Points
- Globacom remains the only Nigerian telecommunications network to retain 100% sole ownership by its founder, Otunba Mike Adenuga Jr.
- The company disrupted the market at launch by introducing per-second billing, forcing competitors to abandon per-minute models.
- Adenuga self-funded the Glo-1 submarine cable, a 9,800-kilometer fiber-optic link connecting the United Kingdom directly to Nigeria.
- Globacom has served as a major corporate sponsor for African sports and entertainment, including the CAF African Footballer of the Year Awards.
- The company has successfully navigated technological transitions from 2G to 5G while maintaining its status as a top-tier regional telecom provider.

### Why it Matters
Globacom’s sustained independence demonstrates the viability of large-scale, indigenous-led infrastructure projects in the African telecommunications sector. By avoiding foreign equity and hostile takeovers, the company serves as a unique model for corporate sovereignty and long-term economic self-reliance.
