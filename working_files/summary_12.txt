Samsung unveils $800 Galaxy Book6 with 14" display and Intel Core 3/5 processors
============================================================

Samsung has expanded its laptop lineup with the new 14-inch Galaxy Book6, featuring Intel Core processors, a 25-hour battery life, and a starting price of $800.

### Key Points
- The 14-inch display features a 1,920 x 1,200 resolution with an anti-glare coating and 350 nits of brightness.
- Hardware configurations include Intel Core 3 or Core 5 processors, with RAM options ranging from 8GB to 16GB and storage up to 1TB.
- Connectivity includes two USB-C ports, one USB-A port, HDMI 1.4, Wi-Fi 6E, and Bluetooth 5.4.
- The device weighs 1.35kg, measures 15.7mm thick, and supports 45W fast charging for its 61.2Wh battery.
- Integrated software features include Quick Share, Second Screen, and AI-powered tools like Note Assist and AI Cut Out.
- The laptop is available in Gray and Violet Silver, with US retail availability beginning October 5.

### Why it Matters
This release provides a budget-friendly entry point into the Samsung ecosystem for users seeking a lightweight, portable Windows 11 machine. By integrating proprietary Galaxy features and AI tools, Samsung aims to strengthen its hardware ecosystem and compete in the mid-range laptop market.
