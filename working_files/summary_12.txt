How Long Do Owners Say Lenovo Laptops Last?
============================================================

Lenovo laptops generally maintain a reputation for durability, with premium ThinkPad models often lasting over a decade while budget-friendly series like IdeaPad and Yoga typically exceed five years.

### Key Points
- Lenovo ThinkPad laptops are noted for exceptional longevity, with some units remaining operational for up to 20 years due to modular component designs.
- Legion gaming laptops typically provide five years of service, though users frequently report battery degradation and the need for external cooling solutions.
- Budget-oriented models like the IdeaPad and ThinkBook are generally reliable, despite occasional user reports regarding hinge durability issues.
- Newer Lenovo models face criticism for soldered RAM, which limits the ability of owners to perform long-term hardware upgrades.
- Proper maintenance, including periodic cleaning to prevent overheating, is cited by owners as a critical factor in extending the lifespan of all Lenovo series.

### Why it Matters
Understanding the expected lifespan of various laptop series helps consumers make informed purchasing decisions based on their specific performance and longevity requirements. While premium models offer superior repairability, budget options remain viable for long-term use provided users account for potential hardware limitations.
