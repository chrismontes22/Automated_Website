Oppo’s AI Heart Ball is a tiny wearable that listens to your life and judges your decisions
============================================================

Oppo has launched the AI Heart Ball, a 9.8-gram wearable device designed to act as an AI growth coach by monitoring daily conversations and providing behavioral feedback.

### Key Points
- The device is available for trial in China for 499 yuan, approximately $75, with user sign-ups currently open.
- It features a 24-hour battery life and a microphone capable of capturing audio from up to 5 meters away.
- The wearable utilizes the PersonaX Memory Symbiosis Engine and O-Mind algorithms to analyze habits and offer actionable suggestions.
- Oppo is opening the device's MCP ecosystem to third-party developers to expand its functionality beyond initial capabilities.
- Privacy protections include end-to-end encryption and a policy stating that the company does not retain raw user recordings.

### Why it Matters
This launch signals Oppo’s strategic entry into the competitive AI wearable market by focusing on behavioral coaching rather than smartphone replacement. The device’s success will likely depend on whether consumers trust its privacy claims while integrating an always-listening assistant into their daily routines.
