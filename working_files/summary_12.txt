Former The O.C. actor on crusade to take down 'stupid' cryptocurrency
============================================================

Actor Ben McKenzie, known for his role in The OC, has transitioned into a vocal critic of the cryptocurrency industry, labeling it a massive Ponzi scheme in his documentary.

### Key Points
- Ben McKenzie, who holds an undergraduate degree in economics, testified before the US Senate regarding the risks of the cryptocurrency market.
- The actor produced and directed the documentary *Everyone Is Lying To You For Money* to expose what he describes as the industry's fraudulent nature.
- The film features an interview with Sam Bankman-Fried, the disgraced founder of the collapsed FTX exchange, which faced an $11 billion accounting deficit.
- McKenzie successfully "shorted" cryptocurrency during the 2022 market crash, using the profits to help fund his investigative film project.
- Despite McKenzie's warnings, Bitcoin continues to see high market valuations and remains a subject of intense debate regarding federal regulation and political support.

### Why it Matters
McKenzie’s public crusade highlights the ongoing tension between decentralized finance advocates and critics who fear the lack of institutional oversight. His work underscores the significant financial risks retail investors face when engaging with volatile digital assets that lack traditional consumer protections.
