This is how Xiaomi's 'smart privacy display' could work on its next-gen phones
============================================================

Xiaomi is reportedly developing a smart privacy display feature for its upcoming smartphones that automatically restricts screen visibility and alerts users when unauthorized individuals are detected watching.

### Key Points
- The feature, leaked by tipster Kacper on X, is expected to debut in the HyperOS 4.0 software skin.
- It automatically protects sensitive information like digital payments, login credentials, and text messages from side-angle viewing.
- Users can manually group specific apps under the privacy protection or rely on the system to trigger it for sensitive pages.
- The technology is rumored to launch alongside the Xiaomi 18 series, potentially arriving as early as October.
- Xiaomi’s implementation aims to compete with similar privacy display technology introduced in the Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra.

### Why it Matters
This development signals a growing industry trend toward integrating AI-driven privacy tools directly into mobile operating systems to combat visual data theft. If successfully implemented, this feature could provide a significant security advantage for users handling sensitive financial or personal information in public spaces.
