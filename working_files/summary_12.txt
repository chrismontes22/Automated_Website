Transcript: Som Seif, Purpose Unlimited
============================================================

Som Seif, founder and CEO of Purpose Unlimited, discusses his career as a financial innovator, his experience building ETF firms, and his mission to modernize investment management for clients.

### Key Points
- Som Seif founded Claymore Investments in 2005, which he later sold to BlackRock for $8 billion in 2012.
- He launched Purpose Investments in 2013, which now manages approximately $40 billion in assets.
- Seif co-founded the Canadian fintech platform Wealthsimple, which currently manages roughly $150 billion.
- He pioneered the world’s first Bitcoin ETF in 2021 and the Longevity Pension Fund, which utilizes risk pooling for lifetime income.
- His investment philosophy emphasizes behavioral science and outcome-oriented strategies over traditional market-cap-weighted benchmarks.

### Why it Matters
Seif’s career highlights the shift from traditional, bank-dominated financial services toward independent, technology-driven platforms that prioritize individual investor outcomes. By integrating behavioral finance and modern infrastructure, his firms challenge legacy models and provide retail investors with tools previously reserved for institutional clients.
