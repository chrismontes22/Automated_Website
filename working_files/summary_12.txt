MarketBeat Week in Review – 09/07 - 09/11
============================================================

Markets faced a volatile week as investors balanced strong corporate earnings from companies like Oracle against rising oil prices and anticipation of the Federal Reserve's upcoming interest rate decision.

### Key Points
- Oracle (ORCL) earnings reinforced the strength of the artificial intelligence infrastructure sector.
- Tesla (TSLA) shares remained under pressure following a Cybercab launch that lacked specific financial details.
- Palantir (PLTR) expanded its defense industry footprint through a new contract with the U.S. Army.
- Revolution Medicines (RVMD) saw stock volatility following FDA approval for its metastatic pancreatic cancer drug.
- Meta Platforms (META) reached an $18 billion settlement regarding a social media addiction trial.
- Railroad stocks emerged as a top-performing sector throughout the 2026 trading period.

### Why it Matters
Market participants are currently navigating a complex environment defined by shifting monetary policy and the transition of AI from speculative hype to tangible corporate growth. Understanding these specific sector catalysts is essential for investors looking to maintain portfolio stability ahead of the Federal Reserve's next policy announcement.
