XRP Ledger upgrade brings back features once pulled over critical bugs
============================================================

The upcoming xrpld 3.3.0 software release introduces five proposed amendments to the XRP Ledger, including revised versions of two features previously withdrawn due to critical security vulnerabilities.

### Key Points
- The xrpld 3.3.0 release includes five amendments: Batch, Permission Delegation, Confidential MPT, Sponsored Fees and Reserves, and Dynamic MPT.
- Batch allows up to eight cross-account transactions to execute atomically, while Permission Delegation enables institutions to grant scoped signing authority to other accounts.
- Previous versions of Batch and Permission Delegation were pulled after researchers identified flaws that could have allowed unauthorized transactions or fee draining.
- Confidential MPT utilizes zero-knowledge proofs and encryption to enable private tokenized-asset activity while maintaining auditability for regulators.
- Sponsored Fees and Reserves allows platforms to cover transaction costs for users, removing the requirement for individuals to hold XRP to transact.
- Amendments require 80% approval from network validators for two consecutive weeks before they are officially integrated into the XRP Ledger.

### Why it Matters
These updates aim to enhance the institutional utility of the XRP Ledger by improving transaction efficiency and lowering barriers to entry for new users. By addressing past security concerns, the release seeks to provide a more robust infrastructure for tokenized assets and decentralized financial services.
