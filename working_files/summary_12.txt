“Hand It Over”: Dad Demands Teenager Give Him the Free Laptop He Won for Top Grades
============================================================

A 16-year-old community college student is facing backlash from his father after refusing to surrender a 2024 Windows Pro Book he earned through high academic achievement.

### Key Points
- A 16-year-old student earned a 2024 Windows Pro Book by maintaining a 3.95 GPA at his community college.
- The student’s father demanded the laptop to sell it for profit, despite having recently purchased a separate device for his son.
- The student, who finished high school at 15, wishes to keep the laptop for academic use and personal sentimental value.
- The father claims ownership of the device based on his status as a parent and taxpayer, labeling his son as selfish for refusing to sell it.
- The dispute escalated into a 20-minute verbal confrontation, occurring on the eve of the student's college midterm exam.

### Why it Matters
This conflict highlights the complex boundaries of personal property and autonomy within parent-child relationships, particularly when academic achievements are involved. It serves as a case study on how differing values regarding financial independence and entitlement can create significant domestic tension.
