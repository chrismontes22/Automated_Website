China's exports surge as demand for high-tech, AI help prop up economic growth
============================================================

China’s export growth accelerated to 25% in August, driven by robust international demand for high-tech goods and AI-related products despite ongoing domestic economic challenges and weak consumer spending.

### Key Points
- China’s exports rose 25% year-on-year in August, while imports increased by 28.2% during the same period.
- High-tech product exports grew 42.9% in the first eight months of the year, with semiconductor values more than doubling.
- Car exports surged by over 50% in both volume and value, supported by strong demand for electric vehicles and lithium-ion batteries.
- The national trade surplus reached $119.09 billion in August, keeping the country on track for an annual surplus exceeding $1 trillion.
- Companies are accelerating shipments to the United States to preempt potential tariff increases amid ongoing global trade tensions.

### Why it Matters
This reliance on foreign demand highlights a significant imbalance as Beijing struggles to stimulate domestic consumption to meet its annual GDP growth targets. The heavy dependence on tech exports exposes the Chinese economy to potential trade curbs from the U.S. and the European Union.
