This $40 smart home display promises up to two years of battery life
============================================================

Seeed Studio has launched the OpenDisplay e-paper smart home dashboard series, offering two pre-assembled models that provide long-lasting battery performance and native integration with Home Assistant platforms.

### Key Points
- The 4.26-inch monochrome model costs $39.90 and offers up to two years of battery life with 15-minute update intervals.
- The 7.3-inch color model is priced at $75.90 and provides approximately one year of battery life with two-hour update intervals.
- Both devices feature Bluetooth Low Energy connectivity and arrive fully assembled with an enclosure, stand, battery, and preinstalled firmware.
- The monochrome unit utilizes an 800x400 e-paper panel powered by a 2,000 mAh battery and a Seeed XIAO EN05 board.
- These displays support partial refreshes to optimize power consumption for data like weather, sensor readings, and calendar schedules.

### Why it Matters
- These low-power displays offer a practical, maintenance-free alternative to traditional tablets for monitoring smart home data without requiring constant power connections. By providing pre-assembled kits, Seeed Studio lowers the barrier to entry for users seeking to integrate e-paper technology into their home automation setups.
