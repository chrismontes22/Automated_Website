An app store for your app store
============================================================

The third-party app store Aptoide is now available on the Google Play Store in the United States, marking a significant shift in Google's long-standing mobile app distribution policies.

### Key Points
- Aptoide, a games-focused app store, is now accessible via the Google Play Store following legal challenges from Epic Games.
- Developers face a significant barrier to entry, including an annual fee of at least $15,000 and additional review costs.
- The policy change could eventually allow major companies like Microsoft and Valve to offer exclusive titles or services directly on Android.
- Google is pursuing a separate strategy for international markets, with registered app stores expected to launch alongside Android 17 in September.

### Why it Matters
This development signals a major shift in the Android ecosystem, potentially breaking Google's long-standing control over app discovery and monetization. By allowing third-party stores to operate within its own platform, Google is opening the door for increased competition and new distribution models from major industry players.
