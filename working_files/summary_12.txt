Bezos-backed consortium buys a third of Liverpool
============================================================

Fenway Sports Group has agreed to sell a 30% minority stake in Liverpool FC to a consortium including Amazon founder Jeff Bezos, valuing the club at £5bn to £6bn.

### Key Points
- The investment group 1892 Holdings is led by businessman Amit Bhatia, who will join the Liverpool board as vice-chairman.
- Jeff Bezos is investing through his venture capital firm K5 Sports, marking his first entry into professional sports ownership.
- Facebook co-founder Eduardo Saverin is also part of the consortium, which holds an option to increase its stake in the future.
- Fenway Sports Group retains majority ownership and operational control of the club, which recently reported record revenues of £703m.
- The deal does not include a new transfer budget, and the club maintains that its approach to player recruitment remains unchanged.

### Why it Matters
This transaction highlights the growing trend of global technology billionaires securing stakes in elite European football clubs to leverage their massive commercial reach. While the deal provides significant capital for long-term growth, it has prompted concerns from fan groups regarding the influence of outside investors on the club's traditional values.
