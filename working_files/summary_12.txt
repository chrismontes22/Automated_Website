Google Wants AI Content Fact-Checked, Gemini UTM Tags – SEO Pulse via @sejournal, @MattGSouthern
============================================================

Google has updated its AI content guidelines to mandate manual fact-checking while navigating ongoing antitrust litigation and shifts in search click-through rates across desktop and mobile platforms.

### Key Points
- Google updated its generative AI guidance on October 1, requiring manual fact-checking for all content, including titles, meta descriptions, and image alt text.
- Gemini is now appending UTM parameters to outbound links, allowing website owners to track referral traffic more accurately through analytics platforms.
- Advanced Web Ranking data for Q2 2026 shows a 5.27 percentage point decline in desktop click-through rates for top positions, contrasted by a 6.59 point increase on mobile.
- Google has expanded its AI Mode information monitoring feature to all global users, allowing for continuous scanning of specific topics and shopping queries.
- U.S. District Judge Amit Mehta dismissed antitrust lawsuits from Penske Media and Chegg, ruling that the plaintiffs failed to prove Google maintained an illegal search monopoly.

### Why it Matters
These developments highlight the increasing complexity of managing digital presence as Google integrates generative AI deeper into its search infrastructure. Businesses must adapt their workflows to account for new metadata requirements and evolving referral tracking methods to maintain accurate performance data.
