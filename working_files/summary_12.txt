Clevetura S Keyboard Review: Swipe Across the Keys Instead of Reaching for a Mouse
============================================================

The Clevetura S is a compact, 78-key keyboard featuring integrated TouchOnKeys technology that allows users to control a cursor and perform gestures directly on the surface of the keys.

### Key Points
- The device features a 78-key layout with scissor switches, an anodized aluminum base, and per-key RGB lighting.
- It supports connectivity for up to four devices, including three Bluetooth channels and one USB-C wired connection.
- Users can customize gesture sensitivity and settings via the TouchOnKeys app, which includes an AI-driven training mode to distinguish typing from swiping.
- The keyboard measures 11.5 by 5.12 inches, weighs 403g, and is priced at $199.
- Battery life is rated for up to 44 hours of continuous use when backlighting is disabled.

### Why it Matters
By merging typing and navigation into a single footprint, the Clevetura S offers a space-saving solution for users working in cramped environments or on the go. While the premium price and learning curve may deter some, it provides a unique workflow efficiency for professionals who frequently switch between text entry and cursor control.
