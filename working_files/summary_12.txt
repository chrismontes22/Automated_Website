UGREEN’s 6-in-1 Steam Deck Dock Brings HDMI 2.1, Ethernet, and Charging to One Stand
============================================================

The UGREEN 6-in-1 Steam Deck Dock offers a foldable stand and multiple connectivity ports, including HDMI 2.1 and Gigabit Ethernet, to enhance handheld gaming setups for $35.99.

### Key Points
- Features HDMI 2.1 output supporting up to 4K at 120Hz, plus Gigabit Ethernet and three USB data ports.
- Compatible with Steam Deck, Steam Deck OLED, ROG Ally, Legion Go, MSI Claw, and other USB-C devices.
- Includes a foldable, adjustable aluminum and PC+ABS stand with a detachable 25cm USB-C host cable.
- Supports up to 100W power delivery input, providing up to 85W to the connected handheld device.
- Does not include a power adapter or charging cable, and is not compatible with Nintendo Switch consoles.

### Why it Matters
This accessory streamlines desktop or living-room gaming by consolidating charging, networking, and peripheral connectivity into a single, portable hub. It provides a practical solution for users looking to maximize their handheld's utility without significantly increasing their hardware footprint.
