Bitpanda fined €70,000 in Austria’s first published MiCA enforcement case
============================================================

Austria’s Financial Market Authority has issued a 70,000-euro fine to crypto broker Bitpanda for failing to comply with mandatory disclosure and white paper submission rules under MiCA regulations.

### Key Points
- The FMA penalty marks the first enforcement action taken under the European Union’s Markets in Crypto-Assets (MiCA) framework.
- Bitpanda failed to submit a required cryptocurrency white paper at least 20 working days before its publication.
- Marketing materials circulated by the firm omitted mandatory disclosures regarding regulatory approval status and essential contact information.
- Bitpanda characterized the violations as formal and timing-related, noting that all issues were corrected following a consensual conclusion with the regulator.
- The Vienna-based broker serves 7.4 million registered users and holds a MiCA license from Germany’s BaFin to operate across the European Economic Area.

### Why it Matters
This enforcement action signals that European regulators are actively monitoring compliance with the new MiCA standards to ensure transparency in crypto asset marketing. It serves as a precedent for how authorities will handle procedural lapses by major industry players as the EU enforces stricter oversight of digital asset providers.
