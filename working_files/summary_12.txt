Fox Boss Rob Wade Commits Long Term To Creator Studios: “We’re Not Here To Flip A House”
============================================================

Fox Entertainment CEO Rob Wade has committed to the long-term growth of Fox Creator Studios, rejecting short-term acquisition strategies to focus on building sustainable intellectual property with creators.

### Key Points
- Fox Creator Studios (FCS) is a digital-first division launched earlier this year to partner with creators across various genres and global platforms.
- The studio has secured partnerships with high-profile talent including Gordon Ramsay, comedian Tom Segura, and YouTube creator Josh Richards.
- Unlike competitors focused on subscriber acquisition, FCS provides capital, advertising infrastructure, and production support without requiring exclusive platform distribution.
- FCS leadership, including head Billy Parks, emphasizes a collaborative, non-traditional development process that avoids top-down studio mandates.
- The division aims to leverage Fox’s existing resources, such as its ad sales portfolio and global distribution arm, Fox Entertainment Global, to monetize content.

### Why it Matters
This strategy marks a shift in how traditional media companies engage with the creator economy by prioritizing long-term IP ownership over quick platform-exclusive deals. By offering infrastructure and financing without restrictive distribution requirements, Fox aims to become a permanent partner for creators rather than a temporary exit vehicle.
