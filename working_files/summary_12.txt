These are the first apps I install on every new Samsung phone
============================================================

Samsung Galaxy users can enhance their device experience by installing third-party applications that offer superior customization, cross-platform password management, and advanced privacy features beyond the default software offerings.

### Key Points
- Good Lock provides deep system customization, including widget resizing, status bar icon management, and advanced automation through the Routines+ module.
- The Standby Mode app transforms charging Galaxy devices into smart displays with photo slideshows, clock widgets, and built-in OLED burn-in protection.
- Proton Pass offers a cross-platform password manager with end-to-end encryption and email alias generation to improve security and reduce spam.
- Brave browser delivers faster page loading, native ad-blocking, and consistent cross-device syncing that outperforms the default Samsung Internet browser.

### Why it Matters
These third-party tools allow users to overcome the limitations of manufacturer-specific ecosystems and proprietary software lock-in. By integrating these applications, Galaxy owners can achieve a more personalized, secure, and consistent digital experience across all their hardware devices.
