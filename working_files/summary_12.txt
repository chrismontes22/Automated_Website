"Melt up"?
============================================================

The Nasdaq 100 index surged by $3.5 trillion in market capitalization over four days, marking a 9.3% rally fueled by strong technology sector earnings and optimistic artificial intelligence projections.

### Key Points
- The Nasdaq 100 experienced its sharpest four-day rally since April 2025.
- Financial analysts describe this rapid, unexpected price increase as a "melt-up."
- Strong corporate earnings reports have significantly bolstered investor confidence in the long-term outlook for artificial intelligence.
- The term "melt-up" serves as a market-specific antonym to "meltdown," reflecting a sudden and dramatic gain in asset value.

### Why it Matters
This rapid market expansion highlights the continued sensitivity of major indices to positive sentiment surrounding artificial intelligence investments. Such volatility underscores how quickly investor confidence can shift, potentially influencing broader economic trends and future capital allocation strategies in the technology sector.
