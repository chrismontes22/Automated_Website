AI Just Broke Vendor Lock-in. Here’s Why That’s a Big Opportunity for Small Businesses.
============================================================

Artificial intelligence is reducing technical barriers and migration costs, effectively ending the era of vendor lock-in for enterprise software and creating new opportunities for smaller, agile competitors.

### Key Points
- AI tools now automate complex tasks like translating legacy COBOL code into modern languages such as Java.
- Businesses can leverage AI to scope out migration costs, making it easier to switch from entrenched, "sticky" software providers.
- Experts recommend that companies request full data exports in open formats before contract renewals to increase their negotiating leverage.
- Smaller software vendors are now using AI to handle the migration process, removing the primary barrier that previously prevented clients from switching providers.
- Legacy systems, such as banking mainframes, are becoming vulnerable to disruption as the technical effort required to move data and integrations decreases.

### Why it Matters
The decline of vendor lock-in shifts power from established enterprise incumbents to smaller, more specialized software providers. Businesses that proactively evaluate their exit options can significantly improve their bargaining position and modernize their technology stacks more affordably.
