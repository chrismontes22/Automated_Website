The Snapdragon X2 Elite Extreme beats Intel's flagship Panther Lake chip by up to 87% while costing significantly less, according to a new lab report
============================================================

Independent testing by Signal65 confirms that Qualcomm’s Snapdragon X2 Elite Extreme processor significantly outperforms competing Intel and AMD laptop chips in both CPU and AI-focused benchmark performance tests.

### Key Points
- The Snapdragon X2 Elite Extreme outperformed the Intel Core Ultra X9 388H and AMD Ryzen AI 9 465 in Geekbench 7 and Cinebench 2026 testing.
- Qualcomm’s Hexagon NPU delivers 80 TOPS, achieving up to 2.4 times the AI performance of competing chips in Procyon Computer Vision tests.
- Testing demonstrated that the Snapdragon chip maintains superior performance on battery power compared to Intel and AMD systems running on AC power.
- The study utilized retail laptops, including the ASUS Zenbook A16, MSI Prestige 16, and HP OmniBook X 16, to ensure real-world hardware relevance.
- While Qualcomm leads in processing and AI, Intel’s integrated Arc B390 graphics still outperformed the Snapdragon platform in specific graphical benchmarks.

### Why it Matters
These results signal a major shift in the Windows laptop market as Qualcomm’s ARM-based architecture challenges the long-standing dominance of x86 processors from Intel and AMD. By delivering high performance and competitive pricing, the Snapdragon X2 platform is effectively narrowing the gap between Windows devices and Apple’s silicon-based hardware.
