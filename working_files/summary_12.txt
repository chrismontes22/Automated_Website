This free AI music detector will scan through your playlists for audio slop
============================================================

Music streaming service Deezer has launched a free AI detection tool that scans playlists across 20 platforms to identify and label fully synthetic tracks with 99.8 percent accuracy.

### Key Points
- The tool analyzes audio signals for specific artifacts left by generative software to distinguish between human and AI-created music.
- Users can scan up to 100 playlists across various streaming services, including Spotify and YouTube Music, without needing a Deezer account.
- Deezer reports that approximately 44 percent of daily uploads to its platform are AI-generated, though these account for only 1-3 percent of total streams.
- Research indicates that 97 percent of listeners cannot reliably differentiate between human-made music and AI-generated tracks in blind tests.
- The scanner does not identify specific songs as AI-generated but provides an overall percentage of synthetic content found within a user's library.

### Why it Matters
This tool addresses growing consumer demand for transparency regarding the authenticity of digital media in an era of rapid AI content proliferation. By providing a way to identify synthetic audio, Deezer helps listeners maintain control over their personal libraries while highlighting the prevalence of AI-generated content in the music industry.
