(PR) AMD to Buy World Labs for $8.2 Billion to Boost 3D Simulation and Robotics Strategy
============================================================

AMD has entered a definitive agreement to acquire San Francisco-based AI research lab World Labs for $8.2 billion to bolster its hardware and software infrastructure development.

### Key Points
- AMD will acquire World Labs, an AI research firm specializing in spatial intelligence and 3D environment simulation, in an all-stock transaction.
- World Labs co-founder Dr. Fei-Fei Li will join AMD as executive vice president and chief scientist, reporting directly to CEO Dr. Lisa Su.
- The acquisition aims to integrate World Labs' expertise in robotics and physical AI into AMD’s future compute platform roadmaps.
- The deal is valued at approximately $8.2 billion and is expected to close by the end of 2026, pending regulatory approval.

### Why it Matters
This acquisition signals AMD's strategic shift toward aligning its hardware development more closely with the evolving requirements of advanced AI models. By securing top-tier research talent, the company aims to strengthen its competitive position against rivals in the rapidly expanding fields of robotics and spatial computing.
