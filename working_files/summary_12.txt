Live updates: Bitcoin holds above $81,000 as the AI rout fades and oil climbs
============================================================

Bitcoin stabilized near $81,500 on Friday following a period of high volatility, while broader financial markets monitored rising oil prices and upcoming Federal Reserve policy discussions.

### Key Points
- Bitcoin traded within a narrow $1,000 range after dropping below the $82,500 support level on Thursday.
- Altcoin performance remained mixed, with BNB rising 2% while XMR and ZEC experienced notable declines.
- Nasdaq 100 futures showed little movement following the index's worst single-day performance since June.
- Brent crude oil reached its highest price point since early May, raising concerns regarding future inflation.
- SpaceX shares climbed amid reports of the company's potential expansion into the wireless carrier market.

### Why it Matters
Rising oil prices complicate the inflation outlook ahead of the Federal Reserve's meeting scheduled for October 27–28. This economic uncertainty influences investor sentiment across both cryptocurrency and traditional equity markets.
