GTA 6 leaker CyberLeek is now reportedly threatening to release a full, playable build of the game
============================================================

A leaker known as CyberLeek has threatened to release a full, playable build of Rockstar Games' upcoming title GTA 6 if their cryptocurrency reaches a $30 million valuation.

### Key Points
- CyberLeek released 25 minutes of legitimate GTA 6 footage, including scenes featuring protagonists Jason and Lucia.
- The leaker demands a $30 million market cap for their cryptocurrency in exchange for releasing the complete story mode.
- Rockstar Games parent company Take-Two Interactive is actively seeking the leaker's identity and has attempted to subpoena Discord.
- Take-Two is offered an alternative to the leak if the company issues a public apology and releases physical game disks.
- GTA 6 is officially scheduled for release on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S on November 19.

### Why it Matters
These leaks represent a significant security breach for Rockstar Games and threaten the controlled marketing rollout of one of the industry's most anticipated titles. The situation highlights the growing risks of extortion-based cyberattacks targeting major intellectual property and the challenges companies face in protecting unreleased software.
