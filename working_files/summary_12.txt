What Is a Smart Lock? Why I Recommend This Security Upgrade for Everyone
============================================================

Smart locks offer homeowners enhanced security and convenience by replacing traditional deadbolts with app-controlled, keyless entry systems that support remote access, temporary guest codes, and biometric authentication methods.

### Key Points
- Smart locks connect to Wi-Fi, allowing users to lock or unlock doors remotely and receive real-time activity alerts via smartphone applications.
- Access methods include fingerprint scanners, digital keypads, and mobile wallet integration through the Aliro protocol for Apple, Google, and Samsung devices.
- Installation options range from full deadbolt replacements to retrofit models that attach to existing hardware, typically requiring only a screwdriver.
- Battery life generally spans several months to a year, with some high-capacity models like the Lockly Lyro Lasta lasting up to three years.
- Pricing varies from budget-friendly $70 retrofit units to premium $300+ models from established brands like Schlage, Yale, Level, and August.

### Why it Matters
Smart locks represent a foundational upgrade for modern home automation, providing significant improvements in security management and accessibility for residents. By integrating with major platforms like Apple Home and Google Home, these devices offer a scalable entry point for users looking to modernize their home security infrastructure.
