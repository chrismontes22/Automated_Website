Vultr targets open composable stacks and AMD partnership to lead cloud AI infrastructure
============================================================

Vultr is expanding its global cloud AI infrastructure by leveraging a strategic partnership with AMD to provide high-performance, cost-effective, and sovereign-compliant computing solutions for enterprise customers worldwide.

### Key Points
- Vultr utilizes AMD EPYC CPUs and Instinct GPUs to deliver up to 33% better performance at 82% lower costs than major hyperscalers.
- The company operates a distributed network across 33 global regions to support low-latency AI inference requirements.
- A new prepackaged open composable stack, developed with VAST Data, SUSE, and AMD, allows for rapid deployment in sectors like healthcare and finance.
- Vultr emphasizes sovereign AI compliance, positioning its infrastructure as a critical utility for nations requiring localized data control.

### Why it Matters
By prioritizing open, composable architectures over proprietary lock-in, Vultr is challenging traditional hyperscalers to capture the growing market for decentralized AI inference. This strategy provides enterprises with the flexibility and regional compliance necessary to scale AI agents while maintaining predictable long-term infrastructure costs.
