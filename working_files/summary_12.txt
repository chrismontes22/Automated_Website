A New CTO, a Nasdaq Move and AI Agents: Hilton Makes Its Tech Pitch
============================================================

Hilton Worldwide is repositioning itself as a technology-focused hospitality leader by appointing a new CTO, transitioning its stock listing to the Nasdaq, and integrating emerging AI agent platforms.

### Key Points
- Hilton appointed former Uber engineering leader Jeff Moore as its new chief technology officer to oversee global digital strategy.
- The company is transitioning its stock listing from the New York Stock Exchange to the Nasdaq to align with major technology firms.
- CEO Christopher Nassetta confirmed that Hilton is actively integrating its booking systems with various AI-driven travel agents.
- These strategic shifts follow a third-quarter earnings report emphasizing the company's "people-led, tech-powered" operational model.

### Why it Matters
This strategic pivot signals Hilton's intent to be valued by investors as a technology-forward company rather than a traditional hospitality firm. By aligning with the Nasdaq and prioritizing AI integration, the hotel giant aims to capture greater market share through improved digital efficiency and automated customer engagement.
