What to expect during the Supermicro Open Storage Summit series: Join theCUBE Aug. 11-Sept. 3
============================================================

The seventh annual Supermicro Open Storage Summit, hosted by theCUBE from August 11 to September 3, explores critical infrastructure advancements required to support enterprise AI and agentic computing workloads.

### Key Points
- The event features 12 sessions with 38 industry leaders from 21 companies, including AMD, Intel, Nutanix, and Vast Data.
- Discussions focus on the shift toward agentic AI, which requires new storage architectures like context memory and KV caches for active reasoning.
- Supermicro recently introduced its H15 server portfolio, utilizing sixth-generation AMD EPYC 9006 Series processors to optimize CPU and GPU orchestration.
- MinIO’s AIStor Memory platform is highlighted as a tool for preserving persistent agent memory and organizational knowledge during multi-step tasks.
- Supermicro reported over $60 billion in new orders during its fiscal fourth quarter, driven by rapid enterprise demand for AI-ready compute infrastructure.

### Why it Matters
The rapid transition to agentic AI is forcing a fundamental redesign of data center infrastructure, moving beyond simple storage toward active, high-performance systems. This shift highlights that competitive advantage in the AI era depends on the efficient integration of CPUs, GPUs, and specialized memory architectures.
