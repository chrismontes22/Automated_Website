I could never leave Samsung for a Pixel or iPhone — these 3 features are why
============================================================

Samsung’s One UI software offers unique productivity and security features that keep power users loyal to Galaxy devices despite the minimalist appeal of competing Android and iOS platforms.

### Key Points
- Secure Folder provides a hardware-encrypted sandbox for sensitive apps and files, isolating data from the main operating system.
- The Good Lock suite allows deep system customization, including per-app volume control via SoundAssistant and advanced gesture navigation.
- Modes and Routines enables complex, silent automation for system settings without requiring coding or third-party software.
- Samsung DeX and S Pen integration offer desktop-like functionality and productivity tools not found on standard Pixel or iPhone devices.
- The upcoming Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra features a 6.9-inch Dynamic Super AMOLED 2X display and up to 16GB of RAM.

### Why it Matters
These specialized software tools provide a level of granular control and automation that currently exceeds the capabilities of stock Android and iOS. For power users, these features translate into significant time savings and enhanced security that make switching to other smartphone ecosystems feel like a functional downgrade.
