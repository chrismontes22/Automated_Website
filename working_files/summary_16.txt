NBA legend Shaquille O’Neal teams up with NordVPN, taking over NYC to raise awareness of digital threats
============================================================

NordVPN partnered with NBA legend Shaquille O’Neal at a Times Square event to promote digital security awareness and highlight the company's transition into a comprehensive cybersecurity suite.

### Key Points
- Shaquille O’Neal has served as the official brand ambassador for NordVPN since September to help educate the public on digital threats.
- The company’s Next Gen Antivirus technology scans 12 million URLs daily and blocks approximately 130,000 malicious pages.
- NordVPN reported blocking nearly 5 million malware attempts in the United States during the first few months of 2026.
- Recent research indicates that 74% of Americans cannot identify fake URLs, and 73% have already fallen victim to online scams.
- Over half of surveyed users continue to reuse passwords across multiple accounts, despite the rising sophistication of phishing and malware attacks.

### Why it Matters
This campaign highlights a significant gap between consumer confidence in digital literacy and the actual prevalence of cyber threats. By leveraging high-profile partnerships, NordVPN aims to shift market perception from simple VPN services toward integrated security tools that mitigate risks for vulnerable users.
