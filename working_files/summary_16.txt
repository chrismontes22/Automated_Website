Optable’s Agentic Marketplace Bets That AI Agents Can Return More Dollars To Working Media
============================================================

Optable unveiled its new Agentic Marketplace on October 8, 2026, using AI agents to automate direct advertising deals and reduce reliance on costly, complex programmatic supply chain intermediaries.

### Key Points
- The Agentic Marketplace enables buyer and seller AI agents to automate the discovery, packaging, and purchasing of publisher inventory.
- Optable aims to reduce the "ad tech tax," noting that approximately 41% of U.S. real-time bidding display spending currently goes toward intermediary fees.
- The platform allows publishers to maintain control over pricing and buyer eligibility while scaling customized deals that were previously too manual to execute.
- Goodway Group reported that the technology reduced the time required to move a campaign from initial briefing to quality assurance to just 30 minutes.
- Emarketer projects the agentic media buying market will grow from $100 million in 2026 to $29.5 billion by 2028.

### Why it Matters
This shift toward agentic advertising seeks to restore the value of direct publisher-advertiser relationships by removing the manual labor that historically limited customized media deals. By prioritizing software-based service fees over percentage-based transaction taxes, the model could significantly increase the portion of advertising budgets that reach actual working media.
