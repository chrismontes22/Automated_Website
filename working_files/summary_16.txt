Google illegally retains customer data,and I am taking legal action against them
============================================================

A user is initiating legal action against Google, alleging that the company intentionally retains AI Studio prompt data on its servers despite claims that it is permanently deleted.

### Key Points
- The user claims Google AI Studio only deletes a JSON "pointer" file in Google Drive rather than the actual prompt data stored on backend servers.
- Evidence provided includes the ability to restore deleted chats using a Google Drive recovery tool, which re-links to the original, un-deleted server-side session.
- The user argues that Google’s interface labels, such as "Your prompt will be permanently deleted after 30 days," are intentionally deceptive.
- Forum discussions from 2025, including comments from a Google developer, suggest that chat metadata and server-side prompts may persist even when the UI indicates deletion.
- The user has documented these findings through video evidence and correspondence sent to Google’s corporate offices.

### Why it Matters
This dispute highlights significant concerns regarding data privacy and the transparency of deletion processes in generative AI platforms. If proven, the intentional retention of user data despite explicit deletion promises could lead to serious legal and regulatory consequences for Google.
