Your Roku could be stuck at 1080p and you’d never know
============================================================

Roku users can significantly improve their streaming experience and device performance by manually adjusting hidden settings rather than relying on the default factory configurations provided by the manufacturer.

### Key Points
- Users can navigate to the Settings menu to disable activity tracking, hide content recommendations, and stop home screen video auto-play.
- Adjusting the "Display type" in settings allows users to match the Roku output to their specific TV resolution, such as 720p, 1080p, or 4K HDR.
- Advanced display settings enable manual refresh rate adjustments to resolve picture quality issues during playback.
- Customization options extend to subtitle appearance and power-saving modes to optimize the device for individual preferences.
- The 2024 Roku Ultra features hardware upgrades including Wi-Fi 6 connectivity, a faster processor, and a backlit voice remote.

### Why it Matters
Default settings often prioritize convenience over performance, potentially limiting the visual quality or privacy of the streaming experience. Taking control of these configurations ensures that users maximize their hardware capabilities while managing bandwidth consumption effectively.
