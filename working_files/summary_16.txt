Pharos brings AI agents onchain with an Agent Native upgrade for RealFi
============================================================

Pharos Network has unveiled an Agent Native upgrade at Korea Blockchain Week, introducing protocol-level infrastructure designed to enable AI agents to function as independent participants in onchain finance.

### Key Points
- The upgrade introduces sponsored transactions, allowing third parties to cover gas fees while maintaining the original sender's identity.
- The Pharos Port Agent Widget enables natural-language interactions for users to manage real-world assets, vault maturity, and yield positions.
- Developers can access the new AI portal at ai.pharos.xyz, which hosts the SkillEngine, Developer Docs MCP, and Pharos Chain MCP.
- A partnership with Anvita Flow provides the AnvitaMate tool, allowing developers to build on Pharos within their local AI environments.
- The company’s multi-year roadmap includes launching PoS staking in 2026 and achieving full end-to-end Agent-Native workflows by 2028.

### Why it Matters
This infrastructure shift aims to transition AI agents from simple chatbots into autonomous economic actors capable of holding assets and executing complex financial transactions. By integrating these capabilities at the protocol level, Pharos seeks to bridge the gap between offchain AI utility and onchain financial operations.
