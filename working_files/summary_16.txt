Meta and Microsoft Limit Employee Use of Claude AI Tools
============================================================

Meta and Microsoft are reducing internal employee reliance on Anthropic’s Claude AI, shifting focus toward proprietary coding tools to better manage operational expenditures and internal development resources.

### Key Points
- Microsoft slashed monthly AI spending limits for employees from $100,000 to $10,000 to prioritize internal tools like GitHub Copilot and OpenAI frameworks.
- Meta’s internal user base for Claude Code dropped from 60,000 to 30,000 as employees transitioned to proprietary solutions MetaCode and Muse Code.
- Despite internal usage cuts, both companies remain significant clients of Anthropic, with client-facing access to Claude models remaining unaffected.
- MetaCode currently supports over 30,000 internal users, while Muse Code has surpassed 6,000 users following its August external testing phase.
- The shift reflects a broader corporate strategy to control developer resources and manage the rising costs of integrating AI into software operations.

### Why it Matters
This transition highlights a growing trend among major tech firms to balance the high costs of AI integration with the need for proprietary control over development environments. While these companies continue to support Anthropic commercially, the move signals an intensifying competitive landscape where internal efficiency and security take precedence over third-party tool flexibility.
