The Dawn of a New Cold War
============================================================

The United States and China are locked in a strategic competition defined by a critical divide, where China controls essential rare earth minerals while America leads in advanced semiconductor technology.

### Key Points
- China dominates the global supply chain for rare earth elements, controlling over 60% of extraction and 90% of processing capacity.
- The United States maintains a technological edge in artificial intelligence hardware, primarily through the design of sophisticated Nvidia semiconductors.
- U.S. efforts to re-shore chip manufacturing via the CHIPS Act face significant environmental and logistical hurdles, including water scarcity and chemical pollution.
- China is aggressively pursuing domestic chip independence, recently demonstrating the ability to produce competitive AI models using its own hardware.
- Both nations have implemented retaliatory trade measures, including export controls and blacklists, targeting critical minerals and dual-use technology.

### Why it Matters
This geopolitical tug-of-war creates a fragile global economic environment where supply chain disruptions for minerals or chips could trigger widespread instability. As both nations prioritize national industrial autonomy over cooperation, the resulting friction threatens to hinder global progress on shared challenges like climate change.
