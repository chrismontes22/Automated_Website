NAKIVO Backup & Replication v11.2.2: Full vSphere 9.1 + Proxmox VE 9.1/9.2 Support (and the Practical Bits That Matter)
============================================================

NAKIVO Backup & Replication version 11.2.2 introduces full support for VMware vSphere 9.1 and Proxmox VE 9.1 and 9.2, ensuring seamless data protection during critical platform infrastructure upgrades.

### Key Points
- Adds full support for VMware vSphere 9.1, including Real-Time Replication for near-zero data loss.
- Provides full agentless backup and replication support for Proxmox VE 9.1 and 9.2.
- Expands physical machine OS support to include Debian 13, RHEL 10, Ubuntu 25.10, and Windows 11 26H1.
- Simplifies NAS licensing with new TB-based subscription SKUs and adds deployment support for Buffalo NAS devices.
- Includes critical bug fixes and reliability improvements for job scheduling and configuration management.

### Why it Matters
This update allows IT administrators to maintain consistent backup workflows while upgrading to the latest hypervisor versions. By supporting both VMware and Proxmox in a single console, the release reduces management complexity for organizations operating heterogeneous or migrating virtual environments.
