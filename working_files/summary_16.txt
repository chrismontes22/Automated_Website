How to Check and Update Your BIOS Version in Windows 11 (Dell, HP, Lenovo, ASUS)
============================================================

Updating your PC's BIOS firmware requires careful preparation, including verifying your current version and suspending BitLocker, to ensure system stability and prevent potential boot failures during the installation process.

### Key Points
- Use the `msinfo32` command or PowerShell to identify your current BIOS version and exact hardware model.
- Only update your BIOS if the manufacturer’s release notes address a specific issue, provide a critical security patch, or support new hardware.
- Always connect your PC to a stable power source or AC adapter before beginning the update to prevent power-related interruptions.
- Suspend BitLocker drive encryption before flashing the firmware to avoid being locked out of your system after the update.
- Download firmware files exclusively from official manufacturer support sites for Dell, HP, Lenovo, ASUS, or your specific motherboard vendor.
- Allow the update process to complete fully without touching the power button, even if the screen remains black or appears frozen.

### Why it Matters
Performing a BIOS update is a high-stakes procedure that carries the risk of rendering a computer unbootable if interrupted or performed incorrectly. Following these standardized safety protocols protects your data and ensures that critical firmware updates are applied only when necessary for system health or security.
