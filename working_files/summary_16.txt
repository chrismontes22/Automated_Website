When Will Apple Announce the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone Ultra Event?
============================================================

Apple is expected to host its annual September event to unveil the new iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max, and the foldable iPhone Ultra smartphone models.

### Key Points
- Apple is projected to hold its next iPhone launch event on September 8 or September 9, 2026.
- The company typically provides two weeks' notice, suggesting an official announcement date of August 25 or August 26.
- The upcoming lineup will feature the iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max, and the new foldable iPhone Ultra.
- Lower-end models, including the iPhone 18, iPhone 18e, and iPhone Air 2, are reportedly delayed until March 2027.
- Recent launch events have utilized pre-recorded videos shown at the Steve Jobs Theater, followed by hands-on sessions for invited reporters.

### Why it Matters
This shift toward a split-launch strategy marks a significant change in Apple's traditional annual release cycle for its flagship hardware. By separating the premium and entry-level models, the company may be attempting to better manage supply chain demands and differentiate its high-end product tiers.
