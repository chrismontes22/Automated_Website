What AI-generated malware looks like under the microscope
============================================================

Lindsay Kaye of HUMAN Security explains how AI-generated malware exhibits distinct characteristics like structured comments and professional logging, while significantly accelerating the speed and scale of cyberattacks.

### Key Points
- AI-generated scripts often feature uniform, well-structured comments and professional logging messages that are atypical for traditional malware authors.
- Threat actors are using AI to rapidly scale advertising fraud, increasing the volume of malicious mobile apps and websites from dozens to hundreds.
- AI tools assist criminals in processing large datasets to identify valuable information, such as session cookies and credentials, for further exploitation.
- Automated agents are now being used to verify stolen credit card data, increasing the market value of compromised financial information.
- Security teams face a shrinking patch window as AI enables attackers to develop and deploy exploits for newly disclosed vulnerabilities much faster than before.

### Why it Matters
The integration of AI into the cybercrime ecosystem is forcing security professionals to rethink traditional defense strategies and incident response timelines. As attackers leverage automation to increase the speed and scale of their operations, organizations must prioritize human-led analysis to validate findings and mitigate risks effectively.
