How ‘Unmasking a Monster: Aileen Wuornos’ Used AI to Bring a Serial Killer Back to Life
============================================================

The new Investigation Discovery documentary series Unmasking a Monster utilizes advanced generative AI to create digital masks over actors, aiming to provide a more authentic visual experience for viewers.

### Key Points
- The series, now streaming on HBO Max, is the first documentary to use AI-aided visual effects to recreate scenes from the life of serial killer Aileen Wuornos.
- Arrow Media developed the "digital mask" technology, which overlays AI-enhanced imagery onto human performances to simulate archival footage.
- Producers required written consent from all living individuals whose likenesses were digitally recreated for the series.
- The production team emphasized that all scripts were written by humans based on court documents, transcripts, and original interviews.
- Actress Morag Peacock provided the physical performance for the Wuornos reenactments, with her own eyes and micro-expressions preserved under the digital layer.

### Why it Matters
This production signals a significant shift in documentary filmmaking by using generative AI to replace traditional, often criticized, reenactment methods. By framing the technology as a modern evolution of prosthetic makeup, the creators are testing audience acceptance of synthetic media in factual storytelling.
