Now in beta: Import and convert your PowerPoint files to client-side encrypted Slides
============================================================

Google Workspace has launched a beta program allowing eligible enterprise and education customers to import and convert PowerPoint files into client-side encrypted Google Slides for secure editing.

### Key Points
- The CSE Office Interop beta enables high-fidelity conversion of encrypted .pptx files into Google Slides.
- Data is encrypted on the client side before being imported into the Google Workspace environment.
- Eligible editions include Enterprise Plus, Education Standard, Education Plus, Frontline Plus, and Assured Controls.
- Workspace administrators must apply for the beta program to enable these features across their domains.
- The update improves interoperability between Microsoft Office formats and Google’s secure document ecosystem.

### Why it Matters
This update bridges a critical security gap for organizations that rely on both Microsoft Office and Google Workspace for sensitive document management. By enabling secure file conversion, businesses can maintain strict data privacy standards without sacrificing the ability to collaborate on presentations across different software platforms.
