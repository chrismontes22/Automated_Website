Crypto market maker Wintermute lands SEC approval to trade equities and ETF blocks
============================================================

Crypto market maker Wintermute has secured U.S. broker-dealer status, allowing the firm to trade stocks and provide liquidity for exchange-traded funds as it expands into traditional financial markets.

### Key Points
- Wintermute USA LLC officially registered with the SEC and joined FINRA to operate as a proprietary trading firm.
- The firm is now authorized to trade U.S. stocks and equity options while acting as an authorized participant for various ETFs.
- Wintermute plans to leverage its new status to provide liquidity on major exchanges, including the New York Stock Exchange and Nasdaq.
- The company currently manages over $10 billion in average daily trading volume across more than 60 centralized and decentralized venues.
- This move aligns Wintermute with other crypto firms like Ripple and GSR that are actively integrating into regulated U.S. securities markets.

### Why it Matters
- This regulatory milestone signals a deepening convergence between the cryptocurrency sector and traditional Wall Street infrastructure. By gaining broker-dealer status, Wintermute can bridge digital asset expertise with institutional equity markets, potentially increasing liquidity and efficiency for crypto-linked financial products.
