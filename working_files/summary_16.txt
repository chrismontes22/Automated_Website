DuRoBo Krono: The Pocket-Sized Android E-Ink Hub That Dials Down Distraction and Powers Up Focus
============================================================

The DuRoBo Krono is a new $279.99 pocket-sized Android 15 e-reader featuring a 6.13-inch E Ink display and a unique mechanical Smart Dial for distraction-free productivity and note-taking.

### Key Points
- The device runs on an octa-core processor with 6 GB of RAM and 128 GB of storage, supporting full Google Play Store access.
- Its 6.13-inch E Ink Carta 1200 display offers a 300 PPI resolution and an adjustable dual-tone frontlight for various lighting conditions.
- The physical Smart Dial provides tactile control for page turning, brightness adjustment, and launching the Spark voice-to-text capture tool.
- Integrated AI features include the Libby AI assistant for quick lookups and automated summaries for recorded voice notes.
- The hardware includes an Axis lighting system with rear-facing "breathing lights" designed to help users maintain focus during reading sessions.

### Why it Matters
The Krono addresses the growing market demand for "distraction-light" technology by offering a dedicated, pocketable alternative to smartphones for readers and knowledge workers. By combining an open Android ecosystem with tactile hardware controls, it provides a versatile tool for users looking to consolidate their reading and note-taking habits.
