As Anthropic’s IPO approaches, CFO Krishna Rao faces a defining test
============================================================

AI developer Anthropic has filed for a Nasdaq initial public offering under the ticker ANT, reporting significant revenue growth alongside substantial infrastructure costs and net losses.

### Key Points
- Anthropic reported $16.29 billion in revenue for the first half of 2026, a sharp increase from $1.16 billion during the same period last year.
- The company recorded a net loss of $80.75 billion, largely driven by an accounting charge related to the fair value of financing instruments.
- Infrastructure and compute expenses reached $8.95 billion in the first half of 2026 as the firm scales its Claude AI model family.
- As of September 30, the company served over 11,900 customers generating at least $100,000 in annual revenue.
- Anthropic currently maintains a valuation of $965 billion following a $65 billion funding round completed in May.

### Why it Matters
This IPO serves as a critical test for the AI sector, highlighting the tension between rapid revenue scaling and the massive capital requirements of high-performance computing. Investors will closely monitor whether Anthropic can achieve sustainable profitability while managing intense competitive pressures and significant infrastructure commitments.
