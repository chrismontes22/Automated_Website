'The best Android phone for students': why I decided to buy the Google Pixel 10a ahead of the Pixel 11 launch
============================================================

Amazon has discounted the Google Pixel 10a to $424, offering a 15% price reduction on the 128GB model just ahead of the upcoming Google Pixel 11 series launch.

### Key Points
- Amazon is selling the 128GB Google Pixel 10a for $424, down from its original $499 retail price.
- The device features a 6.3-inch pOLED display, the Tensor G4 chipset, and IP68 water and dust resistance.
- Google provides seven years of guaranteed operating system and security updates for the handset.
- The phone includes the same primary and ultrawide camera lenses found in the flagship Google Pixel 10.
- The Google Pixel 11 series is scheduled for an official unveiling on August 12, 2026.

### Why it Matters
This discount provides a cost-effective alternative for students and budget-conscious consumers before the more expensive Pixel 11 flagship arrives. By offering a significant price cut on a high-performing midrange device, Amazon creates a competitive option for those prioritizing value over the latest hardware releases.
