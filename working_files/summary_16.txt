OpenAI's ChatGPT Speaker Will Be Hockey Puck-Sized and Cost Over $300
============================================================

OpenAI is developing a portable, doughnut-shaped AI smart speaker designed by Jony Ive that features motorized components and advanced voice-based interaction capabilities for a premium user experience.

### Key Points
- The device is a battery-powered, hockey-puck-sized speaker featuring a premium metal finish and no display.
- Former Apple design chief Jony Ive is leading the hardware development following OpenAI's acquisition of his startup, io.
- Integrated sensors and cameras allow the device to analyze the user's environment to provide personalized, human-like ChatGPT responses.
- The product is expected to retail between $300 and $400, with a potential introduction occurring later this year.
- OpenAI faces allegations from Apple regarding the misuse of trade secrets related to metal finishing techniques used in the device's construction.

### Why it Matters
This hardware represents OpenAI's first major foray into physical consumer electronics, signaling a strategic shift toward integrating AI directly into portable home devices. The project highlights intensifying competition between OpenAI and Apple as both companies race to define the next generation of AI-driven home technology.
