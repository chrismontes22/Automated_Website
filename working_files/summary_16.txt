What Bernie Sanders and Steve Bannon Have in Common
============================================================

The Future of Life Institute’s “Pro-Human Assembly” in Washington, D.C., highlighted growing bipartisan interest in regulating artificial intelligence amid concerns over industry influence and potential superintelligence risks.

### Key Points
- Senator Bernie Sanders and Representative Greg Casar announced upcoming legislation to ban the development of artificial superintelligence.
- The conference featured diverse speakers, including Steve Bannon and Ashley Judd, discussing the risks posed by AI industry oligarchs.
- Representative Lori Trahan advocated for incremental, bipartisan AI regulation, contrasting with the more aggressive legislative proposals from progressive lawmakers.
- Critics at the event targeted the industry-funded PAC "Leading the Future" for its significant spending to influence federal AI policy.
- Disagreements emerged regarding international policy, specifically concerning whether to cooperate with or quarantine China’s AI development ecosystem.

### Why it Matters
The event underscores a deepening populist divide in Washington regarding the role of private corporations in shaping the future of artificial intelligence. While there is broad consensus on the need for oversight, the lack of a unified legislative strategy suggests that meaningful federal action remains uncertain.
