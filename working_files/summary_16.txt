Oracle faces potential delays for its Wisconsin AI datacenter as the project awaits critical power grid connection approvals, threatening the company's scheduled 2027 customer delivery timeline.

### Key Points
- Oracle's planned Wisconsin AI datacenter is currently stalled pending regulatory approval for necessary power grid infrastructure.
- The project's original timeline aimed for full customer delivery by 2027, a goal now at risk due to the energy connection bottleneck.
- Datacenter expansion remains a primary focus for Oracle as it scales infrastructure to meet surging demand for artificial intelligence computing power.

### Why it Matters
This delay highlights the growing tension between the rapid expansion of AI infrastructure and the physical limitations of regional power grids. As tech giants race to build massive data centers, energy availability and regulatory hurdles are becoming significant bottlenecks for the industry's long-term growth.
