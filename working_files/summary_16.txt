Jellyfin does everything Plex does — except charge you for it
============================================================

Jellyfin has emerged as a superior, open-source alternative to Plex for home media servers by offering all premium features for free without intrusive advertisements or cloud-based tracking.

### Key Points
- Jellyfin is an open-source fork of Emby that provides all features, including hardware transcoding and remote access, without subscription paywalls.
- The platform offers a clean, distraction-free interface that focuses exclusively on local media files rather than integrated ad-supported streaming channels or storefronts.
- Jellyfin functions entirely offline and does not rely on external servers for user authentication, ensuring access even during internet outages.
- The software does not collect user telemetry or viewing data, providing a more private experience compared to the default settings of Plex.
- Jellyfin supports a wide range of devices, including Windows, macOS, Android TV, Apple TV, Roku, and Fire TV, alongside a robust third-party plugin ecosystem.

### Why it Matters
Choosing Jellyfin allows users to reclaim full control over their personal media libraries while avoiding the rising costs of subscription-based software services. By eliminating cloud dependencies and data tracking, it provides a more reliable and private solution for long-term home media management.
