EA Sports FC 27 Just Revealed An Even More Expensive ‘Ultimate Plus’ Edition For $150
============================================================

Electronic Arts has announced a $150 Ultimate Plus Edition for EA Sports FC 27, signaling a significant price increase for the company's flagship sports titles ahead of its acquisition.

### Key Points
- The EA Sports FC 27 Ultimate Plus Edition costs $150, which is $50 more than the upcoming Grand Theft Auto VI Ultimate Edition.
- Included bonuses feature 10,000 FC Points distributed over five months and four premium season passes valued at $10 each.
- Early access for the title begins August 31, with the full global release scheduled for September 6.
- The publisher is currently finalizing a $55 billion leveraged buyout by a consortium led by Saudi Arabian investors.
- EA is reportedly exploring cost-cutting measures, including generative AI integration and increased in-game advertising, to manage debt from the acquisition.

### Why it Matters
This pricing strategy reflects a broader industry shift toward aggressive monetization of live-service sports games to offset massive corporate debt. As EA transitions to new ownership, fans may face higher entry costs and increased reliance on microtransactions to access core gameplay features.
