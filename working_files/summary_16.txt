NostradamusGPT: The Psychology of Prophecy and How to Break Free from the Prison of Predictive Technologies
============================================================

Carissa Véliz’s book *Prophecy* examines how modern predictive technologies and AI algorithms function as power structures that restrict human agency by replacing individual choice with statistical determinism.

### Key Points
- Author Carissa Véliz argues that modern AI serves as a contemporary "Oracle," mirroring the historical influence of ancient prophets and medieval astrologers.
- The book traces the shift from spiritual divination to statistical quantification, noting that numbers were adopted to standardize bureaucracies and hide power struggles.
- Predictive algorithms are described as "speech acts" that function more like commands than objective truths, often creating self-fulfilling prophecies.
- Véliz contends that reliance on data-driven predictions undermines human free will and the capacity for self-surprise, which are essential to the human experience.
- The text highlights that society is not inherently statistical, but was made so through the historical implementation of data collection and the "normal curve."

### Why it Matters
This analysis challenges the growing societal reliance on predictive technologies, suggesting that algorithmic control threatens the fundamental human capacity for creativity and autonomy. By framing predictions as tools of power rather than objective facts, the work encourages readers to reclaim their agency from systems that prioritize order over individual expression.
