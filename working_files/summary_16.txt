AI needs $6tn a year by 2031 to pay for its data centres, Bain says
============================================================

Bain & Company’s latest Global Technology Report estimates the AI industry must generate $6 trillion in annual revenue by 2031 to sustain current infrastructure and data center expansion.

### Key Points
- The AI sector requires $6 trillion in annual revenue by 2031 to cover the costs of massive data center and infrastructure investments.
- Current AI products are projected to generate only $1.2 trillion to $1.8 trillion, leaving a significant funding gap for the industry.
- Major tech firms including Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Meta, and Oracle may spend $780 billion on capital expenditure by 2026.
- Bain identifies potential revenue sources in physical AI, industrial automation, and emerging fields like drug discovery to help bridge the financial shortfall.
- Global data center capacity is expected to triple by 2030, though projects face increasing delays due to power shortages and local regulatory opposition.

### Why it Matters
The massive gap between infrastructure spending and projected revenue highlights a significant financial risk for the technology sector as it builds capacity ahead of actual market demand. If the industry fails to monetize AI through new applications, the resulting economic pressure could force a major reassessment of global capital investment strategies.
