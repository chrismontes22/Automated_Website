Watch out for fake image of gorilla 'screaming for help' after caretaker's collapse
============================================================

A viral social media image depicting a gorilla mourning a collapsed caretaker named Julius has been debunked as a fabricated, AI-generated creation by fact-checkers at Snopes.

### Key Points
- Snopes confirmed the image is AI-generated using OpenAI’s detection tools and visual analysis of digital distortions.
- The viral post originated from the social media account StoryTime, which frequently shares fabricated, sentimental animal stories.
- No reputable news outlets or animal sanctuaries have reported on the alleged incident involving a gorilla and a caretaker.
- The image contains high-quality details inconsistent with standard, lower-resolution surveillance footage.
- StoryTime monetizes these fictional narratives by linking to an online merchandise shop within their social media posts.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the growing prevalence of AI-generated "glurge," or sentimental misinformation, designed to exploit user emotions for commercial gain. As these fabricated stories become more sophisticated, they underscore the necessity for users to verify viral content before sharing it across social media platforms.
