Anthropic says it created an AI 'starter pack' for small businesses
============================================================

Anthropic is aggressively expanding its market reach by targeting small businesses with the Claude for Small Business plugin, workshops, and a new network of certified AI trainers.

### Key Points
- Anthropic has recorded 900,000 installations of its Claude for Small Business plugin since launching the tool in May.
- New integrations for the platform include business-focused services from Shopify, Stripe, and Zoom to assist with tasks like financial reconciliation.
- The company is training "Approved Claude SMB Trainers" in cities like San Francisco and New York to host workshops and drive local adoption.
- Data from the Chamber of Commerce shows small business generative AI usage grew from 23% in 2023 to 58% in 2025.
- Anthropic is competing directly with OpenAI to capture the small business sector, which accounts for roughly 44% of U.S. GDP.

### Why it Matters
Small businesses represent a critical growth frontier for AI labs as they prepare for potential public offerings and seek mass-market adoption. By building grassroots training networks, Anthropic aims to overcome common barriers like technical anxiety and customer backlash to integrate AI into the daily operations of the private sector.
