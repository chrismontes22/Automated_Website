Why Do Companies Switch From Google Workspace To Microsoft 365?
============================================================

Businesses are increasingly migrating from Google Workspace to Microsoft 365 to leverage superior offline functionality, advanced data security compliance, and more cost-effective enterprise collaboration and AI features.

### Key Points
- Microsoft 365 provides robust offline access to core applications like Word, Excel, and PowerPoint, whereas Google Workspace primarily relies on consistent internet connectivity.
- Microsoft 365 offers more integrated data protection tools, including regional security management and email encryption, which are often easier to configure for HIPAA compliance.
- Microsoft Teams supports up to 20,000 meeting attendees and includes recording capabilities across all business tiers, outperforming Google Workspace’s 1,000-person limit.
- Microsoft 365 includes the Copilot AI tool within existing subscriptions, potentially reducing the need for businesses to purchase separate third-party AI services.
- Data loss prevention (DLP) tools are standard in Microsoft 365, while Google Workspace often restricts these advanced security features to higher-priced enterprise subscription tiers.

### Why it Matters
The choice between these platforms significantly impacts operational efficiency and long-term IT expenditures for growing organizations. By consolidating security, communication, and AI tools into a single ecosystem, businesses can reduce administrative overhead and ensure better compliance with strict data protection standards.
