Vance’s “Frankenstein” AI defense is a blueprint for 2028
============================================================

Vice President JD Vance has expressed skepticism toward artificial intelligence companies seeking government regulation, marking a subtle shift in tone from President Donald Trump’s enthusiastic support for development.

### Key Points
- JD Vance described AI industry requests for government oversight as a potential "Trojan Horse" and compared tech leaders like Sam Altman to Victor Frankenstein.
- President Trump maintains that AI requires no guardrails beyond a strong president, viewing development as essential to compete with China.
- Reports indicate White House advisers are encouraging Trump to adopt a more cautious stance regarding AI safety and data center expansion.
- Senator Ted Cruz and other Republican figures are similarly balancing market-friendly policies with public concerns over AI-related risks and corporate accountability.
- Despite the rhetorical shift, current policy remains focused on maintaining an unregulated environment for AI investment and data center growth.

### Why it Matters
This emerging skepticism suggests a strategic effort by Republican leaders to distance themselves from the administration's blanket tech optimism without alienating the party's base. By questioning the motives of AI executives, these politicians are attempting to address growing public unease regarding technology while preserving the flow of Silicon Valley capital.
