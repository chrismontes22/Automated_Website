Refrigerators: The Next National Security Frontier?
============================================================

Recent refrigeration failures at U.S. military commissaries, including at Fort Huachuca, have prompted concerns regarding the vulnerability of operational technology to potential foreign cyber interference and infrastructure disruption.

### Key Points
- Multiple U.S. military commissaries recently experienced unexplained refrigeration system failures, raising questions about potential cybersecurity risks.
- Intelligence agencies have warned that Chinese state-sponsored actors, such as Volt Typhoon, are actively infiltrating American critical infrastructure.
- Military installations rely on networked operational technology, including HVAC, water, and power systems, which expand the potential attack surface for adversaries.
- Experts suggest these incidents could be used by foreign powers to test U.S. response times and communication protocols during a crisis.
- Congress is being urged to prioritize the security of installation infrastructure within upcoming defense authorization and appropriations legislation.

### Why it Matters
These incidents highlight a critical blind spot in national security as adversaries increasingly target the mundane infrastructure that supports sophisticated military operations. By disrupting non-combat systems, foreign actors could create logistical confusion and delay, potentially undermining U.S. readiness during a major conflict.
