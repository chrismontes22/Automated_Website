Xiaomi Takes the IFA 2026 Spotlight as Samsung Makes A Quiet Exit After 35 Years
============================================================

Samsung is shifting its IFA 2026 presence to a private venue in Berlin, leaving its traditional trade show floor space to competitors like Xiaomi for the upcoming event.

### Key Points
- Samsung will host its product launches and media briefings at the Humboldt Carré starting September 2, rather than maintaining a traditional booth at Messe Berlin.
- Xiaomi has secured a prominent position in Hall 6.2 to showcase its "Human x Car x Home" ecosystem, supported by a €7.4 billion AI R&D budget.
- Samsung will remain involved at IFA 2026 as the primary sponsor of the IFA Creator Hub located at the Palais am Funkturm.
- The Galaxy S26 FE is expected to launch on September 4, coinciding with the official opening of the IFA trade show.
- Xiaomi’s IoT platform currently connects over 1.16 billion devices, excluding smartphones and laptops, as of mid-2026.

### Why it Matters
Samsung’s move to private venues reflects a growing trend among major tech firms to prioritize controlled environments over the chaotic, competitor-heavy atmosphere of traditional trade show floors. However, this strategy risks ceding organic brand visibility and direct retail engagement to rivals like Xiaomi, who are positioning themselves as the primary anchors for show attendees and industry buyers.
