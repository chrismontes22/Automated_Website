Ugreen NASync DXP8800 Ultra review
============================================================

The Ugreen NASync DXP8800 Ultra improves upon its predecessor with a faster Intel Core 7 150U processor and expanded memory support, though it carries a significantly higher price tag.

### Key Points
- The DXP8800 Ultra features an Intel Core 7 150U processor and supports up to 96GB of DDR5 RAM.
- It retains the eight-bay chassis, dual 10GbE LAN ports, dual Thunderbolt 4 ports, and a PCIe Gen 4 slot.
- The device is priced at $1,779.99, positioning it as a premium alternative to the existing DXP8800 Plus model.
- It runs the UGOS Pro operating system, which supports Docker containers and virtual machines but lacks Hybrid RAID and WORM volume features.
- The unit is available for purchase now through Ugreen’s website and major online retailers like Amazon.

### Why it Matters
The DXP8800 Ultra offers high-end performance for specialized tasks like virtualization and local AI hosting, but its value proposition is limited by the high cost of DDR5 memory and the modest performance gains over the cheaper Plus model. For most users, the original DXP8800 Plus remains a more cost-effective solution for standard file serving and backup needs.
