New Android malware RemControl steals banking PINs and blocks removal attempts
============================================================

A new Android banking trojan called RemControl is targeting customers of over 30 international banks by using fake IPTV apps to steal sensitive PINs and remote device control.

### Key Points
- RemControl malware impersonates the TVTap IPTV application to trick users into granting Accessibility Service permissions.
- The trojan targets banking customers in Italy, France, Spain, Poland, Portugal, Canada, and several Gulf states.
- Attackers use a VPN service to block Google Play Protect from scanning the device during the malicious installation process.
- The malware can capture lock screen patterns, record keystrokes, and prevent users from uninstalling the application.
- Operators utilize AI-assisted development to build backend infrastructure and phishing overlays for this malware-as-a-service platform.
- Command and control server addresses are hidden within public Telegram channels to allow for rapid infrastructure updates.

### Why it Matters
This campaign highlights the growing sophistication of mobile threats that leverage AI to automate malicious infrastructure and evade traditional security detection. Users are at increased risk of financial theft as attackers continue to exploit third-party app distribution channels to bypass standard mobile operating system protections.
