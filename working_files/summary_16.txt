AdExchanger Unveils the 2026 Top Women in Media & Ad Tech Honorees
============================================================

AdExchanger has announced its 2026 Top Women in Media & Ad Tech honorees, recognizing influential leaders driving innovation across the advertising, marketing, and technology sectors this year.

### Key Points
- AdExchanger named a diverse group of leaders from companies including The Walt Disney Company, Microsoft AI, The Trade Desk, and NBCUniversal.
- Honorees are recognized for advancing data-driven advertising, developing AI-powered products, and reshaping media economics.
- The 2026 class will be formally celebrated at an awards gala held on November 3, 2026, at The Lighthouse in New York City.
- Recipients gain membership into the Top Women Network, a community designed to foster professional connection and visibility across the media ecosystem.

### Why it Matters
This recognition highlights the evolving leadership landscape within the advertising and technology sectors as companies navigate significant digital transformation. By fostering a cross-industry network, the program helps elevate female executives who are actively shaping the future of audience monetization and marketing strategy.
