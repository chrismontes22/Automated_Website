Nasdaq-listed Canton Strategic Holdings plans tokenized stock trading from Nov. 2
============================================================

Nasdaq-listed Canton Strategic Holdings will launch tokenized common shares on the Canton Network starting November 2, offering investors round-the-clock trading and immediate settlement under SEC regulatory exemptions.

### Key Points
- Canton Strategic Holdings partnered with TSV LLC and SEC-registered transfer agent Fairmint to facilitate the tokenization process.
- The tokenized shares will maintain identical shareholder rights to the company’s traditional Nasdaq-listed stock, which trades under the ticker CNTN.
- Trading will occur 24/7 via an automated market maker, with transactions settling instantly on the blockchain.
- The initiative utilizes a September SEC innovation exemption to provide a compliant regulatory framework for domestic tokenized stock trading.
- Canton Strategic Holdings currently holds a market capitalization of approximately $170 million and shifted its primary business focus to digital assets in 2025.

### Why it Matters
This move represents a significant shift toward integrating traditional equity markets with blockchain technology by enabling continuous, instant settlement for public company shares. If successful, this model could provide a blueprint for other Nasdaq-listed firms to leverage digital asset infrastructure to increase liquidity and modernize shareholder services.
