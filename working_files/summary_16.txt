Here’s What Capcom Said About Using Generative AI In The RE Engine: ‘AI Is Not A Replacement For Humans’
============================================================

Capcom detailed its REX Initiative at an October 2 engineering conference, outlining plans to integrate AI into development workflows while maintaining a strict policy against AI-generated game assets.

### Key Points
- Capcom is evolving its proprietary RE Engine under the REX Initiative to improve code efficiency and error reporting.
- The company released its RE:Dox and RE:Log tools as open-source software on GitHub to facilitate AI-assisted development.
- AI agents will be utilized to perform automated playtesting, identify bugs, and suggest performance fixes for human developers to implement.
- Capcom explicitly stated that AI is intended to reduce repetitive tasks rather than replace human creative roles in game development.
- The initiative aims to ensure the RE Engine remains scalable across diverse hardware platforms, ranging from PCs to the Nintendo Switch.

### Why it Matters
This strategy highlights a growing industry trend where major studios leverage AI for backend technical optimization while distancing themselves from controversial generative content. By automating bug detection and code maintenance, Capcom aims to increase development efficiency and allow human staff to prioritize creative design.
