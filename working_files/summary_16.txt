A Moment of Truth, Bankruptcy Fears, or Comeback? Plug Power & Nel ASA Fight for Survival! Will Lahontan Gold See a Technical Breakout?
============================================================

Hydrogen companies Plug Power and Nel ASA face significant financial pressure, while mineral explorer Lahontan Gold Corp. advances its Nevada-based mining projects toward a 2027 production target.

### Key Points
- Plug Power is implementing "Project Quantum Leap" to reach positive adjusted EBITDA by late 2026 while divesting infrastructure assets to improve liquidity.
- Nel ASA continues to struggle with weak margins and delayed industrial orders, keeping its stock in a highly speculative position.
- Lahontan Gold Corp. reported successful drilling results at its Santa Fe project, including high-grade gold and silver intersections in the Calvada area.
- Lahontan Gold aims to resume production by 2027 and is currently finalizing its Mineral Resource Estimate and Preliminary Economic Assessment.

### Why it Matters
The hydrogen sector remains volatile as companies struggle to balance high operational costs with the slow pace of global energy transition investments. Conversely, gold and silver explorers like Lahontan Gold are gaining attention as investors seek stable commodities to hedge against rising inflation and market uncertainty.
