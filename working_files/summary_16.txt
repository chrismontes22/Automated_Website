FinCEN ties $13B in crypto scams to non-US operations
============================================================

The U.S. Financial Crimes Enforcement Network reports that transnational criminal organizations operating out of Southeast Asia have defrauded American residents of approximately $13 billion through cryptocurrency scams.

### Key Points
- FinCEN identified $12.7 billion in illicit transactions linked to overseas scam centers between September 2023 and December 2025.
- The agency analyzed over 33,000 suspicious activity reports involving pig butchering, romance scams, and fraudulent investment confidence schemes.
- Criminal operations are primarily based in compounds located throughout Southeast Asia.
- Myanmar and Cambodia have introduced new legislation, including potential life imprisonment, to combat the forced labor and violence used by these scam centers.

### Why it Matters
These findings highlight the growing threat of sophisticated digital asset fraud targeting U.S. consumers from international jurisdictions. The scale of these losses underscores the urgent need for enhanced global regulatory cooperation and stricter enforcement against transnational criminal networks.
