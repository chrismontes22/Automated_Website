As WPP’s Cindy Rose marks a year as CEO, turnaround is underway but far from certain
============================================================

One year into her tenure as CEO, Cindy Rose is attempting to stabilize WPP through the "Elevate28" restructuring strategy, focusing on AI integration and operational simplification to drive growth.

### Key Points
- WPP Media secured over $3 billion in new accounts during 2026, significantly improving its retention rate to 43% compared to 16% the previous year.
- The "Elevate28" strategy reorganized the company into four pillars—media, creative, production, and consulting—supported by a $405 million annual investment in AI.
- The company reduced its headcount by approximately 1,260 employees in the first half of 2026, bringing the total workforce to roughly 104,000.
- WPP faces ongoing financial pressure, including a $3.3 billion debt pile and an operating profit margin of 4.1%, which trails behind competitors like Publicis.
- Major upcoming business reviews, including a critical defense of the Coca-Cola account and a $1 billion IKEA media account, will test the firm's momentum.

### Why it Matters
Rose’s ability to successfully pivot WPP toward an AI-driven, simplified operating model is essential for reversing years of revenue decline and improving stagnant profit margins. The outcome of high-stakes client reviews and potential asset divestments will determine whether her leadership can effectively restore investor confidence and long-term market competitiveness.
