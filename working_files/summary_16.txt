4 Bose Speakers You Can Buy With Built-In Voice Assistants
============================================================

Bose offers a range of high-quality speakers and soundbars featuring built-in voice assistant compatibility, allowing users to integrate smart home controls directly into their audio entertainment systems.

### Key Points
- The $299 Lifestyle Ultra Speaker features Alexa+ for advanced conversational capabilities and curated music suggestions.
- Priced at $399, the Portable Smart Speaker offers 360-degree sound, IPX4 water resistance, and support for both Alexa and Google Assistant.
- The $429 Smart Soundbar includes Dolby Atmos, AI-powered dialogue enhancement, and Voice4Video technology for home theater setups.
- The premium $1,099 Lifestyle Ultra Soundbar serves as a modular hub with Alexa+ support and high-fidelity audio for various room sizes.
- All selected models maintain an Amazon customer rating above 3.5 stars and require no intermediary devices for voice control functionality.

### Why it Matters
Integrating voice-controlled speakers into a home ecosystem allows for seamless management of entertainment and smart devices through simple verbal commands. Choosing hardware with native assistant support ensures a more reliable and responsive user experience compared to relying on external bridge devices.
