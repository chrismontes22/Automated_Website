Huawei Cloud Rolls Out Enterprise AI Products Across the Board, Building an Open Agentic Cloud
============================================================

Huawei Cloud unveiled its new Agentic Cloud strategy at HUAWEI CONNECT 2026, introducing advanced AI cluster services and enterprise-grade agent platforms to accelerate global industrial AI adoption.

### Key Points
- Huawei Cloud launched the AI Cluster Service (AICS), featuring 20% higher token throughput and 10-minute fault recovery, available globally by November 30, 2026.
- The new Context Memory Storage (CMS) solution provides petabyte-scale capacity with 50% faster read performance than current industry standards.
- The AgentArts enterprise platform, which supports over 100 customers, will expand to international markets on December 30, 2026.
- The Industry AI Foundry added Smart Government and AI Hardware zones, bringing the total to seven specialized sectors with over 1,000 deployed projects.
- The openJiuwen open-source community has reached 3.29 million downloads, supporting developers with over 6,000 general and industry-specific Model Context Protocol assets.

### Why it Matters
These developments signal a shift toward "Agentic Infra," where cloud infrastructure is specifically optimized to manage autonomous AI agents within complex production environments. By standardizing these tools, Huawei Cloud aims to lower the barrier for enterprises to integrate large-scale AI into government, manufacturing, and office software workflows.
