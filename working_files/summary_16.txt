I ditched my clipboard manager after finding this cross-device clipboard app
============================================================

OctoClip is a cross-platform clipboard manager that synchronizes copied text, images, and files across Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iPhone to streamline multi-device workflows for modern users.

### Key Points
- OctoClip functions as a cross-device synchronization tool for clipboard history, including text, links, and small files.
- The software supports major operating systems, including Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iOS.
- Users can organize frequently used content into collections, pin important entries, and search through historical clipboard data.
- The platform reduces reliance on messaging apps or cloud storage for transferring small snippets and files between devices.
- Advanced features, such as extended history and increased storage limits, are restricted to a paid subscription model.

### Why it Matters
This tool addresses the friction of moving information between multiple devices, which is increasingly common for professionals managing AI prompts and research. By centralizing data access, it eliminates inefficient workarounds and helps maintain focus during complex, multi-device workflows.
