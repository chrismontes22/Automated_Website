Dutch Police Arrest ‘Reformed’ Hacker in Shiny Hunters Investigation
============================================================

Dutch authorities have arrested 24-year-old Pepijn van der Stap, a convicted cybercriminal suspected of aiding the hacker group ShinyHunters, triggering a wave of retaliatory attacks against the FBI.

### Key Points
- Dutch police arrested Pepijn van der Stap, a former software engineer and convicted hacker, on or around September 16.
- Following the arrest, ShinyHunters breached the FBI’s job application portal, exposing personal data and medical records of over 5,000 officials.
- The group exploited a PeopleSoft vulnerability (CVE-2026-35273) to conduct mass data thefts across multiple industries, including healthcare and government.
- Investigations suggest a teenage hacker known as "Rey" has taken control of the group, escalating operations and targeting the Cl0p ransomware collective.
- Reports indicate Dutch investigators are also probing allegations that Van der Stap attempted to orchestrate two murders abroad.

### Why it Matters
The escalation of these attacks highlights the volatile nature of modern cybercrime syndicates as they pivot toward high-profile targets like the FBI to retaliate against law enforcement. This shift, driven by internal power struggles and the use of zero-day exploits, poses a significant threat to global data security and corporate infrastructure.
