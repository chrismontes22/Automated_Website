Forget Sony and Bose — Jabra's Evolve3 85 is my new meeting headset
============================================================

The Jabra Evolve3 85 emerges as a top-tier professional headset, offering superior comfort, extensive battery life, and versatile connectivity options that outperform rivals like Bose and Sony.

### Key Points
- The Jabra Evolve3 85 weighs 220g, making it significantly lighter than competing models from Bose and Sony.
- It features Bluetooth 5.3, a dedicated USB-C receiver for stable Windows connectivity, and support for Fast Pair on Android devices.
- The headset provides up to 60 hours of battery life and includes wireless charging, with a 5-minute charge offering five hours of playback.
- It utilizes six MEMS microphones and ClearVoice technology to effectively isolate audio during meetings on platforms like Zoom and Microsoft Teams.
- The device is designed for longevity, featuring a user-replaceable battery and a compact, foldable design for easier portability.

### Why it Matters
The Evolve3 85 sets a new standard for professional audio equipment by bridging the gap between high-end office functionality and consumer-grade music performance. Its focus on repairability and specialized connectivity features offers a compelling alternative for remote workers seeking reliable, long-term hardware solutions.
