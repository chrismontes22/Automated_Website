AI Agents Won’t Fix Bad Audience Data, They’ll Amplify It via @sejournal, @gregjarboe
============================================================

AI agents are transforming marketing by scaling audience research, but experts warn that high-quality data remains the critical factor for driving actual conversions rather than just visibility.

### Key Points
- AI agents process thousands of behavioral and intent signals simultaneously, far exceeding the speed and capacity of human research teams.
- Skydeo creative director Mallory Gray notes that increased AI citation frequency does not automatically correlate with higher qualified traffic or sales.
- Relying solely on AI-generated targeting without human oversight can lead to automated blind spots and stagnant conversion rates.
- Brands are cautioned against prioritizing volume-based AI visibility, which can mask ineffective strategies and poor audience alignment.
- Effective AI strategy requires auditing data sources to distinguish between declared customer information, observed behaviors, and model-based inferences.

### Why it Matters
The shift toward AI-driven marketing risks prioritizing superficial metrics like citation counts over the underlying data quality that actually drives business growth. Companies that fail to maintain human oversight of their targeting logic risk scaling ineffective campaigns that prioritize automated output over meaningful customer engagement.
