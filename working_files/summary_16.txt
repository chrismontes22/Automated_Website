Apple is looking for a cellular modem engineer to help shape its transition to 6G
============================================================

Apple has posted a job listing for a 6G Cellular Modem Systems Engineer to lead the development of next-generation wireless technology and improve future hardware performance.

### Key Points
- The position offers an annual base salary between $175,000 and $308,500, plus additional stock awards and benefits.
- Candidates must possess at least 10 years of industry experience and proficiency in programming languages like C/C++, Python, or MATLAB.
- Apple requires expertise in generative AI and LLM-based tools to help accelerate internal engineering workflows and system design.
- The role involves defining 6G standards, hardware architecture, and power optimization strategies for future Apple product integration.
- Industry standards for 6G aim for peak speeds of 200 Gbps and latency as low as 0.1 milliseconds.
- Commercial 6G networks and compatible consumer devices are not expected to reach the market until approximately 2030.

### Why it Matters
This recruitment effort signals Apple's long-term strategy to reduce reliance on third-party modem suppliers by building proprietary wireless technology. By integrating generative AI into the development process, the company aims to accelerate the complex transition from 5G to 6G standards.
