Russia’s largest bank Sberbank plans crypto trading infrastructure by December
============================================================

Russia’s largest financial institution, Sberbank, intends to launch cryptocurrency trading infrastructure and a digital depository by December 1 to align with new national regulations for digital asset management.

### Key Points
- Sberbank will operate active wallets for client deposits and transfers while utilizing a digital depository to record ownership and process transactions off-chain.
- New Russian regulations governing crypto trading, custody, and settlement take effect on September 1, with mandatory intermediary licensing required by July 2027.
- Public crypto trading will be restricted to assets meeting strict liquidity and market capitalization thresholds, such as a 5 trillion ruble minimum market cap.
- The use of cryptocurrency for domestic payments for goods and services remains strictly prohibited under the new legal framework.
- The initiative follows recent legislative efforts to legalize mining and establish experimental regimes for cross-border crypto settlements within the Russian financial system.

### Why it Matters
This development signals a significant shift toward integrating digital assets into Russia’s formal banking sector under strict regulatory oversight. By establishing institutional infrastructure, Sberbank aims to provide a controlled environment for crypto exposure while maintaining the government's ban on using digital currencies for everyday retail payments.
