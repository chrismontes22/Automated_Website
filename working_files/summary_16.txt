AurionMail: E2EE suite (CryptPad/Stalwart) with single-password UX
============================================================

The new AurionMail Suite integrates CryptPad and JMAP-based email into a single, open-source platform that uses one master password for unified, end-to-end encrypted document and email management.

### Key Points
- AurionMail combines CryptPad, Bulwark Webmail, Stalwart Mail Server, and Ory Hydra into a cohesive, self-hosted suite.
- The platform enables a Zero-Knowledge experience where one password manages both email encryption and collaborative document access.
- It utilizes open standards including OpenPGP and JMAP to ensure interoperability without relying on proprietary backends.
- Security features include global logout, emergency account destruction, and automated key synchronization across authorized user devices.
- Administrators can deploy the suite via the Orchestra binary, which bundles all core services and custom integrations into a single package.

### Why it Matters
AurionMail bridges the gap between the convenience of commercial platforms like Proton and the transparency of self-hosted, open-source software. By eliminating the need for fragmented logins and manual PGP key management, it provides a professional-grade privacy solution for users who require full control over their data infrastructure.
