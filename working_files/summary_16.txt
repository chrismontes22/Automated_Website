Apple Upgrade might’ve provided our first hint for what could change with Apple Card
============================================================

Apple has launched its new Apple Upgrade leasing program, a flexible financing initiative that replaces the existing iPhone Upgrade Program and potentially signals the end of Apple Card Monthly Installments.

### Key Points
- Apple Upgrade allows users to lease most Apple products with flexible terms and the option to purchase the device at MSRP upon completion.
- The new program maintains the 3% cash back benefit for Apple Card users while offering more generous leasing terms than previous financing options.
- Apple has partnered with Klarna to underwrite the Apple Upgrade program, rather than its future banking partner, JPMorgan Chase.
- Apple Card is scheduled to transition from Goldman Sachs to JPMorgan Chase by early 2028.
- Apple has not confirmed the long-term future of Apple Card Monthly Installments, stating only that the service remains available for now.

### Why it Matters
The introduction of Apple Upgrade suggests a strategic shift in how Apple manages consumer financing as it prepares for its transition to JPMorgan Chase. If the company continues to favor third-party leasing partners, existing installment programs may be phased out to streamline Apple's retail financial offerings.
