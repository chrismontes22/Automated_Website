============================================================

Nvidia Broadcast utilizes AI-powered processing on RTX graphics cards to enhance audio and video quality for remote workers by suppressing background noise and improving webcam image clarity.

### Key Points
- Nvidia Broadcast uses Tensor Cores in RTX 2060 or newer GPUs to perform local AI-based noise suppression and video enhancement.
- The software isolates human speech to eliminate ambient sounds like fan hums and keyboard clicks without requiring cloud-based processing.
- Webcam features include virtual backgrounds, auto-framing, eye contact correction, and low-light noise reduction to improve professional appearance.
- Users must have at least 8GB of RAM and download approximately 1.5GB of data to install the necessary AI models.
- AMD users can access similar noise suppression via the Adrenalin Edition software on Ryzen 6000 or RX 6000 series hardware and newer.
- Windows Studio Effects offers comparable AI enhancements for systems equipped with a dedicated Neural Processing Unit (NPU).

### Why it Matters
These software-based tools allow professionals to achieve studio-quality audio and video results using existing, lower-cost hardware. By leveraging local AI processing, users can significantly improve their remote meeting presence without the expense of purchasing high-end microphones or cameras.
