Keystone says website breach exposed hardware wallet buyers’ addresses
============================================================

Hardware wallet manufacturer Keystone confirmed a website security breach that exposed customer shipping addresses, names, and order details to an unauthorized third party via a Shopify API vulnerability.

### Key Points
- Keystone identified suspicious activity and confirmed that attackers accessed customer contact information and specific purchase history.
- The company has patched the vulnerability and is currently notifying affected customers via email.
- Security experts warn that leaked physical addresses increase the risk of targeted phishing, scams, and physical "wrench attacks."
- Customers reported receiving breach notifications despite Keystone’s 2023 policy to delete order data after 180 days.
- Blockchain security firm CertiK reported 52 verified physical attacks against cryptocurrency holders in the first half of 2026.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the significant physical and digital security risks associated with storing customer data for hardware wallet purchases. The discrepancy regarding Keystone's data retention policy raises concerns about corporate accountability and the potential for long-term exposure of sensitive user information.
