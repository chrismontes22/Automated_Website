Sega pulls Total War: Shogun 2 - Complete Edition from sale amid AI accusations, admitting it has "fallen short"
============================================================

Sega and Creative Assembly have temporarily pulled Total War: Shogun 2 - Complete Edition from digital storefronts following widespread player criticism regarding the game's quality and technical performance.

### Key Points
- The Complete Edition was removed from sale shortly after its October 13 launch due to negative player feedback.
- Steam user reviews for the title plummeted to "overwhelmingly negative," with only 15 percent of over 4,000 reviews being positive.
- Players have specifically criticized the game for poor upscaling and the suspected use of generative AI tools.
- Existing owners retain access to the game, and the original version is being reinstated on the Steam store.
- Sega and Creative Assembly are currently working with development partners to address technical issues and improve quality standards.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the growing tension between game developers and players regarding the implementation of generative AI in software development. It serves as a cautionary example for the industry as major publishers continue to integrate new technologies into their established franchises.
