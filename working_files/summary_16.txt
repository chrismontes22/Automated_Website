2026 Hackaday Supercon: Bruce Potter’s Keynote Explores the Hacker Mind
============================================================

Cybersecurity expert Bruce Potter will deliver the keynote address at the 2026 Hackaday Supercon in Pasadena, exploring the core mindset and creative problem-solving philosophy behind modern hacking.

### Key Points
- Bruce Potter, Chief Product Officer at Trinity Cyber, will headline the 2026 Hackaday Supercon event.
- The keynote focuses on defining hacking as a versatile mindset rather than a specific set of technical tools.
- Potter’s career includes three decades of experience, ranging from DARPA research to co-founding the ShmooCon security conference.
- The weekend event in Pasadena features over 30 presentations centered on hardware hacking and collaborative engineering.
- Limited tickets remain available for attendees interested in the conference's technical workshops and community networking opportunities.

### Why it Matters
This keynote highlights the evolution of hacking from a narrow security focus to a broader methodology for creative problem-solving in professional and personal projects. By framing hacking as a mindset, the event encourages engineers and hobbyists to apply these analytical techniques to diverse technical challenges.
