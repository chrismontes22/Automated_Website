A $249 Smart Lock That Still Opens When You’ve Forgotten to Charge It
============================================================

Amazon has unveiled its first Ring Smart Lock, featuring a manual crank dial for emergency power and a rechargeable battery pack, scheduled for a 2027 U.S. release.

### Key Points
- The device includes a manual crank dial that generates enough power to operate the keypad if the primary battery dies.
- It features a backlit touchscreen, a circular fingerprint reader, and compatibility with standard single-cylinder deadbolts.
- Remote connectivity and smart features rely on the Amazon Sidewalk network rather than standard home Wi-Fi.
- The lock integrates with the Ring app to share activity feeds with other Ring security products like cameras and doorbells.
- Amazon plans to offer the lock in black, silver, and brass finishes at a retail price of $249.

### Why it Matters
This product addresses the common consumer pain point of smart lock battery failure by providing a reliable, low-tech manual backup system. By integrating with the broader Ring ecosystem and Amazon Sidewalk, the device aims to simplify home security management for users already invested in the company's smart home platform.
