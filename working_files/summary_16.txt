State security authority alerts to intelligence leak risks via courier data
============================================================

China’s Ministry of State Security has issued a formal warning regarding potential intelligence leaks, citing foreign efforts to exploit vast datasets generated by the domestic express delivery industry.

### Key Points
- The Ministry of State Security (MSS) identified a malware attack involving a physical break-in at a courier station to install a Trojan virus.
- Logistics data, including delivery routes and personal waybill details, can be aggregated to track individual routines and consumption habits.
- Intelligence agencies may target delivery records near military-industrial sites to identify personnel and potential recruitment targets.
- Large-scale logistics datasets provide insights into national economic activity, population distribution, and the geographical layout of sensitive industries.
- Current regulations mandate that courier firms encrypt electronic waybills and minimize the collection of sensitive personal information.

### Why it Matters
The rapid growth of the logistics sector has transformed routine shipping data into a high-value intelligence asset that poses significant national security risks. Protecting this information is now a critical priority for both government regulators and private courier companies to prevent foreign espionage and criminal exploitation.
