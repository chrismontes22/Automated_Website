Elon Musk Vows To Give a SpaceX Share To 'Every Kid In America'
============================================================

Elon Musk announced plans to distribute SpaceX shares to every child in America, a proposal that could involve over 73 million minors and cost approximately $10.7 billion.

### Key Points
- Elon Musk pledged on X to give one SpaceX share to every American child starting next year.
- SpaceX shares closed at approximately $146 per share as of the most recent market data.
- The U.S. Census Bureau estimates there are roughly 73 million people under the age of 18 in the country.
- The announcement follows Anthropic’s commitment to donate 5% of its shares to a public benefit fund.
- Specific logistical details regarding the distribution process and eligibility criteria remain undisclosed by Musk.

### Why it Matters
This proposal highlights a growing trend of tech leaders attempting to address public concerns regarding corporate social responsibility and wealth distribution. If implemented, the move would represent a massive transfer of private equity to the public, potentially setting a new precedent for how major corporations engage with societal impact.
