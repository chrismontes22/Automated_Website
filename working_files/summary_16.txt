OpenAI’s ads business hits Europe at the six month mark
============================================================

OpenAI is expanding its ChatGPT advertising platform to 31 European markets on August 24, marking a significant milestone in the company's rapid global push to monetize its AI services.

### Key Points
- OpenAI will launch ChatGPT ads in 31 European countries, including Germany, France, Spain, Italy, and the Netherlands.
- Advertisers can purchase placements through major agencies like Publicis, Omnicom, WPP, Havas, Dentsu, and MediaPlus.
- The expansion follows updated privacy terms and a new European Union privacy policy released by OpenAI on August 14.
- OpenAI reports 900 million weekly active users, with approximately 20% of queries showing direct commercial intent.
- The company is transitioning toward a cost-per-click bidding model to help offset high infrastructure costs and reach ambitious revenue targets.

### Why it Matters
This expansion represents a strategic effort by OpenAI to challenge the dominance of established digital advertising giants like Google and Meta. By capturing intent-based search traffic in Europe, the company aims to build a sustainable revenue stream to support its massive AI infrastructure investments.
