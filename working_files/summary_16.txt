What is cryptocurrency good for? The concept has caught on but is yet to justify its existence
============================================================

Bitcoin recorded its largest weekly gain in over three years, rising 23% following US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent’s bond buyback plan and increased interest from institutional investors.

### Key Points
- Bitcoin surged 23% last week, ending a period of summer stagnation driven by market volatility and short-covering.
- US President Donald Trump reported $1.4 billion in crypto-related earnings in 2025, largely from his venture World Liberty Financial.
- The proposed US Clarity Act, intended to provide regulatory guardrails for crypto, remains stalled in the Senate due to ethics concerns.
- Gold has outperformed Bitcoin in 2026, rising over 7% while Bitcoin remains down nearly 10% for the year despite recent gains.
- Institutional interest continues to grow, though the asset still lacks a consistent narrative to distinguish it from purely speculative investments.

### Why it Matters
The recent price volatility highlights Bitcoin's ongoing struggle to establish itself as a reliable hedge against inflation or a stable alternative to traditional assets. As institutional involvement grows, the lack of a clear, fundamental value proposition leaves the market highly susceptible to political developments and speculative trading patterns.
