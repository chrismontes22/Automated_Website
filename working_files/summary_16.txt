Rate path still divides investors: Five things to know in Bitcoin this week
============================================================

Bitcoin faces heightened volatility this week as investors monitor the Federal Reserve’s upcoming interest rate decision and critical US inflation data amid ongoing geopolitical tensions in the Middle East.

### Key Points
- The Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) will announce its latest interest rate decision on Wednesday, July 29.
- Markets are pricing in a 31% probability of a rate hike this week, with September hike odds reaching 50%.
- The Bureau of Economic Analysis will release June Personal Consumption Expenditures (PCE) inflation data on Thursday.
- Bitcoin whale inflows to the Binance exchange have declined by 44% since June 12, signaling a shift in market participation.
- Bitcoin is currently trading in a compressed range, caught between its 200-week simple moving average and 50-month exponential moving average.

### Why it Matters
Macroeconomic uncertainty and shifting interest rate expectations are creating a volatile environment for risk assets like Bitcoin. Investors are closely watching these indicators to determine if the current decoupling between cryptocurrency and traditional equity markets will persist or if broader economic pressures will force them back into alignment.
