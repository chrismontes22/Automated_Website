Nasdaq 100 Falls 3% as Earnings Stoke AI Reckoning: Markets Wrap
============================================================

Technology stocks and cryptocurrencies faced a sharp sell-off as disappointing earnings from major firms and concerns over artificial intelligence investment returns triggered widespread market volatility on Wall Street.

### Key Points
- The Nasdaq 100 index declined by approximately 3%, erasing over $1.2 trillion in market capitalization throughout October.
- Intel Corp. shares plummeted 28% following a weak financial outlook, while Amazon.com Inc. fell due to increased capital expenditure concerns.
- Bitcoin prices dropped to roughly $77,000 following reports of a record security breach at the cryptocurrency exchange Binance.
- Defensive sectors, including utilities and consumer staples, outperformed the broader market as investors sought stability amid the tech-led downturn.
- Oil prices rose on geopolitical tensions regarding potential United States military strikes against Iran.

### Why it Matters
The recent decline highlights growing investor skepticism regarding whether massive capital spending on artificial intelligence will yield sufficient financial returns for major technology companies. If market confidence continues to erode, investors may abandon sector rotation strategies in favor of holding cash, potentially ending the recent period of stock market resilience.
