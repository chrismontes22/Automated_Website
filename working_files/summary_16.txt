Coinbase Global Maps ‘Everything Exchange’ Push at Citi Conference
============================================================

Coinbase Global is pivoting from a spot cryptocurrency exchange to an "Everything Exchange" by expanding into derivatives, prediction markets, tokenized equities, and on-chain infrastructure for global users.

### Key Points
- CFO Alesia Haas confirmed the company now operates across four distinct trading verticals, including spot crypto, derivatives, traditional equities, and prediction markets.
- The Coinbase One subscription service surpassed 1 million members in the second quarter, with plans to integrate derivatives and prediction market benefits.
- Coinbase launched one-for-one-backed tokenized equities on its Base network for non-U.S. customers, allowing for self-custody and future dividend eligibility.
- The company is maintaining strict expense discipline, projecting 2026 costs to remain consistent with 2025 levels while leveraging AI to boost developer productivity.
- Financial disclosures are being updated to separately report spot and derivatives trading volumes to provide greater transparency as revenue streams diversify.

### Why it Matters
This strategic shift signals Coinbase's attempt to reduce reliance on volatile crypto-trading fees by capturing a broader share of the global financial services market. By unifying diverse asset classes on a single technology stack, the company aims to increase user retention and scale its infrastructure to compete with traditional financial institutions.
