The Onion’s Exclusive Interview With Larry Ellison
============================================================

Oracle co-founder and Paramount Skydance shareholder Larry Ellison discussed his business ventures, personal wealth, and corporate strategies in a satirical interview with the publication The Onion.

### Key Points
- Larry Ellison currently serves as the executive chairman of Oracle and holds the position of largest shareholder at the Paramount Skydance Corporation.
- The billionaire addressed his recent decline in global wealth rankings, moving from the second to the eighth-richest person over a two-month period.
- Ellison commented on the ongoing merger negotiations between Paramount and Warner Bros. Discovery, citing complex corporate challenges.
- The executive joked about his personal life, his physical appearance, and his long-term ambitions regarding land ownership in Hawaii.

### Why it Matters
This interview highlights the intersection of high-stakes corporate consolidation and the public persona of one of the world's wealthiest technology executives. It underscores the growing influence of major shareholders in the entertainment industry as they navigate complex antitrust concerns and media mergers.
