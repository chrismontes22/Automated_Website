Bernie Sanders and Steve Bannon to share a stage to promote curbs on AI
============================================================

Senator Bernie Sanders and strategist Steve Bannon joined a diverse coalition at the Pro-Human Assembly to demand stricter government oversight and safety regulations for the rapidly advancing AI industry.

### Key Points
- Senator Bernie Sanders announced plans to introduce legislation next week to permanently ban the development of "superintelligent" AI systems.
- Steve Bannon advocated for a nationalist approach, calling for the removal of Chinese nationals from U.S. technology labs and restricted chip exports.
- The "Pro-Human Assembly" was organized by the Future of Life Institute, which promotes 33 principles focused on human agency and corporate accountability.
- House Speaker Mike Johnson expressed concerns that aggressive U.S. regulation could allow China to gain a competitive advantage in the global AI race.
- The House is scheduled to vote on the Ratepayer Protection Act to address energy cost spikes caused by the proliferation of AI data centers.

### Why it Matters
The unusual political alignment between Sanders and Bannon highlights how growing public anxiety over AI is transcending traditional partisan divides. This shift signals increasing pressure on federal lawmakers to balance national security and economic competitiveness against the potential societal risks posed by unchecked technological development.
