The Biggest ChatGPT Update Yet Gives Entrepreneurs 7 New Ways to Grow Their Business Fast
============================================================

OpenAI has introduced "dots," a new category of always-on AI agents within ChatGPT designed to handle recurring business tasks and research while maintaining human oversight for critical decisions.

### Key Points
- Dots function as persistent AI agents that operate independently using their own cloud computing resources and connected applications.
- Users can interact with agents via voice calls to assign tasks, which the AI continues to process after the conversation ends.
- Practical applications include monitoring missed invoices, planning daily schedules, reviewing marketing performance, and drafting social media content.
- The technology aims to transition users from one-off chat prompts to repeatable, automated workflows that reduce administrative burdens for solo founders.
- A July 2026 QuickBooks survey indicated that only 18% of small businesses currently consider AI a core component of their daily operations.

### Why it Matters
These persistent agents represent a shift from reactive AI tools to proactive assistants capable of managing ongoing business processes. By automating routine cognitive tasks, founders can scale their operations more efficiently while retaining final authority over high-stakes strategic decisions.
