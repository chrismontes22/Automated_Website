From DOGE to space lasers, Alex Gibney peels back the many layers of Elon Musk
============================================================

Oscar-winning director Alex Gibney explores the life and influence of Elon Musk in his new documentary, *Musk*, which examines the billionaire's business practices and political impact.

### Key Points
- The documentary, which runs three hours and 45 minutes, is scheduled for a theatrical release on October 16.
- Gibney features interviews with Musk’s father, former partners, and business associates to analyze the tech mogul's rise and controversial management style.
- The film highlights the Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE) and Musk’s influence on the 2024 presidential election and federal policy.
- Evidence presented includes a deposition regarding staged Tesla self-driving footage and reports linking Tesla software to 65 fatal crashes.
- The production utilizes an AI-generated avatar of Musk to narrate his own past statements, a stylistic choice that has drawn criticism for its distracting nature.

### Why it Matters
The film frames Musk as a "confidence man" whose immense wealth and power rely on market hype and the erosion of traditional corporate boundaries. By documenting his transition from a tech entrepreneur to a central figure in federal governance, the project raises critical questions about the risks of unchecked influence in the modern era.
