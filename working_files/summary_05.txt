Capcom Data Breach Exposes 11.7 Million Email Addresses
============================================================

Capcom confirmed a significant data breach of its CAPCOM ID service, resulting in the unauthorized exposure of over 11.7 million user email addresses and associated account login information.

### Key Points
- The breach, discovered on October 19, compromised 11,774,247 email addresses and various account metadata including IP addresses and login history.
- Attackers successfully performed unauthorized password changes on 196 accounts, all of which have since been reset by the company.
- Personal details such as dates of birth, gender, and nicknames were exposed for 43 specific user accounts.
- Capcom has notified relevant authorities and established an internal task force to investigate the attack method and strengthen security protocols.
- The company is actively contacting affected users and warning them to remain vigilant against potential phishing attempts impersonating the brand.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the ongoing cybersecurity risks facing major gaming publishers that manage massive databases of sensitive user information. The breach serves as a reminder for users to monitor their accounts for suspicious activity and underscores the necessity for companies to maintain robust defense mechanisms against unauthorized access.
