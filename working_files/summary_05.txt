Mark Zuckerberg says the future will have an ‘abundance of jobs’—and predicts a wave of new careers like world builders and personal biologists
============================================================

Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg predicts that artificial intelligence will create an abundance of new career opportunities rather than causing a widespread collapse of the global labor market.

### Key Points
- Mark Zuckerberg argues that AI will catalyze new roles, including virtual world builders, personal biologists, and independent product designers.
- The Meta CEO criticized peers like Sam Altman and Dario Amodei for suggesting that AI could lead to a dystopian elimination of human labor.
- Zuckerberg acknowledged a potential "difficult period" of transition if automation outpaces the ability of the workforce to acquire new skills.
- Meta has invested $115 million into the America's Workforce Academy to train individuals for high-demand roles as data center technicians.
- The company emphasizes the need for more skilled tradespeople, such as electricians and carpenters, to support the ongoing infrastructure buildout.

### Why it Matters
This perspective offers a counter-narrative to growing industry fears regarding the potential for AI-driven mass unemployment and the concentration of technological power. By focusing on workforce training and new job categories, Zuckerberg’s stance highlights the critical role of human adaptation in navigating the economic shifts caused by rapid automation.
