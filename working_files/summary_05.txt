Anthropic IPO Prospectus Reveals Company Bleeding Cash, Striving for $2T Valuation
============================================================

AI developer Anthropic has filed for an initial public offering that could value the company at over $2 trillion, signaling a major test for investor appetite in artificial intelligence.

### Key Points
- Anthropic reported $4.6 billion in 2025 revenue, representing a 12-fold increase, alongside an $8 billion operating loss.
- The company spent $7.33 billion on compute and infrastructure in 2025, accounting for more than half of its total operating expenses.
- The prospectus warns that the company's lead in generative AI may not persist and notes potential risks regarding model safety and autonomous behaviors.
- Nearly 25% of the company's revenue is concentrated among two customers, with many clients lacking long-term contracts.
- Strategic partners Amazon and Google have provided significant capital and cloud infrastructure to support the development of the Claude model series.

### Why it Matters
This IPO serves as a critical benchmark for the broader AI industry, testing whether public markets will support massive valuations for companies with high growth but significant losses. The offering will reveal investor confidence in the long-term profitability of generative AI amid increasing competition and rising infrastructure costs.
