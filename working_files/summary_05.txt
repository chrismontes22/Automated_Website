Google will let schools turn off AI, but they'll have to give up Search too
============================================================

Google will disable Gemini and other AI tools by default for K-12 student accounts starting in 2027, forcing schools to choose between AI integration and Google Search access.

### Key Points
- Google is disabling Gemini, Gemini Notebook, and Gemini Classroom for K-12 accounts by default beginning in 2027.
- Schools that choose to disable these AI features will also lose access to the standard Google Search engine.
- Google is introducing "Guided Learning" tools that provide step-by-step problem solving rather than direct answers for students.
- Administrators retain the authority to manually enable specific AI services for their student populations.
- The policy change aims to provide schools with more control over AI usage within educational environments.

### Why it Matters
This policy forces a difficult trade-off for educational institutions that rely on Google’s ecosystem but wish to limit AI exposure. By tethering Search access to AI features, Google effectively mandates the adoption of its generative tools for schools that require a functional search engine.
