Pixel 11 Made by Google 2026 launch event: How to watch and what to expect
============================================================

Google will host its Made by Google hardware event on August 12 in New York, where it plans to unveil the Pixel 11 series and new wearable devices.

### Key Points
- The event takes place August 12 at 6:00 PM ET and will be streamed live on the Made by Google YouTube channel.
- The Pixel 11 lineup is expected to include four models featuring a new 2nm Tensor G6 chip and a MediaTek modem.
- Leaks suggest the Pixel 11 series will start at 256GB of storage but may see a $100 price increase across the range.
- The Pixel Watch 5 will likely retain its circular design and size options while receiving a RAM boost to support new on-device AI features.
- Google may announce additional hardware, such as Android XR smart glasses, alongside software updates for Android 17 and Gemini Intelligence.

### Why it Matters
This launch represents a significant shift in Google's hardware strategy as it moves to more efficient TSMC-manufactured processors to address long-standing performance concerns. The potential price hikes and hardware upgrades will test consumer demand for the premium Pixel ecosystem in an increasingly competitive smartphone market.
