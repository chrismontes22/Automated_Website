Business Formation Is Booming. What’s Driving the Surge?
============================================================

The United States is experiencing a historic surge in entrepreneurship, with nearly 2.9 million new businesses formed during the first five months of 2026, according to industry data.

### Key Points
- Registered Agents Inc. reported that 2.9 million new businesses were filed across the U.S. between January and May 2026.
- Gen Z entrepreneurs are increasingly active, accounting for 9% of new business formations in 2025 compared to 5% for Baby Boomers.
- AI-powered tools and cloud infrastructure have significantly lowered startup costs, leading to a 20% increase in one-person firm applications.
- Florida, Texas, and California recorded the highest filing volumes, while Wyoming saw a 21% year-over-year increase in new business entities.
- Many new ventures are non-traditional, including digital agencies, ecommerce shops, and solo consulting firms, rather than brick-and-mortar businesses.

### Why it Matters
This sustained growth in business formation signals a fundamental shift in the American workforce toward independent, flexible, and technology-driven career paths. The trend suggests a more resilient and diversified economy as individuals increasingly leverage digital tools to pursue entrepreneurship as a primary or supplemental income source.
