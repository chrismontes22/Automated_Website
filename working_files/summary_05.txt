CNET Readers Were Right About the iPhone Duo. But There’s One Thing They Got Wrong
============================================================

Apple officially unveiled the iPhone 18 Pro, the foldable iPhone Duo, and the Apple Watch Ultra 4 during its "Surprise and Shine" event led by new CEO John Ternus.

### Key Points
- John Ternus made his debut as Apple CEO, succeeding Tim Cook, who has transitioned to the role of executive chair.
- The iPhone 18 Pro starts at $1,199, while the new foldable iPhone Duo is priced at $1,999.99.
- Apple introduced the Apple Watch Ultra 4 for $800 and the Apple Watch Series 12 for $400, both featuring new processing capabilities.
- The iPhone Duo served as the event's "One More Thing" reveal, featuring a crease-free display and a lightweight design.
- Preorders for the new iPhone models and Apple Watch devices are currently available to consumers.

### Why it Matters
These product launches signal a significant shift in Apple's hardware strategy, particularly with the introduction of a high-priced foldable device and a new leadership era under John Ternus. The premium pricing strategy for the latest iPhone lineup may test consumer demand as the company navigates evolving market expectations for flagship mobile technology.
