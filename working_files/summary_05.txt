★ Retraction: The App Store Rejection of the Week That Was, in Fact, a Correct Rejection
============================================================

Daring Fireball author John Gruber has retracted a recent article after discovering he was misled regarding an Apple App Store rejection involving developer Terry Godier.

### Key Points
- John Gruber retracted his post about an app named "Dark Hours" after learning the developer, Terry Godier, misrepresented the app's original content.
- The app in question was actually submitted to Apple under the name "Asterly" and contained astrology and tarot features, not astronomy.
- Apple’s App Review Board correctly rejected the submission based on its actual content, contradicting the developer's claims of an unfair rejection.
- Godier also faced criticism for naming his web project "Dark Hours," which infringed on an existing open-source project created by Miguel Beher.
- Gruber apologized to his readers and Apple’s review team for publishing inaccurate information based on the developer's false account.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the risks of relying on unverified developer claims when critiquing platform moderation policies. It serves as a reminder of the importance of due diligence in tech journalism to maintain credibility and avoid spreading misinformation about corporate review processes.
