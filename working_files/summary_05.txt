HP EliteBook X G2i laptop review
============================================================

The HP EliteBook X G2i is a premium, ultra-lightweight business laptop featuring an Intel Core Ultra X7 358H processor and Intel Arc B390 graphics for high-performance mobile productivity.

### Key Points
- Weighs less than one kilogram and features a durable magnesium alloy casing.
- Equipped with 32GB of LPDDR5x RAM and a 1TB PCIe NVMe SSD.
- Includes a 14-inch 3K display with a 120Hz refresh rate and 500 nits brightness.
- Features two Thunderbolt 4 ports, full-size HDMI 2.1, and Wi-Fi 7 connectivity.
- Integrated Intel AI Boost NPU supports local AI workloads for applications like Copilot and Photoshop.
- Priced starting at £2,445 or $3,599, targeting professionals who require high power in a portable form factor.

### Why it Matters
This laptop offers a rare combination of high-end processing power and extreme portability, making it a top-tier choice for frequent business travelers who need to perform creative tasks on the go. While the premium price and non-upgradable components may deter budget-conscious users, its robust feature set and superior keyboard quality provide significant value for professional workflows.
