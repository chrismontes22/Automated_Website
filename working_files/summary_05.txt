Google thinks the iPhone Duo could actually be good news for Android foldables
============================================================

Apple is set to enter the foldable smartphone market with the upcoming iPhone Duo, a move Google believes will validate the category and boost overall consumer interest.

### Key Points
- Apple will open pre-orders for the iPhone Duo next month, with projections suggesting it could capture 25% of the foldable market within one year.
- Google’s Android president, Sameer Samat, views Apple’s entry as a positive development that validates years of work by Android manufacturers.
- The increased market presence of foldable devices is expected to incentivize developers to better optimize applications for larger, folding displays.
- Google reports that developers are already actively working on improving app compatibility for Android foldable devices to match the user experience on larger screens.

### Why it Matters
Apple’s entry into the foldable space is expected to drive mainstream adoption and force a higher standard for software optimization across all mobile platforms. This shift could resolve long-standing issues with app scaling on Android foldables, ultimately creating a more consistent and functional experience for users of large-screen devices.
