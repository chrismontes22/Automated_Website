Security Affairs newsletter Round 599 by Pierluigi Paganini – INTERNATIONAL EDITION
============================================================

The latest Security Affairs newsletter highlights a surge in global cybercrime, including major data breaches, international arrests of ransomware actors, and evolving AI-driven hacking threats across multiple sectors.

### Key Points
- Authorities arrested a key ShinyHunters suspect in Jordan and extradited an Iranian national from Montenegro for hacking American universities.
- A massive data breach in Denmark exposed the personal information of 8.8 million individuals, marking a significant security failure.
- German law enforcement successfully arrested a core member of the Qilin ransomware group following an international extradition process.
- The U.S. Department of Justice and FBI seized infrastructure used by state-sponsored Chinese hackers for spear-phishing and vulnerability scanning.
- New research identifies sophisticated threats, including the "Midnight Mimosa" mobile malware and AI-driven campaigns targeting South Korean financial institutions.

### Why it Matters
These developments underscore the increasing effectiveness of international law enforcement cooperation in dismantling global cybercriminal networks and state-sponsored hacking operations. As threat actors adopt AI-driven tools and novel evasion techniques, organizations must prioritize robust defense strategies to protect critical infrastructure and sensitive user data.
