Anthropic’s IPO Prospectus Reveals What’s Under the Hood
============================================================

Artificial intelligence startup Anthropic has officially filed paperwork for an initial public offering on the Nasdaq exchange, signaling a major move toward becoming a publicly traded company.

### Key Points
- Anthropic plans to list on the Nasdaq under the ticker symbol ANT, with a potential IPO launch scheduled for later in November.
- The company reported revenue of over $16 billion for the first half of 2026, a significant increase from $4.5 billion in 2025.
- Despite revenue growth, the firm posted a net loss of $80.7 billion during the first half of 2026.
- The prospectus highlights over 11,900 enterprise customers spending at least $100,000 annually on the company's Claude AI models.
- Risk disclosures include intense industry competition, potential catastrophic AI misuse, and ongoing regulatory challenges regarding government supply chain designations.

### Why it Matters
This IPO represents a pivotal moment for the artificial intelligence sector as investors weigh the massive revenue growth of leading labs against their substantial operational losses. The filing provides a rare, transparent look at the financial sustainability and existential risks inherent in scaling advanced machine intelligence for the public market.
