Not Asus, Not MSI: Consumer Reports Says This Laptop Brand Is The Most Reliable
============================================================

Apple ranks as the most reliable laptop brand according to Consumer Reports, outperforming competitors like Asus and MSI based on data from over 75,000 user surveys.

### Key Points
- Apple secured the top reliability ranking across multiple size categories, including the 12- to 13-inch MacBook Air and the 14-inch MacBook Pro.
- The MacBook Pro MX2X3LL/A is currently rated as one of the most reliable laptops overall for work and multimedia productivity.
- LG emerged as a top-performing Windows-based alternative, with the LG Gram 17-inch Copilot+ PC leading its respective size category.
- Consumer Reports derived these rankings from estimated problem rates collected through extensive consumer surveys.

### Why it Matters
These rankings provide consumers with data-driven insights to help minimize the risk of hardware defects when investing in expensive computing equipment. Understanding brand reliability helps users balance operating system preferences with long-term product durability and performance expectations.
