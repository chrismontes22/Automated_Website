Apple Creator Studio Brings MotionVFX and AI Assistants to Subscribers
============================================================

Apple has announced a series of AI-powered updates for its Creator Studio software suite, alongside new hardware releases including the iMac, MacBook Pros, and an OLED iPad Mini.

### Key Points
- Final Cut Pro will integrate MotionVFX tools, while Logic Pro gains a beta AI assistant developed with OpenAI for US-based music editors.
- Pixelmator Pro is introducing a generative fill feature designed to resize and automatically complete image gaps.
- Apple is adding AI-driven assistants to its productivity applications, including Keynote, Pages, and Numbers.
- These software updates will be available to Apple Creator Studio subscribers as a free download starting in November.
- Apple Creator Studio subscriptions begin at $13 per month, with new MacBook purchasers receiving a complimentary three-month trial.

### Why it Matters
These updates signal Apple's aggressive push to integrate generative AI across its professional creative and productivity software ecosystem. By aligning these tools with its latest hardware, the company aims to maintain its competitive edge against rivals like Adobe and Google in the professional software market.
