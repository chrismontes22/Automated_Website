How Encryption Starts Before a Live Blackjack Session
============================================================

Secure live blackjack platforms in Ontario rely on Transport Layer Security (TLS) and HSTS protocols to encrypt data transmissions between user browsers and gaming servers during online sessions.

### Key Points
- TLS 1.3 handshakes establish encrypted connections by deriving unique traffic keys rather than transmitting reusable passwords across the network.
- HSTS policies ensure browsers exclusively use HTTPS, preventing initial unencrypted requests that could expose sensitive user data.
- Session identifiers and tokens require Secure and HttpOnly flags to prevent unauthorized access to account information during active gaming sessions.
- Secure WebSockets (wss://) and WebRTC standards are utilized to protect real-time player commands and video streaming feeds from interception.
- Content-delivery networks may terminate encrypted connections, meaning end-to-end security requires verification of the entire data path from server to player.
- Encryption protects data transmission but does not verify gambling licenses, ensure physical game integrity, or prevent financial risks associated with gambling.

### Why it Matters
Understanding the technical layers of online security helps players identify how platforms protect personal data and account credentials during live gaming sessions. While encryption is essential for digital safety, it remains distinct from the regulatory oversight and responsible gambling tools required for a secure betting environment.
