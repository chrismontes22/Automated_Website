Apple Flags Discovery Delays in Its Lawsuit Against Jon Prosser
============================================================

Apple reports that tech leaker Jon Prosser has ceased communication regarding discovery in its ongoing trade secret lawsuit concerning unreleased iOS 26 software and confidential device access.

### Key Points
- Apple filed a status report in the Northern District of California noting no contact from Prosser’s legal team since July 6, 2026.
- Prosser’s attorney cited the birth of a second child as the primary reason for the recent delay in providing discovery materials.
- Co-defendant Michael Ramacciotti continues to cooperate with Apple and has agreed to a second deposition scheduled for September.
- The lawsuit, filed in July 2025, alleges Prosser and Ramacciotti misappropriated trade secrets related to the "Liquid Glass" iOS redesign.
- Prosser previously faced a default judgment in October 2025, which was later set aside in June 2026 after he filed a formal answer.

### Why it Matters
This case highlights Apple's aggressive legal strategy to protect its intellectual property and curb the unauthorized disclosure of unreleased product information. A permanent injunction against Prosser could fundamentally alter his ability to operate his technology-focused YouTube channel by restricting his coverage of future Apple developments.
