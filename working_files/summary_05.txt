Muse Creates Detailed Profiles of All Your Friends and Family
============================================================

Meta’s new AI assistant, Muse, is facing scrutiny after researchers extracted internal files revealing the tool’s capability to compile detailed, persistent dossiers on users' personal relationships and contacts.

### Key Points
- Security researcher Karan Joshi extracted Muse’s internal system instructions by prompting the AI to share its own software files.
- The instructions reveal that Muse creates dedicated "pages" for individuals in a user's life, tracking history, shared milestones, and relationship dynamics.
- Meta designed Muse to store user data in individual virtual machines, allowing users to wipe memories or disconnect external services at any time.
- The AI is programmed to suggest ways to "strengthen" relationships, such as reminders for birthdays, anniversaries, or topics to discuss.
- Meta maintains that the system relies on public information and user-provided data to offer personalized assistance, such as remembering professional or personal contacts.

### Why it Matters
The integration of AI assistants into personal data streams creates significant privacy concerns regarding the depth of information companies can infer about users' social lives. As these tools become more proactive in managing human relationships, the potential for data misuse or over-reliance on automated social management continues to grow.
