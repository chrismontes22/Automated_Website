There Is No AI (It's Just People) with Jaron Lanier
============================================================

Computer scientist Jaron Lanier joins StarTalk to discuss the evolution of the internet, the dangers of social media addiction, and why viewing artificial intelligence as human collaboration is essential.

### Key Points
- Jaron Lanier, known as the "father of virtual reality," argues that current tech business models prioritize influence generation, which fuels social media addiction and societal polarization.
- The discussion highlights that AI models are built on human data, and reframing them as collaborative tools rather than independent entities could improve security and creativity.
- Lanier advocates for "data dignity," a concept where individuals are recognized and compensated for the data they contribute to digital systems.
- The panel explores the "network effect," explaining how digital platforms naturally trend toward hyper-centralized power, often at the expense of user well-being and market competition.
- Lanier suggests that the "black box" nature of AI is a choice, and opening it to reveal the human labor behind the data is necessary to solve issues like hallucinations and security flaws.

### Why it Matters
The conversation challenges the prevailing tech industry narrative that AI is an autonomous, inevitable force, instead framing it as a system that can be steered through better business models and regulation. By shifting the focus toward data dignity and human-centric design, society may be able to mitigate the addictive and manipulative aspects of modern digital platforms.
