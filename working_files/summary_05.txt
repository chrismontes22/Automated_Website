Mark Zuckerberg's 'AI is great, really' PR campaign is in full swing. Here's a better idea.
============================================================

Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg has launched a widespread public relations campaign to improve American sentiment toward artificial intelligence by highlighting its potential benefits and countering industry-wide skepticism.

### Key Points
- Meta released a promotional advertisement and Zuckerberg published a Wall Street Journal op-ed to advocate for the positive societal impact of superintelligence.
- Zuckerberg used the company's recent earnings call to reassure investors that Meta’s AI development is building a beneficial future for the general public.
- The campaign aims to counter warnings from OpenAI CEO Sam Altman and Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei regarding potential job losses and existential risks.
- Public perception of AI remains low due to concerns over job displacement and the tech industry's political alignments since late 2024.
- Analysts suggest that direct community investments, such as tax-funded bonuses for local teachers near data centers, may be more effective than traditional marketing.

### Why it Matters
The tech industry is struggling to overcome widespread public skepticism regarding the long-term utility and safety of artificial intelligence. Shifting the narrative from theoretical promises to tangible local economic benefits could be essential for companies seeking to maintain their social license to operate.
