The Galaxy S27 and S27 Pro just leaked, and one of them looks seriously lazy
============================================================

Leaked renders of the upcoming Samsung Galaxy S27 series indicate that the base model will retain its predecessor's design, while the Pro and Ultra models adopt new aesthetics.

### Key Points
- The base Galaxy S27 will maintain the vertical camera layout and dimensions found on the current Galaxy S26.
- Samsung is introducing a horizontal camera plateau design for the Galaxy S27 Pro and Ultra, drawing inspiration from Apple's iPhone 17 Pro.
- Leaked renders confirm the Galaxy S27 Pro will not include an integrated S Pen slot, unlike the Ultra model.
- The Galaxy S27 Ultra is expected to feature a more rounded frame to improve ergonomics and comfort for users.
- Samsung is reportedly repositioning the speaker grille and microphone on the Galaxy S27 Pro compared to previous iterations.

### Why it Matters
These design choices suggest Samsung is segmenting its flagship lineup by reserving premium aesthetic changes for its most expensive devices. This strategy helps differentiate the Pro and Ultra models from the base Galaxy S27, potentially influencing consumer purchasing decisions based on hardware styling and feature sets.
