The Galaxy S27 Ultra looks like an outright iPhone copy in this latest leak
============================================================

Early leaks for the Samsung Galaxy S27 Ultra suggest a significant design overhaul featuring a horizontal camera plateau, native Qi2 magnetic charging support, and two distinct size options.

### Key Points
- Leaked renders indicate a shift from vertical camera layouts to a horizontal plateau design resembling the iPhone 17 Pro.
- The device is expected to include native Qi2 magnetic charging support, as suggested by visible rings in early design mockups.
- Samsung may offer the Galaxy S27 Ultra in two different sizes, potentially introducing a more compact version of the flagship model.
- Prominent leakers including Sonny Dickson, Max Jambor, and Ice Universe provided the initial design details via social media.

### Why it Matters
This potential redesign signals a major departure from the consistent aesthetic Samsung has maintained for its flagship Ultra series over the past several years. By adopting features like Qi2 compatibility and varied sizing, Samsung aims to modernize its hardware to better compete with Apple’s latest smartphone offerings.
