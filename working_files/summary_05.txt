Here’s how iPhone Ultra’s form factor could compare to other Apple products
============================================================

Apple is expected to unveil its first foldable smartphone, the iPhone Ultra, in September, featuring a design and display dimensions similar to the recently released Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8.

### Key Points
- The upcoming iPhone Ultra will reportedly feature a 5.5-inch outer display and a 7.6-inch inner screen.
- Samsung’s newly launched Galaxy Z Fold 8 shares identical screen dimensions, providing a physical reference for the anticipated Apple device.
- Early comparisons suggest the iPhone Ultra will offer a tablet-like experience similar to an iPad mini but with thinner bezels.
- The foldable form factor may appeal to users who previously favored the compact design of the discontinued iPhone mini series.
- Apple is scheduled to officially reveal the iPhone Ultra during a product event next month.

### Why it Matters
The release of the iPhone Ultra marks Apple's first entry into the competitive foldable smartphone market, directly challenging Samsung's established product line. This shift could influence consumer expectations for mobile hardware and potentially revitalize interest in compact, high-performance devices.
