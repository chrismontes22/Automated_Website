Anker’s travel charger works in almost any country and is down to $20
============================================================

Anker’s Nano Travel Adapter is currently discounted to $19.99, offering a compact, multi-port charging solution compatible with electrical outlets in over 200 countries and regions for international travelers.

### Key Points
- The adapter is available for $19.99 at Amazon, Best Buy, and Anker’s website, matching its lowest recorded price.
- It features two USB-C ports, two USB-A ports, and one AC outlet, allowing users to charge up to five devices simultaneously.
- The device weighs 3.8 ounces and measures one inch thick when the prongs are folded for storage.
- USB-C ports provide up to 20W of power, suitable for smartphones, tablets, and earbuds, while the AC outlet supports larger devices like laptop chargers.
- The unit does not convert voltage, meaning users must ensure their appliances are compatible with local electrical standards before use.

### Why it Matters
This portable charger provides a versatile, space-saving solution for travelers needing to power multiple mobile devices across diverse international electrical systems. By consolidating several ports into one compact unit, it simplifies luggage organization while maintaining essential connectivity for modern electronics.
