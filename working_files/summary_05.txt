Optical Company Starman Buys GoPro for $285 Million, Plans Move Into AI, Defense, and National Security
============================================================

GoPro has entered a definitive merger agreement with Starman Optical, Inc. in a deal valued at $285 million to expand into AI infrastructure and defense-related optical markets.

### Key Points
- GoPro shareholders will receive $1.14 per share in cash, with existing investors retaining approximately 10 percent ownership.
- The transaction includes the full repayment of GoPro’s $92 million in outstanding debt.
- Starman Optical specializes in U.S.-manufactured optical transceivers used primarily in AI data centers.
- Content creator Markiplier, who recently acquired an 8.5 percent stake in GoPro, becomes the company's largest single shareholder.
- The combined entity plans to focus on imaging and optics for the defense, government, robotics, and aerospace sectors.
- The merger is expected to close by the end of 2024, pending regulatory approvals.

### Why it Matters
This merger signals a strategic pivot for GoPro from a consumer-focused camera brand toward the high-growth sectors of AI infrastructure and national security. By integrating U.S.-made optical technology, the company aims to capitalize on increasing domestic demand for secure, non-foreign hardware in critical industries.
