Create a handoff doc
============================================================

Users can leverage artificial intelligence to automatically maintain comprehensive handoff documentation, ensuring project continuity and knowledge transfer by prompting models to track decisions, file locations, and project status.

### Key Points
- AI models can be instructed to maintain a living handoff document that tracks project goals, current progress, and specific file locations.
- The proposed documentation structure includes sections for assumptions, open dilemmas, key stakeholders, and step-by-step instructions for project handovers.
- Users are encouraged to store these specific instructions in their AI's permanent settings to ensure the documentation updates automatically after every session.
- The system requires the AI to distinguish between user-provided information and its own inferences while documenting the reasoning behind specific project choices.
- Stale details are managed by condensing them into dated summaries to prevent the document from becoming bloated over time.

### Why it Matters
Automating project documentation reduces the administrative burden on employees while ensuring that critical institutional knowledge remains accessible during staff transitions. This practice minimizes operational downtime and prevents the loss of context when projects are handed off to new team members or migrated to different platforms.
