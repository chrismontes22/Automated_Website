Google’s AI Wants to Call Stores for You — Here Are the Problems With That
============================================================

Google has introduced a new agentic feature for Gemini on Pixel devices that allows the AI to make routine phone calls to businesses on behalf of users.

### Key Points
- Users can trigger the feature using the "@Call for me" prompt to verify business hours, check product availability, or request service pricing.
- The tool is currently restricted to users over 18 in the United States with a Pixel 11 or later and a paid Google AI subscription.
- Gemini is programmed to disclose its identity as an AI to the recipient before initiating the conversation.
- Security protocols prevent the AI from sharing sensitive information like credit card numbers, passwords, or Social Security numbers.
- The feature is currently in public beta and requires the latest versions of the Gemini and Phone by Google applications.
- Google has implemented daily usage limits to prevent potential abuse and manage the energy demands of the AI processing.

### Why it Matters
This development signals a shift toward autonomous AI agents capable of performing real-world tasks, though its utility remains constrained by security safeguards and potential business resistance. The success of this technology depends on how effectively companies adapt their operations to handle high volumes of automated, AI-generated inquiries.
