Apple Retail Stores Preparing for New Home Product Launches
============================================================

Apple is reorganizing its retail store layouts this fall to accommodate new home products, including a rumored smart home hub featuring a seven-inch display and integrated Siri AI.

### Key Points
- Apple is modifying "Avenue" perimeter wall displays to create dedicated accessory bays for upcoming home hardware.
- The retail updates are expected to support the launch of a new smart home hub arriving between October and early next year.
- The upcoming hub will feature a seven-inch square display, a new operating system based on tvOS, and options for wall or tabletop mounting.
- Updated versions of the Apple TV and HomePod mini are also anticipated, potentially featuring faster A17 Pro and S9-series chips.
- macOS Tahoe code suggests the new device will utilize a widget-based interface similar to iPhone Lock Screen customization.

### Why it Matters
This retail shift signals Apple's strategic push to establish a more prominent presence in the competitive smart home market. By creating dedicated physical spaces for these devices, the company aims to demonstrate the utility of its new home hub and its broader ecosystem integration to consumers.
