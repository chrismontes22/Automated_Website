$100 Best Buy gift cards will be $60 at stores Saturday
============================================================

Best Buy is offering a one-day promotion on Saturday, August 22, allowing customers to purchase a $100 gift card for $60 to celebrate the retailer's 60th anniversary.

### Key Points
- The promotion is available exclusively in-store on August 22 while supplies last.
- Customers can purchase a $100 gift card for a discounted price of $60.
- Gift cards cannot be used to purchase other gift cards or prepaid debit cards.
- The cards are compatible with existing store promotions and product discounts.
- Anniversary sales include the iPad Air M4 for $649 and AirPods Max 2 for $429.
- The HP OmniBook X Flip 2-in-1 laptop is discounted by $450 to a price of $749.99.

### Why it Matters
This rare discount on store credit provides significant savings for consumers looking to purchase electronics during the retailer's anniversary sale event. By combining this gift card deal with existing price cuts on major tech products, shoppers can maximize their purchasing power on high-end hardware.
