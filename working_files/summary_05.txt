ShinyHunters Extorted Boeing Spin-off Prior to Arrests
============================================================

Jordanian authorities have detained a teenager known as "Rey," a suspected leader of the ShinyHunters hacking group, who is now cooperating with the FBI following multiple high-profile data breaches.

### Key Points
- The suspect, identified as Saif Al-din Khader, was apprehended while attempting to extort a former Boeing subsidiary, Jeppesen ForeFlight.
- ShinyHunters gained widespread access to corporate and government systems by exploiting a critical vulnerability (CVE-2026-35273) in Oracle’s PeopleSoft software.
- The group’s recent activities included a breach of an FBI recruitment website that exposed sensitive medical and psychiatric records of over 5,000 personnel.
- Dutch authorities recently arrested another alleged ShinyHunters affiliate, Pepijn van der Stap, who is also facing separate allegations of orchestrating murder-for-hire plots.
- Security researchers describe the modern iteration of ShinyHunters as a "franchise" operation, where various freelancers use the brand to extort victims for a share of the profits.

### Why it Matters
The arrest of a key operator highlights the FBI's aggressive pursuit of cybercriminals who exploit widely used enterprise software to compromise sensitive government and private sector data. This case underscores the evolving nature of digital extortion, where fragmented groups leverage established criminal brands to maximize the impact and profitability of their attacks.
