A New Mexico Community Was a Place to Build a Life. Then a Hypersonic Missile Factory Showed Up
============================================================

Defense startup Castelion is constructing a massive hypersonic missile manufacturing facility in Rio Rancho, New Mexico, sparking safety concerns among residents living near the high-risk production site.

### Key Points
- Castelion’s Project Ranger facility is located within 1,000 yards of approximately 450 homes in the Rio Rancho Estates community.
- A Sandia National Labs hazard report warns that a worst-case explosion could shatter windows four miles away and release toxic hydrochloric acid gas.
- Internal documents reveal that local fire departments determined 17 of the 26 site buildings would be left to burn during a chemical fire due to safety risks.
- The company has secured over $500 million in US military contracts, including a $200 million award for its Blackbeard hypersonic missile.
- Sandoval County granted Castelion a 95 percent property tax discount and $125 million in industrial revenue bonds to facilitate the project.

### Why it Matters
The rapid development of Project Ranger highlights the tension between aggressive defense-sector expansion and the safety of surrounding residential communities. This case underscores the potential for significant public health risks when high-stakes military manufacturing is fast-tracked through local government partnerships with limited transparency.
