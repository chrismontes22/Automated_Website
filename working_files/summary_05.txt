Dogecoin and BNB lead majors higher as bitcoin slips near $63,700
============================================================

Dogecoin and BNB outperformed other major cryptocurrencies on Wednesday as bitcoin prices dipped toward $63,700 amid broader market anticipation of upcoming U.S. inflation data and geopolitical tensions.

### Key Points
- Dogecoin rose nearly 3% to 7 cents, while BNB gained 2% to reach $614.
- Bitcoin remained the only major cryptocurrency to record losses on both a daily and weekly basis.
- South Korea’s Kospi index jumped 4.6% following strong earnings reports from Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix.
- Brent crude oil prices climbed above $90 per barrel, marking a six-session winning streak amid ongoing Middle East instability.
- Investors are closely monitoring Thursday’s U.S. Consumer Price Index report for signals regarding potential Federal Reserve interest rate cuts.

### Why it Matters
The upcoming inflation data and geopolitical developments in the Middle East are critical factors currently driving volatility across both cryptocurrency and equity markets. A lower-than-expected inflation print could trigger a relief rally by strengthening the case for Federal Reserve interest rate cuts later this year.
