Elon Musk wants to take on Verizon and AT&T. The price tag could be staggering.
============================================================

SpaceX is pursuing an ambitious plan to challenge major U.S. wireless carriers by negotiating access to 80,000 cell sites to establish a nationwide Starlink mobile network.

### Key Points
- SpaceX is in talks with infrastructure firms like Crown Castle to secure space on 80,000 cell towers across the United States.
- The initiative aims to position Starlink as a fourth major wireless carrier alongside incumbents Verizon, AT&T, and T-Mobile.
- Goldman Sachs analysts estimate that leasing 80,000 cell sites could cost SpaceX approximately $4 billion annually in rental payments.
- Shares of AT&T and T-Mobile fell by up to 6% following reports of the company's aggressive expansion strategy.
- Industry experts warn that matching the performance and infrastructure scale of established telecom giants will require years of significant capital investment.

### Why it Matters
This expansion represents a significant competitive threat to the established telecommunications oligopoly that has dominated the U.S. market for decades. If successful, SpaceX could fundamentally alter the wireless landscape by leveraging its satellite network to provide nationwide mobile coverage.
