MarketBeat Week in Review – 07/20- 07/24
============================================================

U.S. stocks declined this week as investors shifted toward value-oriented assets amid concerns over massive artificial intelligence capital expenditures and rising geopolitical tensions in the Middle East.

### Key Points
- S&P 500 companies are reporting strong earnings, with 88% of early reporters beating earnings per share expectations and 85% exceeding revenue forecasts.
- Oracle Corp. (ORCL) faced a credit rating downgrade to BBB by S&P Global Ratings due to concerns regarding negative free cash flow.
- Tesla (TSLA) shares dropped following an earnings miss and investor apprehension regarding the company's aggressive capital expenditure forecast.
- Microsoft (MSFT) is scheduled to report Q4 2026 earnings on July 29, with investors closely monitoring its fiscal year 2027 capital expenditure guidance.
- The Federal Reserve is expected to maintain current interest rates during its upcoming meeting while investors search for signals regarding future policy shifts.

### Why it Matters
The market is currently recalibrating as investors weigh the long-term potential of artificial intelligence against the immediate financial strain of heavy infrastructure spending. This transition period highlights a broader shift in sentiment, where companies with high capital requirements face increased scrutiny regarding their free cash flow and overall valuation.
