The Most Advanced Home Device I’ve Tested This Year Is in My Garage
============================================================

The Chamberlain Premier Smart Garage Door Opener integrates advanced AI-powered security cameras, 2,000-lumen LED lighting, and remote MyQ app controls into a comprehensive, high-performance home automation and monitoring system.

### Key Points
- The system features a 1.4 HP motor designed for quieter, smoother garage door operation compared to standard models.
- Integrated 2,000-lumen LED lighting provides sufficient illumination to replace existing overhead garage fixtures.
- The built-in security camera offers a 130-degree field of view with AI-driven motion detection for vehicles, packages, and people.
- Users can manage access via the MyQ app, including scheduling, remote operation, and one-time entry for Amazon Key-In deliveries.
- Advanced features, such as cloud video storage and AI recognition, require a MyQ subscription starting at approximately $50 per year.
- Professional installation is recommended due to the complexity of replacing the motor, railing, and sensor components.

### Why it Matters
This device transforms the garage from a simple storage space into a monitored, automated hub that enhances overall home security and convenience. By consolidating lighting, surveillance, and remote access into one unit, it offers a streamlined solution for homeowners looking to modernize their property and protect vulnerable entry points.
