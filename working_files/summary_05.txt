The AI Governance Best Practices Every Small Business Owner Needs to Know
============================================================

Small businesses face significant legal and compliance risks when adopting artificial intelligence tools without proper governance, as they are held to the same regulatory standards as large corporations.

### Key Points
- Small businesses often lack dedicated legal or IT departments to audit AI applications before deployment, increasing exposure to regulatory penalties.
- New automated decision-making regulations, such as those effective in California as of January 1, 2026, apply to all businesses regardless of size.
- Companies are legally responsible for all AI-generated outputs, meaning misleading claims or errors made by bots carry the same liability as human mistakes.
- Many states now mandate clear disclosure when customers are interacting with AI chatbots rather than human representatives.
- The NIST AI Risk Management Framework is increasingly viewed as the standard for responsible governance, even for smaller organizations.

### Why it Matters
Small businesses are disproportionately vulnerable to legal action because they often lack the resources to implement robust data security and compliance oversight. Failing to govern AI tools can lead to severe financial penalties and loss of consumer trust, as regulators do not provide exemptions for smaller entities.
