Honor's Robot Phone is finally here: ARRI partnership, Agile Gimbal spotlight a new future
============================================================

Honor has officially launched its innovative Robot Phone in China, featuring a motorized 3-axis gimbal camera, advanced AI subject tracking, and professional-grade ARRI cinematic imaging capabilities for users.

### Key Points
- The device features a 200MP main camera on a motorized Agile Gimbal capable of autonomous panning and tilting.
- It incorporates ARRI Cinematic Imaging technology, including LogC3 and Wide Gamut 3, powered by the proprietary H1 Image Chip.
- Hardware specifications include a 6.3-inch LTPO OLED display, a 7,060mAh silicon-carbon battery, and the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 processor.
- AI-driven features include YOYO Subject Tracking, voice source localization, and gesture recognition for hands-free operation.
- Pre-orders are currently open in China starting at RMB 9,999, with an official market release scheduled for August 18.

### Why it Matters
This device represents a significant shift in mobile photography by integrating professional-grade mechanical stabilization and cinema-standard color science into a consumer smartphone. By automating complex camera movements, Honor is challenging the boundaries between traditional mobile devices and dedicated handheld cinema cameras.
