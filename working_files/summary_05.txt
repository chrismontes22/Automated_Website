FBI hunting the hackers who stole its employees' sensitive data
============================================================

The FBI has launched an aggressive investigation into the cybercriminal group ShinyHunters after the hackers breached the FBIJobs.gov portal and stole sensitive personnel data from the bureau.

### Key Points
- The cybercriminal group ShinyHunters claimed responsibility for the breach, which potentially exposed terabytes of sensitive FBI employee data.
- Stolen information reportedly includes job applications, medical records, promotion details, and sensitive family information.
- Mandiant research indicates the hackers may have exploited a vulnerability in Oracle’s PeopleSoft human resources software used by the FBI.
- FBI Assistant Director Brett Leatherman publicly vowed to track down the hackers, warning them to come forward before facing legal consequences.
- The breach has caused significant concern among current and former employees regarding the potential weaponization of their personal data by criminal or state actors.

### Why it Matters
This breach represents a major security failure that could compromise the safety of undercover personnel and sensitive government operations. The incident highlights the persistent vulnerability of federal agencies to data extortion and the significant risks posed when third-party software tools are exploited by sophisticated criminal collectives.
