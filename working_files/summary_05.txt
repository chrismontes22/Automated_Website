Bose Just Dropped New Sport Open Earbuds and Ultra Open Earbuds (2nd Gen)
============================================================

Bose has announced the second-generation Ultra Open Earbuds and the new Sport Open Earbuds, both featuring updated designs and improved performance for the growing open-ear audio market.

### Key Points
- The Ultra Open Earbuds (2nd Gen) retail for $299 and feature upgraded drivers, deeper bass, and an increased battery life of up to 9 hours.
- The new Sport Open Earbuds are priced at $199 and offer an IP54 rating for enhanced sweat, splash, and dust resistance.
- Both models are available for preorder starting September 19, with official shipping scheduled for October 1.
- Ultra Open Earbuds (2nd Gen) include Bose’s TrueSpatial technology with a new Cinema mode for Immersive Audio.
- The Sport Open Earbuds utilize a soft silicone construction and provide up to 7 hours of battery life.

### Why it Matters
These product launches signal Bose's attempt to maintain its premium position in the competitive clip-on earbud market against lower-priced alternatives. By diversifying its lineup with both high-end and sport-focused models, the company aims to capture a broader range of active users while defending its market share.
