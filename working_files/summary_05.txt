Fake Cases Hint at New Clear and TechWoven Colors for iPhone 18 Pro
============================================================

Leaked images and videos of third-party iPhone 18 Pro Max cases suggest potential new color options and design features for Apple’s upcoming official accessory lineup launching this September.

### Key Points
- Leaker "DuanRui" shared images of counterfeit cases that often reflect genuine design dimensions and color trends ahead of official Apple product launches.
- A light blue tinted clear case features the MagSafe-concealing opaque square, potentially signaling a matching Light Blue finish for the iPhone 18 Pro.
- New TechWoven case colors include cherry red, which aligns with rumors of a flagship "Dark Cherry" finish for the upcoming Pro models.
- The iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max are expected to debut in September alongside the rumored foldable "iPhone Ultra" device.

### Why it Matters
These early accessory leaks provide a reliable preview of Apple's design direction and color palette for its next flagship smartphone generation. Understanding these hardware trends helps consumers and industry analysts anticipate the aesthetic changes and product ecosystem shifts arriving with the upcoming September launch.
