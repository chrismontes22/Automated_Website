South Korea’s Kospi index sinks more than 10% on heavy selling of chipmaking stocks
============================================================

Wall Street indices showed mixed results on Tuesday as strong corporate earnings reports boosted the Dow Jones Industrial Average while a global sell-off in semiconductor stocks pressured the Nasdaq.

### Key Points
- The Dow Jones Industrial Average rose 537 points, while the S&P 500 gained 0.2% and the Nasdaq composite fell 0.2%.
- Coca-Cola shares climbed 5% following a 7% revenue increase, while Sherwin-Williams and Illinois Tool Works also beat quarterly earnings expectations.
- Micron Technology shares dropped 8.9%, leading a decline in AI-linked chipmakers that also included Advanced Micro Devices and Applied Materials.
- South Korea’s Kospi index plunged 10.8% due to sharp losses for SK Hynix and Samsung Electronics, triggering temporary trading halts.
- Market analysts cited concerns over China’s advancing chipmaking capabilities and the long-term sustainability of AI-driven investment growth.

### Why it Matters
The divergence between strong traditional corporate earnings and the volatility in the semiconductor sector highlights growing investor anxiety regarding the sustainability of the artificial intelligence boom. This shift suggests that markets are increasingly sensitive to potential competition from China and the risk that AI-related capital expenditures may not yield expected productivity gains.
