New iPad Mini Rumored to Have 60Hz OLED Display Ahead of Launch
============================================================

Apple’s upcoming iPad mini 8 is expected to feature a 60Hz LTPS OLED display, potentially disappointing users who anticipated the high-refresh-rate ProMotion technology found in Pro models.

### Key Points
- Reports from supply chain sources indicate the new iPad mini will utilize a 60Hz LTPS OLED panel rather than a 120Hz LTPO display.
- The device is expected to include an upgraded A20 Pro chip, a landscape-oriented FaceTime camera, and a water-resistant design.
- Bloomberg reports that Apple plans to debut the new tablet on or around Tuesday, October 27.
- Unlike the iPad Pro’s two-stack LTPO technology, the LTPS panel is typically limited to fixed refresh rates and lower peak brightness.

### Why it Matters
The decision to stick with a 60Hz display highlights Apple's strategy of segmenting hardware features to differentiate its premium Pro lineup from smaller, more affordable devices. This choice may impact consumer perception as high-refresh-rate screens become increasingly standard across the broader smartphone and tablet market.
