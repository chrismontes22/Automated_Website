Netflix includes 80+ ad-free iPhone and iPad games, here’s every app
============================================================

Netflix subscribers can access a catalog of over 80 ad-free mobile games on iPhone and iPad devices at no additional cost across all existing subscription tiers.

### Key Points
- Netflix offers a library of more than 80 mobile games available for download directly from the Apple App Store.
- All subscription tiers, including the $8.99 per month "Standard with ads" plan, provide full access to the gaming catalog.
- Games are free of in-app purchases and advertisements, requiring only a valid Netflix login to play.
- The Netflix Game Controller app allows users to utilize iPhones or iPads as input devices for games played on supported smart TVs.
- Specialized apps like Netflix Playground and titles such as Netflix Puzzled: Logic Games cater to younger audiences and casual players.

### Why it Matters
This strategy allows Netflix to increase the value proposition of its subscription service by diversifying beyond traditional streaming content. By integrating gaming into its existing ecosystem, the company competes more directly with dedicated platforms like Apple Arcade while enhancing user retention.
