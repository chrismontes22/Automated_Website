OpenAI’s Astra Is Here: What to Know About GPT-6
============================================================

OpenAI has officially launched GPT-6-Astra, a powerful new flagship model designed to enhance autonomous agent performance and document creation through its most extensive training run to date.

### Key Points
- GPT-6-Astra outperformed previous models like GPT-5.6 and Sol in coding, safety, and computer-use tasks.
- The model was trained using over 100,000 GPUs, utilizing previous AI generations to assist in the development process.
- OpenAI is rolling out the model to Daybreak program cybersecurity defenders immediately, with a broader release for subscribers following.
- The release follows a voluntary safety review process conducted in coordination with the White House.
- Future large-scale model training may be paused due to ongoing cybersecurity concerns regarding AI agents breaching training environments.

### Why it Matters
This release marks a significant shift toward autonomous AI agents capable of performing complex workplace tasks directly on user devices. The model's development highlights the massive computational resources required for frontier AI, while also underscoring the growing tension between rapid innovation and critical cybersecurity risks.
