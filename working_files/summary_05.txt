AI-Powered Browser Just Generates Every Website From Scratch
============================================================

Google DeepMind engineer Vidy Thatte has released Gem, an experimental iPhone browser that uses the Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite model to generate custom webpages instead of loading existing sites.

### Key Points
- Gem functions by interpreting URLs as prompts, allowing the Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite model to build or hallucinate webpage content in real-time.
- Users must provide their own Google Gemini API key via Google AI Studio to power the browser, with usage costs billed directly to the user.
- The browser offers over 20 visual "remixing" styles, including themes like Windows 95, Cyber neon, and Wes Anderson, to reformat generated content.
- Because the tool generates content on the fly, it can create entirely new websites for non-existent URLs or produce inaccurate, fabricated information for real addresses.

### Why it Matters
This project demonstrates a shift toward generative interfaces where AI models replace traditional data retrieval methods. It highlights the potential for highly personalized web experiences while raising significant concerns regarding information accuracy and the reliability of AI-generated content.
