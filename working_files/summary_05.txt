Meet Mori, a Cute Robot Mushroom That Doesn’t Really Say (or Do) Anything
============================================================

Startup OKA has launched Mori, a mushroom-shaped home robot lamp designed to provide companionship and meditative experiences by using local AI to react to human movement and behavior.

### Key Points
- Mori is a 12-inch-tall, battery-powered robot that uses a camera and microphone to track user activity and provide interactive, non-verbal responses.
- The device features "Attentive Intelligence" to recognize users, detect stress or inactivity, and offer calming nature soundscapes or breathing exercises.
- Privacy is managed through local data processing, with facial feature data encrypted on the device and raw audio or video files discarded immediately.
- The Founder’s Edition is priced at $649, while the standard edition costs $849, with additional subscription fees required for full access to nature soundscapes.
- Unlike smart assistants like Amazon Echo, Mori intentionally lacks voice control and internet-based smart home integration to focus on emotional presence rather than utility.

### Why it Matters
Mori represents a shift in consumer robotics toward "digital pets" that prioritize emotional well-being and companionship over traditional productivity or smart home automation. Its success will test whether consumers are willing to pay premium prices for specialized, limited-function hardware designed to reduce digital overstimulation.
