The Lawyer Holding Big Tech Accountable by ‘Beating the Tar Out of Them’ in Court
============================================================

Attorney Mark Lanier argues that aggressive litigation and public pressure are essential to holding major social media companies accountable for failing to protect young users from platform harms.

### Key Points
- Attorney Mark Lanier secured a $6 million verdict against Meta and Google in the first social media addiction trial.
- Meta agreed to a $16.7 billion settlement with 51 U.S. states and territories in August to resolve claims regarding youth safety.
- New platform safety measures include default two-hour daily time limits for teenagers on Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok.
- Lanier emphasizes that public awareness and employee sentiment are critical factors in forcing long-term corporate policy changes.

### Why it Matters
These legal actions signal a shift in how regulators and courts address the societal impact of social media algorithms on minors. While multibillion-dollar settlements represent significant financial penalties, the broader industry impact depends on whether these mandates successfully alter platform design and user safety standards.
