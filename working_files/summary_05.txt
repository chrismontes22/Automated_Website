Meta’s next VR device isn’t a headset — it’s glasses
============================================================

Meta is set to release lightweight VR glasses in spring 2027, featuring a 5K MicroOLED display and a pocket-sized computing puck for $1,299.99 to enhance immersive media experiences.

### Key Points
- The glasses weigh 100g and utilize a Snapdragon Reality Elite processor, 12GB of RAM, and 128GB of storage housed in a wired external computing puck.
- The 5K MicroOLED "Infinite Display" supports a 120Hz refresh rate and 2412 x 2288 pixels per eye for high-fidelity media consumption.
- Navigation relies on eye-tracking and hand-gesture recognition via six external cameras, eliminating the need for physical controllers.
- The device launches with over 75 games and supports the existing Meta Quest app catalog, including titles like *Beat Saber* and *Phoenix Wright*.
- Battery life is rated for up to three hours of media playback, with the device remaining functional while the pocket puck is charging.

### Why it Matters
This new form factor positions Meta between the affordable Quest 3 and the premium Apple Vision Pro, targeting users who want immersive capabilities without the bulk of traditional headsets. By prioritizing comfort and portability, Meta is testing whether a lighter, glasses-style design can increase long-term consumer adoption of virtual reality hardware.
