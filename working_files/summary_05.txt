US stocks: Dow, S&P 500 end higher as traders shift from AI to healthcare
============================================================

Major U.S. stock indices posted modest gains despite investor concerns over rising interest rates, persistent inflation, and the vulnerability of AI-focused technology companies to higher borrowing costs.

### Key Points
- The Dow Jones Industrial Average rose 1.08%, while the S&P 500 and Nasdaq Composite saw marginal gains of 0.32% and 0.07%, respectively.
- Chip manufacturers Broadcom and Nvidia declined following cautious long-term guidance from TSMC, despite the company reporting record quarterly profits.
- U.S. producer prices saw their largest increase in 4-1/2 years during September, signaling that inflationary pressures are broadening beyond the energy sector.
- CME FedWatch data indicates a 67% probability that the Federal Reserve will implement an interest rate hike by the December policy meeting.
- Rocket Lab shares fell following the announcement that the inaugural launch of its Neutron rocket is delayed until next year.

### Why it Matters
Market participants are increasingly wary that high interest rates and inflation could dampen the massive earnings growth previously driven by AI-related stocks. This uncertainty is prompting a rotation in investor sentiment as the market evaluates whether current valuations can be sustained amid rising borrowing costs.
