HIFI Raises $37M To Build Stablecoin And Tokenized Market Infrastructure
============================================================

HIFI has secured $37 million in Series A funding led by Left Lane Capital to expand its infrastructure for stablecoin payments and tokenized capital market settlements globally.

### Key Points
- HIFI currently processes over $7 billion in annualized transaction volume across 87 countries.
- The new capital will support the development of card products and infrastructure for tokenized securities.
- The company recently participated in DTCC production trades alongside major firms like BlackRock, Goldman Sachs, and Nasdaq.
- HIFI maintains a strategic partnership with Visa to facilitate stablecoin-funded payouts to global card networks.
- The platform provides APIs designed to bridge traditional banking rails with blockchain-based stablecoin settlement systems.

### Why it Matters
This funding highlights the growing convergence between traditional financial institutions and blockchain-based payment infrastructure. By facilitating the cash side of tokenized trades, HIFI is positioning itself as a critical intermediary for modernizing global capital market settlements.
