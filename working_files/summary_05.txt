The Asus Chromebook Plus CX34 is at one of its lowest prices
============================================================

The Asus Chromebook Plus CX34 is currently available at significant discounts across major retailers, offering budget-conscious consumers a reliable laptop option with enhanced performance and integrated AI features.

### Key Points
- The Asus Chromebook Plus CX34 is available for $399.99 at Amazon with an Intel Core i3-1215U processor and 256GB of storage.
- Walmart offers a 128GB model with a faster 13th Gen Intel Core i3-1315U processor for $349.99.
- Best Buy is selling the high-performance version featuring an Intel Core i5-1335U processor for $399.99.
- All Chromebook Plus models include one year of Google AI Pro, providing access to Gemini tools and 5TB of cloud storage.
- Additional deals include the Igloo Playmate KoolTunes cooler for $69.99, an Anker 100W charger for $47.99, and a refurbished Roborock Q7 M5 Plus for $192.

### Why it Matters
These discounts provide high-value entry points for users seeking capable hardware that leverages the efficiency of ChromeOS for multitasking and productivity. By bundling AI software subscriptions with discounted hardware, these retailers are making premium features more accessible to the budget-conscious consumer market.
