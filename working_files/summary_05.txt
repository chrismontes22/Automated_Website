What price hike? Amazon just slashed $450 off the Galaxy S26 Ultra
============================================================

Amazon is offering a significant $450 discount on the Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra during its Prime Big Deal Days event, bringing the flagship smartphone to a record-low price.

### Key Points
- The Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra is currently available for $950, down from its standard retail price of $1,400.
- The device features the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy processor and a 200MP main camera system.
- Integrated hardware includes an S Pen for productivity and a built-in Privacy Display to restrict viewing angles.
- Software enhancements include various Galaxy AI tools, such as conversational photo editing and the My FanCam video tracking feature.
- Shoppers must have an active Amazon Prime membership or utilize a 30-day free trial to access the promotional pricing.

### Why it Matters
This promotion offers a rare opportunity to purchase a premium flagship device at a substantial discount following recent manufacturer price increases. It provides significant value for consumers seeking high-end productivity features and advanced privacy technology in a mobile handset.
