'This is crazy. This is insane': Bill Gates has changed his mind about AI and jobs
============================================================

Bill Gates is calling for urgent government intervention to address the risks of artificial intelligence, warning that the technology’s broad impact could cause significant economic and societal disruption.

### Key Points
- Bill Gates warns that AI will cause widespread job displacement, arguing that the technology's breadth makes it a unique threat compared to previous industrial revolutions.
- Gates advocates for government policies such as taxing AI compute tokens and robots to discourage companies from replacing human workers.
- The proposal includes designating specific "human-reserved" occupations that must remain performed by people regardless of machine efficiency.
- Gates highlights severe risks in cybersecurity and biology, noting that the threat of AI-enabled bioterrorism is significantly higher than natural pandemic risks.
- Tech leaders including Microsoft’s Mustafa Suleyman and Google DeepMind’s Demis Hassabis have expressed similar concerns regarding the need for oversight and safety standards.

### Why it Matters
The shift in perspective from a prominent tech figure highlights growing concerns that current market-driven AI development lacks necessary safeguards for labor and public safety. If governments fail to implement regulatory frameworks, the resulting economic instability could trigger a public backlash that threatens the long-term potential of AI innovation.
