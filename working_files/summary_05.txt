A Folding iPhone at Last? What to Expect From Apple’s Surprise and Shine Fall Event
============================================================

Apple will host its "Surprise and Shine" event on September 9, featuring the debut of the foldable iPhone Ultra, new Pro models, and a leadership transition to John Ternus.

### Key Points
- Apple CEO Tim Cook will step down, with hardware engineering chief John Ternus assuming the top leadership role just days before the September 9 event.
- The company plans an extended rollout, prioritizing the foldable iPhone Ultra and iPhone 18 Pro models while delaying the base iPhone 18 until 2027.
- The iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max are expected to feature the A20 processor, variable-aperture cameras, and enhanced satellite connectivity.
- The Apple Watch Series 12 is anticipated to include a more energy-efficient display and a new S12 processor, with potential biometric Touch ID integration.
- New AirPods may debut with infrared cameras for visual intelligence, alongside updates to the iOS 27 operating system and its integrated Siri AI features.

### Why it Matters
This event marks a pivotal shift for Apple as it navigates a major leadership change and attempts to stimulate market interest with high-end, luxury hardware. The strategic decision to delay base-model iPhones while focusing on premium devices reflects the company's response to ongoing supply chain pressures and the competitive AI landscape.
