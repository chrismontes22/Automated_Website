Apple's First Water-Resistant iPad to Launch Later This Year
============================================================

Apple is reportedly preparing to launch a new iPad mini by October, featuring a water-resistant design and an advanced vibration-based speaker system to eliminate traditional chassis openings.

### Key Points
- The upcoming iPad mini will be the first Apple tablet to feature an official water-resistant design, utilizing a vibration-based speaker system instead of traditional grilles.
- Samsung Display has reportedly begun mass production of 8.4-inch LTPS OLED panels for the device, which will offer improved contrast and power efficiency.
- Internal code suggests the tablet will receive a significant processor upgrade, potentially utilizing the A19 Pro or A20 Pro chip.
- The new model aims to compete directly with waterproof e-readers like the Kindle Paperwhite by allowing for safe use in environments like pools or bathrooms.
- The device is expected to launch in October, following the previous iPad mini update released in October 2024.

### Why it Matters
This design shift addresses a long-standing hardware gap between Apple’s tablets and dedicated e-readers, potentially increasing the iPad mini's appeal for casual, mobile use. By integrating water resistance and OLED technology, Apple is positioning the device as a more durable and premium option in the compact tablet market.
