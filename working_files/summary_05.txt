Nvidia reveals plans to build AI data centers in 14 days instead of 6 months
============================================================

Nvidia unveiled its DSX POD reference design, a modular, shipping-container-sized data center solution engineered to reduce infrastructure deployment times from six months to just 14 days.

### Key Points
- The DSX POD is a pre-built, modular AI data center designed to be operational within two weeks of arrival.
- Nvidia claims the system utilizes dry-cooler designs, potentially saving up to 54 million liters of water daily compared to traditional facilities.
- The units are powered by Vera Rubin architecture and are intended to supplement, rather than replace, permanent brick-and-mortar data centers.
- Nvidia is collaborating with partners including Dell Technologies, Foxconn, Anduril, and Armada to begin deliveries in early 2027.

### Why it Matters
This modular approach addresses a critical bottleneck in the AI industry by allowing companies to begin generating revenue while waiting for permanent data center construction. By accelerating deployment, Nvidia can increase its own chip sales while helping customers scale their AI infrastructure more efficiently.
