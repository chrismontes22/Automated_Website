YouTube Star and Filmmaker Markiplier Just Became GoPro’s Biggest Shareholder
============================================================

YouTube creator Mark Fischbach, known as Markiplier, has become GoPro’s largest individual shareholder after acquiring an 8.5 percent stake in the struggling camera manufacturer for approximately $9.3 million.

### Key Points
- Mark Fischbach purchased an 8.5 percent stake in GoPro, making him the company's largest single shareholder.
- The investment is valued at roughly $9.3 million based on GoPro’s current $110 million market capitalization.
- GoPro’s stock has declined nearly 57 percent year-to-date and currently trades below the $1 threshold required by NASDAQ.
- Fischbach, who recently directed the $50 million-grossing film *Iron Lung*, cited the potential of the upcoming Mission 1 Pro ILS camera as a primary investment driver.
- The company has faced significant financial challenges, including layoffs and a valuation drop from $13 billion twelve years ago.

### Why it Matters
This investment provides a potential lifeline for GoPro as it struggles to maintain its NASDAQ listing and recover from years of declining market value. By aligning with a prominent content creator, the company may gain new strategic direction and credibility within the modern filmmaking community.
