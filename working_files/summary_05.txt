Tokenized Tesla surges as Elon Musk unveils SpaceX-powered flying Roadster prototype
============================================================

Trading volume for Tesla-linked perpetual contracts on the Hyperliquid exchange surged to $47 million following Elon Musk’s demonstration of a SpaceX-powered flying Roadster prototype while traditional markets were closed.

### Key Points
- Hyperliquid’s Tesla contract volume jumped 1,580% in one hour, rising from $2.8 million to $47 million after the prototype reveal.
- The contract price increased approximately 5% to $378, reflecting investor sentiment ahead of Tesla’s official third-quarter earnings report on October 21.
- Binance remains the largest venue for Tesla-linked contracts with $279 million in 24-hour volume, compared to $159 million on Hyperliquid.
- Hyperliquid’s perpetual contract allows for 20x leverage and settles in USDC, providing a 24/7 trading alternative to traditional Nasdaq-listed shares.

### Why it Matters
The rapid influx of capital into crypto-based derivatives highlights a growing trend where investors use decentralized exchanges to react to corporate news outside of standard market hours. This shift demonstrates how perpetual contracts are increasingly serving as primary venues for price discovery when traditional stock exchanges are closed.
