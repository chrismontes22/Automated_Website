A New Bird-Recognizing Feeder Under $100 Launches for Beginner Bird-Watchers
============================================================

Birdbuddy has launched the Nook, an affordable smart bird feeder starting at $99 that features AI-powered species recognition and a 2K camera without requiring a monthly subscription fee.

### Key Points
- The Birdbuddy Nook is available in a standard $99 model and a $159 version featuring an integrated solar panel.
- The device includes a 3.8-cup seed capacity and a 2K-resolution camera capable of recording in both portrait and landscape orientations.
- A larger model, the Birdbuddy Home, offers a 6-cup capacity and expanded perching space for $219.
- AI software identifies bird species by analyzing physical characteristics like color and markings through the Birdbuddy mobile app.
- Ornithologist Mary Mack Gray recommends placing feeders 5 to 6 feet off the ground and 4 to 10 feet from nearby shrubs.

### Why it Matters
This release significantly lowers the entry price for smart birdwatching technology, which typically costs upwards of $200. By removing subscription requirements for AI features, Birdbuddy is positioning its hardware as a more accessible consumer option for backyard nature enthusiasts.
