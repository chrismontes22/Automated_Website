Radical electric bikemaker is coming to the USA next year
============================================================

Indian electric motorcycle manufacturer Ultraviolette plans to enter the United States market by 2027, aiming to transition from a regional startup into a globally recognized performance brand.

### Key Points
- Ultraviolette secured $85 million in funding led by Yali Capital and TDK Ventures to scale production and develop new electric platforms.
- The company is building a new manufacturing facility in India with an eventual annual capacity of 500,000 motorcycles.
- Current product offerings include the performance-focused F77, the radar-equipped X-47, the Tesseract scooter, and the upcoming Shockwave electric enduro.
- The brand distinguishes itself through in-house engineering of battery technology, software, and safety systems rather than using white-labeled components.
- Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan has joined the company as an advisor to support its global expansion efforts.

### Why it Matters
Successfully entering the US market would validate Ultraviolette’s strategy of prioritizing high-end performance and proprietary technology over the mass-market commuter segment. Establishing a foothold in America represents a critical milestone for the company as it attempts to compete with legacy motorcycle manufacturers on a global scale.
