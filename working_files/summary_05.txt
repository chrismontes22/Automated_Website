Sennheiser’s new Momentum 5 earbuds feature easily replaceable batteries
============================================================

Sennheiser has unveiled its Momentum True Wireless 5 earbuds, featuring a user-replaceable battery design and improved technical specifications, launching on September 3rd for $299.95.

### Key Points
- The Momentum True Wireless 5 earbuds and charging case allow users to replace batteries using a standard screwdriver, eliminating the need for professional repair services.
- Battery life has increased to 12 hours per charge, with a total of 40 hours provided by the slimmer, Qi-compatible charging case.
- New technical features include Bluetooth 6.0 support, a robust connectivity mode, and spatial audio with interactive head tracking.
- The earbuds will be available in five metallic finishes: lavender, copper, denim, graphite, and cream.
- This design shift aligns with upcoming European sustainability regulations scheduled to take effect in early 2027.

### Why it Matters
This release marks a significant shift in the consumer audio market by prioritizing repairability in a product category typically defined by disposable, glue-sealed components. By enabling user-led battery replacements, Sennheiser is setting a new standard for product longevity that could influence future industry design practices and consumer expectations.
