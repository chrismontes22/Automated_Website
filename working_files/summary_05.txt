GTA 6 Boss Seems A Bit Grumpy And Defensive While Answering An Investor’s Question About Frequent Delays
============================================================

Take-Two Interactive CEO Strauss Zelnick defended his leadership and company performance during a recent shareholder meeting following investor concerns regarding project delays and reliance on major franchises.

### Key Points
- CEO Strauss Zelnick addressed shareholder criticism regarding repeated game delays and the company's dependence on a limited number of major intellectual properties.
- Zelnick highlighted that Take-Two’s stock has appreciated approximately 1,200% since his management team took over in March 2007.
- The company reported current annual revenue guidance between $8 billion and $8.2 billion, a significant increase from under $1 billion in 2007.
- Take-Two has expanded its portfolio to include over 11 franchises that have each sold more than 5 million units per release.
- The highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto 6 is currently scheduled for release in November.

### Why it Matters
This exchange highlights the mounting pressure on Take-Two leadership to deliver on the massive expectations surrounding the upcoming launch of Grand Theft Auto 6. It also underscores the ongoing tension between long-term financial growth and investor frustration over operational delays in the gaming industry.
