Live: Local data centre giant suspends trading ahead of announcement
============================================================

National Australia Bank shares fell after the lender announced plans to cut 2,500 jobs by mid-2027, while data centre operator NextDC entered a trading halt amid infrastructure disputes.

### Key Points
- National Australia Bank will reduce its workforce by 6 per cent, including 1,500 roles by the end of this year, to simplify operations.
- The ASX 200 index closed 0.3 per cent lower at 8,816 points, with mining and energy sectors leading the decline.
- NextDC requested a trading halt to address power supply delays at its M3 Melbourne data centre linked to local community opposition.
- Former Rex airline director Lim Kim Hai was disqualified for four years and fined $800,000 for continuous disclosure contraventions.
- BHP reported a 19 per cent drop in quarterly copper production due to operational disruptions in Chile and South Australia.
- South Korean AI services firm Elice Group saw its share price plummet 30 per cent during its market debut.

### Why it Matters
The significant job cuts at NAB and the market struggles of AI-linked infrastructure firms highlight growing investor anxiety regarding the profitability of the artificial intelligence boom. These developments suggest that companies are facing increased pressure to justify high capital expenditure as economic conditions remain challenging.
