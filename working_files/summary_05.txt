New report finds that rapid expansion of tokenization is giving rise to new styles of investing
============================================================

Tokenized real-world assets have surpassed $34 billion in total supply as investors increasingly utilize blockchain technology to trade individual stocks and private credit around the clock.

### Key Points
- The total supply of tokenized real-world assets exceeded $34 billion in 2026, with cash equivalents and commodities doubling over the past year.
- Tokenized stocks experienced a 2,000% growth rate, with active holders now surpassing one million users globally.
- Individual stocks represent 81% of tokenized equities, contrasting with the traditional financial market's heavy reliance on passive index-based ETFs.
- Decentralized lending platforms primarily utilize tokenized private credit as collateral, which accounts for approximately 75% of all real-world assets deposited.
- Major financial institutions and firms, including BlackRock, Franklin Templeton, Robinhood, and Nasdaq, are actively expanding their presence in the tokenized asset sector.

### Why it Matters
The shift toward 24/7 blockchain-based trading of individual assets signals a fundamental departure from traditional passive investment strategies. This evolution suggests that digital ledger technology is creating a distinct financial ecosystem that prioritizes active, high-yield trading behaviors over conventional institutional models.
