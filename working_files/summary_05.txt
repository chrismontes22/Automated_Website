This doorbell camera lets a human security guard watch your front door
============================================================

SimpliSafe has launched the Video Doorbell Series 2, a $199.99 device that integrates AI-powered threat detection with live human monitoring to proactively deter potential intruders and package thieves.

### Key Points
- The Video Doorbell Series 2 features 2K resolution, a 150-degree field of view, two-way audio, and a built-in siren.
- Active Guard monitoring requires a Pro or Plus subscription plan starting at $49.99 per month.
- Live agents can view footage and speak to visitors only when the security system is armed and a trigger occurs.
- The device uses on-device AI and facial recognition to distinguish between familiar faces and potential threats.
- Privacy features include encrypted data storage and color-coded LED lights to indicate when an agent is actively monitoring.

### Why it Matters
This integration represents a shift toward human-verified security, potentially reducing false alarms and providing more accurate information to emergency responders. While it offers enhanced protection, the high monthly subscription cost may limit its appeal to users seeking a more affordable or less intrusive home monitoring solution.
