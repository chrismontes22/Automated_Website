LG Denies Allegations Its Televisions Are Always Recording
============================================================

LG has officially denied allegations that its smart TVs function as eavesdropping devices, asserting that voice recognition features are opt-in and do not record ambient household conversations.

### Key Points
- LG stated that voice data is only processed when a remote button is pressed or a specific wake word is detected locally.
- The company clarified that voice data is not transmitted to servers unless the device successfully recognizes a wake word.
- Researchers from GamersNexus and Level1Techs claimed that a man-in-the-middle attack could potentially turn LG G5 TVs into listening devices.
- The report alleged that vulnerabilities in the Automatic Content Recognition feature could allow unauthorized access to audio inputs and network device lists.
- LG maintains that its network scanning capabilities are standard functions for smart home connectivity and not security flaws.

### Why it Matters
These claims highlight growing consumer concerns regarding privacy and the potential for smart home devices to be exploited by malicious actors. While smart-home attacks remain rare, the incident underscores the importance of network security and the need for manufacturers to maintain transparent data collection practices.
