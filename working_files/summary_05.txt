Pay once, store a ridiculous amount while Internxt's 10TB plan is just $269.97
============================================================

Internxt is offering a 10TB lifetime cloud storage subscription for a one-time payment of $269.97, providing new users with significant savings compared to the original $2,900 retail price.

### Key Points
- The promotional price of $269.97 is available through September 29 as part of a September Flash Sale.
- The plan provides 10TB of encrypted storage accessible across unlimited devices, including Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iOS.
- Internxt utilizes end-to-end, zero-knowledge, and post-quantum encryption to ensure user privacy and data security.
- The service is open-source, GDPR compliant, and has undergone an independent security audit by Securitum.
- This offer is restricted to new users and excludes additional features like file versioning, antivirus, and advanced sharing tools.

### Why it Matters
This lifetime subscription model offers a cost-effective alternative to recurring monthly cloud storage fees for users requiring large-scale data capacity. By prioritizing zero-knowledge encryption and open-source transparency, the service appeals to privacy-conscious consumers looking for a secure, long-term digital storage solution.
