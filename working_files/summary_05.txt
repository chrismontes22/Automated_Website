Nubank’s owner is in talks to buy Monzo at up to £10bn
============================================================

British digital bank Monzo is currently in early-stage acquisition talks with Brazil’s Nu Holdings, a deal that could value the London-based fintech firm at up to £10 billion.

### Key Points
- Nu Holdings, the parent company of Nubank, is exploring a potential cash-and-stock acquisition of Monzo.
- Monzo has engaged Morgan Stanley, Qatalyst, and law firm Slaughter and May to advise on the potential sale.
- The bank is simultaneously considering a new funding round to support its planned expansion into mainland Europe.
- Monzo currently serves 16 million customers and reported an adjusted pre-tax profit of £172.6 million in its latest annual results.
- A successful sale to Nu would likely cancel Monzo’s potential plans for an initial public offering on the London Stock Exchange.

### Why it Matters
A sale would mark a significant consolidation in the global digital banking sector, providing Nu Holdings with an immediate entry point into the UK and European markets. This move would also shift the competitive landscape for fintech firms like Revolut, which are currently pursuing independent public listings.
