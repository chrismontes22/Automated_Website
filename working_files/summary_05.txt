Nvidia is investing $1 billion into American scientific computing infrastructure while TSMC partners with GlobalFoundries to expand domestic semiconductor packaging capabilities for high-performance AI datacenters.

### Key Points
- Nvidia is funding at least seven AI-optimized supercomputers to bolster United States scientific computing dominance.
- TSMC and GlobalFoundries signed a $2 billion agreement to increase domestic production of silicon interposers for advanced semiconductors.
- Microsoft is transitioning its Surface Laptop Ultra line to Nvidia SoCs, with pricing for the new models ranging from $2,599 to $5,899.
- EQT acquired a majority stake in the Swiss cybersecurity firm Acronis at a valuation exceeding $3.5 billion.
- Shopify acquired the Tailwind CSS framework to provide a stable long-term home for the open source project.
- DEF CON is expanding its Franklin project to include the entire conference, focusing on hardening critical infrastructure against cyber threats.

### Why it Matters
These developments highlight a significant shift toward domesticating high-end semiconductor supply chains and strengthening national cybersecurity infrastructure. As major tech firms integrate AI deeper into hardware and software, the industry is prioritizing both physical computing capacity and the security of critical digital systems.
