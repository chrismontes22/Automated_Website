Napster Is Back, and It Wants to Digitally Clone Teachers
============================================================

Napster has pivoted from its origins as a music-sharing service to an artificial intelligence company, partnering with Gems Education in Dubai to develop AI-powered digital teaching assistants.

### Key Points
- Napster was acquired by Infinite Reality for $207 million in 2025 and has since transitioned into an AI-focused platform.
- The partnership with Gems Education will create digital twins of teachers to provide students with 24/7 support and personalized learning.
- Samuel Huber, former CEO of Landvault, has been appointed as the CEO of Napster for the Middle East and Africa.
- The company is establishing a regional hub in Dubai and plans to expand its local workforce to 500 employees by 2030.
- Napster is collaborating with G42 in the UAE and Detasad in Saudi Arabia to ensure data sovereignty by hosting AI models on local infrastructure.

### Why it Matters
This shift represents a significant rebranding for a company historically associated with music piracy, positioning it as a competitor to major global AI model developers. By focusing on data sovereignty and educational augmentation, Napster aims to align with the UAE’s aggressive national strategy to integrate agentic AI across government and public services.
