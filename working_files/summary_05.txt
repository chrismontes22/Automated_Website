The Delicate Art of Making a 1907 House Smarter
============================================================

A Portland couple is successfully integrating modern smart home technology into their historic 1907 Craftsman house while preserving the property's original aesthetic and architectural integrity through careful product selection.

### Key Points
- The homeowners installed an Ecobee Enhanced smart thermostat to manage heating and improve energy efficiency in the drafty, uninsulated historic structure.
- Midea U-shaped window air conditioners were chosen for their quiet operation and ability to fit into the home's narrow, original window openings.
- A Philips 4200 smart lock provides keyless entry via Wi-Fi and fingerprint scanning without requiring complex hardwiring or external hubs.
- The Toto S7A bidet system was integrated into an existing bathroom to provide modern hygiene features, despite requiring plumbing and electrical modifications.
- An OtO solar-powered smart sprinkler is being used to manage irrigation for a newly planted wild meadow in the home's quarter-acre yard.
- The couple utilized Feit Electric bulbs and Zoiinet Bluetooth sockets to add smart lighting to rooms lacking overhead wiring.

### Why it Matters
Integrating modern technology into historic homes allows owners to improve comfort and energy efficiency without compromising the character of older architecture. This approach demonstrates that smart home devices can be effectively retrofitted into aging infrastructure to provide convenience and long-term utility.
