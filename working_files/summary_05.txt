Musk pledges $10bn in SpaceX shares for US children: Who really gains?
============================================================

Elon Musk has pledged to gift one SpaceX share to every child in the United States next year, a donation valued at approximately $10.7 billion based on current prices.

### Key Points
- The pledge involves distributing shares to roughly 73 million children under 18 through government-backed TRUMP ACCOUNTS.
- SpaceX shares are currently trading at approximately $146.03, making the total gift value roughly $10.7 billion.
- Recipients generally cannot sell the gifted shares for five years or until they reach age 17, whichever occurs sooner.
- Musk, the largest donor to Republican groups this election cycle, has not yet specified the logistics or eligibility criteria for the distribution.
- The donation could provide Musk with significant tax benefits by allowing him to avoid capital gains taxes on the appreciated stock.

### Why it Matters
This unprecedented transfer of corporate equity links private wealth to federal investment infrastructure, potentially influencing public perception of the administration's economic policies ahead of the midterm elections. The move also highlights the growing role of high-net-worth donors in shaping national financial programs and political discourse.
