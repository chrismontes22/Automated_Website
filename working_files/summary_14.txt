Data center backlash is panicking tech oligarchs ahead of midterms
============================================================

Corporate political donations have reached a record $646 million this cycle, with tech industry leaders heavily funding Republican candidates to protect AI, cryptocurrency, and data center interests.

### Key Points
- Corporate political spending has surged 40% compared to the 2024 presidential election, with over half of traceable funds originating from the tech sector.
- Major donors include Elon Musk ($200 million), Andreessen Horowitz founders ($115 million), Jeff Yass ($94 million), and OpenAI founders Greg and Anna Brockman ($50 million).
- Public opposition to data centers is high, with a Gallup poll showing 71% of Americans oppose their construction in local communities.
- Tech executives are targeting state and local races to preemptively block regulations on AI, cryptocurrency, and online gambling industries.
- While Republican leadership, including Donald Trump and JD Vance, has defended data centers, some GOP officials are distancing themselves due to voter backlash.

### Why it Matters
This record-breaking influx of capital highlights a strategic effort by tech oligarchs to secure political influence against a growing grassroots movement demanding industry accountability. The outcome of these elections will determine whether corporate donors can successfully override public opposition to data centers and emerging tech regulations.
