The AI Data Center Boom Faces a New Reality Check
============================================================

U.S. electric utilities are implementing stricter interconnection rules and tariffs to manage a surge in speculative data center power requests that threaten grid stability and long-term planning.

### Key Points
- Over $170 billion in AI data center capacity has been stalled or withdrawn since January 2024 due to community opposition and infrastructure constraints.
- Texas has paused new data center interconnections to audit a 474-GW queue, which is five times the state's current peak demand record.
- Utilities are adopting "gating" conditions, including upfront infrastructure payments and exit fees, to ensure only viable projects proceed to construction.
- Research from Grid Strategies suggests utility-reported data center load forecasts are approximately 40% higher than credible industry projections through 2030.
- The Electric Power Research Institute estimates data centers will account for up to 20% of total U.S. electricity demand by 2035.

### Why it Matters
The disconnect between speculative project filings and actual construction creates significant risks of overbuilding or underbuilding the electric grid. Accurate forecasting is essential to protect ratepayers from unnecessary costs while ensuring that legitimate AI infrastructure receives the reliable power necessary for operation.
