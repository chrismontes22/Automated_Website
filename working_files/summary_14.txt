Adjust iPhone Duo's Outer Display to Accommodate Your Case
============================================================

Apple has introduced a "Shift Left Edge" display setting for the iPhone Duo, allowing users to adjust screen content to prevent obstruction by specific protective cases or frames.

### Key Points
- The Shift Left Edge feature insets on-screen content to ensure visibility when using cases that cover the left edge of the outer display.
- Apple’s official iPhone Duo Case triggers an automatic prompt to enable the setting upon installation.
- Users can manually activate the feature by navigating to Settings, selecting Display & Brightness, and toggling the Shift Left Edge option.
- Enabling the setting requires a brief system refresh, resulting in a narrow black border on the left side of the outer screen.
- The feature is compatible with both official Apple accessories and third-party cases that may obscure the display.

### Why it Matters
This feature addresses a common usability challenge for foldable devices where protective hardware can interfere with edge-to-edge interface elements. By providing a software-based adjustment, Apple ensures that users can utilize various case designs without sacrificing access to critical on-screen information.
