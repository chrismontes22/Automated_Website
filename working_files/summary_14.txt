DailyObjects raises ₹332 cr in Series C funding round
============================================================

Lifestyle tech brand DailyObjects has secured ₹332 crore in a Series C funding round led by Xponentia Capital Partners, valuing the company at approximately ₹1,050 crore for future expansion.

### Key Points
- The funding round was led by Xponentia Capital Partners, Anicut Capital, and Axiom Asia Private Capital.
- DailyObjects plans to expand its retail footprint from 18 to 150 Exclusive Brand Outlets over the next five years.
- The company reported ₹110 crore in revenue for FY25 and projects growth to ₹400 crore by FY27.
- Capital will be directed toward research and development, international market exploration, and eventual IPO preparations.
- Early investor Roots Ventures secured a partial exit through this transaction while remaining a shareholder.

### Why it Matters
This investment signals strong investor confidence in the potential for Indian design-led consumer brands to scale globally. By aggressively expanding its physical retail presence and targeting international markets, DailyObjects aims to transition from a niche tech accessory provider into a major global lifestyle player.
