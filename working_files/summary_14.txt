Apple Releases visionOS 27 With Siri AI and New Spatial Experiences
============================================================

Apple has released visionOS 27 for the Vision Pro headset, introducing advanced Siri AI capabilities, spatial panorama features, and significant system improvements to enhance user productivity and immersion.

### Key Points
- Siri AI now supports Visual Intelligence, onscreen awareness, and conversational interactions without the "Hey Siri" wake word.
- New spatial features include transforming panoramas into 3D scenes and immersive 360-degree web environments within Safari.
- The Image Playground app allows users to generate and modify high-quality images using natural language prompts.
- Home app updates include summarized activity notifications and AI-powered video descriptions for HomeKit Secure Video recordings.
- System enhancements feature 3x faster Wi-Fi connectivity, 4K recording capabilities, and new accessibility tools like eye-controlled wheelchair navigation.
- The update is available as a free download for all Apple Vision Pro models via the Software Update section in Settings.

### Why it Matters
This update significantly expands the utility of the Vision Pro by integrating Apple Intelligence across core system functions and spatial computing workflows. These enhancements position the headset as a more capable tool for both creative tasks and daily environment management, signaling Apple's commitment to evolving its spatial operating system.
