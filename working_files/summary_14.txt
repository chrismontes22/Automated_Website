Oops – Another Aussie State Just Forgot to Enforce Prime Minister Albanese’s Green Data Center Mandate
============================================================

The Northern Territory government is challenging federal renewable energy mandates by proposing a $40 billion data centre project powered by natural gas from the Beetaloo Basin near Darwin.

### Key Points
- The Northern Territory plans to build hyper-scale AI data centre campuses requiring 3 GW of electricity.
- Proposed facilities would rely on gas extraction from the Beetaloo Basin rather than renewable energy sources.
- Federal government rules currently aim to force new data centres to operate exclusively on renewable power.
- Queensland officials have joined the Northern Territory in opposing the federal mandate, citing a lack of economic modelling.
- State governments argue that prioritizing gas-powered infrastructure is essential to securing billions in high-tech investment and tax revenue.

### Why it Matters
This conflict highlights a growing tension between federal climate policy and state-level economic development strategies regarding the rapidly expanding AI data centre sector. The outcome of this dispute will determine whether Australia can attract major international technology investments or if strict renewable requirements will drive capital to more flexible jurisdictions.
