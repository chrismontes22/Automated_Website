Lord Of The Rings RPG Game Studio Hit By ‘Serious’ Cyberattack As The Internet Continues Breaking
============================================================

Warhorse Studios, the developer behind Kingdom Come: Deliverance 2, has confirmed a significant cyberattack involving unauthorized access to company systems and internal data currently under investigation by experts.

### Key Points
- Warhorse Studios is working with cybersecurity professionals and authorities to assess the full extent of the data breach.
- CEO Martin Frývaldský stated that the company is prioritizing system security and the protection of employees, partners, and the community.
- The incident follows a recent trend of cyberattacks affecting major gaming companies, including recent security issues at Capcom and Square Enix.
- Capcom recently suspended online functionality and sales for Street Fighter V on Steam and PlayStation to address vulnerabilities related to the PHP scripting language.

### Why it Matters
The gaming industry is facing an increasing frequency of cyberattacks, potentially exacerbated by new AI-driven exploitation methods. These security breaches threaten sensitive corporate data and disrupt online services, forcing developers to prioritize infrastructure stability over ongoing operations.
