Evernorth clears shareholder vote ahead of Nasdaq debut with 473M XRP treasury
============================================================

Evernorth is set to debut on the Nasdaq under the ticker XRPN after shareholders approved a merger with Armada Acquisition Corp. II, creating a major XRP treasury company.

### Key Points
- The business combination is scheduled to close on October 7, with trading on the Nasdaq beginning the following day.
- Evernorth will hold approximately 473 million XRP, valued at roughly $710 million based on current market prices.
- The transaction is expected to generate $300 million in gross cash proceeds through private placements, convertible notes, and trust funds.
- Key investors backing the company include Ripple, Kraken, Pantera Capital, SBI Group, Arrington Capital, and GSR.
- The firm plans to increase its XRP holdings per share by utilizing yield strategies and participating in broader capital market activities.

### Why it Matters
This listing establishes Evernorth as the largest publicly traded pure-play XRP treasury company, providing institutional and retail investors with a new vehicle for crypto exposure. The move highlights a growing trend of crypto firms utilizing SPAC mergers to access public markets despite recent volatility and regulatory scrutiny in the sector.
