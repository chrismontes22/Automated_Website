BigCommerce warns customers of potential data leaks following cyber incident
============================================================

BigCommerce confirmed a supply-chain breach involving the third-party Ribon application, which allowed unauthorized access to customer data across multiple merchant storefronts between September 13 and 17, 2026.

### Key Points
- Attackers compromised credentials for the Ribon and Ribon 1.5 applications, owned by Fastr company "Be A Part Of."
- Malicious scripts were injected into merchant storefronts, exposing customer names, email addresses, phone numbers, and physical addresses.
- The spirits retailer Master of Malt confirmed its customers were affected and reported the incident to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.
- BigCommerce revoked access to the compromised application on September 17, 2026, to prevent further unauthorized data exposure.
- Law firm Emery Reddy is investigating potential claims, warning that affected customers face an increased risk of phishing and scam attempts.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the significant security risks posed by third-party integrations within ecommerce platforms, where a single compromised application key can impact hundreds of independent retailers. It serves as a reminder for businesses to rigorously vet third-party software and for consumers to remain vigilant against targeted phishing attacks following data breaches.
