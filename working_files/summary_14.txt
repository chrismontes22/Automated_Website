Silent Hill And Metal Gear Publisher Konami Does ‘Not Believe The Market Will Shift Significantly’ Because Of AI
============================================================

Konami president Hideki Hayakawa stated that while artificial intelligence offers development efficiencies, the company believes human creators remain essential for producing innovative and meaningful gaming experiences.

### Key Points
- Konami president Hideki Hayakawa emphasized that human collaboration and diverse team perspectives are irreplaceable in the game development process.
- The company acknowledged that AI presents notable opportunities for efficiency but remains cautious regarding its implementation across development teams.
- Konami is currently establishing internal guardrails and protocols to manage the use of AI technology within its studios.
- The studio is developing a user-generated content platform titled Wai Wai World Craft, which serves as a competitor to Roblox.
- Management believes that existing game engines and user-generated content have already lowered industry barriers more significantly than current AI advancements.

### Why it Matters
This stance highlights a growing trend among major game studios to prioritize human-led creativity over full automation in software development. By resisting a total shift toward AI, Konami aims to preserve the collaborative team dynamics that define its unique gaming experiences.
