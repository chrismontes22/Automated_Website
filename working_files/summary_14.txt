Bitcoin trades at $79,000, next week’s US inflation data to test rate-cut hopes and crypto valuations
============================================================

The global cryptocurrency market capitalization declined by 1.4% to $2.77 trillion as major digital assets including Bitcoin and Ethereum experienced moderate price pullbacks over the last 24 hours.

### Key Points
- Bitcoin fell 1.8% while Ethereum dropped 2.3% to trade at the $2,452 level.
- Major altcoins including BNB, XRP, Solana, and Dogecoin recorded a 5.4% decline.
- Tron bucked the downward trend, posting a 0.9% gain during the same period.
- Analysts identify $78,500 as a critical support zone for Bitcoin following its recent rejection near $82,000.
- Ethereum requires a recovery above the $2,500 threshold to signal renewed market momentum.

### Why it Matters
The recent price consolidation reflects a broader market sensitivity to upcoming macroeconomic data releases. Investors are closely monitoring these key support levels to determine if the current rally remains sustainable amid shifting liquidity conditions.
