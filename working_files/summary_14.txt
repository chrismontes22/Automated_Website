SpaceX, OpenAI, and Anthropic Are Woth More Than Every Single IPO in the Last 45 Years
============================================================

SpaceX, OpenAI, and Anthropic have reached a combined valuation of $5.2 trillion, surpassing the total first-day market value of over 3,300 U.S. technology IPOs from the last 45 years.

### Key Points
- SpaceX currently holds a market capitalization of approximately $2.1 trillion following its June IPO.
- OpenAI is reportedly seeking a $1.2 trillion valuation in its latest private funding round.
- Anthropic is targeting a $2 trillion valuation for a potential IPO now expected in November.
- These three companies’ combined $5.2 trillion valuation exceeds the $4.07 trillion total value of 3,365 U.S. tech IPOs between 1980 and 2025.
- OpenAI projects $278 billion in negative free cash flow between 2026 and 2030 due to massive infrastructure spending.
- Anthropic does not expect to achieve positive cash flow until 2028.

### Why it Matters
These valuations reflect extreme investor optimism regarding the future profitability of artificial intelligence rather than current financial performance. If these companies fail to generate the massive cash flows required to justify their infrastructure costs, the broader technology sector could face significant market volatility and economic headwinds.
