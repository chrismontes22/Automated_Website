Apple Reportedly Cuts iPhone 18 Pro Orders After Price Hike Dampens Demand
============================================================

Apple has reduced component orders for its iPhone 18 Pro models by at least 15 percent following lower-than-expected consumer demand and rising memory chip costs.

### Key Points
- Apple cut October component orders for the iPhone 18 Pro series by 15 percent compared to initial projections.
- The iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max models launched at $1,199 and $1,299, representing a $100 price increase over previous versions.
- Rising costs for memory chips, attributed to AI-driven demand, have impacted pricing across Apple’s iPhone, MacBook, and iPad lineups.
- Apple is shifting to a split launch strategy, with the standard iPhone 18, iPhone 18e, and iPhone Air 2 expected to debut in spring 2027.
- The iPhone 18 Pro models include 12GB of RAM, despite internal rumors that Apple considered upgrading the lineup to 16GB.

### Why it Matters
This production adjustment highlights how rising component costs and aggressive pricing strategies are beginning to dampen consumer demand for Apple's flagship hardware. The shift toward a split launch schedule suggests the company is attempting to manage supply chain pressures while diversifying its release cycle to maintain market relevance.
