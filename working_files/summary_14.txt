Best Buy launches a surprise weekend flash sale with up to 50% off — I've found the 18 best deals on TVs, laptops, appliances, headphones, and more
============================================================

Best Buy has launched a surprise weekend flash sale featuring significant discounts on electronics, appliances, and home goods, with most promotional offers expiring at the end of Sunday.

### Key Points
- The sale includes deep discounts on major brands like Apple, Samsung, Sony, Dyson, and LG.
- Notable deals feature an $1,100 price reduction on the 65-inch LG C6 OLED TV and up to $1,200 off select iPhone and Samsung smartphone models.
- Consumers can find savings of up to 50% on kitchen appliances from Ninja and Bella, as well as 65% off smart home devices from Ring and Blink.
- Additional offers include laptops starting at $179, gaming titles from $19.99, and various discounts on cameras, tablets, and audio equipment.
- All promotional pricing is strictly limited and scheduled to conclude by the end of the day on Sunday.

### Why it Matters
This flash sale provides consumers with an opportunity to secure holiday-level discounts ahead of major retail events like Amazon's Prime Big Deal Days. By offering aggressive pricing on high-end electronics, Best Buy aims to capture early consumer spending as the fall shopping season begins.
