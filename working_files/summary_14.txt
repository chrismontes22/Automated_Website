Top Stories: 'Apple Upgrade' Launches, iOS 26.6 Released, and More
============================================================

Apple has launched its new Klarna-backed Apple Upgrade leasing program in the United States while preparing for upcoming hardware releases and major software updates ahead of September.

### Key Points
- Apple replaced its existing iPhone Upgrade Program and payment options with the new Klarna-backed Apple Upgrade leasing service for iPhones, iPads, Macs, and Apple Watches.
- The company released iOS 26.6 and iPadOS 26.6, which include critical security patches and optimizations for the upcoming iOS 27 launch.
- Reports indicate a new smart home hub featuring Siri AI is expected to launch between October and early next year in tabletop and wall-mounted versions.
- Updated Apple TV and HomePod mini models are reportedly ready for release pending the final integration of Siri AI features.
- Apple is reportedly developing privacy-focused smart glasses for a potential unveiling at WWDC 2027 to compete with existing wearable products.

### Why it Matters
These updates signal a shift in Apple's consumer financing strategy and a broader push to integrate advanced AI across its entire hardware ecosystem. By aligning new device releases with upcoming software capabilities, the company aims to maintain its market position while addressing long-term concerns regarding the sustainability of the current AI industry boom.
