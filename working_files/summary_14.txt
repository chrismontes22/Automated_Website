RemControl Banking Trojan Gives Attackers Remote Control of Android Devices
============================================================

Researchers at Group-IB have identified a new Android banking trojan called RemControl that exploits Accessibility Services to steal sensitive financial credentials from users across six different countries.

### Key Points
- RemControl targets retail banking customers in Western Europe, the Middle East, and Canada, impacting over 30 financial institutions.
- The malware developer, tracked as UNKK, reportedly used an AI assistant to build the command and control infrastructure by disguising the project as a parental monitoring app.
- Attackers distribute the trojan via fake Google Play Store pages that impersonate the TVTap IPTV application to lure victims into downloading the malicious payload.
- The malware employs a local VPN to suppress Google Play Protect and uses the Android Keystore to sign payloads, effectively evading standard security detection.
- Once granted Accessibility Service permissions, the trojan captures PINs, banking codes, and screen data while preventing users from uninstalling the application or performing factory resets.

### Why it Matters
This campaign highlights a growing trend of cybercriminals leveraging AI tools to accelerate the development of sophisticated, multi-language banking fraud platforms. The use of advanced evasion techniques, such as VPN-based suppression of security software, poses a significant risk to mobile banking users globally.
