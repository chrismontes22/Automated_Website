HYPE jumps 20% as Trump signals legal US path for Hyperliquid
============================================================

Hyperliquid’s native token HYPE surged 20% to $72 after President Donald Trump announced that the CFTC is working to establish a compliant pathway for US platform access.

### Key Points
- HYPE reached a 24-hour high of $72.28 following the announcement, with daily trading volume hitting $1.4 billion.
- President Trump stated that CFTC Chair Michael Selig is actively working to bring the decentralized exchange into the United States legally.
- Hyperliquid currently leads the decentralized perpetuals sector, accounting for approximately 40% of total market volume over the last 30 days.
- Shares of the unrelated firm Hyperliquid Strategies, trading under the ticker PURR, rose 30.4% amid unusual options activity prior to the announcement.
- No formal regulatory proposal or timeline for a compliant US launch has been released by the CFTC or Hyperliquid.

### Why it Matters
This development signals a potential shift in US regulatory policy toward decentralized finance platforms that have historically operated outside domestic oversight. If successful, the integration of Hyperliquid could set a significant precedent for how other decentralized perpetual exchanges navigate American compliance requirements.
