Transcript: Bill McNabb, Vanguard former Chairman and CEO
============================================================

Former Vanguard CEO Bill McNabb discusses his transition from leading the world’s largest mutual fund company to his current roles in corporate governance and the venture capital startup ecosystem.

### Key Points
- Bill McNabb served as CEO and Chairman of The Vanguard Group, overseeing its growth to over $5 trillion in assets during his tenure.
- He currently serves on the boards of IBM and UnitedHealth Group, focusing on talent, strategy, and corporate culture.
- McNabb acts as a senior advisor to the venture capital firm Venrock, where he mentors founders of fintech startups like Altruist, Vanilla, and Finny.
- He emphasizes the importance of long-term investment discipline and warns against the risks of excessive leverage in modern financial markets.
- McNabb advocates for a "human-in-the-loop" approach to financial advice, arguing that technology should enable advisors rather than replace them.

### Why it Matters
McNabb’s shift from a massive, passive-investing mutual fund giant to the agile world of venture-backed fintech highlights a broader industry trend toward hyper-personalized, tech-enabled wealth management. His insights into governance and succession planning provide a roadmap for how legacy corporations and new startups can navigate market volatility while maintaining a focus on long-term investor outcomes.
