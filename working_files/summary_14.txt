Anthropic Says Users Can't Be Needlessly Cruel to Claude
============================================================

Anthropic has updated its usage policy to prohibit sustained abuse of Claude models while introducing stricter regulations regarding deceptive campaigns, weapon development, and surveillance tool creation.

### Key Points
- The new policy, effective November 12, bans users from subjecting Claude models to repeated, purposeless cruel behavior.
- Anthropic added specific prohibitions against creating non-consensual intimate imagery and using AI to develop biological or chemical weapons.
- A reorganized section on deceptive campaigns targets the use of Claude for astroturfing, fake reviews, and state-sponsored disinformation networks.
- New rules restrict law enforcement from using the AI to suggest arrests, track individuals without consent, or build surveillance systems.
- Users controlling hardware with Claude must now ensure a qualified human supervisor is present to prevent potential physical harm.

### Why it Matters
These policy updates reflect Anthropic's efforts to mitigate real-world risks identified through observed misuse by state media and commercial entities. By formalizing these restrictions, the company aims to prevent its technology from being weaponized for disinformation, illegal surveillance, or physical harm.
