Tapo DL130 Palm Recognition Smart Lock Scans Veins for Contactless Entry
============================================================

The Tapo DL130 Palm Smart Lock enhances home security by utilizing advanced vein pattern recognition alongside multiple digital access methods, remote app control, and a durable, weather-resistant design.

### Key Points
- Features contactless palm vein recognition for secure, hygienic entry.
- Includes a fingerprint scanner supporting up to 100 unique prints.
- Offers remote management and voice control via Alexa and Google Assistant.
- Protects biometric data using local AES 128-bit encryption.
- Operates on a rechargeable battery lasting up to one year with a USB-C emergency power port.
- Rated IP65 for weather resistance and includes a built-in doorbell and automatic locking sensor.

### Why it Matters
This device represents a shift toward biometric-heavy smart home security that prioritizes both user convenience and data privacy. By integrating multiple backup power and entry options, it addresses common consumer concerns regarding reliability and potential lockouts in smart home technology.
