Professor Layton Developer Level-5 Scrambles to Reupload Trailers as AI Backlash Intensifies
============================================================

Level-5 has issued a formal apology and pledged to revise promotional materials after fans identified suspected generative AI artifacts in recent trailers for upcoming titles like Professor Layton.

### Key Points
- Level-5 released the "VISION 2026 II: Dreams" showcase on September 10, featuring titles such as *Decapolice* and *Professor Layton and the New World of Steam*.
- Social media users identified visual inconsistencies in trailers, prompting accusations of "AI slop" and concerns regarding the use of generative tools in game development.
- CEO Akihiro Hino admitted to using AI for "flashy" presentation effects but insisted that no AI-generated data is present within the actual game software.
- The studio attributed several flagged errors to human mistakes, such as simplified hand-drawn background elements, and promised to release corrected trailer versions.
- Level-5 stated it will limit future AI usage to internal efficiency tasks and avoid using it in public-facing promotional videos to restore fan trust.

### Why it Matters
The backlash highlights a growing tension between game developers seeking to accelerate production cycles and a player base increasingly wary of generative AI's ethical and creative implications. By failing to fully address concerns regarding the technology's role in their workflow, Level-5 risks long-term damage to its brand reputation and consumer loyalty.
