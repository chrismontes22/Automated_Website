Top Stories: iOS 27 and macOS Golden Gate Out Now, Siri AI Waitlist, and More
============================================================

Apple has launched the iPhone 18 Pro, Apple Watch Series 12, and AirPods 5 alongside major software updates including iOS 27 and macOS Golden Gate for global users.

### Key Points
- Apple released iOS 27 and iPadOS 27, featuring new Siri AI capabilities and a Liquid Glass design update.
- macOS Golden Gate is now available, introducing a dedicated Siri AI app and Visual Intelligence to Mac computers.
- The U.S. version of the iPhone 18 Pro Max utilizes a Qualcomm Snapdragon X80 modem, while other global models feature Apple’s proprietary C2 modem.
- Siri AI features in iOS 27 require users to join a waitlist and are currently restricted to iPhone 15 Pro models or newer.
- Apple expanded iCloud+ subscriptions in over 100 countries to include Apple TV and Apple Arcade at no additional cost.
- The first beta for iOS 27.2 has been released to developers, previewing a revamped Health app.

### Why it Matters
These updates represent a significant shift in Apple's ecosystem by integrating advanced AI features and bundling core services into existing storage subscriptions. The hardware modem discrepancy and regional service limitations highlight the complex regulatory and technical challenges Apple faces when deploying new technology across global markets.
