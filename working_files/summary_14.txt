73% of Organizations Say They Are Not Fully Ready for a Major Cyberattack
============================================================

New research reveals that 73% of organizations feel unprepared for major cyberattacks due to critical gaps in executive alignment, cross-departmental coordination, and visibility across complex digital environments.

### Key Points
- The "State of Incident Response Readiness 2026" report surveyed 600 senior IT security decision-makers regarding their ability to manage cyber incidents.
- 76% of organizations experienced at least one cyberattack in the past year, yet fewer than 40% consider their response components highly effective.
- 90% of respondents anticipate difficulty coordinating stakeholders, with 75% citing legal and communications delays as major bottlenecks during active incidents.
- 78% of security leaders report that visibility blind spots in cloud, identity, and on-premises systems allow attackers to maintain persistent access.
- 84% of organizations express concern over attackers moving laterally from corporate IT networks into operational technology (OT) and industrial control systems.

### Why it Matters
Effective incident response is no longer a purely technical task but a complex business discipline requiring seamless integration between security, legal, and executive leadership. Organizations that fail to bridge these coordination gaps risk significant financial losses, operational shutdowns, and long-term reputational damage when inevitable cyber incidents occur.
