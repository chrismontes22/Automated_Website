Top Stories: Apple 'Pro and Tell' Debut, iPhone Duo Launch, and More
============================================================

Apple has launched its first foldable smartphone, the iPhone Duo, while preparing for a new product event on October 28 featuring potential touchscreen MacBook and iPad updates.

### Key Points
- Apple’s iPhone Duo launch triggered long lines at retail stores due to high demand and limited initial stock.
- A "Pro and Tell" media event scheduled for October 28 in Los Angeles is expected to introduce new MacBook Pro models and an OLED iPad mini 8.
- Verizon announced a $10 monthly fee for the iPhone Handoff feature, which allows users to share a single phone number across two devices.
- Apple released the iOS 27.2 candidate, featuring Apple Music branding updates and expanded Siri AI language support.
- Reports indicate Apple is developing a "HomeView Max" smart hub featuring a 9-inch, 360-degree swiveling display.

### Why it Matters
The launch of the iPhone Duo and the introduction of carrier fees for Handoff signal a shift in how Apple manages multi-device ecosystems and premium hardware pricing. These developments force consumers to weigh the convenience of new form factors against the rising costs of specialized cellular service plans.
