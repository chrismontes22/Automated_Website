Silicon Valley sees AI as the solution – for everyone else
============================================================

Tech industry leaders are increasingly criticized for promoting "solutionism," an ideology that risks creating a deeply stratified society as AI development reshapes global labor and economic structures.

### Key Points
- Tech moguls like Elon Musk and Anthropic’s Dario Amodei are accused of ignoring complex social realities in favor of AI-driven "solutionism."
- Economist Branko Milanović predicts AI could create a four-tier class system, ranging from a "surplus population" to a new aristocracy of tech owners.
- The "surplus population" could reach 15% to 20% of the workforce, potentially requiring government intervention to maintain social stability.
- The "homoploutic" class, consisting of high-earning professionals and capital owners, is expected to expand as they leverage AI to increase their wealth.
- Critics argue that Silicon Valley’s vision of AI-driven abundance may inadvertently lead to chronic inequality and political instability.

### Why it Matters
The unchecked deployment of AI threatens to concentrate wealth among a small elite while rendering significant portions of the workforce obsolete. This economic shift could fundamentally destabilize society if the gains from technological advancement are not distributed equitably.
