Microsoft made Windows 11 more responsive on these 8GB laptops
============================================================

Microsoft has optimized Windows 11 to improve system responsiveness by 20% on devices with 8GB of RAM, helping maintain performance amid rising global hardware and memory costs.

### Key Points
- Microsoft updated Windows 11 to enhance efficiency for systems with 4GB, 8GB, and 16GB of RAM.
- The performance improvements aim to mitigate the impact of rising component costs driven by high demand for AI data centers.
- Major manufacturers including Dell, HP, and Acer are currently offering 8GB laptops priced between $599 and $699.
- Specific models highlighted include the Dell XPS 13, HP OmniBook 5, and Acer Swift Air 14, all featuring Intel Core 5 320 processors.

### Why it Matters
These optimizations allow budget-conscious consumers to maintain a smooth computing experience on entry-level hardware despite the ongoing global memory and storage crisis. By reducing the resource footprint of Windows 11, Microsoft is helping PC manufacturers keep affordable 8GB laptops viable in a market where high-end memory prices have surged.
