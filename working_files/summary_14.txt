The $6.3bn Binance hack explained — 5 things you need to know
============================================================

Hackers linked to North Korea’s TraderTraitor group stole $6.36 billion from the Binance cryptocurrency exchange in a sophisticated 20-minute heist that has triggered global security investigations.

### Key Points
- Hackers allegedly exploited a compromised employee laptop to gain access to Binance withdrawal systems.
- The theft occurred over a 20-minute window following approximately 36 hours of preparatory testing by the attackers.
- Binance has frozen platform deposits and withdrawals while pledging to reimburse affected customers using company funds.
- The U.S. Secret Service is currently collaborating with private sector partners to trace and recover the stolen digital assets.
- Security experts warn that cybercriminals are now launching phishing campaigns using the hack as a lure for fraudulent account recovery schemes.

### Why it Matters
This record-breaking theft highlights significant vulnerabilities in large-scale cryptocurrency exchanges and will likely accelerate calls for stricter global financial regulations. While the incident does not threaten broader financial stability, it serves as a critical reminder for both institutions and individuals to prioritize robust security protocols and phishing awareness.
