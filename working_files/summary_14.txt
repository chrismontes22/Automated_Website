Leaked iPhone 18 Pro Parts Point to Three Color Options Excluding Black
============================================================

Leaker Sonny Dickson has revealed images of SIM card trays suggesting the upcoming iPhone 18 Pro will be available in Silver, Dark Cherry, and Sky Blue color options.

### Key Points
- Leaked SIM tray images indicate Apple may offer the iPhone 18 Pro in Silver, Dark Cherry, and Sky Blue.
- The current iPhone 17 Pro lineup is available in Cosmic Orange, Silver, and Deep Blue.
- While some dummy units previously featured a black option, reports suggest Apple may exclude black for the second consecutive year.
- SIM card trays remain standard for iPhone models sold in markets including Europe, Japan, China, and Australia.
- Apple is expected to officially unveil the iPhone 18 Pro and the new foldable "iPhone Ultra" at its September event next week.

### Why it Matters
Color availability serves as a key differentiator for Apple’s flagship devices and often influences consumer purchasing trends during the initial launch phase. By potentially moving away from traditional black, Apple continues to refine its aesthetic strategy for the premium smartphone market.
