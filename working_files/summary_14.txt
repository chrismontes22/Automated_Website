Bitcoin bulls have one price level to defend
============================================================

Bitcoin is currently testing a critical support level between $81,500 and $83,000 following a recent pullback from highs above $87,400, leaving investors watching for potential further market volatility.

### Key Points
- Bitcoin faces a pivotal test at the $82,000 support level, a price point that previously acted as a significant ceiling during May and early September.
- Analysts warn that a sustained drop below $82,000 could trigger a correction into the high $70,000s, while holding the current zone may support a rally toward $100,000.
- Rising U.S. Treasury yields and volatility in the fixed-income market are currently exerting downward pressure on risk assets like cryptocurrency.
- Market participants are monitoring spot bitcoin exchange-traded fund (ETF) flows and upcoming Personal Consumption Expenditures (PCE) inflation data for signs of future momentum.
- Analysts from firms including STS Digital, Bitget Wallet, and Nexo Dispatch emphasize that the $80,000 to $83,000 range is essential for maintaining a constructive market structure.

### Why it Matters
The stability of this price floor is crucial for determining whether bitcoin maintains its bullish trajectory or enters a deeper period of consolidation. Investors are closely watching macroeconomic indicators, as shifts in inflation data and bond yields continue to influence institutional appetite for digital assets.
