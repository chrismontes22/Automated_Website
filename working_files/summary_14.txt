'Something was thrown out with the bathwater when we moved to modern smartphones': Clicks co-founder Jeff Gadway on bringing physical keyboards back to mobile phones
============================================================

Hardware company Clicks Technology is reviving the tactile smartphone experience with its new Power Keyboard accessory and the upcoming Android-powered Clicks Communicator device featuring a physical keyboard.

### Key Points
- The Clicks Power Keyboard is a magnetic, MagSafe-enabled accessory that functions as both a physical keyboard and a power bank for various mobile devices.
- The Clicks Communicator, launching in December, is an Android 17 smartphone featuring a 4.03-inch 120Hz display and a dedicated physical QWERTY keyboard.
- The Communicator includes a MediaTek Dimensity 8300 processor, 12GB of RAM, 256GB of storage, a 3.5mm headphone jack, and a microSD card slot.
- Clicks is partnering with Niagara to develop a custom launcher that allows users to manage messages directly from the home screen using the physical keyboard.
- The company reports that nearly half of its original keyboard case customers had never used a BlackBerry, indicating broad interest in tactile, intentional technology.

### Why it Matters
These devices cater to a growing consumer desire for more intentional, less distracting mobile experiences that prioritize communication over endless content consumption. By blending modern Android capabilities with nostalgic hardware, Clicks is carving out a niche for users seeking to reclaim screen real estate and improve productivity through tactile input.
