You Can Use Gemini 4 Argon Now: Google’s New AI Is Built for Coding, Math and Robotics
============================================================

Google has expanded access to its Gemini 4 Argon AI model, making the powerful tool available to enterprise customers and premium subscribers across various developer and mobile platforms.

### Key Points
- Gemini 4 Argon is now available to Google AI Ultra and Pro subscribers, as well as users in Android Studio and the Google Antigravity developer platform.
- Introductory pricing is set at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, with rates doubling after November 30.
- Google utilizes the model for internal tasks including Python code optimization, cybersecurity patching, and advancing robotics research.
- The model underwent a voluntary safety review process in coordination with the White House to address potential risks.
- Gemini 4 Argon competes directly with frontier models such as OpenAI’s GPT-6 Astra and Meta’s Muse Wonder.

### Why it Matters
This release signals Google's strategic pivot toward marketing AI as a specialized tool for STEM professionals and complex industrial research rather than consumer-facing creative applications. By positioning Gemini 4 Argon as a high-utility asset for software engineering and physics, the company aims to regain its competitive edge in the enterprise AI market.
