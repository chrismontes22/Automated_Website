Stocks Close Lower on Worries About US Economy
============================================================

US stock indices closed lower on Friday as weak retail sales and consumer sentiment data sparked concerns regarding economic growth and the potential for future corporate earnings declines.

### Key Points
- The S&P 500 fell 0.17%, the Dow Jones Industrial Average dropped 0.20%, and the Nasdaq 100 declined 0.13%.
- July US retail sales fell 0.6% month-over-month, significantly missing market expectations of a 0.1% increase.
- The University of Michigan’s consumer sentiment index dropped to 51.0 in August, falling well below the anticipated 55.0 level.
- The 10-year T-note yield rose 4.0 basis points to 4.682% as investors remained concerned about persistent inflation.
- Reddit shares surged over 12% following the announcement that the company will be added to the S&P 500 index on August 18.

### Why it Matters
The combination of disappointing consumer data and rising Treasury yields has heightened market anxiety regarding the strength of the US economy. While strong second-quarter earnings growth provides some support, investors are increasingly cautious about the Federal Reserve's path for interest rates amid ongoing inflation pressures.
