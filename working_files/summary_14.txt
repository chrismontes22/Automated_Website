Rockstar confirms GTA 6 won't have microtransactions or generative AI at launch
============================================================

Rockstar Games has officially confirmed that Grand Theft Auto 6 will launch on November 19 without microtransactions or generative AI, bucking current industry trends toward automated development tools.

### Key Points
- Grand Theft Auto 6 is scheduled for release on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S.
- Rockstar Games confirmed the title contains no in-game monetization or generative AI technology.
- The £90 Ultimate Edition includes exclusive content like the Electric Fang tattoo parlour and Stock 305 clothing store.
- Standard edition owners can purchase an upgrade to the Ultimate Edition to access bonus content unlocked throughout the story.
- Parent company Take-Two Interactive previously laid off its internal AI team, signaling a strategic departure from industry-wide automation trends.

### Why it Matters
This decision highlights a significant divergence between Rockstar Games and other major publishers that are increasingly integrating generative AI into their development pipelines. By prioritizing human-led design over microtransactions and automation, the studio is positioning the title as a premium, handcrafted experience in a market currently dominated by live-service models.
