Stock market today: Dow, S&P 500, Nasdaq rise ahead of crucial Fed interest rate decision
============================================================

US stocks declined on Wednesday as the Federal Reserve unanimously raised interest rates by 25 basis points to a target range of 3.75% to 4% to combat persistent inflation.

### Key Points
- The Dow Jones Industrial Average fell 1.2%, while the S&P 500 dropped 0.4% following the Federal Reserve's policy announcement.
- Federal Reserve officials signaled the potential for at least one additional rate hike in 2026 to reach their 2% inflation goal.
- The 10-year Treasury yield climbed above 5%, reaching levels not seen since the financial crisis.
- August retail sales grew by 1.2%, exceeding the 0.9% growth expected by economists despite ongoing inflation concerns.
- Benchmark oil prices remained elevated above $100 per barrel, contributing to broader market uncertainty regarding long-term price stability.

### Why it Matters
The Federal Reserve's decision to prioritize price stability over market growth highlights the central bank's aggressive stance against stubborn inflation. This shift in monetary policy creates a challenging environment for equities as investors weigh the impact of higher borrowing costs against resilient consumer spending.
