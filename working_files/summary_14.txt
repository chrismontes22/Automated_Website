Tom Lee's Bitmine bought more ether, added to stock buyback last week
============================================================

Bitmine Immersion increased its Ethereum treasury by purchasing 10,399 ETH for $19.1 million while simultaneously repurchasing 4.5 million shares of company stock to bolster investor value.

### Key Points
- Bitmine acquired 10,399 ETH last week, bringing its total holdings to approximately 5.8 million ETH, representing 4.8% of the circulating supply.
- Chairman Tom Lee noted that ether outperformed the Nasdaq 100 by 2,500 basis points in July, signaling strengthening cryptocurrency fundamentals.
- The company repurchased 4.5 million shares of common stock, increasing its total recent buybacks to 16 million shares.
- Bitmine currently holds $11.3 billion in total assets, including 209 bitcoin and equity stakes in Beast Industries and Eightco Holdings.
- Approximately 85% of the company's ETH holdings are staked, generating an estimated $247 million in annualized revenue through the MAVAN platform.

### Why it Matters
The aggressive accumulation of Ethereum by Bitmine highlights a growing corporate trend of utilizing crypto-heavy treasury strategies to signal market confidence. This approach contrasts with other major firms like Strategy, which recently opted to trim its bitcoin holdings and raise capital through share sales.
