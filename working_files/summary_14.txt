Ynon Kreiz Anointed Co-CEO Of Newly Minted ParaBros; David Ellison Says Mega-Studio Is Transformational Moment For Our Industry”
============================================================

David Ellison has appointed former Mattel CEO Ynon Kreiz as co-CEO of the newly merged Paramount and Warner Bros. Discovery entity, effective upon the deal's expected closing next week.

### Key Points
- David Ellison will oversee long-term strategy, creative vision, and capital allocation for the combined media giant.
- Ynon Kreiz will manage day-to-day operations and the integration of the two companies.
- The appointment follows a federal judge's approval of the antitrust settlement between Paramount and Warner Bros. Discovery.
- Kreiz previously served as CEO of Mattel, where he oversaw the successful 2023 release of the Barbie film.
- The leadership structure mirrors the co-CEO models currently utilized by companies like Netflix, Comcast, and Spotify.

### Why it Matters
This leadership shift signals a major consolidation in the media industry as the new entity attempts to integrate massive production assets under a dual-executive structure. By splitting strategic and operational duties, the company aims to stabilize its business model while navigating the complex integration of two major global entertainment firms.
