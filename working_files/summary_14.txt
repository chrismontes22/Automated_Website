Bitcoin price defends $76K lows as analysts see stocks pricing in ‘political gridlock’
============================================================

Bitcoin prices held steady near $76,000 during Tuesday’s Wall Street opening as investors navigated market volatility and uncertainty surrounding the potential impact of the United States midterm elections.

### Key Points
- Bitcoin traded near $77,000, maintaining support levels established during the previous week's market activity.
- Analysts at The Kobeissi Letter suggest financial markets are currently pricing in two years of potential political gridlock in Washington.
- Apple shares faced downward pressure after the company forecasted lower gross margins for the December quarter due to rising memory-chip costs.
- WTI crude oil prices fell to $93.59 per barrel, marking the lowest valuation since October 15 despite recent US military strikes near Iran.
- The S&P 500 and Nasdaq Composite Index experienced recent declines as investors weighed conflicting economic data and political polling results.

### Why it Matters
The current market behavior reflects a broader investor strategy to hedge against the unpredictability of a divided US Congress. By pricing in potential legislative impasses, traders are signaling that political instability remains a primary driver of volatility across both cryptocurrency and traditional equity sectors.
