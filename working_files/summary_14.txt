Snap is launching a new Specs AI tool, and it’s coming to iOS and Mac
============================================================

Snap has launched Specs Intelligence, an anticipatory AI assistant designed to manage personal and professional tasks by integrating data from connected applications like Gmail and Slack.

### Key Points
- Specs Intelligence is available now in an iOS preview and will expand to Mac via an early-access waitlist.
- The service integrates with Snap’s new augmented reality glasses to display relevant information directly in the user's field of view.
- The AI analyzes user routines and goals to proactively surface meeting agendas, travel itineraries, and deadline reminders.
- Snap utilizes a proprietary mix of open-source and local large language models to power the assistant's functionality.
- The company states that personal data from connected accounts will not be used to train AI models or serve targeted advertisements.

### Why it Matters
This launch marks Snap’s strategic pivot toward integrating personal context into wearable augmented reality hardware. By positioning the tool as an anticipatory assistant, the company aims to differentiate itself in a crowded AI market while navigating significant user privacy and data security concerns.
