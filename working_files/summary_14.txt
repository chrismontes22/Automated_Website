RGB Laser TV throws super bright, super snappy big-screen visuals
============================================================

AWOL Vision has unveiled the LuxVision, a high-end 4K ultra-short-throw projector featuring a 120Hz refresh rate, 6,000 ISO lumens of brightness, and innovative one-touch optical lens shift technology.

### Key Points
- The LuxVision delivers 4K resolution at 120Hz with a 0.18:1 throw ratio, capable of projecting images up to 200 inches.
- It features 6,000 ISO lumens of brightness and 110% Rec.2020 color coverage for high-performance viewing in ambient light.
- Advanced gaming specifications include one-millisecond input lag, three HDMI 2.1 ports, and support for 4-channel multiview.
- The unit includes Anti-RBE algorithms to reduce rainbow effects and a motorized lens cover to prevent dust accumulation.
- Connectivity options include Wi-Fi 7, Google TV, and integration with major smart home platforms like Control4 and Apple HomeKit.
- The projector is scheduled for launch within the next month at a suggested retail price of $6,499.

### Why it Matters
The LuxVision represents a significant advancement in the ultra-short-throw market by combining high-brightness laser output with professional-grade installation features like motorized lens shift. This release intensifies competition among premium home theater brands as they race to standardize 4K/120Hz performance for both cinema enthusiasts and gamers.
