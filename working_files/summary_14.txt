iPhone 18 Pro Pre-Orders May Start at an Unusual Time
============================================================

Apple is reportedly delaying iPhone 18 Pro pre-orders to Saturday, September 12, to avoid coinciding with the 25th anniversary of the September 11 attacks in the United States.

### Key Points
- Apple will host its annual product event on Wednesday, September 9, to unveil the iPhone 18 Pro, Apple Watch Series 12, and Apple Watch Ultra 4.
- The reported shift to a Saturday pre-order date mirrors Apple’s 2015 decision to avoid launching products on the anniversary of the September 11 attacks.
- Pre-orders for the new hardware are rumored to begin at midnight PT on September 12, deviating from the company's traditional 5 a.m. PT start time.
- The company's first foldable iPhone may follow a different release schedule, with pre-orders potentially delayed until late September.

### Why it Matters
Adjusting the launch schedule demonstrates how major corporations navigate sensitive cultural anniversaries to maintain brand reputation and public perception. This shift also highlights potential changes to Apple's long-standing retail logistics and the timing of its global product rollouts.
