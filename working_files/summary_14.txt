Texas Gov. Greg Abbott says data centers deserve the backlash they're getting: 'They basically dug their own grave'
============================================================

Texas Governor Greg Abbott has criticized the artificial intelligence industry for failing to secure local support, leading to widespread public backlash against the construction of massive data centers.

### Key Points
- Governor Abbott stated that AI companies face well-earned criticism for poor communication regarding the environmental and utility impacts of their large-scale data center projects.
- A Gallup survey indicates that 70% of Americans oppose the construction of AI data centers in their local communities, with 48% expressing strong opposition.
- Abbott has halted approximately 1,800 data center projects in Texas to implement new audit requirements and protect residents from rising infrastructure costs.
- The governor is now considering a ban on data center developments in rural Texas to address concerns regarding noise, water usage, and local utility prices.
- These developments create a political challenge as the Trump administration continues to support major initiatives like the $500 billion Stargate Project led by OpenAI and Oracle.

### Why it Matters
The growing public resistance to data centers forces a difficult reconciliation between national AI expansion goals and local community interests. This shift in policy could significantly slow the deployment of critical infrastructure and force tech companies to adopt more transparent community engagement strategies.
