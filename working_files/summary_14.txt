Studio Behind Critically-Acclaimed PSVR Game Shuts Down After 12 Years
============================================================

Polyarc, the acclaimed independent game studio behind the award-winning virtual reality adventure series Moss, is officially shutting down after twelve years of operation in the gaming industry.

### Key Points
- Polyarc gained industry recognition for its 2018 VR title Moss, which featured the protagonist Quill and earned numerous critical awards.
- The studio released the sequel, Moss: Book II, in 2022, which subsequently won Best VR Game at The Game Awards.
- Earlier this year, the developer attempted to expand its audience by releasing 2D versions of the Moss games for standard television displays.
- The studio announced its closure via LinkedIn, citing the end of its 12-year development journey and assisting staff in finding new employment.

### Why it Matters
The closure of Polyarc highlights the ongoing challenges faced by developers specializing in virtual reality despite producing high-quality, critically acclaimed software. This trend reflects a broader cooling of the VR market as major hardware manufacturers scale back their investments in the immersive gaming sector.
