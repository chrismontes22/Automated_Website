Maine Watches DraftKings AI as Massachusetts Opens Inquiry
============================================================

Maine’s Gambling Control Unit is monitoring how sports-betting operators like DraftKings utilize artificial intelligence to target promotional offers, despite the state currently lacking specific regulations for such technology.

### Key Points
- Maine regulators are observing DraftKings' use of machine learning following reports that the company analyzes betting histories to tailor promotional offers to specific customers.
- DraftKings denies allegations that it uses artificial intelligence to target users based on gambling losses or indicators of potential addiction.
- Massachusetts regulators have launched a formal investigation into the company's promotional practices, distinguishing their approach from Maine’s current monitoring status.
- Maryland officials are considering legislation to prohibit betting apps from using algorithms to identify and target users who exhibit patterns of problem gambling.
- Maine currently has no AI-specific gambling laws, and the state has not announced a formal timetable for potential rulemaking or enforcement actions.

### Why it Matters
This situation highlights a growing regulatory tension between the use of predictive analytics in sports betting and the protection of vulnerable consumers. As states evaluate these automated marketing practices, the outcome could lead to new industry standards that limit how operators leverage customer data for promotional targeting.
