Most Laptops Mute Your Camera in Software, This One Cuts the Wire
============================================================

The Purism Librem 16 is a privacy-focused Linux laptop featuring physical hardware kill switches and a disabled Intel Management Engine to ensure complete user control over system security.

### Key Points
- The laptop includes physical kill switches that electrically disconnect the camera, microphone, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth components from the motherboard.
- It is powered by an Intel Core i7-13620H processor and supports up to 64GB of RAM and 16TB of storage.
- The device runs PureOS and utilizes PureBoot firmware with the Heads security framework to detect unauthorized firmware tampering.
- Purism offers an optional anti-interdiction service to provide physical tamper-evidence measures during the shipping process.
- Pricing for the Librem 16 ranges from $2,899 for the base model to $9,799 for the fully configured 64GB RAM and 16TB storage version.

### Why it Matters
This device addresses security concerns by moving privacy protections from the software layer to the physical hardware level. By eliminating hidden processors and providing verifiable boot processes, it offers a specialized solution for users who require high-assurance computing environments.
