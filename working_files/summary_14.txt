Ubisoft Is Closing Down Yet Another Studio, Possibly Ending A 25-Year Fan-Favorite Franchise
============================================================

Ubisoft has proposed the closure of its Finnish studio RedLynx and announced staff reductions at Ubisoft Singapore as part of an ongoing corporate restructuring effort throughout 2026.

### Key Points
- Ubisoft initiated a proposal to close RedLynx, a studio acquired in 2011 that is best known for the Trials motorbike stunt game series.
- The potential closure of RedLynx would impact 52 employees and is currently subject to mandatory Finnish "co-operation negotiations."
- Ubisoft Singapore is downsizing its workforce by 70 positions to align the studio with current production requirements.
- Ubisoft Singapore previously led the development of Skull and Bones, a title that reportedly resulted in a $200 million loss for the publisher.
- Ubisoft’s stock price has declined by approximately 95 percent since its 2018 peak, currently trading near €5 per share as of March 2026.

### Why it Matters
These layoffs reflect Ubisoft's broader struggle to stabilize its finances and operations amid a significant multi-year decline in market valuation. The potential loss of RedLynx raises questions about the future of the Trials franchise and highlights the publisher's aggressive efforts to cut costs across its global development network.
