How to remove your personal information from the internet
============================================================

Protecting your digital privacy is increasingly essential as data brokers and security breaches expose personal information, but you can reclaim control through manual cleanup and automated removal services.

### Key Points
- Data breaches rose by 211% in 2024, while the FTC reported 1.1 million identity theft cases, highlighting the growing risk to personal information.
- Personal identifiable information (PII) includes sensitive data like Social Security numbers, medical records, and home addresses often sold by third-party brokers.
- Services like Incogni automate the opt-out process across more than 4,000 data broker sites and provide continuous monitoring to prevent data from reappearing.
- Users can manually reduce their digital footprint by deleting dormant accounts, adjusting app privacy permissions, and requesting Google remove specific search results.
- Proactive security measures, such as using unique passwords, enabling two-factor authentication, and utilizing VPNs, help prevent future data harvesting by malicious actors.

### Why it Matters
Unsecured personal data serves as a primary target for scammers, spammers, and individuals seeking to exploit private information for real-world harm. By actively managing your digital footprint, you significantly reduce your vulnerability to identity theft and unwanted surveillance in an increasingly connected landscape.
