Bitcoin’s Surge Leaves Strategy With $1.4 Billion Profit
============================================================

Bitcoin prices surged to $77,300 this week, returning major corporate holder Strategy to profitability with an unrealized gain of $1.4 billion on its massive cryptocurrency investment portfolio.

### Key Points
- Bitcoin rose 23% from $62,800 to $77,300 following U.S. Treasury bond market intervention and proposed Clarity Act legislation.
- Strategy currently holds 840,447 Bitcoin, valued at approximately $64.97 billion at the latest market price.
- The company’s average acquisition cost per Bitcoin is $75,385, placing its current holdings back into a profitable position.
- Strategy previously faced an unrealized loss of $13 billion when Bitcoin prices dropped to a yearly low of $58,000 in June.
- To manage cash reserves and preferred stock dividends, the firm sold nearly 7,000 Bitcoin at a loss earlier this year.

### Why it Matters
This recovery highlights the extreme volatility inherent in corporate cryptocurrency strategies and the significant impact of macroeconomic policy on digital asset valuations. As the largest corporate Bitcoin owner, Strategy’s return to profitability serves as a key indicator for institutional investors monitoring the long-term viability of holding crypto on a corporate balance sheet.
