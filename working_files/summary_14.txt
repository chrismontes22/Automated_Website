Improved Neurable One ANC headphones actively track brain activity while sounding great
============================================================

Neurable has launched the Neurable One, a new pair of consumer headphones featuring integrated EEG sensors designed to track cognitive performance and brain health throughout the day.

### Key Points
- The Neurable One headphones utilize 12 integrated EEG sensors within the ear pads to monitor brainwave activity in real time.
- Users can access focus insights, meditation tracking, and "Smart Brain Breaks" via a dedicated companion app for Android and iOS.
- The hardware includes 40mm beryllium drivers, six microphones for active noise cancellation, and a battery life of up to 12 hours with EEG tracking enabled.
- CEO Ramses Alcaide stated the device represents over a decade of research aimed at transitioning brain-sensing technology from laboratory settings to everyday consumer use.
- The headphones are currently priced at $499, positioning them as a premium wearable for users interested in long-term cognitive data analysis.

### Why it Matters
This product signals a shift toward integrating clinical-grade neuroscience into mainstream consumer electronics, similar to how smartwatches popularized physical health tracking. By providing actionable data on mental focus and fatigue, these headphones could change how professionals manage productivity and long-term cognitive well-being.
