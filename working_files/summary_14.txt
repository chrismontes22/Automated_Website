Your Nintendo Switch's Battery Has A Hidden Risk If You Forget To Use It
============================================================

Nintendo Switch owners should charge their consoles at least once every six months to prevent permanent battery degradation caused by leaving the device in a fully depleted state.

### Key Points
- Lithium-ion batteries can lose the ability to hold a charge if left unpowered for extended periods.
- Nintendo recommends maintaining battery levels between 20% and 80% for optimal long-term health.
- Users should store and operate the console in temperatures ranging from 41 to 95 degrees Fahrenheit.
- Neglecting to charge the device periodically can lead to permanent capacity loss, rendering the hardware unusable.
- These maintenance guidelines apply to all lithium-rechargeable electronics, including the Steam Deck, smartphones, and tablets.

### Why it Matters
Proper battery maintenance significantly extends the functional lifespan of portable gaming consoles and other mobile electronics. By following these simple charging habits, users can avoid premature hardware failure and costly device replacements.
