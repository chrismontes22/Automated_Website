OpenAI President on Apple Lawsuit: We Don't Need Your Secrets
============================================================

OpenAI President Greg Brockman addressed ongoing litigation regarding alleged trade secret theft while confirming the company is developing a new family of AI-powered hardware devices for future release.

### Key Points
- Greg Brockman denied allegations that OpenAI misappropriated Apple trade secrets, asserting the company remains focused on its own internal innovation and long-term development roadmap.
- OpenAI is currently developing a series of hardware devices, including a rumored screenless, voice-first AI companion designed for home use.
- The company is actively building its own proprietary chips to support a new ecosystem of products intended to be maximally useful in the AI era.
- Brockman acknowledged that the current ChatGPT app interface is cluttered and confirmed plans to remove the separate Work tab by the end of the year.
- Development of these new hardware products involves collaboration with former Apple designer Jony Ive, though Ive is not a named party in the current litigation.

### Why it Matters
This development signals OpenAI’s strategic shift from a software-only provider to a hardware manufacturer competing directly with established tech giants. Successfully launching a proprietary AI device could redefine consumer interaction with artificial intelligence while testing the company's ability to manage complex legal challenges and product integration.
