How Sci-Fi Films Predicted AI's Psychological Toll
============================================================

A 2026 study in the Annals of Neurosciences reveals that while AI tools boost productivity, they also contribute to cognitive fatigue, attention deficits, and reduced decision-making confidence.

### Key Points
- A 2026 study of 500 young adults found that AI reliance increases productivity but weakens attention spans and memory retention.
- Over one-third of psychologists report that patients are increasingly seeking mental health guidance from unregulated AI chatbots.
- Terms like "digital dementia" and "AI psychosis" are gaining traction to describe cognitive decline and delusions linked to excessive AI interaction.
- Experts warn that replacing human intimacy with AI companions can lead to emotional isolation and a loss of authentic interpersonal connection.
- Five classic science fiction films, including "Her" and "Ex Machina," serve as cautionary tales regarding the risks of over-relying on AI for judgment and companionship.

### Why it Matters
The growing reliance on AI for daily decision-making and emotional support poses significant risks to long-term cognitive health and human social development. As these technologies become integrated into personal lives, the lack of regulation and potential for psychological dependency highlight an urgent need for greater awareness regarding digital habits.
