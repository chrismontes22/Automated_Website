I tested the Google Pixel 11, and it's a fun blend of social media and AI magic
============================================================

The Google Pixel 11 introduces enhanced AI capabilities and social media integration, retailing for $899 with improved base storage, faster Qi2 wireless charging, and the new Tensor G6 processor.

### Key Points
- The Pixel 11 features a $100 price increase over the previous model but includes higher base storage and 25W Qi2 wireless charging.
- New software tools include Magic Capture for automated photo selection and Pause Point, a Digital Wellbeing feature designed to reduce social media distraction.
- The device is powered by the Tensor G6 chip, which prioritizes AI tasks like the Daily Brief and Rambler voice-to-text transcription.
- Hardware design remains thick at 8.6mm, and the device lacks a vapor chamber, leading to noticeable heat during intensive gaming sessions.
- Camera performance is largely consistent with the Pixel 10, though images exhibit a warmer color profile compared to the Pixel 11 Pro.

### Why it Matters
The Pixel 11 highlights Google's strategic shift toward integrating AI directly into daily social media workflows and personal productivity tasks. By balancing flagship-level software features with a more accessible price point than the Pro model, Google aims to maintain its competitive edge in the crowded smartphone market.
