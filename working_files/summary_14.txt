Google Answers If Some Sites Can Ignore GEO And Just Focus On SEO via @sejournal, @martinibuster
============================================================

Google Search Advocate John Mueller clarified that businesses do not need specialized strategies for Generative Engine Optimization because AI search results are derived directly from the existing index.

### Key Points
- John Mueller stated that Google’s AI-generated search responses rely on the same crawled index used for traditional search results.
- AI search features pull content from top-ranking pages and related queries, requiring no unique optimization tactics beyond standard SEO practices.
- Many consultants and plugins currently market "Generative Engine Optimization" services, despite Google confirming these specialized efforts are unnecessary for ranking.
- The clarification followed a query regarding whether specific industries, such as gambling or adult content, require distinct approaches for AI-driven discovery.

### Why it Matters
This guidance simplifies the evolving search landscape by confirming that traditional SEO remains the primary driver for visibility in AI-powered search results. Businesses can avoid investing in unproven optimization tools and instead focus on maintaining high-quality, indexable content to ensure performance across all Google search formats.
