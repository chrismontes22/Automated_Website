Qualcomm Announces Renewed Agreement With Apple
============================================================

Qualcomm has officially renewed its global patent licensing agreement with Apple, extending the long-standing partnership between the two technology giants beyond the current March 2027 expiration date.

### Key Points
- The renewed patent licensing agreement between Qualcomm and Apple is scheduled to take effect on April 1, 2027.
- This patent deal remains distinct from the separate supply agreement for cellular modems, which is currently slated to conclude in 2026.
- Apple is actively transitioning to its own custom-designed hardware, utilizing the C2 modem in most iPhone 18 models and the C1X in recent iPad devices.
- Only the U.S. version of the iPhone 18 Pro Max currently retains a Qualcomm cellular modem as Apple shifts toward internal component production.

### Why it Matters
This extension ensures continued legal stability for Apple’s hardware ecosystem while the company works to reduce its reliance on third-party modem suppliers. It highlights the ongoing shift in the mobile industry as Apple prioritizes the integration of its proprietary silicon across its global product lineup.
