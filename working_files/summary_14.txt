Baseus Security U2 Pro Launches With Dual 3K Cameras and a Spotlight That Tracks Intruders
============================================================

Baseus is launching the Security PrimeSentinel U2 Pro outdoor camera system in the United States on October 20, 2026, featuring dual 3K lenses and an active tracking spotlight.

### Key Points
- The U2 Pro features a fixed 3K camera paired with a motorized 360-degree pan-and-tilt camera for comprehensive property monitoring.
- A specialized Target-Focused Deterrence Spotlight automatically tracks and illuminates moving subjects, such as people or vehicles, in real time.
- The system includes AI-powered recognition for people, pets, and vehicles, with support for up to 512GB of local encrypted storage.
- A 7,800mAh battery provides up to 150 days of operation, supplemented by an included 3W solar panel for extended power.
- The device is rated IP65 for weather resistance and functions in temperatures ranging from -4°F to 122°F.
- The camera will retail for $279.99 in the U.S. and €269.99 in Europe, with the European release scheduled for November 17.

### Why it Matters
This product introduces active tracking technology to the consumer security market, moving beyond passive recording to provide real-time visual deterrence. By integrating solar charging and local storage, the system offers homeowners a flexible, subscription-free alternative to traditional cloud-based security setups.
