EA Tells Devs Its Saudi Arabia Owners Won’t Make Creative Decisions, Queer Fans Don’t Buy It
============================================================

Electronic Arts has been acquired for $55 billion by a consortium including Saudi Arabia’s Public Investment Fund, Silver Lake Partners, and Affinity Partners, sparking significant concerns regarding future layoffs.

### Key Points
- The $55 billion acquisition was finalized on Tuesday, August 4, following an initial announcement in September 2025.
- EA plans to cut $170 million in costs through "organizational efficiencies" to manage $18 billion in debt.
- Employees have expressed frustration over a lack of transparency regarding potential layoffs during recent internal town hall meetings.
- Fans of franchises like *The Sims*, *Dragon Age*, and *Apex Legends* fear the new ownership will restrict LGBTQIA+ representation due to Saudi Arabia’s human rights record.
- Executives have claimed that the new partners will not influence creative decisions, though many developers and fans remain skeptical of these assurances.

### Why it Matters
The acquisition marks a major shift in the gaming industry, raising questions about the future of inclusive storytelling in AAA titles and the stability of long-standing development studios. For players and investors, the deal highlights the growing influence of sovereign wealth funds in gaming and the potential for corporate restructuring to prioritize debt repayment over creative autonomy.
