Last-Minute Leak Reveals Foldable iPhone Name, Price, and Colors
============================================================

Apple is set to unveil its first foldable smartphone, the iPhone Duo, at the "Surprise and Shine" event on September 9, featuring a $2,000 starting price point.

### Key Points
- The iPhone Duo will launch with a 256GB base storage capacity and is expected to reach retail shelves as early as October.
- Pricing for the foldable device is confirmed to start at $2,000, with high-capacity models reaching approximately $3,000.
- Apple will offer the new foldable handset in two color options: white and dark blue.
- The device announcement coincides with the reveal of the iPhone 18 Pro models during the company's scheduled event.

### Why it Matters
This launch marks Apple's official entry into the competitive foldable smartphone market, challenging existing devices from manufacturers like Samsung. The high price point suggests the company is positioning the iPhone Duo as a premium, ultra-luxury product for early adopters.
