Our top Windows 11 laptops of 2026: all the models that impressed us most in our tough tests
============================================================

TechRadar has released its curated list of top-rated Windows 11 laptops for 2026, highlighting high-performing models that earned at least four out of five stars during rigorous testing.

### Key Points
- TechRadar evaluated numerous Windows 11 laptops, selecting only those that achieved a minimum rating of four out of five stars.
- The HP OmniBook 7 Aero is recommended for mobile professionals due to its lightweight design, robust performance, and extensive port selection.
- The Samsung Galaxy Book6 Ultra is highlighted as a premium, high-performance option featuring a high-quality display and powerful internal components.
- The guide serves as a resource for consumers planning potential hardware upgrades ahead of upcoming retail events like Black Friday.

### Why it Matters
This curated list helps consumers navigate a crowded laptop market by identifying devices that meet specific performance and portability standards. These recommendations provide a reliable starting point for buyers looking to maximize value during major seasonal sales events.
