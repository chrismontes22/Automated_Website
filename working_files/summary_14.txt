Here’s What Capcom Said About Using Generative AI In The RE Engine: ‘AI Is Not A Replacement For Humans’
============================================================

Capcom detailed its REX Initiative at an October 2 engineering conference, outlining plans to integrate AI into the RE Engine to streamline development while avoiding AI-generated game assets.

### Key Points
- The REX Initiative aims to evolve the RE Engine through tools like RE:Dox for code simplification and RE:Log for error reporting.
- Capcom released RE:Dox and RE:Log as open-source tools on GitHub to allow AI programs to learn and assist in internal development workflows.
- AI agents will be utilized to perform automated playtests, detect performance bugs, and suggest fixes for human developers to implement.
- The company explicitly stated that AI will not be used to create game assets, focusing instead on reducing repetitive tasks for human staff.
- These technological advancements are designed to maintain the engine's scalability across diverse hardware, ranging from PCs to the Nintendo Switch.

### Why it Matters
By automating technical debugging and playtesting, Capcom aims to shift developer focus toward creative tasks rather than repetitive maintenance. This strategy highlights a growing industry trend of using AI as a productivity tool to improve development efficiency without replacing human artistry.
