The First Snapdragon C Laptop Just Showed Up, and It Isn’t $300
============================================================

The Acer Aspire Go 15 AG15-Q31P, the first laptop powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon C processor, is now available for pre-order in the UK for £499.99.

### Key Points
- The Aspire Go 15 features an eight-core Snapdragon C processor, 8GB of soldered LPDDR5 memory, and 128GB of storage.
- Pre-orders are currently listed on Amazon UK with deliveries scheduled to begin on September 11.
- Hardware specifications include a 15.6-inch 1080p display, Wi-Fi 6E, Bluetooth 5.4, and a 53Wh battery.
- The device includes a 1080p webcam, two USB-C ports, one HDMI output, and a 3.5mm headphone jack.
- The £499.99 price point exceeds the initial industry expectations for the Snapdragon C platform, which was marketed to compete with budget-friendly Chromebooks.

### Why it Matters
This launch serves as a critical test for the Snapdragon C platform's ability to deliver affordable, efficient Windows hardware to the entry-level market. While the current pricing positions the device as a mid-tier option rather than a budget alternative, its performance will influence future manufacturer adoption and the competitive landscape against traditional x86 laptops.
