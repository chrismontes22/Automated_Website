Amazon Is Building the Country’s Single Most Polluting Power Plant for a Texas Data Center
============================================================

Amazon has received regulatory approval to construct a massive natural gas power plant in West Texas to support a new data center, significantly increasing its carbon emissions.

### Key Points
- The facility will utilize 35 natural gas turbines to generate up to 7.65 gigawatts of power exclusively for the Amazon data center.
- Projected annual emissions reach 33 million tons of carbon dioxide, more than doubling the output of the current most polluting U.S. power plant.
- Located in Pecos County, the project is designed to operate independently of the local power grid to avoid impacting residential electricity costs.
- The development highlights a growing trend among tech companies prioritizing rapid AI infrastructure deployment over previous corporate carbon neutrality commitments.

### Why it Matters
This project underscores the escalating tension between the massive energy demands of commercial artificial intelligence and corporate sustainability pledges. It signals a potential shift in the tech industry toward on-site fossil fuel generation, which could significantly complicate national efforts to reduce greenhouse gas emissions.
