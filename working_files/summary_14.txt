The $899 Pixel 11’s Longer 30x Zoom and New Camera Bar Design Are Impressive
============================================================

Google has announced the Pixel 11, featuring an upgraded Tensor G6 processor, improved 30x digital zoom, and faster wireless charging, with a new starting price of $899.

### Key Points
- The Pixel 11 starts at $899 for the 256GB model, as Google has discontinued the $799 entry-level 128GB version.
- New hardware features include a 40% thinner camera bar, a 4,985 mAh battery, and 25-watt Qi2 wireless charging.
- The device runs on Android 17 and is powered by the new Tensor G6 processor with seven years of guaranteed software support.
- Digital zoom capabilities have been expanded to 30x, utilizing software enhancements to improve image detail.
- The phone will be available for purchase on August 20 in four colors: frost, pistachio, hibiscus, and obsidian.

### Why it Matters
The shift in the Pixel 11's pricing reflects broader industry trends as manufacturers grapple with rising component costs and ongoing RAM shortages. By increasing the entry-level storage and price, Google is aligning its flagship strategy with competitors like Samsung and Motorola to maintain margins in a challenging hardware market.
