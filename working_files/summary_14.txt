Apple Releasing HomePod Mini 2 Tomorrow With These New Features
============================================================

Apple’s HomePod mini 2 launches in stores and arrives to customers this Friday, October 30, featuring the S10 chip, Wi-Fi 6E connectivity, and a new $149 price point.

### Key Points
- The HomePod mini 2 is powered by the S10 chip, the same processor found in the Apple Watch Series 10.
- New hardware features include Wi-Fi 6E support and a Sound Glow interface that pulses in sync with music.
- Apple Intelligence-powered Siri AI features will not be available at launch and require a future software update.
- The device supports surround sound configurations when paired with the fourth-generation Apple TV 4K.
- Available in five colors, the speaker is priced at $149 in the United States.
- The microphone array has been reduced from four microphones in the original model to three in the new version.

### Why it Matters
This release signals Apple's strategy to integrate its smart speakers more deeply into the home theater ecosystem through improved connectivity and surround sound capabilities. While the hardware upgrade offers faster processing, the delayed launch of Siri AI features highlights the company's ongoing challenge in deploying its generative intelligence software across all product lines.
