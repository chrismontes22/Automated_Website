Nanoleaf’s Smart LED Monitor Stand brings ambient lighting and desk organization together
============================================================

Nanoleaf has launched a new Smart LED Monitor Stand, featuring integrated task lighting, customizable RGB ambient effects, and a modular design to enhance ergonomic desk setups and workspace organization.

### Key Points
- The stand features a solid wood construction with a walnut veneer and supports monitors weighing up to 70 kg with its center support column.
- It includes 23 standard 1/4-inch threaded mounting holes for attaching cameras, microphones, and other desk accessories.
- The forward-facing task light offers 550 lumens with a tunable color temperature range of 1,700K to 6,500K and an anti-glare UGR<10 rating.
- Rear-facing RGB lighting provides 102 addressable zones that sync with screen activity or music via the Nanoleaf Desktop App.
- The device supports Matter, Wi-Fi, Thread, and Bluetooth, allowing for integration with major smart home platforms like Apple Home and Google Home.
- The product is priced at $169.99 and requires a separate 15W USB-C power adapter for operation.

### Why it Matters
This product represents a shift toward multifunctional smart furniture that consolidates lighting, ergonomics, and cable management into a single, connected ecosystem. By integrating Matter support, Nanoleaf provides users with a versatile tool that simplifies complex desk setups while maintaining compatibility with broader smart home automation standards.
