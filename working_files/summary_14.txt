Apple Adds Three More Macs to Obsolete Products List
============================================================

Apple has officially designated the 2018 MacBook Air and two 2017 and 2018 MacBook Pro models as obsolete, ending standard hardware repair support for these specific laptop devices.

### Key Points
- Apple moved the 2018 Retina MacBook Air, 13-inch 2018 MacBook Pro, and 13-inch 2017 MacBook Pro from the vintage list to obsolete status.
- Products are classified as obsolete seven years after Apple stops distributing them for sale, meaning the company no longer provides hardware repair services.
- Apple Authorized Service Providers generally stop stocking repair components for obsolete devices, limiting options for owners seeking hardware fixes.
- Owners may still qualify for battery-only replacements, as Apple maintains a 10-year repair policy for laptop batteries following the final distribution date.

### Why it Matters
This policy change marks the end of official support for several popular Intel-based Mac models that introduced significant design updates like Retina displays and Touch ID. Users with these devices must now rely on third-party repair shops or consider hardware upgrades as official parts become unavailable.
