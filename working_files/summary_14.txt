Fraud alerts, credit locks, dark web scans — this $99.99 LifeLock plan does the worrying for you
============================================================

LifeLock is offering a 15-month subscription to its Core Identity Theft Protection service for $99.99, providing new subscribers a significant discount off the standard $187.35 retail price.

### Key Points
- The discounted plan covers one adult for 15 months and includes real-time alerts for suspicious credit applications or loan attempts.
- Features include dark web monitoring, monthly credit reports, and AI-powered detection for exposed payment card information.
- Subscribers receive access to U.S.-based Identity Restoration Specialists and up to $1.05 million in coverage for stolen funds and recovery expenses.
- The service provides automatic data broker removal and monitoring for unauthorized Buy Now, Pay Later accounts.
- This promotional offer is exclusively available to new Norton subscribers and features automatic renewal at the end of the term.

### Why it Matters
Identity theft remains a significant financial risk that can be difficult to detect through standard bank alerts alone. This discounted subscription provides a comprehensive security layer for consumers looking to protect their credit profiles and personal data against evolving digital fraud threats.
