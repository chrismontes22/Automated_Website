Sonnet Breakaway Box 850 T5 Thunderbolt 5 eGPU
============================================================

Sonnet Technologies has launched the Breakaway Box 850 T5, an external GPU enclosure featuring Thunderbolt 5 connectivity and an 850W power supply for high-performance desktop graphics cards.

### Key Points
- Supports triple-wide NVIDIA GeForce RTX 50, 40, and 30 series, plus AMD Radeon RX 9000, 7000, and 6000 series cards.
- Includes an integrated 80 Gbps Thunderbolt 5 dock with three USB-A ports and a 5 Gigabit Ethernet port.
- Features a PCIe 4.0 x16 slot and provides up to 100W of power delivery to connected Windows 11 computers.
- Utilizes a temperature-controlled, ultra-quiet cooling fan to manage thermal output during intensive processing tasks.
- Available now for $599.99 USD to support demanding workflows like 3D rendering, video editing, and local AI model execution.

### Why it Matters
This device allows professionals to bypass the thermal and power limitations of mobile hardware by utilizing desktop-class GPUs on portable workstations. It provides a scalable solution for users requiring high-performance computing power for complex visual effects and emerging AI applications.
