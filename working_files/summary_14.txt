Roku didn't let me change my DNS settings, but I found the perfect workaround
============================================================

Roku devices do not allow users to manually configure DNS settings, requiring owners to update their router’s network configuration to block tracking or implement custom ad-filtering services.

### Key Points
- Roku devices lack a native menu for manual DNS configuration, forcing the device to rely on automatic settings provided by the local router.
- Users can override Roku’s default DNS by updating settings in their router’s administrative interface, which applies the change to all connected network devices.
- Some Roku models may attempt to bypass custom router settings by sending DNS requests directly to Google’s public servers (8.8.8.8 and 8.8.4.4).
- Services like AdGuard DNS and NextDNS can be used to filter ads and trackers, though users should be aware that blocking certain domains may break specific streaming channels.
- Router-level DNS changes require a full system restart of the Roku device to ensure it drops old network configurations and adopts the new server settings.

### Why it Matters
Implementing custom DNS at the router level provides a centralized way to enhance privacy and reduce ad tracking across all home devices, including streaming hardware that lacks granular controls. While this approach requires careful configuration to avoid breaking internet connectivity, it remains the most effective method for users to regain control over their network traffic.
