Quantitative Event-Driven Modeling: BTC Balance Sheet Shocks with SEC EDGAR Filings in R
============================================================

A new quantitative analysis framework demonstrates that MicroStrategy’s equity valuation is primarily driven by spot Bitcoin price movements and structural changes in its corporate treasury balance sheet holdings.

### Key Points
- The model utilizes the R programming language and the `tidymodels` framework to analyze MicroStrategy (MSTR) stock performance.
- Automated SEC EDGAR parsing via the `secfile` package extracts real-time digital asset holdings directly from XBRL financial filings.
- The structural model achieved an 82.08% out-of-sample R-Squared, significantly outperforming a naive baseline benchmark of 4.12%.
- The pipeline incorporates a "Balance Sheet Shock" metric to quantify the impact of debt-funded Bitcoin acquisitions on daily equity returns.
- Interactive visualizations provide dynamic price projections and 2-sigma volatility bands to identify potential overbought or oversold market conditions.

### Why it Matters
This research highlights that traditional corporate finance metrics are insufficient for evaluating MicroStrategy, which now functions primarily as a Bitcoin treasury vehicle. By automating the ingestion of regulatory filings, investors can better model the relationship between corporate capital allocation and digital asset market volatility.
