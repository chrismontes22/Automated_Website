Why all‑in‑one online security isn't just easier for modern households — it's essential
============================================================

Modern households face increased cyber risks as fragmented security across multiple devices creates vulnerabilities that attackers can exploit through lateral movement within a shared home network.

### Key Points
- Bitdefender offers unified security suites including Total, Premium, and Ultimate tiers to protect Windows, macOS, iOS, and Android devices.
- The 2025 Verizon Data Breach Investigations Report notes that 60 percent of breaches involve human error, such as misconfigurations or phishing.
- Bitdefender Total Security provides a centralized dashboard for real-time threat detection and webcam protection across five devices.
- Premium Security tiers include an unlimited VPN to secure public Wi-Fi connections and a password manager to prevent credential reuse.
- Bitdefender Ultimate Security adds identity monitoring and up to $2 million in identity theft insurance to protect personal and financial data.
- Independent testing by AV-Comparatives shows Bitdefender maintains a 99.5 percent protection rate against real-world threats.

### Why it Matters
Using a single, integrated security platform eliminates the configuration friction and coverage gaps that occur when managing multiple disparate apps. This unified approach simplifies digital maintenance for families while significantly reducing the risk of lateral malware attacks across connected home devices.
