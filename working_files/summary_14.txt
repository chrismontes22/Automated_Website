Google's AI-Powered Lifestyle App 'Dreambeans' Now Free in the US
============================================================

Google has launched Dreambeans, a new AI-powered app for US users that integrates personal data from Google services to curate daily, illustrated stories designed to spark inspiration.

### Key Points
- Dreambeans utilizes Google’s Personal Intelligence to aggregate information from Gmail, Calendar, Photos, YouTube, and Search History.
- The app provides a limited daily feed of 10 to 14 stories to discourage doomscrolling and promote offline activity.
- Each story features custom artwork generated by Nano Banana 2, creating an illustrated, personalized visual style.
- Users can customize their feed preferences, provide feedback via chat, and choose which specific Google services to connect.
- The app is currently available for free to Google account holders in the United States.
- Privacy features allow users to delete their data at any time and ensure that only the individual user can access their generated stories.

### Why it Matters
This app represents a growing industry trend of using AI to combat digital addiction by providing curated, finite content rather than infinite feeds. By leveraging deep integration with personal data, Google is testing whether hyper-personalized lifestyle suggestions can offer meaningful utility without compromising user privacy.
