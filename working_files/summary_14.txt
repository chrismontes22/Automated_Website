What to know about Elon Musk's massive $16.8 billion chip factory
============================================================

Elon Musk has announced plans to construct Terafab, a $16.8 billion semiconductor manufacturing facility in southeastern Texas that is projected to become the world's largest building.

### Key Points
- The 100-million-square-foot facility will be located approximately 70 miles outside of Houston.
- Terafab will utilize a vertically integrated production model, housing manufacturing, packaging, and testing under one roof.
- The plant aims to support the massive compute requirements of Musk’s companies, including Tesla’s electric vehicles and the xAI artificial intelligence division.
- The project is expected to create 3,000 jobs and will source water from a nearby reservoir to minimize local environmental impact.
- Musk anticipates that the combined chip demand from his companies will eventually exceed one terawatt of compute power.

### Why it Matters
This massive investment highlights the intensifying global race to secure semiconductor supply chains as artificial intelligence development drives unprecedented demand for high-performance computing hardware. By vertically integrating production, Musk aims to bypass traditional supply bottlenecks and ensure his companies maintain the necessary infrastructure to lead in the robotics and AI sectors.
