Crypto's resilience tested as oil rises after Iran strikes, Fed signals rates could still rise
============================================================

Bitcoin maintained a price near $64,000 despite global market volatility triggered by rising oil prices, geopolitical tensions in the Middle East, and a hawkish Federal Reserve interest rate outlook.

### Key Points
- Bitcoin held steady at $63,915 while U.S. equities declined following Iran's ballistic missile strikes on U.S. troops.
- The Federal Reserve kept interest rates unchanged, though three committee members voted for an increase, signaling potential future hikes.
- Approximately $286 million in crypto futures positions were liquidated within 24 hours due to erratic market swings.
- Oil prices surged 8% overnight, contributing to a 2.2% drop in the Dow and pushing the Nasdaq to a three-month low.
- DeFi tokens, including Injective and Uniswap, outperformed the broader market despite a slightly bearish long/short ratio in crypto futures.

### Why it Matters
The resilience of digital assets during this period of geopolitical and macroeconomic instability suggests a decoupling from traditional risk-off sentiment. However, the combination of potential rate hikes and low volatility levels indicates that the market remains vulnerable to sudden, sharp price corrections.
