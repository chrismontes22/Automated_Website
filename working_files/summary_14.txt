Less of an object, more of a place: Oio’s Mur Mur is a portable speaker that holds an entire ecosystem within
============================================================

Creative studio Oio and design practice Mattering have introduced Mur Mur, a portable speaker that uses generative AI to simulate autonomous digital ecosystems rather than streaming traditional audio.

### Key Points
- Mur Mur features three distinct virtual ecosystems—Pond, Plot, and Block—each populated by unique AI-driven inhabitants like otters, skaters, and vendors.
- The device generates unpredictable, continuous soundscapes based on the simulated interactions, weather, and rhythms of its internal digital world.
- Developed using tools like Houdini and three.js, the project incorporates over 300,000 custom sound effects created in collaboration with musical artist Morgan.
- Users can physically interact with the speaker, such as shaking the device to trigger reactions from the virtual agents living inside.
- While currently a speculative project, Oio is exploring production options due to significant public interest in purchasing or building the device.

### Why it Matters
This project represents a shift in home technology by prioritizing imaginative world-building over the utility-focused AI assistants currently dominating the market. By treating speakers as habitats rather than mere playback devices, Oio and Mattering are exploring new ways for consumers to interact with ambient digital environments.
