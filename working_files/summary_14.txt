Exclusive-Chinese military researchers tap US AI models to train defence systems
============================================================

Chinese military researchers are utilizing outputs from leading U.S. artificial intelligence models, including those from OpenAI and Anthropic, to train domestic systems for advanced defense and surveillance capabilities.

### Key Points
- Researchers are using "model distillation" to transfer reasoning capabilities from U.S. AI models into smaller, locally deployable systems.
- A review of over 80 academic papers identified military-linked institutions, including the People's Liberation Army, utilizing these techniques for cyber warfare and tactical decision-making.
- PLA Unit 96941 reportedly used OpenAI’s GPT-3.5 to process sensitive military source code for use within secure, domestic networks.
- The National University of Defense Technology applied distillation to enable real-time image processing and targeting for unmanned aerial vehicles.
- Researchers at the North University of China utilized Anthropic’s Claude 3 Haiku to generate synthetic training data for social media monitoring tools.

### Why it Matters
This practice allows Chinese military entities to bypass U.S. export controls on high-end chips by leveraging the reasoning power of Western AI to build specialized, local systems. The trend highlights a significant national security challenge regarding intellectual property and the potential for U.S. technology to inadvertently bolster foreign defense capabilities.
