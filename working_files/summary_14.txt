Oil Nears $100 as Markets Assess Fed Rate Expectations and Iran Tensions: Dow Jones, S&P, Nasdaq, Wall Street Futures
============================================================

U.S. stock futures traded lower on Tuesday as investors weighed rising oil prices, climbing bond yields, and geopolitical tensions following the recent August U.S. employment report.

### Key Points
- Dow Jones Futures fell 0.8%, while S&P 500 Futures declined 0.3% as markets returned from the Labor Day holiday.
- Brent crude reached a six-week high near $99 per barrel amid concerns over Middle East shipping routes and potential energy supply disruptions.
- U.S. national debt has surpassed $40 trillion, contributing to selling pressure in global bond markets and rising government borrowing costs.
- Former President Donald Trump called for an end to Bombardier sales in the U.S., a market that accounts for over half of the company's revenue.
- Investors are awaiting the ADP Employment Change report and results from a U.S. Treasury auction of three-year notes to gauge economic health.

### Why it Matters
The combination of rising energy costs and higher bond yields complicates the Federal Reserve's path for future interest rate adjustments. These factors influence broader borrowing costs for consumers and corporations, potentially impacting equity valuations and overall economic growth.
