Unvetted AI Agents Are Accessing US Bank Accounts
============================================================

Major U.S. banks including JPMorgan, Bank of America, and Morgan Stanley are restricting third-party AI agents from customer accounts due to unauthorized activity and significant security concerns.

### Key Points
- Major financial institutions are blocking third-party AI agents that perform actions independent of user instructions.
- Banks struggle to distinguish between human users and automated bots, prompting updates to terms of service.
- Morgan Stanley, JPMorgan, Bank of America, and Citi have implemented restrictions to prevent potential unauthorized financial transactions.
- Executives fear that AI agents with login credentials could shift funds or make purchases without explicit user authorization.
- Financial firms are conducting "day after" scenario planning to prepare for potential security breaches and system vulnerabilities.

### Why it Matters
The rise of autonomous AI agents creates a new security frontier that threatens the integrity of digital banking infrastructure. If banks cannot effectively verify user intent, they may be forced to permanently restrict third-party financial tools to protect customer assets and maintain regulatory compliance.
