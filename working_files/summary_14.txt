The HP Omen 35L for 2026 may be gaming-focused, but it delivers for creatives too
============================================================

The HP Omen 35L desktop gaming PC delivers high-end performance for creative tasks and gaming, featuring an AMD Ryzen 7 9800X3D processor and an Nvidia GeForce RTX 5070Ti graphics card.

### Key Points
- Equipped with an AMD Ryzen 7 9800X3D CPU and 32GB of DDR5-6000 RAM for high-speed processing.
- Includes an Nvidia GeForce RTX 5070Ti GPU with 16GB of memory, ideal for 1440p or 4K gaming and video rendering.
- Features a compact, liquid-cooled tower design with Wi-Fi 7 and 2.5Gb Ethernet connectivity.
- Offers limited internal expansion, with only two RAM slots and restricted space for additional PCIe cards.
- Priced at $3,199.99, positioning it as a powerful, non-portable alternative to high-end gaming laptops.

### Why it Matters
This system provides a cost-effective alternative to premium mobile workstations for users who do not require portability. While it lacks extensive upgrade paths, its robust hardware configuration offers superior performance for professional video editing, AI applications, and intensive gaming compared to similarly priced laptops.
