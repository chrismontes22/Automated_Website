Thinking about a career pivot? A Google engineer shares what he learned from taking a leap of faith.
============================================================

Google security engineer Prerit Pathak shares his professional journey from software development at Dell to a specialized cybersecurity role, offering advice for those looking to enter the field.

### Key Points
- Prerit Pathak transitioned from a software engineering role at Dell Technologies to a full-time security engineer position at Google in 2023.
- He earned a master’s degree from Carnegie Mellon University to formalize his expertise after gaining practical experience through vulnerability disclosures and nonprofit work.
- Pathak’s current responsibilities at Google include running threat simulations to replicate adversary behavior and reporting findings to internal stakeholders.
- He emphasizes that software engineers have a unique advantage in cybersecurity because they understand how to build systems and, consequently, how to exploit them.
- Pathak recommends that aspiring security professionals prioritize networking, communication skills, and "failing forward" through interview practice and industry conferences.

### Why it Matters
The rise of artificial intelligence has significantly increased the demand for skilled cybersecurity professionals to protect complex enterprise environments. Pathak’s career path highlights how technical foundations in software development can be effectively leveraged to address critical security vulnerabilities in the modern tech industry.
