These social media posts promise big online discounts on Lego, Calvin Klein and more — but really they're just phishing scams
============================================================

Security researchers at Group-IB have identified a sophisticated phishing campaign called Milk Dragon that uses fake ecommerce websites and social media advertisements to steal global consumer payment data.

### Key Points
- The Milk Dragon campaign has targeted victims across 66 countries, with the highest concentration of attacks occurring in Malaysia, Singapore, and Thailand.
- Attackers use the BytePress malware to capture keystrokes in real-time, allowing them to intercept credentials and multi-factor authentication codes before a form is even submitted.
- The phishing kit impersonates 21 major brands and 36 financial institutions, often promoting fraudulent discounts on platforms like Facebook and TikTok.
- The Milk Dragon phishing kit is available for purchase on Telegram as a service, with subscription plans starting at 300 USDT per month.
- Victims are often deceived by fake order confirmations, which delay the discovery of the theft and prevent immediate reporting to financial institutions.

### Why it Matters
This campaign highlights a significant evolution in phishing tactics by shifting from email-based lures to social media platforms to bypass traditional security filters. The ability to intercept multi-factor authentication in real-time poses a severe threat to both individual consumers and the security protocols of financial institutions.
