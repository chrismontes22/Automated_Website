iPhone Jailbreak Gets Past Lockdown Mode and Survives an Update
============================================================

The hacker team Calif has successfully demonstrated a persistent jailbreak on an iPhone 18 Pro running Apple’s Lockdown Mode, maintaining access even after an iOS 27.2 system update.

### Key Points
- The jailbreak was initiated by sending a malicious PDF file to the target device via AirDrop.
- The exploit remains active on the iPhone 18 Pro following the installation of the iOS 27.2 software update.
- Lockdown Mode is Apple’s security feature designed to protect high-risk users from sophisticated mercenary spyware by restricting system functionality.
- Calif stated it intends to disclose these vulnerabilities to Apple and does not plan to release a public jailbreak tool.
- Apple previously claimed it had no evidence of successful mercenary spyware attacks against devices operating in Lockdown Mode.

### Why it Matters
This demonstration challenges the perceived security of Apple’s Lockdown Mode, which is marketed as an extreme protection measure for high-risk users. If these vulnerabilities are confirmed, it could force Apple to accelerate security patches to maintain user trust in its most restrictive privacy settings.
