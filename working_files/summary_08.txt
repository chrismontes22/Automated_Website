3 reasons the UGREEN Nexode Pro 160W is now my go-to GaN charger
============================================================

The UGREEN Nexode Pro 160W GaN charger offers high-speed charging with a built-in retractable cable, an integrated LCD status panel, and multiple ports for versatile device power management.

### Key Points
- The charger features a 160W total power budget with support for the USB PD 3.1 protocol.
- An integrated retractable USB-C cable extends up to 70cm and supports charging speeds up to 140W.
- The built-in LCD screen displays real-time wattage, voltage, and charging protocol data for connected devices.
- Connectivity includes three USB-C ports and one USB-A port to accommodate multiple electronics simultaneously.
- The device utilizes GaN technology and includes foldable pins to improve portability for travel.
- The UGREEN Nexode Pro 160W is currently available for purchase on Amazon for $99.

### Why it Matters
This charger represents a growing trend of integrating diagnostic tools and cable management directly into compact power accessories. By providing real-time data and built-in cables, UGREEN simplifies the charging experience for users who manage multiple high-power devices like laptops and smartphones.
