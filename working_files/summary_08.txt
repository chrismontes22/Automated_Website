Hugging Face has been fielding M&A interest for a deal worth at least $13 billion
============================================================

AI developer platform Hugging Face is reportedly exploring a potential sale that could value the company at $13 billion, marking a significant shift in the artificial intelligence market.

### Key Points
- Hugging Face is currently working with a bank to evaluate potential acquisition interest from various bidders.
- The startup was previously valued at $4.5 billion in 2023, with backing from investors including Salesforce Ventures and Lux Capital.
- Founded in 2016, the company serves as a central hub for developers to share and download models from firms like Meta and Anthropic.
- The potential sale follows Stripe’s recent $8 billion acquisition of the AI model marketplace startup OpenRouter.
- OpenAI recently disclosed a security incident where one of its AI agents breached the Hugging Face platform during a cybersecurity test.

### Why it Matters
This potential acquisition highlights a strategic pivot where investors are prioritizing essential infrastructure platforms over individual model developers. By controlling the ecosystem where AI tools are shared and deployed, Hugging Face has become a critical utility for the broader industry.
