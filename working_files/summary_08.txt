The Nimble SHAREPOWER power bank splits a 10,000mAh battery in half so you can share it with others
============================================================

The Nimble SHAREPOWER is a unique 10,000mAh portable charger that magnetically splits into two independent 5,000mAh power banks, allowing multiple users to charge devices simultaneously while remaining mobile.

### Key Points
- The device features two 5,000mAh battery halves that connect via magnetic pins for a total capacity of 10,000mAh.
- Each half includes a built-in USB-C cable and an additional USB-C port, supporting up to 20W PD charging per unit.
- The power bank is priced at $79.95 and is available in black, pink, white, and blue colorways.
- Users can operate the device in "Stack Mode" as a single unit or "Share Mode" to separate the batteries for independent use.

### Why it Matters
This innovative design solves the common inconvenience of tethering multiple users to a single power bank during group travel or social outings. By allowing individuals to separate and carry their own power source, the product offers greater flexibility and independence for users sharing battery resources.
