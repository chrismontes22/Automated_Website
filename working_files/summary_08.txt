Nvidia hits its first record since May, within $300B of $6T
============================================================

Nvidia shares reached a record high, bringing the chipmaker within $300 billion of a historic $6 trillion valuation following a surge in AI agent demand and buybacks.

### Key Points
- Nvidia’s market capitalization reached approximately $5.7 trillion after a 2.9% share price increase on Friday.
- The company authorized a record $150 billion share buyback, bringing total authorized repurchases to $235 billion through fiscal 2028.
- Morgan Stanley analyst Joseph Moore reinstated Nvidia as a top semiconductor pick, citing an undemanding valuation despite a 27% year-to-date gain.
- Nvidia launched the Open Agent Safety Platform to secure AI systems, supported by over 100 companies including SAP.
- The company confirmed a $12.93 billion acquisition of Hugging Face to bolster its AI infrastructure and security offerings.
- Europe’s most valuable company, ASML, remains $300 billion away from reaching a $1 trillion market valuation.

### Why it Matters
Nvidia’s rapid growth highlights the massive market confidence in AI infrastructure and the increasing integration of autonomous AI agents into enterprise workflows. This valuation milestone underscores the company's dominant position in the semiconductor industry and its strategic expansion into AI safety and software platforms.
