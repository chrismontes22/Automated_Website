Honor’s Magic 9 Pro Max has a big camera and a bigger battery
============================================================

Honor has officially launched its Magic 9 flagship smartphone series in China, headlined by the Pro Max model featuring a high-capacity 8,800mAh battery and advanced Arri-engineered camera technology.

### Key Points
- The Magic 9 Pro Max features a 200-megapixel main camera and a 3.5x telephoto lens developed in collaboration with cinema equipment manufacturer Arri.
- Camera capabilities include Arri LogC3 support, 14 preset Arri Look LUTs, and a professional-grade 10-bit 4:2:2 codec for high-quality video production.
- The device is powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 processor and utilizes a high-density battery with 40 percent silicon content.
- A new square-format front-facing camera provides automatic framing adjustments similar to Apple’s Center Stage technology.
- Honor confirmed an international release for the Magic 9 series is planned, though specific global availability dates remain unannounced.

### Why it Matters
This partnership with Arri signals Honor’s strategic shift toward professional-grade mobile cinematography to differentiate its flagship hardware in a competitive market. By prioritizing battery capacity and specialized imaging tools, the company aims to attract power users and content creators who demand high-performance mobile hardware.
