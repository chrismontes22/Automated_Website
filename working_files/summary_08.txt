FBI Seizes 7 Domains, Disrupts Flax Typhoon Tools Used in Critical Infrastructure Intrusions
============================================================

The U.S. Department of Justice and FBI have disrupted a China-linked botnet operated by Integrity Technology Group that compromised hundreds of thousands of critical infrastructure devices globally.

### Key Points
- The FBI seized multiple domains used by the Flax Typhoon threat group to manage a botnet of over 260,000 compromised IoT and SOHO devices.
- Integrity Technology Group, a Beijing-based contractor, allegedly utilized the "Sparrow" application and "Microscan" tool to conduct large-scale vulnerability scanning and network infiltration.
- Forensic data from June 2024 identified over 385,000 unique U.S. victim devices targeted by the group's command-and-control infrastructure.
- The operation targeted diverse sectors, including U.S. power companies, international airports, and universities, using tools like FishHub for spear-phishing and credential harvesting.
- A joint international advisory from seven nations confirmed the group’s use of automated AI tools and brute-force utilities to exploit Microsoft 365 environments.

### Why it Matters
This operation highlights the growing reliance of state-sponsored actors on private, for-profit contractors to scale malicious cyber activities against global critical infrastructure. By dismantling these commercial enablers, authorities aim to increase the operational costs for foreign intelligence services attempting to infiltrate sensitive Western networks.
