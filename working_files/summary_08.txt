BCG is betting that the best AI leaders know how to lead people, too
============================================================

Boston Consulting Group has appointed three veteran leaders to oversee its North American AI and technology divisions, signaling a strategic shift toward prioritizing human-centric AI integration and organizational change.

### Key Points
- Amanda Luther will lead regional AI and functional activation, while Silvio Palumbo takes over BCG X and Natasha Taylor manages technology and digital operations.
- The firm is prioritizing employee upskilling and experimentation to ensure staff can effectively leverage generative AI tools in client-facing projects.
- Consulting firms are increasingly recruiting diverse technical talent, including engineers and technologists, to meet the growing demand for hands-on digital transformation.
- Leadership mandates now emphasize managing organizational change and helping clients achieve measurable outcomes rather than traditional advisory services.
- Data indicates that 72% of CEOs now act as primary decision-makers for AI initiatives, requiring consultants to provide more sophisticated, data-driven strategic support.

### Why it Matters
The consulting industry is undergoing a fundamental transformation as firms move away from time-based billing toward outcome-based models driven by AI implementation. By elevating leaders who prioritize both technical expertise and change management, firms like BCG are attempting to bridge the gap between complex AI deployment and the human organizational shifts required to realize actual ROI.
