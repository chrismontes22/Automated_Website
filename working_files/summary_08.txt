Apple is reportedly working on iPhone game controllers
============================================================

Apple is reportedly developing two new game controllers designed for the iPhone, with the company expected to release the accessories under its subsidiary Beats brand.

### Key Points
- Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman reports that Apple is actively developing two distinct game controller models for iPhone users.
- References to two first-party game controllers were recently discovered within the code for macOS 16.1.
- The devices will likely be branded under Beats to allow for more affordable pricing and broader market appeal.
- Beats executives are leading the development, utilizing the brand to experiment with new product categories outside the premium Apple lineup.

### Why it Matters
This move signals Apple's intent to capture a larger share of the mobile gaming market by offering dedicated hardware that complements its existing ecosystem. By leveraging the Beats brand, Apple can maintain its premium image while effectively competing in the more accessible, lifestyle-oriented gaming accessory space.
