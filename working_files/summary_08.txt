Smart ring battle: Oura and Ultrahuman are in court again over a 'patent infringement'
============================================================

Oura has filed a patent infringement lawsuit against competitor Ultrahuman, alleging that the Ring Pro device utilizes proprietary designs and internal components protected by Oura’s recent intellectual property.

### Key Points
- Oura filed the lawsuit on October 22, 2025, just two days after receiving patent 12,769,363 for internal ring components.
- The legal complaint targets specific Ring Pro hardware, including the curved battery, circuit board, magnets, and integrated sensors.
- Ultrahuman CEO Mohit Kumar stated that the Ring Pro remains available for purchase in the United States despite the ongoing litigation.
- This legal action follows a 2025 dispute where Ultrahuman accused Oura of copying its own health-tracking technology and features.
- Oura is seeking monetary damages, citing a history of alleged patent infringement by Ultrahuman regarding smart ring innovations.

### Why it Matters
This ongoing legal battle highlights the intensifying competition and high stakes within the rapidly growing smart ring market. As companies fight to protect their intellectual property, these lawsuits could influence product availability and the pace of innovation for wearable health technology.
