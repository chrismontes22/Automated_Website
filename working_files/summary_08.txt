These are the stock market's winners and losers in the AI agent economy
============================================================

Meta’s Muse AI app has triggered a significant stock market rally, highlighting a shift toward autonomous agentic systems that could reshape industries, corporate infrastructure, and consumer spending habits.

### Key Points
- Meta’s Muse app became the most-downloaded free tool on the App Store, driving an 11% surge in Meta stock and record highs for the Nasdaq.
- Chipmakers saw major gains, with Intel rising 12%, AMD up 10%, and Qualcomm up 9% as investors pivoted toward CPU suppliers.
- Shopify shares climbed 14% over two days following a partnership allowing Muse AI agents to make purchases via the Shop Pay service.
- Analysts identify hyperscalers, hardware manufacturers, and industrial firms as potential winners, while software-as-a-service companies and online marketplaces face disruption.
- Sectors reliant on entry-level labor, such as finance and customer service, may see cost-cutting through AI-driven headcount reductions.

### Why it Matters
The rise of autonomous AI agents signals a transition from simple generative tools to systems capable of executing complex tasks like shopping and administrative work. This shift forces investors to re-evaluate the long-term viability of traditional software models and transactional middlemen that may be bypassed by direct AI-to-vendor interactions.
