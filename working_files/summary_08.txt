The UGREEN DXP4800 Pro is a high-performance 4-bay network-attached storage device that stands out as a top-tier media server for Plex users following extensive long-term reliability testing.

### Key Points
- The DXP4800 Pro features an Intel Core i3-1315U processor, 8GB of RAM, and 10 Gigabit Ethernet connectivity.
- It supports hardware-assisted transcoding, enabling the simultaneous streaming of multiple high-bitrate 4K video files.
- The device includes a 128GB built-in SSD and two dedicated NVMe M.2 slots for enhanced storage performance.
- Testing involved over 2,500 hours of operation and the successful transfer of 54TB of data using various media and utility services.
- The unit is currently available for purchase at a price point of $719.

### Why it Matters
This NAS provides a robust, enthusiast-grade solution for users looking to centralize media libraries and smart home services on a reliable platform. Its combination of powerful hardware and flexible software support makes it a competitive choice for those requiring high-speed data management and consistent 4K streaming capabilities.
