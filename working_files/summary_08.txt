Russia-Aligned UAC-0099 Evolves MATCHBOIL Malware
============================================================

The Russia-aligned cyber espionage group UAC-0099 has significantly upgraded its MATCHBOIL downloader with advanced obfuscation and sandbox detection techniques to target critical infrastructure across Ukraine.

### Key Points
- ESET researchers documented the evolution of the MATCHBOIL C# downloader, which has been under active development by UAC-0099 since at least April 2024.
- The malware now utilizes the Eziriz .NET Reactor obfuscator to implement code virtualization and control-flow obfuscation, complicating security analysis.
- New versions include sandbox checks to detect automated inspection environments and a timer-based execution model for retrieving secondary payloads from command-and-control servers.
- UAC-0099 has targeted Ukrainian transportation, manufacturing, and energy sectors, with malicious activity observed as recently as June 2026.
- The group frequently updates persistence mechanisms, alternating between Windows Registry Run keys and scheduled tasks to maintain access to compromised systems.

### Why it Matters
This sustained development indicates that UAC-0099 views MATCHBOIL as a core, long-term component of its espionage toolkit rather than a disposable tool. The group's focus on evading detection suggests a heightened threat to critical infrastructure providers who must now contend with increasingly sophisticated and adaptive malware.
