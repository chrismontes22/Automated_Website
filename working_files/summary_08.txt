America, You Can’t Get This Powerhouse Phone. It’s a Shame
============================================================

Oppo has launched its flagship Find X10 and Find X10 Pro smartphones in Europe, signaling a strategic push to challenge the Apple and Samsung market duopoly globally.

### Key Points
- Oppo unveiled the photography-focused Find X10 and Find X10 Pro during a global launch event held in Mallorca, Spain.
- Market research firms Counterpoint and Omdia rank Oppo as the world's fourth-largest smartphone manufacturer by shipment volume.
- The company plans to increase its investment in the European market by 30% over the next three years to capture premium segment growth.
- Chinese manufacturers like Oppo, Xiaomi, and Honor are currently avoiding the US market due to geopolitical barriers and a lack of established carrier partnerships.
- Data shows that Oppo’s premium market share in Europe quadrupled between 2025 and 2026, driven largely by younger consumers prioritizing hardware specs over brand loyalty.

### Why it Matters
The expansion of Chinese smartphone brands into Europe offers consumers more high-end alternatives to the dominant Apple and Samsung devices. However, US buyers remain excluded from these competitive options, highlighting how geopolitical and logistical barriers continue to restrict global consumer choice in the premium mobile market.
