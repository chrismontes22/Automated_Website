Kushner book author reveals bombshell detail he left out about hacked phone
============================================================

Investigative journalist Matthew Cole reports that Dutch intelligence allegedly hacked Jared Kushner’s phone, revealing potential access to secret Gulf-funded bank accounts during his tenure in the White House.

### Key Points
- Investigative journalist Matthew Cole alleges in his book "Princes and Thieves" that Dutch military intelligence accessed Jared Kushner’s iPhone.
- The report claims Dutch intelligence discovered screenshots of Kushner’s phone on a Russian server and subsequently monitored his activity.
- Cole states that other NATO allies were reportedly aware of the Dutch intelligence operation involving Kushner’s device.
- The book alleges Kushner accessed a Dubai-based account containing hundreds of millions of dollars while serving as a senior White House official.
- Jared Kushner and his legal representatives have issued an emphatic denial regarding all allegations presented in the book.

### Why it Matters
These allegations raise significant questions regarding national security protocols and the potential vulnerability of high-ranking U.S. officials to foreign intelligence surveillance. If verified, the report suggests a major breach of diplomatic trust between the United States and its NATO allies.
