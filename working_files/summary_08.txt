Tesla sunsets its Solar Roof tiles
============================================================

Tesla has officially discontinued its Solar Roof product line, shifting its energy strategy to focus exclusively on conventional solar panels following years of production challenges and low adoption.

### Key Points
- Tesla informed its third-party installer network that Solar Roof tiles are no longer available for new orders.
- The company’s dedicated Solar Roof webpage now redirects users to its standard solar panel product page.
- Launched in 2016, the product reached mass production in 2020 but struggled with significant delays and over-expenditure.
- A 2023 Wood Mackenzie report estimated that only 3,000 U.S. homes had the solar shingles installed since their debut.
- Tesla is pivoting its energy division toward traditional solar panels and plans to build a new $10 billion factory near Houston, Texas.

### Why it Matters
The discontinuation marks the end of a high-profile attempt to integrate renewable energy technology directly into residential building materials. By abandoning the niche product, Tesla is streamlining its energy business to prioritize more scalable and financially viable solar solutions.
