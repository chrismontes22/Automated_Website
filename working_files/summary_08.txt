MacBook Pro's Switch to OLED Could Spell Trouble for Mini-LED Industry
============================================================

Apple is expected to transition its MacBook Pro lineup to OLED display technology, a shift projected to cause a 75% decline in mini-LED laptop panel shipments by 2027.

### Key Points
- Counterpoint Research forecasts mini-LED notebook panel shipments will drop 38% in 2026 and 60% in 2027 as OLED adoption accelerates.
- Notebook OLED panel shipments are projected to grow 86% by 2025, driven by demand for premium AI PCs and Apple's new hardware.
- Samsung Display will reportedly serve as the exclusive supplier for the new 14-inch and 16-inch OLED touch panels produced in Asan, South Korea.
- Apple plans to unveil the new MacBook Pro models on October 28, featuring a lighter design and the replacement of the display notch with Dynamic Island.
- The new OLED laptops may launch with starting prices up to $500 higher than current mini-LED models, which Apple intends to phase out over time.

### Why it Matters
This transition signals a major shift in display manufacturing standards as OLED becomes the preferred choice for premium laptops due to its superior image quality and thinner profile. The move could influence broader industry pricing and force competitors to accelerate their own adoption of OLED technology to remain relevant in the high-end PC market.
