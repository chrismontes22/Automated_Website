Samsung unveils an extremely small 256TB SSD for AI data centers
============================================================

Samsung Electronics unveiled a new 256TB enterprise SSD and high-performance PCIe 6.0 storage solutions during the Global Memory Innovation Forum 2026 held in Shenzhen, China.

### Key Points
- The BM1773 QLC SSD offers 256TB capacity in an E3.S form factor using 9th-generation NAND flash and PCIe 5.0 architecture.
- Samsung’s PM1763 PCIe 6.0 enterprise SSD achieves read speeds of 28.4GB/s and features improved power efficiency for liquid-cooled servers.
- The company introduced V10 NAND, the industry's first flash memory featuring over 400 stacked layers for increased density and faster transfer speeds.
- New zNAND-O technology provides a high-speed bridge between DRAM and flash, enabling local AI model execution on smartphones and laptops.
- Enterprise drives include advanced security features such as Post-Quantum Cryptography, CNSA 2.0 support, and intelligent die-failure recovery mechanisms.

### Why it Matters
These advancements significantly increase data density and processing speeds for data centers managing the growing demands of enterprise AI workloads. By improving power efficiency and local AI capabilities, Samsung is positioning its hardware to lower operational costs while enhancing performance for next-generation computing infrastructure.
