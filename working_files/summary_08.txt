7 Best Virtualization Platforms for Hybrid Cloud Environments
============================================================

Enterprises are reevaluating their virtualization strategies following Broadcom’s VMware acquisition, with seven key platforms emerging as top contenders for managing modern hybrid cloud and on-premises infrastructure workloads.

### Key Points
- Sangfor aSV offers a KVM-based alternative that bundles networking, storage, and security to compete with VMware’s modular pricing.
- VMware remains the industry leader for ecosystem depth, though recent licensing changes have prompted many organizations to model total costs more aggressively.
- Nutanix provides a highly-rated, integrated hyper-converged infrastructure (HCI) platform that includes the AHV hypervisor at no additional license cost.
- Proxmox serves as a cost-effective, open-source option for teams comfortable with manual configuration and self-managed infrastructure.
- Red Hat OpenShift Virtualization is optimized for organizations prioritizing container modernization and Kubernetes-native workflows.
- Microsoft Hyper-V and Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI) offer specialized advantages for teams already heavily invested in Windows or Oracle-specific application stacks.

### Why it Matters
Selecting the right virtualization platform is a critical architectural decision that dictates long-term operational costs and technical flexibility. Choosing a platform that aligns with existing infrastructure and future modernization goals prevents costly rework and ensures seamless workload portability across hybrid environments.
