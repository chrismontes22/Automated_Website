Raspberry Pi Stock Jumps 30% as Demand Surges. (And Boards Now Locked to Their Original RAM Size)
============================================================

Raspberry Pi Holdings shares surged over 30% following a 90% revenue increase to $256.9 million, driven by strong OEM adoption and robust demand in the smart home and aerospace sectors.

### Key Points
- Revenue reached $256.9 million for the six months ending June 30, with profit before tax rising 216% to $19.6 million.
- Total unit shipments climbed 17% to 4.2 million, while the customer order backlog doubled to 2.6 million units.
- The company is expanding production capacity at its Pencoed, Wales facility to address supply chain constraints and rising global demand.
- Raspberry Pi introduced the AI HAT+ 2 for the Raspberry Pi 5 to expand its edge-AI product offerings.
- New bootloader updates now restrict RAM upgrades on Pi 4 and Pi 5 models to prevent unauthorized hardware modifications by third-party resellers.

### Why it Matters
The company's financial performance highlights a successful shift toward industrial and OEM markets, where supply chain reliability has become a critical competitive advantage. While recent bootloader restrictions have sparked debate among hobbyists, the move aims to protect the brand's integrity and ensure revenue is reinvested into future research and development.
