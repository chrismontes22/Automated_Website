Samsung’s Z Fold 8 is hundreds off ahead of iPhone Duo launch
============================================================

Samsung is offering significant discounts on the Galaxy Z Fold 8 and Z Fold 8 Ultra during October Prime Day ahead of the upcoming iPhone Duo release.

### Key Points
- The standard Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 with 256GB of storage is discounted by $350, bringing the price down to $1,549.99.
- The premium Z Fold 8 Ultra is available for $1,699.99, marking a $400 reduction from its original $2,099.99 retail price.
- These promotional prices represent the lowest recorded costs for both foldable smartphone models to date.
- The Z Fold 8 features a 4:3 aspect ratio inner screen, while the Ultra model offers upgraded camera systems, a faster chipset, and a reduced display crease.

### Why it Matters
These aggressive price cuts position Samsung to compete directly with the upcoming iPhone Duo launch by offering substantial savings on high-end foldable hardware. Consumers looking for premium mobile technology can now access flagship devices at record-low price points, potentially influencing market share during the competitive holiday shopping season.
