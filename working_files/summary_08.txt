ServiceNow AI Control Tower: Selling a penthouse while building the frame
============================================================

ServiceNow’s AI Control Tower aims to govern enterprise artificial intelligence by leveraging the company’s existing workflow and operational data to manage AI assets, accountability, and remediation processes.

### Key Points
- ServiceNow reports over 500 customers are using AI Control Tower, contributing to an AI annual contract value exceeding $1 billion as of Q2.
- A field study of 15 large enterprises found that while ServiceNow excels at governance workflows, 14 respondents rely on external tools for runtime enforcement.
- The platform’s primary strength lies in its ability to connect AI risks to existing CMDB assets, business owners, and ITSM remediation processes.
- Customers frequently encounter technical gaps regarding model-specific evidence, such as lineage, bias, and performance history, which are often generated by third-party tools.
- Implementation of the platform often involves significant friction, with seven of eight surveyed customers reporting costs exceeding their initial expectations.

### Why it Matters
ServiceNow is positioning itself as the central "system of action" for AI governance, but its ability to serve as a universal technical control plane remains unproven at scale. Organizations should treat the platform as a powerful orchestration layer while maintaining independent security and validation tools to ensure comprehensive enterprise coverage.
