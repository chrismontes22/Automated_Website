Ariana Grande is suing the hackers who’ve been leaking her songs and videos for years
============================================================

Ariana Grande has filed a lawsuit in Los Angeles Superior Court to identify and stop anonymous hackers who have stolen and leaked hundreds of her unreleased songs and videos.

### Key Points
- The lawsuit targets "John Doe" defendants accused of stealing unreleased masters, demos, and behind-the-scenes footage since 2011.
- Legal filings state that 45 unreleased songs were stolen and leaked during 2023 alone.
- Hackers allegedly gained access by targeting the mobile devices, cloud storage, and email accounts of Grande’s producers and photographers.
- The stolen content is reportedly being sold and distributed across social media platforms including X, TikTok, and YouTube.
- The complaint cites violations of California’s Comprehensive Data Access and Fraud Act regarding unauthorized computer access.

### Why it Matters
This legal action highlights the persistent security vulnerabilities faced by high-profile artists when their creative collaborators are targeted by cybercriminals. By pursuing these unidentified hackers, Grande is setting a legal precedent for how entertainers can utilize data protection laws to combat the unauthorized distribution of intellectual property.
