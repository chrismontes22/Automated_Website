SpaceX has more neocloud revenue
============================================================

SpaceX reported $2.6 billion in AI revenue this quarter, surpassing its space division earnings as the company pivots toward providing large-scale compute capacity for major industry partners.

### Key Points
- SpaceX’s AI division revenue tripled year-over-year, though the segment recorded a $1.5 billion loss for the quarter.
- The company secured major compute-provisioning deals with Anthropic in May and Google in June.
- Capital expenditures reached $18.37 billion as the firm scales its data center infrastructure and AI model development.
- Starlink remains the company's only profitable segment, generating $4.2 billion in quarterly revenue.
- SpaceX is nearing the acquisition of Cursor to bolster its enterprise AI product offerings.
- Development costs for the Starship rocket increased by $389 million, driven by the need to launch heavier satellites for connectivity.

### Why it Matters
This shift highlights SpaceX’s strategic transition from a traditional aerospace firm into a major player in the competitive cloud computing and AI infrastructure market. While the company’s massive capital spending on AI and Starship development continues to weigh on its bottom line, its ability to monetize compute capacity is becoming a central pillar of its valuation.
