Why Software Quality Is Now a Founder-Level Problem, Not Just an Engineering One
============================================================

The rapid rise of AI-generated code has created a critical assurance gap, forcing founders to prioritize software quality to prevent security breaches, compliance failures, and significant reputational damage.

### Key Points
- A December 2025 analysis found AI-co-authored code contains 1.7 times more issues and 2.74 times more security vulnerabilities than human-written code.
- Approximately 25% of Y Combinator’s Winter 2025 startups utilized codebases that were 95% AI-generated, highlighting the industry's shift toward automated development.
- Security flaws in AI-generated code have led to major incidents, including the exposure of 1.5 million API tokens and 35,000 email addresses at the social network Moltbook.
- The platform Lovable experienced critical security vulnerabilities in over 10% of its sampled live applications due to AI-generated code skipping basic security configurations.
- Experts recommend treating AI output as an unverified draft and integrating continuous quality checks directly into the development pipeline rather than at the end.

### Why it Matters
Software quality has evolved from a technical concern into a fundamental business risk that directly impacts investor confidence and regulatory compliance. Founders must now treat verification as a leadership priority to ensure that rapid development cycles do not compromise the long-term viability of their companies.
