Eric Trump Says Our Mission Hasn't Changed as American Bitcoin Marks One Year of Going Public, but ABTC Stock Got Battered
============================================================

American Bitcoin Corp. marks its one-year anniversary on the Nasdaq as Eric Trump reaffirms the company's mission despite a 90% decline in stock value since its public debut.

### Key Points
- American Bitcoin Corp. currently holds 8,300 BTC, valued at approximately $670 million, making it the 15th-largest corporate holder of the cryptocurrency.
- The company operates a fleet of nearly 90,000 mining machines and reports achieving gross mining profit margins of approximately 50%.
- Despite record quarterly production, the company's stock has plummeted 90% since its initial public offering and remains down 63% year-to-date.
- Eric Trump dismissed reports of $500 million in lost shareholder value, attributing the criticism to political bias against the firm.

### Why it Matters
The significant disparity between the company's operational growth and its stock market performance highlights the volatility inherent in publicly traded Bitcoin mining firms. Investors remain divided on whether the company's aggressive accumulation strategy can overcome the high capital costs and market pressures that have eroded shareholder value.
