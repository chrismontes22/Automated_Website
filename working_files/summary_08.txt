Canva Chases AI Domination With a New AI Image Model for Designers
============================================================

Canva has launched its first proprietary AI image model, Canva Craft, designed to help users generate complex, layout-focused designs by mimicking the professional creative process of human designers.

### Key Points
- Canva Craft is now available globally to all users following the company's acquisition of Leonardo.Ai and internal research efforts.
- The model utilizes a two-stage architecture that allows it to "reason" through layout and element placement before generating final images.
- Training data for the model includes Canva’s internal library, licensed databases, and content from users who opted into the program.
- Early benchmarking on DesignArena ranks Canva Craft fourth overall, outperforming models from competitors like Google and Microsoft.
- The tool integrates with Magic Layers, allowing users to break down and edit individual elements within AI-generated designs.

### Why it Matters
By bringing AI development in-house, Canva aims to reduce cloud costs and improve processing speeds while offering designers more granular control over generated content. This move intensifies the ongoing industry debate regarding the role of generative AI in creative fields and the potential displacement of human artists.
