‘Killmonger Locs’ Are Everywhere in Video Games. This Artist Is Sick of It
============================================================

Technical artist Danielle Udogaranya has developed a new Blender plug-in designed to automate the complex process of creating realistic Black hairstyles for 3D video game characters.

### Key Points
- Danielle Udogaranya, known as Ebonix, created the Loc & Kinky Strand Generator to reduce the time required to design textured hair from weeks to under an hour.
- The tool utilizes coding techniques from the 2024 paper "Curly-Cue: Geometric Methods for Highly Coiled Hair" to improve the movement and appearance of digital coils and braids.
- While the tool uses a trained local AI to assist with automation, it requires manual design input and does not rely on generative AI prompts.
- The software is currently in beta testing and is patent-pending, with interest from developers of major titles like Paralives, Roblox, and Second Life.
- Historically, video games have struggled with Black hair representation due to rendering software and code primarily optimized for straight hair textures.

### Why it Matters
This innovation addresses a long-standing technical barrier that has historically limited diversity and self-expression in digital character customization. By streamlining the creation process, the tool could encourage major game studios to move beyond the "default" hair options that have dominated the industry for decades.
