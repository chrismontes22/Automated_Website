Google Home Stuns by Announcing AI Support for Claude and OpenClaw
============================================================

Google is expanding its smart home ecosystem by allowing third-party AI agents, including Anthropic’s Claude, to control devices through the Model Context Protocol technology for premium subscribers.

### Key Points
- Google Home now supports third-party AI agents like Claude, OpenClaw, and Hermes via the Model Context Protocol.
- The integration allows external AIs to manage smart devices, perform complex tasks, and generate security camera summaries.
- Early access is currently limited to Google Home Premium Advanced subscribers in the United States.
- Users can create custom dashboards and advanced automations that may exceed the capabilities of the native Gemini for Home assistant.
- Google warns that integrating third-party agents introduces potential security risks, such as prompt injection vulnerabilities.

### Why it Matters
This update marks the first time a major smart home platform has opened its ecosystem to third-party AI, signaling a shift toward more customizable, interoperable home automation. While it offers users greater flexibility, it also forces consumers to weigh the benefits of advanced AI control against potential security and privacy vulnerabilities.
