In a First, Apple Maps Navigation To Be Embedded In Ford UEV Pickups
============================================================

Ford will become the first automaker to integrate Apple Maps directly into its vehicles, launching the technology in a new $30,000 electric pickup truck arriving in 2027.

### Key Points
- Ford’s upcoming midsize electric pickup will be the first vehicle built on the company's new Universal Electric Vehicle platform.
- The integration utilizes Apple’s MapKit for Automotive SDK to provide real-time traffic, natural language directions, and specialized EV routing.
- Ford’s Latitude AI team will leverage Apple’s road-level mapping data to enhance the next generation of the BlueCruise hands-free driving system.
- The new EV platform will support battery thermal management, allowing the vehicle to pre-condition for driving or fast-charging based on route data.
- Ford plans to expand this technology to additional electric vehicles, including a small SUV, following the initial 2027 pickup launch.

### Why it Matters
This partnership marks a significant shift in automotive software by embedding third-party navigation directly into the vehicle's core operating architecture rather than relying on smartphone mirroring. By combining Apple’s mapping data with Ford’s BlueCruise technology, the automaker aims to improve both driver convenience and the safety of its hands-free driving features.
