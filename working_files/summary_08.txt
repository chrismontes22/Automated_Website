Apple's Home Hub Will Have a Widget Gallery With iPhone Widget Support
============================================================

Apple is developing a new smart home hub featuring a seven-inch display and Siri integration, with a potential product launch expected as early as this year.

### Key Points
- The device, codenamed J490, will feature a customizable home screen using Apple’s Widget Gallery framework.
- Users can mirror widgets from linked iPhones, supporting both native Apple apps and third-party applications.
- A Smart Stack feature will automatically rotate relevant information throughout the day, similar to the Apple Watch interface.
- Privacy protections ensure widget content remains hidden unless the primary linked device is detected within the home.
- The hardware is designed with a seven-inch square display that can be wall-mounted or placed on a base.
- Integration with HomeKit will allow the hub to function as a central controller for smart home scenes and automations.

### Why it Matters
This device represents Apple's strategic push to deepen its presence in the competitive smart home market by centralizing control through a dedicated hardware interface. By leveraging existing iOS widget technology, the company aims to create a seamless ecosystem experience that encourages users to integrate more Apple products into their living spaces.
