Google Messages RCS is breaking for Galaxy users after using Smart Switch
============================================================

Samsung Galaxy users are experiencing persistent RCS messaging errors after using the Smart Switch tool to transfer data to new devices, prompting an official investigation by Google.

### Key Points
- Users report receiving error code 3100 when attempting to enable RCS features within the Google Messages app on new Samsung smartphones.
- Google identified that corrupted cache files transferred during the Samsung Smart Switch process are likely causing the connectivity failures.
- Recommended troubleshooting steps include updating Google Messages and clearing Carrier Services data, though these methods have not resolved the issue for all users.
- Some affected customers have resorted to factory resetting their devices to restore messaging functionality, while others report issues linked to incomplete carrier number-porting.
- Google has confirmed it is actively working with carrier partners to develop a permanent resolution for the widespread messaging disruption.

### Why it Matters
This issue highlights the technical challenges of migrating messaging data as Samsung mandates a transition to Google Messages for all Galaxy users. Reliable RCS functionality is critical for modern Android communication, and these persistent bugs may frustrate users during the standard device upgrade process.
