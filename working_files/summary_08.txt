Huawei copies Samsung’s privacy display in its latest trifold
============================================================

Huawei has launched the Mate XT 2 in China, featuring an inward-folding design and an optional privacy-enhancing OLED display that mirrors technology recently introduced by competitor Samsung.

### Key Points
- The Mate XT 2 features a 10.2-inch flexible screen that folds inward to protect the display, departing from the previous accordion-style design.
- Huawei introduced an optional privacy OLED display that narrows viewing angles, available for an additional ¥1,000 on high-end 16GB RAM and 1TB storage models.
- The device is powered by the new Kirin 9050 Pro chipset and includes an IP59 water-resistance rating.
- The smartphone launched in China with a starting price of ¥19,999, approximately $3,000, with no confirmed international release date.

### Why it Matters
This release signals Huawei's aggressive push into the premium foldable market by adopting design and security features previously pioneered by Samsung. The move highlights a growing industry trend toward integrating advanced privacy hardware into high-end mobile devices to differentiate flagship products.
