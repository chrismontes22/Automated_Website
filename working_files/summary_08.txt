Singapore Airlines is Latest Global Carrier to Be Caught Up in Massive Amadeus Data Breach
============================================================

A massive data breach at travel technology provider Amadeus has exposed the booking information of passengers across six major global airlines, including Singapore Airlines and Japan Airlines.

### Key Points
- The breach affected approximately 46,000 Singapore Airlines bookings, alongside records from Cathay Pacific, Japan Airlines, ANA All Nippon Airways, Korean Air, and Condor.
- Compromised data includes passenger names, booking reference numbers, travel itineraries, and booking classes, though credit card information remains secure.
- The unauthorized access occurred within the Amadeus Altéa platform, which supports booking and operations for roughly 130 airlines worldwide.
- Security experts identified that the breach persisted for over three years, spanning from August 2023 until its discovery in September 2026.
- Hackers reportedly gained access through social engineering tactics, tricking IT support staff into providing legitimate login credentials.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the significant security risks posed by third-party vendors that manage critical infrastructure for the global aviation industry. Passengers should remain vigilant against sophisticated phishing attempts that leverage stolen booking details to distribute malware or harvest additional personal information.
