Muse Creates Detailed Profiles of All Your Friends and Family
============================================================

Meta’s new AI assistant, Muse, is facing scrutiny after researchers extracted internal instructions revealing the tool actively catalogs detailed personal relationship data to provide proactive user suggestions.

### Key Points
- Security researcher Karan Joshi extracted Muse’s internal system prompts by requesting the AI to share its own operating files.
- The instructions reveal Muse creates dedicated profiles for people in a user's life, tracking milestones, shared history, and relationship dynamics.
- Meta states the AI uses this data to offer personalized assistance, such as remembering birthdays or suggesting ways to strengthen specific social connections.
- Muse operates within individual virtual machines, allowing users to wipe stored memories or disconnect external services like bank accounts and email.
- Privacy experts warn that the tool’s design encourages users to share vast amounts of sensitive personal information, significantly expanding corporate data collection.

### Why it Matters
The development of Muse highlights a growing trend where AI assistants are designed to act as comprehensive personal repositories rather than simple task-oriented tools. This shift raises significant privacy concerns regarding how much intimate social and behavioral data users are willing to entrust to large technology companies.
