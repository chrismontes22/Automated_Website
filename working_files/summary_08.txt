iFixit’s look inside the iPhone Duo may explain those broken screens and hinges
============================================================

iFixit’s teardown of the new iPhone Duo reveals a complex hinge design and specialized display technology, earning the foldable device a 5/10 repairability score from the firm.

### Key Points
- The iPhone Duo received a 5/10 repairability score, marking the highest rating iFixit has awarded to a foldable smartphone to date.
- Apple utilizes a matte nano-texture screen cover and angled oval-shaped subpixels to minimize the visibility of the display's central crease.
- The device showed improved dust resistance in the hinge and ports compared to the Samsung Z Fold8 and Google Pixel 11 Pro Fold.
- Analysts suspect that a complex hinge mechanism and an internal accordion cable may be concentrating pressure, potentially causing reported screen and hinge cracks.
- While the outer nano-texture layer is replaceable, repairing the underlying OLED display remains a difficult and costly process for users.

### Why it Matters
This teardown highlights the ongoing engineering challenges manufacturers face when balancing foldable screen durability with internal component accessibility. As foldables gain market share, these repairability insights provide consumers and technicians with a clearer understanding of the long-term maintenance costs associated with complex hinge and display technologies.
