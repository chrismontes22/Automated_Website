Nvidia Finds Itself at the Center of an AI Spy Saga
============================================================

US intelligence officials identified a Singapore-based data center executive as a potential Chinese operative who allegedly maintained a close relationship with Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang to gather information.

### Key Points
- Alice Huang, an executive at the Singapore-based firm Megaspeed, is under electronic surveillance for allegedly funneling information to the Chinese government.
- Megaspeed operates data centers that utilize advanced Nvidia chips to provide computing power for Chinese AI labs, including Alibaba’s Qwen AI division.
- US investigators reportedly kept the surveillance operation secret from Jensen Huang to avoid compromising the ongoing probe into the executive’s activities.
- Nvidia stated that its interactions with Megaspeed are standard customer relations and that the company does not share sensitive trade secrets with regional cloud providers.
- The investigation follows recent espionage concerns, including the FBI's arrest of an individual for planting a recording device at OpenAI’s headquarters.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the intense geopolitical struggle to control the supply chain of advanced AI hardware, which has become a critical asset for national security. As AI infrastructure becomes a central pillar of the global economy, the vulnerability of industry leaders to foreign espionage poses significant risks to both corporate intellectual property and international trade policy.
