Apple Planning to Unveil Privacy-Focused Smart Glasses at WWDC 2027
============================================================

Apple plans to debut its first pair of smart glasses at WWDC in June 2027, focusing on advanced privacy features to differentiate the hardware from existing competitor products.

### Key Points
- Apple is targeting a late 2027 shipping date for its inaugural smart glasses following a planned unveiling at WWDC.
- The device will likely feature integrated speakers and microphones to support Siri, phone calls, and media playback.
- Prototyping efforts include versions with and without cameras to address consumer concerns regarding secret recording.
- Marketing strategies will emphasize privacy protections to distinguish the product from Meta’s Ray-Ban smart glasses.
- Development teams are currently testing unique hardware and software privacy features not found in Apple's current product lineup.

### Why it Matters
This launch represents Apple’s strategic entry into the wearable smart glasses market, positioning privacy as a primary competitive advantage against established rivals. By addressing surveillance concerns, the company aims to overcome consumer hesitation and set a new standard for data security in personal augmented reality hardware.
