My Friend Aaron
============================================================

A man named Aaron achieves viral fame by launching a prediction market platform where users bet on his daily life, ultimately leading to a tragic and violent conclusion.

### Key Points
- Aaron, a programmer with a history of gambling, developed an AI-driven exchange called The Aaron Show.
- The platform utilized thousands of autonomous AI bots to create and resolve betting markets based on Aaron's personal activities.
- A viral post on the Daily Tech message board attracted over 700,000 users, turning the site into a lucrative, real-money gambling operation.
- During a 10K race, Aaron suffered heatstroke and psychosis while attempting to manipulate betting outcomes regarding his athletic performance.
- An unidentified assailant fatally shot Aaron with an air pistol, fulfilling a high-stakes bet placed on his death during the event.

### Why it Matters
This story illustrates the dangerous intersection of unregulated prediction markets, artificial intelligence, and the gamification of human life. It highlights how digital platforms can incentivize extreme behavior and create real-world consequences when financial speculation is detached from ethical boundaries.
