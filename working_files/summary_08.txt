Trump and House Speaker Mike Johnson will reportedly meet tech CEOs on AI on 29 September
============================================================

President Donald Trump and House Speaker Mike Johnson are reportedly planning a meeting with technology executives on September 29 to discuss the future of artificial intelligence development.

### Key Points
- Reports from Axios, Reuters, and ABC News indicate the meeting will address AI safety and industry responsibility.
- House Speaker Mike Johnson has advocated for a summit between government leaders and major AI laboratory heads for several weeks.
- President Trump recently proposed establishing an "AI Force" and appointing an AI czar to oversee national policy.
- The president has publicly dismissed calls from companies like OpenAI and Anthropic to slow development, labeling such warnings as a hoax.
- No official agenda or list of participating technology companies has been released by the White House or congressional offices.

### Why it Matters
This meeting highlights the growing tension between federal policymakers and private tech firms regarding the regulation and safety of emerging artificial intelligence. The outcome could signal a shift in U.S. policy as the government balances the need for technological innovation against increasing pressure to implement formal safety safeguards.
