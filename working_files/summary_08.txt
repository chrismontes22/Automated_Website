Revolut Customers Targeted with New Wave of Phishing Attacks
============================================================

Hackers are targeting Revolut customers with sophisticated smishing attacks following a recent data breach, using fraudulent text messages to harvest login credentials and sensitive personal identity information.

### Key Points
- Security firm Malwarebytes identified phishing messages appearing within legitimate Revolut text threads, urging users to click links to verify their identities.
- Attackers utilized fake video-based identity checks to deceive victims into providing passwords and biometric data, such as selfies or video recordings.
- The initial data breach originated from compromised Italian Ministry of the Interior email accounts used to submit fraudulent information requests to Revolut.
- Reports indicate that several hundred accounts were impacted, with attackers specifically targeting high-net-worth cryptocurrency users identified through blockchain analysis.
- Revolut customers are advised to avoid clicking links in unsolicited messages and to verify account status directly through the official banking application.

### Why it Matters
These attacks demonstrate how threat actors leverage legitimate data breaches to conduct highly convincing social engineering campaigns that bypass standard user skepticism. The incident highlights significant risks for financial institutions when attackers successfully impersonate law enforcement to gain unauthorized access to sensitive customer records.
