Google Pixel 12 Pro Fold: Everything we know so far and want to see
============================================================

Google is expected to launch the Pixel 12 Pro Fold in August 2027, with analysts calling for significant hardware improvements to better compete against rival foldable smartphone manufacturers.

### Key Points
- The Pixel 11 Pro Fold currently retails for $1,899, a price point likely to persist for the 2027 model despite potential global memory shortages.
- Critics are urging Google to reduce the device weight to under 220g, as the current 239g design remains heavier than most competing foldable handsets.
- The 2027 model faces pressure to upgrade its 4,800mAh battery, which is currently smaller than the 5,000mAh standard found in rival devices.
- Industry experts are calling for improved auxiliary camera lenses and the inclusion of advanced features like 120x zoom to match high-end market competitors.
- Google is expected to maintain its signature rounded aesthetic and IP68 dust and water resistance ratings for the upcoming release.

### Why it Matters
The foldable smartphone market is becoming increasingly competitive, with brands like Samsung and others offering thinner designs and superior battery performance. If Google fails to address these hardware gaps, it risks losing market share to rivals that are currently outpacing the Pixel series in technical specifications.
