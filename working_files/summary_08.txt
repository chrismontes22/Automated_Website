Huawei says AI will burn 100,000 times more tokens by 2035
============================================================

Huawei’s latest Intelligent World 2035 report forecasts a 100,000-fold increase in global token consumption, signaling a strategic shift toward an agent-centric digital economy driven by autonomous AI.

### Key Points
- Huawei projects global annual token consumption will grow 100,000-fold by 2035, with autonomous agents generating over 90% of that traffic.
- The company and Tsinghua University estimate $27 trillion in cumulative AI economic value over the next five years.
- Huawei’s roadmap prioritizes scaling computing clusters 100-fold and reducing the cost of agent tasks by 1,000-fold.
- The report introduces ten strategic directions, including the Tau Scaling Law and advancements in embodied and scientific intelligence.
- Huawei plans to launch its AI cluster service internationally on November 30, followed by an enterprise agent platform on December 30.

### Why it Matters
These reports serve as a detailed product roadmap for Huawei, highlighting how the company intends to address hardware constraints and infrastructure gaps through its own technology. By framing the future around agentic AI, Huawei is positioning its networking, computing, and storage hardware as the essential foundation for the global digital economy.
