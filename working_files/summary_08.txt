I used the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra, and it does just enough to be called an upgrade
============================================================

The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra offers internal hardware upgrades like a larger battery and improved hinge, but its high $2,099 price tag and design flaws limit appeal.

### Key Points
- The device features a 5,000mAh silicon-carbon battery and a new hinge that allows for a fully flat design.
- Internal specifications include the latest Qualcomm processor, 12GB of RAM, and 256GB of storage.
- The foldable runs Android 17 with One UI 9 and is supported by seven years of software updates.
- Notable drawbacks include a persistent camera-induced wobble, lack of Qi 2 magnetic support, and an underwhelming telephoto lens.
- The phone is priced at $2,099, marking a significant cost increase over the previous Z Fold 7 model.

### Why it Matters
The Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra represents a stagnant design strategy that struggles to justify its premium price point against increasingly competitive foldable alternatives. While the internal improvements offer better performance, the lack of meaningful hardware innovation and persistent usability issues make it a difficult recommendation for most consumers.
