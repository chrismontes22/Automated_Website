Trump-Xi meeting comes amid tensions on Iran, Taiwan and trade: What to know
============================================================

President Donald Trump and Chinese leader Xi Jinping are meeting at the White House this week to address critical tensions regarding Taiwan, trade, artificial intelligence, and Iran.

### Key Points
- This state visit marks the first time a Chinese leader has visited the U.S. for such an event in over a decade.
- Discussions will focus on a proposed $14 billion arms sale to Taiwan, which China is pressuring the U.S. to halt.
- The agenda includes establishing AI safety guardrails and a potential renewal of the current tariff truce between the two nations.
- High-profile tech executives, including leaders from Amazon, Meta, Tesla, and OpenAI, are scheduled to attend the state dinner.
- Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent is coordinating with Chinese counterparts to improve transparency regarding national security incidents involving artificial intelligence.

### Why it Matters
This summit serves as a high-stakes attempt to stabilize the fragile relationship between the world's two largest economies amid deep mutual distrust. The outcome of these negotiations could significantly influence global market stability, international trade policies, and the geopolitical landscape regarding Middle Eastern conflicts and regional security in the Indo-Pacific.
