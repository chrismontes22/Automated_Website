Want Your New iPhone Duo Fast? Lock in Your Details Now
============================================================

Apple has launched a pre-order staging page for the new iPhone Duo, the company’s first foldable smartphone, which officially becomes available for purchase this Friday at 5 a.m. PT.

### Key Points
- The iPhone Duo features a 7.6-inch foldable display, split-screen multitasking, and support for the Apple Pencil.
- Pricing for the device ranges from $1,999 for the 512GB model to $3,199 for the 2TB version.
- Customers can save their configuration, carrier, and payment details on Apple’s website to expedite the checkout process.
- The device runs on the new iOS 27 operating system and was introduced during Apple’s recent Surprise and Shine event in Cupertino.
- This launch marks the first major product release under the leadership of new Apple CEO John Ternus.

### Why it Matters
The iPhone Duo represents Apple’s entry into the competitive foldable smartphone market, signaling a significant shift in the company's hardware design strategy. Its premium pricing and innovative form factor will test consumer demand for high-end foldable technology and influence the future trajectory of the iPhone lineup.
