The Stunning Ceramic Apple Watch Series 12 Is Here, and It’s $49 Off on the Final Amazon Prime Day
============================================================

Amazon is offering a $49 discount on the new Apple Watch Series 12, bringing the entry-level model price down to $350 following the conclusion of Prime Day.

### Key Points
- The Apple Watch Series 12 features updated heart-rate and health-sensing technology alongside a new Readiness app.
- Integrated Siri AI capabilities allow users to perform tasks and manage functions using voice commands.
- The base model is currently priced at $350, while the 42mm aluminum cellular version is discounted to $450.
- Discounts apply to various colors and configurations, though availability remains subject to change as post-Prime Day inventory fluctuates.

### Why it Matters
This promotion offers a rare early discount on Apple's latest wearable technology shortly after its initial market release. Consumers looking to upgrade their devices or enter the Apple ecosystem can secure significant savings, though the limited-time nature of these prices necessitates quick purchasing decisions.
