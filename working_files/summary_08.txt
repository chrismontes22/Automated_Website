AI Benchmark Scores Don’t Tell the Whole Story. Here’s What You Need to Look For.
============================================================

OpenAI’s release of the GPT-6 Astra model has sparked debate over the arrival of artificial general intelligence, shifting industry focus toward the infrastructure surrounding these powerful AI systems.

### Key Points
- Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang claimed GPT-6 Astra signifies the arrival of AGI, though OpenAI has not officially adopted this terminology for the new model.
- Researchers describe current AI capabilities as "jagged," noting that systems often excel in specific domains while remaining unreliable in others.
- Benchmark scores for models like Astra and Anthropic’s Claude Fable 5.1 can be misleading because performance depends heavily on the "harness" or infrastructure used.
- A harness—comprising prompt assembly, memory management, and tool access—can cause a single model's accuracy to swing by nearly 40 percentage points.
- SpaceX’s $60 billion acquisition of Cursor highlights a strategic shift toward controlling the application layer rather than just the underlying AI models.

### Why it Matters
The industry is moving away from prioritizing raw model performance toward the infrastructure that enables practical, reliable AI workflows. Businesses should evaluate AI tools based on their specific operational harness and real-world reliability rather than relying on subjective AGI claims or standardized leaderboard scores.
