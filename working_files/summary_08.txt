How Xiaomi made connected living feel more believable at IFA Berlin 2026
============================================================

At IFA Berlin 2026, Xiaomi showcased its "Human x Car x Home" ecosystem, demonstrating how integrated smart appliances and vehicles can automate daily routines for European consumers.

### Key Points
- Xiaomi debuted its "Human x Car x Home" ecosystem, connecting smartphones, home appliances, and vehicles into a unified, automated smart network.
- The company announced that Xiaomi Auto will officially enter Germany and other European markets starting in 2027.
- Practical demonstrations included car-to-home connectivity, such as automatic TV shut-offs and synchronized kitchen appliance tasks.
- New hardware featured a three-section washing machine, a zero-degree fresh-keeping refrigerator, and an air conditioner with motion-tracking airflow.
- The product design emphasizes a "quiet" aesthetic intended to blend into domestic environments rather than appearing overly clinical or futuristic.

### Why it Matters
Xiaomi is attempting to pivot its global brand identity from a smartphone manufacturer to a comprehensive provider of integrated smart living and mobility solutions. By expanding into the European automotive market, the company aims to challenge established tech and car brands with a seamless, friction-reducing ecosystem.
