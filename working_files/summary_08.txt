Amgen discloses data breach involving patient health information
============================================================

Amgen disclosed a significant cybersecurity breach involving third-party cloud storage systems that resulted in the unauthorized access of sensitive company data and patient health information.

### Key Points
- Amgen confirmed the data breach on July 29 after evaluating the volume and sensitivity of the compromised files.
- The company has activated its cybersecurity response plan and engaged independent forensic experts to investigate the incident.
- Current assessments indicate no disruption to manufacturing operations, product supply, or financial reporting systems.
- The breach follows a series of recent cyberattacks targeting major healthcare firms, including Abbott Laboratories, Medtronic, and Novo Nordisk.
- Amgen is currently determining the full scope of the stolen data, which may include intellectual property and research information.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the growing vulnerability of the healthcare sector to third-party cloud security failures and sophisticated cyberattacks. As companies increasingly rely on external providers, the potential for large-scale data exposure poses significant regulatory, legal, and reputational risks to the pharmaceutical industry.
