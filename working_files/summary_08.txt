Trump rolls out red carpet for Xi as high-stakes summit begins
============================================================

President Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping met at the White House for a two-day summit to discuss artificial intelligence, trade relations, and the ongoing crisis in Iran.

### Key Points
- President Trump and President Xi Jinping held three hours of talks at the White House, with a state dinner attended by executives from Tesla, Apple, Amazon, OpenAI, and Nvidia.
- Both leaders expressed a shared interest in maintaining human control over artificial intelligence, though China continues to invest heavily in state-led AI industrial integration.
- The U.S. is pressuring China to leverage its status as Iran's largest trading partner to help mediate peace talks amid a naval blockade in the Strait of Hormuz.
- Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent indicated that Washington and Beijing are discussing a one-year extension of the trade truce established last year in Busan.
- Current trade tensions remain high, with the U.S. maintaining an average tariff rate of over 36% on Chinese goods, while China’s rate on U.S. products sits at approximately 30%.

### Why it Matters
This summit highlights the delicate balance between economic competition and the necessity of cooperation on global security issues like AI regulation and Middle Eastern stability. The involvement of major U.S. tech leaders underscores the critical role private industry plays in the geopolitical dialogue between the world's two largest economies.
