ESR’s iPhone accessory lineup is ready for Apple’s foldable era
============================================================

ESR has launched a comprehensive accessory ecosystem for the new iPhone 18 and iPhone Duo, featuring protective cases, advanced MagSafe charging solutions, and durable screen protectors.

### Key Points
- The Atelier Vegan Leather Case for iPhone Duo and iPhone 18 features a zinc alloy Flex Stand tested for 3,000 cycles and 1,400g magnetic strength.
- UltraFit Armorite Pro screen protectors utilize Accessory Glass by Corning™ to provide 10x the impact absorption of generic alternatives.
- The Cyclone 25W Magnetic Charging Pad incorporates CryoBoost active cooling technology to maintain battery health and enable faster Qi2 wireless charging.
- The Xtend retractable charger series offers high-speed power delivery for both wall and car use, supporting up to 100W output for multiple devices.
- The Geo Magnetic Wallet Stand features Apple-certified Find My integration, RFID blocking, and a multi-view stand design for everyday utility.

### Why it Matters
These accessories address the specific durability and thermal management challenges posed by Apple’s latest hardware, particularly the folding iPhone Duo. By integrating active cooling and specialized materials, ESR aims to extend the functional lifespan of premium mobile devices while improving daily user convenience.
