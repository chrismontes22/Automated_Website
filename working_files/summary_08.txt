This 100TB cloud storage sticks with you for life, and it just went on sale
============================================================

Internxt is offering a lifetime subscription for 100TB of zero-knowledge encrypted cloud storage at a discounted price of $974.97, available through the StackSocial platform until October 11.

### Key Points
- The lifetime subscription provides 100TB of storage space with support for individual file uploads up to 10GB.
- Internxt utilizes zero-knowledge, post-quantum encryption to ensure that files remain private and inaccessible to the provider.
- The service is compatible with Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS, and web browsers for cross-platform file syncing.
- The platform’s codebase is open source and has undergone an independent security audit by Securitum.
- This promotional offer represents a significant reduction from the standard $9,900 retail price.

### Why it Matters
This deal offers a permanent alternative to recurring monthly cloud storage subscriptions, potentially saving users significant long-term costs. By providing a large-capacity, encrypted storage solution, it addresses growing consumer demand for both data privacy and simplified digital asset management.
