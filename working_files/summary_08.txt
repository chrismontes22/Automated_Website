Grok Bot Brings Always-On AI Agents to macOS and iOS
============================================================

SpaceXAI has launched Grok Bot, a suite of autonomous agents capable of executing complex tasks across websites and applications, following the company's $60 billion acquisition of Cursor.

### Key Points
- Grok Bot operates in the cloud, allowing automated workflows to continue running even when a user's device is powered off or disconnected.
- The system enables multiple specialized bots to coordinate in group chats, delegating tasks and managing workflows without constant human intervention.
- Users can train bots by demonstrating a task once, allowing the AI to learn the routine and execute it independently in the future.
- The technology is currently in beta for SuperGrok Heavy, Cursor Ultra, and Cursor Teams Premium subscribers on macOS and iOS platforms.
- Practical applications include automated CRM updates, invoice processing in Gmail, and engineering tasks like bug reproduction and ticket filing.

### Why it Matters
This launch signals a significant shift toward agentic AI that can interact directly with web interfaces rather than relying solely on traditional software APIs. By integrating these capabilities with Cursor, SpaceXAI is positioning itself to automate complex professional workflows that previously required manual human input.
