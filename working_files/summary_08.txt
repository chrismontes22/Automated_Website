Made by Google 2026 Launch Live: Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro Fold, Pixel Watch 5, Gemini, and all the news
============================================================

Google will host its annual Made by Google event on August 12 at 3 p.m. PT, featuring the launch of the Pixel 11 series and potential new hardware.

### Key Points
- The event will be streamed live via the official Made by Google YouTube channel and the Google Store website.
- Expected product announcements include the Pixel 11 smartphone series, a new foldable device, and the Pixel 5 smartwatch.
- The presentation will be hosted by Trevor Noah and feature appearances by celebrities including Stephen Curry and Shubman Gill.
- New hardware may incorporate the "Pixel Glow" light bar design and advanced Gemini-powered AI automation features.
- Discussions may also cover the high-end "Googlebook" laptop, which emphasizes deep integration with Gemini and the Magic Pointer tool.

### Why it Matters
This event serves as a critical showcase for Google’s strategy to position Android as an "intelligence system" rather than a traditional mobile operating system. By integrating Gemini deeply into both hardware and software, Google aims to differentiate its ecosystem against competitors like Apple and Samsung in an increasingly AI-driven market.
