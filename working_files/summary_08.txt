Tape companies ship 160 exabytes of storage in 2025, AI data demands drive 'unprecedented data growth' — capacity shipped in Q1 2026 rose 57% YOY driven by AI, LTO‑9 momentum, and early LTO‑10 uptake
============================================================

LTO tape storage shipments surged by 57% in the first quarter of 2026, driven by rising demand for AI data centers, cyber-resilient backups, and long-term archival storage.

### Key Points
- The LTO Program Technology Provider Companies, including HPE, IBM, and Quantum Corp., reported the record-breaking quarterly growth.
- Total compressed LTO tape capacity shipped reached 176.5 exabytes in 2024, followed by 160.3 exabytes in 2025.
- Current LTO-9 cartridges support up to 45TB of compressed data, while the newly introduced LTO-10 supports up to 100TB per cartridge.
- Future industry roadmaps extend to LTO-14, which aims to provide up to 1,440TB of compressed storage capacity per cartridge.
- Tape storage is increasingly prioritized for its low cost per terabyte, energy efficiency, and ability to protect data against cyber threats.

### Why it Matters
Tape storage remains a critical component of modern data infrastructure as organizations seek cost-effective and secure methods to manage unprecedented data growth. By providing a reliable, air-gapped solution for long-term retention, the technology serves as a vital hedge against both rising energy costs and digital security disruptions.
