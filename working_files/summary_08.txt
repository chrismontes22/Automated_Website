Google Buys All of Spirit Airlines' Data to Feed Its AI Models
============================================================

Google has agreed to purchase a comprehensive dataset from the defunct Spirit Airlines for $10 million to help train its artificial intelligence models using historical company information.

### Key Points
- Google acquired Spirit Airlines' internal communications, booking records, and frequent-flyer data following the carrier's bankruptcy and May shutdown.
- The $10 million transaction includes employee human resources information, transaction logs, and various internal spreadsheets.
- Court filings state that the acquired data has been anonymized to remove personally identifiable information before being integrated into Google's systems.
- The sale is part of a broader liquidation process involving the airline's remaining assets, including equipment and real estate.

### Why it Matters
This acquisition highlights the growing market value of corporate data as a primary resource for training large-scale artificial intelligence models. It raises significant questions regarding how companies handle sensitive internal information during bankruptcy proceedings and the long-term privacy implications for former customers and employees.
