Meta Has Quietly Become One of Microsoft’s Largest AI Customers
============================================================

Meta Platforms has emerged as a major customer for Microsoft’s Azure cloud service, spending hundreds of millions annually to access artificial intelligence models for its software development operations.

### Key Points
- Meta utilizes trillions of AI tokens weekly through Microsoft’s Azure cloud platform to support internal software development.
- Microsoft’s AI marketplace, Foundry, serves a diverse client base including Adobe, Perplexity, and the startup Sierra.
- ByteDance remains one of the largest spenders on Microsoft’s AI infrastructure services, according to industry reports.
- OpenAI currently accounts for approximately 70% of Microsoft’s total AI-related revenue as of the most recent fiscal year.
- Meta developers leverage third-party models via Foundry to benchmark and evaluate the performance of their own proprietary AI systems.

### Why it Matters
The heavy reliance on tech-sector clients highlights a potential challenge for the long-term sustainability of the artificial intelligence market. For the industry to mature, AI adoption must expand beyond sophisticated tech firms into traditional sectors like manufacturing and transportation.
