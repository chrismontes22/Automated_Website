Sony could release a cheaper version of its WH-1000XM4 headphones, according to leaks
============================================================

Sony is reportedly preparing to launch the WH-1000XM4C, a more affordable version of its popular wireless noise-canceling headphones, featuring familiar design elements and performance specifications for budget-conscious consumers.

### Key Points
- The Sony WH-1000XM4C is expected to launch in early September 2026 in black, silver, and lavender.
- Pricing is set at approximately €249.99 or £219.99, positioning the model below recent premium releases.
- Key features include the QN1 chip for active noise cancellation, spatial audio support, and integrated earcup touch controls.
- The device offers up to 34 hours of total playback, representing a slight reduction in battery life compared to the original 2020 model.
- The new version retains the classic folding design preferred by many users over the updated aesthetics of newer 1000X iterations.

### Why it Matters
This release provides a cost-effective alternative for fans of the legacy 1000XM4 design who want modern performance without the high price tag of newer flagship models. By refreshing a proven design, Sony aims to capture market share from users seeking reliable, high-quality audio hardware at a more accessible price point.
