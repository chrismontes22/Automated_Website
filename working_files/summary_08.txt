The Swiss government is actively testing a transition from Microsoft 365 to open-source software alternatives to reduce reliance on American cloud-based applications and enhance digital sovereignty.

### Key Points
- The Swiss Army is leading a pilot program to replace proprietary Microsoft 365 tools with free and open-source software (FOSS) solutions.
- This initiative aims to mitigate risks associated with foreign cloud dependency and improve data control for national infrastructure.
- Switzerland joins a growing trend of European organizations seeking to move away from dominant US-based enterprise software providers.
- The project emphasizes the use of modular, transparent, and community-driven platforms to ensure long-term operational independence.

### Why it Matters
This shift reflects a broader European movement toward digital sovereignty as governments prioritize data privacy and independence from major US tech corporations. If successful, the Swiss model could provide a blueprint for other nations looking to reduce their reliance on proprietary cloud ecosystems.
