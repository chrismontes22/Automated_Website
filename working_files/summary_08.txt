Rivian’s CFO is leaving after six years
============================================================

Rivian Chief Financial Officer Claire McDonough will depart the electric vehicle manufacturer in late October to join energy equipment company GE Vernova as its new finance chief.

### Key Points
- Claire McDonough served as Rivian’s CFO for six years, overseeing the company's initial public offering and the launch of three vehicle models.
- Derek Mulvey, currently the vice president of finance, will serve as interim CFO while the company conducts a search for a permanent successor.
- Volkswagen remains Rivian’s largest shareholder with a 15.9% stake and has committed up to $5.8 billion to a joint venture focused on software-defined vehicle architecture.
- Rivian is currently scaling production for its upcoming R2 model, with annual delivery targets set between 65,000 and 70,000 vehicles.
- The joint venture with Volkswagen employs over 1,500 people and aims to support future vehicle platforms for Audi, Porsche, and Scout through the end of the decade.

### Why it Matters
The leadership transition occurs at a critical juncture as Rivian balances its mainstream R2 production ramp with complex technical milestones tied to its multibillion-dollar partnership with Volkswagen. Investors are closely monitoring whether the company can maintain its development schedule and meet delivery targets under new financial leadership.
