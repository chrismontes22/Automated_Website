Canadian Man Pleads Guilty in Snowflake Extortions
============================================================

Canadian national Connor Riley Moucka has pleaded guilty to orchestrating massive cyberattacks against 165 Snowflake customers and stealing sensitive call records from over 100 million AT&T customers.

### Key Points
- Connor Riley Moucka, 26, admitted to computer fraud, wire fraud, and conspiracy charges involving the theft of terabytes of sensitive data.
- The hackers exploited Snowflake accounts that lacked multi-factor authentication to extort major corporations like Ticketmaster and Neiman Marcus.
- Moucka and his co-conspirators collected over $2.5 million in ransom payments by threatening to publish stolen personal and financial information.
- U.S. Army soldier Cameron Wagenius, a co-conspirator, pleaded guilty to extorting telecommunications providers and faces sentencing in September 2026.
- Alleged co-conspirator John Erin Binns remains at large in Turkey, where he reportedly obtained citizenship to avoid extradition to the United States.

### Why it Matters
These breaches highlight the critical vulnerability of cloud-based infrastructure when organizations fail to enforce basic security protocols like multi-factor authentication. The case underscores the severe real-world consequences of large-scale data theft, including the exposure of government officials and the potential for ongoing re-extortion of victims.
