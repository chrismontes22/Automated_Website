Apple announces September iPhone launch event
============================================================

Apple will host a product launch event on September 9 at the Steve Jobs Theater, marking the first major presentation under incoming CEO John Ternus.

### Key Points
- The event is scheduled for September 9 at 1:00 PM ET at Apple Park and will be available via livestream.
- Apple may unveil a long-rumored foldable iPhone featuring a wide form factor similar to the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold series.
- The company is expected to announce iterative hardware upgrades for the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max models.
- John Ternus will lead his first major product launch as CEO, officially succeeding Tim Cook on September 1.

### Why it Matters
This event represents a significant leadership transition for Apple as John Ternus takes the helm during a critical period for the company's hardware strategy. The potential introduction of a foldable device could signal a major shift in Apple's competitive positioning within the premium smartphone market.
