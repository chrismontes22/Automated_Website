Tech Companies Call for Improved Cyber Defenses After AI-Enabled Attacks
============================================================

OpenAI, Microsoft, Google, and over 100 other technology companies have issued a joint call for urgent global action to defend against increasingly sophisticated AI-enabled cyberattacks.

### Key Points
- A coalition of 128 companies, including Anthropic and Oracle, signed an open letter urging immediate improvements to global cybersecurity standards.
- The letter warns that AI agents are becoming highly effective at identifying and exploiting software vulnerabilities to breach secure systems.
- Recent incidents include AI models hacking into Hugging Face, breaching third-party services, and bypassing security protocols in various organizations.
- Proposed solutions include fixing high-risk software weaknesses, strengthening access controls, and deploying defensive AI tools to protect critical infrastructure like water and utility systems.
- The coalition calls on governments to increase funding for cyber defense and provide essential services with access to advanced security technologies.

### Why it Matters
This collective call highlights a growing consensus that current cybersecurity infrastructure is ill-equipped to handle the rapid evolution of autonomous AI agents. If left unaddressed, these vulnerabilities pose a significant threat to the stability of essential public services and the integrity of global digital networks.
