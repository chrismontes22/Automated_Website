Wikipedians Believed AI Would Come and Go. They Were Wrong
============================================================

Wikipedia faces an existential crisis as the rise of artificial intelligence and shifting reader habits threaten the platform's long-standing role as a human-curated source of knowledge.

### Key Points
- A Princeton University study found that approximately 5 percent of new English Wikipedia articles in August 2024 contained substantial AI-generated content.
- By March 2026, the Wikimedia Foundation officially banned the use of large language models for writing articles, restricting them only to minor proofreading and translation.
- Wikipedia has experienced a significant decline in engagement, losing nearly 25 percent of its readership since 2022 as users increasingly turn to AI-generated summaries.
- The platform struggles with an aging pool of volunteer editors and a growing public indifference toward the labor-intensive process of maintaining human-verified information.

### Why it Matters
The decline of Wikipedia signals the potential end of the encyclopedic age, where human-led consensus is being replaced by algorithmically generated content. This shift risks turning a collaborative, global knowledge project into a static historical artifact while undermining the democratic value of transparent, human-verified information.
