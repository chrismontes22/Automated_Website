Deals: iPhone 17 series on sale, OLED-packing iPad mini and touch-enabled MacBook Pro launch
============================================================

Apple has expanded its hardware lineup with the launch of a new OLED-equipped iPad mini and a MacBook Pro featuring the first touch-enabled Tandem OLED display.

### Key Points
- The new iPad mini features an 8.5-inch OLED display, an A20 Pro chipset, 12GB of RAM, and an IP67 water resistance rating.
- Apple’s updated MacBook Pro introduces a 120Hz Tandem OLED touch-enabled screen and replaces the traditional notch with a Dynamic Island.
- The iPhone 17 remains available with a €180 discount on the 256GB model, while the iPhone Air offers a thinner, lighter alternative with a 6.5-inch display.
- The iPhone 17 Pro includes a 48MP telephoto camera and an A19 Pro chipset, positioning it as a performance-focused option against the newer iPhone 18 series.
- The new iPad mini is 5.4mm thick and weighs 255g, representing a slimmer and lighter design compared to the 2024 model.

### Why it Matters
These releases signal a significant shift toward OLED technology across Apple’s tablet and laptop portfolios, potentially setting a new standard for display quality in mobile computing. Consumers now face complex trade-offs between the latest high-performance hardware and discounted previous-generation models that may offer better value for specific use cases.
