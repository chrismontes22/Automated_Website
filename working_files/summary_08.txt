4 reasons why you should buy a Galaxy Z Fold 8, and 4 reasons why you should skip it
============================================================

Samsung has launched the Galaxy Z Fold 8, a compact and lightweight foldable smartphone that offers flagship performance and multitasking capabilities at a lower price point than the Ultra.

### Key Points
- The Galaxy Z Fold 8 features the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 processor with 12GB or 16GB of RAM.
- It includes advanced AI features like My FanCam and integrated Gemini tools for task automation and data privacy.
- The device offers a creaseless inner display and a form factor comparable to a passport when folded.
- It lacks S Pen support and features a camera system that underperforms compared to the Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra.
- The phone is priced at $1,900, positioning it as a more affordable alternative to the $2,100 Ultra model.

### Why it Matters
The Galaxy Z Fold 8 represents a strategic shift for Samsung by offering a more portable, tablet-like experience that challenges the design standards of current foldable devices. Its market success could influence future industry trends, particularly if it successfully establishes a new blueprint for balancing high-end performance with a more accessible form factor.
