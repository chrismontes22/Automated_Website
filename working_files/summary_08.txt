WhatsApp Ends Support For Older Phones: Requirements And Security Impact
============================================================

WhatsApp has officially restricted access for smartphones running outdated operating systems, requiring users to upgrade to Android 6.0 or iOS 15.5 to maintain connectivity on the messaging platform.

### Key Points
- WhatsApp implemented a server-side block that prevents users on unsupported legacy software from accessing the application.
- The platform now requires a minimum of Android 6.0 (Marshmallow) for Android devices and iOS 15.5 for Apple hardware.
- Users on older devices receive a mandatory, non-dismissible full-screen warning until they upgrade their operating system or migrate to a newer device.
- The policy change aims to mitigate cybersecurity risks associated with unpatched vulnerabilities in aging mobile operating systems.
- Legacy devices are susceptible to local malware that can intercept messages before end-to-end encryption occurs.

### Why it Matters
This update forces users with older hardware to upgrade their devices to ensure continued access to essential communication services. By enforcing these technical baselines, WhatsApp prioritizes platform-wide security over support for legacy systems that no longer receive critical vendor patches.
