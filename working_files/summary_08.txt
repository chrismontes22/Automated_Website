12 New Things to Expect From Apple in October
============================================================

Apple is preparing a significant product rollout in October, featuring the new foldable iPhone Duo, updated Mac Studio configurations, and several rumored smart home and computing devices.

### Key Points
- The foldable iPhone Duo launches October 23, with pre-orders beginning October 16 and iOS 27.1 shipping as the device's native operating system.
- A high-end Mac Studio configuration featuring 512GB of RAM is scheduled for release in late October.
- Apple is expected to unveil a new smart home hub, an updated Apple TV 4K, and a HomePod mini 2 on October 13.
- Future product announcements may include MacBook Pro models with OLED displays, a 24-inch iMac, and an iPad mini 8 featuring an A20 Pro chip.
- iOS 27.2 is slated for an October release to introduce support for five additional Siri AI languages.

### Why it Matters
This aggressive release schedule signals Apple's commitment to integrating advanced AI capabilities across its entire hardware ecosystem, from smart home hubs to professional-grade laptops. The expansion of the product lineup suggests a strategic effort to capture market share during the critical holiday shopping season.
