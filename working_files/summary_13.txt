What Growing Businesses Should Expect From an HR and Payroll Provider
============================================================

Small business leaders are shifting their HR and payroll software selection criteria from basic feature sets toward integrated platforms that combine artificial intelligence with expert human support.

### Key Points
- Business leaders should prioritize AI tools that provide measurable operational impact rather than those simply marketing a high volume of automated capabilities.
- Research from UKG indicates that 84% of employees support using AI to automate workplace processes and reduce administrative friction.
- Modern HCM providers are increasingly offering managed payroll and HR advisory services to supplement internal teams lacking dedicated specialists.
- Companies with fewer than 75 employees should select technology partners based on future scalability requirements rather than their current headcount or immediate needs.
- Relying on manual workarounds and spreadsheets as a company grows creates significant operational risk and inconsistent data management.

### Why it Matters
Selecting the right HR and payroll partner is critical for businesses to maintain compliance and efficiency as they scale across different states or locations. By choosing a provider that offers both intelligent software and human expertise, leaders can avoid the disruption of switching systems during periods of rapid growth.
