Stock market today: Dow, S&P 500, Nasdaq rise as oil slips, Fed rate hike pacifies markets' inflation worries
============================================================

US stocks rebounded on Thursday as easing oil prices and the Federal Reserve’s recent interest rate hike helped restore investor confidence regarding the central bank's commitment to curbing inflation.

### Key Points
- The S&P 500 rose 1.1%, the Nasdaq Composite climbed 1.7%, and the Dow Jones Industrial Average gained 0.6%.
- The Federal Reserve implemented a 25-basis-point interest rate hike on Wednesday, its first increase in three years.
- Brent crude prices fell to approximately $104 per barrel following reports that Saudi Arabia’s East-West pipeline will soon resume operations.
- The Bank of England maintained its benchmark lending rate at 3.75% during its Thursday policy meeting.

### Why it Matters
The market recovery signals that investors are beginning to accept the Federal Reserve's hawkish stance as a necessary step to stabilize the economy. By prioritizing inflation control over political pressure, the central bank is attempting to regain credibility, though ongoing uncertainty regarding future rate hikes may continue to drive volatility in the coming weeks.
