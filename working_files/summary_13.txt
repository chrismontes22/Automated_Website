Apple's Camera AirPods May Launch as 'AirPods Pro,' Not 'AirPods Ultra'
============================================================

Apple is reportedly preparing to launch new camera-equipped AirPods, likely under the AirPods Pro branding, to enhance Siri’s visual intelligence capabilities through integrated hardware sensors and software.

### Key Points
- Apple is developing camera-equipped AirPods, identified by the internal codename B790, which are expected to launch soon.
- Evidence found in macOS Tahoe 26.7 software files suggests the new hardware will be marketed as part of the AirPods Pro lineup.
- The integrated camera will not capture photos or videos but will instead provide visual data to improve Siri’s Visual Intelligence features.
- Development of the device has involved multiple internal prototypes, including the B798 model, with the B790 version currently closer to a public release.

### Why it Matters
This integration represents a significant shift in wearable technology by moving beyond audio to provide AI-driven visual context for users. If successful, this hardware could redefine how consumers interact with voice assistants by allowing Siri to process real-world environments in real time.
