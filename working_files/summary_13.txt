This Week in AI: Who Controls AI?
============================================================

Governments and major technology firms are navigating a complex landscape of infrastructure spending, regulatory oversight, and scientific breakthroughs as the global race for artificial intelligence dominance intensifies.

### Key Points
- Google reported its first quarter of negative free cash flow since going public, driven by $44.9 billion in AI infrastructure spending.
- Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei proposed that policymakers prioritize model capabilities over open or closed status when assessing safety risks.
- The U.S. government implemented new restrictions on foreign-made humanoid robots, primarily targeting Chinese manufacturers to protect domestic development.
- OpenAI plans to grant 100,000 academic researchers free access to its advanced models to foster scientific discovery.
- Claude Fable 5 successfully helped researchers solve a counterexample to the 87-year-old Jacobian conjecture, a breakthrough with potential implications for cryptography.
- Google DeepMind reorganized its structure, moving the AlphaFold team into the broader organization to prioritize the development of the Gemini model.

### Why it Matters
The rapid escalation in infrastructure costs and regulatory intervention highlights the growing tension between corporate innovation and national security interests. As AI models demonstrate real-world scientific utility, businesses must balance aggressive development cycles with the need for rigorous safety testing to maintain public trust.
