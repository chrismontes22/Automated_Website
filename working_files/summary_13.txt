I Pretended to Be On Vacation to Test This Rental-Ready Smart Lock
============================================================

The new Lockly Lyro Lasta smart lock offers an affordable, rental-focused security solution featuring an extended three-year battery life and integrated TapCom communication tools for property managers.

### Key Points
- The Lockly Lyro Lasta is priced at $169 and features an IP65-rated weatherproof exterior.
- It utilizes an eight-battery housing to achieve a battery life of up to three years.
- Access methods include a fingerprint sensor, RFID tags, physical keys, and a touchscreen keypad.
- The TapCom platform allows guests to communicate with owners via QR codes and NFC plates for check-ins.
- Unlike most smart locks, the deadbolt requires a manual thumbturn operation after the internal mechanism unlocks.

### Why it Matters
This device targets the growing short-term rental market by simplifying guest management and reducing maintenance requirements for property owners. Its combination of long-term power and integrated communication tools provides a specialized alternative to standard residential smart locks.
