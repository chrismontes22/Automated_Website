HomePod 27.2 hands-on: default wireless speaker for Mac, Sound Glow and more [Video]
============================================================

Apple’s 27.2 software update introduces low-latency Mac audio support, new Lock Controls for all models, and immersive surround sound configurations for the latest HomePod and HomePod mini devices.

### Key Points
- HomePod (2nd generation) and HomePod mini 2 now support low-latency wireless audio for Macs equipped with Wi-Fi 6E.
- The new Lock Controls feature disables touch-surface inputs on all HomePod generations to prevent accidental activation by users or pets.
- Sound Glow provides ambient LED lighting that syncs with the rhythm and colors of album artwork on supported HomePod models.
- Users can now configure four-speaker surround sound setups when paired with the fourth-generation Apple TV 4K.
- The update enables an ad hoc point-to-point connection, allowing HomePods to function as viable speakers for real-time audio applications like Logic Pro.

### Why it Matters
These updates transform the HomePod lineup from simple smart speakers into more versatile components for professional desktop setups and home theater environments. By reducing latency and expanding configuration options, Apple is positioning its hardware as a more competitive alternative to dedicated third-party audio systems.
