Ditch the Cloud: MINISFORUM Unveils a 131 TOPS Local AI Powerhouse Duo at IFA 2026
============================================================

MINISFORUM unveiled the MS-S1 MAX-P495 workstation and N5 MAX-P495 NAS at IFA Berlin, featuring AMD Ryzen AI MAX+ PRO 495 processors for high-performance local edge computing tasks.

### Key Points
- Both systems utilize the AMD Ryzen AI MAX+ PRO 495 processor to deliver up to 131 TOPS of AI compute performance.
- The hardware supports up to 192GB of high-speed memory and 160GB of graphics memory for complex local AI workloads.
- The N5 MAX-P495 NAS provides up to 200TB of storage capacity, designed for hosting vector databases and persistent AI agents.
- The MS-S1 MAX-P495 workstation is optimized for real-time AI inference, computer vision, and generative development in a compact form factor.
- MINISFORUM has not yet announced specific retail pricing or official commercial release dates for either of the new edge computing platforms.

### Why it Matters
These systems enable businesses and developers to process sensitive data locally, reducing reliance on cloud-based infrastructure and improving privacy for AI workflows. By combining high-capacity storage with powerful edge inference, the hardware provides a scalable, private ecosystem for managing complex automated tasks behind a firewall.
