I Can’t Put the Galaxy Z Fold 8 Down. Can Apple’s Foldable Change That?
============================================================

The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 offers a highly portable, passport-sized foldable design that challenges current market standards ahead of Apple’s anticipated entry into the foldable smartphone category.

### Key Points
- The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 features a 7.6-inch inner display, weighs 201 grams, and measures 4.5 millimeters thick when unfolded.
- The device retails for $1,900 and includes a 50-megapixel wide and 50-megapixel ultrawide camera system, but lacks a telephoto lens and Qi2 magnetic charging.
- International Data Corporation (IDC) forecasts global foldable shipments will grow over 12% this year, with Apple expected to capture nearly 40% market share by 2027.
- Apple is rumored to unveil a foldable iPhone at its September 9 event, potentially featuring a similar 4:3 aspect ratio to the Z Fold 8.

### Why it Matters
The release of a foldable iPhone is expected to significantly accelerate consumer adoption of the foldable form factor despite potential trade-offs in camera and battery performance. This shift could reshape the premium smartphone market as Apple leverages its brand influence to drive mainstream interest in compact, versatile device designs.
