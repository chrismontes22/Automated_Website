I love the Fitbit Air, but Google's leaked Fitbit Edge might be even better
============================================================

Google is reportedly preparing to launch the Fitbit Edge, a new wearable featuring a small display and seven-day battery life, positioned between the Fitbit Air and Pixel Watch.

### Key Points
- The Fitbit Edge will feature an oval-shaped display for health metrics, notifications, and basic controls like Google Maps and YouTube Music.
- The device is expected to offer seven-day battery life and cross-platform compatibility with both Android and iPhone devices.
- Leaked renders show the tracker in three colors: Obsidian, Olive, and Canyon.
- Estimated pricing for the new wearable is expected to fall between $149 and $199.
- The product is anticipated to launch later this month as a successor to the Fitbit Charge 6.

### Why it Matters
This device fills a strategic gap in Google’s wearable lineup by offering a middle-ground option between the entry-level Fitbit Air and the premium Pixel Watch. By maintaining cross-platform support, Google aims to capture a broader market segment that prioritizes health tracking and essential smart features over a full smartwatch experience.
