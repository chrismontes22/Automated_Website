Brevo Supply Chain Attack Injects Malware Into 100,000 Websites
============================================================

Customer engagement platform Brevo suffered a significant supply chain attack that injected malicious scripts into over 100,000 websites, exposing users to sophisticated social engineering and potential backdoor installations.

### Key Points
- Attackers exploited a SAML SSO vulnerability on September 10 to access 138 Brevo accounts, including those belonging to Trezor.
- A compromised Cloudflare API key allowed hackers to deploy a malicious worker on September 14 for approximately five and a half hours.
- The injected script utilized a "ClickFix" social engineering technique to trick visitors into running malicious commands on their personal computers.
- WordPress sites using Brevo widgets were specifically targeted with attempts to install unauthorized administrative plugins.
- Cybersecurity firm Sansec estimates that more than 100,000 websites were impacted by the malicious code injection.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the severe risks posed by supply chain attacks where third-party service providers become vectors for widespread malware distribution. Businesses relying on embedded widgets must now conduct thorough security audits to ensure their platforms remain free of unauthorized backdoors and compromised administrative access.
