Red Violet Eyes M&A Growth as AI Identity Platform Expands Across Key Markets
============================================================

Red Violet (NASDAQ: RDVT) is leveraging its cloud-native identity intelligence platform and a recent $115 million capital raise to pursue strategic acquisitions and expand its market reach.

### Key Points
- Red Violet aggregates data into a 75-year longitudinal identity graph to support fraud detection, risk assessment, and investigative services.
- The company serves five primary verticals, including collections, law enforcement, financial risk, emerging markets, and real estate.
- Its FOREWARN safety application, currently used by over 600 real estate associations, is expanding into the home healthcare sector.
- Red Violet powers approximately 1,000 U.S. law enforcement agencies and recently secured a major multiyear contract with the nation's largest agency.
- Management estimates a $10 billion total addressable market and aims for long-term gross profit margins exceeding 90%.
- The company maintains over 10,000 customers, with 80% of its revenue derived from contractual, usage-based agreements.

### Why it Matters
Red Violet’s transition from a niche data provider to a scalable, cloud-native platform positions it to compete against legacy systems in the multi-billion dollar identity verification market. By utilizing recent capital for acquisitions, the company aims to accelerate growth and solidify its footprint across both public and private sector risk management.
