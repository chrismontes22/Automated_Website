Google's next TV Streamer sounds like a massive upgrade — Apple should be worried
============================================================

Google is reportedly developing a next-generation TV Streamer featuring far-field microphones for hands-free Gemini interaction, a faster processor, increased storage, and a redesigned remote with additional shortcut buttons.

### Key Points
- The upcoming device will feature a narrower, more compact design while retaining the aesthetic of the current Google TV Streamer.
- Built-in far-field microphones will allow users to trigger voice commands and interact with Gemini without using the remote.
- New presence-sensing technology will enable the device to automatically wake the TV and display information when it detects someone in the room.
- Hardware upgrades include a faster processor, expanded storage capacity, and a remote with dedicated buttons for YouTube and Netflix.
- The redesigned remote will include a dedicated "Find My Remote" button to help users locate the device.

### Why it Matters
These hardware and software enhancements signal Google's intent to deepen the integration of Gemini AI into the living room experience. By prioritizing hands-free interaction and presence-sensing, the company aims to position its streaming hardware as a central hub for smart home automation and personalized content delivery.
