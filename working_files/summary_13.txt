MarketBeat Week in Review – 09/14 - 09/18
============================================================

Investors navigated a volatile week marked by AI sector fluctuations and shifting interest rate expectations, while preparing for critical upcoming economic data releases on inflation and employment.

### Key Points
- The S&P 500 and NASDAQ indexes are poised for weekly gains despite broader market volatility driven by inflation, interest rates, and oil prices.
- Investors are closely monitoring the upcoming August Personal Consumption Expenditures (PCE) index on September 30 and the September Jobs report on October 2.
- Chip stocks experienced significant movement due to AI regulation concerns, though companies like Micron Technology and Qualcomm remain central to infrastructure growth narratives.
- Nike faces removal from the S&P 500, while Tesla is addressing federal regulatory inquiries regarding the design of its Cybercab.
- European markets are seeing activity as the European Central Bank continues rate hikes and energy companies address low natural gas inventories ahead of winter.

### Why it Matters
Market participants are balancing near-term AI infrastructure optimism against macroeconomic uncertainty and potential shifts in consumer spending. These upcoming economic indicators will be vital for determining whether current market valuations can be sustained through the end of the year.
