iOS 27 gives Apple Calendar my favorite new Home Screen upgrade
============================================================

Apple has introduced new extra-large Home Screen widgets in iOS 27, featuring expanded functionality for apps like Calendar, Notes, and Home to display more information at a glance.

### Key Points
- iOS 27 adds an extra-large widget size equivalent to a 4x6 grid of app icons.
- The Apple Calendar app now features two specific extra-large widgets titled List and Up Next.
- These widgets allow users to view multiple days of upcoming events and meetings without opening the app.
- Other Apple apps, including Home and Notes, have been updated to support the larger widget format for increased utility.
- Users can place these widgets on their Home Screen or within the Today view for easier access.

### Why it Matters
These larger widgets signal a shift toward more information-dense interfaces, potentially preparing users for future hardware with larger displays like the rumored iPhone Ultra. By reducing the need to open individual apps, this update improves overall productivity and streamlines daily task management for iPhone users.
