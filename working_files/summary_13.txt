Tech stocks tumble as AI debt fears rattle Wall Street
============================================================

Technology stocks led a broad market decline on Friday as investors grew concerned over the high debt levels of AI-focused companies and persistent inflationary pressures impacting interest rates.

### Key Points
- The Nasdaq Composite dropped 2.1% to 16,140, marking its worst weekly performance since June.
- CoreWeave shares fell 12% on Friday, contributing to a 24% weekly decline amid investor scrutiny of its debt-financed capital expenditures.
- Major chipmakers including Micron Technology, Advanced Micro Devices, Broadcom, and Intel saw share prices fall between 4.5% and 6%.
- Reports indicate Anthropic may lower its valuation target to $1.5 trillion ahead of its upcoming public offering.
- CME FedWatch data shows the probability of a Federal Reserve interest rate hike in December has risen to 74.3% due to stubborn inflation.

### Why it Matters
The market downturn highlights growing investor skepticism regarding the long-term profitability of the capital-intensive artificial intelligence sector. If inflation remains elevated, the Federal Reserve may be forced to maintain or increase interest rates, which would further tighten borrowing conditions for technology firms and broader financial markets.
