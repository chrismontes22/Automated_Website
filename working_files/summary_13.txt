TMY Google TV projector drops to $175.99 in its first Amazon price drop
============================================================

The TMY Smart Projector featuring an integrated Google TV system is currently available on Amazon for $175.99, marking the first price reduction since the device's initial market release.

### Key Points
- The projector is discounted by 30% from its original $249.99 retail price, resulting in a total savings of $74.
- The device features built-in Google TV support, allowing direct access to streaming services like Netflix, Prime Video, and YouTube.
- Technical specifications include 1080p Full HD playback with support for 4K input, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth connectivity.
- The hardware includes a rotatable design for flexible placement and comes with a one-year warranty and a 30-day money-back guarantee.

### Why it Matters
This discount positions the TMY projector as a highly competitive entry-level option for consumers seeking an all-in-one home theater solution without external streaming hardware. The price drop may signal a broader effort by budget projector brands to capture market share by emphasizing integrated smart software features.
