SpaceX’s Starship test successfully reaches space, deploys satellites after record-breaking IPO
============================================================

SpaceX successfully completed its 13th Starship test flight from Starbase, Texas, deploying 20 next-generation Starlink V3 satellites while demonstrating critical engine relight capabilities during a controlled suborbital mission.

### Key Points
- SpaceX launched the Starship/Super Heavy vehicle on July 24, marking the company's first mission since its record-breaking $86 billion IPO.
- The mission successfully deployed 20 operational Starlink V3 satellites, designed to provide enhanced network speeds and capacity.
- Engineers successfully relit the Raptor engines in space, a technical milestone required for future orbital and Mars-bound missions.
- The upper stage achieved a controlled descent into the Indian Ocean, while the Super Heavy booster completed a water landing in the Gulf of America.

### Why it Matters
- The successful deployment of V3 hardware signals a major advancement in global satellite internet infrastructure, offering high-bandwidth connectivity to remote regions. This progress directly impacts the scalability of decentralized networks and remote digital operations that rely on consistent, high-speed satellite data links.
