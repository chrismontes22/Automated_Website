SpaceX Reports $540 Million Loss On Bitcoin Holdings
============================================================

SpaceX reported strong quarterly revenue of $7.81 billion, though the company’s stock price declined following a significant increase in capital expenditures dedicated to artificial intelligence development projects.

### Key Points
- SpaceX reported revenue of $7.81 billion, exceeding Wall Street forecasts and marking a 92% increase compared to the previous year.
- The company maintained its holdings of 18,712 Bitcoin, which decreased in value to $1.10 billion due to a 33% drop in cryptocurrency prices.
- Quarterly earnings per share resulted in a loss of $0.09, performing better than the anticipated $0.26 loss.
- Capital expenditures rose sixfold to $18.40 billion in the second quarter, primarily driven by heavy investment in artificial intelligence.
- Shares of SPCX have fallen 50% from their post-IPO peak of $225.64 following the announcement of increased spending.

### Why it Matters
The company's pivot toward aggressive artificial intelligence spending has triggered investor concern, leading to a sharp decline in share price despite strong core revenue growth. This shift highlights the high costs associated with scaling AI infrastructure and the market's sensitivity to capital allocation strategies in the aerospace sector.
