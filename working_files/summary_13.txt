Chancellor unveils Britain’s ambitious plan for AI
============================================================

The UK Chancellor has announced a major investment package exceeding £600 million to bolster national AI security, support homegrown technology firms, and drive economic growth across all regions.

### Key Points
- A £165 million National AI Biosecurity Initiative includes a world-first AI Biosecurity Lab supported by OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, and Basecamp Research.
- The AI Security Institute will receive £120 million to advance testing, research, and technical standards for AI safety.
- New cyber defense measures include a £60 million National Cyber Shield and a £70 million National Exposure and Real-Time Vulnerability (NERV) Centre.
- The Sovereign AI Fund will receive an additional £60 million to scale UK-based companies, with a new focus on physical and industrial AI.
- A new AI Centre of Excellence in Manchester and an Advanced Chip Design Academy in Edinburgh will foster regional talent and hardware capabilities.
- £30 million is allocated for local AI partnerships to help businesses and public services adopt technology across every UK postcode.

### Why it Matters
This investment aims to reduce British reliance on foreign technology while positioning the UK as a global leader in AI safety and industrial resilience. By integrating security infrastructure with regional economic development, the government seeks to mitigate job displacement risks and ensure that AI-driven growth benefits the entire country.
