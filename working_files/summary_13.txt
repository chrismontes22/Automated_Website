Fed Decision, Tech Earnings and Other Can't Miss Items this Week
============================================================

Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh will announce a pivotal interest rate decision this Wednesday, coinciding with a critical week of mega-cap technology earnings and key economic growth data.

### Key Points
- The Federal Reserve announces its rate decision Wednesday at 2:00 p.m., followed by Chair Kevin Warsh’s first press conference.
- Meta, Microsoft, Apple, and Amazon report earnings Wednesday and Thursday, testing the sustainability of massive artificial intelligence infrastructure spending.
- Thursday’s 8:30 a.m. release of Q2 GDP and the June Core PCE Price Index will provide a comprehensive assessment of U.S. economic health.
- Ongoing Middle East conflict and potential energy market volatility continue to threaten inflation stability and limit the Federal Reserve's policy flexibility.

### Why it Matters
This week serves as a major inflection point for 2026 market direction as investors weigh the reality of AI monetization against persistent inflation and economic growth concerns. The convergence of central bank policy, corporate earnings, and macroeconomic data will likely dictate whether the technology sector undergoes further valuation corrections or stabilizes.
