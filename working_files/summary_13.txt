Animoca Brands delays IPO plans, suspends merger talks with Currenc
============================================================

Animoca Brands has officially suspended merger negotiations with Currenc Group, effectively delaying the digital asset investment firm's plans to secure a public listing on the Nasdaq exchange.

### Key Points
- Animoca Brands and Currenc Group ended merger talks due to incompatible timelines for finalizing the transaction.
- The proposed deal would have granted Hong Kong-based Animoca a 95% stake in the combined entity.
- Animoca co-founder Yat Siu confirmed the company remains committed to pursuing a public listing on a major exchange.
- Currenc Group shares experienced a 0.93% decline in after-hours trading following the announcement on Monday.
- Animoca’s diverse portfolio includes investments in decentralized finance, artificial intelligence, non-fungible tokens, and gaming.

### Why it Matters
The collapse of this deal forces Animoca Brands to seek alternative strategies for entering public markets as it continues to expand its influence in the digital asset sector. This development highlights the ongoing challenges firms face when attempting to navigate complex regulatory and timeline requirements for public listings.
