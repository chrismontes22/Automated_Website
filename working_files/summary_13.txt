Is this the new cyber normal?
============================================================

A surge of critical software vulnerabilities, including the widespread PHP2Shell exploit, has triggered global security concerns as AI-driven tools accelerate the timeline from discovery to active exploitation.

### Key Points
- The PHP2Shell vulnerabilities (CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619) affect an estimated 70% of the internet, impacting major platforms like the Wikimedia Foundation and various government systems.
- Security researchers utilized frontier AI models, such as GPT-5.6 and Gemini 4, to identify these flaws and generate functional exploits, significantly shortening the window for defensive patching.
- The Indian certificate authority eMudhra announced its exit from the SSL/TLS business after researchers discovered the use of critically weak private keys for intermediate certificates.
- GitLab reported multiple maximum-severity (CVSS 10.0) vulnerabilities in its CI/CD pipeline, while the Python package LiteLLM suffered a credential-stealing compromise via a malicious update.
- Cyber extortion group FulcrumSec claimed to have exfiltrated 10.6 TB of data from the database company PingCAP, highlighting ongoing risks to enterprise cloud infrastructure.

### Why it Matters
The rapid integration of AI into both vulnerability research and exploit development has compressed the response time for security teams from months to mere hours. This shift forces organizations to reconsider traditional disclosure timelines and security postures, as the barrier to entry for sophisticated cyberattacks continues to drop.
