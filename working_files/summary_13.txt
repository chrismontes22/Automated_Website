Siena raises $17M Series A to build AI agents for consumer brands
============================================================

New York-based startup Siena has secured $17 million in Series A funding to expand its AI agent platform designed to automate customer service and commerce tasks for brands.

### Key Points
- The $17 million funding round, led by York IE, brings Siena’s total capital raised to $29.7 million.
- Founded by Andrei Negrau and Lisa Popovici, the company provides AI agents that manage customer inquiries, social media interactions, and product purchases.
- Siena’s "Agent of Record" system integrates data from orders, subscriptions, and reviews to provide a unified customer experience across different communication channels.
- The platform is currently used by hundreds of brands, including SPANX, FIGS, HexClad, and Comfrt, to handle up to 80% of customer interactions.
- The company plans to use the new capital to develop autonomous agents and expand its workforce.

### Why it Matters
Siena’s outcome-based pricing model challenges traditional per-seat software costs by aligning expenses directly with brand performance and data usage. By consolidating fragmented customer service tools into a single intelligent system, the company aims to become a foundational infrastructure layer for modern e-commerce brands.
