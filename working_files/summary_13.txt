Attackers Abuse ChatGPT Custom GPTs to Deliver RAT via ClickFix Lures
============================================================

Threat actors are exploiting ChatGPT’s Custom GPT feature to distribute remote access trojans by redirecting users to malicious Google Sites pages that employ deceptive ClickFix installation lures.

### Key Points
- Researchers at Huntress identified a campaign where attackers created "Plus 5.6" Custom GPTs to trick at least 40 users into downloading malicious MSI installers.
- The attack chain utilizes DLL sideloading via a legitimate Canon-signed binary to execute shellcode hidden within a .WAV audio file.
- Victims are coerced into executing malicious PowerShell commands through fake Cloudflare CAPTCHA prompts, a technique known as ClickFix.
- The deployed remote access trojan (RAT) can capture camera and microphone input, steal browser data, and bypass security software like Microsoft Defender.
- Command-and-control communication is masked using DNS-over-HTTPS, allowing the malware to evade detection by standard network monitoring tools.

### Why it Matters
This campaign demonstrates how cybercriminals are increasingly weaponizing trusted AI platforms and legitimate hosting services to bypass traditional security filters. As these social engineering tactics evolve, users and organizations must remain vigilant against deceptive prompts that encourage manual execution of scripts or installers.
