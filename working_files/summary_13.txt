Should you wait for the Google Pixel 11? Here's everything you should consider and how I'd spend my money
============================================================

As Google prepares to launch the Pixel 11 series, consumers must decide whether to purchase discounted Pixel 10 models now or wait for the upcoming flagship's rumored hardware upgrades.

### Key Points
- The Google Pixel 11 is expected to launch with a $100 price increase across the lineup, starting at $899 for the base model.
- Leaked pricing suggests the Pixel 11 Pro will cost $1,099, while the Pixel 11 Pro XL will be priced at $1,299.
- The Pixel 10a is currently available for $424, offering a budget-friendly alternative to the upcoming flagship series.
- The Pixel 11 is rumored to feature the Tensor G6 chipset and a potential "Pixel Glow" rear lighting interface.
- Official details for the Pixel 11 will be revealed during the Made by Google event on Wednesday, August 12, at 3 p.m. PT.

### Why it Matters
Rising smartphone prices and the integration of AI features are changing traditional consumer buying habits, making the decision between current and next-generation hardware more complex. Evaluating these trade-offs helps users determine if the latest performance improvements and design novelties justify the higher entry costs of new flagship devices.
