Top Stories: Apple's 'Surprise and Shine' Event, Plus New Mac mini and Mac Studio
============================================================

Apple has announced a September 9 event to unveil the iPhone 18 Pro and its first foldable smartphone, alongside updates to the Mac mini and Mac Studio hardware.

### Key Points
- Apple’s "Surprise and Shine" event takes place September 9 at 10:00 a.m. PT at Apple Park in Cupertino.
- The new Mac mini features the M6 chip, offering 40% faster CPU performance and significantly improved AI task processing.
- Updated Mac Studio models now include M5 Max and M5 Ultra chips, Thunderbolt 5 support, and Wi-Fi 7 connectivity.
- Early reports suggest the foldable "iPhone Ultra" features a durable hinge and optimized app layouts, though it lacks a telephoto lens.
- Apple TV monthly subscription prices increased by $2 in the U.S., with corresponding hikes for the Apple One Individual bundle.

### Why it Matters
These hardware updates and the upcoming foldable iPhone launch signal Apple's aggressive push to maintain its premium market position through significant performance gains and new form factors. Meanwhile, the recurring price increases for Apple TV reflect a broader industry trend of streaming services raising costs to improve profitability and offset content production expenses.
