The New Non-Negotiable for Small Businesses? Knowing Your Cyber Risk. Here’s Where to Begin.
============================================================

Small and medium-sized enterprises are increasingly prioritizing proactive cybersecurity measures to ensure operational resilience and protect their long-term growth against the rising threat of digital attacks.

### Key Points
- Mastercard research indicates 46% of SMEs have experienced a cyberattack, with 18% of affected businesses filing for bankruptcy and 17% closing permanently.
- While 71% of small business owners prioritize cyber protection, only 37% currently utilize dedicated security tools to manage their digital risks.
- Many entrepreneurs lack the resources for specialized security teams, creating a need for integrated, accessible tools that simplify complex risk management.
- Mastercard has introduced MyCyberRisk, a proactive assessment tool integrated into The Mastercard Collection for Business, to help owners identify and address vulnerabilities.
- Modern business strategy is shifting from reactive recovery plans to building security directly into daily workflows to maintain customer trust and operational continuity.

### Why it Matters
Cybersecurity has evolved from a technical IT concern into a fundamental pillar of business sustainability and competitive growth for smaller organizations. By embedding security into existing digital tools, entrepreneurs can mitigate risks early, protecting their assets while maintaining the confidence necessary to scale their operations.
