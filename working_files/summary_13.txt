Workday is doing another round of layoffs
============================================================

Workday is laying off approximately 500 employees, representing 2.5% of its workforce, while simultaneously lowering its fiscal third-quarter earnings forecast due to ongoing corporate restructuring expenses.

### Key Points
- The job cuts primarily impact the company's product and technology departments to align with strategic growth priorities.
- This marks the second round of layoffs for the software-as-a-service provider this year, following a reduction of 400 roles in February.
- Workday reported over 21,000 employees as of January and intends to continue targeted hiring through fiscal 2027.
- Company shares have declined approximately 12% year to date amid broader investor concerns regarding the enterprise software market.
- Cofounder Aneel Bhusri currently leads the firm following the departure of former CEO Carl Eschenbach earlier this year.

### Why it Matters
These recurring workforce reductions highlight the ongoing pressure on enterprise software companies to manage costs while navigating investor skepticism regarding the long-term impact of artificial intelligence. The restructuring reflects a broader industry trend where established firms must balance profitability with the need to maintain competitive growth in a shifting technological landscape.
