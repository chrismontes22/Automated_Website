Azoma wants to help brands win the AI shopping race. See the pitch deck that helped it raise $11.2 million.
============================================================

AI commerce startup Azoma has secured $11.2 million in Series A funding to help consumer brands optimize their product visibility across emerging generative AI shopping assistants and platforms.

### Key Points
- Azoma raised $11.2 million in a funding round led by Puma Growth Partners, with participation from Dunnhumby Ventures and others.
- The company provides "agentic commerce optimization" to help brands like Unilever, L'Oréal, and Mars appear in results from ChatGPT, Gemini, and Alexa.
- Azoma utilizes "digital twin" technology to simulate how AI agents present products to specific consumer demographics.
- The startup plans to double its headcount to 100 employees and expand its tracking capabilities to include platforms like TikTok’s Shopping Assistant.
- Morgan Stanley projects that AI-powered shopping assistants could influence up to $385 billion in U.S. e-commerce sales by 2030.

### Why it Matters
As consumers increasingly rely on AI agents for product discovery, brands face a shrinking window of opportunity to influence purchasing decisions. Azoma’s technology addresses this shift by ensuring products remain discoverable in a landscape where generative AI is rapidly replacing traditional search engines.
