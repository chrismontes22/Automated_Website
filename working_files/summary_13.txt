I almost bought a MacBook Neo until I saw what $600 buys used
============================================================

The Dell Latitude 7320 offers a powerful, upgradeable alternative to the MacBook Neo for users seeking versatile port selection, Linux compatibility, and high-performance hardware on a budget.

### Key Points
- The Dell Latitude 7320 features an 11th Gen Intel Core i7 processor, 32 GB of RAM, and 1 TB of user-replaceable storage.
- Connectivity options include dual Thunderbolt ports, HDMI, USB-A, a microSD card slot, and a WWAN module for cellular data.
- Unlike the MacBook Neo, the Latitude 7320 supports Linux distributions like Arch and allows for component upgrades such as storage and batteries.
- The MacBook Neo maintains advantages in display quality, battery life, and the inclusion of a manufacturer warranty for new units.
- Used enterprise laptops like the Latitude 7320 provide significant value for power users, though they often lack the premium screen panels found in newer consumer models.

### Why it Matters
Choosing between a new entry-level laptop and a used enterprise machine highlights the trade-off between modern convenience and hardware flexibility. While new devices offer better displays and warranties, older business-grade laptops provide superior port variety and upgrade paths that extend the machine's functional lifespan for professional or technical users.
