Attacks on Tech Firms Behind Most Third-Party Data Breach Victims
============================================================

A new Kroll report reveals that 69% of data breach victims were impacted by third-party incidents, with technology companies serving as the primary source of these widespread compromises.

### Key Points
- Technology firms accounted for 79% of third-party data breaches between June 2025 and June 2026.
- Finance and professional services were the most frequently breached sectors, each representing 20% of total incidents.
- While technology companies caused 60% of all victim cases, they represented only 6% of total breach incidents.
- 69% of surveyed security leaders reported at least one organizational breach within the past 12 months.
- Cybercriminal groups, including the Scattered Spider collective, frequently target IT service providers to access downstream customer systems.

### Why it Matters
The data highlights a significant supply chain risk where a single compromise at a technology vendor can trigger a domino effect across multiple industries. Organizations must now prioritize third-party risk management to protect their data from vulnerabilities inherent in their software and service provider ecosystems.
