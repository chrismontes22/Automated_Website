Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang says cybersecurity is AI's next blockbuster app
============================================================

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang identifies cybersecurity as the next major application for artificial intelligence, predicting that continuous automated threat detection will become a significant market for the industry.

### Key Points
- Jensen Huang stated that AI-driven bug detection and cybersecurity are natural, high-growth extensions of current software coding capabilities.
- Unlike prompt-based coding assistants, AI cybersecurity systems will operate continuously, creating a recurring revenue model for technology providers.
- Nvidia is actively expanding its presence in the sector through strategic partnerships with CrowdStrike, Cisco, and Palantir.
- OpenAI CFO Sarah Friar confirmed that her company also views cybersecurity as a primary commercial opportunity for artificial intelligence.

### Why it Matters
This shift signals a transition toward AI tools that provide persistent, around-the-clock infrastructure protection rather than just task-based assistance. As major tech firms prioritize these security integrations, businesses may soon rely on autonomous systems to defend against increasingly sophisticated digital threats.
