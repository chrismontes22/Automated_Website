Here’s What to Expect From AI’s Two Biggest 2026 Spenders in 2027
============================================================

Amazon and Alphabet are investing nearly $400 billion in AI infrastructure during 2026, shifting the industry focus toward which company will generate durable free cash flow by 2027.

### Key Points
- Amazon and Alphabet are on track to spend approximately $200 billion and $180 billion respectively on capital expenditures throughout 2026.
- AWS reported 37% year-over-year revenue growth, while Google Cloud experienced an 82% increase during the same period.
- Amazon’s strategy centers on a "bring-your-own-model" approach via Bedrock, whereas Alphabet utilizes a vertically integrated model featuring proprietary TPUs and Gemini AI.
- Alphabet’s long-term debt rose to $98.2 billion as it funded infrastructure expansion, leading to a temporary suspension of share buybacks.
- Amazon CEO Andy Jassy noted that data-center servers typically require nearly three years to reach a break-even point.

### Why it Matters
The massive capital expenditure cycle highlights a critical divergence in business models between the two tech giants as they race to monetize AI infrastructure. Investors are now closely monitoring whether Amazon’s flexible cloud ecosystem or Alphabet’s vertically integrated stack will prove more efficient at converting heavy spending into long-term profitability.
