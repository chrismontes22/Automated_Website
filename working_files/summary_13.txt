Trump gets richer while gas prices soar
============================================================

Chinese scientists have achieved a breakthrough in hydrogen extraction efficiency, highlighting a global contrast as the U.S. faces a severe energy crisis amid rising fuel costs.

### Key Points
- Chinese researchers improved hydrogen and fresh water extraction efficiency by 15%, according to a study published in Nature Energy on Sept. 15.
- U.S. diesel prices reached a record $6.52 per gallon on Sept. 22, contributing to the bankruptcy of at least 16 trucking companies.
- The Trump administration’s attempts to lower prices, including promoting red-dyed diesel and purchasing Russian oil, have faced skepticism from industry experts.
- Major Western oil companies, including BP, Chevron, and ExxonMobil, are projected to report combined third-quarter profits of approximately $53 billion.
- President Trump held up to $61.1 million in oil and gas stocks as of 2025, benefiting financially from the market surge caused by the conflict with Iran.

### Why it Matters
The ongoing energy crisis and the administration's reliance on fossil fuel expansion over renewable alternatives have created significant economic strain for U.S. consumers and businesses. This situation raises concerns about the influence of personal financial interests on national policy and the long-term stability of the domestic energy market.
