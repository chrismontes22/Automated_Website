Apple’s $1,999 iPhone Duo Costs the Same as a Budget MacBook, iPhone, Watch, and AirPods Combined
============================================================

Apple’s new $1,999 iPhone Duo foldable highlights a record price gap, as the flagship device now costs more than a combined bundle of four essential Apple hardware products.

### Key Points
- The iPhone Duo features a 2nm A20 Pro chip, titanium frame, and advanced folding hinge, justifying its $1,999 price point.
- A $1,996 alternative bundle includes a 512GB MacBook Neo, iPhone 17e, Apple Watch SE 3, and AirPods Pro 3.
- Apple’s pricing strategy has diverged, with the MacBook Neo optimized for affordability and the iPhone Duo positioned as a premium luxury item.
- The company’s current lineup lacks mid-range iPhone options, as standard models were reportedly delayed until spring 2027.
- The iPhone Duo is priced $100 higher than the competing Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8, which retails for $1,899.

### Why it Matters
This pricing inversion signals a shift in Apple’s product strategy, where the company is simultaneously targeting budget-conscious consumers and luxury buyers while abandoning the traditional mid-range market. For consumers, this creates a unique dilemma where purchasing a complete ecosystem of devices may offer more practical value than investing in a single, high-end folding smartphone.
