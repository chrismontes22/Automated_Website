AI Has a Cost Most Companies Aren’t Accounting for Yet. Here’s What They’re Missing.
============================================================

Over-reliance on generative AI tools can erode human critical thinking and judgment, potentially causing organizations to lose their competitive edge by converging on a predictable machine-generated average.

### Key Points
- Research from MIT, Microsoft, and Carnegie Mellon indicates that excessive trust in AI outputs leads to decreased neural engagement and diminished critical thinking skills.
- Organizations risk losing unconventional ideas and dissent when employees routinely accept AI-generated first drafts as the standard for decision-making.
- The author argues that the "zeroth step" of AI adoption is establishing clear guidelines for how humans should think alongside machines, rather than focusing solely on data governance.
- Recommended strategies include mandating independent verification of AI outputs and rewarding employees for challenging or rejecting machine-generated recommendations.
- Companies are advised to stop equating high token usage with productivity, as this metric does not reflect the quality of human judgment or creative output.

### Why it Matters
The shift toward machine-driven decision-making threatens to hollow out the unique human expertise that drives innovation and long-term business value. By prioritizing critical oversight over automated convenience, companies can ensure that AI remains a tool for enhancement rather than a replacement for essential human judgment.
