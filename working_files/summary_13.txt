Don’t overlook Elektron’s budget electronic music instruments
============================================================

Elektron’s Model:Samples and Model:Cycles grooveboxes offer an accessible, budget-friendly entry point into electronic music production, featuring powerful sequencing capabilities that often outperform more expensive, complex hardware alternatives.

### Key Points
- The Model:Cycles utilizes FM synthesis, while the Model:Samples allows users to load audio files via USB for playback.
- Both devices feature six-track sequencers with "trigs" and "locks," enabling complex, evolving arrangements within a 64-step pattern.
- The hardware includes six velocity-sensitive pads and dedicated knobs for hands-on control, prioritizing live performance over deep menu diving.
- Advanced features like "Control-all" allow users to manipulate all tracks simultaneously for creative sound design and chaotic breakdowns.
- These units are priced at $349 new, with secondary market prices on platforms like Reverb or Craigslist often dropping as low as $200.

### Why it Matters
These grooveboxes provide a high-value alternative for beginners who might otherwise be intimidated by the complex workflows of premium electronic music hardware. By balancing ease of use with deep sequencing features, they remain relevant tools for both novice producers and experienced musicians seeking a portable, performance-oriented instrument.
