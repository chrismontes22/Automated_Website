Elon Musk’s SpaceX reports losses but less than expected
============================================================

SpaceX reported a $541 million loss in its first quarterly earnings as a public company, though revenue surged to $7.8 billion, significantly exceeding analyst expectations for the period.

### Key Points
- Quarterly revenue reached $7.8 billion, marking a 90 percent increase compared to the same period last year.
- The company secured $6 billion in new U.S. government contracts for its Starshield national security satellite system.
- SpaceX reported $100 billion in cash reserves following its record-breaking initial public offering earlier this year.
- Capital expenditures totaled $18.37 billion, driven by investments in Starlink, Starship development, and new AI infrastructure.
- A partnership with Nvidia was announced to integrate specialized chips into upcoming Starmind orbital compute satellites.

### Why it Matters
These results highlight the company's aggressive transition toward becoming an AI-first enterprise while balancing the high costs of its space-based infrastructure. Investors remain focused on whether SpaceX can achieve long-term profitability as it scales its satellite network and navigates regulatory challenges surrounding its AI technologies.
