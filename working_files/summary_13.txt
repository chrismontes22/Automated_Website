Apple launches second legal challenge to UK iCloud backdoor order, per report
============================================================

Apple has filed a legal challenge with the Investigatory Powers Tribunal to contest a British government mandate requiring the company to weaken encryption for UK iCloud users.

### Key Points
- Apple initiated the legal complaint in July 2025 to oppose government demands for access to encrypted cloud backups.
- The dispute centers on the UK government's request to bypass Advanced Data Protection, which currently prevents Apple from accessing user encryption keys.
- Apple has restricted new UK users from enabling Advanced Data Protection while the legal proceedings remain ongoing.
- The original 2025 order initially targeted global users before being narrowed to focus specifically on British citizens following international pressure.
- Apple maintains its policy of refusing to build backdoors or master keys into its security architecture for any government entity.

### Why it Matters
This legal battle highlights the ongoing tension between national security surveillance requirements and the privacy protections offered by end-to-end encryption. The outcome could set a significant precedent for how global technology companies manage data security standards when faced with conflicting international regulatory demands.
