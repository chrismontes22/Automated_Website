DHQ alerts public over attempts to breach official X account
============================================================

The Nigerian Defence Headquarters has launched a cybersecurity investigation after detecting an unauthorized attempt to compromise its official X account, prompting a temporary warning to the public.

### Key Points
- The Defence Headquarters (DHQ) identified an unauthorized access attempt on its official X account.
- Major General Samaila Uba confirmed that technical measures are currently active to secure the platform.
- The military has advised the public to disregard any suspicious or misleading content posted during this security breach.
- Official updates will continue to be shared via the DHQ’s verified Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp channels.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the ongoing vulnerability of high-profile government accounts to cyber threats and potential misinformation campaigns. Maintaining secure communication channels is critical for the military to ensure public trust and the dissemination of accurate operational information.
