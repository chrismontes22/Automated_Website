The computer hacker who tried to take down Man City
============================================================

Rui Pinto, the Portuguese hacker behind the Football Leaks website, remains a polarizing figure whose unauthorized disclosure of confidential documents triggered major financial investigations into elite European football clubs.

### Key Points
- Rui Pinto launched Football Leaks in 2015, exposing over three terabytes of internal data from clubs, agents, and players.
- The leaked documents provided the foundation for the Premier League’s investigation into Manchester City regarding 115 alleged breaches of financial rules.
- Pinto has faced multiple legal proceedings, receiving suspended sentences in Portugal and France for crimes including illegal data access and attempted extortion.
- Supporters, including former politicians, argue Pinto is a vital whistleblower, while critics and legal representatives for clubs label him a criminal hacker.
- Pinto currently lives under witness protection due to the sensitive nature of his disclosures and the powerful entities affected by his actions.

### Why it Matters
Pinto’s actions have fundamentally altered the landscape of professional football by forcing unprecedented transparency regarding club finances and regulatory compliance. His case highlights the ongoing tension between the illegal acquisition of data and the public interest in exposing corruption within the global sports industry.
