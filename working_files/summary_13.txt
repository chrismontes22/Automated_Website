The unusually muted Tesla Cybercab launch
============================================================

Tesla officially unveiled its driverless Cybercab at a private event in Austin, Texas, marking a significant milestone in Elon Musk’s long-term strategy to pivot toward autonomous vehicle technology.

### Key Points
- The Cybercab is a two-seat, driverless sedan featuring gullwing doors, eight cameras, and no traditional steering wheel or pedals.
- Technical specifications include a 219-horsepower motor, a 48kWh battery, and a curb weight of 3,113 pounds, making it Tesla’s most range-efficient vehicle.
- Tesla currently has 45 Cybercabs registered in Texas, contributing to a total fleet of 256 unsupervised autonomous vehicles across six cities.
- The company has not announced a public release date or consumer pricing for the vehicle, despite previous suggestions of a $30,000 price point.
- Tesla faces stiff competition from Alphabet’s Waymo, which currently operates approximately 4,000 unsupervised vehicles across 14 cities.

### Why it Matters
The successful deployment of a robotaxi fleet is central to justifying Tesla’s $1.18 trillion valuation as the company faces slowing electric vehicle sales and increased global competition. This launch represents a high-stakes transition for the firm as it attempts to move from traditional automotive manufacturing into the competitive and heavily regulated autonomous ride-hailing market.
