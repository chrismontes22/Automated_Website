OLED MacBook Pro to help boost premium display shipments, per report
============================================================

Apple’s anticipated adoption of OLED technology in upcoming MacBook Pro models is projected to drive a 50% surge in notebook display shipments throughout 2026, according to Counterpoint Research.

### Key Points
- Counterpoint Research forecasts an 11% growth in advanced notebook display shipments for 2026, despite a projected 3% decline in the broader notebook market.
- OLED technology is expected to capture 75% of the advanced notebook display market by 2026, significantly outpacing MiniLED’s projected 25% share.
- Apple is rumored to integrate tandem OLED architecture into new 14-inch and 16-inch MacBook Pro models to enhance brightness and power efficiency.
- OLED notebook panel shipments are forecast to maintain a 26% compounded annual growth rate through 2030, with annual revenue growth projected at 35%.

### Why it Matters
The shift toward OLED displays signals a major transition in premium hardware standards driven by Apple’s supply chain influence and the rising demand for AI-capable PCs. This trend will likely force competitors to adopt tandem OLED architectures to remain relevant in the high-end laptop market.
