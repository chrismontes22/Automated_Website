Nvidia Soars 7% In Premarket As It Leads Upswing In Chip Stocks After Strong Earnings
============================================================

Nvidia shares surged over 7% in premarket trading after the company reported strong quarterly earnings and projected a 70% revenue increase for the upcoming fiscal year.

### Key Points
- Nvidia reported $96.2 billion in second-quarter revenue, marking a 106% year-over-year increase that exceeded Wall Street expectations.
- CEO Jensen Huang stated that the projected 70% sales growth is limited by supply chain constraints rather than a lack of market demand.
- The positive earnings report triggered gains for other chipmakers, including Intel, Broadcom, AMD, and Micron.
- Asian semiconductor manufacturers, such as SK Hynix, Samsung, and Kioxia, also saw share prices rise following the announcement.
- Nasdaq Futures and S&P 500 futures climbed as investor anxiety regarding a potential slowdown in AI compute demand subsided.

### Why it Matters
Nvidia’s performance serves as a critical bellwether for the broader artificial intelligence sector and global semiconductor market. The company's ability to maintain high growth rates helps stabilize investor confidence in the long-term viability of AI infrastructure spending.
