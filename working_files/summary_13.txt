Forget Figma and Miro: I turned my Obsidian vault into a whiteboard with one plugin
============================================================

The Excalidraw plugin for Obsidian offers a versatile, local-first whiteboarding solution that bridges the gap between brainstorming sketches and structured note-taking for improved design and research workflows.

### Key Points
- The Excalidraw plugin, developed by Zsolt Viczian, integrates a hand-drawn style whiteboard directly into the Obsidian note-taking environment.
- Unlike cloud-based tools like Figma or Miro, Excalidraw files are stored locally as Markdown and JSON, allowing for seamless linking and embedding of research notes.
- The tool supports advanced features including Mermaid-to-Excalidraw conversion, custom shape libraries, and optional AI integration with providers like OpenAI, Claude, and Gemini.
- Drawings function as standard Markdown files, enabling users to add frontmatter, tags, and text transclusion while maintaining visibility in the Obsidian graph view.
- The plugin is designed for early-stage ideation, such as lo-fi wireframing, mind mapping, and affinity diagramming, rather than high-fidelity team collaboration.

### Why it Matters
Integrating whiteboarding directly into a personal knowledge base allows users to maintain context between rough brainstorming sessions and final project documentation. This local-first approach eliminates the friction of exporting files between disparate apps, ensuring that research notes and visual diagrams remain interconnected and accessible to AI-driven analysis tools.
