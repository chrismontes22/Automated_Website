Apple Limiting U.S. iPhone Duo Orders to Two Per Customer
============================================================

Apple has implemented a two-unit purchase limit for the new iPhone Duo in the United States, restricting customers from buying more than two devices per order online.

### Key Points
- Apple’s U.S. website and app now automatically block orders exceeding two iPhone Duo units per customer.
- Similar purchase restrictions were previously confirmed for customers in China, Singapore, and Hong Kong.
- Initial launch day inventory for the iPhone Duo sold out within minutes of the October 16 pre-order launch.
- Some customers who secured early pre-orders are seeing their estimated delivery dates moved up from November or December.
- Reports from users remain inconsistent regarding whether this policy was enforced at the start of the pre-order period.

### Why it Matters
This purchase cap suggests that Apple is managing tight supply chain constraints to prevent bulk buying and ensure broader product availability for individual consumers. The move reflects ongoing efforts to stabilize inventory distribution as the company works to fulfill high demand and improve delivery timelines.
