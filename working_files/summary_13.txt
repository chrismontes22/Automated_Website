iPhone Duo reviews: The absolute best foldable phone, but not without compromise
============================================================

The new iPhone Duo has received widespread praise as the most polished foldable smartphone available, though reviewers highlight significant trade-offs regarding weight, camera capabilities, and software optimization.

### Key Points
- The iPhone Duo features a refined hinge and a nano-texture display that minimizes the screen crease more effectively than competing foldable devices.
- Early adopters face compromises, including the absence of Face ID, a lack of a telephoto lens, and limited third-party app optimization for the dual-screen layout.
- Stress testing by HTX Studio showed the crease depth increases from 48 microns to 167 microns after 3,000 folds, while hinges may become stiffer over time.
- Critics note that the device’s aspect ratio forces users to choose between a narrow outer screen or a tablet-like inner display, creating a zero-sum user experience.
- High demand has led to backorders extending into December and January, providing developers additional time to update applications for the unique form factor.

### Why it Matters
The iPhone Duo represents Apple’s first entry into the foldable market, signaling a major shift in the company's hardware strategy after years of standard slab-style designs. While the device generates significant industry buzz, its status as a first-generation product suggests that mainstream adoption may depend on future iterations addressing weight, battery life, and camera limitations.
