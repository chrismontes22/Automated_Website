I'm a VC who finds $120 worth of AI subscriptions more productive than an intern. This is my tech stack.
============================================================

Marc Palet, a 26-year-old venture capitalist at OMVC, leverages a sophisticated AI-driven tech stack to automate operational tasks and manage investments across the Asia Pacific region.

### Key Points
- Marc Palet manages investments for OMVC in Singapore, utilizing AI tools to handle workflows as the firm's sole employee outside the United States.
- His monthly software expenditure exceeds $120, covering subscriptions for Claude, ChatGPT, n8n, and various transcription APIs.
- He uses Fireflies to record founder meetings, which are then automatically summarized into structured investment notes covering market and due diligence data.
- Palet built a custom "company brain" using Claude Code that automatically ingests and organizes data from Gmail, Slack, and podcasts into portfolio-specific pages.
- AI automation has increased his content output from one deep-dive article every two months to one every three weeks, directly boosting deal flow.

### Why it Matters
This approach demonstrates how individual venture capitalists can use AI agents to achieve the operational capacity of a larger team without increasing headcount. By automating routine due diligence and reporting, investors can dedicate more time to high-value activities like networking and in-person meetings.
