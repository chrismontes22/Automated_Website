Salesforce Alternatives for Behavioral Health
============================================================

Behavioral health organizations are increasingly moving away from Salesforce, as the platform’s generic sales-focused architecture creates significant operational, financial, and regulatory hurdles for addiction treatment providers.

### Key Points
- Salesforce lacks native support for critical behavioral health requirements like ASAM level-of-care assessments, bed utilization tracking, and 42 CFR Part 2 compliance.
- Implementing Salesforce for clinical use requires expensive custom development and complex HIPAA configurations, often referred to as a "configuration tax."
- Health Cloud pricing starts at approximately $300 per user per month, which often fails to scale effectively for smaller or rural treatment centers.
- Purpose-built platforms like HITJMS integrate CRM, clinical documentation, and billing into a single environment designed specifically for addiction treatment workflows.
- Switching to specialized software can improve admissions velocity, referral source attribution, and staff efficiency by automating manual tasks like insurance verification.

### Why it Matters
Relying on a generic CRM for behavioral health creates fragmented workflows that increase administrative burden and clinical risk. Transitioning to a purpose-built platform allows facilities to prioritize patient care and regulatory compliance while reducing the overhead costs associated with retrofitting enterprise sales software.
