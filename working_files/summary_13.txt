The Samsung News app is being discontinued, and there's no replacement yet
============================================================

Samsung will officially discontinue its native Samsung News app and associated home screen widgets on December 21, 2026, marking the end of the company's integrated news-feed service.

### Key Points
- Samsung News will cease operations globally on December 21, 2026, at 00:00 UTC.
- The shutdown includes the removal of the dedicated app, the home screen widget, and the media page accessed by swiping right.
- Samsung has begun issuing shutdown notifications to users in India and South Korea.
- All user data, including account profiles and personalization settings, will be permanently deleted following the service closure.
- The platform, which launched in April 2023, follows a history of discontinued predecessors including Samsung Free, Samsung Daily, and Bixby Home.

### Why it Matters
This move signals a shift in Samsung’s software strategy as the company moves away from maintaining proprietary news aggregation services on its mobile devices. Users will likely transition to third-party alternatives like Google Discover, reflecting a broader industry trend toward streamlining native software experiences.
