Trump says he’s creating an AI force and appointing a czar amid concerns over the rapidly developing technology
============================================================

President Donald Trump announced plans to appoint an artificial intelligence czar and establish a federal AI task force to oversee industry growth while maintaining national technological competitiveness.

### Key Points
- President Trump intends to form an "AI Force" to monitor the industry, drawing a parallel to the existing U.S. Space Force.
- The administration aims to prioritize U.S. leadership in AI development to maintain a strategic advantage over China.
- Nobel laureate Geoffrey Hinton recently warned Congress that lawmakers have approximately one year to implement effective industry regulations.
- California Governor Gavin Newsom signed an executive order requiring state task forces to evaluate the implementation of AI kill switches.
- Former AI and cryptocurrency czar David Sacks, who previously stepped down, remains an advisor and is expected to attend a state dinner with Xi Jinping.

### Why it Matters
This initiative signals a shift toward centralized federal oversight of artificial intelligence as the administration balances rapid technological innovation with national security concerns. The move highlights the growing tension between industry leaders, who fear stifling growth, and experts calling for urgent regulatory frameworks to mitigate existential risks.
