Here’s how much it costs to repair the new Apple HomeView
============================================================

Apple has released official service and repair pricing for its new $299 HomeView smart display, revealing that out-of-warranty repairs will cost customers nearly the price of a new unit.

### Key Points
- The Apple HomeView and HomeView (Wall Mounted) models are available for pre-order now, with official shipments scheduled to begin on November 13.
- Out-of-warranty service for either smart display model is priced at $279, suggesting Apple may opt for full-unit replacements rather than individual component repairs.
- Customers with AppleCare+ coverage can access accidental damage repairs for a reduced service fee of $39 per incident.
- AppleCare+ for the HomeView is available for purchase at $2.49 per month or $24.99 for an annual subscription.
- The wall-mounted version of the device does not include a power adapter or mounting hardware, which must be purchased separately by the user.

### Why it Matters
The high cost of out-of-warranty repairs relative to the device's retail price highlights the importance of considering AppleCare+ for long-term ownership. This pricing structure suggests that Apple intends to treat the HomeView as a modular unit that is replaced rather than serviced, which could influence consumer purchasing decisions and repair expectations.
