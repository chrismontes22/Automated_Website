MarketBeat Week in Review – 09/07 - 09/11
============================================================

Investors are navigating a volatile week as rising oil prices and anticipation of the Federal Reserve’s upcoming interest rate decision weigh on broader market sentiment and stock performance.

### Key Points
- Oracle (ORCL) earnings highlighted the continued strength of the artificial intelligence infrastructure sector despite broader market headwinds.
- Tesla (TSLA) faced scrutiny following its Cybercab launch, while Apple (AAPL) saw mixed reactions to its foldable iPhone Duo preview.
- Palantir (PLTR) expanded its defense industry footprint through a new U.S. Army contract, positioning itself as a key systems integrator.
- Revolution Medicines (RVMD) gained momentum after receiving FDA approval for a clinical-stage oncology drug targeting metastatic pancreatic cancer.
- Meta Platforms (META) reached an $18 billion settlement regarding a social media addiction trial, which analysts suggest may clear the path for future growth.

### Why it Matters
Market participants are currently balancing strong corporate earnings reports against macroeconomic uncertainty driven by inflation and interest rate policy. Understanding these specific company catalysts is essential for identifying growth opportunities as investors prepare for the upcoming third-quarter earnings season.
