What is MATCHBOIL? The Russia-aligned malware that installs a spying backdoor
============================================================

Researchers at ESET have identified MATCHBOIL, a sophisticated downloader used by the Russia-aligned group UAC-0099 to deploy espionage backdoors against critical infrastructure organizations throughout Ukraine.

### Key Points
- The UAC-0099 group uses spear-phishing emails to deliver MATCHBOIL, which subsequently installs a C# backdoor known as MATCHWOK for desktop surveillance and command execution.
- Victims identified by ESET include Ukrainian transportation, manufacturing, and energy companies targeted between July 2025 and June 2026.
- MATCHBOIL employs advanced evasion techniques, including sandbox detection based on system uptime and OS installation age, alongside commercial code obfuscation via Eziriz .NET Reactor.
- The malware maintains persistence on Windows systems by creating scheduled tasks or registry keys, often masquerading as legitimate-sounding applications like SMTPClient.
- UAC-0099 functions as an initial access broker, potentially providing network footholds to other advanced persistent threat actors such as the Russia-aligned group Sandworm.

### Why it Matters
The expansion of UAC-0099’s targeting into critical infrastructure sectors suggests a strategic effort to maximize disruption within Ukraine. By acting as an access broker, this group increases the risk to organizations by facilitating follow-on attacks from more sophisticated state-sponsored threat actors.
