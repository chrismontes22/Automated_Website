Longing for a Time Before Smartphones
============================================================

Gen Z is increasingly embracing analog nostalgia and pre-digital habits as a way to reclaim agency and community in an era dominated by constant smartphone and social media use.

### Key Points
- Research indicates that approximately 60% of Gen Z adults express a desire to return to a time before society was "plugged in."
- Psychologists define this trend as a self-regulatory resource that helps individuals navigate modern anxiety and uncertainty.
- Popular analog trends among younger generations include the use of digital cameras, vinyl records, physical books, and retro gaming consoles.
- Experts suggest that this behavior is not a rejection of progress, but a creative effort to balance convenience with intentional, in-person human connection.
- The podcast *How to Touch Grass*, hosted by Julie Beck and Natalie Brennan, explores these shifting relationships with technology.

### Why it Matters
This cultural shift highlights a growing desire for more meaningful, tactile experiences in a world where digital algorithms often dictate social interaction. By seeking out analog alternatives, younger generations are attempting to renegotiate their relationship with technology to prioritize personal control and genuine community.
