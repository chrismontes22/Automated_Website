Netskope Q2 Earnings Call Highlights
============================================================

Netskope reported strong fiscal 2027 second-quarter results, exceeding revenue and margin guidance as enterprise demand for its cloud-native security platform and new AI-focused product suite continues to accelerate.

### Key Points
- Annual recurring revenue (ARR) grew 27% year over year to $899 million, with total revenue reaching $220.5 million.
- The company improved its adjusted operating margin by 11 percentage points year over year to negative 9%.
- Adoption of the Netskope One platform increased, with 59% of customers now utilizing four or more products.
- New AI security tools, including AI Guardrails and Agentic Broker, are seeing significant traction with one-third of the pipeline in proof-of-concept stages.
- Netskope raised its full-year fiscal 2027 revenue guidance to a range of $888 million to $892 million.
- The company reduced its workforce by 5% to reallocate resources toward AI infrastructure and research and development.

### Why it Matters
Netskope’s performance highlights a growing enterprise trend of prioritizing secure AI integration without compromising network performance. By successfully linking security and network modernization, the company is positioning itself as a critical partner for organizations navigating the complexities of AI adoption.
