AirPods 5 vs. AirPods Pro 3 Buyer's Guide: Which Should You Buy?
============================================================

Apple has released the 2026 AirPods 5 lineup, introducing Active Noise Cancellation to the entry-level $129 model while maintaining a clear feature distinction from the premium AirPods Pro 3.

### Key Points
- The base AirPods 5 start at $129, while the $149 version adds wireless charging, volume controls, and a Find My speaker.
- AirPods Pro 3, priced at $249, feature a sealed in-ear fit and noise cancellation performance rated up to three times more effective.
- Battery life for the AirPods 5 ranges from 4 to 5 hours, compared to 8 hours for the AirPods Pro 3.
- The Pro 3 model includes advanced health features like hearing tests, hearing aid functionality, and heart rate sensing for workouts.
- Both AirPods 5 models utilize an open-ear design, whereas the Pro 3 uses silicone ear tips in five different sizes.

### Why it Matters
These new models narrow the feature gap for casual listeners while preserving the AirPods Pro 3 as the superior choice for users prioritizing noise isolation and health tracking. Understanding these differences helps consumers decide if the premium price is justified by the Pro model's specialized features or if the standard AirPods 5 meet their daily needs.
