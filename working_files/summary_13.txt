GTA 6 leaker CyberLeek is back with another video and this time, Lucia's naked
============================================================

Leaker Cyberleek has released additional unauthorized footage of Grand Theft Auto 6, featuring the character Lucia in various in-game scenarios just weeks before the title's official November launch.

### Key Points
- The hacker Cyberleek released multiple clips showing the character Lucia, including scenes of her walking through Vice City and performing a parachute jump.
- The footage appears to utilize a developer menu, suggesting the character's state may be a debug feature rather than standard gameplay content.
- Rockstar Games and parent company Take-Two Interactive are actively investigating the source of these leaks, which they previously described as heartbreaking.
- Grand Theft Auto 6 is scheduled for release on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S on November 19.

### Why it Matters
These recurring leaks highlight ongoing security challenges for Rockstar Games as they approach the highly anticipated release of one of the industry's most significant titles. The situation underscores the difficulty major publishers face in protecting intellectual property and maintaining controlled marketing rollouts in the digital age.
