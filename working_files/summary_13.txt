These Founders Are Betting Your Bed Will Diagnose You Before Your Doctor Does
============================================================

Eight Sleep, a health-tech company co-founded by Alexandra Zatarain and Matteo Franceschetti, is transforming the mattress industry by utilizing biometric data to optimize sleep and provide medical diagnostics.

### Key Points
- Eight Sleep reached a $1.5 billion valuation in March 2026 following a $50 million funding round led by Tether Investments.
- The company has achieved profitability with cumulative Pod sales approaching $1 billion across 37 countries.
- The newly launched Pod 6 features nine times more sensors than previous models and offers faster temperature regulation.
- The firm is currently pursuing FDA clearance to use its technology to detect and mitigate sleep apnea.
- Specialized software modes, including Pregnancy Mode and Hot Flash Mode, are designed to address specific hormonal and physiological sleep disruptions.

### Why it Matters
By shifting the focus from traditional furniture to "sleep fitness" and medical intervention, Eight Sleep is positioning the bed as a critical diagnostic tool for preventative healthcare. This evolution from a consumer product to a data-driven health device signals a significant shift in how wearable technology and home infrastructure can integrate to monitor long-term wellness.
