Inside DAZN’s Push Into the U.S. Sports Media Rights Race
============================================================

Streaming platform DAZN is aggressively expanding into the U.S. market by securing regional media rights for various NBA, NHL, and MLB franchises following recent industry-wide broadcast instability.

### Key Points
- DAZN signed a multi-year deal with MSG Networks and YES Network to stream non-national games for the Knicks, Rangers, Islanders, Devils, Sabres, Yankees, and Nets.
- The company secured exclusive local broadcast rights for the Minnesota Timberwolves, marking its first team-specific agreement in the United States.
- DAZN is reportedly in negotiations with the Cleveland Cavaliers, Indiana Pacers, San Antonio Spurs, and Memphis Grizzlies to further expand its regional sports footprint.
- The platform plans to offer interactive features, including real-time statistics and alternate broadcast feeds, alongside a "freemium" tier featuring 15 live games per season.
- DAZN aims to establish itself as a viable partner for the NBA’s planned centralized local broadcast hub, which is expected to launch for the 2027-28 season.

### Why it Matters
DAZN’s entry into the U.S. market capitalizes on the collapse of regional sports networks to provide a new direct-to-consumer alternative for professional sports fans. By offering flexible, tech-forward streaming solutions, the company hopes to stabilize the fragmented local media landscape while proving its value to major leagues before future centralized broadcast shifts.
