I did everything to hide my browsing from my ISP, and it could still see most of where I went
============================================================

Internet service providers can still track user activity through DNS lookups, Server Name Indication fields, and destination IP addresses, even when standard privacy settings and encryption are enabled.

### Key Points
- Internet Service Providers (ISPs) can monitor unencrypted DNS lookups and identify websites via Server Name Indication (SNI) during the initial TLS handshake.
- A 2023 research paper demonstrated that website fingerprinting can identify user activity with 95% accuracy by analyzing the first 50 packets of encrypted DNS traffic.
- Encrypted Client Hello (ECH), standardized in March 2026, mitigates privacy leaks by using decoy messages to hide hostnames from network observers.
- Browser-based HTTPS-only modes and encrypted DNS providers like Cloudflare or Quad9 provide essential, though incomplete, layers of protection against ISP tracking.
- Virtual Private Networks (VPNs) remain the most effective method for obscuring traffic, as they route DNS, SNI, and destination data through an encrypted tunnel.

### Why it Matters
While individual privacy settings improve security, they cannot fully prevent ISPs from observing metadata and destination traffic patterns. Relying on a VPN is currently the only way to comprehensively mask online activity from service providers.
