Trump, House Speaker Johnson expected to meet with tech CEOs on AI next week: Sources
============================================================

President Donald Trump and House Speaker Mike Johnson are scheduled to meet with prominent technology executives on September 29 to discuss the future of artificial intelligence regulation.

### Key Points
- The meeting aims to address industry concerns regarding AI safety and potential government oversight of the rapidly evolving technology sector.
- Leaders from major firms, including OpenAI and Anthropic, have recently advocated for slowing development to mitigate existential risks posed by advanced AI systems.
- President Trump has publicly dismissed warnings about AI-driven threats as a hoax while simultaneously proposing the creation of an "AI Force" and a new AI czar.
- Speaker Mike Johnson supports the dialogue to determine the appropriate balance between corporate responsibility and federal legislative action.
- The House of Representatives is currently in recess until after the midterm elections, complicating immediate legislative efforts to regulate the industry.

### Why it Matters
This meeting highlights a significant ideological divide between industry leaders seeking safety guardrails and political leadership skeptical of the necessity for strict government intervention. The outcome could shape the future of federal AI policy and influence how the United States balances technological innovation with national security concerns.
