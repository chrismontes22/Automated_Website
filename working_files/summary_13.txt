Apple Store is down ahead of iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone Duo foldable and more announcements
============================================================

Apple has taken its online store offline ahead of today’s September event, where CEO John Ternus will debut the foldable iPhone Duo, iPhone 18 Pro, and new wearables.

### Key Points
- Apple is launching the foldable iPhone Duo, featuring a 7.8-inch inner display and a custom-designed hinge to minimize screen creasing.
- The iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max will introduce new processors, an improved camera system, a cherry red color, and a narrower Dynamic Island.
- Starting prices for the iPhone 18 Pro lineup are expected to increase by approximately $100.
- The event includes the unveiling of Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4, which feature new AI-driven health tracking and daily recap capabilities.
- Apple is shifting its release schedule, moving the launch of entry-level iPhone models to the spring instead of the traditional fall window.
- Fifth-generation AirPods are also expected to be announced during the presentation at Apple’s Cupertino headquarters.

### Why it Matters
This event marks a significant strategic shift for Apple under new CEO John Ternus, particularly with the introduction of the company's first foldable device. By adjusting the release cycle for entry-level phones and expanding into the foldable market, Apple is attempting to maintain its premium positioning while diversifying its hardware ecosystem.
