CLARITY hopes fade, BitMEX shuts as lawsuit looms: Hodler’s Digest, July 26
============================================================

The Clarity Act faces legislative uncertainty as BitMEX prepares to shut down, highlighting a broader trend of market consolidation and regulatory challenges across the cryptocurrency industry this week.

### Key Points
- The Clarity Act faces dimming prospects as Senate Majority Leader John Thune questions whether the bill has sufficient support to pass before the August recess.
- BitMEX will cease operations in September after 11 years, as the exchange faces a class action lawsuit and declining market share against larger competitors.
- S&P Dow Jones Indices launched a new digital asset index featuring 18 constituents, notably excluding Bitcoin and XRP in favor of assets like Ether and Solana.
- CertiK reported that crypto-related home invasions surged to 20 incidents in the first half of 2026, with total financial losses from physical "wrench attacks" reaching $124.1 million.
- Hackers stole $31.6 million in two separate bridge exploits targeting the AFX exchange and Verus Protocol within a seven-hour window.

### Why it Matters
The simultaneous struggle of legislative reform and the exit of legacy exchanges like BitMEX signal a structural shift toward a more consolidated, institutional-grade crypto market. As mid-tier platforms struggle to compete, the industry is increasingly defined by regulatory scrutiny and the need for robust security standards to protect against rising physical and digital threats.
