Future of Marketing Briefing: The holdcos long, awkward courtship of the C-suite
============================================================

Publicis Groupe CEO Arthur Sadoun is shifting the agency’s strategy to pitch directly to corporate CEOs, aiming to bypass traditional marketing departments to secure long-term, high-value business partnerships.

### Key Points
- Publicis Groupe is leveraging its Sapient division to position itself as a technology and AI consultant for C-suite executives.
- The strategy aims to secure large accounts without traditional competitive pitches, as seen with recent moves by clients like PepsiCo.
- Critics argue that agency leaders lack the deep boardroom access held by established consultancies like Accenture and Deloitte.
- Moving away from CMO-led relationships risks alienating traditional marketing contacts who often serve as the primary agency advocates.
- The holdco model faces increased scrutiny from finance chiefs regarding opaque data charges, tech fees, and principal media margins.

### Why it Matters
This shift reflects a broader industry attempt to move marketing from a siloed expense to a core business driver integrated into the C-suite's strategic agenda. If successful, this approach could redefine agency-client relationships, though it risks undermining the essential partnerships agencies have historically maintained with chief marketing officers.
