The $146 Await camera brings back the disposable with 24 photos per roll, so every shot counts
============================================================

Swiss company Rolling Square has launched Await, a pocket-sized digital camera that limits users to 24 shots per roll to encourage intentional photography and physical print production.

### Key Points
- The Await camera features an 8MP Sony IMX179 sensor, a fixed f/2.0 lens, and a genuine xenon flash for authentic vintage-style lighting.
- Designed with a translucent frosted shell, the device measures 3.86 x 2.68 x 0.59 inches and weighs approximately 92.7 grams.
- Users operate the camera via an optical viewfinder and two physical buttons, with an OLED screen displaying remaining shots and battery status.
- The companion app applies vintage film filters to images, with an optional service to order 24 physical 6x4-inch prints per roll.
- Currently available on Kickstarter, the camera is priced at approximately $101 for early backers, with shipping scheduled for December 2026.

### Why it Matters
This device challenges the modern habit of digital over-documentation by imposing physical constraints that mimic the intentionality of traditional film photography. By prioritizing tangible prints over endless digital storage, the product offers a market alternative for consumers seeking to reduce digital clutter and preserve memories in a physical format.
