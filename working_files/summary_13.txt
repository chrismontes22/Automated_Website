ThreatsDay: AI Secrets Leak, PingCAP Source Theft, Signal Hang-Up Flaw, Laravel Auth Bypass + 21 More
============================================================

This week’s cybersecurity landscape saw a surge in critical vulnerabilities, data breaches, and sophisticated phishing campaigns targeting global organizations, government entities, and individual users across multiple platforms.

### Key Points
- Bluesky confirmed a phishing attack compromised over 4,500 accounts, while Zeabur announced a service shutdown following a breach that exposed 3.2 million environment variable records.
- Critical remote code execution (RCE) flaws were disclosed in Laravel, Eptura Archibus, and OpenCode, alongside a high-severity Signal bug that keeps audio active after calls end.
- The U.S. government banned DeepSeek-related technology from federal IT systems, citing national security risks and potential data transfers to the People's Republic of China.
- A sophisticated NFC bank fraud scheme in the Netherlands used AI-generated audio to steal €380,000, while the SafePay ransomware gang has hit over 600 organizations since 2024.
- Researchers identified that certain AI models with pro-China alignments may generate more vulnerable code when prompted for Western-focused applications.

### Why it Matters
These incidents highlight the increasing intersection of AI-driven social engineering, supply chain vulnerabilities, and geopolitical tensions in the digital space. Organizations must prioritize robust authentication, regular patching, and strict oversight of AI integrations to mitigate the risk of unauthorized access and data exfiltration.
