RBI chief says the next financial crisis may start with a cyberattack
============================================================

Reserve Bank of India Governor Sanjay Malhotra warned that the next global financial crisis could be triggered by geopolitical conflicts, cyberattacks, or systemic failures linked to artificial intelligence.

### Key Points
- Governor Sanjay Malhotra identified AI, cyberattacks, and technological failures as primary threats to global financial stability during the Kautilya Economic Conclave.
- The Reserve Bank of India official highlighted risks from faulty AI models, over-reliance on third-party suppliers, and reduced human oversight.
- High valuations in AI-related stocks could lead to a sharp market correction if corporate earnings or spending growth slows down.
- The Financial Stability Board and European Central Bank President Christine Lagarde have similarly identified AI-driven cyber threats as top systemic risks.
- Despite current economic resilience and 7.8% growth, India faces inflationary pressures and rising commodity costs due to ongoing geopolitical tensions.

### Why it Matters
These warnings highlight a shift in focus from traditional banking vulnerabilities toward complex, technology-driven risks that transcend national borders. As financial institutions integrate advanced AI, the potential for rapid, automated market disruptions increases, necessitating more robust regulatory oversight and contingency planning.
