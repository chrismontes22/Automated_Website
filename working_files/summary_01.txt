Just say no to OpenAI and Anthropic
============================================================

Open weight artificial intelligence models offer a viable, private alternative to proprietary systems from companies like OpenAI and Anthropic, allowing users to bypass restrictive corporate gatekeeping and surveillance.

### Key Points
- Open weight models like GLM-5.3, DeepSeek, and Mistral Large 4 provide performance levels comparable to top-tier proprietary models for complex tasks like software engineering.
- Proprietary services like ChatGPT and Claude maintain strict, centralized guardrails and monitor user sessions, often restricting access to cybersecurity-related tools.
- Secure enclaves provided by services like Tinfoil and Maple AI enable private inference, preventing providers from accessing or training on sensitive user data.
- Users can avoid corporate lock-in by utilizing inference routers like OpenCode Go or hosting models locally on high-RAM hardware.
- While Chinese-developed models are technically impressive, users are advised to avoid sharing data directly with those companies due to privacy and security concerns.

### Why it Matters
Relying on closed-source AI models concentrates significant power in the hands of a few corporate entities that dictate access and monitor user activity. Transitioning to open weight models empowers individuals and developers to maintain data sovereignty while accessing high-performance tools without restrictive oversight.
