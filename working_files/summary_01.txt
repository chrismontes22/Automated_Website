This VC used AI to try to snag a restaurant reservation. Resy wasn't having it
============================================================

An AI agent named Instinct caused a venture capitalist to be banned from the reservation platform Resy after the tool triggered spam filters with excessive automated API requests.

### Key Points
- JC Bahr-de Stefano, a principal at Better Tomorrow Ventures, used the AI tool Instinct to attempt to secure a reservation at 4 Charles Prime Rib.
- The AI agent sent approximately 200 API requests per hour to Resy, including checks every 0.4 seconds during peak reservation windows.
- Resy, owned by American Express, temporarily suspended Bahr-de Stefano’s account for violating terms of service regarding unapproved automated activity.
- The platform reinstated the account but issued a formal warning that repeat violations could result in a permanent ban from both Resy and American Express services.
- Resy currently prohibits third-party bots but supports official integrations with AI services like OpenAI’s ChatGPT and Anthropic’s Claude.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the growing friction between autonomous AI agents and existing web security protocols designed to prevent bot-driven abuse. As users increasingly delegate tasks to AI, platforms must navigate the challenge of distinguishing between helpful personal assistants and malicious automated traffic.
