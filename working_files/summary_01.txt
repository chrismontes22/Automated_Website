We View Consumer Data as Toxic Waste
============================================================

Obscura VPN introduces a two-party relay system that splits traffic between its own servers and Mullvad to eliminate the need for users to trust a single provider's privacy policy.

### Key Points
- Obscura uses a two-hop architecture where the first hop handles identity and the second hop, managed by Mullvad, handles encrypted traffic.
- The service utilizes the QUIC protocol to mimic HTTP/3 traffic, making it more resilient against deep packet inspection and internet censorship.
- Founder Carl Dong, a former Bitcoin Core contributor, emphasizes "trust minimization" by open-sourcing the client and supporting reproducible builds.
- The platform requires no email for registration and accepts anonymous payments via Monero and Bitcoin over the Lightning Network.
- Obscura operates as a small, independent team of six, avoiding venture capital influence to maintain a subscription-based business model.

### Why it Matters
This model challenges the traditional "no-logs" promise by ensuring that no single entity can correlate a user's identity with their browsing activity. By decentralizing trust, Obscura provides a technical alternative for users concerned about the risks of data breaches or legal subpoenas inherent in centralized VPN services.
