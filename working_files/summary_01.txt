Comcast's Xfinity Shield turns your router into a motion sensor — and it may share what it sees
============================================================

Comcast has launched Xfinity Shield, a new home security platform that utilizes compatible Xfinity Gateways to provide motion detection, cybersecurity features, and parental controls for residential customers.

### Key Points
- Xfinity Shield uses existing Wi-Fi signals between gateways and stationary devices to detect household motion.
- The platform includes a free tier with network-level malware protection and a $15 monthly Shield Select tier featuring cameras and sensors.
- Wi-Fi Motion is an opt-in feature that remains disabled by default, requiring manual activation through the Xfinity app.
- Comcast reserves the right to disclose motion-generated data to third parties during legal proceedings, investigations, or in response to court orders.

### Why it Matters
This platform deepens customer reliance on ISP-provided hardware by integrating home security directly into the internet gateway. While it offers convenient protection, users must weigh the privacy implications of allowing their internet provider to record and potentially share data regarding physical activity within their homes.
