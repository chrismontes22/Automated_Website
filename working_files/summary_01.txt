China’s state security minister names two US AI models as a cyber threat
============================================================

China’s Minister of State Security, Chen Yixin, has officially identified two American AI models, Claude Mythos and GPT-5.5-Cyber, as significant threats to the nation’s critical information infrastructure.

### Key Points
- Chen Yixin published the warning in the Cyberspace Administration of China’s official journal, *China Cyberspace*, on September 13.
- The article claims these AI models increase the efficiency of malware development and vulnerability discovery, posing risks to national security.
- Chen outlined six primary AI-related risks, including political instability, espionage, data leaks, and shifts in the nature of military conflict.
- The report does not cite specific instances of attacks on Chinese systems or provide evidence of these models being used against the country.
- Anthropic and OpenAI market these models as tools for cybersecurity defense, emphasizing their ability to identify and patch software flaws.

### Why it Matters
This official designation signals a shift in Beijing’s strategy, moving from private concerns to public, high-level warnings regarding American AI capabilities. It highlights the growing geopolitical tension surrounding AI development and suggests that China may implement stricter domestic controls and international regulatory demands to counter perceived technological hegemony.
