Apple Planning AI Home Security Camera and Service for 2027
============================================================

Apple is reportedly developing a privacy-focused home security camera and monitoring service for a 2027 release, aiming to expand its smart home ecosystem beyond existing HomeKit features.

### Key Points
- Apple plans to launch a proprietary home security camera that utilizes AI for environmental monitoring rather than traditional video recording.
- The company is preparing to release a new smart home hub by the end of 2024 to centralize device control.
- Current HomeKit Secure Video technology already provides end-to-end encryption for third-party cameras integrated with iCloud.
- New iOS 18 features for the Home app include AI-generated motion alert summaries and consolidated footage highlights for users.
- Future security services may integrate directly with existing iCloud+ subscription tiers, which currently support various smart home functionalities.

### Why it Matters
This move signals Apple's intent to compete directly with established home security providers by leveraging its reputation for user privacy and hardware integration. By expanding its smart home portfolio, the company aims to increase long-term service revenue through deeper ecosystem lock-in and iCloud+ subscriptions.
