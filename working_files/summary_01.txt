ClickFix Lures Deploy ChainScript RAT Using Polygon to Rotate C2 Infrastructure
============================================================

Cybersecurity researchers have identified a new remote access trojan called ChainScript that uses deceptive ClickFix lures and blockchain-based command-and-control infrastructure to compromise Windows systems globally.

### Key Points
- ChainScript disguises itself as legitimate software like Spotify, Zoom, and Microsoft Teams to trick users into executing malicious Windows installers.
- The malware utilizes a Polygon smart contract for command-and-control discovery, allowing operators to rotate infrastructure and evade detection.
- Capabilities include remote command execution, file manipulation, screenshot capture, and the theft of cryptocurrency wallet data from browsers and desktop applications.
- Attackers are increasingly using ClickFix tactics, including the recent hijacking of a verified HBO Max Reddit account to distribute information-stealing malware.
- Campaigns targeting macOS users, such as those distributing MacSync and Atomic Stealer, are leveraging browser-fingerprinting to bypass security crawlers and sandboxes.

### Why it Matters
These campaigns demonstrate a shift toward decentralized infrastructure and the abuse of trusted platforms to bypass traditional security heuristics. By leveraging blockchain technology and verified corporate accounts, threat actors are significantly increasing the difficulty of detecting and neutralizing persistent malware threats.
