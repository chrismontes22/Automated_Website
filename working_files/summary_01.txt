'Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 2' PC remote code exploits have been fixed, says Activision
============================================================

Activision has released a security update for the PC version of Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 2 to address critical Remote Code Execution vulnerabilities and ongoing hacking issues.

### Key Points
- Activision confirmed that a recent update patched all known Remote Code Execution vulnerabilities in the PC version of Modern Warfare 2.
- The company stated it is actively working on additional updates to support other legacy Call of Duty titles.
- Players are encouraged to report new security issues through the official Activision Bug Bounty Portal.
- Classic Call of Duty titles remain popular, with Black Ops 1 and 2 recently dominating sales on the PlayStation store.
- Xbox backwards-compatible versions of these games currently remain unpatched and vulnerable to hackers and aimbots.

### Why it Matters
Addressing these security flaws is essential for maintaining the long-term viability and reputation of the Call of Duty franchise's legacy catalog. As these older titles continue to generate significant revenue, ensuring a safe multiplayer environment is critical for player retention and brand trust.
