How I stopped being my parents' emergency IT hotline — The complete list of Windows Security settings I made them fix once and for all
============================================================

This comprehensive guide outlines essential cybersecurity best practices for Windows 11 users to protect their personal data and devices from common digital threats, scams, and unauthorized system access.

### Key Points
- Enable Windows Hello for secure biometric or PIN-based logins and ensure Windows Update and Windows Defender are active to receive critical security patches and malware protection.
- Activate the "Find My Device" feature in Windows 11 to allow for remote location tracking and device locking in the event of theft or loss.
- Switch from a local account to a Microsoft account with two-factor authentication (2FA) to improve account recovery and add an extra layer of security.
- Enable device encryption, such as BitLocker or standard device encryption, to prevent unauthorized access to data if a physical drive is removed from the computer.
- Transition from an Administrator account to a Standard user account for daily tasks to limit the potential system-wide impact of malicious software.
- Utilize reputable password managers like NordPass, 1Password, or Bitwarden to generate and store unique, complex passwords for all online accounts.

### Why it Matters
Implementing these foundational security measures significantly reduces the risk of identity theft, data loss, and unauthorized remote access to personal computers. By adopting these proactive habits, users can better defend themselves against increasingly sophisticated phishing scams and common technical support fraud.
