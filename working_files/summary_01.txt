Banks, fintechs urged to strengthen infrastructure
============================================================

First City Monument Bank CTO Blessing Ehize urged Nigerian financial and technology stakeholders to collaborate on infrastructure and cybersecurity to accelerate the nation’s digital transformation and economic resilience.

### Key Points
- Blessing Ehize spoke at the inaugural GrowthX by Techeconomy 2026 conference held in Victoria Island, Lagos.
- The event focused on critical topics including data localisation, artificial intelligence, digital payments, and cybersecurity.
- Participants discussed the Central Bank of Nigeria’s data localisation directive and its impact on digital sovereignty and operational costs.
- The conference aimed to foster practical cooperation between banks, fintech firms, regulators, and digital infrastructure providers.

### Why it Matters
Strengthening local data infrastructure is essential for building consumer trust and ensuring the long-term stability of Nigeria’s digital financial services. This collaborative approach helps firms navigate regulatory requirements while creating a more secure and scalable foundation for the country's growing digital economy.
