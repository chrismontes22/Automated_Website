Five makers, one laptop: Googlebook pre-orders open from $899
============================================================

Google has opened pre-orders for its new Googlebook laptop series, starting at $899, with initial shipments arriving in the United States on October 4 and international markets following.

### Key Points
- The Googlebook series features five distinct models from manufacturers Acer, ASUS, Lenovo, Dell, and HP.
- Hardware specifications include Intel Core Ultra Series 3 or Qualcomm Snapdragon X Elite processors, 16GB to 32GB of RAM, and 2.8K OLED touchscreens.
- The operating system integrates Android app support with ChromeOS desktop functionality and a secure, walled-off Linux environment via a Level 5 security-certified pKVM hypervisor.
- Every purchase includes a 12-month subscription to Google AI Pro, providing 5TB of storage and access to Gemini Advanced tools.
- Devices feature premium builds using aluminum, magnesium alloy, or carbon fiber, alongside customizable "Glowbar" LED lighting for developers.

### Why it Matters
This launch represents a significant strategic shift for Google by merging the Android and ChromeOS ecosystems into a unified hardware platform for both consumers and developers. By offering high-end security features and native support for professional creative software, Google is positioning the Googlebook as a direct competitor to established premium laptop brands.
