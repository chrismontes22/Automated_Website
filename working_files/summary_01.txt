Comcast is turning millions of its routers into motion detectors
============================================================

Comcast is rolling out a free Wi-Fi motion sensing feature to millions of existing Xfinity gateways, allowing users to monitor home activity without purchasing additional security hardware.

### Key Points
- The WiFi Motion feature detects movement by analyzing disruptions in Wi-Fi signals between the router and connected devices.
- Compatible hardware includes Xfinity XB7 gateways or newer, with the update available through the Xfinity Internet app starting August 18.
- Users can manage the system via three modes: Home Watch, Away Watch, and Dark Watch for nighttime monitoring.
- The rollout is part of the new Xfinity Shield service, which also includes enhanced malware protection and improved parental controls.
- Comcast does not intend for this technology to replace professional security systems, though it offers a basic level of home monitoring.

### Why it Matters
This update allows Comcast to provide value-added security features to its existing customer base without requiring new hardware installations. By leveraging existing infrastructure, the company is positioning itself to compete in the broader smart home and aging-in-place markets.
