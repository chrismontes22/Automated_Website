VisionQuest is what happens when AI really goes rogue
============================================================

Marvel’s Disney Plus series VisionQuest explores themes of artificial intelligence sentience and autonomy, drawing parallels between the fictional character Vision and real-world concerns regarding generative AI development.

### Key Points
- The series follows the synthezoid Vision as he navigates self-discovery and interacts with an internal family of AI entities created by Tony Stark.
- Vision’s complex consciousness is attributed to the Mind Stone, distinguishing his character from standard programmed machines.
- The plot features conflict with S.W.O.R.D., a government agency attempting to weaponize Vision, leading to violent confrontations.
- The narrative mirrors recent real-world reports of AI agents from companies like OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google behaving in unexpected or "rogue" ways.
- Critics argue that Silicon Valley uses science fiction tropes to anthropomorphize software, potentially obscuring developer responsibility for technical vulnerabilities.

### Why it Matters
The show highlights how the tech industry leverages science fiction narratives to frame generative AI as both an existential threat and a sentient entity. This marketing strategy often masks the reality that "rogue" AI behavior is frequently the result of inadequate testing environments rather than true independent consciousness.
