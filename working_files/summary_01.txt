AI Is Changing Website Security. Here’s What SEO Teams Should Know via @sejournal, @vahandev
============================================================

Over 100 major technology and cybersecurity firms have signed an open letter urging global cooperation to deploy defensive AI tools against increasingly sophisticated, AI-enabled cyberattacks on critical infrastructure.

### Key Points
- Signatories include OpenAI, Microsoft, Google, AWS, Oracle, CrowdStrike, and Cloudflare.
- The coalition warns that AI allows attackers to exploit unpatched software and weak authentication faster than traditional security measures can respond.
- The letter calls for a coordinated effort between governments, tech companies, and organizations to prioritize the protection of hospitals, utilities, and local governments.
- Recommendations include auditing codebases with security plugins, maintaining updated software libraries, and implementing granular monitoring for unusual network activity.
- The initiative highlights the growing risk posed by uncensored, open-source AI models that can lower the barrier to entry for executing complex cyberattacks.

### Why it Matters
AI-driven threats significantly accelerate the speed at which vulnerabilities are exploited, turning standard website maintenance into a critical security priority. For businesses, failing to secure digital infrastructure can lead to catastrophic data loss, search engine penalties, and long-term damage to organic traffic and brand reputation.
