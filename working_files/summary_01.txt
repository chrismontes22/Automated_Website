Identity Visibility in 2026: The Foundation of Identity Security
============================================================

Identity visibility provides a continuous, comprehensive view of all human and machine credentials, enabling organizations to detect unauthorized access and secure complex, multicloud identity environments against modern threats.

### Key Points
- Identity visibility combines inventory, entitlement mapping, and behavioral telemetry to distinguish between intended access policies and actual runtime execution.
- "Identity dark matter"—including local application accounts, embedded service credentials, and legacy authentication flows—often bypasses centralized identity providers and governance platforms.
- Modern attack surfaces are increasingly exploited through compromised legitimate credentials, making it critical to monitor non-human identities like API keys and autonomous AI agents.
- Effective visibility requires normalizing identity models across diverse platforms, such as AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, and various SaaS applications.
- Identity Visibility and Intelligence Platforms (IVIP) like Orchid Security, Veza, and SailPoint help bridge the gap between IAM configuration and actual infrastructure usage.

### Why it Matters
Organizations often suffer from a widening gap between documented access and real-world permissions, which attackers exploit to move laterally through cloud environments. Implementing continuous identity visibility transforms security from static, manual governance into a proactive, data-driven defense that aligns with zero-trust principles.
