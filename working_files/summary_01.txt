An Undercover Google Analyst Infiltrated a Notorious Supply Chain Hacking Gang
============================================================

Google’s threat intelligence team successfully infiltrated the hacker group TeamPCP, allowing researchers to monitor a massive supply-chain attack and assist law enforcement in identifying the group's primary members.

### Key Points
- TeamPCP compromised hundreds of open-source projects and major platforms, including OpenAI and the European Commission, using a self-spreading worm called Mini Shai-Hulud.
- A Mandiant analyst embedded within the group’s inner circle provided Google with real-time visibility into stolen credentials and an AI-generated zero-day exploit.
- Google disrupted the campaign by coordinating with cloud providers to revoke stolen credentials and patching vulnerabilities before they could be exploited.
- Australian authorities arrested Ruben Ian Thomson and Louis Michael Gaebler last month following intelligence provided by Google and other sources.
- The investigation was led by Google’s Cyber Disruption Unit, which focuses on actively intervening in cybercriminal operations rather than just reporting on them.

### Why it Matters
This operation highlights a strategic shift toward proactive disruption in cybersecurity, where tech giants use undercover intelligence to neutralize threats before they cause widespread damage. By successfully infiltrating a sophisticated hacking collective, Google demonstrated that direct intervention can effectively mitigate large-scale supply-chain risks that traditional defensive measures might miss.
