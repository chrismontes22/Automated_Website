Singaporean man pleads guilty in US to massive crypto heist
============================================================

Singaporean national Malone Lam pleaded guilty to a racketeering conspiracy charge in a US court for orchestrating a massive cryptocurrency heist that resulted in $245 million in stolen assets.

### Key Points
- Malone Lam admitted to leading an international criminal network that stole $245 million in bitcoin from victims through deception and home break-ins.
- The conspiracy, which operated from October 2023 to May 2025, involved co-conspirators recruited via online gaming platforms.
- Stolen funds were used to finance a lavish lifestyle, including $500,000 nightly nightclub tabs, luxury cars, private jets, and high-end watches.
- Lam faces a maximum sentence of 20 years in federal prison following his arrest by FBI agents in September 2024.
- Co-conspirator Evan Tangeman was previously sentenced to 70 months in prison for his role in laundering the stolen cryptocurrency proceeds.

### Why it Matters
This case highlights the growing sophistication of international cybercrime rings that leverage online gaming communities to coordinate large-scale financial theft. It serves as a significant warning regarding the vulnerability of digital assets and the commitment of federal authorities to dismantle global criminal enterprises.
