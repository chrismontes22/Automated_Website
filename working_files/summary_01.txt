I Don’t Travel Without These Online Safety Tools and Tricks
============================================================

Travelers can protect their personal data and financial accounts from cyber threats by adopting essential security habits and using digital tools like VPNs, password managers, and multi-factor authentication.

### Key Points
- Use a Virtual Private Network (VPN) on public Wi-Fi to encrypt data and prevent packet-sniffing or adversary-in-the-middle attacks.
- Verify the legitimacy of booking websites and QR codes to avoid typosquatting scams and malicious payment redirects.
- Enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) and use password managers like Apple Passwords or 1Password to secure sensitive login credentials.
- Keep devices updated with the latest software patches and utilize built-in security features like Microsoft Defender or Apple Find My.
- Configure bank transaction alerts and spending limits to mitigate potential financial losses if accounts are compromised during a trip.

### Why it Matters
Cybersecurity threats are increasingly sophisticated, and losing access to digital accounts while traveling can cause significant logistical and financial distress. Implementing these proactive measures ensures that travelers maintain control over their personal information and assets, regardless of their location.
