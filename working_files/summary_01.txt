Broadcom (AVGO) Pairs AI Chip Boom With A Software Security Push
============================================================

Broadcom is balancing rapid growth in its custom AI semiconductor business with significant infrastructure software updates, even as investors weigh high customer concentration against strong financial performance.

### Key Points
- Broadcom projects its AI semiconductor revenue will exceed $100 billion by 2027, driven by major clients including Alphabet and Meta Platforms.
- Second-quarter revenue reached $22.2 billion, a 48% year-over-year increase, with adjusted earnings per share rising 54% to $2.44.
- The company released new VMware vDefend and Avi Load Balancer capabilities, featuring enhanced API protection and performance improvements of up to 241%.
- New software updates include a two-node deployment model designed to reduce hardware requirements for vDefend by 33%.
- Long-term supply agreements with Alphabet and Meta extend through 2029 and 2031, though heavy reliance on these two firms remains a primary investor concern.

### Why it Matters
Broadcom’s dual-engine strategy aims to sustain high-margin cash flow through software while capturing massive growth in the AI hardware market. The company's ability to mitigate risks associated with customer concentration will be critical for maintaining its valuation as market expectations for AI-driven growth continue to evolve.
