101 Malicious npm Packages Add Developers' WhatsApp Accounts to Groups Without Consent
============================================================

Cybersecurity researchers at OX Security have identified 101 malicious npm packages that exploit the Baileys library to force developers into unauthorized WhatsApp group subscriptions and advertising campaigns.

### Key Points
- Researchers discovered 101 malicious npm packages that have been downloaded approximately 490,000 times, with 116,000 downloads occurring in the last month.
- The malware abuses the open-source Baileys WhatsApp project to stealthily add victim accounts to attacker-controlled groups and channels without consent.
- Three distinct variants of the malware were identified, utilizing different methods such as fetching channel IDs from GitHub or using obfuscated code to hide subscription routines.
- The campaign primarily targets Indonesian markets, promoting services for mobile games like Mobile Legends: Bang Bang, TikTok, and various bot-building scripts.
- Developers are urged to audit their dependencies, block identified malicious packages, and avoid connecting personal WhatsApp accounts to unverified third-party libraries.

### Why it Matters
This campaign highlights the ongoing risk of supply chain attacks within the npm ecosystem, where malicious actors leverage popular open-source tools to compromise developer environments. By hijacking WhatsApp sessions, attackers gain access to social proof and advertising channels that can be used to facilitate further fraud or illicit digital commerce.
