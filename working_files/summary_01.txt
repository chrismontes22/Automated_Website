Amazon launches a faster Fire TV Stick 4K with a new remote design
============================================================

Amazon has launched a new Fire TV Stick 4K featuring the proprietary Vega operating system, which improves performance speeds but restricts users from sideloading third-party applications.

### Key Points
- The new Fire TV Stick 4K runs on the Vega OS, replacing the previous Android-based software used by Amazon.
- Amazon claims the device loads content 20 to 40% faster than competing streaming sticks at similar price points.
- The redesigned remote features an asymmetric shape, a metal home button, and a customizable star button.
- Sideloading is disabled on the new system, a move Amazon states is intended to protect users from malware and piracy.
- Because Amazon’s operating systems are not designated as "gatekeepers" under the EU Digital Markets Act, the company is not legally required to allow third-party app stores.
- The device is currently available in Britain, Germany, France, Italy, and Spain, with a U.S. promotional price of $29.99 during Prime Big Deal Days.

### Why it Matters
This transition to a closed operating system highlights Amazon's strategy to exert greater control over its hardware ecosystem while avoiding the interoperability mandates currently imposed on rivals like Apple and Google. By bypassing Digital Markets Act requirements, Amazon maintains a walled garden that could influence future regulatory scrutiny regarding its cloud and advertising services.
