These Google Pixel Phones No Longer Receive Software Support
============================================================

Google has officially ended software and security update support for all Pixel smartphone models ranging from the original Pixel through the Pixel 5 series.

### Key Points
- Google Pixel 1 through Pixel 5 devices no longer receive official Android OS updates or critical security patches.
- Users of unsupported devices face increased risks from malware and potential incompatibility with modern mobile applications.
- Owners can continue using older devices, install custom operating systems, or upgrade to newer models to maintain security.
- Google now guarantees seven years of software support for the Pixel 8 series and newer devices released in the United States.
- Models released between the Pixel 6 and Pixel 7 series receive five years of support, with expiration dates ranging from 2026 to 2028.

### Why it Matters
The discontinuation of support for older hardware highlights the growing importance of long-term software commitments in the smartphone industry. Consumers must now weigh the longevity of update cycles when purchasing devices to avoid security vulnerabilities and premature hardware obsolescence.
