Uncensored and Offensive Security AI Models Benchmark
============================================================

A curated list of open-weight, uncensored large language models provides security professionals with specialized tools for authorized red teaming, penetration testing, and advanced vulnerability research as of September 2026.

### Key Points
- The collection features 27 models, including specialized agents like RavenX-CyberAgent and DeepHat V2, trained on extensive security datasets such as CVE writeups and bug bounty reports.
- Technical methods for removing alignment constraints include abliteration, supervised fine-tuning (SFT), and data filtering to ensure models remain responsive to offensive security queries.
- Deployment options range from consumer-grade hardware using Ollama and GGUF formats to high-performance datacenter setups utilizing vLLM and multi-GPU configurations.
- Specialized architectures like Mixture of Experts (MoE) and models with native tool-calling capabilities support complex workflows, including automated exploit generation and threat intelligence analysis.
- Platforms such as OrcaRouter and Featherless AI offer managed inference, while local tools like LM Studio provide accessible interfaces for security researchers.

### Why it Matters
These uncensored models remove the restrictive safety guardrails found in commercial AI, allowing security researchers to simulate real-world attack vectors and automate complex defensive tasks. By providing specialized, open-weight alternatives, this resource enables more effective vulnerability discovery and threat modeling within authorized professional environments.
