Binance Says Hackers Used Well-Known Tricks to Carry Out a $6.34 Billion Heist
============================================================

Binance suffered a massive $6.34 billion cryptocurrency theft on October 29 after North Korean-linked hackers compromised internal systems through a sophisticated social engineering attack on company engineers.

### Key Points
- Hackers gained network access by tricking a Binance engineer into downloading malicious software during a fake job interview process.
- The attackers exploited an internal AI tool and a wallet signing service to bypass standard withdrawal procedures within 20 minutes.
- The FBI and security experts attributed the breach to the North Korean-linked group TraderTraitor, which has been active since June.
- Binance has pledged to cover user losses and has already rebuilt the internal systems compromised during the incident.
- Industry partners successfully froze nearly half of the stolen funds, though the remaining billions are currently being laundered across multiple blockchains.

### Why it Matters
This record-breaking heist highlights the extreme vulnerability of centralized cryptocurrency exchanges to highly targeted, long-term social engineering campaigns. The potential conversion of these stolen assets into cash poses a significant global security risk, as experts believe the funds may be used to finance North Korea's nuclear and ballistic missile programs.
