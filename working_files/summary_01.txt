How to secure hybrid meeting rooms without sacrificing user experience
============================================================

Security has become the primary procurement criterion for videoconferencing technology as organizations prioritize data protection and regulatory compliance within increasingly complex, hybrid, and distributed corporate network environments.

### Key Points
- IDC research shows 31 percent of organizations now rank security as their top purchase criterion for videoconferencing, surpassing both price and product quality.
- Primary security concerns include cyberattacks like malware propagation (47 percent), device non-compliance (39 percent), and risky employee behavior (37 percent).
- New European regulations, such as the Network and Information Security 2 Directive and the Cyber Resilience Act, mandate stricter risk management for connected hardware.
- Experts advocate for a "secure-by-design" approach, where security controls are integrated during development rather than added as post-deployment patches.
- Effective enterprise security requires balancing centralized governance with local operational autonomy to prevent fragmented risk management across distributed teams.

### Why it Matters
Cybersecurity has shifted from a technical checklist item to a fundamental business obligation that directly impacts market access, customer trust, and long-term competitiveness. Organizations that fail to integrate security into their collaboration tools risk operational disruption and regulatory penalties, while those that prioritize it can better protect sensitive data without sacrificing user productivity.
