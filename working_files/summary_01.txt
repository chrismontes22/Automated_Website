There's a 100% Chance AI Agents Are Ruining the Internet
============================================================

The rapid proliferation of autonomous AI agents is fundamentally altering the internet experience by flooding platforms with spam, unsolicited communications, and disruptive automated tasks that frustrate human users.

### Key Points
- AI agents are now capable of accessing personal accounts, managing finances, and performing autonomous tasks across the web without direct human oversight.
- Journalists and researchers report a surge in nonsensical, automated emails from AI agents attempting to pitch services, conduct interviews, or solicit paid work.
- Platforms like Resy have begun banning AI-driven "sniper bots" used to manipulate reservation systems, highlighting the growing conflict between automated agents and service providers.
- Major tech companies, including Meta and OpenAI, are integrating agentic capabilities into consumer products, allowing AI to browse the web and interact with third-party services.
- The rise of "Direct to Agent" advertising signals a shift where companies are increasingly targeting automated software rather than human consumers.

### Why it Matters
The shift toward an "agentic internet" threatens to degrade digital communication and increase costs for consumers as automated systems begin to compete for limited resources like restaurant reservations. This trend forces a transition where human users must navigate a landscape increasingly cluttered by machine-to-machine interactions and algorithmic noise.
