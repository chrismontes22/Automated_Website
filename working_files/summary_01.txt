Mapping the malware blast radius a single alert won’t show you
============================================================

Stairwell has launched Backstory, an AI-powered investigation platform designed to automatically map the full blast radius of malware campaigns by identifying hidden variants across enterprise endpoint environments.

### Key Points
- Backstory uses "Variant Discovery" technology to identify structurally related malicious files that often evade traditional signature-based detection.
- Research by Stairwell found that every published malware sample hides an average of 2.4 undocumented variants.
- The platform maintains "ground truth" by permanently archiving every executable file, including binaries and DLLs, that runs on customer endpoints.
- Unlike log-based systems, Backstory allows security teams to re-examine historical files whenever new threat intelligence becomes available.
- The tool automates investigative pivots, connecting disparate data points like file rarity, shared infrastructure, and code reuse to uncover complete attack campaigns.

### Why it Matters
This platform addresses the growing challenge of AI-generated malware, which allows attackers to create unique, low-cost variants that bypass traditional security defenses. By shifting the focus from reactive alert triage to proactive, evidence-based investigation, organizations can better identify the full scope of a breach rather than just individual incidents.
