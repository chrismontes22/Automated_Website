Lunex Stealer Abuses AMD Driver to Disable Security Monitoring and Steal Browser Credentials
============================================================

The Lunex malware-as-a-service platform is actively targeting Ukrainian users with the Psychedelic Stealer, utilizing sophisticated ClickFix lures and vulnerable drivers to bypass Windows security and exfiltrate data.

### Key Points
- The attack chain uses fake Cloudflare CAPTCHA pages and MSI installers to deploy the LunexLoader, which facilitates credential and cryptocurrency theft.
- Attackers exploit a vulnerable AMD Radeon driver (CVE-2023-20598) to disable security software while keeping processes running to avoid detection.
- The malware targets seven Chromium-based browsers and multiple desktop and extension-based cryptocurrency wallets to harvest sensitive user information.
- Persistence is maintained through Registry keys, scheduled tasks, and a PowerShell-based Native Messaging Host that survives system reboots.
- Researchers identified 28 active command-and-control panels across 13 countries, indicating a rapid expansion of the platform since June 2026.

### Why it Matters
This campaign demonstrates a significant evolution in malware-as-a-service operations by combining advanced kernel-level evasion with persistent browser-based data theft. The ability to bypass standard security blocklists highlights a critical vulnerability in how enterprise environments manage and validate third-party drivers.
