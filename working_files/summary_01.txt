AI Decision Models and Their Impact on AI SOC Platforms
============================================================

New "System One" decision models from companies like TypeSafe AI and OpenAI are transforming security operations by providing fast, low-cost, and auditable automated triage for cyber alerts.

### Key Points
- Decision models skip text generation to provide structured, probabilistic outputs, such as scores or yes/no verdicts, for automated security workflows.
- These models are highly cost-effective, with providers like TypeSafe AI charging approximately $42 per billion input tokens, enabling full-scale triage of all alerts.
- Outputs are fixed-schema and include probability metrics, making them significantly easier to audit and automate than traditional generative AI responses.
- Despite their efficiency, these models remain susceptible to "garbage in, garbage out" risks, as they rely entirely on the quality of the input data provided.
- Effective security triage requires "Security Context Engineering," which involves gathering forensic evidence and organizational knowledge before the model makes a final decision.

### Why it Matters
The emergence of decision models allows security teams to automate the triage of every alert regardless of severity, effectively removing the economic barriers that previously forced analysts to prioritize only high-risk events. However, the accuracy of these automated verdicts depends heavily on the quality of forensic evidence and organizational context fed into the system, rather than the model's inherent intelligence.
