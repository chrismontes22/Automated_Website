Muse sure looks a lot like OpenClaw
============================================================

Meta’s new AI agent Muse and the platform Instinct are gaining significant traction, sparking industry debate over their design similarities to the open-source project OpenClaw.

### Key Points
- Meta’s Muse app has reached 600,000 daily active users in the U.S. shortly after its release.
- Critics allege Muse shares core file names and personality documentation with the open-source project OpenClaw.
- Meta’s Nat Friedman denies copying OpenClaw but acknowledges the product was heavily inspired by its design.
- The AI agent platform Instinct is currently fundraising at a $2.5 billion valuation.
- Security researchers have identified potential vulnerabilities in Muse, including a zero-day exploit that allows unauthorized agent hijacking.

### Why it Matters
The rapid rise of Muse and Instinct highlights how Big Tech is aggressively adopting and commercializing grassroots innovations to capture the growing consumer AI agent market. While these corporate versions offer improved accessibility and ease of use, they raise significant questions regarding user privacy, data security, and the future of open-source influence in the industry.
