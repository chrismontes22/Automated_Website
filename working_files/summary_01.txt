Ransomware Recovery: What Happens in the First 24 Hours
============================================================

Organizations can mitigate the impact of a ransomware attack by following a structured 24-hour response plan focused on immediate system containment, expert coordination, and strategic damage assessment.

### Key Points
- Isolate infected devices from networks immediately by disconnecting Wi-Fi and ethernet cables to prevent the malware from spreading laterally.
- Preserve system states rather than powering down machines to ensure forensic investigators can identify the root cause of the breach.
- Activate an incident response team within four hours, including IT staff, legal counsel, communications experts, and executive leadership.
- Verify the integrity of offline or immutable cloud backups to determine if data restoration is possible without paying a ransom.
- Consult with legal counsel regarding mandatory notification requirements for regulators, customers, and partners to manage potential liability.

### Why it Matters
A rapid and organized response during the first day of a cyberattack significantly reduces the risk of catastrophic data loss and long-term operational disruption. Organizations that prioritize preparation and clear communication are better positioned to navigate the legal, financial, and reputational challenges inherent in ransomware incidents.
