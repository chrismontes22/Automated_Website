Don't buy the new Fire TV Stick if you actually plan to use Jellyfin or Kodi
============================================================

Amazon has introduced a proprietary Linux-based operating system called Vega OS for its new Fire TV Select 4K sticks, significantly restricting user customization and third-party application sideloading capabilities.

### Key Points
- Vega OS is a locked-down, proprietary Linux-based system that replaces the Android-based Fire OS on select new streaming devices.
- The new platform prohibits sideloading Android APKs, preventing the installation of popular media tools like Kodi and Jellyfin.
- Amazon’s curated app store for Vega OS currently offers only about 3,000 applications, compared to the vast library available on traditional Fire OS devices.
- While the Plex app is available on Vega OS, it currently faces technical limitations regarding video and audio codec support.
- A third-party client named Astra has emerged as a limited workaround for users attempting to access Jellyfin or Emby media servers on the new hardware.

### Why it Matters
This shift toward a closed ecosystem signals Amazon's move to prioritize platform security and monetization over the flexibility previously enjoyed by power users. Consumers who rely on sideloading or open-source media software may find these new devices incompatible with their existing home entertainment setups.
