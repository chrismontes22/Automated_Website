Booming AWS Revenue Shields Amazon From Meta-Sized Backlash
============================================================

Amazon shares surged 9% after the company reported strong second-quarter earnings driven by a 37% revenue increase in its cloud computing division, Amazon Web Services.

### Key Points
- Amazon Web Services revenue reached $42.2 billion, marking the fifth consecutive quarter of accelerating growth for the cloud platform.
- CEO Andy Jassy increased the company’s capital expenditure guidance by 10% to $220 billion to support ongoing infrastructure investments.
- Amazon’s internal AI chip business and AWS AI services both surpassed $25 billion annual run rates with triple-digit year-over-year growth.
- Major AI firms including Anthropic and OpenAI have committed to using Amazon’s proprietary Trainium chips for their computing needs.
- The company is reportedly winding down its internal Nova AI model development to focus on supplying hardware and infrastructure to the broader industry.

### Why it Matters
Investors are increasingly distinguishing between tech companies that can demonstrate immediate returns on massive artificial intelligence spending and those that cannot. Amazon’s ability to monetize its cloud infrastructure and custom silicon provides a clear roadmap for profitability that reassures markets amid rising capital expenditure requirements.
