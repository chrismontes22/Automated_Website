Sen. Adam Schiff on AI regulation, free speech, and impeaching Trump one more time
============================================================

Senator Adam Schiff, a member of key Senate subcommittees overseeing technology, argues that Congress must urgently establish a robust, expert-led regulatory framework to address the national security risks of AI.

### Key Points
- Sen. Schiff advocates for a dedicated regulatory agency for AI, similar to the FDA, to provide oversight that can adapt faster than the legislative process.
- He criticizes the current administration’s non-binding AI safety pledges as "meaningless" and insufficient to address real-world threats.
- The senator highlights the need for transparency regarding copyrighted material used to train AI models, noting the existential threat to the creative industry.
- Schiff expresses concern that the Supreme Court’s overturning of Chevron deference will allow corporations to tie new AI regulations up in endless litigation.
- He identifies a growing bipartisan coalition concerned about AI’s impact on jobs, data center expansion, and personal privacy.

### Why it Matters
The debate over AI regulation is shifting from a partisan issue to a broader concern about economic stability, labor rights, and national security. As Congress considers its next steps, the tension between rapid technological advancement and the need for public safeguards remains a central challenge for future policy.
