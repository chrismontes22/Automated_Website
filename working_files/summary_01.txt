Protect Yourself From Cybercrime for as Little as $2 Per Month
============================================================

Top-rated VPN services including ExpressVPN, Surfshark, and NordVPN are currently offering significant discounts, with some subscription plans available for as little as $2 per month for new users.

### Key Points
- Surfshark offers plans starting at $2.49 per month, supporting unlimited devices and including optional features like virus protection and ad blocking.
- ExpressVPN provides a 24-month plan that averages $3 per month, maintaining high performance and security standards despite its acquisition by Kape Technologies.
- NordVPN features discounts of up to 75% on multi-year plans, which support up to 10 devices and include tools like password management and malware protection.
- Private Internet Access and IPVanish serve as budget-friendly alternatives, with current promotional pricing around $2 per month for long-term commitments.
- Proton VPN remains the only free service recommended by experts, though its paid "Plus" tier offers enhanced functionality for $3 per month.
- Most providers offer a 30-day money-back guarantee, though users should note that many advertised deals require upfront payments for multi-year subscriptions.

### Why it Matters
Using a VPN provides essential encryption for sensitive data, protecting users from cybercrime while shopping or banking online. These current discounts make professional-grade digital security accessible for a low monthly cost, though consumers should carefully review long-term contract commitments and ownership changes before purchasing.
