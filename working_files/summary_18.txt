Nvidia Q2 Preview: Strong Earnings and Low Valuation Set the Stage for Upside
============================================================

Nvidia is expected to report strong fiscal second-quarter 2027 results on August 26, driven by surging demand for its AI infrastructure, Blackwell architecture, and data-center networking solutions.

### Key Points
- Nvidia projects Q2 revenue of approximately $91 billion, representing a 95% year-over-year increase.
- Analysts forecast earnings of $2.09 per share, marking a 111% increase compared to the previous year.
- The data-center segment remains the primary growth driver, supported by high demand for Blackwell and Hopper-based systems.
- Nvidia is expanding into the $200 billion CPU market, with expectations to generate $20 billion in annual CPU revenue.
- The company maintains a forward price-to-earnings ratio of 25.1, which is significantly lower than industry peers like AMD and Intel.

### Why it Matters
Nvidia’s performance serves as a critical barometer for the broader artificial intelligence investment cycle and the health of global hyperscale capital expenditures. Sustained growth in this sector suggests that AI infrastructure demand remains robust, potentially influencing market sentiment across the technology industry.
