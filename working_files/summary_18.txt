Why privacy must survive the age of AI: Not just humans, even the economy needs it
============================================================

The debate over corporate and individual privacy intensifies as technological advancements and AI surveillance challenge the traditional boundaries of data protection, transparency, and personal autonomy in modern society.

### Key Points
- Tata Sons maintains its private status to prioritize charitable missions and long-term wealth creation without the immediate pressures of public market scrutiny.
- The 2005 merger of Tata Finance Ltd into Tata Motors followed a scandal involving off-market equity trades, highlighting risks inherent in complex corporate structures.
- India’s evolving data protection laws struggle to provide individuals with meaningful control over personal information harvested by large technology companies.
- Digital payment systems like UPI can inadvertently limit financial agency, underscoring the need for digital currencies to replicate the anonymity of physical cash.
- Market efficiency relies on a diverse flow of information, which requires protected spaces for private analysis and independent decision-making to function effectively.

### Why it Matters
Privacy serves as a fundamental requirement for both individual liberty and the healthy functioning of a diverse market economy. Without protected spaces for private strategy and personal action, the ability to generate unique ideas and maintain financial autonomy is significantly diminished.
