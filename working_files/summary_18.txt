3 AI Infrastructure Stocks to Watch Beyond NVIDIA
============================================================

Investors are expanding their focus beyond NVIDIA to secondary AI infrastructure providers like Broadcom, Arista Networks, and Vertiv, which manage essential data center networking, power, and cooling systems.

### Key Points
- Broadcom designs custom AI accelerators and networking silicon to connect thousands of processors within large-scale data centers.
- Arista Networks provides high-speed Ethernet switching and network fabric, enabling massive clusters of AI chips to communicate without performance bottlenecks.
- Vertiv supplies critical power distribution and thermal management systems required to support the high energy density of modern AI hardware.
- All three companies are experiencing significant revenue growth as hyperscalers increase capital expenditures to build out AI-ready infrastructure.
- Each firm represents a distinct physical or technical bottleneck in the AI supply chain, rather than a direct competitor to GPU manufacturers.

### Why it Matters
These companies represent the "second layer" of the AI trade, providing the essential physical and networking foundation required for high-performance computing to function at scale. As data centers face increasing power and connectivity constraints, these infrastructure providers offer a way to invest in the broader AI buildout beyond chip-specific demand.
