Crypto catches its breath as bitcoin settles into a holding pattern amid July rally
============================================================

Bitcoin is currently consolidating between $64,000 and $66,800 as the cryptocurrency market pauses following a 13% recovery from July lows while awaiting a new macro-economic catalyst for direction.

### Key Points
- Bitcoin is trading in a stable range between $64,000 and $66,800 after failing to break the $66,000 resistance level on Tuesday.
- The WLFI token surged 12.18% to reach a $2 billion market capitalization, standing out as the session's primary mover.
- Bitcoin’s 30-day implied volatility index has risen for five consecutive days, a metric that historically signals potential downward price pressure.
- Futures open interest for Bitcoin has declined to 743,000 BTC, suggesting an unwinding of positions as the recent rally stalls.
- Traditional markets, including the Nasdaq 100 and S&P 500, remain flat, providing no clear tailwinds or headwinds for crypto assets.

### Why it Matters
The current market stasis reflects a lack of directional conviction among traders as they wait for fresh economic data to influence price action. While Bitcoin consolidates, the stability of major exchanges like Binance and shifting options demand suggest that investors are hedging against volatility while preparing for the next major move.
