Israeli Ad Firm Doubled as Candiru’s Zero-Click Spyware Delivery Channel
============================================================

Citizen Lab reports that the Israeli firm Discover-Tech used malicious advertisements to deploy Candiru spyware against Al Jazeera staff, infecting at least 45 devices via zero-click attacks.

### Key Points
- Citizen Lab identified the "AdFection" campaign, which utilized Discover-Tech’s Controlium platform to de-anonymize and infect users through real-time bidding ad auctions.
- Between 2024 and 2025, at least 45 devices belonging to Al Jazeera employees were compromised by Candiru spyware.
- Discover-Tech’s infrastructure exposed four billion ad bid records, showing its ads appeared on nearly 200,000 websites and mobile applications.
- Google, IAB Europe, Beeswax, and Eskimi removed Discover-Tech from their ad vendor registries in October following the discovery of these surveillance activities.
- Researchers also linked Discover-Tech to "Dark Cartographer," a group mass-de-anonymizing users by connecting mobile advertising identifiers to private phone numbers.

### Why it Matters
This operation highlights a critical security vulnerability within the global real-time bidding advertising ecosystem, where legitimate ad exchanges can be weaponized to deliver sophisticated spyware. The incident demonstrates how mercenary vendors can bypass traditional defenses to target specific individuals, prompting experts to recommend blocking targeted ads at the network gateway to ensure user safety.
