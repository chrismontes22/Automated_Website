Nintendo Wipes Out 400+ Switch Emulator Repos in Single-Day GitHub Sweep
============================================================

Nintendo has filed seven DMCA anti-circumvention notices with GitHub, resulting in the removal of over 400 repositories associated with various Nintendo Switch emulators and their community-maintained forks.

### Key Points
- Nintendo targeted over 400 repositories, including the popular Yuzu successor "suyu" and the Android-based Skyline emulator.
- The legal notices argue that these emulators violate the DMCA by bypassing cryptographic protections required to run unauthorized Nintendo Switch game files.
- GitHub processed the takedowns by removing entire networks of repositories, including dormant projects and independent forks.
- Nintendo cited the 2024 Tropic Haze settlement and a recent default judgment against streamer Jesse Keighin as legal precedents for their enforcement actions.
- Many affected repositories now display removal notices or 404 errors, though the open-source nature of the code allows for potential future redistribution.

### Why it Matters
These mass takedowns demonstrate Nintendo's aggressive strategy to curb software piracy by systematically removing emulator source code from major hosting platforms. While these actions effectively clear GitHub of current projects, the ease of forking open-source code suggests that developers may continue to revive or mirror these tools, creating an ongoing cycle of enforcement.
