OpenAI rolls out GPT-6 Astra, and Greg Brockman says AGI has arrived
============================================================

OpenAI President Greg Brockman claims the company’s new Astra model has achieved artificial general intelligence, marking a significant milestone in the development of human-level autonomous computing systems.

### Key Points
- OpenAI defines Astra as an AGI system capable of performing complex tasks like financial modeling and software development with minimal human oversight.
- The model was released to business users in the Daybreak program on Thursday, with a version for paid ChatGPT subscribers expected soon.
- Astra reached a critical cybersecurity threshold, meaning it can autonomously identify and build zero-day exploits against hardened computer systems.
- OpenAI implemented cybersecurity guardrails to restrict access to the model's most advanced offensive capabilities.
- Neither the U.S. government nor European regulators formally pre-cleared the model, as current oversight frameworks rely on voluntary reporting and post-market evaluations.

### Why it Matters
The arrival of AGI represents a fundamental shift in the capabilities of autonomous software, raising urgent questions about safety and regulatory oversight. As these models gain the ability to perform complex technical tasks independently, the lack of mandatory pre-release government certification highlights a growing gap between rapid technological advancement and existing policy frameworks.
