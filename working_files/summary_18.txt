Here Are Tuesday’s Top Wall Street Analyst Research Calls: Advanced Micro Devices, Dynatrace, Five Below, Fortune Brands, Klarna Group, Jersey Mike’s Subs, Moderna, Sherwin-Williams, and More
============================================================

Wall Street analysts issued several key research updates on Tuesday, including a strong buy upgrade for Advanced Micro Devices alongside various initiations for Fortune Brands and Jersey Mike’s.

### Key Points
- Raymond James upgraded Advanced Micro Devices to Strong Buy, increasing the price target to $641.
- Morgan Stanley raised Dynatrace to Overweight with a new price target of $65.
- Wolfe Research downgraded Klarna Group to Peer Perform and upgraded Bread Financial Holdings to Outperform.
- DA Davidson initiated coverage on Sherwin-Williams and Fortune Brands with Buy ratings.
- Goldman Sachs initiated Jersey Mike’s Subs with a Neutral rating and a $26 price target.
- Bitcoin continued its recent momentum, trading between $77,000 and $80,000 following a significant short-squeeze rally.

### Why it Matters
These analyst adjustments provide investors with updated sentiment on major tech and retail companies ahead of critical economic data releases. Market participants are closely monitoring upcoming Federal Reserve commentary and inflation reports to gauge the future direction of interest rates.
