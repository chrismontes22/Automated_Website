Everything New in iOS 27.2 Beta 1
============================================================

Apple has released the first iOS 27.2 beta, introducing a redesigned Health app, expanded Siri AI language support, and new accessibility features for FaceTime and Apple TV.

### Key Points
- The revamped Health app features new Insights and Longevity tabs, including a Health Age metric calculated via iPhone camera assessments.
- Siri AI now supports French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Spanish, while Apple TV adds user profiles and sign language interpretation settings.
- A new FaceTime API allows developers to integrate human sign language interpreters directly into video calls.
- CarPlay Ultra receives customizable themes for wallpapers, gauge styles, and ambient lighting synchronization.
- Apple updated App Tracking Transparency to include expanded prompts for users in France, Germany, Italy, Poland, and Romania.

### Why it Matters
This update signals a strategic shift in Apple's software development cycle to protect upcoming hardware secrets while accelerating the rollout of health and AI-driven features. By prioritizing these tools, Apple is deepening its ecosystem integration and addressing regulatory requirements in the European market.
