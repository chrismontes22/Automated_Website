Bitcoin drops under $83K as liquidity hunting keeps bulls from targeting yearly open
============================================================

Bitcoin prices fell below $83,000 on Monday as geopolitical tensions and significant sell-side liquidity on exchange order books halted the cryptocurrency's recent upward momentum toward yearly highs.

### Key Points
- Bitcoin dropped below $82,700 for the first time since September 21 following a failed test of the $87,000 resistance level.
- Over $30 million in ask liquidity appeared at $85,700, effectively blocking further price gains and triggering approximately $70 million in long liquidations.
- Market volatility increased after U.S. President Donald Trump refused to rule out further military strikes against Iran during a weekend appearance.
- Nasdaq futures declined by 0.9% while WTI crude oil prices climbed above $95 per barrel amid rising regional instability.
- Analyst Aksel Kibar warned that hesitant price action could force Bitcoin back into its previous trading range of $60,000 to $80,000.

### Why it Matters
The recent price drop highlights how Bitcoin remains sensitive to both large-scale liquidity manipulation and broader geopolitical instability. Investors are closely watching these levels to determine if the asset can maintain its recent gains or if it will revert to its long-term trading range.
