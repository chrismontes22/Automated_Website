The AI Productivity Gap
============================================================

Artificial intelligence tools provide measurable productivity gains for software engineering teams, but the impact remains limited because coding represents only a fraction of a developer's daily responsibilities.

### Key Points
- AI tools reduce daily coding time for senior developers by one hour and for junior developers by 1.75 hours.
- Total daily productivity gains are estimated at 15% for senior engineers and 25% for junior engineers.
- Junior developers experience higher efficiency boosts because a larger portion of their workload involves writing new code.
- Non-coding tasks, including system architecture, meetings, and code reviews, remain largely unaffected by current AI capabilities.
- AI-generated documentation can sometimes decrease efficiency by requiring more time to distill information compared to human-written content.

### Why it Matters
The data suggests that leadership expectations for massive, immediate productivity spikes from AI are often misaligned with the reality of software development workflows. Organizations that prioritize hiring senior staff under the assumption that AI replaces junior roles may overlook the significant efficiency gains that entry-level developers can achieve with these tools.
