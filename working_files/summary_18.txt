Final UI for Googlebook ‘Desktop Camera’ app revealed [Gallery]
============================================================

Google has updated its Desktop Camera application on the Play Store with a responsive interface designed specifically for upcoming Googlebook laptops and tablets launching this fall.

### Key Points
- The application features a modern design language consistent with Google’s first-party Pixel software.
- The interface is optimized for larger screens, supporting landscape orientation for tablets, laptops, and desktops.
- Users can connect and switch between external USB webcams for improved video capture.
- Integrated machine learning tools enable automatic document cropping, enhancement, and QR code scanning.
- Controls are positioned along the screen edges to accommodate mouse, keyboard, and touch-based navigation.

### Why it Matters
This release signals Google's commitment to optimizing its software ecosystem for the new Googlebook hardware lineup. By providing a dedicated camera experience for larger form factors, the company aims to improve productivity and usability for users transitioning away from traditional mobile devices.
