Cyberattack Targets French Supermarket Refrigerators, Forcing Mass Food Disposal
============================================================

Hackers targeted hundreds of French supermarkets by exploiting outdated firmware in Danfoss controllers, forcing refrigeration units into defrost mode and causing significant food spoilage and equipment fires.

### Key Points
- Major grocery chains including E. Leclerc, Intermarche, and Cooperative U reported coordinated attacks on their refrigeration systems.
- Attackers remotely forced connected fridges and freezers to reach temperatures of 50 degrees Celsius (122 degrees Fahrenheit).
- The breach targeted Danfoss AK-SM800 and AK-SC355 system controllers that were running outdated, internet-exposed firmware.
- Danfoss is currently working with affected retailers to implement security patches and remove direct internet exposure for these industrial devices.
- French authorities and retail executives have launched investigations into the incident, citing the unprecedented nature of the threat to the national food supply.

### Why it Matters
This incident demonstrates how vulnerabilities in operational technology can lead to physical damage and significant disruptions to critical supply chains. It highlights the urgent need for businesses to secure internet-connected industrial equipment against cyber threats that can manipulate real-world infrastructure.
