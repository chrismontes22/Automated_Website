Coinbase CEO Says America Needs CLARITY Act: 'It's a Win for the Future of America'
============================================================

Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong is urging the U.S. Senate to pass the CLARITY Act, citing polling data that suggests strong bipartisan voter support for comprehensive cryptocurrency regulatory reform.

### Key Points
- Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong claims one in four American adults currently own cryptocurrency, with a majority of holders under age 45.
- Polling data cited by Armstrong indicates that voters are twice as likely to support political candidates who back the CLARITY Act.
- The proposed legislation aims to establish a comprehensive regulatory framework to strengthen consumer protections and combat illicit financial activity.
- The bill includes provisions to grant banks increased authority to integrate stablecoins and other digital asset services into their operations.
- Approximately 70% of Americans believe the United States should implement clear regulatory standards for the cryptocurrency industry, according to survey data.

### Why it Matters
The passage of the CLARITY Act would provide a formal legal framework for digital assets, potentially encouraging crypto firms to remain in the U.S. rather than moving operations offshore. This shift could significantly influence the future of American financial innovation and determine how traditional banking institutions integrate emerging blockchain technologies.
