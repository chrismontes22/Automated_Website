HomeKit Weekly: This iPad app turns your HomeKit setup into a wall-mountable command center
============================================================

The Dashboard for your home app transforms older iPads into centralized smart home control centers, allowing users to manage Apple HomeKit devices, cameras, and calendars from one screen.

### Key Points
- The app supports live previews for up to four cameras, with unlimited access available through a $9.99 annual Premium subscription.
- Users can control up to four thermostats and eight switches, organized by room, alongside a six-day local weather forecast.
- A 3D floor plan feature allows users to upload home layouts and toggle devices like lights and locks via interactive icons.
- The interface includes a seven-day calendar view with countdown timers and supports automatic wake-up via motion sensors or the iPad's front camera.
- Configuration settings can be synced across multiple devices using iCloud, and the developer states the app collects no user data.
- The software requires iPadOS 17 or later and is compatible with existing Apple HomeKit setups.

### Why it Matters
This application provides a practical way to repurpose older hardware into a dedicated interface for managing complex smart home ecosystems. By centralizing controls and visual data, it enhances the utility of Apple HomeKit for users seeking a streamlined, wall-mounted management solution.
