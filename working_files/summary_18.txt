ClioCon 2026: A Quick Wrap-Up Of Everything Clio Announced On Opening Day (And It’s A Lot)
============================================================

Clio unveiled a major platform redesign and several AI-driven tools at its annual ClioCon conference, aiming to transition legal software from administrative tracking to autonomous task execution.

### Key Points
- Clio consolidated its Grow, Manage, and Work products into a single, unified interface featuring a persistent navigation system and a centralized inbox.
- The company introduced "Agentic Workflows," allowing firms to automate complex tasks like document drafting, conflict checks, and medical record analysis using plain-language instructions.
- New features include "Clio Phones" for automated call transcription and timekeeping, and a comprehensive document management system with automatic categorization.
- Clio acquired the AI-powered regulatory monitoring platform Page and the legal content provider Jurisage to expand its research and intelligence capabilities.
- The company launched "judiciary-grade AI" initiatives, appointing former Michigan Supreme Court chief justice Bridget Mary McCormack to lead its efforts in court-facing technology.

### Why it Matters
These updates signal a shift in the legal tech market toward agentic AI that performs substantive legal work rather than just managing administrative data. By integrating these tools directly into a unified platform, Clio is positioning itself to capture more billable hours and increase firm profitability through automation.
