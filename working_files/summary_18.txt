Crypto Bears Burned as Short Liquidations Hit $1.06 Billion in a Day
============================================================

Cryptocurrency markets experienced a massive short squeeze as $1.06 billion in bearish positions were liquidated following a 3.7% surge in total market capitalization led by Bitcoin and XRP.

### Key Points
- Bitcoin accounted for $789.68 million in liquidations as its price climbed 8.4% to reach an intraday high of $75,744.
- Ethereum and XRP recorded $206.88 million and $41.94 million in liquidations, respectively, with XRP gaining 16.2% to reach $1.26.
- A total of 141,191 traders were liquidated across the market within a 24-hour period.
- The rally was fueled by a self-reinforcing squeeze where forced exchange purchases of Bitcoin to cover short positions drove prices higher.
- Market momentum traces back to Treasury debt buyback operations that began on August 19, catching bearish traders off guard.

### Why it Matters
This liquidation event highlights the volatility inherent in leveraged crypto trading when market sentiment shifts rapidly due to macroeconomic policy changes. The sustainability of these price gains now depends on whether spot market buyers can maintain momentum as the initial short-covering squeeze subsides.
