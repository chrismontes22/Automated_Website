The IT Manager Upgraded Everyone To Wireless Laptops, But One Manager Called Them “Junk”
============================================================

A company’s IT manager successfully navigated a workplace conflict after a general manager incorrectly labeled new wireless laptops as defective because he failed to charge their batteries.

### Key Points
- A family-owned business upgraded its management team to wireless laptops to improve efficiency and reduce costs.
- The company’s general manager publicly complained that his new device was "junk" after it failed to power on.
- The IT manager demonstrated that the laptop was functional once plugged into a power source, revealing the device had simply run out of battery.
- The company owner publicly corrected the general manager, noting that portable devices require regular charging.
- Following the public embarrassment, the general manager resigned from his position two weeks later.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the importance of basic digital literacy for leadership roles in an increasingly technology-driven workplace. It also serves as a reminder that technical support staff often face challenges stemming from user error rather than actual hardware failures.
