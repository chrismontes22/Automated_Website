How To Change IP Address On iPhone: 7 Free Ways
============================================================

iPhone users can change their public or local IP addresses using various methods, including VPNs, network switching, or router adjustments, to improve privacy and resolve connectivity issues.

### Key Points
- Public IP addresses are assigned by ISPs and identify your device to websites, while local IP addresses are managed by routers for internal network communication.
- VPN services like X-VPN provide the most effective way to change your public IP while simultaneously encrypting traffic for enhanced security on public Wi-Fi.
- Switching between cellular data and Wi-Fi or restarting your home router are quick, free ways to obtain a new dynamic public IP address.
- Onion Browser (Tor) offers high anonymity for browsing but significantly reduces connection speeds compared to standard VPN or proxy configurations.
- Manual IP configuration in iOS Wi-Fi settings allows users to resolve local network conflicts by assigning a static address to their device.

### Why it Matters
Changing your IP address is a practical step for users looking to bypass regional content restrictions, mask their physical location from advertisers, or secure data on public networks. Understanding the distinction between public and local addresses ensures users select the correct method to either protect their online privacy or troubleshoot home network connectivity.
