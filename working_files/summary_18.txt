One of the best AMD gaming laptops on sale delivers huge value from an RTX 5070 GPU, giving top-tier performance without paying $1,000 for the card alone
============================================================

Walmart is currently offering an 18% discount on the ASUS ROG Strix G16 gaming laptop, providing a cost-effective alternative to purchasing standalone NVIDIA RTX 5070 graphics cards.

### Key Points
- The ASUS ROG Strix G16 is discounted to $1,649.99, down from its original $1,999.99 price point.
- The laptop features an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5070 GPU paired with an AMD Ryzen 9 8940HX processor.
- Hardware specifications include 16GB of RAM and a 1TB SSD, with upgradeability up to 64GB of RAM and 8TB of storage.
- The device includes a 165Hz FHD+ display, an advanced cooling system, and a three-month Xbox Game Pass subscription.
- Standalone RTX 5070 cards currently retail between $850 and $1,000, making pre-built laptops a more economical choice for many consumers.

### Why it Matters
Rising prices and limited availability for standalone NVIDIA RTX 5000 series graphics cards have made pre-built gaming laptops a more viable value proposition for PC gamers. This discount offers a practical solution for users seeking high-end performance without the inflated costs associated with individual component purchases.
