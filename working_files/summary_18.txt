Apple Home Collection Introduces New Cameras, Doorbell, Lock, and Thermostats
============================================================

Apple has introduced the Apple Home Collection, a new lineup of smart home security cameras, locks, and thermostats developed in collaboration with LG Electronics and Schneider Electric.

### Key Points
- The collection includes four LG security cameras ranging from $199.95 to $399.95, featuring 2.5K resolution and end-to-end encryption via iCloud+.
- The $349.95 LG Smart Deadbolt Lock utilizes Ultra Wideband technology for automatic unlocking and supports the Matter and Aliro standards for local control.
- Two smart thermostats from LG and Schneider Electric are priced at $199.95 and include Adaptive Temperature and Clean Energy Guidance features.
- Products integrate directly into the Apple Home app and Siri, requiring no third-party accounts or additional software for setup.
- Availability begins in December 2026, with professional installation services offered through ADT for customers in the United States.

### Why it Matters
This partnership allows Apple to curate a premium, unified smart home experience without the operational burden of manufacturing low-margin hardware. By leveraging established partners, Apple aims to solve market fragmentation while driving higher adoption of its iCloud+ subscription services and ecosystem-exclusive features.
