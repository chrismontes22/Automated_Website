Demand For Dead Data; ChatGPT’s Miraculous, Disappearing Advertisers
============================================================

Google acquired a massive trove of operational data from the bankrupt Spirit Airlines for $10 million to help train and improve its artificial intelligence models and products.

### Key Points
- Google purchased 100 million emails, 500 million Microsoft Teams records, and various operational data from Spirit Airlines.
- The deal excludes sensitive passenger information and loyalty program profiles, focusing instead on business and productivity data.
- AI recruitment firm Mercor.io was the second-highest bidder for the data, offering $7.5 million.
- OpenAI is expanding advertising into Mexico, Brazil, and Europe while addressing technical bugs in its ad-serving units.
- A study in the "Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences" found that X’s algorithm often amplifies content that contradicts user values to drive engagement.

### Why it Matters
The acquisition highlights the growing trend of major tech companies purchasing internal corporate data from failed businesses to fuel the development of large language models. This practice raises significant questions regarding data privacy and the ethical standards governing how AI developers source information to train their systems.
