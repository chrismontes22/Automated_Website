How Crypto Trading Is Changing With Zero Fees and AI
============================================================

Japan’s Arumando won the $100,000 Alpha Arena S03 trading competition in Bali, highlighting how MEXC is evolving beyond crypto to offer AI-driven tools and diverse financial products.

### Key Points
- The Alpha Arena S03 competition, held during CoinFest Asia, featured 20 traders competing in a live, simulated environment.
- MEXC reported that its zero-fee trading initiatives saved 3.44 million users approximately 1.1 billion USDT throughout 2025.
- The exchange now offers access to over 7,000 global stocks and ETFs, alongside tokenized stocks and index futures.
- CEO Vugar Usi is prioritizing AI-assisted tools to provide retail traders with institutional-grade market analysis and order book insights.
- MEXC maintains transparency through monthly Proof of Reserves audits by Hacken and is expanding its Guardian Fund toward a $500 million target.

### Why it Matters
As trading fees approach zero across the industry, crypto exchanges are shifting their competitive focus toward product quality, advanced analytical tools, and comprehensive financial services. This transition toward "financial super apps" reflects a broader effort to capture market share by integrating traditional equities, AI-driven insights, and enhanced security measures for retail users.
