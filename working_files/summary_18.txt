OpenAI launches Codex Micro hardware for AI coding
============================================================

OpenAI has launched the Codex Micro, a $230 programmable macro pad designed to streamline workflows for users managing multiple AI coding agents through the company’s specialized Codex platform.

### Key Points
- The device features 13 mechanical keys, a rotary dial for adjusting AI reasoning levels, and a joystick for triggering common coding workflows.
- Six translucent "Agent Keys" use RGB lighting to provide real-time status updates on active AI tasks, such as idle, working, or error states.
- Developed in collaboration with Work Louder, the hardware is based on the existing Creator Micro 2 and connects via Bluetooth or USB-C.
- The product is sold through OpenAI’s Supply Co. store and sold out within 24 hours of its initial release.
- Codex has reached 5 million weekly active users as of June, marking a sixfold increase since February.

### Why it Matters
This release signals OpenAI's strategic shift toward hardware integration to support its rapidly growing base of power users and AI-assisted developers. By physicalizing the management of multiple AI agents, the company aims to reduce workflow friction and deepen user reliance on the Codex ecosystem.
