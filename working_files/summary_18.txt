This Week in AI: The Guardrails Are Getting Tested
============================================================

OpenAI’s restricted release of its new cyber model and surging data center energy demands highlight the rapid, often unpredictable, evolution of artificial intelligence across global security and infrastructure.

### Key Points
- OpenAI restricted access to its new GPT-5.6 Cyber model, citing concerns over its advanced vulnerability-detection capabilities.
- IBM reports a 56% year-over-year increase in AI-enabled security breaches, with total victim notifications exceeding 471 million this year.
- Global data center electricity consumption is projected to nearly double by 2030, reaching 950 terawatt-hours as demand outpaces grid capacity.
- Unitree’s recent Shanghai IPO raised $905 million, highlighting investor interest despite humanoid robots struggling with basic tasks like operating fire extinguishers.
- AMD launched its Helios system using EPYC 9006 chips to challenge NVIDIA’s market dominance in high-performance AI hardware.

### Why it Matters
The rapid acceleration of AI development is creating significant friction between technological capability and the physical infrastructure required to support it. As security threats evolve and energy scarcity grows, companies must prioritize robust governance and power-ready sites to ensure long-term operational stability.
