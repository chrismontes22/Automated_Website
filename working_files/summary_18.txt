Most Laptops Seal the Battery In, Dynabook’s New One Lets You Swap It
============================================================

Dynabook has introduced new Panther Lake-powered laptops featuring user-replaceable batteries to combat the accelerated hardware degradation caused by intensive on-device artificial intelligence processing tasks.

### Key Points
- Dynabook’s 2026 XP9/XP7 and X9/X7 models feature a tool-free sliding panel allowing users to replace batteries independently.
- The laptops utilize Intel’s Core Ultra Series 3 Panther Lake processors to support local AI workloads and privacy features.
- The 14-inch XP9/XP7 weighs 999 grams, while the 13.3-inch X9/X7 weighs 948 grams, both meeting MIL-STD-810H durability standards.
- Connectivity options include Thunderbolt 4, USB 3.2, HDMI, LAN, and microSD slots, eliminating the need for external adapters.
- The G9 model offers a lighter 899-gram alternative, though it lacks the user-replaceable battery design found in the X-series.
- All models launched in Japan on August 7, with no official confirmation regarding a global release date.

### Why it Matters
On-device AI processing significantly increases power consumption, leading to faster battery wear that often renders otherwise functional laptops obsolete. By enabling user-replaceable batteries, Dynabook addresses both the financial burden of professional repairs and the environmental impact of premature electronic waste.
