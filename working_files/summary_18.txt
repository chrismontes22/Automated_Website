uBlock v1.75.1b0
============================================================

uBlock Origin faces significant operational changes as Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge prepare to phase out Manifest V2 extensions by late 2026, impacting future content blocker compatibility.

### Key Points
- Google Chrome will remove all remaining Manifest V2 extensions from its Web Store by August 31, 2026.
- Microsoft Edge plans to complete its transition from Manifest V2 to Manifest V3 extensions by the end of 2026.
- uBlock Origin remains fully supported on Firefox, which continues to offer the most stable environment for the extension.
- Users of Chromium-based browsers and Thunderbird may face manual update requirements or limited functionality as official store support declines.
- The developer advises against using uBlock Origin alongside other content blockers to ensure optimal privacy and anti-malware performance.

### Why it Matters
The shift toward Manifest V3 forces a major architectural change for ad-blocking software, potentially limiting the effectiveness of traditional tools like uBlock Origin on Chromium browsers. Users may need to transition to alternative versions or different browsers to maintain their current level of privacy and ad-blocking capabilities.
