Sam Altman: 'The World Should Accept Some Bad Things Happening' For the Benefits of AI
============================================================

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman argues that society must accept minor negative consequences from artificial intelligence to ensure widespread public access and the realization of the technology's broader benefits.

### Key Points
- Sam Altman stated that the potential for positive societal impact outweighs risks like scams or minor misuse of AI technology.
- OpenAI has shifted its strategy to support stricter state-level safety requirements and bipartisan House proposals for independent AI model evaluations.
- Altman explicitly rejected the idea of a single lab controlling powerful AI, labeling such centralized oversight an unacceptable trade-off for safety.
- The CEO clarified that he does not accept catastrophic risks, specifically citing the potential for a serious loss of human control over AI systems.

### Why it Matters
This shift in regulatory philosophy highlights a growing divide between major AI labs regarding the balance between safety, innovation, and market competition. As OpenAI aligns with stricter legislative frameworks, the industry faces ongoing debates about whether centralized control or open access best serves the public interest.
