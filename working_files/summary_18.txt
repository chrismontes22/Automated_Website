Cyberattack on Ceva Logistics warehouses in Europe impacts retailers
============================================================

Ceva Logistics is investigating a cyberattack that disrupted operations at eight European warehouses, causing shipping delays and potential data exposure for retail customers including bol and De Bijenkorf.

### Key Points
- Eight Ceva Logistics warehouses in Europe experienced operational disruptions following a cyber intrusion discovered around August 1.
- Retailers bol and De Bijenkorf confirmed that unauthorized parties may have accessed customer data processed through the affected distribution centers.
- Ceva Logistics, a global freight provider with $18.3 billion in annual revenue, has not publicly commented on the breach.
- The Dutch Data Protection Authority and law enforcement agencies are currently investigating the incident.
- Air, ocean, ground, and rail transportation management systems remain unaffected by the security breach.
- Affected retailers have temporarily suspended data exchanges with the logistics provider and canceled some customer orders.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the significant vulnerability of global supply chains to cyberattacks targeting third-party logistics providers. The breach demonstrates how a localized digital intrusion can create cascading operational and data privacy risks for major e-commerce retailers and their customers.
