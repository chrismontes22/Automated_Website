US tech stocks tumble as investors fret over AI spending
============================================================

US tech stocks experienced a sharp decline as investors grew increasingly concerned about the sustainability of massive capital investments in artificial intelligence and rising global oil prices.

### Key Points
- The Nasdaq Composite fell 2.6 percent on Thursday, marking its steepest single-day decline since June 5.
- Meta shares dropped nearly 7 percent after the company projected annual capital spending between $135 billion and $145 billion.
- Major tech firms including Amazon, Nvidia, Intel, and Advanced Micro Devices saw significant losses, while Apple shares rose 1.2 percent.
- Brent crude oil prices climbed above $114.20 per barrel, contributing to broader market instability and investor anxiety.
- Gartner forecasts global AI investment will reach $2.7 trillion by 2026 and $3.6 trillion by 2027.

### Why it Matters
The sell-off highlights growing market skepticism regarding whether the massive capital expenditures in AI infrastructure will yield sufficient long-term returns. This volatility reflects a broader debate among analysts over whether the current tech rally is a sustainable revolution or a speculative bubble similar to the dot-com era.
