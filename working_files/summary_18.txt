Ahead of Market: 10 things that will decide stock market action on Monday
============================================================

Indian equities closed higher on Friday as the Sensex rose 363 points to 76,515, while investors remained cautious ahead of critical US employment and inflation data releases.

### Key Points
- The Sensex climbed to 76,515, while the Nifty 50 ended the session slightly below the 23,898 mark.
- Market sentiment was bolstered by resilient domestic economic growth and strong earnings momentum despite persistent geopolitical tensions in the Middle East.
- Vodafone Idea led volume-based trading with 52.55 crore shares, while Swiggy recorded the highest turnover at Rs 5,089 crore.
- The broader market showed mixed results, with Nifty Smallcap 100 indices reaching fresh intraday lifetime highs while Nifty Midcap 100 declined.
- European markets saw modest gains, with the STOXX 600 rising 0.1% as Volkswagen shares surged 5.9% following a new turnaround plan.

### Why it Matters
Global market participants are closely monitoring US economic data to gauge the Federal Reserve's future interest rate trajectory and potential policy shifts. This uncertainty continues to influence investor behavior, keeping trading volumes volatile as markets balance domestic growth against international inflationary pressures.
