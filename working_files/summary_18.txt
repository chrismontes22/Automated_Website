Standard Chartered expands Bitcoin and Ether trading to professional investors in UAE
============================================================

Standard Chartered has expanded its spot Bitcoin and Ether trading services to professional investors in the United Arab Emirates, allowing eligible clients to trade assets through existing bank accounts.

### Key Points
- Professional investors must hold at least 4 million dirhams, approximately $1.1 million, in net assets excluding their primary residence.
- The service allows eligible clients to trade Bitcoin and Ether directly within their established Standard Chartered bank accounts.
- This expansion follows the bank's initial launch of crypto trading services for institutional clients in the UAE during early September.
- Other financial entities, including Kraken and Revolut, have recently secured regulatory approvals to expand digital asset offerings within the region.

### Why it Matters
This move signals the increasing integration of digital assets into mainstream wealth management as traditional banks seek to provide regulated environments for crypto trading. By offering these services, Standard Chartered aims to capture growing demand from high-net-worth individuals who prioritize institutional security and oversight.
