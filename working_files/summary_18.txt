I spent $200 on thrifted 4K Blu-rays instead of digital rentals, and my local Plex library paid for itself in months
============================================================

Building a personal media server using thrifted 4K Blu-rays and software like MakeMKV offers superior video quality and permanent ownership compared to expensive, compressed commercial streaming service rentals.

### Key Points
- Commercial streaming services compress 4K video to 15Mbps–25Mbps, while 4K Blu-ray discs provide higher bitrates ranging from 60Mbps to over 100Mbps.
- Secondhand 4K discs can be sourced from thrift stores, pawn shops, and online marketplaces for $5 to $8, significantly undercutting the cost of digital rentals.
- Users can utilize MakeMKV software to rip uncompressed movie files from physical discs for integration into a personal Plex media server.
- Building a high-quality library requires an Ultra HD-friendly Blu-ray drive, which typically costs around $100 as a one-time hardware investment.
- Physical media provides permanent access to content, avoiding the recurring fees and library rotations associated with subscription-based streaming platforms.

### Why it Matters
This approach allows consumers to bypass the limitations of digital rental models, which often restrict access and provide lower-quality, compressed video files. By investing in physical media and local hosting, users gain long-term ownership of their content while achieving reference-grade audio and visual performance.
