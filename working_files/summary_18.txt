iPhone 18 Pro Sales Surge in China, Offsetting Decline in U.S. and Japan
============================================================

Apple’s iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max global sales rose 3.1% during their initial three-week launch period, driven by strong demand in China despite declines in other markets.

### Key Points
- Counterpoint Research reported a 29% sales increase in China, helping offset a 4% decline in the U.S. and a 27% drop in Japan.
- Apple increased the starting price for the iPhone 18 Pro to $1,199 and the Pro Max to $1,299 in the U.S. market.
- The iPhone 18 Pro Max saw its share of total sales dip slightly from 57% to 56% as smaller models gained traction in India and Southeast Asia.
- Analysts attributed China's growth to improved initial supply chain availability and higher price points for competing Android flagship smartphones.
- Market observers are now monitoring how the October 23 launch of the foldable iPhone Duo will impact future sales of the Pro series.

### Why it Matters
The shift in regional performance highlights Apple's growing reliance on the Chinese market to sustain global growth as consumer interest in the U.S. wanes due to perceived lack of innovation. This trend forces the company to navigate complex pricing strategies and evolving hardware preferences as it prepares to compete in the emerging foldable smartphone category.
