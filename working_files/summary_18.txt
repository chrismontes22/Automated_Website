7 Must-Have Back-to-School Tech Gadgets Every Teacher Needs
============================================================

These seven practical technology gadgets are designed to help teachers streamline daily classroom operations by solving common logistical frustrations rather than adding unnecessary complexity to their teaching routines.

### Key Points
- The IPEVO V4K Pro ($199) allows teachers to project physical worksheets and science experiments for the entire class to view simultaneously.
- Logitech’s R500s Wireless Presenter ($35.99) enables instructors to navigate slide decks while moving freely around the room.
- The Brother P-touch CUBE ($69.99) simplifies classroom organization by allowing teachers to print custom labels directly from a smartphone via Bluetooth.
- The Time Timer MOD ($17.96) provides a visual representation of remaining time to help students manage transitions and independent work periods.
- The Anker Nano 7-in-1 Charging Station ($69.99) and the 10K Power Bank ($27.99) help manage multiple device power needs and prevent mid-day battery depletion.
- The JBL Clip 5 ($69.95) offers a portable, dust-resistant audio solution for classrooms that lack adequate built-in sound systems.

### Why it Matters
By focusing on specific, recurring classroom friction points, these tools help educators reduce daily administrative burdens and improve instructional flow. Investing in targeted technology can save time and minimize distractions, allowing teachers to dedicate more energy to student engagement rather than managing equipment.
