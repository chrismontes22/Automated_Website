PubX Raises $5 Million And Buys Digital Governance Startup Compliant
============================================================

UK-based ad tech startup pubX has secured $5 million in Series A funding and acquired media governance firm Compliant to advance its autonomous agent-to-agent advertising platform.

### Key Points
- PubX raised $5 million in a Series A round led by Chicago Ventures to fund US expansion and integrate new data integrity tools.
- The acquisition of Compliant, co-founded by former Unilever executive Jamie Barnard, adds media quality and governance signals to the pubX platform.
- PubX utilizes the Ad Context Protocol (AdCP) to enable direct, autonomous transactions between buyer and seller agents without traditional DSP or SSP intermediaries.
- The platform provides a live log of agent decision-making, allowing brands to calibrate autonomy levels and review the rationale behind specific media buys.
- Jamie Barnard will join pubX as president of global demand growth to oversee the integration of quality metrics into the agentic workflow.

### Why it Matters
The shift toward agentic advertising aims to simplify open-web media buying, potentially making it as efficient and user-friendly as the dominant walled-garden ecosystems. By embedding governance and transparency directly into the transaction process, pubX seeks to overcome the trust barriers that currently prevent brands from fully automating their digital media budgets.
