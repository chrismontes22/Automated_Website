Data Centers Are a $6 Trillion Time Bomb, Analysts Warn
============================================================

A new report from Bain and Co suggests the global AI industry must reach $6 trillion in annual revenue by 2031 to justify current data center infrastructure investments.

### Key Points
- Bain and Co estimates that AI infrastructure requires $6 trillion in annual revenue by 2031 to remain financially sustainable.
- Major hyperscalers including Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, and Oracle may spend up to $780 billion on data centers in 2026 alone.
- Praetorian Capital founder Harris Kupperman warns that high equipment turnover costs make current data center buildouts economically precarious.
- Achieving the necessary revenue growth would require adding approximately one percent to the annual global GDP growth rate.
- Analysts suggest that if AI application demand fails to materialize, the current buildout could represent a historic misallocation of capital.

### Why it Matters
The massive capital expenditure on data centers creates a significant financial risk if the AI industry cannot generate sufficient revenue to cover infrastructure costs. This potential disconnect between infrastructure investment and actual market demand could trigger a broader economic crisis if the expected technological growth fails to occur.
