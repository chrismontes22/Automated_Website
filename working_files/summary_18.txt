Alibaba Is A Tech Giant, But Who Actually Owns It?
============================================================

Alibaba Group, a massive Chinese e-commerce conglomerate founded in 1999, operates as a publicly traded company with a diverse portfolio spanning global retail, digital payments, and artificial intelligence.

### Key Points
- Alibaba Group moved over $1.26 trillion in products during 2025, significantly outpacing major global retailers like Walmart.
- The company was co-founded in Hangzhou by 18 individuals, including Jack Ma, Eddie Wu, and Joseph Tsai.
- Ownership is distributed among approximately 1,800 institutional shareholders, with directors and executives holding a combined 1.9% stake.
- The firm’s ecosystem includes the Alipay payment platform, the Taobao marketplace, and the Qwen artificial intelligence model.
- Alibaba maintains a broad media and technology footprint through assets like the South China Morning Post, Weibo, and the AliOS operating system.

### Why it Matters
Alibaba’s extensive diversification across e-commerce, finance, and AI allows it to capture significant market share across multiple global consumer verticals. Its ability to integrate these services creates a powerful technological ecosystem that influences digital infrastructure and consumer behavior far beyond its original retail roots.
