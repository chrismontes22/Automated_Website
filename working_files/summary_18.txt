Apple weekend deals: AirPods, up to $200 off new Macs, Apple Watch, more from $41

Following the 2026 fall Prime Day event, major retailers are offering significant discounts on Apple hardware, including all-time low prices on the M6 Mac mini and M5 Mac Studio.

- The M6 Mac mini is available starting at $780, while the M5 Max Mac Studio has dropped to $2,299.

- Various iPad Air and iPad Pro models, including M4 and M5 configurations, are seeing price cuts of up to $200.
- Accessories like the new 2026 Apple Magic Keyboard and iPhone 18 Pro cases are currently discounted alongside exclusive deals on Nomad products.

### Why it Matters
These post-Prime Day deals provide consumers with rare opportunities to purchase the latest Apple silicon hardware at their lowest recorded prices. The widespread nature of these discounts across both new and flagship devices suggests a strong push to clear inventory following the major retail event.
