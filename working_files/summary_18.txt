India GCCs move from execution hubs to strategic centres as AI reshapes work, talent and workspace
============================================================

India’s Global Capability Centers are evolving into strategic innovation hubs, driving significant growth in AI talent demand and record-breaking commercial office leasing across major metropolitan technology centers.

### Key Points
- India currently hosts 2,117 Global Capability Centers (GCCs) employing over 2.36 million professionals and generating an estimated $98.4 billion in revenue for FY26.
- The nation’s AI and machine learning talent pool is projected to reach 250,000 professionals by 2026, ranking India as the world's second-largest AI market.
- GCCs accounted for 9.1 million square feet of office leasing in Q1 2026, representing 44% of India's total quarterly commercial real estate demand.
- Bengaluru and Hyderabad remain the primary hubs, collectively capturing 67% of the total office space demand from GCCs.
- Approximately 67% of GCCs plan to increase their allocation of flexible workspaces to over 10% of their total real estate portfolios to improve operational agility.

### Why it Matters
The shift of GCCs from cost-saving execution centers to strategic innovation hubs signals a fundamental change in India's role within the global corporate value chain. This transition forces companies to prioritize flexible, scalable infrastructure and specialized talent to maintain competitiveness in an AI-driven market.
