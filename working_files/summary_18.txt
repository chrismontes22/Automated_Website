Presentation: You Can't Trust Your AI Bill. And Neither Can I
============================================================

CloudZero founder Erik Peterson warns that uncontrolled AI spending is creating a financial crisis, as businesses struggle to manage rising costs and lack of visibility into token consumption.

### Key Points
- Enterprise AI spending has surged by 222% despite a 1,000% decrease in individual token costs, a phenomenon Peterson describes as an inversion of traditional efficiency.
- CloudZero data shows a 14-fold increase in generative AI spending over a five-month period, with 80% of costs attributed to overhead, R&D, and inefficient system loops.
- The "cost to build" has eclipsed the "cost to serve," as companies prioritize rapid AI adoption over tracking the business outcomes of their investments.
- Lack of standardized billing and "invisible" costs make it difficult for organizations to audit AI expenses, leading to potential "Denial-of-Wallet" security risks.
- Peterson predicts that by 2028, autonomous AI systems will handle the majority of code writing, deployment, and management, necessitating a shift toward unit-economic metrics.

### Why it Matters
The rapid, top-down adoption of AI is forcing a shift in corporate financial responsibility from engineering teams to the CFO’s office. Companies that fail to connect AI spending to measurable business outcomes risk significant financial instability and operational inefficiency as autonomous systems begin to dominate infrastructure.
