Synology reveals a host of new NAS devices it says can balance budget and power requirements for all users
============================================================

Synology has launched the DiskStation neo+ Series, a new lineup of four NAS models featuring lower default memory configurations to provide users with more flexible, cost-effective entry points.

### Key Points
- The neo+ lineup includes the DS725neo+, DS925neo+, DS1525neo+, and DS1825neo+ models.
- All units ship with 4GB of memory, which users can manually expand up to 32GB.
- The DS725neo+ utilizes an AMD Ryzen R1600 processor, while the other three models feature the AMD Ryzen V1500B.
- Larger models include PCIe 3.0 slots for 10GbE or 25GbE networking and M.2 slots for SSD caching.
- Every device runs the standard Synology DiskStation Manager (DSM) operating system and supports DX525 storage expansion units.

### Why it Matters
This strategy allows Synology to lower the barrier to entry for businesses and home users by reducing initial hardware costs. By offering expandable memory, the company provides a scalable path that enables customers to increase performance as their storage and data requirements grow over time.
