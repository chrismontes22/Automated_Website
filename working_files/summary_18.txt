New Mexico Judge Appoints Himself Meta’s New Product Manager
============================================================

A New Mexico judge has ordered Meta to implement significant product design changes for minors, effectively acting as a product manager to address alleged harms to youth mental health.

### Key Points
- Judge Bryan Biedscheid ordered Meta to pay an additional $567 million in remedies following a state lawsuit regarding child safety.
- The ruling mandates age verification for all users to enforce restrictions on accounts belonging to individuals under 18.
- Meta must disable push notifications during school hours and overnight, and hide "like" counts for minor accounts by default.
- The court requires Meta to display daily information screens about safe platform use and fund a state-approved educational campaign.
- The judge prohibited Meta from re-enabling end-to-end encryption on Instagram, citing concerns over adolescent safety.

### Why it Matters
This ruling sets a controversial precedent by allowing a judge to dictate specific software design features and user interface elements rather than relying on industry experts or empirical data. It raises significant concerns regarding the limits of judicial authority over private technology companies and the potential for unintended negative consequences when courts mandate product changes without rigorous testing or scientific consensus.
