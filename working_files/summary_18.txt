Android-powered Arc X1 gaming handheld launches with 7-inch display, Snapdragon G2 Gen 2
============================================================

India-based gaming company Arc has officially launched the Arc X1, a new handheld gaming device featuring a Snapdragon G2 Gen 2 chipset and a custom Android-based operating system.

### Key Points
- The Arc X1 is powered by a Qualcomm Snapdragon G2 Gen 2 processor paired with 8GB of LPDDR5X RAM and 128GB of expandable storage.
- The device features a 7-inch Full HD LCD display with a 120Hz refresh rate and Corning Gorilla Glass 3 protection.
- It runs on OwlOS, a custom Android-based operating system that supports native gaming, emulation, and cloud streaming services.
- Hardware includes a 9,300mAh battery, active cooling, replaceable analog sticks, and dual Linear Resonant Actuators for haptic feedback.
- Sales begin October 2 with a starting price of INR 29,500 for early waitlisters and INR 33,500 for general customers.

### Why it Matters
The entry of the Arc X1 into the handheld market provides a localized, budget-conscious alternative to established global gaming hardware. By utilizing a custom operating system and competitive pricing, the company aims to capture a significant share of the growing mobile gaming demographic in India.
