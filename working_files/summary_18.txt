"Total War: Shogun 2 Complete Edition has fallen short of both your expectations and our own standards", Sega say as they pull it from stores and restore the original
============================================================

Sega and Creative Assembly are removing Total War: Shogun 2 – Complete Edition from digital storefronts following widespread player criticism regarding poor quality and undisclosed generative AI usage.

### Key Points
- Sega and Creative Assembly pulled the Complete Edition from sale after receiving overwhelmingly negative reviews on Steam.
- The original version of Total War: Shogun 2 will be reinstated as the primary purchasable option during the investigation.
- Players have specifically criticized the use of generative AI to upscale unit card artwork without proper disclosure.
- Existing owners of the Complete Edition retain full access to the game in their personal libraries despite its removal from stores.
- Developers are currently working with partners to identify technical issues and improve the product to meet established quality standards.

### Why it Matters
This decision highlights the growing tension between game developers and players regarding the implementation of generative AI in professional software. It serves as a cautionary example for publishers on the importance of transparency and quality control when updating legacy titles for modern audiences.
