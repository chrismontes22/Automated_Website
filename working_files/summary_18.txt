Interdependence and Its Limits | Sinification: September 2026
============================================================

Chinese scholars and policy analysts are debating strategies for navigating global economic interdependence, AI governance, and shifting geopolitical dynamics amid ongoing trade tensions with the United States.

### Key Points
- Analysts advise Chinese firms to diversify supply chains and develop sanctions-evasion tools to mitigate the impact of expanding US trade controls.
- Experts suggest that China’s absence of a formal business delegation during recent Washington summits reflects concerns over state-linked scrutiny and domestic political signaling.
- Economists are debating fiscal stimulus and institutional reforms to address moral hazard and deflationary pressures within the domestic Chinese economy.
- Scholars are evaluating the risks and opportunities for Chinese enterprises in Brazil, emphasizing the need for local compliance and cultural adaptation.
- Research highlights concerns that AI-driven "pseudo-history" and algorithmic content could undermine national narratives and cultural security among younger generations.

### Why it Matters
These internal debates reveal how Chinese policymakers and academics are attempting to balance economic growth with national security in an increasingly fragmented global landscape. Understanding these perspectives provides critical insight into how China may adjust its industrial, technological, and foreign policies in response to external pressures.
