Alpha Modus Stock Falls 29% After $200M Bitcoin Treasury Plan
============================================================

Alpha Modus Holdings shares fell 29% after the company announced a deal to acquire 3,170 Bitcoin in exchange for issuing over 100 million shares and warrants to investors.

### Key Points
- Alpha Modus stock dropped to $2.72, resulting in an intraday market capitalization of approximately $18.9 million.
- The transaction values Bitcoin at $71,000 per token, with the total asset value exceeding the company's current equity by ten times.
- The agreement involves issuing 51.6 million Class A shares and an equal number of warrants to non-U.S. investors.
- Management intends to use the Bitcoin acquisition to address an outstanding Nasdaq listing deficiency and expand the company's asset base.
- Completion of the deal remains subject to final closing conditions, accounting reviews, and regulatory approval from Nasdaq.

### Why it Matters
This aggressive treasury strategy highlights the risks of significant shareholder dilution when small-cap firms attempt to pivot toward large-scale digital asset holdings. Investors must now weigh the potential for improved balance sheet compliance against the impact of issuing massive amounts of new equity.
