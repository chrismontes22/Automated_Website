What new Google Messages features are rolling out [August 2026]
============================================================

Google is actively testing and rolling out several new features for the Messages for Android app, including updated read receipts, customizable chat themes, and improved cross-platform RCS functionality.

### Key Points
- Google is testing a new read receipt design that uses swipe gestures to reveal timestamps and encryption details.
- Users can now customize conversations with new "Chat themes," offering nine color options and various background wallpaper collections.
- Upcoming iOS 27 integration will bring inline replies and emoji-based photo reactions to Android-iPhone RCS conversations.
- Samsung is transitioning US users to Google Messages, which will automatically replace the default messaging app on Android 14 devices.
- The "Tap to draft" feature allows users to edit Smart Replies in the text field before sending to prevent accidental messages.
- Google is phasing out QR code pairing for Messages for web in favor of mandatory Google Account sign-in.

### Why it Matters
These updates reflect Google's ongoing effort to modernize the Android messaging experience and achieve feature parity with competitors like iMessage. By standardizing RCS protocols and enhancing customization, Google aims to increase user retention and streamline communication across different mobile platforms.
