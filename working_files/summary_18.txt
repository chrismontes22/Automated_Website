I Tried Two Extraordinary Security Gadgets Made for Spots Other Tech Can’t Reach
============================================================

Abode has launched two specialized security sensors for 2026, designed to monitor garage doors and outdoor gates, offering homeowners expanded protection for areas typically ignored by standard security systems.

### Key Points
- The $35 garage door sensor uses internal gyroscope technology to detect tilt and orientation for accurate open/closed status monitoring.
- The $50 outdoor access sensor features weather-resistant housing designed for gates, sheds, and barns, though its physical installation process is currently cumbersome.
- Abode’s hub enables automatic device pairing and integrates with the CUE automation app to trigger lights, locks, or sirens based on sensor activity.
- The standard Abode hub requires a permanent Ethernet connection to a router, which may limit placement options for some users.
- Both sensors are compatible with existing Abode security kits and support third-party smart home ecosystems, including Google Home and Apple devices.

### Why it Matters
These sensors provide a cost-effective way to secure detached structures and garage entry points without requiring expensive smart-motor replacements or complex custom installations. By integrating these niche monitoring tools into a unified app, Abode allows users to create sophisticated home automations that improve both security and convenience.
