U.S. may have overthrown Venezuela due to chat with Grok
============================================================

President Donald Trump reportedly consulted Elon Musk’s Grok chatbot for strategic advice before ordering the January military operation that successfully captured Venezuelan leader Nicolas Maduro from his country.

### Key Points
- President Trump utilized the Grok AI chatbot to gauge potential public reaction in Venezuela prior to authorizing the mission to seize Nicolas Maduro.
- Following the operation, Maduro was taken into U.S. custody and currently faces federal drug-trafficking charges in court.
- Trump has rebranded artificial intelligence as "super intelligence" and continues to promote AI-generated imagery on his Truth Social platform.
- The administration recently hosted tech leaders, including Jeff Bezos, Mark Zuckerberg, and Jensen Huang, to discuss AI policy focused on self-policing.
- The White House launched America.gov, an AI-powered portal designed to streamline government services like Medicare enrollment and passport renewals for citizens.

### Why it Matters
This reliance on AI for high-stakes military decision-making highlights a significant shift in how executive power is exercised and informed in the modern era. By prioritizing self-regulation over federal oversight, the administration is setting a precedent that could reshape both national security protocols and the future of government digital infrastructure.
