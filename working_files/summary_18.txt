EFF to Governor Newsom: Veto California’s AB 1709
============================================================

The California legislature has passed Assembly Bill 1709, a controversial measure that would effectively ban social media use for individuals under 16 by restricting essential platform recommendation algorithms.

### Key Points
- Assembly Bill 1709 prohibits platforms from using recommendation algorithms and basic engagement tools for users under the age of 16.
- Critics argue the bill restricts constitutionally protected speech and limits access to supportive online communities for marginalized youth.
- Implementation would likely require invasive age-verification methods, such as government ID checks or biometric scanning, to confirm user ages.
- The legislation creates potential data privacy risks by forcing companies to collect and store sensitive personal information to verify user identity.
- Legal experts note that the bill conflicts with existing state laws, including A.B. 1043 and S.B. 976, likely leading to prolonged court challenges.

### Why it Matters
This legislation represents a significant shift in digital regulation that could fundamentally alter how minors interact with online platforms and services. By mandating strict age-gating, the law forces a trade-off between youth safety initiatives and the widespread collection of sensitive personal data by technology companies.
