Latest Galaxy S26 beta update comes with plenty of goodies from the Fold 8
============================================================

Samsung has released the One UI 9 Beta 7 update for the Galaxy S26 series, introducing new Galaxy AI features previously exclusive to the Galaxy Z8 foldable devices.

### Key Points
- The update adds My FanCam, which uses AI to re-center and crop subjects in recorded videos.
- Call Brief functionality now surfaces relevant emails, calendar events, and reminders during active phone calls.
- Users can now customize Now Brief with up to three generative AI cards based on health, weather, or video data.
- A new home screen setting allows users to toggle between unified Google Search and Finder widgets.
- These features are currently available in beta for the Galaxy S26 series and were previously launched on the Galaxy Z8 and Galaxy S26 FE.

### Why it Matters
This update signals Samsung's commitment to standardizing its advanced generative AI tools across its flagship smartphone lineup. By migrating features from foldable devices to the S26 series, the company is increasing the value of its software ecosystem for a broader user base.
