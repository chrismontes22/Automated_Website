AJ Scaramucci’s Solari Capital emerges from stealth with $350 million deployed and a case that companies stay private too long
============================================================

AJ Scaramucci’s new venture firm, Solari Capital, has emerged from stealth with $350 million to invest in "programmable reality" startups while addressing the growing scarcity of public market access.

### Key Points
- Solari Capital launched with $350 million in deployed capital, backed by prominent investors including Eric Schmidt, Jim Breyer, and Ron Conway.
- The firm focuses on "programmable reality" companies, with a portfolio including xAI, Suno, Tessera Therapeutics, Varda Space, and Northwood Space.
- Data from University of Florida professor Jay Ritter shows the median age of VC-backed tech companies at IPO has risen from roughly 6–9 years in the 1990s to 13.5 years in 2024.
- Solari aims to bridge the liquidity gap for ordinary investors by utilizing tokenization and secondary market platforms to provide exposure to private assets.
- The firm’s strategy includes incubating companies like Radial Health and supporting infrastructure projects like Fission Labs and Architect Financial.

### Why it Matters
The extended timeline for startups to reach an IPO has increasingly locked retail investors out of early-stage value creation, which now largely remains within private portfolios. By leveraging tokenization and alternative assets, Solari Capital seeks to democratize access to high-growth private markets that were previously reserved for institutional players.
