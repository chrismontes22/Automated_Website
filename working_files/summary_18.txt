Nigeria must embrace AI, tech revolution or be left behind — Sowore
============================================================

Former presidential candidate Omoyele Sowore has urged Nigeria to prioritize investment in science and technology to successfully integrate into the global Fourth Industrial Revolution and secure future economic growth.

### Key Points
- Omoyele Sowore warned that Nigeria’s failure to capitalize on previous industrial revolutions has resulted in long-term technological dependency on foreign nations.
- The Fourth Industrial Revolution encompasses emerging fields including artificial intelligence, machine learning, blockchain, robotics, biotechnology, and quantum computing.
- Sowore attributed Nigeria's past technological stagnation to decades of poor leadership, systemic corruption, and insufficient funding for education and innovation.
- The activist called for a shift toward visionary governance that emphasizes human capital development to ensure the country remains competitive in the global digital economy.

### Why it Matters
Embracing these technological advancements is critical for Nigeria to transition from a consumer of foreign innovation to a creator of its own digital infrastructure. Failure to adapt could leave the nation further behind in an increasingly automated global market, while strategic investment could catalyze sustainable economic development.
