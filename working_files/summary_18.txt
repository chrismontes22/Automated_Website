Apple Still Planning 'Glass-Centric' iPhone Redesign for 2027
============================================================

Bloomberg reporter Mark Gurman confirms that Apple’s glass-centric iPhone redesign remains on schedule for 2027, refuting recent analyst claims that the ambitious project had been officially canceled.

### Key Points
- Apple plans to release new iPhone Pro models featuring a glass-heavy design that wraps around the device edges.
- The upcoming hardware, internally codenamed V73 and V74, will utilize a thin metal band sandwiched between front and rear glass panels.
- Mark Gurman clarified that a more extreme, fully glass-based prototype was discarded early in the development cycle.
- The report contradicts recent speculation from Jefferies analyst Edison Lee, who suggested the project had been recently scrapped.

### Why it Matters
This clarification stabilizes expectations for Apple’s future hardware roadmap as the company seeks to differentiate its premium iPhone Pro lineup. Investors and consumers now have a clearer outlook on the design evolution of the flagship devices expected in 2027.
