Waymo Puts Toronto on the Map for Its Robotaxis
============================================================

Waymo plans to launch its autonomous ride-hailing service in Toronto by 2028, marking the company's first international expansion beyond its current operations in several major United States cities.

### Key Points
- Waymo will deploy a fleet of Jaguar I-Pace electric SUVs to provide autonomous transportation services throughout the Toronto area.
- Human-driven vehicles will begin mapping local streets and testing traffic patterns in neighborhoods like Liberty Village and Yorkdale immediately.
- The company developed a sixth-generation autonomous driving system specifically engineered to navigate Toronto’s diverse urban environment and extreme winter weather conditions.
- Toronto-based Element Fleet Management will provide essential support services, including vehicle maintenance, validation driving, and customer assistance.
- Interested users can currently sign up on the official Waymo website to receive updates and track the progress of the Canadian launch.

### Why it Matters
This expansion represents a significant milestone for Waymo as it tests its autonomous technology in a new international market with challenging seasonal weather. Successfully navigating Toronto’s climate could provide the company with critical data to scale its robotaxi operations into other cold-weather regions globally.
