Android Auto isn’t just for cars — I use it beside my PC
============================================================

Repurposing an old Android device as a dedicated desktop dashboard using the Headunit Revived app helps users minimize digital distractions by limiting access to non-essential smartphone applications.

### Key Points
- Users can transform spare Android phones or tablets into dedicated smart dashboards by installing the Headunit Revived app.
- The setup requires the WiFi Helper companion app on a primary device to establish a wireless connection via Wi-Fi Direct.
- The interface provides simplified access to essential tools like Spotify, smart home controls, calendar alerts, and voice-activated reminders.
- Headunit Revived is available as a paid app on the Google Play Store or as a free, open-source project via GitHub.
- The system functions as a distraction-free alternative to standard smartphones, preventing users from engaging in social media doomscrolling during work hours.

### Why it Matters
This DIY setup offers a practical way to repurpose aging hardware while creating a focused, minimalist workspace environment. By isolating essential tasks from social media and entertainment apps, users can improve productivity and reduce the temptation of constant digital interruptions.
