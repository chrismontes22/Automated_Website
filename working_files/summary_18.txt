WTF is scene-level targeting for CTV?
============================================================

Scene-level targeting allows CTV advertisers to align ad creative with the specific emotional tone and context of onscreen content, significantly improving viewer attention and campaign performance metrics.

### Key Points
- Scene-level targeting uses generative AI to analyze audio, visual, and dialogue cues in real time to categorize content before ad breaks.
- Research indicates that ads matched to the emotional context of a scene see a 2.4x increase in viewer attention.
- The strategy improves brand safety by allowing advertisers to target specific, appropriate moments within genres like news that were previously excluded.
- A QSR campaign using scene-level targeting achieved a 40% lift in incremental restaurant visits and a 48% increase in sales.
- This approach functions without cookies or personal identifiers, ensuring compliance with privacy regulations like the Video Privacy Protection Act.

### Why it Matters
By moving beyond broad audience demographics to granular, moment-based targeting, brands can reduce ad dissonance and increase consumer engagement across the entire marketing funnel. This shift enables publishers to monetize inventory more effectively while providing advertisers with a scalable, privacy-compliant method to maximize the impact of their CTV investments.
