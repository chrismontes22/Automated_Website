Microsoft: AI Cuts Post-Compromise Attack Time to Minutes
============================================================

Microsoft’s Digital Defense Report 2026 warns that threat actors are using artificial intelligence to accelerate cyberattacks, reducing the time required for data exfiltration from days to minutes.

### Key Points
- AI tools now enable attackers to discover vulnerabilities, generate custom malware, and execute phishing campaigns at a significantly faster scale.
- Phishing as an initial access technique surged from 7% of incidents in 2025 to 23% in 2026, while public-facing application exploitation rose to 24%.
- Government agencies were the most targeted sector in 2026, accounting for 27% of all cyberattacks, followed by the IT and research sectors.
- The United States experienced the highest volume of global attacks at 25.5%, followed by Israel, Ukraine, and Taiwan.
- Microsoft identified the rise of autonomous AI attacks, such as the July JadePuffer campaign, as a growing threat to enterprise security.

### Why it Matters
The rapid integration of AI into attack lifecycles forces organizations to modernize their defenses to match the speed and scale of automated threats. Companies must prioritize identity security and AI-based defensive tools to protect increasingly interconnected digital ecosystems from sophisticated, low-intervention campaigns.
