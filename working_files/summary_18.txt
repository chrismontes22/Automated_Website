HomeKit Weekly: The Amazon Basics Matter plug delivers Apple Home support for under $10
============================================================

The new Amazon Basics smart plug offers Matter compatibility for under $10, providing an affordable and compact automation solution that integrates directly with Apple Home, Alexa, and Google Home.

### Key Points
- The smart plug features Matter support, allowing direct integration with Apple Home hubs like the HomePod mini or Apple TV 4K without requiring the Amazon app.
- Its compact design ensures the device occupies only a single wall outlet, leaving the adjacent socket accessible for other appliances.
- The device operates on a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network, offering a budget-friendly alternative to more expensive Thread-enabled smart home hardware.
- Users can automate various household items, including seasonal lighting, climate control appliances, and coffee makers, through simple outlet-based scheduling.

### Why it Matters
This product represents a significant shift toward universal smart home interoperability by allowing budget-conscious consumers to integrate Amazon hardware into the Apple ecosystem seamlessly. By prioritizing the Matter standard, the device lowers the barrier to entry for home automation while maintaining compatibility across competing major technology platforms.
