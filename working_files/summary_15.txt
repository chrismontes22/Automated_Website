Rentomojo Centre Table and Chair Rentals Start at ₹86 a Month Across Pune, Hyderabad and Chennai as Shared Flats Avoid ₹8,000–₹18,000 Furniture Purchases
============================================================

Rising demand for flexible living in Indian cities like Pune, Hyderabad, and Chennai is driving professionals to rent small furniture items through platforms such as Rentomojo.

### Key Points
- Rentomojo offers monthly furniture rentals starting at ₹86 for chairs and centre tables, targeting high-churn shared-tenancy households.
- The service operates in major tech hubs including Hinjewadi, HITEC City, and OMR, where floating tenures make furniture ownership a logistical burden.
- Subscription plans range from three to 36 months and include free professional assembly, maintenance, repairs, and relocation services.
- Renting small furniture eliminates the high effective net cost of transporting and disposing of low-value items during frequent flat moves.
- As of March 2026, Rentomojo serves over 227,000 subscribers across 22 Indian cities, positioning itself as a leading tech-driven rental platform.

### Why it Matters
The shift toward furniture subscriptions reflects a broader move away from asset ownership among transient professionals who prioritize mobility and convenience over long-term capital investment. By converting high-friction household items into predictable monthly expenses, this model addresses the specific logistical challenges faced by renters in high-churn urban markets.
