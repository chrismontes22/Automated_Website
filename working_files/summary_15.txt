Artists Behind Olivia Rodrigo ‘Expectations’ Music Video Debunk AI Accusations
============================================================

Olivia Rodrigo and Chappell Roan’s new music video for the “Expectations” remix faced online accusations of AI usage, which the production team refuted with behind-the-scenes evidence of manual animation.

### Key Points
- Fans speculated that generative AI created inconsistent finger details in a scene featuring retro logos and animations.
- Designer Jack Harrison utilized analog treatments, 2D software, and 3D modeling to achieve the video's low-fi aesthetic.
- Production footage shows the team filming digital animations off a Windows Vista monitor to create the final retro effect.
- The video parodies classic game shows like The Price Is Right and Jeopardy! with cameos from David Byrne and Aquaria.
- Rodrigo previously stated in a Hollywood Reporter interview that she maintains a strict policy against using AI in her creative work.

### Why it Matters
This controversy highlights the growing public sensitivity toward AI-generated content in professional media and the resulting scrutiny of artistic production methods. By providing transparent behind-the-scenes documentation, the production team successfully countered misinformation and reinforced the artist's commitment to human-led creative processes.
