CarPlay is Coming to Pontoon Boats
============================================================

MasterCraft Boat Holdings will integrate Apple CarPlay and Savvy Navvy navigation technology into select 2027 Crest and Balise pontoon models to modernize the onboard boating experience.

### Key Points
- MasterCraft is adding Apple CarPlay support to its 2027 Crest and Balise pontoon lineups.
- The integration features the Savvy Navvy app, providing nautical charts, GPS tracking, and live weather forecasts.
- Savvy Navvy functionality will come standard on all new Crest Conquest and Caribbean models.
- The Savvy Navvy app is available globally today for any compatible CarPlay display or aftermarket marine head unit.
- New boat owners will receive a limited Savvy Navvy Premium subscription as part of the vehicle purchase.

### Why it Matters
This partnership addresses long-standing consumer demand for automotive-style infotainment systems within the marine industry. By bridging the gap between mobile navigation apps and boat displays, the integration simplifies trip planning and enhances safety for recreational boaters.
