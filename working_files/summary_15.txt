Trump’s AI Framework Is So Bad They Won’t Show You What’s In It
============================================================

The Trump administration is implementing a secretive, voluntary AI oversight framework that requires leading tech companies to submit advanced models for federal cyber security vetting before public release.

### Key Points
- The White House held private briefings with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, and Nvidia to outline the new voluntary review process.
- Participating companies must submit new AI models to the federal government for evaluation up to 30 days before their public launch.
- The administration is keeping the specific testing criteria and covered models classified, excluding open-source AI models from the framework.
- Critics argue the lack of transparency creates an uneven playing field that favors large, established tech firms over smaller startups and independent researchers.
- The framework aims to vet cyber capabilities by sharing models with federal agencies and select corporate partners.

### Why it Matters
The lack of transparency surrounding these federal AI standards raises concerns about regulatory capture and the potential for large corporations to establish market moats. By keeping the evaluation criteria secret, the administration risks stifling competition and undermining the development of open-source AI technology.
