Frontline Education Breach Impacts K-12 School District Staff
============================================================

Frontline Education, a major provider of administrative software for U.S. K-12 school districts, confirmed a data breach involving the theft of employee Social Security numbers and contact information.

### Key Points
- Frontline Education discovered a vulnerability in a third-party software product on August 14, 2026.
- Stolen data includes employee Social Security numbers, email addresses, and home addresses.
- The company stated it has remediated the vulnerability and engaged with law enforcement.
- Affected individuals will receive notifications via email and postal mail regarding the incident.
- The total number of impacted school districts and staff members remains currently undisclosed.

### Why it Matters
This breach exposes sensitive personal information for thousands of school employees, increasing their long-term risk of identity theft and targeted phishing attacks. The incident highlights critical security vulnerabilities within the supply chain of educational software providers that manage sensitive human capital data.
