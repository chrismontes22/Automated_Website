Massive Pentagon hack sees records of 2.7 million US military personnel leaked during months-long data breach — names, military service records and Social Security numbers all revealed
============================================================

A massive data breach at the U.S. Department of Defense’s Manpower Data Center exposed the sensitive personal information of three million current and former military-affiliated individuals.

### Key Points
- Attackers accessed unencrypted records, including Social Security numbers and military occupational specialties, between October 2025 and July 2026.
- The breach affected approximately 2.76 million living individuals and 294,000 deceased persons connected to the defense community.
- Unauthorized access occurred through a security vulnerability in a file-sharing system used by the Defense Manpower Data Center.
- The agency patched the identified vulnerability on July 16, 2026, and is currently offering 12 months of identity theft monitoring services to victims.
- Officials have not yet confirmed the specific software provider involved, though the incident mirrors previous large-scale attacks on managed file-transfer services.

### Why it Matters
This incident poses a significant national security risk, as the stolen data could enable foreign adversaries to identify and target specific military personnel for espionage or phishing campaigns. The exposure of millions of Social Security numbers and personnel details highlights critical vulnerabilities in the government's supply chain and data management infrastructure.
