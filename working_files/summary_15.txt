Bitcoin slides 2% after U.S. close while Korea’s Kospi plunges 10%
============================================================

Bitcoin prices dropped 2.7% to approximately $63,200 as a sharp sell-off in Asian equity markets and uncertainty surrounding upcoming U.S. Federal Reserve interest rate decisions triggered broader volatility.

### Key Points
- Bitcoin fell to $63,200, dragging down major cryptocurrencies including ether, XRP, and solana.
- South Korea’s Kospi index dropped 10%, marking a 25% decline from its mid-June peak amid heavy losses for chipmakers like Samsung.
- The U.S. Senate delayed the CLARITY Act to prioritize a Russia sanctions bill, pushing potential crypto regulatory votes until next week.
- Analysts expect increased market volatility ahead of the Federal Reserve’s interest rate decision on Wednesday and key U.S. economic data releases on Thursday.
- Binance maintained a 55% share of user funds and 24% of spot trading volume despite broader market outflows in early July.

### Why it Matters
The decline highlights how macroeconomic stress and interest rate concerns continue to drive correlation between digital assets and traditional equity markets. Investors are now closely monitoring upcoming Federal Reserve policy updates and U.S. economic data to gauge the potential for further market repricing.
