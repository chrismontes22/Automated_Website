The Frist Line of Defense
============================================================

A massive data breach in Denmark’s Central Register of Persons and a sophisticated supply chain attack on Ledger hardware wallets highlight critical vulnerabilities in modern digital security infrastructure.

### Key Points
- Denmark’s Central Register of Persons suffered a breach exposing personal data for 8.8 million people after a third-party partner used the password "123456."
- The Danish system intrusion began on September 10 and remained undetected for over three weeks until anomalous search volume triggered an internal review on October 2.
- Hackers compromised Ledger hardware wallets by physically installing microcontrollers and cellular modems to intercept recovery codes, resulting in nearly $100 million in cryptocurrency theft.
- The Ledger attack bypassed standard security measures by targeting the physical supply chain, allowing attackers to exfiltrate sensitive keys despite the devices being offline.

### Why it Matters
These incidents demonstrate that even systems designed for high security are susceptible to failure due to human error, such as weak password management or physical supply chain tampering. They underscore the growing necessity for organizations to enforce strict authentication standards and for users to remain vigilant against physical hardware compromises.
