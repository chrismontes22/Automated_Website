Market veterans favour value plays over crowded, expensive themes
============================================================

Leading Indian investment experts from firms like Mirae Asset, DSP, and Axis Mutual Fund offer a constructive outlook on domestic equities while advising caution regarding high-valuation sectors.

### Key Points
- Mirae Asset and Axis Mutual Fund favor manufacturing, financials, and energy transition themes while remaining cautious about overcrowded, narrative-driven sectors like IT and consumer staples.
- Templeton Global Investments suggests a strategy of "cautious optimism," noting that while large-cap valuations are reasonable, mid- and small-cap stocks require increased prudence.
- DSP Mutual Fund warns against chasing stocks where promoters or private equity firms are diluting stakes, as these often signal that performance and valuations have peaked.
- Shankar Sharma of GQuant maintains a contrarian view, predicting aggregate market disappointment and focusing exclusively on technology and pharmaceutical sectors for future growth.
- Key market risks identified include elevated crude oil prices, rising bond yields in developed markets, and potential disruptions from global geopolitical conflicts.

### Why it Matters
These expert insights provide a roadmap for investors navigating a market where domestic fundamentals remain sound but global headwinds and high valuations create volatility. By shifting focus from momentum-driven narratives to sector leaders and value-oriented stocks, investors can better manage risk while targeting sustainable long-term returns.
