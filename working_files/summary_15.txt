How Long Do Owners Say ASUS Laptops Last?
============================================================

ASUS laptops typically last an average of five years according to user reports, though longevity varies significantly based on specific models, maintenance habits, and individual component reliability.

### Key Points
- ASUS laptops, including Zenbook, Vivobook, ROG, and TUF series, generally maintain a five-year lifespan for most users.
- Common hardware complaints involve faulty network cards, motherboard failures in high-end ROG models, and potential screen issues.
- Regular maintenance, such as cleaning internal fans and replacing thermal paste, is frequently cited as essential for extending device longevity.
- Users report that gaming-focused ROG and TUF models can exceed five years of use, though battery performance often degrades significantly over time.
- ASUS provides Accidental Damage Protection and comprehensive warranties to mitigate risks associated with manufacturing defects or unexpected hardware failures.

### Why it Matters
Understanding the typical lifespan of ASUS hardware helps consumers make informed purchasing decisions in a market where rising tech costs make long-term reliability a priority. Proper maintenance and awareness of warranty coverage can significantly improve the return on investment for users seeking a durable laptop.
