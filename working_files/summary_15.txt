Apple’s Other Recent ‘Duo’
============================================================

The Apple MagSafe Duo Charger enables simultaneous wireless charging for iPhone 12, 13, and 14 models alongside all Apple Watch versions using a compatible USB-C power adapter.

### Key Points
- Requires a 20W or greater USB-C power adapter to achieve peak wireless charging speeds of up to 14W for compatible iPhones.
- Supports all Apple Watch models, though it does not provide fast charging for Series 7, Series 8, or Apple Watch Ultra.
- Users must plug the charger into a power source before placing devices on the pad to ensure maximum power delivery.
- The charger is compatible with Qi-certified devices, though charging speeds may be slower than with dedicated MagSafe-optimized hardware.
- Large Apple Watch models, such as the 49mm Ultra, may require band removal or position adjustments to align correctly with the magnets.

### Why it Matters
Using the correct power adapter is essential for optimizing charging efficiency and ensuring the device functions as intended. Understanding these hardware requirements helps users avoid slower charge times and potential compatibility issues with newer Apple Watch models.
