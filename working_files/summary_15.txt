Claude’s New Rules Target Fake Sources Built To Sway AI Answers via @sejournal, @MattGSouthern
============================================================

Anthropic has updated its usage policy, effective November 12, to explicitly prohibit using Claude to manipulate search engine results or AI-generated answers through deceptive, coordinated content networks.

### Key Points
- The new "Deceptive Campaigns or Artificial Activity" section bans seeding search engines with content that misrepresents its origin, authorship, or independence.
- Prohibited activities include creating networks of fake personas, websites, or outlets designed to corroborate claims and artificially boost search authority.
- Anthropic previously identified a network of 70 websites using Claude to publish over 8,900 articles to manipulate search rankings.
- Violating these policies may result in the suspension or termination of access to Anthropic’s API and Claude-based applications.
- The updated policy removes "automated publishing" from the list of high-risk use cases while maintaining strict disclosure requirements for legal, medical, and financial advice.

### Why it Matters
This policy shift aligns Anthropic’s enforcement with broader industry efforts to combat AI-generated spam and search engine manipulation. By targeting the source of deceptive content, the company aims to prevent its models from being used to pollute the information ecosystem and undermine the reliability of search results.
