If the iPod Nano and Game Boy had a baby, you’d get this $85 Hi-Fi Audio Player
============================================================

Hong Kong audio brand Hidizs has launched the AP30 Music Boy, a retro-styled, portable high-resolution music player designed to offer a distraction-free listening experience for modern audiophiles.

### Key Points
- Features a Cirrus Logic CS43198 DAC and RT6863 amplifier, supporting DSD256 native decoding and various lossless formats.
- Offers dual wired outputs (3.5mm and 4.4mm) and Bluetooth 5.3 connectivity for versatile headphone compatibility.
- Available in lightweight plastic (56g) or CNC-machined aluminum (80g) editions, both featuring a 2.0-inch non-touch HD IPS display.
- Includes a 1200mAh battery providing up to 12 hours of playback and a microSD slot supporting up to 256GB of storage.
- Designed for portability with integrated neck strap, bag clip, and magnetic phone attachment options.
- Includes a built-in retro puzzle game and supports eBook reading, emphasizing a focused, offline user experience.

### Why it Matters
The AP30 represents a pushback against the modern attention economy by providing a dedicated, high-fidelity device that functions independently of smartphone notifications. By prioritizing tactile controls and specialized audio hardware, it offers a niche alternative for users seeking to reclaim their focus while listening to music.
