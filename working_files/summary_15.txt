Cloud Gaming Handhelds Need Wi-Fi, so Lenovo Built One That Doesn’t
============================================================

Lenovo has unveiled the Legion Cloud Gaming Handheld in China, a unique device featuring a specialized USB dongle that enables local PC streaming without requiring an internet connection.

### Key Points
- The device features a 7.82-inch 120Hz FHD LCD screen and is powered by a MediaTek Dimensity 7400 processor.
- A bundled Legion Stream USB dongle allows users to stream games directly from a Windows PC over a local network.
- The handheld includes high-end input hardware, specifically TMR thumbsticks and Hall Effect sensors for triggers.
- It weighs 556g and contains an 8,000mAh battery capable of providing up to nine hours of cloud gaming.
- The device launches in China on September 8, priced at CNY 2,199 for the 8GB RAM and 256GB storage model.

### Why it Matters
This device addresses the primary limitation of cloud gaming by providing a reliable offline streaming solution for home users. By prioritizing input quality and local connectivity, Lenovo is attempting to carve out a distinct niche in the competitive handheld market.
