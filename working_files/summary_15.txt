My parents’ Android phones were ruined by invasive ads. Here’s how I fixed them for good
============================================================

Android users are increasingly encountering deceptive, predatory advertisements within legitimate applications, prompting many to adopt network-wide DNS filtering solutions to protect less tech-savvy family members from potential scams.

### Key Points
- Deceptive ads, often disguised as system updates or PDF alerts, are appearing in popular apps, including those developed by Google.
- NextDNS provides a customizable, network-wide filtering service that allows users to block malicious domains across multiple devices.
- The service supports individual profiles, remote management, and utilizes Android’s Private DNS feature for a simple, set-it-and-forget-it configuration.
- DNS filtering is not a total solution for platforms like YouTube, which often serve ads through the same domains as their primary content.
- A multi-layered security approach, including the use of alternative apps like SmartTube and regular permission audits, is recommended for comprehensive device protection.

### Why it Matters
The prevalence of manipulative advertising on mobile platforms poses a significant security risk to users who may be tricked into installing malicious software. Implementing robust filtering tools helps mitigate these threats, ensuring a safer digital environment for vulnerable individuals who rely on their devices for sensitive tasks.
