Moloco launches an agency partner program, in part to expand beyond mobile programmatic
============================================================

Performance ad-tech firm Moloco is launching a new agency partner program with roughly a dozen agencies to expand its reach into CTV and broader programmatic advertising markets.

### Key Points
- Moloco’s new partner program includes agencies such as Dentsu UK & Ireland, PMG, M+C Saatchi Performance, SplitMetrics, RocketLab, and Admiral Media.
- The initiative focuses on three pillars: co-selling, co-building the program framework, and highlighting agency case studies to drive performance outcomes.
- Moloco aims to leverage its AI-driven ad recommendation algorithms to transition from mobile-focused advertising into the CTV and streaming sectors.
- Agency partner PMG served as an alpha tester for Moloco’s performance CTV product, reporting significant growth in early campaign results.
- The program seeks to educate agencies on integrating AI into performance marketing to better compete for incremental ad spend outside of walled gardens.

### Why it Matters
This partnership strategy signals a shift in the ad-tech landscape as Moloco seeks to scale by empowering agencies rather than bypassing them. By integrating its AI-driven performance tools into the CTV ecosystem, the company aims to capture market share from established giants like Google and The Trade Desk.
