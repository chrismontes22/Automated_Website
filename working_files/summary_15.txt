Steam Machine And Controller User Data Leaks After Valve’s European Supplier Gets Hit With Cyber Attack
============================================================

Valve is notifying European Steam hardware customers that their personal information may have been compromised following a late July cyberattack on the shipping partner CEVA Logistics.

### Key Points
- CEVA Logistics, Valve’s European shipping partner, suffered a cyberattack between July 29 and August 1.
- Compromised data includes customer names, email addresses, phone numbers, and physical shipping addresses.
- Valve confirmed that Steam account credentials and payment information remain secure and were not accessed.
- The breach potentially affects European customers who purchased Steam hardware within 90 days of the incident.
- Valve warned affected users to remain vigilant against phishing attempts via email, SMS, or phone calls.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the significant security risks associated with third-party logistics providers handling sensitive customer data. Affected users should exercise caution regarding unsolicited communications to avoid falling victim to targeted phishing scams resulting from the leaked contact information.
