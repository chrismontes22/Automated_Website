Amazon has launched early fall Prime Day deals featuring significant discounts on new Apple products, including AirPods 5, M5 MacBook Air configurations, and pre-order savings on the Apple Watch Series 12.

### Key Points
- AirPods 5 with ANC are available for pre-order at $125, while AirPods Pro 3 are discounted by $50 to $199.
- The 13-inch M5 MacBook Air (16GB RAM/1TB storage) is $150 off, now priced at $1,449.
- Apple Watch Series 12 pre-orders start at $390, with the Apple Watch Ultra 4 featuring a new Translucent Ocean Band priced at $780.

- Anker and UGREEN have released discounted 25W MagSafe charging accessories, including the Magflow Pro station at $125.
- iPhone 18 Pro pre-order options are available through major carriers, with some monthly payment plans starting as low as $12.

### Why it Matters
These early promotions allow consumers to secure the latest Apple hardware at lower-than-retail prices ahead of the official fall Prime Day event. The aggressive pricing on pre-order items like the Apple Watch and AirPods suggests a competitive retail environment aimed at capturing early holiday spending.
