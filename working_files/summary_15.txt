The American Worker vs. the Most Qualified
============================================================

Tech entrepreneur Paul Graham and Palmer Luckey are debating the ethics of hiring foreign workers, sparking a broader discussion on the role of H-1B visas in American industry.

### Key Points
- Paul Graham argues that American firms should prioritize hiring domestic talent, while Palmer Luckey defends the strategic use of international expertise.
- The H-1B visa program is intended to bring in uniquely qualified specialists who possess knowledge that can elevate an entire team's capabilities.
- Historical examples, such as the development of Apollo heat shields, demonstrate that foreign experts often serve as mentors to train the next generation of domestic workers.
- Effective hiring strategies should balance immediate technical needs with a long-term commitment to developing a skilled, homegrown workforce.

### Why it Matters
This debate highlights a fundamental tension in the tech sector regarding whether companies have a responsibility to cultivate domestic talent or simply hire the most efficient global labor. By reframing the H-1B program as a tool for knowledge transfer rather than just filling roles, companies can better balance immediate innovation with long-term national workforce development.
