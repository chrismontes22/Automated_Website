Apple Reportedly Waiting on Samsung for Ultimate Bezel-Free iPhone Display
============================================================

Apple is reportedly collaborating with Samsung to develop a revolutionary full-surround OLED display that wraps around all four edges of an iPhone to eliminate physical bezels and buttons.

### Key Points
- The proposed design features an OLED panel that bends at 90-degree angles to replace traditional metal or plastic device frames.
- Samsung is currently working to resolve technical hurdles, including optical distortion, color shifts, and antenna placement on the curved edges.
- This technology aims to create a truly bezel-free smartphone experience by moving internal wiring and components behind the display.
- The design differs from the quad-curved display rumored for the 20th-anniversary iPhone model expected to launch in 2027.

### Why it Matters
This development signals a significant shift in smartphone industrial design by potentially removing all physical buttons and side frames. If successful, the technology could set a new standard for display-to-body ratios and influence the future manufacturing roadmap for premium mobile devices.
