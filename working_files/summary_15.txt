Netgear reveals new dark web monitoring tool which will tell you if your personal data turns up online
============================================================

Netgear has launched a new dark web monitoring service for Armor and Armor Plus subscribers to alert users when their personal data appears in illicit online marketplaces.

### Key Points
- The service monitors for compromised emails, phone numbers, financial details, and National Insurance numbers.
- It is available exclusively to Netgear router customers subscribed to Armor ($99/year) or Armor Plus ($149.99/year) plans.
- Users receive real-time alerts and an Identity Protection Score to track their personal data exposure levels.
- The feature integrates into the existing Netgear app alongside malware protection, VPN services, and DDoS detection tools.
- Experts estimate that over 15 billion personal records are currently available for purchase on the dark web.

### Why it Matters
This service provides consumers with proactive notification of data breaches that companies may not have yet disclosed. By identifying exposed information early, users can take immediate steps to secure their accounts and mitigate the risk of identity theft.
