iPhone Takes Bigger Slice of a Shrinking Smartphone Market
============================================================

Apple achieved a record 22% share of global smartphone shipments in the third quarter of 2026, despite an industry-wide decline that pushed total shipments to 2012 levels.

### Key Points
- Apple captured 22% of the global market, trailing Samsung’s 23% share during the July-to-September period.
- Total global smartphone shipments dropped 20% year-over-year due to rising production costs and memory chip shortages.
- Apple’s shipment volume was impacted by the decision to delay the standard iPhone 18 model, leaving only Pro versions available at launch.
- Strong demand for iPhone 18 Pro models in China and India helped Apple outperform the broader market.
- Global retail smartphone prices have increased by 20% this year, with premium devices showing higher consumer demand than budget models.

### Why it Matters
The smartphone industry is facing its lowest shipment volumes in over a decade as rising component costs drive up retail prices for consumers. Apple’s ability to maintain a record market share despite these headwinds highlights the growing consumer preference for premium devices over budget alternatives.
