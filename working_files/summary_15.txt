Best Buy's 60th Anniversary Sale Is Live With Deals on Video Games and More
============================================================

Best Buy is celebrating its 60th anniversary with a week-long online sale featuring significant discounts on electronics, video games, and collectibles through Sunday, August 23, 2026.

### Key Points
- The anniversary sale includes deals across various categories, including laptops, appliances, cell phones, and toys.
- Final Fantasy 7: Rebirth for the Switch 2 is discounted to $34.99 and includes an exclusive Magic: The Gathering card.
- Star Wars Outlaws for PlayStation 5 is available for $12, while Apple AirPods Pro 3 are priced at $189.99.
- The Magic: The Gathering Foundations Starter Collection is marked down to $47.99.
- The Lego Creator 3-in-1 Astronaut set is currently available for $38.49.

### Why it Matters
This event provides consumers with an unexpected opportunity to secure holiday-season discounts on popular tech and gaming products before the traditional Black Friday shopping period begins. The sale highlights Best Buy's ongoing efforts to maintain market share in the competitive retail electronics sector during the transition between summer and fall.
