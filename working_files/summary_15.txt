Everything you need to head back to school with a new Dell PC is here — fast Windows laptops with essential accessories on sale
============================================================

Dell has launched back-to-school discounts on its hardware lineup, featuring the redesigned XPS 13 laptop and various accessories available at reduced prices for students and remote workers.

### Key Points
- The new Dell XPS 13 features a 13.4-inch 2.5K touchscreen with a 120Hz refresh rate in a 2.2-pound chassis.
- Student pricing for the XPS 13 is set at $599.99, while the standard retail price is $699.99.
- The laptop includes a webcam with Windows Hello support and USB-C 3.2 Gen 2 ports for external display connectivity.
- Dell is offering a bundle including the laptop and peripheral accessories for under $740.
- The hardware is compatible with the company's EcoLoop backpack line for portable storage and travel.

### Why it Matters
These discounts provide students and professionals with a more affordable entry point into premium Windows hardware ahead of the new academic semester. By positioning the XPS 13 as a competitive alternative to other flagship laptops, Dell is aiming to capture a larger share of the back-to-school market.
