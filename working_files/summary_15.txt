Your Android VPN Is Only Useful If You Configure It Properly
============================================================

Android users can improve their mobile privacy by moving beyond simple app installation to configuring system-level settings like always-on connections and carefully vetting their chosen VPN service provider.

### Key Points
- Enable "Always-on VPN" in Android network settings to ensure the service activates automatically upon internet connection.
- Use the "Block connections without a VPN" feature to create a system-level kill switch that prevents data leaks during connection drops.
- Prioritize providers that offer transparent data handling and encryption standards over those marketing high server counts.
- Optimize performance by selecting nearby servers, updating connection protocols, and excluding the VPN app from aggressive battery-saving restrictions.
- Exercise caution with free VPNs, as they often monetize through data collection, advertising, or limited service tiers.

### Why it Matters
Mobile devices now serve as primary hubs for sensitive banking, work, and personal data, making robust network security a necessity rather than an enthusiast hobby. Properly configuring these settings ensures that privacy protections remain active and reliable, reducing the risk of accidental data exposure during daily use.
