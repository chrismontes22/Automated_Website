Hedge fund Situational Awareness has entered crisis mode and liquidated its entire public stock portfolio to Citadel following significant losses in its artificial intelligence-focused investment strategy.

### Key Points
- Situational Awareness, managed by a former OpenAI researcher, sold its $45 billion portfolio to Ken Griffin’s Citadel in a single block trade.
- The fund had previously reported net gains of 439% through June before experiencing a rapid collapse in late July.
- Despite the massive liquidation, the fund's leadership is reportedly seeking additional capital from investors.
- The event highlights growing volatility and skepticism surrounding high-stakes AI-driven investment funds in the current market.

### Why it Matters
This sudden collapse underscores the extreme risks associated with AI-focused hedge funds that rely on aggressive, high-leverage strategies. The incident serves as a cautionary tale for investors regarding the sustainability of rapid growth in the artificial intelligence sector.
