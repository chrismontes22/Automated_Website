HP’s $699 OmniBook 5 Has the One Thing the MacBook Neo Doesn’t
============================================================

HP’s new $699.99 OmniBook 5 14 laptop targets budget-conscious users with a slim aluminum design, a 14-inch OLED touchscreen, and a variety of vibrant color options for portability.

### Key Points
- The laptop features a 14-inch OLED touchscreen with a 1920×1200 resolution and a 180-degree hinge for improved collaboration.
- Available in four colors, the device weighs 2.6 pounds and measures 0.46 inches thick.
- Connectivity includes an HDMI 2.1 port, two USB-C ports, and a headphone jack, though it lacks USB-A ports.
- The base configuration is powered by an Intel Core 5 320 processor with 8GB of RAM and a 256GB SSD.
- HP includes a 60Wh battery that supports a 50% charge in approximately 30 minutes.

### Why it Matters
This device represents a competitive alternative to premium laptops by prioritizing display quality and aesthetic design over high-end internal specifications. It highlights a growing market trend where manufacturers offer stylish, portable hardware at lower price points to attract students and mobile professionals.
