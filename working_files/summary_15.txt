Cybersecurity CEO Indicted For Allegedly Scamming Millions From Ransomware Victims
============================================================

Florida-based data recovery firm owner Zohar Pinhasi faces federal fraud charges for allegedly paying hackers to unlock client files while falsely claiming to use proprietary decryption methods.

### Key Points
- Zohar Pinhasi, operating under the alias Zack Green, allegedly defrauded clients of his company, MonsterCloud, by secretly paying ransoms he claimed to bypass.
- Federal investigators report that Pinhasi collected over $19 million from victims while paying out approximately $8 million in ransom demands.
- One documented case involved Pinhasi charging a client $150,000 for a recovery process that cost him only $8,200 in ransom payments.
- The indictment follows a 2019 ProPublica investigation that exposed the firm's deceptive business practices and misleading claims regarding its recovery methods.
- Pinhasi faces a potential sentence of up to 20 years in federal prison if convicted of the charges.

### Why it Matters
This case highlights the significant risks businesses face when hiring third-party firms to manage ransomware incidents without verifying their recovery protocols. It serves as a warning to organizations that deceptive intermediaries can exploit security crises to extract excessive fees, further victimizing those already targeted by cybercriminals.
