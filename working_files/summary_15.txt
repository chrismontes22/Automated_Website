Battery drain in One UI 9? Some Galaxy S24 users are having a rough time
============================================================

Samsung Galaxy S24 and S25 users are reporting significant battery drain and software stability issues following the recent rollout of the One UI 9 operating system update.

### Key Points
- Galaxy S24 Ultra users report severe battery depletion, with one device losing 95% charge in just over five hours of usage.
- Some Galaxy S25 owners report excessive idle battery drain, requiring multiple charges throughout the day to maintain functionality.
- A Korean user reported a "webpage unavailable" bug preventing access to home screens after updating to the Android 17-based software.
- Samsung has advised users experiencing setup errors to update their browser and ensure a stable Wi-Fi connection during the installation process.
- Reports of these performance issues remain inconsistent, appearing to affect specific devices rather than the entire user base.

### Why it Matters
These performance inconsistencies create uncertainty for Samsung customers relying on the latest software updates for daily device stability. If these reports indicate a widespread firmware defect, Samsung may need to issue a formal patch to maintain consumer trust in its flagship smartphone series.
