Oppo Pad 5 review: a solid budget-friendly iPad rival
============================================================

The Oppo Pad 5 is a £349 Android tablet featuring a vibrant 12.1-inch display, long-lasting battery life, and a clean software experience, though it lacks top-tier processing power.

### Key Points
- The tablet is powered by a MediaTek Dimensity 7300-Ultra processor with 8GB of RAM and 128GB of storage.
- It features a 10,050mAh battery, though it is limited by relatively slow 33W wired charging speeds.
- The device runs ColorOS 16.0 and is backed by a commitment of five years of OS updates and six years of security patches.
- The 12.1-inch LCD screen offers a 2.8K resolution, 120Hz refresh rate, and 900 nits of peak brightness.
- Weighing 597g, the tablet features an aluminium frame but lacks water resistance with an IP40 rating.

### Why it Matters
The Oppo Pad 5 serves as a competitive mid-range option for users prioritizing software longevity and battery endurance over high-performance gaming. Its clean interface and extended update support make it a reliable, cost-effective choice for everyday productivity and media consumption.
