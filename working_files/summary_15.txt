Stagwell buys Freestar, putting publisher ad sales inside a holdco
============================================================

Stagwell has acquired publisher monetization firm Freestar to integrate its programmatic sales technology into the holding company’s Owned Media unit and gain direct access to premium publisher inventory.

### Key Points
- Stagwell announced the acquisition of Freestar on October 26 to bolster its supply-side capabilities.
- Freestar will operate within Stagwell’s Owned Media division, which manages properties like Ink and ReachTV.
- The deal allows Stagwell to bypass traditional supply-side platforms by controlling the infrastructure used to sell publisher ad inventory.
- This move follows the July launch of Stagwell Curate, an in-house marketplace designed to package publisher inventory for advertisers.
- The acquisition aims to capture programmatic margins by reducing reliance on third-party ad tech vendors and their associated fees.

### Why it Matters
By bringing ad sales infrastructure in-house, Stagwell gains greater control over programmatic margins and direct access to publisher data. This strategy highlights a broader industry shift where holding companies are increasingly prioritizing ownership of the tech stack to improve transparency and reduce reliance on external intermediaries.
