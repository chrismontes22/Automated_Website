Doxxing Safety Pt I: Prevention and Footprint Management
============================================================

Doxxing involves the malicious exposure of private information, but individuals can proactively reduce their digital footprint and mitigate risks by utilizing specific data privacy tools and security strategies.

### Key Points
- Use breach notification services like haveibeenpwned to identify if your personal data has been leaked in corporate security incidents.
- Minimize your digital presence by tightening social media privacy settings and using username search tools like Namechk to audit registered accounts.
- Remove personal information from data broker sites manually or via automated services, utilizing resources like the BADBOOL project for guidance.
- Leverage Address Confidentiality Programs and opt-out tools like California’s DROP to limit the accessibility of public records.
- Monitor for unauthorized image usage using facial recognition services like PimEyes, while weighing the privacy trade-offs of uploading your data.
- Treat privacy as a collective effort by coordinating security strategies and responsibilities with community members using encrypted communication platforms like Signal.

### Why it Matters
Proactive data management is essential because current privacy legislation often fails to protect individuals from the harms of targeted harassment and surveillance capitalism. By systematically reducing their digital footprint, users can regain agency and significantly increase the difficulty for bad actors to conduct successful doxxing campaigns.
