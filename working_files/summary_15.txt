Adobe Brings ACR’s Powerful Non-Destructive Exposure Tools Into Photoshop Itself
============================================================

Adobe has released a significant update for Photoshop, introducing AI-powered editing tools, advanced lighting controls, and a new beta interface designed to streamline professional image manipulation workflows.

### Key Points
- The new Light Adjustment Layer integrates professional-grade controls for exposure, contrast, and shadows directly into the Photoshop workspace.
- Powered by Firefly Image 5, the Instruct Edit with Masks feature allows users to modify specific image areas using natural language descriptions.
- New AI Markup tools enable photographers and clients to communicate edit requests through sketches and annotations directly on the image.
- A beta AI Assisted Editor provides a natural language interface to automate complex tasks like Generative Fill, background removal, and image upscaling.
- Improved Adobe Stock integration now offers access to over 900 million assets through a dockable panel within the application.

### Why it Matters
These updates represent a strategic shift toward integrating generative AI directly into professional creative workflows to reduce manual editing time. By lowering the technical barrier for complex adjustments, Adobe aims to keep Photoshop competitive as the industry standard for photographers and digital designers.
