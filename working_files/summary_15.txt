MacBook Ultra OLED Touch Panel to Be Exclusively Supplied by Samsung
============================================================

Apple has reportedly selected Samsung Display as the exclusive supplier for OLED touch panels intended for the upcoming 14-inch and 16-inch MacBook Pro models expected by early 2027.

### Key Points
- Samsung Display will manufacture the OLED panels at its 8.6-generation facility in Asan, South Korea.
- Initial production volume is estimated at 2.5 million units, with mass manufacturing scheduled to begin in August.
- Apple bypassed LG Display and BOE Technology Group due to concerns regarding production yields and existing manufacturing capacity.
- The redesigned MacBook Pro models are expected to feature M5 Pro and M5 Max chips alongside a thinner chassis design.
- Future iterations may adopt "MacBook Ultra" branding and integrate a touch-optimized interface within the macOS 27 operating system.

### Why it Matters
This exclusive partnership signals a significant shift in Apple’s hardware strategy as it prepares to introduce touch-screen functionality to its professional laptop lineup. By securing Samsung as a sole supplier, Apple aims to ensure high-quality display integration while navigating the complex manufacturing requirements of its next-generation MacBook Pro refresh.
