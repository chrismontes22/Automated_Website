Purposeful innovation key to capital market growth — Owoturo
============================================================

Coronation Registrars Limited CEO Oluseyi Owoturo highlights the necessity of technology-driven innovation to improve investor experiences and resolve long-standing operational challenges within Nigeria’s growing capital market ecosystem.

### Key Points
- Coronation Registrars received three international awards in 2026 for innovation in unclaimed dividend resolution and excellence in shareholder services.
- The firm utilizes digital platforms to automate dividend administration, corporate actions management, and shareholder registry tracking.
- Digital verification tools and e-mandate onboarding are being deployed to address the structural issue of unclaimed dividends in the Nigerian market.
- The company operates as a subsidiary of the Coronation Group, focusing on enhancing corporate governance and market efficiency.

### Why it Matters
Modernizing registrar operations is critical for unlocking dormant capital and increasing retail investor participation in the Nigerian Exchange Limited. By replacing legacy paper-based systems with automated digital infrastructure, firms can improve regulatory compliance and foster long-term trust between corporate issuers and shareholders.
