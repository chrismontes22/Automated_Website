Help TDS Linked to Hotejka as 20,000+ Cloudflare Pages Domains Host Tech Support Scams
============================================================

The long-running Help Traffic Distribution System, linked to the Hotejka affiliate program, continues to facilitate widespread tech support scams by redirecting users from compromised websites to fraudulent call centers.

### Key Points
- Help TDS has been active since 2017 and is identified as a rebranded successor to the ROI777 operation.
- The system uses malicious WordPress plugins and custom HTML blocks to redirect traffic from compromised sites to fake Microsoft security alerts.
- Victims are coerced into installing remote desktop tools, allowing attackers to gain persistent access to their devices for further exploitation.
- Hotejka utilizes Cloudflare Pages to host thousands of disposable scam domains, generating approximately 300 new landing pages daily.
- The operation coordinates with various affiliates, including Balada Injector and Detour Dog, to monetize traffic through scams and malware distribution.
- Researchers estimate the criminal enterprise has generated millions of dollars in illicit revenue over the past eight years.

### Why it Matters
This operation highlights the sophisticated infrastructure behind modern cybercrime, where specialized routing systems allow diverse threat actors to monetize compromised web traffic at scale. By evolving through rebrands and leveraging legitimate platforms, these groups maintain long-term profitability while posing a persistent risk of remote access and financial fraud to unsuspecting users.
