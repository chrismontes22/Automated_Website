iPhone 18 Pro Event Could Hit Apple Shares, KeyBanc Warns
============================================================

KeyBanc Capital Markets warns that Apple’s upcoming September 9 iPhone announcement could trigger stock volatility as investors evaluate potential price hikes and their impact on future consumer demand.

### Key Points
- KeyBanc maintains an "Underweight" rating on Apple stock with a $250 price target.
- Analysts project a $150 price increase for the iPhone 18 Pro and a $200 increase for the Pro Max model.
- The firm estimates total iPhone 18 production at 80 million units, down from 91 million units during the previous cycle.
- A new foldable "iPhone Ultra" model is expected to enter the market with a starting price of $2,199.
- Historical data shows Apple shares typically decline by an average of 1.22% in the five trading days following an iPhone announcement event.

### Why it Matters
Investors are concerned that rising hardware costs and higher retail prices may suppress unit sales and pressure Apple's profit margins. This shift highlights the company's difficult balancing act between maintaining premium brand positioning and sustaining volume growth in a competitive smartphone market.
