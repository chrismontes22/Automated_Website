Music industry targets AI songs in crackdown on streaming fraud...
============================================================

The International Federation of the Phonographic Industry has launched the Streaming Integrity Initiative, a global effort uniting major music companies to combat digital streaming fraud and royalty manipulation.

### Key Points
- The initiative requires participants to implement Know Your Customer checks and robust content vetting to identify AI-related risks and fraudulent activity.
- Major industry players including Universal Music Group, Sony Music Group, Warner Music Group, and Merlin have signed on to the new commitments.
- The program mandates intelligence sharing among partners to prevent repeat offenders from moving fraudulent content between different streaming platforms.
- DistroKid, the largest DIY music distributor, is currently not among the organizations supporting the initiative.
- The effort aims to address streaming fraud, which experts estimate costs the music industry hundreds of millions of dollars annually.

### Why it Matters
Streaming fraud siphons royalties away from legitimate artists and songwriters, creating a significant financial drain on the global music economy. By standardizing anti-fraud practices, the industry hopes to mitigate the impact of AI-generated bot farms and protect the integrity of digital streaming platforms.
