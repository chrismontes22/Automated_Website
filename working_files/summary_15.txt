Zcash (ZEC) To Hit $5,000 By The End Of 2026: Co-founder
============================================================

Zcash co-founder Eli Ben-Sasson projects that the privacy-focused cryptocurrency ZEC could reach a valuation of $5,000 by the end of 2026 following recent record-breaking market performance.

### Key Points
- ZEC recently hit an all-time high of $1,680, pushing its market capitalization to a peak of $27 billion.
- Eli Ben-Sasson, who currently leads StarkWare, previously predicted ZEC would exceed $1,200 by September 25, 2026.
- Large-scale investors are withdrawing significant amounts of ZEC from major exchanges like Binance, OKX, and Kraken, creating a supply shock.
- The Zcash core team is actively integrating quantum-proofing standards to enhance the long-term security of the network.
- Privacy coins have shown resilience following the recent failure of the CLARITY Act in the United States Senate.

### Why it Matters
This price surge highlights the growing market demand for privacy-focused digital assets despite ongoing regulatory scrutiny and historical volatility. Investors are closely monitoring these developments as the asset's performance increasingly influences its standing among the top ten global cryptocurrencies.
