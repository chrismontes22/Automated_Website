Links 10/30/2026
============================================================

Alphabet shares plummeted by a record $482 billion as a broader sell-off in the technology sector intensified following disappointing quarterly earnings reports from major U.S. companies.

### Key Points
- Alphabet’s market value dropped by $482 billion, marking the largest single-day decline in the company's history.
- Asian technology stocks experienced significant slumps as investor confidence in artificial intelligence growth began to waver.
- The market downturn was triggered by underwhelming U.S. corporate earnings, leading to widespread volatility across global tech indices.
- Financial analysts noted that the sell-off reflects growing skepticism regarding the immediate profitability of heavy AI infrastructure investments.

### Why it Matters
This historic loss signals a potential shift in investor sentiment toward the high-valuation AI sector, which has driven much of the recent market growth. If major tech firms fail to demonstrate clear returns on their massive AI spending, the broader economy could face increased volatility and a cooling of capital investment.
