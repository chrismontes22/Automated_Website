The People—OK, the Men—Who Still Love Bitcoin
============================================================

The Venetian Resort in Las Vegas recently hosted a major Bitcoin convention, drawing thousands of attendees seeking financial independence through cryptocurrency amid widespread distrust of traditional American economic institutions.

### Key Points
- The event attracted 25,000 attendees, many of whom expressed deep skepticism toward central banks and the U.S. dollar.
- Bitcoin’s price has experienced extreme volatility, fluctuating between $76,000 and $126,000 in recent years.
- Michael Saylor, executive chairman of Strategy Inc., emerged as the event's central figure by promoting a financial model that uses investor capital to accumulate Bitcoin.
- Activists like Evan Mawarire and Roya Mahboob highlighted Bitcoin’s potential utility for citizens living under oppressive regimes or hyperinflation.
- Despite the focus on "self-sovereignty," industry experts note that actual adoption remains low, with many participants primarily motivated by the prospect of wealth accumulation.

### Why it Matters
The rise of Bitcoin as a perceived alternative to traditional finance reflects a growing segment of the population that has lost faith in national institutions and government-managed currencies. While proponents view it as a tool for liberation, the reliance on complex financial schemes and speculative investment highlights the significant risks ordinary individuals face when attempting to opt out of the established economic system.
