I tested 8.8.8.8 and 1.1.1.1 head-to-head, and Google won on speed but lost on everything else
============================================================

Google’s 8.8.8.8 and Cloudflare’s 1.1.1.1 are the leading public DNS resolvers, offering users faster and more private alternatives to the default settings provided by most internet service providers.

### Key Points
- Google DNS (8.8.8.8) demonstrated slightly faster response times in speed tests, though the difference of approximately 0.3ms is generally imperceptible to users.
- Cloudflare (1.1.1.1) maintains a stricter privacy policy, deleting most query data within 24 hours and explicitly excluding browsing data from advertising profiles.
- Google retains DNS query data for up to 48 hours, including domain names and request types, though it does not link this information to personal Google accounts.
- Cloudflare provides specialized resolvers, such as 1.1.1.2 for malware blocking and 1.1.1.3 for adult content filtering, which are not available through Google.
- Cloudflare offers a dedicated mobile and desktop application that allows users to toggle DNS settings without manually configuring network parameters.

### Why it Matters
Switching to a third-party DNS provider can improve browsing security and privacy by bypassing ISP-level tracking and filtering. While both services offer reliable performance, the choice between them depends on whether a user prioritizes marginal speed gains or enhanced privacy controls and content filtering features.
