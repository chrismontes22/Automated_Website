Dell's XPS 13 redefined entry level laptops, and this deal for 16GB RAM is even better
============================================================

The 2026 Dell XPS 13 laptop is currently available at Best Buy for $799.99, offering a 20% discount on a device praised for its portability and entry-level performance.

### Key Points
- The Dell XPS 13 features a 13.4-inch 3K touchscreen with a 120Hz refresh rate and weighs 2.2 pounds.
- Internal hardware includes an Intel Core 5 Series 3 320 CPU, 16GB of soldered RAM, and support for up to 4TB of SSD storage.
- The device offers nearly 15 hours of battery life, Wi-Fi 7 connectivity, and dual Thunderbolt 4 ports.
- Best Buy has reduced the price from the standard $999.99 to $799.99 for a limited time.
- The laptop is positioned as a direct competitor to the MacBook Neo, targeting students and mainstream PC users.

### Why it Matters
This discount provides a high-quality, portable computing option for budget-conscious consumers amid rising hardware costs caused by the ongoing RAM supply crisis. Securing the device at this price point may be more advantageous than waiting for future sales, as market volatility could offset potential Black Friday savings.
