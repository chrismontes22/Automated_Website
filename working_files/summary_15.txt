Xbox CEO Asha Sharma re: PlayStation's disc backlash — "I know players love physical media," but what about next-gen Helix plans?
============================================================

Microsoft is addressing the future of physical media by granting digital entitlements to 1,000 Xbox disc titles, positioning the company against PlayStation’s plan to phase out physical games.

### Key Points
- CEO Asha Sharma announced that 1,000 physical Xbox titles will now grant users digital entitlements, including access to Xbox Cloud Gaming and Play Anywhere.
- The initiative serves as a direct contrast to Sony’s strategy, which involves phasing out all disc-based games on the PlayStation platform by 2028.
- Industry supply chains for optical disc drives are collapsing as manufacturers shift production toward more profitable sectors like camera lenses.
- Microsoft is prioritizing consumer choice and backwards compatibility as it develops the next-generation Xbox Helix console.
- Current digital-only gaming models face criticism due to the lack of consumer ownership rights and the inability to easily resell or share content.

### Why it Matters
This policy shift highlights a growing divide in the gaming industry regarding digital rights and the long-term ownership of media. By supporting physical disc owners, Microsoft aims to differentiate its ecosystem while navigating the logistical challenges of a shrinking optical drive manufacturing market.
