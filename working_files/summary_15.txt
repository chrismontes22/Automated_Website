Weekend reads: OpenAI’s preprint dump; Taylor & Francis to leave Informa; arXiv’s preprint cap
============================================================

Retraction Watch reports a busy week of academic integrity challenges, including high-profile journal retractions, OpenAI preprint withdrawals, and ongoing debates regarding the role of AI in research.

### Key Points
- OpenAI withdrew three preprints just one day after releasing 722 manuscripts on unsolved mathematical problems.
- IOP Publishing retracted nearly 100 conference papers due to the presence of "tortured phrases."
- The Retraction Watch Database now tracks over 67,000 retractions, while the Hijacked Journal Checker exceeds 450 entries.
- India’s NIRF university rankings face delays as officials consider penalizing institutions with repeat research misconduct.
- A paleontologist is seeking the retraction of a paper that labeled his fossil discovery a forgery.

### Why it Matters
These developments highlight the growing systemic pressure on academic publishing as AI tools and paper mills complicate the verification of scientific integrity. The increasing volume of retractions and misconduct cases underscores a critical need for more robust peer review and data transparency standards across global research institutions.
