Booking.com Bets Travelers Will Research With AI but Book With It
============================================================

Booking.com is positioning its platform as the primary destination for travelers to finalize reservations after using generative AI tools like ChatGPT and Gemini for initial trip planning.

### Key Points
- A Booking.com survey indicates that 64% of travelers in the Asia-Pacific region currently utilize AI tools to assist with their travel research and itinerary planning.
- Booking Holdings reports that direct traffic referrals from large language models currently account for less than 1% of total room nights booked on the platform.
- Vice President Laura Houldsworth notes that travelers frequently switch between multiple AI services, including Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini, before visiting Booking.com to complete their transactions.
- The company aims to capture consumer intent at the point of purchase, regardless of the fragmented research process used by travelers across various digital platforms.

### Why it Matters
This strategy highlights the ongoing challenge for travel companies to maintain direct customer relationships as AI chatbots increasingly influence the early stages of the consumer journey. By focusing on the final booking phase, Booking.com seeks to remain a critical intermediary in a digital landscape where users rely on multiple AI tools for information.
