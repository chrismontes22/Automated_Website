Texas Stock Exchange Raises $155 Million After First Listings
============================================================

The Dallas-based TXSE Group Inc. has secured $155 million in its third funding round, bringing total capital to $430 million to challenge the NYSE and Nasdaq for listings.

### Key Points
- TXSE Group Inc. raised $155 million, with existing shareholders contributing approximately 75% of the new capital.
- The company has reached a total of $430 million in funding to support its operations as a national securities exchange.
- Early primary listings include Energy Transfer LP and Texas Capital Bancshares Inc., both of which have leadership ties to the exchange.
- Major financial backers of the project include BlackRock Inc., Citadel Securities, and JPMorgan Chase & Co.
- The exchange aims to compete directly with the New York Stock Exchange and Nasdaq by focusing exclusively on equities trading.

### Why it Matters
The emergence of the Texas Stock Exchange represents a significant challenge to the long-standing dominance of the NYSE and Nasdaq in the U.S. equities market. By securing substantial institutional backing, the exchange is positioning itself as a viable alternative for companies seeking primary listings outside of traditional financial hubs.
