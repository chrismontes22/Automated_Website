Bitcoin price spikes to $83.3K as US stocks rise on Q3 earnings, oil falls
============================================================

Bitcoin reached a high of $83,300 as cryptocurrency markets rallied alongside US stocks, which were bolstered by strong third-quarter earnings reports from major corporations like General Motors.

### Key Points
- Bitcoin gained 1.5% to reach $83,300 before facing resistance at the 21-day simple moving average of $83,230.
- The Dow Jones Industrial Average rose 1.2%, while the S&P 500 and Nasdaq Composite increased by 0.6% and 0.5%, respectively.
- Strong Q3 earnings from companies including 3M and General Motors helped offset earlier volatility in oil prices.
- Crypto markets experienced $125 million in short liquidations as total open interest on exchanges climbed to $53.86 billion.
- West Texas Intermediate (WTI) crude oil prices dropped nearly 5% to below $96 per barrel following an early session spike.

### Why it Matters
The simultaneous rally in equities and digital assets suggests that investor sentiment is stabilizing following recent tech-sector sell-offs. Strong corporate earnings are currently serving as a primary catalyst for market confidence, helping to mitigate concerns regarding energy price fluctuations and volatility.
