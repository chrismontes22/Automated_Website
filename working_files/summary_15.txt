Spam Guide Update, Restaurant Controls & AI Mode Revenue – SEO Pulse via @sejournal, @MattGSouthern
============================================================

Google has updated its spam guidelines for shareable pages, granted restaurants direct control over ordering providers, and addressed concerns regarding delayed data in Search Console indexing reports.

### Key Points
- Google now recommends using noindex tags or robots.txt blocks for webpages designed for direct sharing or collaboration rather than public search discovery.
- Restaurants can now independently remove third-party delivery and pickup providers directly from their Google Business Profile dashboard.
- Google Search Console users are advised to check report date stamps, as indexing data may lag behind real-time performance metrics.
- Analysis of over 193,000 AI-generated answers suggests that pages providing direct, concise answers in their opening lines are more frequently cited by AI models.
- Alphabet reported a 12% year-over-year increase in Google Search revenue, noting that lower monetization in AI Mode contributed to slower growth compared to previous quarters.

### Why it Matters
These updates highlight the increasing importance of manual oversight for website owners, particularly regarding how content is indexed and how business profiles are presented to customers. As AI integration shifts search behavior and monetization models, businesses must proactively manage their digital presence to ensure accuracy and visibility.
