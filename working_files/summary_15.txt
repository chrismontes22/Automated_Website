UK working to identify exposure after global cyber-security warning
============================================================

The UK government and global organizations are rushing to patch critical security flaws in the PHP programming language that potentially expose millions of websites to malicious cyberattacks.

### Key Points
- Researcher Ryan Austin discovered the critical PHP vulnerability, which had remained undetected for at least a decade.
- The flaw allows attackers to execute unauthorized code, steal sensitive data, or plant backdoors on affected web servers.
- Amazon reported detecting and blocking multiple "PHP2Shell" exploitation attempts targeting its systems following a premature leak of the vulnerability details.
- Government agencies in the UK, EU, Australia, and Finland have advised organizations to apply patches immediately or take vulnerable systems offline.
- Estimates suggest the vulnerability impacts a significant portion of the internet, including major platforms like Wikipedia and WordPress.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the growing role of artificial intelligence in both discovering complex software vulnerabilities and enabling attackers to exploit them rapidly. Because PHP underpins a vast segment of the global web infrastructure, these flaws pose a systemic risk to data security and service availability for both public and private sectors.
