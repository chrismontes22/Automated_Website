Open-Source ExpressLRS Receiver Reaches for Range
============================================================

Electronics enthusiast Stan has developed custom ExpressLRS drone receivers that utilize Chirp Spread Spectrum modulation to maintain reliable long-range control links over significant distances using 2.4 GHz frequencies.

### Key Points
- The custom receivers utilize the SX1281 radio chip and ESP32 microcontroller to implement the ExpressLRS protocol.
- Chirp Spread Spectrum modulation allows the system to decode signals below the receiver's noise floor by providing an equivalent gain of 24 dB.
- Testing confirmed the receivers maintained stable connections over five kilometers, even when obstructed by dense forest terrain.
- The design successfully utilized unconventional antenna via placement without compromising signal integrity or impedance matching.
- ExpressLRS builds upon LoRa technology to achieve high-reliability, low-data-rate communication suitable for long-range drone operations.

### Why it Matters
This development demonstrates how advanced modulation techniques can significantly extend the operational range of compact, low-power drone hardware. By leveraging open-source protocols like ExpressLRS, hobbyists and engineers can achieve professional-grade connectivity that rivals much more expensive proprietary systems.
