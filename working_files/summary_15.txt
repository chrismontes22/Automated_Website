Greece prepares to levy 10% capital gains tax on cryptocurrency
============================================================

Greece is set to introduce a 10% capital gains tax on cryptocurrency transactions, with a draft bill expected to reach the national parliament for review this November.

### Key Points
- The proposed legislation imposes a 10% tax rate on profits generated from cryptocurrency investments.
- Annual gains totaling 500 euros ($560) or less will remain exempt from the new tax requirements.
- The bill is scheduled for formal submission to the Greek parliament in November.
- Greece’s 10% rate is significantly lower than the 25% or higher rates currently applied in countries like France, Italy, and Germany.
- Officials have not yet released revenue projections due to the difficulty of tracking assets held on international platforms.

### Why it Matters
This policy shift reflects a broader European trend toward treating digital assets similarly to traditional financial instruments like stocks. By establishing a clear tax framework, Greece aims to integrate cryptocurrency into its formal economy while maintaining a competitive rate compared to other European Union members.
