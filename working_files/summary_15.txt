Cisco Warns of Active Attacks Exploiting New Critical Secure Email Gateway Vulnerability
============================================================

Cisco has issued an advisory regarding a critical vulnerability in its Secure Email Gateway that allows unauthenticated attackers to execute commands as root via malicious email attachments.

### Key Points
- The vulnerability, tracked as CVE-2026-76519, carries a critical CVSS score of 9.8.
- Affected appliances must have the File Reputation service enabled and Content Scanner Tools version older than 25.3.0.5525.
- Administrators can remediate the flaw by updating to Content Scanner Tools version 25.3.0.5525 or installing AsyncOS releases 15.5.5-014 or 16.5.0-780.
- Cisco has confirmed active exploitation of the flaw and is currently assisting compromised customers with remediation and recovery efforts.
- Users can check for potential compromise by running the "grep 'System Security' system_logs" command via the appliance's command-line interface.

### Why it Matters
This flaw represents the third root-level vulnerability in Cisco Secure Email Gateways to be exploited in the wild since December, highlighting a persistent security risk for enterprise email infrastructure. Because successful exploitation grants attackers full control over the appliance, organizations must prioritize immediate patching to prevent unauthorized access and potential data breaches.
