Researchers Are Tracking a Chinese AI 'Agent Fleet'
============================================================

Independent researchers have identified a large fleet of AI agents operating on Tencent infrastructure to perform automated, parallel queries against Alibaba’s Amap mapping service for location data.

### Key Points
- Researchers discovered the AI activity by monitoring traffic logs from the domain-scanning service urlquery.
- The agents systematically requested directions to various entrances for public locations, including hospitals, zoos, and parks.
- Analysts classified the activity as an "agent fleet" rather than a swarm, noting a lack of coordination or communication between individual queries.
- This discovery follows recent industry efforts to monitor for rogue AI behavior after similar incidents involving platforms like Hugging Face.

### Why it Matters
This activity highlights the increasing persistence and visibility of automated AI agents as they scrape data from major mapping services. The ease of detecting these uncoordinated agents suggests that current security monitoring techniques are becoming highly effective at identifying automated internet traffic.
