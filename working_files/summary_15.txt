Block ads on nine devices for life for $11 (reg. $169.99)
============================================================

AdGuard is currently offering a lifetime subscription to its Family plan for $11, providing comprehensive ad blocking, privacy protection, and parental controls for up to nine individual devices.

### Key Points
- The promotional price of $11 represents a significant discount from the standard $169.99 retail cost.
- A single purchase covers up to nine devices, including smartphones, tablets, and computers running Android or iOS.
- The software blocks banner ads, video advertisements, pop-ups, and online trackers to improve browsing privacy.
- Integrated security features include filters designed to detect and prevent access to known malware and phishing websites.
- Parental control settings allow users to restrict access to inappropriate content across connected devices.
- The lifetime license includes automatic updates and new feature rollouts without requiring recurring subscription renewals.

### Why it Matters
This deal offers a cost-effective solution for households looking to secure multiple devices under a single, one-time payment model. By eliminating recurring subscription fees, users can maintain long-term digital privacy and ad-free browsing across their entire family's hardware.
