MoonPay to Acquire North Capital in $60M Deal to Expand Tokenization
============================================================

Cryptocurrency payments firm MoonPay has announced an all-stock acquisition of private-markets infrastructure platform North Capital for over $60 million to expand its reach into tokenized assets.

### Key Points
- The deal makes Salt Lake City-based North Capital a wholly owned subsidiary of MoonPay upon regulatory approval.
- North Capital provides regulated financial services and technology for private securities, having processed over $8.7 billion in transaction volume.
- The acquisition includes North Capital’s SEC-registered broker-dealers, a transfer agent, and the PPEX alternative trading system.
- MoonPay is integrating these capabilities to support its Trade platform, which connects financial institutions with tokenized assets and stablecoin liquidity.

### Why it Matters
This acquisition signals MoonPay’s strategic shift from a consumer-facing crypto payments provider toward becoming a comprehensive infrastructure provider for institutional finance. By absorbing North Capital’s regulated framework, the company aims to bridge the gap between traditional private markets and emerging blockchain-based asset tokenization.
