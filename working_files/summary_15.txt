Screens Got Louder, These 5 E-Ink Gadgets Went Deliberately Quiet
============================================================

New E Ink devices from manufacturers like XTEINK, BOOX, and Seeed Studio are prioritizing specialized, distraction-free functionality over the feature-heavy designs typical of modern smartphone and tablet technology.

### Key Points
- The XTEINK X4 Classic features a 4.3-inch display and magnetic backplate, designed as a minimalist companion device for smartphones.
- BOOX introduced the 3.97-inch Picco, a compact, Linux-based e-reader that fits into wallets and prioritizes simple, offline reading.
- Seeed Studio’s reTerminal Sticky is a magnetic, voice-transcribing kitchen display that replaces paper notes with a shared digital notice board.
- The Flowtica Verso clips onto phones to provide real-time meeting transcriptions and always-on calendar displays with 72-hour battery life.
- The DuRoBo Krono utilizes a 6.13-inch Carta 1200 screen and Android 15, offering full app store access and a tactile Smart Dial interface.

### Why it Matters
These devices represent a growing market shift toward "calm technology" that aims to reduce digital fatigue by offering single-purpose, distraction-free alternatives to smartphones. By focusing on specific tasks like reading, note-taking, or scheduling, these gadgets provide users with functional tools that avoid the constant interruptions of algorithmic feeds and notifications.
