90% of my searches don’t touch Google anymore — here’s what I use instead
============================================================

Increasing numbers of internet users are abandoning Google Search in favor of Brave Search due to frustrations with excessive advertisements, sponsored content, and inaccurate AI-generated search overviews.

### Key Points
- Users report that Google Search results are increasingly cluttered with sponsored links, irrelevant shopping suggestions, and SEO-driven spam.
- Brave Search utilizes an independent index, allowing it to provide results without the heavy reliance on advertising and promotional content found on Google.
- The integration of AI Overviews in Google has drawn criticism for frequent hallucinations and the inability for users to disable the feature.
- Brave Search offers optional AI summaries that users can toggle off, providing a more customizable and streamlined browsing experience.
- Google remains a primary tool for some users specifically for local business listings and broad retail product availability in international markets like South Africa.

### Why it Matters
The shift toward alternative search engines highlights a growing consumer demand for cleaner, ad-free interfaces that prioritize organic results over commercial interests. This trend suggests that established tech giants may face long-term retention challenges if their pursuit of AI-driven monetization continues to degrade the core utility of their search products.
