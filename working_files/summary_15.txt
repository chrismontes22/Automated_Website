- Anthropic’s Mythos model is identifying critical vulnerabilities as researchers track exploitation attempts originating from China-hosted IP addresses.

- EQT has acquired a majority stake in the Swiss cybersecurity firm Acronis, valuing the company at more than $3.5 billion.
- Shopify has acquired the Tailwind CSS framework to provide a stable future for the open-source project amid shifting web development trends.
- Microsoft is currently addressing a zero-day attack targeting on-premises SharePoint servers after previous security patches failed to resolve the vulnerability.

### Why it Matters
These updates reflect the growing tension between rapid AI infrastructure expansion and the persistent security risks facing global enterprise networks. As companies navigate hardware shortages and regulatory hurdles, the consolidation of critical software tools and increased focus on infrastructure hardening remain essential for long-term stability.
