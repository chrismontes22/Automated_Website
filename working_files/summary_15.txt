Dow surges 1,000 points as Democrats take control of Congress
============================================================

The Dow Jones Industrial Average surged over 1,500 points on Wednesday as Democratic victories in the midterm elections secured control of both the U.S. House and Senate.

### Key Points
- The Dow Jones Industrial Average rose 3%, marking its strongest single-day performance since April 9, 2025.
- Democrats secured 54 Senate seats, including key victories in Republican strongholds like Texas and South Carolina.
- West Texas Intermediate oil futures dropped over 7% to $88.60 per barrel following comments from Vice President JD Vance.
- Microsoft and Amazon shares each climbed 6%, while Nvidia stock declined 3% amid concerns over future AI regulation.
- GasBuddy analysts project the national average price of gasoline could fall below $4 per gallon within the next week.

### Why it Matters
The market rally reflects investor relief that the election results aligned with historical norms and pre-election betting market predictions. However, the shift in legislative power introduces potential regulatory headwinds for the artificial intelligence sector and major tech companies that have driven recent market growth.
