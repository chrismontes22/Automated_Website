Your Customers Are Buying in 20 Different Places. Is Your Brand There?
============================================================

Modern retail strategies must evolve beyond single-funnel models as consumers now engage with nearly 20 touchpoints across multiple platforms before completing a purchase in today's fragmented digital landscape.

### Key Points
- The average consumer interacts with seven different platforms monthly, with 82% of buyers checking social media before visiting a brand's website.
- AI tools like Walmart’s Sparky assistant are increasingly used for conversational shopping, with related query volume in ChatGPT rising significantly in early 2025.
- Customers require consistent information, pricing, and branding across all channels to build the trust necessary for conversion.
- Brands like Zara and PATTERN Beauty are successfully integrating direct-purchase social features and AI-driven product recommendations to reduce friction.
- Loyalty is best maintained through "always-on" engagement strategies rather than sporadic, large-scale marketing campaigns.
- Global expansion requires localized content and cultural adaptation, as 72% of consumers prefer shopping on native-language websites.

### Why it Matters
Relying on a single sales funnel is no longer effective because the modern customer journey is decentralized and unpredictable. Businesses that prioritize consistent, multi-channel presence and AI-optimized product data are better positioned to capture sales in an increasingly complex digital marketplace.
