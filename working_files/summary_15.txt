AI Data Centers Are Running Out of Water. This $6 Stock Has a Fix
============================================================

AirJoule Technologies is gaining retail investor attention as its atmospheric water generation systems offer a potential solution to the critical water cooling shortages facing modern AI data centers.

### Key Points
- AirJoule produces the Core unit for residential use and the Prime unit, which generates up to 2,000 liters of distilled water daily for industrial applications.
- The company has secured strategic partnerships with GE Vernova, Carrier Global, and Japanese water giant Kubota to scale manufacturing and distribution.
- AirJoule is currently testing its technology at a Nexus Data Centers hyperscale campus in Hubbard, Texas, to address water supply constraints for AI infrastructure.
- The equipment represents only 1% to 3% of total data center build costs, providing a cost-effective alternative to traditional water permitting and sourcing.
- A three-year research contract with the U.S. Army focuses on developing portable water generation systems for military personnel in forward-deployed locations.

### Why it Matters
Water scarcity has emerged as a significant bottleneck for the expansion of energy-intensive AI data centers, forcing some developers to abandon major projects. By providing an on-site, atmospheric water source, AirJoule offers a scalable solution that could accelerate infrastructure development and reduce operational reliance on local municipal supplies.
