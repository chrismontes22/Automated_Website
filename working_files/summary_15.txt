Gemini’s new blurred UI is finally reaching Android phones
============================================================

Google is rolling out a refreshed Gemini AI overlay for Android devices, featuring a new blurred background effect that visually separates the assistant from the underlying screen content.

### Key Points
- The updated interface is currently reaching users on devices including the Google Pixel 10 Pro XL and Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra.
- The design replaces the standard floating box with a prominent, softened blur effect applied to the background behind the Gemini panel.
- This server-side update follows previous refinements like thinner icons, Dynamic Color support, and the integration of Gemini Live into the overlay.
- Google has not provided a manual toggle, meaning the rollout is being managed automatically and may not appear for all users simultaneously.

### Why it Matters
This design change signals Google's ongoing effort to make its AI assistant feel more natively integrated into the Android operating system. By improving visual clarity and reducing distraction, the company aims to streamline user interactions with Gemini across various hardware platforms.
