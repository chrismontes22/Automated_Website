Back to the '90s: I turned my Frankenstein’s monster of a gaming PC into a retro beige beauty in honor of a time when computers used to be fun
============================================================

Rising costs for essential computer components like RAM and SSDs, driven by high demand from AI services, are forcing PC enthusiasts to repurpose existing hardware instead of upgrading.

### Key Points
- Memory-based components, including RAM, SSDs, and graphics cards, have seen significant price increases throughout 2026.
- Consumer electronics like the PS5, Xbox Series consoles, and Apple MacBooks have experienced multiple price hikes, bucking historical trends of declining tech costs.
- Valve’s Steam Deck saw an 80% sales drop following a $240 price increase, highlighting the impact of rising hardware costs on consumer demand.
- PC users are increasingly turning to creative case swaps and software modifications to refresh aging systems without purchasing expensive new components.
- Modular power supplies and proprietary fan ecosystems, such as Corsair’s iCUE LINK, require careful cable management and planning during custom PC builds.

### Why it Matters
The shift toward higher hardware prices threatens the long-standing consumer expectation that technology becomes more affordable and accessible over time. This trend forces users to extend the lifespan of their current devices, potentially slowing the pace of innovation adoption across the broader gaming and computing markets.
