Why is Everyone in Tech so sad?
============================================================

The rapid integration of artificial intelligence into corporate environments is triggering an existential crisis among knowledge workers, causing many to question the long-term purpose and value of their careers.

### Key Points
- Knowledge workers are increasingly experiencing "Workism," a trend where professional roles are used to derive meaning and community, similar to religious devotion.
- AI automation is accelerating the abstraction of tasks, distancing employees from the direct experience of their work and eroding the collaborative "messy middle."
- Experts suggest that intrinsic motivation, which drives high-quality creative output, is threatened by environments that prioritize relentless efficiency and outcome-focused metrics over human connection.
- The disillusionment is leading some professionals to seek fulfillment in analog hobbies or alternative lifestyles, potentially impacting future talent retention in corporate sectors.
- Guy Debord’s "The Society of the Spectacle" is being cited to explain how modern work has become a mediated performance that risks losing its human foundation.

### Why it Matters
The widespread loss of faith in corporate careers could fundamentally disrupt organizational structures and labor markets as top talent seeks more authentic, human-centric alternatives. If companies continue to prioritize AI-driven efficiency at the expense of the collaborative human experience, they risk alienating the very workforce necessary for genuine innovation.
