Bitcoin soars to nearly $80,000—but crypto’s new favorite coin, Hyperliquid, is stealing its thunder
============================================================

Bitcoin climbed above $78,200 while the decentralized exchange Hyperliquid experienced record growth, signaling a shift in investor interest toward perpetual futures trading platforms over direct token purchases.

### Key Points
- Bitcoin reached a price of $78,200 on Friday, supported by $4.5 billion in short liquidations over the past week.
- Hyperliquid’s HYPE token has surged over 195% this year, with the platform processing $633 billion in volume during the first quarter of 2026.
- President Donald Trump announced that his administration is working to bring Hyperliquid into the United States in a compliant manner.
- Trump is urging Congress to pass the Clarity Act to establish a formal regulatory framework for digital assets.
- Bitwise Investments analysts suggest that Hyperliquid’s popularity is drawing trading volume away from spot Bitcoin and other smaller crypto assets.

### Why it Matters
The rise of decentralized perpetual futures platforms like Hyperliquid is fundamentally changing how capital flows through the cryptocurrency market by prioritizing leveraged speculation over traditional asset ownership. This shift could force regulators to accelerate the development of clear market frameworks as decentralized exchanges continue to capture significant trading volume from centralized alternatives.
