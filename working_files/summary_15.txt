I almost want this new Windows wallpaper more than any of the RTX Spark laptops
============================================================

Microsoft fans are using AI to recreate an unreleased wallpaper spotted on new RTX Spark laptops during a recent Windows and Surface hardware event.

### Key Points
- Microsoft showcased a new, unreleased wallpaper on RTX Spark laptops during a recent company event.
- The official image remains unavailable to the public, prompting users to generate recreations using ChatGPT.
- RTX Spark laptops are currently available for preorder and are scheduled to begin shipping on October 16, 2026.
- Generative AI tools, including Adobe Photoshop, are increasingly used by the community to expand or modify iconic Windows imagery.

### Why it Matters
The high demand for official Microsoft wallpapers highlights the strong brand identity associated with Windows hardware aesthetics. As users turn to AI to replicate these designs, it underscores a growing trend of consumers using generative technology to bridge the gap between official product reveals and public availability.
