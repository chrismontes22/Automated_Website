Bessent says U.S. proposed AI incident alert system in talks with China
============================================================

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent announced a proposed U.S.-China notification mechanism for artificial intelligence incidents to improve transparency between the world’s two leading powers ahead of upcoming presidential talks.

### Key Points
- Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and Chinese Vice Premier He Lifeng discussed the AI safety framework during weekend meetings in New York.
- The proposed mechanism aims to share information regarding AI incidents that could potentially threaten national security.
- U.S. Trade Representative Jamieson Greer confirmed the operationalization of a new "Board of Trade" to facilitate ongoing economic cooperation.
- Officials are currently negotiating a list of nonsensitive goods, including agricultural and medical products, for potential tariff reductions.
- President Donald Trump and President Xi Jinping are scheduled to hold formal discussions this Thursday to address these trade and technology issues.

### Why it Matters
This agreement establishes a critical precedent for international cooperation on AI safety between the world's largest economies. By moving toward greater transparency, both nations aim to mitigate systemic risks while maintaining their competitive positions in the global technology market.
