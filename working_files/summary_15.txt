iPhone 18 Pro Camera Upgrades: Variable Aperture and Pro Controls
============================================================

Apple unveiled the iPhone 18 Pro featuring a 48-megapixel camera with variable aperture technology, advanced AI-powered focus tracking, and new professional-grade manual controls for photography enthusiasts.

### Key Points
- The new 48-megapixel main camera utilizes a six-blade polymer composite system to adjust light intake and depth of field.
- Pro Controls allow users to manually adjust shutter speed, white balance, aperture, and skin texture during photo capture.
- Smart focus tracking uses on-device AI to maintain subject detail even when subjects move in and out of the frame.
- Apple introduced a "digital negative" feature that uses pixel-level data signing to verify photos were not AI-generated.
- Video capabilities now include 4K time-lapse in Dolby Vision HDR and cinematic mode support at 60 frames per second.
- Preorders for the iPhone 18 Pro begin September 12, with general retail availability starting September 18.

### Why it Matters
These hardware and software upgrades signal Apple's continued push to position the iPhone as a primary tool for professional photographers and videographers. By integrating verifiable image authenticity and advanced manual controls, the company is addressing growing concerns regarding AI-generated content while expanding its footprint in the high-end creative market.
