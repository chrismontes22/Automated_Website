China Is Ditching Touchscreens For Essential Controls In New Cars
============================================================

China’s Ministry of Industry and Information Technology will mandate physical controls for essential vehicle functions in all new cars starting July 1, 2027, to improve driver safety.

### Key Points
- New regulations require physical buttons for turn signals, windshield wipers, window defrosters, and gear shifting.
- The mandate updates the GB4094-2016 national standard to ensure controls are operable by touch without requiring visual attention.
- Digital touchscreen gear shifters, similar to those currently used in Tesla models, will be prohibited under the new rules.
- Safety requirements specify that essential switches must have defined sizes and provide tactile feedback to the driver.
- Research from the University of Washington and Toyota Research Institute indicates that touchscreen interaction significantly increases cognitive load and driver distraction.

### Why it Matters
This regulatory shift signals a major reversal in automotive interior design, prioritizing tactile ergonomics over the minimalist, screen-heavy aesthetic favored by many modern manufacturers. By forcing a return to physical interfaces, the policy could influence global vehicle design standards and pressure automakers to balance digital innovation with proven safety practices.
