Python Software Foundation: Georgi Ker: 2026 PSF Board Election Candidate Interview
============================================================

PSF Board member Georgi Georgiev is seeking re-election to address the organization's financial sustainability and improve long-term operational planning for the Python programming language's global infrastructure.

### Key Points
- Georgi Georgiev has served on the Python Software Foundation (PSF) Board since 2023, previously acting as Treasurer and currently as Vice Chair.
- The candidate advocates for moving beyond reliance on PyCon US revenue by diversifying sponsorship models to fund core infrastructure and legal protections.
- Key policy goals include implementing evidence-based financial planning, increasing organizational transparency, and strengthening internal systems to support global community growth.
- Georgiev’s background includes leadership roles in PyLadiesCon, EuroPython, and various regional Python communities across Asia.
- The platform emphasizes professionalizing PSF operations while maintaining the community-driven, volunteer-focused nature of the Python ecosystem.

### Why it Matters
As Python becomes critical global infrastructure, the PSF faces increasing pressure to manage its resources and legal responsibilities more effectively. A shift toward sustainable financial models ensures the foundation can support the language's security and development without relying solely on conference cycles.
