I went hands-on with the iPhone Duo, and I'm genuinely excited
============================================================

Apple has officially entered the foldable market with the iPhone Duo, a premium device featuring a creaseless 7.6-inch OLED display, advanced hinge mechanics, and the powerful A20 Pro chip.

### Key Points
- The iPhone Duo features a 7.6-inch creaseless inner OLED panel utilizing ultra-thin glass and a glare-reducing nano-texture coating.
- The device is powered by the 2nm A20 Pro silicon, matching the performance and thermal management of the iPhone 18 Pro.
- Weighing 254g, the foldable utilizes optimized weight distribution to improve ergonomics compared to competitors like the Pixel 11 Pro Fold.
- Software features include intuitive split-screen multitasking and a taskbar, with the hinge supporting various angles for hands-free content viewing.
- The iPhone Duo is priced at $1,999 and is scheduled to go on sale starting October 23.

### Why it Matters
The entry of Apple into the foldable segment signals a major shift that challenges the dominance of established players like Samsung and Google. By prioritizing hardware polish and display innovation, the iPhone Duo sets a new benchmark that may force Android manufacturers to refine their own foldable designs and software integration.
