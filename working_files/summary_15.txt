I was wrong about Pokémon Sleep, and the Fitbit Air is the perfect companion
============================================================

Google has released a special edition Fitbit Air featuring a Pokémon Sleep design, offering users a lightweight, comfortable wearable to gamify their nightly rest and track sleep data.

### Key Points
- The Fitbit Air Special Edition features a deep blue band with a sleeping Pikachu design to celebrate the 30th anniversary of the Pokémon franchise.
- The device weighs approximately 12 grams, making it a lightweight alternative to bulkier smartwatches for overnight sleep tracking.
- Users can integrate the Fitbit Air with the Pokémon Sleep app by syncing data through Google Health to gamify sleep cycles.
- The wearable provides reliable sleep metrics comparable to high-end devices like the Oura Ring and Garmin watches.
- The Fitbit Air offers a battery life of approximately one week, reducing the need for frequent charging.
- The special edition device is currently available for purchase on the Google Store for $129.

### Why it Matters
This collaboration highlights the growing trend of gamifying health and wellness metrics to encourage consistent sleep habits among consumers. By integrating popular entertainment franchises with wearable technology, companies can increase user engagement with health-tracking platforms.
