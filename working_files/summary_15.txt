U-KNOCK 2026 Brings Korean Content Companies To Hollywood For Investment & Global Partnerships
============================================================

The U-KNOCK 2026 event will bring South Korean content and technology firms to The Biltmore Los Angeles from November 4–6 to foster partnerships with U.S. investors.

### Key Points
- The Korea Creative Content Agency is hosting the three-day event to facilitate deal-making between Korean media companies and Hollywood studios.
- The International Content Investment Forum on November 5 will feature leaders from firms like The Raine Group, J.P. Morgan, and Amblin Entertainment.
- Five companies, including Modhaus and YN Culture & Space, will present business models during a dedicated showcase focused on North American expansion.
- A total of 12 Korean companies specializing in IP, generative AI, and fan platforms will participate in one-on-one meetings on November 6.
- Participating Korean firms include CJ ENM, YLAB, IPX, and various startups focused on webtoons, virtual production, and interactive digital humans.

### Why it Matters
This event serves as a strategic bridge for Korean media companies seeking to monetize intellectual property and secure capital within the competitive U.S. entertainment market. By connecting local creators with major financial institutions and production studios, the program aims to transform cultural content into sustainable, long-term global franchises.
