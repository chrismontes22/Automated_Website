Apple Maps to Power Navigation Experience for Ford’s New EVs
============================================================

Ford Motor Company will integrate Apple Maps into its upcoming Universal Electric Vehicle Platform starting in 2027, utilizing the new MapKit for Automotive SDK to enhance navigation and driving.

### Key Points
- Ford’s Universal Electric Vehicle (UEV) Platform will feature native Apple Maps integration beginning in 2027.
- The integration utilizes Apple’s new MapKit for Automotive SDK to provide real-time traffic, natural language directions, and EV-specific routing.
- Ford will leverage Apple’s road-level mapping data to improve its next-generation BlueCruise hands-free highway driving technology.
- The upcoming midsize electric vehicle platform is expected to be priced at approximately $30,000.
- Apple’s SDK maintains user privacy by ensuring location data and activity cannot be linked back to individual drivers.

### Why it Matters
This partnership marks a significant shift in automotive software by embedding Apple’s navigation technology directly into vehicle hardware rather than relying solely on smartphone projection. It allows Ford to offer a premium, integrated digital experience in its affordable electric vehicles while strengthening the competitive capabilities of its BlueCruise hands-free driving system.
