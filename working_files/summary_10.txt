Kalshi ordered to shut down sports and election prediction markets in Washington state by Sept. 2
============================================================

A King County judge has ordered prediction market platform Kalshi to cease operations for election, sports, and entertainment contracts in Washington state by September 2 or face daily fines.

### Key Points
- Judge John McHale ordered Kalshi to geofence Washington users from non-financial markets, including politics, technology, and culture, by the September 2 deadline.
- The company faces a $120,000-per-day penalty for non-compliance, mirroring similar regulatory challenges Kalshi currently faces in Nevada.
- Kalshi may continue offering contracts related to commodities, climate, economics, and finance within the state.
- The court ruled that Kalshi willfully ignored a December 2025 notice from the Washington State Gambling Commission regarding unauthorized event-based contracts.
- Kalshi maintains that the U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission holds exclusive jurisdiction over its platform and is considering further legal appeals.

### Why it Matters
This ruling highlights the growing regulatory friction between state gambling authorities and emerging prediction market platforms operating under federal oversight. The outcome sets a significant legal precedent for how states may restrict event-based wagering platforms that operate across multiple jurisdictions.
