Best Google Pixel Watch 5 screen protectors
============================================================

Selecting the right screen protector for the Google Pixel Watch 5 requires choosing between various 41mm and 45mm options that offer self-healing materials, oleophobic coatings, and installation kits.

### Key Points
- The ZAGG Ultra Eco Smart Watch Screen Protector features plant-based, self-healing technology and a lifetime replacement warranty for $24.99.
- EGV offers six-pack TPU film kits for both 41mm and 45mm models, featuring water and dust-repellent oleophobic coatings for approximately $10.
- Ringke provides a two-pack of EPU films under $20 that includes a specialized installation and alignment frame for easier application.
- ArmorSuit manufactures U.S.-made MilitaryShield HD Clear TPU films, available in six-packs for both watch size variants.
- Most available screen protectors utilize self-healing materials designed to resist scratches and maintain edge-to-edge clarity on the Pixel Watch 5 display.

### Why it Matters
Protecting the display of a wearable device is essential for maintaining its resale value and long-term aesthetic appeal. These accessories provide a cost-effective way to prevent permanent damage to the watch face while offering features like self-healing technology to extend the lifespan of the screen guard.
