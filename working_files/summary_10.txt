Fixing The Back-Office Problem That Eats Into Agency Margins
============================================================

Digital advertising agencies are increasingly adopting AI orchestration layers to automate complex financial reconciliation processes, reducing manual labor costs and improving cash flow across the media supply chain.

### Key Points
- Manual reconciliation of fragmented data across multiple advertising platforms creates significant administrative costs and erodes agency profit margins.
- Delayed billing cycles force agencies to act as temporary banks for clients, often leading to cash-flow pressures and reliance on credit lines.
- Payment delays ripple through the industry, sometimes extending vendor payment terms to as long as 150 days.
- Agencies are moving away from platform consolidation in favor of "best-of-breed" technology stacks that prioritize interoperability.
- New AI orchestration tools, such as the integration between Basis and Mediaocean Prisma, connect planning, activation, and financial data to create a single source of truth.

### Why it Matters
Automating back-end financial operations allows agencies to maintain competitive, specialized technology stacks without sacrificing operational efficiency. By streamlining these workflows, firms can improve their bottom line and gain the financial leverage necessary to negotiate better terms with media partners.
