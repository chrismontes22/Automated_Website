The Future is for Everyone
============================================================

Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg outlines a philosophy for the future of superintelligence, advocating for the broad distribution of AI tools to empower individuals rather than centralizing power within institutions.

### Key Points
- Meta aims to provide personal AI agents to billions of users, focusing on individual empowerment, privacy, and tools for creation and learning.
- The company plans to resume releasing open-source AI models to prevent the risks associated with centralized, proprietary superintelligence.
- Zuckerberg proposes a "balance of power" model where competing AI agents and diverse user goals act as natural checks against systemic risks.
- Meta is implementing a new governance structure that grants its independent board of directors authority over safety criteria and model releases.
- The strategy includes proactive collaboration with the U.S. government to harden critical infrastructure against cybersecurity and biological threats.

### Why it Matters
By prioritizing the widespread distribution of AI, Meta seeks to counter the narrative that superintelligence must be restricted to a few elite institutions to remain safe. This approach aims to foster economic growth and personal agency while positioning open-source development as a vital component of national security and democratic stability.
