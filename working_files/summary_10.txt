Latin America is becoming a laboratory for tech-supremacism
============================================================

Latin American nations are increasingly adopting a model of tech-supremacism and extreme deregulation, driven by Silicon Valley influence and political strategies that prioritize surveillance and corporate-state integration.

### Key Points
- Argentina has implemented the "Super RIGI" framework, offering tax exemptions and resource access to attract global artificial intelligence and data-processing infrastructure.
- The Argentine government is developing a "Social Digital Twin" platform to simulate public policy outcomes, raising concerns over data privacy and lack of transparency.
- Argentina’s Ministry of Security has partnered with the FBI to expand genetic profiling through the CODIS software, mandating registration for all indicted citizens.
- Palantir Technologies, co-founded by Peter Thiel, serves as a primary model for the integration of private surveillance infrastructure into state security apparatuses.
- Regional political leaders are utilizing "tough-on-crime" narratives and biometric tools to implement selective urban segregation and monitor marginalized populations.

### Why it Matters
This shift toward algorithmic governance and corporate-led security represents a significant erosion of national sovereignty in Latin America. By embedding private tech interests into state functions, these nations risk creating permanent systems of social control that prioritize colonial-era hierarchies over democratic accountability.
