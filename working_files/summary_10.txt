How many jobs is your state losing to AI? This dashboard tracks the cuts – and the cost
============================================================

The Center for AI Safety’s new Wage Loss Dashboard estimates that AI-driven layoffs have caused $8.5 billion in annual wage losses across the United States throughout 2026.

### Key Points
- The dashboard tracks over 117,000 job cuts explicitly attributed to AI by companies since January 2026.
- California faces the highest economic impact, with an estimated $56 in annual wage loss per person due to a high concentration of tech-sector layoffs.
- Data is compiled using reports from Challenger, Gray, and Christmas, Bureau of Labor Statistics wage data, and US Census Bureau population estimates.
- The Remote Labor Index shows AI agents can now complete 15.8% of professional freelance tasks, a figure that is doubling every 3.7 months.
- The tracker excludes potential economic benefits like job creation or productivity gains, focusing solely on the monetary impact of workforce displacement.

### Why it Matters
Quantifying the financial impact of AI-related layoffs helps policymakers and communities move beyond speculation to understand the tangible economic consequences of automation. By visualizing these losses, the dashboard highlights how AI adoption creates uneven regional impacts that ripple through local businesses and household stability.
