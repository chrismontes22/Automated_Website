Senate Republicans say crypto bill likely to fail
============================================================

Republican senators warn that the bipartisan CLARITY Act faces a likely defeat next week due to ongoing disputes regarding ethics provisions governing the president and his family.

### Key Points
- Republican senators, including Mike Rounds and Thom Tillis, indicate the cryptocurrency regulatory bill lacks the necessary support to pass the Senate.
- Negotiations remain stalled over Democratic demands for stricter ethics requirements concerning the president and his family members.
- The White House maintains that the current proposal already includes the most comprehensive ethics provisions in history.
- President Donald Trump continues to urge Congress to pass the legislation to ensure American leadership in global financial innovation.
- Some lawmakers, such as Senator Roger Marshall, report that cryptocurrency regulation is not currently a primary concern for their constituents.

### Why it Matters
A failure to pass the CLARITY Act could signal a significant setback for federal efforts to establish a comprehensive regulatory framework for the digital asset industry. This legislative impasse may prompt crypto-focused interest groups to increase political spending in upcoming congressional races to influence future policy outcomes.
