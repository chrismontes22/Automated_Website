"A cold dead place that's slowly decaying" - EA employees reveal a culture of secrecy and terrible morale following layoffs
============================================================

Electronic Arts has initiated a restructuring plan involving 400 layoffs and the closure of the BioWare studio to prioritize core franchises and address significant corporate debt obligations.

### Key Points
- Electronic Arts is cutting 400 jobs and closing the BioWare studio to focus on high-revenue projects.
- Employees report that layoff notifications are conducted via restricted Zoom calls without opportunities for questions or farewells.
- Staff morale has declined due to a perceived lack of transparency and the company's increasing reliance on generative AI tools.
- The restructuring follows a $55 billion acquisition, leaving the publisher with $20 billion in debt to recoup.
- Industry data indicates 2026 is currently on track to become the worst year for layoffs in the gaming sector.

### Why it Matters
These layoffs reflect a broader trend of instability within the gaming industry as major publishers struggle to manage debt and shifting market expectations. The resulting decline in employee morale and project continuity highlights the human cost of corporate efforts to prioritize short-term profitability over long-term development stability.
