There Are 1,000 Private Business Owners Worth $25 Million for Every Large Public Company CEO
============================================================

New research using anonymous IRS tax data reveals that for every large public company CEO, there are 1,000 private business owners worth at least $25 million in America.

### Key Points
- Economists Eric Zwick and his co-author analyzed anonymous IRS microdata to identify a massive, previously uncounted population of wealthy Americans.
- The study found that the wealthiest private business owners are typically auto dealers, beverage distributors, contractors, dentists, and medical practice owners.
- Researchers identified these individuals by cross-referencing tax income signatures with private yacht and jet registration data.
- Many of these mid-market business owners are increasingly involved in private equity buyouts and rollups.
- The findings suggest that public-facing wealth metrics, such as CEO pay packages, represent only a tiny fraction of the actual high-net-worth population.

### Why it Matters
Current economic debates and wealth-tax models often rely on a narrow, public-company-centric view of the wealthy that ignores the vast majority of high-net-worth individuals. Understanding this hidden cohort is essential for accurately analyzing regional real estate markets, municipal bond demand, and the broader flow of private capital.
