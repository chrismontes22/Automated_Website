‘The most eye-watering thing I’ve seen since the solar eclipse’ — experiencing the MSI Claw 8 EX AI+’s staggering performance honestly blew my mind but I’m afraid I can’t get over that absurd price tag
============================================================

The MSI Claw 8 EX AI+ is a powerful new handheld gaming console featuring the Intel Arc G3 Extreme chipset, though its high price point challenges its market competitiveness.

### Key Points
- The device features an Intel Arc G3 Extreme chipset, Intel Arc B390 GPU, and 32GB of RAM.
- It includes an 8-inch 120Hz FHD+ touchscreen display and Hall effect thumbsticks for improved durability.
- The console launched on June 23, 2026, with a premium retail price of $1,799.99.
- Performance benchmarks show significant graphical gains over competitors, though it lacks built-in mouse controls.
- The unit is powered by an 80Whr battery, which typically lasts between 90 and 150 minutes during intensive gaming.

### Why it Matters
- The MSI Claw 8 EX AI+ pushes the boundaries of portable gaming performance but enters a difficult price bracket occupied by capable mid-range gaming laptops. This positioning forces consumers to choose between the convenience of a handheld form factor and the superior raw power offered by traditional portable PCs.
