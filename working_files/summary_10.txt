9to5Mac Overtime 082: Origins of the Apple Store w/ special guest Ron Johnson
============================================================

Former Apple retail executive Ron Johnson discusses his career, his new book Shop Different, and his experiences working alongside Steve Jobs on the latest 9to5Mac Overtime podcast.

### Key Points
- Ron Johnson details the early development of the Apple Store and the launch of the original iPhone.
- The discussion covers Johnson’s transition from Apple to his tenure as CEO of JCPenney.
- Johnson provides professional insights into the current state of Apple’s retail strategy and the iPhone Duo.
- The interview is featured on the 9to5Mac Overtime podcast, hosted by Fernando Silva and Jeff Benjamin.

### Why it Matters
This interview offers a rare, firsthand perspective on the foundational retail strategies that helped transform Apple into a global consumer powerhouse. Understanding these historical business decisions provides valuable context for how the company maintains its unique brand identity and retail dominance today.
