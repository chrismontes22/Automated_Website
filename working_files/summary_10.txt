Guillermo del Toro Promises ‘Absolutely No Goddamn AI’ on ‘Pan’s Labyrinth’ 3D Re-Release
============================================================

Filmmaker Guillermo del Toro confirmed that his 2006 masterpiece Pan’s Labyrinth will receive a 3D theatrical re-release on October 9 without the use of any artificial intelligence technology.

### Key Points
- Guillermo del Toro announced the 3D re-release of Pan’s Labyrinth to celebrate the film's 20th anniversary.
- The director explicitly rejected the use of AI in the conversion process, citing a commitment to preserving artistic craft.
- Pan’s Labyrinth originally earned $83 million globally and secured three Academy Awards for cinematography, art direction, and makeup.
- Del Toro has consistently voiced strong opposition to generative AI, stating he intends to avoid the technology throughout his career.

### Why it Matters
This stance highlights a growing divide in Hollywood regarding the integration of automated tools in creative production. By prioritizing traditional craftsmanship over cost-effective technology, del Toro underscores the ongoing debate about the value of human labor in the future of cinema.
