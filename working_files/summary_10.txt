Jill Lepore: AI Is Eroding Civil Society
============================================================

Harvard historian Jill Lepore argues in her new book that Silicon Valley leaders are eroding democratic institutions by wielding artificial intelligence as a tool for global corporate control.

### Key Points
- Jill Lepore’s book, *The Rise and Fall of the Artificial State*, examines how Big Tech corporations increasingly replace human governance with machine-led systems.
- Lepore characterizes Silicon Valley’s influence as cult-like, noting that leaders prioritize business interests and AI acceleration over democratic stability and public safety.
- The author suggests that figures like Sam Altman may currently hold more global influence than political leaders like Donald Trump.
- Silicon Valley’s political alignment shifted toward Trump following the Biden administration’s efforts to regulate cryptocurrency and implement AI safety safeguards.
- Public resistance is emerging through local activism, such as community opposition to the construction of new data centers.

### Why it Matters
This analysis highlights a growing tension between the unchecked power of global technology firms and the preservation of democratic civil society. It suggests that the public must find ways to engage in governance outside of corporate-controlled digital platforms to reclaim influence from the "Artificial State."
