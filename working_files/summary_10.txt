I’ve Been a Software Developer For 20 Years. Here’s Why I’m All in on Vibe Coding.
============================================================

Jotform founder Aytekin Tank explores how "vibe coding," the use of AI to generate software from plain-language prompts, accelerates startup development and streamlines daily operational tasks for entrepreneurs.

### Key Points
- Vibe coding allows non-technical founders to build minimum viable products (MVPs) and custom tools without needing traditional programming expertise.
- Popular AI development tools mentioned include Claude Code, Lovable, and Base44.
- Research from Daily.dev indicates that approximately 45% of AI-generated code may contain security weaknesses, including critical vulnerabilities in data handling.
- Complex tasks such as payment processing, encryption, and user authentication still require human oversight and professional technical expertise.
- A foundational understanding of programming logic remains essential for effectively directing AI models and ensuring the quality of the final output.

### Why it Matters
Vibe coding significantly lowers the barrier to entry for innovation by allowing entrepreneurs to prototype ideas and automate repetitive tasks with minimal resources. However, users must balance this speed with caution, as relying solely on AI without technical oversight can introduce serious security risks to their applications.
