In A ‘Who Could Have Possibly Seen This Coming’ Event, Ubisoft Will Shut Down Its NFT Game
============================================================

Ubisoft will officially shut down its Web3-enabled strategy game, Champions Tactics: Grimoria Chronicles, on October 30 following a failed transition away from its original blockchain-based infrastructure.

### Key Points
- Ubisoft announced the permanent closure of Champions Tactics: Grimoria Chronicles servers effective October 30.
- The game originally launched in 2024 as a Web3 title allowing players to trade units as NFTs on the Oasys blockchain.
- A non-blockchain version titled Champions Tactics Reforged failed to gain significant traction on Steam after removing NFT functionality.
- Ubisoft is currently incentivizing remaining players to migrate to its new collectible card game, Might and Magic Fates.
- Might and Magic Fates, which entered early access in February, continues to incorporate a marketplace for cash and cryptocurrency transactions.

### Why it Matters
This closure highlights the ongoing difficulty major publishers face when attempting to integrate blockchain technology and NFT marketplaces into traditional gaming ecosystems. The move signals a potential shift in Ubisoft's strategy as it struggles to find a sustainable audience for crypto-integrated titles despite offering migration incentives for its player base.
