Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 is coming for the top Android phones
============================================================

Qualcomm has unveiled its new Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 and Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 mobile platforms, featuring 2nm architecture designed to enhance on-device AI capabilities.

### Key Points
- Both chipsets utilize custom Oryon CPUs, redesigned Adreno GPUs, and Hexagon NPUs to support local, context-aware AI assistants.
- The 2nm platforms introduce Adreno Neural Fusion technology to improve gaming visuals and extend battery life during intensive sessions.
- New camera features include Advanced Professional Video (APV) support and Intelligent Pixel Control for more natural image processing.
- The Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 is expected to power upcoming flagship devices like the Samsung Galaxy S27 series and Motorola Signature 27.

### Why it Matters
These new processors signal a strategic shift toward integrating complex AI agents directly into the hardware of premium Android smartphones. By prioritizing local processing, Qualcomm aims to improve device responsiveness and privacy while setting a new performance standard for the next generation of mobile flagship devices.
