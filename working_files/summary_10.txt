Momentic debuts Mo AI agent to automate software testing without scripts
============================================================

Momentic Inc. has launched Mo, an AI-powered agent designed to automate software testing by eliminating the need for developers to write and maintain complex, manual test scripts.

### Key Points
- Mo allows developers to initiate software testing by providing a URL and specific instructions rather than writing code-based test scripts.
- The platform utilizes a swarm of AI agents to execute thousands of test permutations and edge cases across web and mobile applications.
- Developers can provide context for testing through product requirement documents, Jira issues, Linear tickets, or Confluence pages.
- Upon completion, Mo delivers a detailed report including bug reproduction steps and video evidence of all interactions.
- Early adopters of the technology include companies such as Notion, Superpower, Iris, Committee for Children, and Boundless.

### Why it Matters
This technology addresses the significant maintenance burden of traditional test suites, which often become liabilities as software applications grow in complexity. By shifting from script-based testing to intent-based verification, Momentic aims to improve development velocity and reduce the manual labor required to ensure software quality.
