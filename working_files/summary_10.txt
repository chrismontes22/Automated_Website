Norrsken and Nvidia to open Stockholm AI Lab for founders in 2027
============================================================

The Norrsken Foundation and Nvidia are partnering to launch a specialized AI startup hub in central Stockholm, scheduled to open its doors to European founders in 2027.

### Key Points
- The Stockholm AI Lab will be located at Birger Jarlsgatan 37 and serve as the new regional office for Nvidia.
- Startups focused on climate, health, food, and societal challenges can apply for membership to access computing power and developer tools.
- Nvidia will provide resources through its Inception program, while the ground floor will feature a demo lab operated with Root Bio.
- This initiative follows Norrsken’s June 2025 commitment of a €300 million fund dedicated to supporting European AI ventures for social and environmental impact.
- The facility complements Norrsken’s existing global network, which includes hubs in Barcelona, Kigali, Brussels, and Amsterdam.

### Why it Matters
This collaboration strengthens Europe’s AI infrastructure by providing startups with direct access to Nvidia’s high-performance computing resources and specialized mentorship. It signals a significant shift in the regional tech landscape, aiming to accelerate the development of scalable solutions for global environmental and health crises.
