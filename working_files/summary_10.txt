Your AI Strategy Is Only as Strong as Your Leadership — 3 Ways to Turn Executive Vision Into Employee Adoption
============================================================

Corporate leaders must shift from issuing mandates to fostering supportive environments to bridge the significant gap between executive expectations for artificial intelligence adoption and actual employee usage rates.

### Key Points
- A Slingshot report reveals 86% of executives require AI usage, yet only 49% of middle managers actively reinforce this expectation with their teams.
- Leaders are encouraged to replace command-and-control tactics with coaching to help employees build confidence through iterative experimentation.
- Modeling AI usage in daily tasks and meetings helps normalize the learning process and reduces employee anxiety regarding new technology.
- Positioning AI as a tool for professional growth rather than a replacement for human labor helps mitigate job displacement fears among younger workers.
- Approximately 19% of Gen Z and 17% of millennial employees express concern that AI could eventually replace their current roles.

### Why it Matters
The disconnect between leadership mandates and workforce adoption suggests that many companies are failing to realize the productivity benefits of their AI investments. By reframing AI as an enhancement tool rather than a threat, organizations can improve employee engagement and ensure long-term technological integration.
