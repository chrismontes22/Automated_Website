Bitcoin reclaims $82,500 as improved ETF flows lift market sentiment. Here is what experts say
============================================================

Bitcoin remains stable above the $78,000 support level as the broader cryptocurrency market experiences mixed trading results and a shift in investor sentiment toward a neutral position.

### Key Points
- Bitcoin gained 0.3% over the last 24 hours, while Ethereum declined 0.2% to trade at approximately $2,492.
- Major altcoins including BNB, XRP, Dogecoin, and Cardano saw gains of up to 5.5% during the recent trading session.
- The market Fear and Greed Index dropped from 60 to 55, signaling a shift in investor sentiment from greed to neutral.
- Derivatives data reported $1.02 billion in liquidations affecting 164,899 individual traders within a 24-hour period.
- Analysts suggest that Bitcoin maintaining its $78,000 to $79,000 price range is critical to preventing increased institutional selling pressure.

### Why it Matters
- The recent volatility and high liquidation volume highlight increased risk for leveraged traders in the current digital asset environment. Maintaining key support levels is essential for sustaining institutional confidence and preventing further market corrections.
