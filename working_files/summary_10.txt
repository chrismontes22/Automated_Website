OKX draws investment from StanChart, Circle, Ripple as it pushes beyond crypto exchange roots
============================================================

Crypto exchange OKX has secured new investment from Standard Chartered, Circle, and Ripple at a $25 billion valuation to support its transition into a global financial technology platform.

### Key Points
- OKX raised capital from SC Ventures, Circle, Ripple, and Qube Research & Technologies at a $25 billion pre-money valuation.
- The exchange is partnering with NYSE owner Intercontinental Exchange to launch a 24/7 platform for trading tokenized U.S. stocks.
- The proposed service will utilize the X Layer blockchain and stablecoins like USDC and USDT to facilitate trading for 63 U.S. companies.
- The initiative operates under a five-year SEC framework, allowing tokenized shares to retain standard dividend and voting rights.
- Analysts from Macquarie and TD Securities noted that regulatory uncertainty and integration costs may limit initial institutional adoption of the platform.

### Why it Matters
This expansion signals a significant shift as major crypto exchanges attempt to bridge the gap between digital assets and traditional financial markets. By integrating stablecoins into regulated stock trading, OKX is testing the viability of blockchain technology as a primary settlement tool for global finance.
