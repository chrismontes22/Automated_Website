Google Home Speaker is downloading huge amounts of data, and users need it to stop
============================================================

Google Home Speaker users are reporting massive, unexplained data consumption reaching several terabytes per month, prompting the company to begin rolling out a software fix for the issue.

### Key Points
- Users reported individual Google Home Speakers consuming up to 140GB of data in a single day.
- Total data usage across multiple devices reached 6TB in less than one month for some households.
- Reports suggest the issue may stem from a failed firmware update loop causing repeated, unsuccessful download attempts.
- Google has acknowledged the problem and confirmed that a software patch is currently being deployed to affected devices.
- The issue appears isolated to the Google Home Speaker, with other models like the Nest Audio remaining unaffected.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the potential for significant financial impact on users with data-capped internet plans when smart home devices malfunction. It also underscores the risks associated with background firmware updates, which can lead to excessive bandwidth consumption if software installation processes fail repeatedly.
