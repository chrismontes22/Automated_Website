Israel’s Largest Bank Partners With Galaxy Digital To Offer Crypto Trading
============================================================

Bank Leumi has partnered with Galaxy Digital to become the first Israeli lender to offer customers the ability to buy, hold, and sell cryptocurrencies starting in 2027.

### Key Points
- Bank Leumi will integrate Galaxy Digital’s "GalaxyOne Institutional" platform to facilitate crypto trading services.
- The partnership includes the use of Galaxy’s custody infrastructure to secure digital assets for bank clients.
- Customers will gain access to major cryptocurrencies, including Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Solana, through the bank's mobile application.
- Bank Leumi shares have increased by 20% on the Tel Aviv Stock Exchange over the past year.
- Galaxy Digital, based in New York, currently trades at $21.58 per share following a 25% decline over the last 12 months.

### Why it Matters
This collaboration marks a significant shift in the Israeli banking sector by legitimizing digital assets within a traditional financial framework. It provides Galaxy Digital with a strategic foothold in the Middle Eastern market while allowing Bank Leumi to modernize its service offerings to meet growing investor demand.
