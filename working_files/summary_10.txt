What Does It Take to Get Travel AI Out of the Pilot Stage?
============================================================

Travel companies are shifting focus from experimental AI pilots to long-term operational integration, prioritizing measurable business outcomes and cost-effectiveness ahead of the Skift Data + AI Summit Europe.

### Key Points
- The Skift Data + AI Summit Europe will take place in London on October 6, 2026, focusing on the commercial realities of deploying AI.
- Industry leaders from companies including IAG, Accor, Amadeus, and American Express Global Business Travel will discuss scaling AI beyond initial pilot programs.
- Successful AI deployment requires addressing complex variables like data governance, cross-market language requirements, and handling customer service exceptions.
- Experts emphasize that businesses must evaluate AI based on specific job performance, employee time savings, and actual revenue impact rather than broad automation metrics.
- The summit aims to help operators decide which AI investments to maintain, expand, or terminate during the 2027 budget planning cycle.

### Why it Matters
Moving AI from a controlled pilot to a live service requires travel companies to solve for real-world operational friction and long-term maintenance costs. Determining which technologies provide genuine commercial value is essential for firms looking to optimize budgets and improve customer experiences in a competitive market.
