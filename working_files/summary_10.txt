U.S. warns of active Chinese cyber probes on power grids and water systems using AI-generated attack tools
============================================================

U.S. intelligence agencies have confirmed that state-sponsored adversaries are actively using artificial intelligence to develop custom code for scanning and exploiting sensitive industrial control systems across critical infrastructure.

### Key Points
- A joint August 19 advisory warns that state-sponsored actors are using AI to create scripts that mimic legitimate monitoring tools to probe industrial installations.
- Threat actors are leveraging open-source libraries like snap7.dll to gain unauthorized read/write access to Siemens S7 Series programmable logic controllers.
- The FBI has identified Integrity Technology Group as a key enabler of these activities, noting its ties to the Chinese government and use of large-scale botnets.
- Adversaries are targeting six critical infrastructure sectors, including power, water, and manufacturing, to potentially disrupt essential services and cause equipment damage.
- The NSA recommends that organizations immediately patch known vulnerabilities, disable external management for internet-exposed controllers, and implement strict network segmentation.

### Why it Matters
The integration of AI into cyber warfare significantly lowers the technical barrier for adversaries to disrupt the foundational systems that power modern society. This shift creates an unprecedented threat environment where critical infrastructure, such as power grids and water treatment facilities, faces a heightened risk of systemic failure.
