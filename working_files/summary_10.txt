I've spent over $3,000 on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, and only one was worth the money
============================================================

OpenAI’s ChatGPT Plus has emerged as the most justifiable AI subscription, outperforming competitors like Anthropic’s Claude and Google’s Gemini in coding capabilities, research depth, and overall usage value.

### Key Points
- ChatGPT Plus is currently favored for its superior coding performance via Codex and more generous usage limits compared to Claude.
- Anthropic’s Claude Max subscription is criticized for high costs, restrictive usage caps, and inconsistent research quality compared to OpenAI’s models.
- Google AI Pro provides value through bundled perks like 5TB of cloud storage and NotebookLM, despite Gemini’s performance trailing behind competitors.
- The author reports spending over $3,000 on AI subscriptions, concluding that ChatGPT Plus offers the best balance of utility and price.
- Competitive pressure has forced rapid innovation, with OpenAI currently outpacing Anthropic in feature rollouts and model reliability.

### Why it Matters
Choosing the right AI subscription is increasingly important as monthly costs for these tools accumulate for professional and personal users. Evaluating these services based on actual performance and usage limits helps consumers avoid paying for redundant or underperforming software in a rapidly evolving market.
