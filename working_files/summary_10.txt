Which Galaxy Z 8 phone should you buy? Here's what I think after using all three
============================================================

Samsung has officially unveiled its new Galaxy Z 8 series, featuring the Galaxy Z Flip 8, the compact Galaxy Z Fold 8, and the high-performance Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra.

### Key Points
- The Galaxy Z 8 series launches for sale on August 7, with all models running One UI 9 based on Android 17.
- Samsung is offering seven years of guaranteed OS upgrades across the entire Z Flip 8 and Z Fold 8 lineup.
- The Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra features an 8-inch display and a 200MP main camera, positioning it as a premium productivity device.
- The standard Galaxy Z Fold 8 offers a more portable, passport-sized form factor compared to the larger Ultra model.
- The Galaxy Z Flip 8 serves as the entry-level option at $1,200, focusing on a compact, fashionable design and specialized camera modes.
- All three devices are powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 processor and carry an IP48 water and dust resistance rating.

### Why it Matters
This release marks a strategic shift for Samsung by expanding its book-style foldable category to include two distinct size options for different consumer needs. By offering a wider range of price points and form factors, Samsung aims to solidify its dominance in the foldable market while extending software support to compete with long-term device longevity trends.
