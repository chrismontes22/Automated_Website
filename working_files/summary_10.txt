Here’s How Job Applicants Are Trying to Make Their Resumes Stand Out: ‘There’s No Way to Fight It’
============================================================

Job seekers and employers are increasingly integrating artificial intelligence terminology into resumes and job descriptions as the labor market adapts to rapid technological shifts and widespread employment uncertainty.

### Key Points
- LinkedIn reports a 70% increase in U.S. users adding AI-related terms to their professional profiles over the past year.
- Data from Indeed shows that 8% of U.S. job postings mentioned AI in early 2026, compared to only 3% in 2022.
- Stanford economists found that current LinkedIn profiles contain 30% more AI references than archived versions from before 2023.
- A free AI training course at the University of Maryland attracted 62,000 registrants, far exceeding the initial expectation of 500 sign-ups.
- AI-related job requirements are expanding beyond technical roles to include diverse positions like physical therapists and autonomous truck drivers.

### Why it Matters
This trend reflects a fundamental shift in how workers and companies navigate the uncertainty surrounding AI's long-term impact on job security and productivity. As professional standards evolve, the ability to demonstrate AI competency is becoming a critical factor for remaining competitive in a rapidly changing global labor market.
