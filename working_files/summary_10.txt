Elon Musk Eyes ‘Complete Phone Coverage in America’ as SpaceX Clears Hurdle
============================================================

SpaceX has agreed to acquire Grain Management’s 800 MHz low-band spectrum for $8 billion to enhance Starlink Mobile’s ability to provide direct satellite-to-cellular coverage across the United States.

### Key Points
- SpaceX is purchasing nationwide 800 MHz low-band spectrum from investment firm Grain Management for an estimated $8 billion.
- The acquisition aims to improve signal penetration through physical obstacles like building walls and dense foliage for satellite-to-phone connectivity.
- The Federal Communications Commission recently authorized SpaceX to launch 15,000 next-generation satellites capable of delivering high-speed data to unmodified mobile devices.
- The deal remains subject to regulatory approval by the Federal Communications Commission, with no specific timeline currently established for a final decision.
- Industry analysts and competitors, including T-Mobile, have questioned whether satellite-based services can effectively compete with the coverage provided by traditional terrestrial cellular towers.

### Why it Matters
This acquisition represents a strategic move by SpaceX to transform Starlink into a viable nationwide cellular carrier capable of challenging established providers like AT&T, T-Mobile, and Verizon. If successful, the integration of low-band spectrum and a massive satellite fleet could eliminate traditional dead zones, though experts remain skeptical about the service's ability to match the reliability of ground-based networks.
