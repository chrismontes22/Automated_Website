Bitcoin slides below $82,000 as AI doubts return, with DeFi tokens leading declines
============================================================

Bitcoin fell below $82,000 as mixed earnings reports from Tesla and Meta sparked renewed concerns over artificial intelligence spending, triggering a broader sell-off across global equity markets.

### Key Points
- Bitcoin dropped 2.7% to approximately $81,800, reversing gains from a late-Wednesday surge that had pushed the cryptocurrency toward $84,800.
- The CoinDesk DeFi index slumped 5.6%, marking the sector as the hardest hit during the broader market downturn.
- Nasdaq 100 futures fell 1.32% and major Asian indices, including the Hang Seng and Kospi, recorded significant losses following disappointing tech sector outlooks.
- Crypto derivatives saw $431.1 million in liquidations, a 52% increase, as traders moved to close existing positions rather than opening new ones.
- Monero emerged as a notable outlier, gaining 4.9% while most other tokens, including Ether and Solana, experienced downward price pressure.

### Why it Matters
The decline highlights how closely crypto assets are currently tethered to broader macroeconomic sentiment and the performance of major technology stocks. As AI-driven market optimism cools, investors are shifting focus toward risk management, evidenced by the widespread closing of leveraged positions across digital asset markets.
