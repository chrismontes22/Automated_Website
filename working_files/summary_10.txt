Comcast Xfinity Wants to Use Your Wi-Fi as a Motion Sensor in Your Home
============================================================

Comcast has launched Xfinity Shield, a new home security platform that utilizes existing Wi-Fi signals to detect movement and provide cybersecurity protections for eligible Xfinity internet customers.

### Key Points
- The new WiFi Motion feature detects human movement by monitoring signal interference patterns without requiring cameras or dedicated motion sensors.
- Xfinity Shield integrates existing cybersecurity tools, including firewalls and parental controls, into a centralized management platform within the Xfinity app.
- The system requires an Xfinity Gateway and at least one additional connected device, such as a smart speaker or thermostat, to function.
- Users can choose between the standard free service or a $15 monthly Shield Select plan that includes a wireless security camera and cloud storage.
- Security modes like Home Watch, Away Watch, and Dark Watch allow customers to customize alert settings based on their daily routines.

### Why it Matters
This rollout marks a significant shift in how internet service providers leverage existing network infrastructure to offer value-added home security services. By utilizing Wi-Fi sensing technology, Comcast provides a privacy-focused alternative to traditional camera-based monitoring systems for its broad subscriber base.
