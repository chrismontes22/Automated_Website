Delete Someone From a Group Photo Without Ever Opening Photoshop
============================================================

Adobe Lightroom users can now remove unwanted individuals from group photos by leveraging Adobe Firefly’s generative AI models to close gaps without requiring complex Photoshop masking skills.

### Key Points
- Adobe Lightroom now integrates with Adobe Firefly, allowing users to export images for generative editing directly from the software's photo menu.
- Users can select from multiple AI models, including Adobe Firefly Image 5, ChatGPT, and Google Gemini 3.1, to perform complex object removal and repositioning.
- Specific text prompts are required to maintain consistency in facial features, hair, clothing, and background details during the generative process.
- Generative edits consume varying amounts of credit, with high-quality outputs from third-party models like ChatGPT costing up to 80 credits per edit.
- Regenerated images often require manual upscaling after download, as the output resolution may be lower than the original camera file.

### Why it Matters
This workflow demonstrates a significant shift in Adobe’s strategy to democratize advanced generative editing tools by moving them outside the professional-grade Photoshop environment. It provides casual photographers with a practical, accessible method to refine personal images while highlighting the growing trend of model-agnostic AI integration in creative software.
