16 Malicious Firefox Extensions Pose as Rabby and OKX Wallets to Steal Recovery Phrases
============================================================

Cybersecurity researchers identified 16 malicious Mozilla Firefox extensions designed to steal cryptocurrency wallet recovery phrases and private keys by masquerading as legitimate wallet portals and desktop utilities.

### Key Points
- Researchers discovered 16 malicious Firefox extensions that intercept and exfiltrate sensitive cryptocurrency credentials to attacker-controlled Cloudflare Workers.
- The extensions impersonate popular services, including clones of Rabby Wallet and OKX Wallet, to deceive users during wallet import processes.
- All identified malicious add-ons were removed from the Mozilla Firefox ecosystem as of October 5, 2026.
- Affected users are advised to immediately create new wallets on clean systems and transfer their assets to prevent further unauthorized access.
- This campaign is part of a broader trend of malicious browser extensions targeting Chrome, Edge, and Firefox to harvest data or redirect traffic.

### Why it Matters
These findings highlight a persistent security risk where threat actors continuously rotate malicious infrastructure to bypass browser protections and compromise digital assets. Users and organizations must regularly audit installed extensions and adopt behavior-based monitoring to mitigate the risk of credential theft and unauthorized data exfiltration.
