Hackers Breach Enterprise Firewalls and VPNs, Exposing Over 70,000 Credentials
============================================================

Cybersecurity researchers have identified a widespread campaign by the threat actor k0t targeting exposed network edge devices, including Fortinet and Palo Alto Networks firewalls, to facilitate data exfiltration.

### Key Points
- Threat actor k0t, also known as CryptoFeel, has compromised global organizations by exploiting vulnerabilities like CVE-2025-64446 and CVE-2026-0257.
- The campaign utilizes reconnaissance tools like FOFA and ZoomEye to identify vulnerable VPN and firewall appliances for initial access.
- Attackers establish persistence using legitimate remote management software such as MeshCentral, Atera, and the AdaptixC2 framework.
- Stolen data is exfiltrated to Wasabi S3 buckets and MEGA cloud drives, with over 70,000 credentials identified in connection with the activity.
- Victims are geographically diverse, with the highest concentrations located in the Asia Pacific region at 38% and Latin America at 31.9%.
- The actor maintains suspected ties to ransomware groups Qilin and The Gentlemen, likely acting as an initial access broker.

### Why it Matters
This campaign highlights the significant risk posed by internet-facing network appliances that remain unpatched or improperly secured against known vulnerabilities. By acting as an initial access broker for ransomware gangs, these attackers provide a critical entry point that can lead to widespread corporate data breaches and operational disruption.
