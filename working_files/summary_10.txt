Twitch confirms Amazon is training its AI models on livestreams
============================================================

Twitch has confirmed that Amazon is utilizing user livestreams to train its artificial intelligence models, prompting significant backlash from creators who are now required to manually opt out.

### Key Points
- Twitch users are automatically opted into AI training, requiring them to manually disable the setting within their privacy dashboard.
- The announcement triggered widespread criticism from high-profile streamers and community members on social media platforms like X.
- Twitch executives Mary Kish and Mike Minton confirmed the training practices during a livestream but stated they lack oversight of Amazon’s specific data usage.
- Twitch currently uses AI for internal tools like captions and content classification, though it does not currently employ generative AI features.
- Amazon, which acquired Twitch in 2014, is aggressively scaling its AI infrastructure to compete with industry leaders like OpenAI and Anthropic.

### Why it Matters
This development highlights growing tensions between content creators and major technology companies regarding the use of user-generated data for training large-scale AI models. As Amazon accelerates its efforts to compete in the frontier model market, the controversy underscores the increasing demand for transparency and user control over digital intellectual property.
