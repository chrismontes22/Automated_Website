Qualcomm and SpaceX ink a deal to begin working on a Starlink 5G network for 2027
============================================================

Qualcomm has partnered with SpaceX to develop a high-capacity 5G mobile network using Dragonwing infrastructure, with the companies targeting a commercial launch in the United States by 2027.

### Key Points
- Qualcomm will provide its Dragonwing QRU100 and QDU100 platforms along with Layer 1 software to support SpaceX’s Starlink mobile network.
- The infrastructure will utilize Massive MIMO radio units to enhance antenna capabilities, network efficiency, and overall mobile coverage.
- Qualcomm described this collaboration as its largest 5G deal to date, aiming to establish a foundation for future AI-native 6G technology.
- The announcement follows reports that T-Mobile recently declined a partnership proposal from SpaceX regarding the development of a satellite-based mobile network.

### Why it Matters
This partnership marks a significant shift in the U.S. telecommunications market as SpaceX seeks to bypass traditional carrier rejections to build its own independent cellular infrastructure. By leveraging Qualcomm’s specialized hardware, the collaboration could accelerate the integration of satellite and terrestrial networks, potentially setting a new standard for global mobile connectivity.
