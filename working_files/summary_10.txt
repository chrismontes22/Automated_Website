I put this foldable Android phone through the ultimate durability test, and it refuses to die, even after being slathered in mud and tossed in the dishwasher
============================================================

The Honor Magic V6 foldable smartphone successfully survived a rigorous durability test involving thick mud submersion and a full cycle in a high-temperature household dishwasher.

### Key Points
- The Honor Magic V6 features IP68 and IP69K ratings, certifying resistance to dust, high-pressure water jets, and high-temperature water.
- During testing, the device was fully coated in wet mud, including its internal hinge and charging ports, before being cleaned.
- The phone underwent a full dishwasher cycle at temperatures between 120 and 140 degrees Fahrenheit without suffering internal hardware failure.
- Despite minor cosmetic scratches on the screen protector from the mud, all three rear cameras and the folding mechanism remained fully functional.
- The device displayed a temporary overheating warning during the wash cycle but continued to operate normally afterward.

### Why it Matters
This extreme stress test highlights the significant advancements in durability for foldable smartphone technology, which has historically been considered fragile. By meeting IP69K standards, these devices are becoming increasingly viable for users who require rugged hardware capable of withstanding harsh environmental conditions.
