Iran could face economic collapse in months, US Treasury’s Bessent warns
============================================================

US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent reports that Iran is feeling the impact of the ongoing Operation Economic Outcast sanctions as the United States increases pressure on the regime.

### Key Points
- The US Treasury has imposed new sanctions on five Iranian economic sectors, including aviation, technology, shipping, gold, and digital assets.
- Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent stated that the US plans to unveil additional sanctions on a weekly basis to force Iran into peace negotiations.
- Recent penalties targeted the United Arab Emirates branches of Egypt’s Banque Misr due to alleged financial ties with the Iranian government.
- The European Union has officially expressed support for the US-led economic pressure campaign against Iran.
- Market volatility has increased, with major Wall Street indices trending downward and gold prices falling to a two-week low.

### Why it Matters
These escalating sanctions represent a significant shift in US foreign policy aimed at destabilizing the Iranian economy to force diplomatic concessions. The strategy carries substantial risks for global financial markets, as evidenced by recent declines in major stock indices and rising energy-driven inflation.
