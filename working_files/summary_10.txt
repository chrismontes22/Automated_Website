German airport drone-bomb: Could Russia be involved?
============================================================

German authorities are investigating a drone carrying explosives discovered at Leipzig airport, raising significant concerns about potential foreign-led hybrid warfare tactics targeting European logistics and military supply chains.

### Key Points
- A quadcopter equipped with explosives was discovered at Leipzig airport, forcing a runway lockdown near Ukrainian cargo planes.
- Investigators are examining a potential connection to a separate incident where a DHL cargo plane collided with an unidentified object.
- The incident follows a 2024 plot involving Russian-linked proxies who transported RDX explosives and drone components across Europe.
- Nearly two dozen suspects are currently on trial in Poland and Lithuania for their roles in a network allegedly directed by Russian intelligence.
- Experts suggest the drone activity may be a calculated effort to intimidate Germany, a major provider of military aid to Ukraine.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights a growing trend of sabotage and hybrid threats aimed at destabilizing European nations that support Ukraine. Such attacks on critical infrastructure create significant security risks for international logistics hubs and signal a potential escalation in covert operations across the continent.
