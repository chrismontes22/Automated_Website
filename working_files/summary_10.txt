Xiaomi makes a huge splash at IFA, shows off upcoming Galaxy Z Fold 8 competitor and futuristic AI capabilities
============================================================

Xiaomi is making its major debut at IFA 2026, showcasing its expansive Human x Car x Home ecosystem and the upcoming Xiaomi 18 Fold foldable smartphone to global audiences.

### Key Points
- Xiaomi occupies a 3,300-square-meter exhibition space at IFA, marking its largest overseas presence to date.
- The company is highlighting its Human x Car x Home portfolio, which integrates over 300 devices through the HyperOS platform.
- The upcoming Xiaomi 18 Fold features a triple-camera system and is powered by the proprietary XRING 03 chipset.
- The official launch for the Xiaomi 18 Fold is scheduled for September 7.
- The ecosystem demonstrates AI-driven connectivity between smart home appliances, vehicles like the SU7 Max, and mobile devices.

### Why it Matters
This expansion signals Xiaomi's intent to challenge established competitors like Samsung by offering a deeply integrated, AI-powered hardware ecosystem. The launch of the Xiaomi 18 Fold represents a significant move to capture market share in the premium foldable smartphone segment.
