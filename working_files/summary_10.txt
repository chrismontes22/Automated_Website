"Steam's review queue is clogged up by AI slop": Salacious Fire Emblem-ish Food Devils gets a delay because its devs think Valve is inundated with genAI games
============================================================

Developer Studio Daimon has delayed the release of its tactical game Food Devils due to significant approval backlogs within the Steam platform's mandatory review process.

### Key Points
- Studio Daimon submitted its game build on October 12, exceeding the standard 3-to-7-day review window by over two weeks.
- The developer attributes the delay to a surge in low-quality AI-generated games flooding the Steam review queue.
- Data shows that over 50% of games released on Steam in October 2026 included AI disclosures, up from 25% in January 2026.
- Monthly game releases on Steam nearly doubled from approximately 2,000 in October 2025 to nearly 4,000 in October 2026.
- Valve has not provided a revised release date for Food Devils, leaving the developer unable to confirm a new launch window.

### Why it Matters
The massive influx of AI-generated content is creating operational bottlenecks that threaten the release schedules of independent developers. This trend highlights growing concerns regarding platform moderation and the sustainability of Steam's current review infrastructure as submission volumes continue to climb.
