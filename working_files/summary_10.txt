Anthropic IPO valuation hinges on $190B-$200B 2028 revenue forecast: sources
============================================================

Anthropic is preparing for a potential initial public offering by projecting 2028 revenue between $190 billion and $200 billion to justify its valuation to prospective Wall Street investors.

### Key Points
- Anthropic’s projected 2028 revenue of $190 billion to $200 billion significantly exceeds its current $47 billion annual revenue run rate.
- Investors are utilizing enterprise value-to-revenue multiples based on long-term forecasts to value the company despite its heavy infrastructure and model training expenditures.
- The company is using public firms like Palantir, Cloudflare, and SpaceX as valuation benchmarks to establish its market position.
- Anthropic reported a revenue run rate of $9 billion in late 2025, which surged to over $47 billion by May 2026.
- The firm anticipates its first quarterly operating profit of $559 million in the second quarter of 2026.

### Why it Matters
This valuation strategy highlights the extreme difficulty of pricing high-growth AI companies that currently prioritize massive capital expenditure over immediate profitability. By looking years into the future, investors are betting that Anthropic’s operational efficiency will scale rapidly enough to justify its massive infrastructure investments.
