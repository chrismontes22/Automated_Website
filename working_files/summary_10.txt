This free app isn't a VPN, but it encrypts everything my phone sends
============================================================

**Cloudflare’s 1.1.1.1 app provides a simplified, account-free VPN service that secures mobile internet traffic through an encrypted tunnel without requiring complex configuration or offering location-spoofing features.**

### Key Points
- The 1.1.1.1 app, available on Google Play and the Apple Store, features a single-toggle interface to activate system-wide traffic encryption.
- It offers two modes: a DNS-only encryption setting and a full WARP mode that routes all app traffic through Cloudflare’s global edge network.
- The service utilizes the MASQUE protocol, carrying encapsulated traffic over HTTP/3 and QUIC on UDP port 443.
- Unlike traditional VPNs, the app does not require user accounts, subscriptions, or manual server selection to function.
- While the basic service is free, Cloudflare offers an optional upgrade for faster connection speeds at $4.99 per month.

### Why it Matters
This tool provides a streamlined privacy solution for users who want to secure their data on untrusted public Wi-Fi without the complexity of traditional VPNs. By shifting trust to Cloudflare’s infrastructure, it prevents local network operators and ISPs from monitoring specific browsing habits or DNS queries.
