S&P 500 Hits Record High as Tech Gains Mask Broad Market Weakness
============================================================

The S&P 500 reached a record high of 7,819.04 as a narrow rally in megacap technology stocks offset broader market declines and rising Treasury yields.

### Key Points
- The S&P 500 rose 0.6% to a record 7,819.04, while the Nasdaq composite also hit a new high.
- Market gains were driven by the "Magnificent Seven" tech companies, which reached a combined market capitalization of approximately $25 trillion.
- Meta Platforms has gained 24% since August 13, and Nvidia shares climbed 4.5% over the past week to reach an all-time high.
- Fewer than half of the stocks in the S&P 500 are currently trading above their 200-day moving average, signaling narrowing market breadth.
- The 10-year Treasury yield settled at 5.270% following a period of volatility that saw yields reach multi-decade highs.

### Why it Matters
The heavy reliance on a small group of technology stocks creates potential instability if AI-driven growth expectations fail to materialize. Investors remain divided on whether this concentration reflects sustainable sector leadership or a risky departure from broader market health.
