Yulu raises $93 mn in Series C funding round, plans to scale EV fleet to 2 lakh
============================================================

Electric mobility platform Yulu has secured $93 million in Series C funding to quadruple its electric vehicle fleet to 200,000 units and prepare for a future public listing.

### Key Points
- The funding round includes $63 million in equity led by GEF Capital Partners and $30 million in debt.
- Yulu plans to expand its service hubs and launch Yulu Express, a high-payload electric scooter designed for e-commerce and parcel delivery.
- The company maintains strategic partnerships with Bajaj Auto and Magna International to support its vertically integrated ecosystem.
- Yulu currently operates across 12 primary Indian metros, including Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, and Hyderabad.
- The firm reported a sevenfold revenue increase since fiscal year 2023 and has sustained positive EBITDA since April 2025.

### Why it Matters
This capital injection positions Yulu to dominate India's growing urban logistics sector by scaling its infrastructure and diversifying into high-capacity delivery vehicles. Achieving consistent profitability and operational scale provides a clear pathway for the company to transition into the public markets.
