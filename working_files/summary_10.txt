PM Modi wants India to ‘create an aura’ that can draw space talent; urges startups to build global businesses
============================================================

Prime Minister Narendra Modi urged Indian space startups to cultivate a global ecosystem that attracts international talent and positions the nation as a premier hub for aerospace innovation.

### Key Points
- Prime Minister Modi met with CEOs and founders from 20 space startups during the third National Space Day celebration at Seva Teerth.
- Participating companies included Skyroot Aerospace, Agnikul Cosmos, Pixxel, Dhruva Space, and several others specializing in satellite technology and launch vehicles.
- Startup leaders reported a growing trend of skilled professionals returning to India from the United States and Europe to join the domestic space sector.
- India’s competitive advantages in the global market include lower operational costs, a deep pool of technical talent, and a high capacity for risk-taking.
- The government aims to expand the private space industry beyond satellite manufacturing into specialized fields like robotics, space-based pharmaceuticals, and satellite servicing.

### Why it Matters
This initiative signals a strategic shift to transform India from a cost-effective service provider into a global center for aerospace research and commercial development. By attracting international talent and investment, the government hopes to accelerate the growth of its private space economy and secure a dominant position in the global market.
