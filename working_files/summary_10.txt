★ Ads in Software Are Like Stickers on Laptops
============================================================

Apple faces criticism for increasing advertisements within its digital services, drawing unfavorable comparisons to the company’s long-standing refusal to place third-party marketing stickers on its physical hardware products.

### Key Points
- Apple has expanded advertising placements across its App Store and Apple News platforms, with potential plans to include Apple Maps.
- Critics argue these digital ads contradict the company's historical commitment to a clean, minimalist user experience on its physical devices.
- During a 2007 event, Steve Jobs famously rejected "Intel Inside" stickers on Macs, stating that customers prefer products free of intrusive branding.
- Modern Apple hardware, such as the iPhone, remains devoid of model numbers or regulatory text to maintain a clean aesthetic.
- The current App Store interface now prioritizes paid advertisements in prominent search result positions.

### Why it Matters
This shift in strategy highlights a growing tension between Apple’s historical focus on premium user experience and its increasing reliance on high-margin advertising revenue. The move risks alienating long-term customers who associate the brand with a clean, ad-free ecosystem.
