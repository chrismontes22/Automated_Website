Exclusive: 'We want to keep people away from doctors': Why Samsung has gone all-in on AI health, according to its executives — but do users trust it?
============================================================

Samsung is integrating advanced artificial intelligence into its Galaxy Health ecosystem to provide personalized wellness insights, while simultaneously addressing growing consumer concerns regarding data privacy and user consent.

### Key Points
- Samsung showcased new AI-driven health features for the Galaxy Watch and Galaxy Ring during the 22 July Galaxy Unpacked event.
- New tools include the Vitals feature for monitoring heart health and respiratory metrics, alongside an upcoming beta for AI-powered nutrition tracking.
- Samsung executives emphasized that users maintain control over their data through opt-in settings, clarifying that health features remain functional without sharing information for AI training.
- The company aims to shift toward preventative care by providing users with actionable health nudges to reduce the necessity for frequent doctor visits.
- Regulatory bodies like the UK's MHRA have endorsed specific Samsung health tracking capabilities, such as sleep-based ECG monitoring.

### Why it Matters
The integration of AI into wearable technology represents a significant shift toward proactive, data-driven personal healthcare. However, the long-term success of these platforms depends on the company's ability to maintain user trust and ensure that sensitive biometric data remains secure and private.
