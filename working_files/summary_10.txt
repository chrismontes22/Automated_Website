The AI sell-off is exposing the risks of betting on Wall Street’s favorite stocks
============================================================

S&P 500 stocks with the highest concentration of analyst Buy ratings have significantly underperformed less-favored equities throughout 2026, marking the third-worst performance stretch since 1995.

### Key Points
- Piper Sandler strategist Michael Kantrowitz reports that Wall Street’s most favored stocks are currently trailing the least-favored group.
- The AI-focused trade has lost approximately $5 trillion in market value since October 6, with Nvidia and Alphabet accounting for significant portions of that decline.
- Large-cap stocks like Eli Lilly, Walmart, and Exxon Mobil have gained $1.3 trillion during the same period, providing a buffer against the tech-heavy slump.
- Six of the nine stocks on Piper Sandler’s "most favored" list have declined in value throughout 2026.
- Historical data shows similar underperformance patterns occurred only in 2001-02 and 2010, both of which preceded major market turning points.

### Why it Matters
The underperformance of analyst-favored stocks highlights the risks of crowded trades where positive expectations are already fully priced into valuations. Because these companies hold significant weight in major indices, their volatility directly impacts the performance of most retail 401(k) plans and passive index funds.
