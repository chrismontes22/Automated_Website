Transcript: Seth Bernstein, Chief Executive Officer of AllianceBernstein
============================================================

AllianceBernstein CEO Seth Bernstein discusses the firm's strategic evolution, the benefits of its relocation to Nashville, and the growing role of private credit in modern investment portfolios.

### Key Points
- AllianceBernstein manages over $905 billion in client assets and has been led by Seth Bernstein since 2017.
- The firm relocated its headquarters from New York to Nashville in 2017, resulting in approximately $85 million in annual recurring savings.
- Bernstein oversaw the 2022 acquisition of CarVal Investors, expanding the firm's private market credit and debt platform to nearly $100 billion.
- The company has launched 31 active ETF strategies, accumulating $21 billion in assets, as part of a shift toward more efficient investment vehicles.
- AllianceBernstein is currently collaborating with Carlyle and Brookfield to integrate private market assets into 401(k) target-date funds.

### Why it Matters
The firm’s transition from a traditional active manager to a diversified global player highlights the broader industry shift toward private credit and tax-efficient investment wrappers. By prioritizing fiduciary responsibility and operational efficiency, AllianceBernstein serves as a case study for how legacy financial institutions must adapt to fee compression and changing investor demands.
