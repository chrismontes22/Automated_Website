watchOS 27: 12 New Things To Check Out on Your Apple Watch
============================================================

Apple released watchOS 27 on September 14, introducing a dedicated Siri app, a redesigned dynamic app grid, enhanced fitness tracking capabilities, and significant changes to navigation gestures.

### Key Points
- The new Siri app enables cross-device conversation syncing via iCloud and features a contextual Siri Modular watch face.
- A new "Tap" gesture allows users to open Smart Stack widgets, while the Walkie-Talkie app has been permanently removed.
- The Find My app now consolidates device, item, and people tracking into a single, map-centric interface.
- Workout Buddy no longer requires an iPhone connection, and indoor treadmill distance tracking now utilizes improved machine learning algorithms.
- Cycle Tracking now includes notifications for patterns suggestive of perimenopause for users aged 40 and older.
- Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4 models receive exclusive features, including a real-time steps complication and the upcoming Live Rewind transcription tool.

### Why it Matters
These updates represent a significant shift in how users interact with their Apple Watch by prioritizing AI-driven contextual information and streamlining navigation. The removal of legacy features alongside new health and fitness capabilities signals Apple's ongoing effort to position the device as a standalone health-monitoring tool.
