Best Buy's Labor Day Sale has brought one of our favorite high-class gaming laptops' Memory-Crisis-induced price hike down by 24%
============================================================

Best Buy has discounted the ASUS ROG Strix G16 gaming laptop to $1,749.99, offering a significant price reduction during its Labor Day sale event ending September 7, 2026.

### Key Points
- The ASUS ROG Strix G16 is currently discounted by $550 from its original $2,299.99 list price.
- The laptop features an AMD Ryzen 9 8940HX processor and an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5070 TI graphics card.
- Hardware specifications include 16GB of RAM, a 1TB SSD, and a 1600p display with a 165 Hz refresh rate.
- Users can expand memory up to 64GB via two DDR5 slots and increase storage up to 4TB using two M.2 2280 slots.
- The promotion is available exclusively through Best Buy until the conclusion of the Labor Day sale on September 7, 2026.

### Why it Matters
Rising memory costs have driven up the price of high-performance gaming hardware, making limited-time retail discounts essential for budget-conscious consumers. This deal provides a more accessible entry point for users seeking premium mobile gaming performance despite current market volatility.
