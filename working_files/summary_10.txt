What Nearly a Decade of Betting on AI Taught Me About Growth
============================================================

Starkey, a leading American hearing technology manufacturer, attributes its recent record-breaking sales and industry growth to a strategic commitment to reinvesting 13% of annual revenue into continuous innovation.

### Key Points
- Starkey reinvested 13% of its total revenue into research and development during the previous fiscal year.
- The company began integrating artificial intelligence into its hearing aid technology in 2017 to differentiate itself from global competitors.
- A "fail-fast" corporate culture encourages risk-taking, which the company claims improves organizational agility and helps attract top-tier talent.
- Strategic investments in automation and quality control contributed to record-breaking sales performance in October 2025.
- The company focuses on expanding health-related features within its devices to maintain long-term market leadership and customer loyalty.

### Why it Matters
Prioritizing consistent investment in innovation allows companies to maintain a competitive edge and adapt to rapidly changing market demands. By fostering a culture that embraces risk, businesses can secure long-term growth and build the organizational momentum necessary to sustain success.
