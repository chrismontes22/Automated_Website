Zerostack’s Crypto Holdings Top $1 Billion
============================================================

Pharmaceutical distributor Zerostack has pivoted toward asset management, disclosing that its cryptocurrency treasury now holds 925.9 million MemeCore tokens valued at approximately $18.19 per share for investors.

### Key Points
- Zerostack, a German-based pharmaceutical distributor, is shifting its business model to include asset management and cryptocurrency acquisition.
- The company currently holds 925.9 million MemeCore tokens, a Layer 1 blockchain project designed to support long-term economic utility for memecoins.
- Zerostack’s crypto treasury valuation is estimated at $18.19 per share, significantly higher than the company's current trading price of $5.33.
- Shares of Zerostack (NASDAQ: ZSTK) have experienced a 74% decline over the past 12 months.
- The company is also exploring integration with 0G, a modular blockchain infrastructure built to scale decentralized artificial intelligence applications.

### Why it Matters
This strategic pivot highlights a growing trend of traditional companies attempting to bolster shareholder value by integrating volatile digital assets into their corporate balance sheets. Investors must now weigh the company's legacy pharmaceutical operations against the speculative risks and potential rewards associated with its significant cryptocurrency holdings.
