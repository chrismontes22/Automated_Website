Ray-Ban Meta glasses update makes them more useful on the go
============================================================

Meta has released the v28 firmware update for Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses, introducing hands-free pedestrian navigation, immersive Spatial Audio, and expanded language support for Mandarin Chinese users.

### Key Points
- Pedestrian navigation allows users to request walking directions via voice commands or by sharing locations from mobile mapping apps.
- Spatial Audio is now available for music and podcasts on second-generation Ray-Ban Meta glasses to provide a more immersive listening experience.
- The update adds full support for Mandarin Chinese across device controls, messaging, media capture, and Meta AI features.
- Navigation services are currently available in the United States, Puerto Rico, Canada, and select international cities.
- Firmware v28 includes security enhancements designed to improve camera anti-tampering protections on the wearable devices.

### Why it Matters
These updates signal Meta's commitment to evolving its smart eyewear into a more practical, daily-use navigation and audio tool. By integrating hands-free mapping and broader language support, the company is positioning its hardware as a viable alternative to traditional smartphone-based navigation.
