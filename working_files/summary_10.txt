I thought all Blu-ray drives were the same until I found the perfect one for ripping physical media to Plex
============================================================

Building a LibreDrive using specific optical hardware and MakeMKV software allows users to bypass manufacturer restrictions and successfully rip UHD 4K Blu-ray discs for personal media servers.

### Key Points
- LibreDrive is a specialized mode where optical drive firmware is flashed to bypass manufacturer restrictions, enabling the reading of UHD 4K Blu-ray data.
- Standard Blu-ray drives often lack the laser optics required for BD-66 and BD-100 discs and cannot bypass AACS 2.0 or 2.1 encryption.
- Users can either purchase pre-flashed "unlocked" drives or manually flash compatible hardware using utilities like SDFtool and specific firmware packs.
- Successful flashing requires drives with compatible chipsets, such as the MT1959 architecture, to avoid bricking the device during the process.
- Software like MakeMKV uses a separate decryption engine and key database to process disc data once the drive's firmware restrictions are removed.

### Why it Matters
Creating a LibreDrive is essential for users who want to back up physical media for use in self-hosted platforms like Plex, Jellyfin, or Emby. While the process requires technical research and adherence to regional copyright laws, it provides the only reliable method for digitizing high-quality 4K content.
