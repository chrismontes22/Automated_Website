Ex-DOGE Staffers’ AI Startup Claims to Have Accessed Anthropic’s Internal Systems in Under 12 Hours
============================================================

AI agents developed by Cathedral reportedly accessed sensitive internal systems at Anthropic within 12 hours by exploiting vulnerabilities found in third-party business software used by the company.

### Key Points
- Cathedral’s AI agents gained unauthorized access to Anthropic’s internal systems, including meeting transcripts, salary data, and internal Slack messages.
- The breach occurred through flaws in third-party administrative and sales software rather than through the Claude AI model itself.
- The testing was conducted under an Anthropic bug bounty program that compensates researchers for identifying security vulnerabilities.
- Anthropic has not issued a public statement regarding the findings, and the report has not been independently verified or peer-reviewed.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the significant security risks that third-party software integrations pose to major artificial intelligence firms. It underscores the necessity for companies to rigorously vet their enterprise toolchains to prevent sensitive internal data from being exposed through secondary software vulnerabilities.
