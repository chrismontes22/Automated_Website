AI ETFs Have Now Become a $65 Billion Market
============================================================

Assets in thematic artificial intelligence exchange-traded funds have surged to $64.9 billion, marking a significant increase from $10.3 billion last year as investors seek broad exposure to the sector.

### Key Points
- Total assets under management for AI-focused thematic funds reached $64.9 billion by the end of June, according to J.P. Morgan Asset Management.
- Artificial intelligence currently ranks as the top investment theme by assets under management, outpacing infrastructure, defense, cybersecurity, and natural resources.
- The broader thematic ETF market grew by nearly 33% during the first half of the year, reaching a total valuation of approximately $430 billion.
- The iShares AI Innovation and Tech Active ETF (BAI) has recorded a year-to-date gain of roughly 30%, while the Global X Artificial Intelligence & Technology ETF (AIQ) is up 15%.

### Why it Matters
These funds provide institutional and retail investors with a liquid, diversified method to capitalize on the AI boom without the concentration risks associated with individual stock volatility. While these vehicles offer immediate exposure to the sector, analysts caution that they function primarily as sentiment-driven tools rather than long-term core holdings for retirement portfolios.
