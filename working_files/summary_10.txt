Can the Shark Tank Water Bottle Really Keep Drinks Cold for 36 Hours? I Tested One to Find Out
============================================================

A comparative performance test between the Coldest water bottle and an Owala model reveals that both containers maintain cool temperatures over a 36-hour period with similar results.

### Key Points
- The Coldest water bottle, featured on "Shark Tank," claims to keep beverages cold for 36 hours.
- A side-by-side test measured water temperatures at intervals ranging from 3 to 36 hours.
- After 36 hours, both the Coldest and Owala bottles reached an identical temperature of 63°F.
- Owala maintained slightly lower temperatures during the initial 24 hours of the testing period.
- Coldest differentiates itself in the market through limited-edition design drops and a variety of aesthetic styles.

### Why it Matters
This comparison highlights that premium water bottle performance is often comparable across popular brands despite aggressive marketing claims. Consumers prioritizing substance over style may find that standard insulated vessels provide sufficient cooling without the need for specific brand loyalty.
