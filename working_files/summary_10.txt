Windows Wrap: Microsoft's best Windows 11 and Surface news got lost in AI talk
============================================================

Microsoft unveiled the new Surface Laptop Ultra and updated Windows 11 search features during a recent event that focused heavily on AI integration and developer-centric technical tools.

### Key Points
- Microsoft introduced the Surface Laptop Ultra, marketed as the company's most powerful Surface device to date.
- The event showcased a redesigned, more efficient Search experience for the Windows 11 operating system.
- Presentations included technical deep dives into Llama.cpp, Microsoft Execution Containers, and RTX Spark technology.
- Microsoft launched a marketing campaign targeting MacBook users to encourage switching to the new Surface hardware.

### Why it Matters
The event highlights Microsoft's ongoing struggle to balance technical developer updates with consumer-facing product announcements. This fragmented presentation style may impact how effectively the company communicates its hardware value proposition to the broader PC market.
