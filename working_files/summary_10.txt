Airbnb’s CEO Was Drowning in Meetings. Then He Built an AI ‘Brain’ That Cut Them in Half.
============================================================

Airbnb CEO Brian Chesky has successfully reduced his meeting schedule by utilizing a custom AI intelligence corpus trained on thousands of internal company documents and presentations.

### Key Points
- Brian Chesky developed an AI "brain" using 18,000 Keynote presentations and 50,000 Google Docs to monitor company operations.
- The implementation of these AI tools allowed the CEO to cut his total meeting time in half.
- Airbnb has deployed AI to handle nearly 50% of customer service tickets across 50 different languages.
- Operational efficiency gains from AI helped Airbnb reduce support costs per booking by 16% year over year.
- The company plans to launch a sophisticated AI agent in 2027 that offers a richer interface than standard chatbots.

### Why it Matters
This shift demonstrates how corporate leaders are using generative AI to bypass traditional management bottlenecks and gain real-time operational oversight. By automating internal information gathering and customer support, companies can significantly reduce overhead costs while accelerating the pace of product development.
