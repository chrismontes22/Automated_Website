Your Apple Watch Can Automatically Identify Songs Around You
============================================================

Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4 models now feature automatic music recognition, allowing users to identify songs playing in their environment directly through the device's Smart Stack.

### Key Points
- The new Music Recognition feature uses Shazam technology to identify songs without requiring manual input from the user.
- Automatic detection is exclusive to Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4 devices paired with iPhones running iOS 27.
- Users can access identified song titles and artists by swiping up to view the Smart Stack widget.
- Identified tracks can be saved directly to a user's Shazam history or Apple Music library with a single tap.
- Apple processes audio through a protected buffer in the Secure Exclave, ensuring no actual audio recordings are stored or transmitted.

### Why it Matters
This update integrates advanced audio intelligence into the wearable ecosystem, streamlining how users discover and catalog music in real-time. By automating the identification process, Apple enhances the utility of the Smart Stack while maintaining strict privacy standards regarding user data.
