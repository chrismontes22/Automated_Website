A New Free Seminar Teaches Documentary Filmmakers When and How to Opt Out of AI
============================================================

Field of Vision and the Freedom of the Press Foundation are launching a free digital security seminar series to help filmmakers navigate AI risks and protect intellectual property.

### Key Points
- The sixth annual Digital Security Clinic offers five online sessions for journalists and documentary filmmakers starting August 13.
- A dedicated session on September 10 will teach professionals how to identify and disable hidden AI features within video editing software.
- Trainers will provide guidance on protecting raw footage from AI crawling and securing intellectual property in transcription services.
- Additional curriculum covers secure communication, encryption methods for footage storage, and strategies for minimizing personal digital footprints.
- The program has supported over 500 filmmaking teams since its inception, with current registration numbers exceeding previous years.

### Why it Matters
These clinics address the growing concern that AI integration in creative software could compromise the authenticity and ownership of journalistic media. By teaching professionals to manage their digital security, the program helps maintain public trust in documentary footage and protects sensitive intellectual property from unauthorized data usage.
