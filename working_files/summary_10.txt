Did the Surface Phone FINALLY leak? The Windows Central Podcast breaks down what we know about the mysterious Windows Phone prototype, plus a recap of the latest news from Windows and Xbox
============================================================

The latest Windows Central Podcast episode explores leaked Microsoft hardware prototypes, the evolution of Copilot AI features, and the upcoming integration of Xbox backward compatibility for Windows PCs.

### Key Points
- Hosts Zac Bowden and Jez Corden analyze newly surfaced images of a 2013-era Microsoft "Surface Phone" prototype from the Nokia acquisition period.
- Microsoft is consolidating its Copilot experience while simultaneously scaling back specific features like Podcast mode and Deep Research.
- Official plans were confirmed to bring original Xbox and Xbox 360 emulation to Windows PCs, including support for achievements and Play Anywhere integration.
- The discussion addresses the current state of the Halo franchise and development rumors surrounding Bethesda’s The Elder Scrolls VI.
- New Xbox console updates include the introduction of a "Platinum Trophy" equivalent and improved party audio recording for Game DVR captures.

### Why it Matters
These updates highlight Microsoft's ongoing efforts to unify its gaming ecosystem across console and PC platforms while refining its broader AI software strategy. The shift in Copilot functionality reflects a growing industry trend toward balancing aggressive AI integration with practical, user-focused workflows.
