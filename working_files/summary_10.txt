Carbonato Botnet Compromises Docker Hosts to Deploy Telegram-Controlled Hermes AI Agent
============================================================

Cybersecurity researchers have identified a new botnet called Carbonato that exploits unauthenticated Docker daemons to deploy AI-driven Hermes Agent frameworks for automated, persistent cyberattacks and credential theft.

### Key Points
- Carbonato targets Docker daemons exposed on port 2375 to install privileged containers and establish persistent remote access.
- The malware uses a custom prompt to transform the Hermes Agent into a "senior hacker" persona that operates without ethical restrictions.
- Operators manage the botnet via Telegram, using the AI agent to execute terminal commands and exfiltrate sensitive credentials.
- Infrastructure analysis suggests the threat actors are based in Costa Rica, though the group remains unattributed.
- The botnet employs worm-like propagation, scanning neighboring networks every five minutes to identify and compromise additional vulnerable hosts.

### Why it Matters
This campaign highlights a growing trend of threat actors integrating AI agents to automate complex attack lifecycles, significantly increasing the speed and scale of malicious operations. Organizations must prioritize securing exposed management interfaces to prevent autonomous systems from gaining persistent, high-level access to their infrastructure.
