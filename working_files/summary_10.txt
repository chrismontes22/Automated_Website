iPhone 20's Curved Glass Design to Be Made Like iPhone Air
============================================================

Apple is reportedly utilizing advanced glass manufacturing processes developed for the iPhone Air to produce a redesigned, curved smartphone model marking the iPhone's 20th anniversary in 2027.

### Key Points
- Apple plans to use modified iPhone Air manufacturing equipment to create rounded glass components for its upcoming 20th-anniversary device.
- The new production process is expected to improve device heat dissipation and overall thinness while simultaneously reducing manufacturing costs.
- Reports suggest the anniversary model will feature a mostly glass, curved design without display cutouts, wrapping glass around all sides of the phone.
- The device is scheduled for a fall 2027 launch, following the release of the iPhone 18, iPhone 18e, and iPhone Air 2 earlier that year.

### Why it Matters
This manufacturing shift signals Apple's commitment to refining its hardware design language by leveraging existing thin-profile technology for future flagship models. Successfully scaling these complex glass production techniques could set a new standard for durability and aesthetics in the premium smartphone market.
