Michael Burry says it would be for the 'good of humanity' if a market crash stops OpenAI and Anthropic from going public
============================================================

Investor Michael Burry argues that a significant market downturn is necessary to prevent the initial public offerings of AI leaders OpenAI and Anthropic for the benefit of humanity.

### Key Points
- Michael Burry claims that AI companies will destroy trillions of dollars in capital and cause broader societal damage.
- OpenAI CEO Sam Altman has suggested that a 2026 IPO would be ill-advised due to ongoing AI safety concerns.
- Reports indicate that Anthropic is preparing for a potential public offering following the upcoming U.S. midterm elections.
- Burry has increased his bearish bets against major technology firms, including Nvidia, Palantir, Micron, and Oracle.
- The investor contends that current debt-fueled spending on AI infrastructure and data centers is unsustainable under high interest rates.

### Why it Matters
Burry’s warnings highlight growing skepticism regarding the long-term financial viability of the current artificial intelligence investment boom. If his bearish thesis proves correct, a market correction could significantly disrupt the capital-intensive growth strategies currently fueling the AI sector.
