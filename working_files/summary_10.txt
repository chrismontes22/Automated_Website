Famous Science YouTuber Admits He Has Unhealthy Relationship With AI After Facing Backlash Over Recent Video
============================================================

Science content creator Hank Green plans to pause his main YouTube channel after admitting to an unhealthy overreliance on ChatGPT for script research and content generation.

### Key Points
- Hank Green faced backlash after viewers accused him of using AI-generated language in a recent episode of the Complexly channel.
- Green confirmed he has been using ChatGPT for research notes, acknowledging that his recent scripts sounded too much like AI-generated text.
- The creator plans to reduce his overall content output to address his dependency on large language models and improve his personal well-being.
- Green publicly apologized to his audience on Reddit, citing concerns from his family regarding his unsustainable workload and digital habits.

### Why it Matters
This situation highlights the growing tension between content creators and the integration of generative AI in professional workflows. It serves as a case study on how reliance on automated tools can impact audience trust and the perceived authenticity of digital media.
