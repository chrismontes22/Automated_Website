Google hires former Gmail lead to be Chrome’s new VP of Product
============================================================

Google has appointed former Relay.app founder Jacob Bank as the new Vice President of Product for Chrome to lead the browser's integration of advanced artificial intelligence features.

### Key Points
- Jacob Bank returns to Google after previously serving as Director of Product Management for Gmail, Calendar, and Chat.
- Bank founded the workflow automation platform Relay.app in 2021, which officially ceased operations in August.
- Several former Relay.app employees are joining the Google Chrome team alongside Bank to support new development initiatives.
- Bank intends to focus on transforming Chrome into a primary workspace for AI-driven productivity and agent-based collaboration.
- Google currently integrates its Gemini AI assistant into Chrome to provide browser-specific capabilities and automated browsing tools.

### Why it Matters
This leadership change signals Google's intent to aggressively evolve Chrome from a simple web browser into a sophisticated AI-powered productivity platform. By leveraging Bank's background in workflow automation, the company aims to deepen the integration of intelligent agents directly into the user's primary digital workspace.
