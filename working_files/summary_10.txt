CUKTECH’s 300W Desktop Charger Wants to Replace Your Entire Drawer of Power Bricks
============================================================

CUKTECH, a former OEM supplier for Xiaomi, has launched the 30 Charging Station Ultra, a 300W GaN-powered device that recently earned two European Product Design Awards.

### Key Points
- The 30 Charging Station Ultra features five ports, including three USB-C, one USB-A, and a dedicated DC output capable of 300W total power.
- It utilizes third-generation Gallium Nitride (GaN) technology and a PFC plus LLC dual architecture to maintain stability and thermal efficiency.
- The device supports 13 fast-charging protocols, including PD 3.1, PPS, and proprietary standards from Xiaomi and Huawei.
- A 1.83-inch IPS display provides real-time, color-coded monitoring of wattage, voltage, and current for all connected devices.
- Designed by Chong Wang and Ziyao Wang, the unit retails for $159.99 and includes a magnetic tilting base for improved desk ergonomics.

### Why it Matters
This product signals a shift in the power accessory market by offering high-wattage consolidation that replaces multiple proprietary laptop bricks with a single, intelligent station. By undercutting competitors on price while providing superior power headroom, CUKTECH is successfully transitioning from a behind-the-scenes manufacturer to a premium consumer brand.
