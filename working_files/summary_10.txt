Bitcoin slides below $82,000 as AI doubts return, with DeFi tokens leading declines
============================================================

Bitcoin prices retreated below $82,000 as mixed earnings reports from Tesla and Meta Platforms reignited investor concerns regarding artificial intelligence spending and triggered a broader market sell-off.

### Key Points
- Bitcoin fell 2.7% to approximately $81,800, reversing gains from a late-Wednesday surge that had pushed the cryptocurrency toward $84,800.
- The CoinDesk DeFi index dropped 5.6%, leading a wider decline across altcoins as market-order selling intensified across major digital assets.
- Global equities faced pressure, with Nasdaq 100 futures falling 1.32% and the Hang Seng index declining more than 2% amid broader economic uncertainty.
- Crypto derivatives activity surged to $226.6 billion in volume, though open interest fell 2.46% as traders closed existing positions rather than opening new ones.
- Monero emerged as a notable outlier, gaining 4.9% while other privacy coins like Zcash declined, amid high leverage and significant funding premiums.

### Why it Matters
The recent volatility highlights how closely cryptocurrency markets are currently tethered to broader equity trends and investor sentiment regarding AI-driven tech stocks. As crypto funding rates compress, the shift in capital toward commodity derivatives suggests that traders are increasingly cautious and prioritizing defensive positioning over speculative growth.
