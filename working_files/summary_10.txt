iTrustCapital vs Caleb and Brown
============================================================

iTrustCapital and Caleb & Brown offer distinct services, with iTrustCapital specializing in tax-advantaged retirement accounts while Caleb & Brown provides a full-service brokerage experience for diverse digital assets.

### Key Points
- iTrustCapital focuses on US-based self-directed IRAs, supporting Traditional, Roth, and SEP accounts for cryptocurrency and precious metals.
- Caleb & Brown operates as an international brokerage, offering access to over 250 digital assets and personalized support from dedicated brokers.
- iTrustCapital charges a standard 1% transaction fee, whereas Caleb & Brown utilizes an all-inclusive brokerage fee structure based on transaction value.
- iTrustCapital maintains a closed-loop custodial system, while Caleb & Brown allows for more flexible external wallet transfers.
- Both platforms utilize institutional-grade security measures, including cold storage and infrastructure from providers like Fireblocks.

### Why it Matters
Choosing between these platforms depends primarily on whether an investor prioritizes tax-advantaged retirement planning or personalized brokerage assistance and asset variety. Selecting the wrong account structure can lead to significant tax consequences or unintended limitations on asset control.
