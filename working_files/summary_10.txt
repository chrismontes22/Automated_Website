AMD reports significant gains in AI energy efficiency while Google expands its custom chip strategy by pitting Marvell against Broadcom to secure its position in the AI market.

### Key Points
- AMD claims its latest AI systems are four times more energy-efficient than hardware released two years ago.
- Google is evaluating Marvell as a competitor to Broadcom for custom AI chip production, supported by a $12.2 billion investment proposal.
- Cerebras introduced its CS-4 rack systems, which double per-chip performance and increase rack density by three times.
- Stripe is reportedly preparing a $7 billion acquisition to integrate AI token sales and model orchestration into its payment gateway services.

### Why it Matters
These developments highlight an industry-wide push to optimize AI infrastructure through both hardware efficiency and strategic supply chain diversification. As companies scale AI operations, the focus is shifting toward reducing power consumption and securing reliable, high-performance chip manufacturing partnerships.
