AI will keep uncovering critical software flaws for years, says Cloudflare CEO
============================================================

Cloudflare CEO Matthew Prince warns that AI-driven vulnerability discovery will cause frequent, widespread internet disruptions as automated tools rapidly expose critical security flaws in essential global software infrastructure.

### Key Points
- Cloudflare CEO Matthew Prince reports that AI tools are significantly accelerating the discovery of severe vulnerabilities in critical software, leading to frequent, large-scale outages.
- Recent PHP flaws, which impacted major platforms like GOG and various government websites, serve as a primary example of the risks posed by automated exploit development.
- The Electronic Frontier Foundation (EFF) notes that 3% of all recorded software vulnerabilities were discovered in October alone, with 25% of total known issues identified this year.
- Frontier AI models from companies like OpenAI and Anthropic have reduced the time required to find and exploit bugs from weeks to mere hours.
- Security experts advise users to maintain updated software, utilize password managers, and employ tracker blockers to mitigate risks during this period of increased instability.

### Why it Matters
The rapid automation of vulnerability research removes the traditional barrier of complexity that once protected obscure code, making the internet significantly more susceptible to frequent attacks. This shift forces a transition toward more aggressive patching cycles and highlights the urgent need for better support and regulation of the open-source software that powers global digital infrastructure.
