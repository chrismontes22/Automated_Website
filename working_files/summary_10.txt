9 Used Laptop Scams To Watch Out For
============================================================

Buying a used laptop can offer significant savings, but consumers must remain vigilant against common scams involving fake listings, stolen hardware, hidden damage, and insecure payment methods.

### Key Points
- Verify online sellers using Trustpilot and Whois.com to avoid fake stores and recently registered websites.
- Use reverse image searches or AI-detection tools to identify stolen or fabricated product photos in listings.
- Request proof of purchase and check serial numbers with local authorities to ensure the device is not stolen.
- Run diagnostic tools like CPU-Z, GPU-Z, and battery reports to confirm hardware specifications and component health.
- Perform a complete factory reset and OS reinstallation to eliminate potential malware or pre-installed spyware.
- Avoid unprotected payment methods like cryptocurrency, wire transfers, or PayPal "Friends and Family" to maintain buyer protection.

### Why it Matters
Falling victim to a used laptop scam can result in the total loss of funds or the acquisition of unusable, stolen, or compromised hardware. Exercising due diligence protects your financial investment and ensures the device is secure, functional, and legally obtained.
