Stock market today: S&P 500, Nasdaq futures recover in countdown to Nvidia earnings, Fed Jackson Hole summit
============================================================

US stocks rose on Tuesday as investors balanced escalating trade tensions with Canada against falling Treasury yields and anticipation for Nvidia’s upcoming quarterly earnings report.

### Key Points
- The Dow Jones and S&P 500 gained 0.3%, while the Nasdaq Composite rose 0.6% as tech stocks recovered.
- Nvidia shares climbed 2%, ending a seven-day losing streak ahead of its Wednesday earnings release.
- Canada announced retaliatory tariffs on $20 billion of US goods, including steel and dairy, following recent US duties.
- New home sales and consumer confidence both fell to their lowest levels since January, signaling economic pressure.
- DICK’s Sporting Goods shares dropped 30% after the retailer missed second-quarter expectations and lowered its full-year outlook.
- Bitcoin surged above $80,000 as investors sought alternative assets amid concerns regarding Treasury bond market volatility.

### Why it Matters
Market participants are closely monitoring the intersection of geopolitical trade friction and corporate performance to gauge the health of the broader economy. These developments, alongside upcoming Federal Reserve commentary, are shaping investor sentiment as they navigate high interest rates and shifting consumer demand.
