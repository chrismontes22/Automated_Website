Asia dominates Crypto Adoption Index, Bitget’s $351M hack: Asia Express
============================================================

The Asia-Pacific region leads global cryptocurrency adoption as Bitget reports a $352 million security breach and various nations advance digital asset infrastructure and regulatory frameworks across the continent.

### Key Points
- Nine of the top 20 nations in the 2026 Global Crypto Adoption Index are located in the Asia-Pacific region.
- Crypto exchange Bitget suspended withdrawals following a $351.6 million security breach, though the firm maintains a $464 million protection fund.
- The Bank of Korea launched a 24-hour won settlement pilot program involving four major domestic lenders to facilitate international transactions.
- Binance invested $100 million in stablecoin issuer Circle to expand USDC promotion and integration across its global trading platform.
- Saudi Arabia withdrew from the China-backed mBridge central bank digital currency project after completing a proof-of-concept phase.
- North Korean cyber group WaterPlum infected 30,000 devices globally through fake job recruitment schemes, resulting in $10.7 million in stolen cryptocurrency.

### Why it Matters
The concentration of crypto adoption and infrastructure development in Asia highlights the region's growing influence over global digital finance and stablecoin settlement trends. These developments, ranging from institutional blockchain adoption to significant security challenges, underscore the complex regulatory and technical landscape facing international investors and financial institutions.
