Your Windows laptop has crucial security settings that you're probably ignoring
============================================================

Windows 11 users can significantly enhance their laptop security by manually enabling key features like Controlled folder access, Dynamic lock, device encryption, and Tamper Protection to prevent unauthorized access.

### Key Points
- Controlled folder access prevents ransomware from modifying or deleting files in protected directories like Documents and Pictures.
- Dynamic lock automatically secures your device by monitoring the Bluetooth signal strength of a paired smartphone when you walk away.
- BitLocker device encryption protects data on your hard drive or SSD, ensuring information remains unreadable if the hardware is stolen.
- Tamper Protection prevents malicious apps and processes from disabling your Windows Security settings or security infrastructure.
- These settings are accessible through the Windows Settings menu under Privacy & security or the Virus & threat protection dashboard.

### Why it Matters
Enabling these built-in Windows features provides a critical layer of defense for mobile devices that are frequently exposed to public networks and physical theft. By hardening these settings, users can effectively mitigate the risk of data breaches and ransomware attacks without needing third-party security software.
