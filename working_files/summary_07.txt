The leader of PwC's 2,200-person DC office says clients expect some consulting work twice as fast as a year ago
============================================================

Professional services firms like PwC and McKinsey are facing increased client pressure to accelerate project delivery timelines as generative AI tools significantly reduce the time required for research.

### Key Points
- Clients now expect project milestones to be completed in half the time previously required, shifting focus toward rapid pilots and proofs of concept.
- McKinsey reports that AI has reduced the initial problem-solving cycle from one week to one hour, allowing consultants to focus on deeper analysis.
- Employees at firms like Deloitte and EY report that AI has drastically shortened tasks, such as coding and due diligence, from days to minutes or hours.
- PwC managing partner Jeanelle Johnson notes that while AI increases speed, the complexity of client challenges—including geopolitical and macroeconomic pressures—continues to grow.
- Major firms including PwC, Deloitte, KPMG, EY, BCG, and Accenture are actively integrating internal AI chatbots to maintain competitive delivery speeds.

### Why it Matters
The integration of AI into professional services is forcing a fundamental reevaluation of traditional labor-based pricing models as efficiency gains shorten project lifecycles. Firms must now balance the demand for faster, AI-driven results with the need to address increasingly complex global business challenges.
