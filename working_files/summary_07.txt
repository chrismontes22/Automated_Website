Google raises prices on the TV Streamer and Nest cameras effective today
============================================================

Google has implemented significant price increases across its smart home hardware lineup, raising the cost of the Google TV Streamer and several Nest security cameras and doorbells.

### Key Points
- The Google TV Streamer price increased by $50, moving from $100 to $150 at major retailers like the Google Store and Best Buy.
- Five Nest camera and doorbell models saw price hikes ranging from $20 to $50 effective August 31.
- The wired Nest Cam Indoor rose to $130, while the battery-powered Nest Cam and both Nest Doorbell versions now retail for $200.
- Google attributed the adjustments to rising component costs, specifically citing memory expenses for the streaming device.
- Some third-party retailers, including Amazon, may still offer remaining inventory at original launch prices until current stock is depleted.

### Why it Matters
These price adjustments signal a shift in Google's hardware strategy as the company navigates rising manufacturing and component costs. Consumers looking to build or expand their smart home ecosystems will face higher entry costs for essential streaming and security devices moving forward.
