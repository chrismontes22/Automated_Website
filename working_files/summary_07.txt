GrayKey maker can reportedly bypass the iPhone’s ‘Inactivity Reboot’ security feature
============================================================

Magnet Forensics has reportedly developed technology to bypass the iPhone Inactivity Reboot feature, allowing law enforcement to maintain access to device data even after a forced restart.

### Key Points
- The iOS 18.1 Inactivity Reboot feature forces iPhones into a secure "Before First Unlock" state if left locked for 72 hours.
- Magnet Forensics introduced the GrayKey Preserve device and Evidence Preservation Mode to maintain an "After First Unlock" state during forensic investigations.
- The new tools prevent the loss of encryption keys that typically occurs when an iPhone reboots, keeping data accessible to investigators.
- Security researchers suggest the workaround may involve manipulating the device clock or disabling the processes that trigger the reboot.
- The existence of these tools was revealed via an internal tutorial video dated early 2025, though their deployment history remains unconfirmed.

### Why it Matters
This development challenges Apple’s recent efforts to enhance user privacy and security by limiting the window for forensic data extraction. It highlights an ongoing technological arms race between smartphone manufacturers and forensic firms regarding the accessibility of encrypted mobile data.
