No Mic Needed: You Can Create Music and Speech With Adobe’s AI Audio Tools
============================================================

Adobe has launched a new suite of Firefly generative AI tools designed to help creators produce professional-grade speech, background music, and sound effects for video projects.

### Key Points
- The new tools allow users to generate speech from scripts in over 20 languages, featuring adjustable emotion tags to reduce robotic-sounding output.
- Users can create instrumental background music and custom sound effects using text prompts or by uploading reference audio for the AI to transform.
- Adobe guarantees all generated audio is commercially safe and includes a universal license to protect creators from copyright infringement claims.
- The features are integrated into the Firefly AI hub and are accessible through Creative Cloud subscriptions or a standalone plan starting at $10 per month.
- Adobe states its models are trained exclusively on licensed or public domain content and do not utilize customer-uploaded work for training purposes.

### Why it Matters
These tools provide professional creators with a legally secure way to integrate AI-generated audio into their workflows without the risk of copyright litigation. By focusing on utility and commercial safety, Adobe aims to standardize AI adoption in creative industries while addressing common concerns regarding intellectual property and platform-specific content policies.
