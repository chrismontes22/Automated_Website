Google Maps relabels Lake Ontario as Lake America for US users
============================================================

Google Maps has updated its platform to label Lake Ontario as Lake America for users in the United States following a recent change to federal geographic naming databases.

### Key Points
- The name change was triggered by an August 27 update to the US Geographic Names Information System.
- Canadian users continue to see the name Lake Ontario, while international users see both names displayed.
- Alphabet stated the update aligns with its policy of reflecting country-specific naming conventions for bodies of water.
- Apple Maps and MapQuest have not adopted the change, with MapQuest allowing users to customize labels instead.
- This follows a similar precedent set last year when the Gulf of Mexico was renamed the Gulf of America on Google Maps.

### Why it Matters
This development highlights how government database updates can directly influence the digital geography presented by major technology platforms. It raises significant questions regarding the regulatory oversight of large online platforms, particularly concerning their responsibility to manage systemic risks and potential impacts on public discourse.
