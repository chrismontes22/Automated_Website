Gemini's Daily Brief drops its subscription fee — here's what you get
============================================================

Google is expanding access to its Gemini Daily Brief feature by removing subscription requirements for free users in the United States to provide personalized morning task summaries.

### Key Points
- The Daily Brief feature is now available to free users without requiring a Google AI Plus, Pro, or Ultra subscription.
- The tool aggregates information from Gmail, Google Calendar, connected applications, and previous Gemini chat history.
- Users must enable Personal Intelligence, Memory, and Google Workspace integration to receive the automated morning notifications.
- The rollout is currently limited to the United States and is being distributed to personal accounts in a gradual release.
- Future updates may include audio-based briefings and integration directly onto the Android lock screen.

### Why it Matters
This expansion makes Google’s proactive AI assistant more accessible to the general public by removing the paywall previously required for personalized daily organization. By integrating Gemini deeper into daily workflows, Google aims to increase user engagement and solidify its AI as a central tool for personal productivity.
