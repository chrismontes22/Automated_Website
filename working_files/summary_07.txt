Foldable 'iPhone Ultra 3' Rumored to Feature Larger Displays
============================================================

Apple is reportedly developing a third-generation foldable iPhone for a potential 2028 release, expanding its long-term hardware roadmap to include larger displays and iterative design improvements over time.

### Key Points
- Apple plans to launch its first foldable iPhone this year, featuring a 7.8-inch inner display, an A20 chip, and a price point starting around $2,000.
- The second-generation "iPhone Ultra 2" is officially in development and is currently scheduled for a fall 2027 release alongside the 20th-anniversary iPhone.
- A third-generation foldable model is projected for 2028, with early reports indicating it will feature larger internal and external screen dimensions than its predecessors.
- The initial foldable device will reportedly prioritize an ultra-thin design and utilize Touch ID instead of the standard Face ID authentication system.

### Why it Matters
This extended roadmap signals Apple's commitment to establishing a long-term presence in the competitive foldable smartphone market. By planning three generations of hardware, the company aims to refine its foldable technology and maintain its premium market position against established rivals.
