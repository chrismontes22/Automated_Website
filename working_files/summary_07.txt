Surface Laptop Ultra review: a different kind of MacBook Pro
============================================================

The Microsoft Surface Laptop Ultra features Nvidia’s Arm-based RTX Spark chip, offering a premium Windows alternative to the MacBook Pro that targets developers interested in local AI experimentation.

### Key Points
- The laptop starts at $2,599, with the tested high-end model retailing for $5,900.
- Hardware includes a 15-inch mini-LED touchscreen, 128GB of unified memory, and a 20-core CPU codesigned by Nvidia and MediaTek.
- The device supports local AI agents like Hermes and OpenClaw, though the software experience is currently in a beta-like, experimental state.
- Gaming performance is bolstered by an RTX GPU with support for DLSS and Frame Generation, allowing for playable frame rates in demanding titles.
- Notable hardware features include a magnetic USB-C charging system, a large haptic trackpad, and a diverse port selection including HDMI 2.1b and an SD card reader.

### Why it Matters
The Surface Laptop Ultra represents a significant attempt by Microsoft and Nvidia to challenge Apple’s dominance in the high-end laptop market through specialized AI hardware. While the device offers impressive build quality and unique capabilities, its high price and unrefined software make it a niche tool primarily suited for developers rather than general consumers.
