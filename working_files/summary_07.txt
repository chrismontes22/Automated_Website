Gap unveils AI-assisted shopping, three decades after its first website
============================================================

Gap Inc. is launching new AI-powered shopping tools across its brand portfolio, including Gap, Banana Republic, Old Navy, and Athleta, to enhance personalized customer discovery and digital retail experiences.

### Key Points
- Gap Inc. is integrating AI platforms Alta Daily and Daydream to provide personalized outfit curation and natural language search capabilities for shoppers.
- The new technology will be available across all four company brands: Gap, Banana Republic, Old Navy, and Athleta.
- CTO Sven Gerjets announced the initiative during San Francisco Tech Week, emphasizing the goal of reducing friction in the online shopping process.
- The company is hosting a "Never Finished: The Next Chapter" event at its headquarters to discuss the future of retail innovation.
- Research indicates that while consumers are interested in AI shopping, many remain cautious regarding data privacy and the accuracy of apparel recommendations.

### Why it Matters
This rollout represents a strategic effort by Gap Inc. to modernize its digital infrastructure and maintain customer loyalty through intuitive, AI-driven personalization. By addressing common consumer concerns regarding AI reliability, the company aims to set a new standard for how legacy retailers integrate emerging technology into the shopping journey.
