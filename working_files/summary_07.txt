How to get the 'spectacular' Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 8 for the price of a monthly latte
============================================================

AT&T is offering a promotional deal that reduces the price of the new Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 8 to $5.56 per month ahead of its August 7th retail launch.

### Key Points
- Customers can receive up to $1,000 in credits by trading in an eligible device and adding a new line on an unlimited plan.
- The promotion requires a trade-in of a Samsung Galaxy S22 Plus, Z Flip 4, Z Flip 3, or newer for the maximum discount.
- Older devices, including the Galaxy S10 series, qualify for a $500 discount toward the $1,199.99 retail price.
- The Galaxy Z Flip 8 features a Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 processor, a lighter design, and seven years of guaranteed software support.
- The promotional credits are applied over a 36-month installment period.

### Why it Matters
This aggressive pricing strategy helps offset the $100 price increase over the previous model, making the premium foldable more accessible to new subscribers. By tying the discount to long-term service contracts, AT&T aims to secure customer loyalty while driving adoption of Samsung's latest flagship hardware.
