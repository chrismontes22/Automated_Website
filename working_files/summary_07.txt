IT Teams are Spending 11 Hours a Week on Cloud Connectivity Problems
============================================================

A new survey from DE-CIX reveals that enterprises spend over 11 hours weekly troubleshooting cloud connectivity issues, prompting a shift toward private interconnection to support growing AI workloads.

### Key Points
- IT teams spend an average of 11 hours per week resolving cloud connectivity problems despite 96% of firms claiming network readiness.
- Primary connectivity concerns include latency (28%), security vulnerabilities (27%), and reliability issues (26%).
- Approximately 61% of surveyed companies currently use private interconnection, with another 31% actively considering the transition.
- Global cloud infrastructure spending reached $143 billion in Q2 2026, marking a 43% year-on-year growth driven largely by AI demand.
- Amazon Web Services, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud now control 67% of the total cloud infrastructure market.

### Why it Matters
Reliable network architecture is becoming a critical bottleneck for businesses attempting to scale AI applications that require rapid, secure data movement. As cloud infrastructure spending surges, companies are increasingly adopting private interconnection to gain the performance and control necessary to maintain a competitive advantage.
