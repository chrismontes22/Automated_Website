AT&T Acknowledges iPhone 18 Pro Max Issues: 'Update Immediately'
============================================================

AT&T has alerted iPhone 18 Pro Max users to persistent cellular connectivity failures, urging customers to install iOS 27.0.1 despite reports that the update fails to resolve issues.

### Key Points
- AT&T customers report intermittent or total loss of calls, texts, and data, often forcing devices into SOS mode.
- The carrier officially recommended updating to iOS 27.0.1, though Apple’s release notes only mention a fix for Face ID.
- Thousands of users have documented these connectivity problems across Reddit, MacRumors, and Apple Support forums.
- The U.S. version of the iPhone 18 Pro Max is the only model using a Qualcomm cellular modem, distinguishing it from other global variants.
- Apple has not yet issued a public statement or confirmed whether the root cause is a software bug or a hardware defect.

### Why it Matters
This connectivity failure poses a significant safety risk by potentially preventing users from accessing emergency services like 911. If the issue stems from a hardware defect in the Qualcomm modem, Apple and AT&T could face widespread device recalls or costly repair programs.
