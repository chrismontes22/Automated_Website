Apple Event Live Blog: Folding iPhone, Apple Watch Series 12, AirPods, and More
============================================================

Apple unveiled its latest product lineup at a Cupertino media event, highlighted by the introduction of the new Duo smartphone and advanced intelligent listening features for Apple Watches.

### Key Points
- Apple hosted a media showcase at its headquarters in Cupertino, California, to debut new hardware and software features.
- The new Duo smartphone features a dual-screen design, improved battery life, and faster processing chips.
- New Apple Watch models include integrated intelligent listening tools designed with specific privacy and security protocols.
- Pricing for the new Duo starts at $1,999, reflecting a broader trend of rising costs across Apple’s MacBook and iPad product lines.
- Apple is prioritizing high profit margins and its growing services business to offset fluctuations in global product shipment volumes.

### Why it Matters
The introduction of the high-priced Duo signals Apple's strategy to maintain profitability by leveraging premium hardware designs and expanded service offerings. This shift highlights the company's ongoing effort to balance technical innovation with the financial necessity of sustaining margins amid shifting consumer demand.
