Binance Suffers Biggest Crypto Hack In History With $6.3 Billion Affected [UPDATE]
============================================================

Binance has temporarily suspended customer withdrawals following a massive security breach that resulted in the theft of approximately $6.3 billion in cryptocurrency assets from the exchange.

### Key Points
- Binance estimates $6.3 billion in funds were impacted, with roughly $3 billion in assets currently frozen on-chain.
- Blockchain analysis firms TRM Labs and Elliptic have linked the attack to the North Korean hacking group known as TraderTraitor.
- The attackers reportedly compromised Binance’s wallet-signing infrastructure, potentially starting with a breach of an engineer's laptop.
- Tether and Circle assisted in the recovery efforts by freezing approximately $1.8 billion in stablecoins associated with the stolen funds.
- The incident occurred alongside a separate security breach at the prediction market Polymarket, which resulted in a $2.7 million loss.

### Why it Matters
This event represents the largest cryptocurrency hack in history, raising significant concerns regarding the security infrastructure of major global exchanges. The incident highlights the ongoing threat posed by state-sponsored cyber actors and underscores the critical role of stablecoin issuers in mitigating large-scale digital asset theft.
