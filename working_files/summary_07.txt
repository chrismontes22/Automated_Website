Nacon’s new PS5 controller can mix audio from your phone and console
============================================================

Nacon has unveiled the Revolution 5 Unlimited, the first officially licensed PlayStation 5 controller featuring an integrated screen for real-time settings adjustments and advanced multi-source audio mixing capabilities.

### Key Points
- The controller retails for $229.99 and is currently available for preorder through Nacon’s official online store.
- It features drift-resistant tunneling magnetoresistance (TMR) thumbsticks with an asymmetrical layout and swappable D-pad designs.
- Users can adjust the controller's physical weight using included 10, 14, and 16-gram inserts for a personalized feel.
- The device includes adjustable trigger travel, multiple shortcut buttons, and compatibility with PC and Android platforms.
- A dedicated mobile app allows for further customization of response curves, RGB lighting, and audio EQ profiles.

### Why it Matters
This controller represents a significant shift toward high-end, modular hardware for console gamers who demand professional-grade customization and performance. By integrating a screen and advanced audio mixing, Nacon is setting a new standard for peripheral functionality in the competitive gaming market.
