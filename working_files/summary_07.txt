The Apple HomePad Might Basically Be a Giant Apple Watch
============================================================

Apple is reportedly developing the "HomePad," a smart home hub featuring a 7-inch display, FaceTime camera, and advanced Siri integration, with a planned market launch in early 2027.

### Key Points
- The device will launch in two versions: a $599 tabletop model with speakers and a $399 wall-mounted digital panel.
- It runs on "Pebble," a hybrid operating system combining elements of tvOS, watchOS, and iPadOS for a customized interface.
- Hardware includes an A18 or newer chip to support advanced Siri voice commands and proximity-based user profile switching.
- The device integrates with Apple’s ecosystem to manage HomeKit-enabled devices, AirPlay media, and future security camera hardware.
- Privacy features include on-device processing and end-to-end encryption to secure individual user data within a shared household.

### Why it Matters
This device signals Apple's intent to capture a larger share of the smart home market by centralizing control within its proprietary ecosystem. By offering a premium, privacy-focused hub, Apple aims to deepen user reliance on its hardware while competing directly with established smart display manufacturers.
