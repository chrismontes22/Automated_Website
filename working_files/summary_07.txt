CISA Alert: Water Sector PLC Targeting
============================================================

CISA has issued an urgent warning regarding threat actors targeting internet-exposed programmable logic controllers in the water sector, leading to operational disruptions and mandatory boil-water notices nationwide.

### Key Points
- Threat actors are locking out operators by changing passwords and disconnecting devices by modifying IP addresses on critical water infrastructure.
- CISA identified Rockwell Automation, Siemens, and Schneider Electric equipment as primary targets for these malicious cyber activities.
- Cellular modems are frequently overlooked in security scans, creating significant blind spots for critical infrastructure operators and system integrators.
- Censys data from July 30, 2026, reveals over 10,000 internet-exposed hosts across the three identified vendors globally.
- Recommended mitigations include removing PLCs from the public internet and routing all remote access through secure VPNs or gateway devices.

### Why it Matters
The targeting of water and wastewater systems poses a direct threat to public health and the reliability of essential utility services. By securing operational technology, organizations can prevent unauthorized access that leads to service outages and dangerous water quality issues.
