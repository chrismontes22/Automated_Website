Hide.me VPN's latest Android update brings a new dashboard and SmartGuard for Android TV - here's what you need to know
============================================================

Hide.me VPN has launched a major update for Android and Android TV, featuring a redesigned dashboard, enhanced security tools, and improved connection stability for mobile and streaming users.

### Key Points
- The redesigned dashboard simplifies navigation and server selection across Android mobile and Android TV platforms.
- SmartGuard, a security feature that blocks ads, trackers, and malicious websites, is now available on Android TV.
- Stability improvements were implemented for WireGuard and SSTP protocols, including MTU optimization and IPv6 compatibility.
- New speed indicators allow users to identify and connect to the fastest available servers at a glance.
- The application now includes support for the Korean language.

### Why it Matters
This update enhances privacy and performance for users who rely on streaming devices that often track viewing habits and serve targeted advertisements. By bringing robust security tools to Android TV, Hide.me provides a more consistent and secure experience across the increasingly fragmented ecosystem of connected home devices.
