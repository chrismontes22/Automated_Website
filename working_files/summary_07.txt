Amazon’s new Alexa Tablet finally gives the iPad lineup some much needed competition
============================================================

Amazon has launched a new lineup of Alexa Tablets that transition from a proprietary operating system to stock Android, featuring full Google Play Store access and premium hardware designs.

### Key Points
- The new lineup includes an 8.7-inch model for $229, an 11-inch model for $329, and a 12-inch Pro model for $499.
- These devices replace Amazon’s previous proprietary OS with a stock Android experience, allowing native access to the Google Play Store.
- The 11-inch model features 8GB of RAM and a 90Hz display, positioning it as a lower-cost alternative to Apple’s entry-level iPad.
- Amazon is offering a 20% discount on the new tablets for customers who participate in a trade-in program.
- Integrated features include a specialized reader mode designed to reduce blue light exposure for Kindle users.

### Why it Matters
This shift to Android represents a significant strategic pivot for Amazon, potentially challenging Apple’s dominance in the tablet market by offering high-end hardware at competitive price points. Increased competition in this space may pressure Apple to accelerate innovation and feature updates for its own iPad lineup.
