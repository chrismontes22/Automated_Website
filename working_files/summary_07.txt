Five days after buying Cursor for $60bn, SpaceX tried to buy Cognition
============================================================

SpaceX recently approached AI coding startup Cognition regarding a potential acquisition, but the company’s leadership rejected the offer, stating they intend to remain an independent business entity.

### Key Points
- Cognition CEO Scott Wu publicly confirmed the company is not for sale following reports of an acquisition attempt by SpaceX.
- Cognition reached a $26 billion valuation in May and is currently discussing a potential valuation of at least $40 billion with investors.
- SpaceX recently completed a $60 billion acquisition of the AI coding firm Cursor on August 14.
- Cognition’s Devin AI agent is currently utilized by major enterprise clients, including Mercedes-Benz and GE Aerospace.
- SpaceX and Cognition are reportedly discussing a separate partnership involving the use of SpaceX’s computing infrastructure and GPU capacity.

### Why it Matters
This acquisition attempt highlights Elon Musk’s aggressive strategy to rapidly scale SpaceX’s AI division to meet ambitious enterprise revenue targets. If Cognition enters a computing partnership with SpaceX, the startup may become strategically dependent on the infrastructure of the very company that sought to acquire it.
