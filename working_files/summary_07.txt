5 Costco Finds With Deep Discounts In August 2026
============================================================

Costco is offering significant limited-time discounts throughout August 2026 on various high-rated electronics, including laptops, televisions, wireless earbuds, and home security systems for its members.

### Key Points
- The Dell 16-inch Touchscreen Laptop is discounted by $300 to $999 until August 16, including software subscriptions.
- TCL QM7L Pro 75-inch TVs are priced at $1,299 through August 31, featuring a five-year total protection plan.
- Apple AirPods Pro 3 are available for $199 until August 23, bundled with a complimentary two-year AppleCare+ plan.
- The Blink Whole Home 2K+ Security Bundle is priced at $149 until August 16, offering a five-camera system.
- Beats Studio Pro headphones are reduced to $149 until August 23, marking a significant discount from their typical retail price.

### Why it Matters
These promotions highlight Costco's strategy of leveraging exclusive bundles and extended warranties to drive consumer electronics sales during the competitive back-to-school season. By offering value-added services like free AppleCare+ or multi-year protection plans, the retailer differentiates its inventory from standard online marketplace pricing.
