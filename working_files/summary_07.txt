Apple Wins Big in CNET’s People’s Picks Awards for Its MacBooks
============================================================

Apple secured the top spot in CNET’s 2026 People’s Picks awards, outperforming competitors across eight categories including overall satisfaction, battery life, display quality, and hardware reliability.

### Key Points
- Apple achieved a 96.7% overall satisfaction rating, with 68% of MacBook owners stating they would purchase the same model again.
- Survey data indicates 44.4% of MacBook users experience battery life of eight hours or more, significantly outpacing Lenovo, Dell, and HP.
- Despite higher price points, 94.6% of Apple users reported that their laptops provide exceptional or fair value for the investment.
- Asus earned recognition as a top midrange choice, while Acer was highlighted for budget-friendly value and Lenovo for hardware durability.
- The survey, conducted by Alchemer, gathered insights from 1,003 U.S. adults between September and October 2026.

### Why it Matters
These findings highlight a strong consumer preference for premium hardware and ecosystem integration, which continues to drive high brand loyalty for Apple despite the availability of cheaper alternatives. For shoppers, the data suggests that while non-Apple brands like Asus and Acer offer competitive value and specific performance strengths, Apple remains the market leader in long-term user satisfaction and reliability.
