Google Pixel 11 Pro vs. Pixel 10 Pro: Should you bump your Pixel Pro to the newest one when it comes out?
============================================================

**Upcoming leaks suggest the Google Pixel 11 Pro will feature a new Tensor G6 chipset and increased base storage, though it faces stiff competition from the existing Pixel 10 Pro.**

### Key Points
- The Pixel 11 Pro is expected to launch with a 2nm Tensor G6 chipset, offering improved efficiency and gaming performance via Vulkan support.
- Base storage for the Pixel 11 Pro will start at 256GB, compared to the 128GB starting capacity of the Pixel 10 Pro.
- The new model introduces "Pixel Glow" LED lighting in the camera bar but features a downgraded 13MP selfie camera compared to the 10 Pro's 42MP sensor.
- Pricing for the Pixel 11 Pro is rumored to start at $899, representing a $100 increase over the previous generation.
- Both devices share similar display specs, IP68 water resistance, and a seven-year software update commitment from Google.

### Why it Matters
The marginal hardware improvements and potential downgrades in the Pixel 11 Pro suggest that current Pixel 10 Pro owners may see little incentive to upgrade. Prospective buyers might find better value in purchasing a discounted Pixel 10 Pro once the new flagship officially hits the market.
