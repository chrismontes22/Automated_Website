This unofficial port brings Pixel 11's Rambler to any Android phone
============================================================

Developer Akshay Kadam has released PixelBoard, a modified Gboard APK that allows users to access Google’s exclusive Pixel 11 Rambler AI voice typing feature on other Android devices.

### Key Points
- Rambler uses AI to remove filler words, correct grammar, and format text automatically after voice input.
- The feature was previously exclusive to the Google Pixel 11 series smartphones launched earlier this year.
- Users can access the tool by installing the unofficial PixelBoard APK and setting it as their default keyboard.
- The modified app is available via GitHub, though it is not an official product supported by Google.
- Security risks exist when installing modified APK files, and Google may block this workaround via future updates.

### Why it Matters
- This workaround provides early access to advanced AI productivity tools for users who do not own the latest flagship hardware. However, relying on unofficial software poses potential security and privacy risks that could lead to data exposure or loss of functionality if Google patches the exploit.
