How to Play Your Favourite Games at Uni with Top Graphics on Your Laptop
============================================================

NVIDIA’s GeForce NOW cloud gaming service allows students to stream high-end titles to standard laptops, eliminating the need for expensive gaming hardware while living in university accommodation.

### Key Points
- GeForce NOW enables cloud-based gaming across laptops, tablets, and mobile devices without requiring local storage or high-end internal components.
- The platform supports over 4,500 titles from major storefronts including Steam, Epic Games Store, and Xbox Game Pass.
- Users can access advanced graphical features like NVIDIA DLSS, Reflex, and Ray Tracing via remote GeForce RTX 50-series server hardware.
- Membership options include a free tier, monthly subscriptions starting at £9.99, and an Ultimate tier providing up to 5K resolution and 360 FPS.
- Flexible Day Passes are available for £3.99 or £7.99, allowing users to access premium features for 24-hour periods without a long-term commitment.

### Why it Matters
This service provides a cost-effective solution for students to maintain their gaming hobbies without the financial burden or physical space requirements of a dedicated desktop rig. By shifting processing power to the cloud, it democratizes access to high-performance gaming regardless of the user's local hardware limitations.
