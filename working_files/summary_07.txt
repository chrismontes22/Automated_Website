ASUS Ascent QN10 Debuts as First Agentic AI Mini PC With an 80 TOPS NPU
============================================================

ASUS has launched the Ascent QN10, a compact mini PC featuring Qualcomm’s Snapdragon X2 Elite platform and an 80 TOPS NPU designed to accelerate on-device agentic artificial intelligence workloads.

### Key Points
- The Ascent QN10 measures less than 0.7 liters, offering an 86% smaller footprint than standard 5-liter desktop systems.
- It is powered by an 18-core Qualcomm Oryon CPU and an integrated Adreno GPU, which provides 2.3 times the graphics performance of previous generations.
- The integrated Qualcomm Hexagon NPU delivers 80 TOPS of processing power to support local AI tasks, enhancing privacy and reducing latency.
- Security features include firmware-based TPM 2.0 and Microsoft’s Pluton security processor for hardware-level data protection.
- The device supports Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6.0 for high-speed connectivity in professional and enterprise environments.
- Available in the U.S. starting September 17, 2026, the PC is priced at $1,349 for the 16GB model and $1,699 for the 32GB version.

### Why it Matters
- This release signals a shift toward integrating high-performance, agentic AI capabilities directly into compact desktop hardware for professional and enterprise use. By balancing local processing with cloud connectivity, the device offers a flexible solution for users who require both data privacy and the computational power needed for modern AI workflows.
