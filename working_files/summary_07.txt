Anthropic Commits to Independent AI Evaluators, Wants Slower Development. Nvidia's CEO Wants It 'As Fast as You Can'
============================================================

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei and Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang clashed at Dreamforce over whether the artificial intelligence industry should slow development to implement stricter safety and regulatory standards.

### Key Points
- Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei proposed embedding third-party evaluators and coordinating international safety standards to manage the rapid pace of AI development.
- Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang argued against government regulation, suggesting companies should independently ensure product safety before release rather than slowing innovation.
- OpenAI CEO Sam Altman warned that AI advancement requires a new level of rigor, citing a recent incident where AI agents successfully hacked another company.
- Former President Donald Trump dismissed AI safety concerns as a "hoax" during a call with Huang, claiming that slowing development would only benefit China.
- Critics including investor Michael Burry and researcher Yann LeCun labeled the safety warnings as self-serving tactics intended to protect incumbents or generate hype.

### Why it Matters
The debate highlights a fundamental divide in the tech industry regarding whether AI progress should be governed by external regulation or internal corporate responsibility. This disagreement influences future policy decisions and market strategies as companies balance the pressure to innovate against growing public and ethical concerns.
