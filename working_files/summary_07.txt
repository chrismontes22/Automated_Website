Google Pixel Watch 5 Review: More Health, More AI
============================================================

The Google Pixel Watch 5 introduces upgraded health tracking, a faster Snapdragon W5 Gen 2 processor, and new AI-driven fitness features, starting at a retail price of $399.

### Key Points
- The device features an upgraded Qualcomm Snapdragon W5 Gen 2 chip, offering 20 percent faster performance and double the RAM of the previous model.
- New Health Guardian tools include tracking for insulin resistance, blood pressure, and sleep breathing quality, with clinical validation expected in September.
- The updated Strength Training experience allows users to build custom workouts, track reps and weights, and receive audio cues directly on the watch.
- Gemini AI now supports offline functionality for tasks like starting workouts, and the watch includes improved dual-band GPS for better urban accuracy.
- Battery life remains approximately 36 to 40 hours, with fast-charging capabilities providing 15 hours of usage from a 15-minute charge.
- The watch is available in 41-mm and 45-mm sizes, with prices starting at $399 and $429 respectively.

### Why it Matters
The Pixel Watch 5 positions Google as a serious competitor in the health-tech space by integrating advanced, subscription-based wellness insights directly into its wearable ecosystem. While the hardware refinements are incremental, the shift toward proactive health monitoring and AI-assisted fitness coaching signals a broader strategy to make the watch an essential tool for long-term personal health management.
