Hasbro Data Breach Exposed Employee Personal Information
============================================================

Hasbro has begun notifying current and former employees that their personal information, including financial data and national identification numbers, may have been compromised in a recent security breach.

### Key Points
- Hasbro confirmed that exposed data may include names, email addresses, postal addresses, phone numbers, and financial information.
- The breach follows a late March cyberattack that forced the company to take systems offline, resulting in $11 million in cleanup costs.
- Massachusetts state filings indicate at least 436 residents were impacted, though the total number of affected employees remains unconfirmed.
- Hasbro is offering identity protection services to affected individuals despite stating there is no current evidence of data misuse.
- The company has not officially confirmed if this specific data exposure is directly linked to the March cyberattack.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights the ongoing financial and operational risks large corporations face from sophisticated cyberattacks that compromise sensitive employee records. The breach underscores the importance of robust data protection protocols and the long-term recovery costs associated with securing enterprise networks after a security failure.
