Snap SPECS can work and play—AI intelligence platform 'anticipates' your needs
============================================================

Snap has officially unveiled its new SPECS smart glasses, featuring an AI-native operating system and augmented reality capabilities, with pre-orders now open for a late fall market launch.

### Key Points
- Snap SPECS are available for pre-order at $2,196, with a Verizon-partnered cellular bundle offered at $2,395.
- The glasses feature a virtual 115-inch display for streaming content from platforms like YouTube, HBO Max, and Spotify.
- Integrated AR tools include real-time translation for 60 languages, navigation assistance, and Tripadvisor location services.
- The device utilizes "SPECS Intelligence," an anticipatory AI platform designed to proactively assist users based on their routines and goals.
- Initial launch markets include the United States, the United Kingdom, and France, with hands-on demonstrations beginning October 1 in Los Angeles.

### Why it Matters
This launch marks a significant push by Snap into the competitive wearable computing market by emphasizing hands-free, AI-driven interactions over traditional smartphone usage. The success of these glasses could signal a shift in consumer demand toward proactive, context-aware augmented reality devices for daily productivity and entertainment.
