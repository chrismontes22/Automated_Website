Hyperliquid Strategies Shares Rise After Reporting $1.9B HYPE Position
============================================================

Hyperliquid Strategies Inc. reported total assets of $2.06 billion as of June 30, driven largely by significant unrealized gains in its HYPE token treasury holdings during the quarter.

### Key Points
- Hyperliquid Strategies held 29.3 million HYPE tokens valued at $1.904 billion by the end of June.
- The firm raised $647 million through a committed equity facility to expand its digital asset treasury.
- Net unrealized gains on HYPE tokens reached $709.9 million, significantly outpacing the $9.5 million earned from staking and validator commissions.
- Hyperliquid’s decentralized perpetuals open-interest share reached approximately 63% as of August 23.
- Since the fiscal year closed, the company spent $773.4 million to acquire additional HYPE tokens and $27.8 million to repurchase PURR shares.

### Why it Matters
The company’s financial performance is now heavily correlated with the market volatility of the HYPE token. This treasury-focused model highlights a shift toward active digital asset management, balancing token appreciation with strategic share repurchases and infrastructure participation.
