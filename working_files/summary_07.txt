With Switch 2, iPhone, and laptop tricks, the Sharge Disk Pro 2 is finally a worthy EDC
============================================================

Sharge has launched the Disk Pro 2, a versatile portable SSD enclosure that functions as a 4K HDMI dock and USB-C hub for laptops, phones, and gaming consoles.

### Key Points
- The device supports M.2 2280, 2242, and 2230 SSDs with up to 8TB of storage capacity.
- Integrated features include a 4K 144Hz HDMI 2.1 port, a 5Gbps USB-A port, SD and microSD card readers, and USB-C passthrough charging.
- Built-in MagSafe-compatible magnets allow the device to attach directly to smartphones like the iPhone or Pixel.
- Two models are available: the Ultra, which supports 4K 144Hz output, and the Lite, which is limited to 4K 60Hz.
- The unit includes an active cooling fan and is currently available via Kickstarter with a retail price of $89.

### Why it Matters
This device consolidates multiple essential peripherals into a single, pocket-sized form factor, significantly reducing the amount of hardware users need to carry for mobile workstations. By bridging the gap between high-speed storage and display connectivity, it offers a practical solution for power users and gamers who frequently switch between mobile devices and external monitors.
