5 Android Phones That Can See In The Dark
============================================================

Modern Android smartphones equipped with dedicated infrared cameras allow users to capture clear, monochromatic images in complete darkness, offering a specialized alternative to standard software-based night mode photography.

### Key Points
- The Doogee S200 Plus features a 20-megapixel IR camera and a 10,100 mAh battery, retailing for approximately $499.
- The Oukitel WP58 Pro provides an 8-megapixel IR camera and integrated camp lights for $249, targeting budget-conscious users.
- Ulefone’s Armor 27T Pro Plus combines a 64-megapixel night vision sensor with a FLIR thermal imaging camera for specialized fieldwork.
- Blackview’s Xplore series offers high-capacity 20,000 mAh batteries and 20-megapixel IR cameras, with the Xplore 2 featuring a premium OLED display.
- The legacy Lumigon T3, released in 2016, remains notable as one of the first luxury smartphones to integrate dedicated infrared night vision technology.

### Why it Matters
These specialized devices provide essential utility for outdoor enthusiasts, field professionals, and emergency responders who require visibility in total darkness where standard smartphone cameras fail. While these rugged handsets often sacrifice high-end processing power and software support, they fill a critical niche for users prioritizing durability and unique hardware capabilities over mainstream flagship features.
