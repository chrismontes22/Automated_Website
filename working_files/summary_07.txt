Commvault (CVLT) Turns Cyberattacks Into Automated Recovery Through New CrowdStrike (CRWD) Deal
============================================================

Commvault has integrated its cyber recovery platform with CrowdStrike’s Charlotte Agentic SOAR workflows, enabling security teams to automate data restoration and incident response directly within their existing tools.

### Key Points
- The integration allows users to automatically suspend backup aging policies and restore compromised assets into a Commvault Cleanroom for forensic investigation.
- Commvault reported fiscal first-quarter 2027 subscription revenue of $267 million, marking a 16% year-over-year increase.
- SaaS revenue surpassed $100 million for the first time, reflecting a 39% growth rate compared to the previous year.
- The company’s subscription annualized recurring revenue reached $1.054 billion, while free cash flow rose 71% to $51 million.
- Commvault maintains a GAAP operating margin of 8.2%, significantly lower than its 22.8% non-GAAP figure, highlighting a reliance on adjusted financial metrics.

### Why it Matters
This partnership bridges the historical gap between security and data recovery teams by automating manual workflows during active cyber incidents. By embedding recovery capabilities directly into CrowdStrike’s platform, Commvault strengthens its enterprise value proposition while navigating the challenge of balancing rapid cloud-based growth with GAAP profitability.
