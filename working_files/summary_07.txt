Apple Could Owe Even More After $5.7 Billion Patent Verdict
============================================================

Taction Technology is seeking additional damages, interest, and ongoing royalties from Apple following a $5.7 billion jury verdict regarding patent infringement in the company's Taptic Engine technology.

### Key Points
- A federal jury previously found Apple’s Taptic Engine infringed on two patents held by Taction Technology.
- Taction is requesting compensation for an additional six months of sales not covered in the original $5.7 billion award.
- The company seeks a 7% annual interest rate on damages, potentially adding hundreds of millions to the total payout.
- Taction wants ongoing royalties on future iPhone and Apple Watch sales until its patents expire in September 2035.
- Apple disputes the verdict, arguing its technology is distinct, and is currently seeking a new trial or a reversal of the judgment.

### Why it Matters
This legal dispute highlights the significant financial risks major tech companies face regarding intellectual property and haptic feedback components. If the court grants Taction's request for ongoing royalties, it could establish a long-term financial burden for Apple's future hardware releases.
