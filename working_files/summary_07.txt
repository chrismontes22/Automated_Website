Certified Android TV manufacturer put unauthorized backdoor in 1.3 million set-top boxes, Google reveals — OEM sold access to criminal affiliates, used massive network as botnet for ad fraud, residential proxy
============================================================

Google has terminated its partnership with manufacturer Videostrong Technology after discovering the company embedded backdoors into 1.3 million Android TV set-top boxes to facilitate global cybercriminal activities.

### Key Points
- Videostrong Technology pre-installed malicious backdoors into 1.3 million Android TV devices to enable ad fraud and residential proxy networks.
- The manufacturer sold access to these infected devices to criminal affiliates, allowing for the remote installation of additional malware.
- Google confirmed the malicious software evolved into a complex, three-stage design throughout 2024 to evade detection.
- Google has officially banned Videostrong from producing devices that include preloaded Google applications or services.
- The compromised hardware was sold under various brands and models over several years, impacting a significant global user base.

### Why it Matters
This incident highlights a critical vulnerability in the consumer electronics supply chain where manufacturers can compromise device security at the firmware level. It raises significant concerns regarding the integrity of pre-installed software and the long-term security risks posed by untrusted hardware partners in the Android ecosystem.
