5 Android Phones That Had Variable Aperture Cameras Before The iPhone 18 Pro
============================================================

Before the iPhone 18 Pro introduced variable aperture technology, several Android manufacturers, including Samsung, Sony, Huawei, Xiaomi, and Honor, experimented with the feature to enhance mobile photography capabilities.

### Key Points
- Samsung pioneered the feature in 2018 with the Galaxy S9, offering a dual f/1.5 and f/2.4 aperture to mimic the human eye.
- Sony’s 2021 Xperia Pro-I utilized a Zeiss-partnered lens with an f/2.0 to f/4.0 aperture before shifting focus to continuous optical zoom.
- Huawei remains a consistent proponent of the technology, integrating variable aperture into both its flagship Pura series and mid-range Nova lineups.
- Xiaomi featured a 10-blade stepless aperture in the 14 Ultra but abandoned the hardware in the 15 Ultra to reduce device thickness and mechanical complexity.
- Honor’s Magic6 Pro and Magic7 Pro utilize a two-step variable aperture alongside AI-driven software features like Magic Portal.

### Why it Matters
Variable aperture technology allows smartphone users to manually control light intake and depth of field, offering creative flexibility typically reserved for professional cameras. While many manufacturers eventually phased out the hardware due to cost, weight, and mechanical wear, these early implementations paved the way for modern mobile photography standards.
