open-slopware – Alternatives to FOSS projects choosing to use LLMs/AI
============================================================

The Open Slopware project maintains a comprehensive, community-driven list of free and open-source software projects that have integrated artificial intelligence or large language models into their development.

### Key Points
- The repository categorizes software as "tainted" if it utilizes AI for code generation, automated reviews, art, or data processing.
- It provides documentation for the "last untainted commit" of various projects, allowing users to fork software before AI integration.
- Merit badges, such as "Slop-Free Fork" and "Proof of Heart," are awarded to alternatives that maintain strict "No AI" policies.
- The project defines "vibecoding" as the practice of relying on AI-generated code without thorough human review.
- Users are encouraged to submit evidence-based pull requests to update the status of projects or suggest AI-free alternatives.

### Why it Matters
This resource serves as a critical tool for developers and organizations seeking to maintain software integrity and avoid the potential legal or ethical risks associated with AI-generated code. By providing a clear path to untainted versions and verified alternatives, it empowers users to uphold strict development standards in an increasingly automated landscape.
