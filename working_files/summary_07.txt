iPhone 18 Pro: Using the New Variable Aperture Lens
============================================================

The new iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max feature a significant camera upgrade with a four-stop variable aperture lens to improve low-light performance and depth control.

### Key Points
- The iPhone 18 Pro models feature a new variable aperture ranging from ƒ/1.48 to ƒ/4.0 on the 48MP Main camera.
- This hardware change delivers approximately 50 percent better low-light performance compared to previous fixed-aperture models.
- Users can manually adjust aperture settings via the Camera app or allow the device to manage light intake automatically.
- The system allows for greater creative control over depth of field, enabling sharper images in challenging lighting conditions.

### Why it Matters
This hardware shift marks a major evolution in mobile photography by providing users with professional-grade optical control previously unavailable on iPhones. By allowing manual adjustment of depth and light intake, Apple is bridging the gap between smartphone convenience and the creative flexibility of dedicated DSLR cameras.
