Take-Two's legal action to find GTA 6 leaker begins, as it subpoenas Microsoft and Discord for any data "sufficient to identify the users person"
============================================================

Take-Two Interactive has filed federal subpoenas against Microsoft and Discord to identify the individuals responsible for leaking unauthorized gameplay footage of the upcoming title Grand Theft Auto 6.

### Key Points
- Take-Two filed subpoenas in the Southern District Court of New York on August 20 to uncover the identity of the leaker known as CyberLeek.
- Microsoft must provide account details, including IP addresses, phone numbers, and linked Google or Xbox accounts, by September 4.
- Discord is required to disclose information regarding users associated with specific servers, including those linked to the aliases CinematicRockstar and DarkViperAU.
- The leaked content includes multiple videos showcasing GTA 6 gameplay, featuring the protagonist Jason and the fictional setting of Vice City.
- Reports indicate the leaker is promoting a cryptocurrency scheme involving a $CYBERLEEK meme coin to fund the unauthorized release of additional game footage.

### Why it Matters
This legal action highlights the aggressive measures major publishers take to protect intellectual property and mitigate the financial impact of high-profile development leaks. The involvement of cryptocurrency schemes suggests that such security breaches are increasingly tied to broader digital fraud and market manipulation tactics.
