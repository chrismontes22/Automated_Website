Suno Says It Will Start Watermarking Songs
============================================================

AI music startup Suno is implementing new watermarking, fingerprinting, and stricter download policies to combat copyright infringement and prevent the unauthorized cloning of voices and likenesses.

### Key Points
- Suno will introduce audio watermarking and fingerprinting to track AI-generated content and prevent misuse on streaming platforms.
- The company partnered with Musixmatch to utilize the Sentinel system for enhanced copyright detection.
- New community guidelines explicitly prohibit the creation of deceptive audio and the unauthorized use of a person's voice or likeness.
- Updated download policies aim to restrict the mass distribution of AI-generated tracks across third-party music services.
- These policy shifts follow ongoing copyright litigation and an adverse legal ruling in Germany regarding the company's training data.

### Why it Matters
These measures represent a significant effort by Suno to address mounting legal pressure and industry concerns regarding AI-generated intellectual property. By adopting stricter oversight, the company aims to mitigate risks associated with copyright infringement and platform manipulation while attempting to standardize ethical practices in generative music.
