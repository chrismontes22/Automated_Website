Anthropic CEO Warns AI Could ‘Outrun Our Ability to Understand and Control’ It
============================================================

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei and OpenAI CEO Sam Altman have called for a global slowdown in AI development to ensure safety measures can keep pace with rapid technological advancements.

### Key Points
- Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published a 3,800-word essay advocating for a pause in the development of increasingly powerful artificial intelligence models.
- OpenAI CEO Sam Altman publicly supported the call for a slowdown, noting that the topic has been a primary focus of recent internal discussions.
- The warnings follow the resignation of Anthropic researcher Jacob Coxon, who cited concerns regarding the trajectory of the AI industry.
- Amodei proposed implementing independent safety evaluations and fostering greater coordination between international governments to manage potential risks.
- Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang challenged these warnings, suggesting that AI companies may be exaggerating security fears to drive demand for new safety-focused products.

### Why it Matters
This rare alignment between the leaders of the world's top AI labs highlights growing industry anxiety regarding the potential for autonomous systems to outpace human control. The debate underscores a significant tension between the rapid commercial pursuit of artificial intelligence and the urgent need for standardized global safety regulations.
