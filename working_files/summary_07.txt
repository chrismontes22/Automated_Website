GM updates its trucks’ native software to coexist with CarPlay
============================================================

General Motors is introducing a redesigned infotainment interface for 2027 Chevrolet Silverado and GMC Sierra trucks, featuring picture-in-picture support for Apple CarPlay and Android Auto alongside native software.

### Key Points
- The new interface debuts later this year on 2027 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 and GMC Sierra 1500 models.
- Features include picture-in-picture phone projection, allowing users to view CarPlay or Android Auto alongside native vehicle controls.
- The system utilizes over 60 inches of combined screen space with larger touch zones to improve usability while driving.
- New software includes specialized layouts for trailering and off-roading, plus a swipe-down notification menu for quick settings access.
- Physical buttons remain for essential functions like HVAC, though some tools, such as oil measurement, are now digital-only.

### Why it Matters
This update represents a strategic shift for GM, which previously signaled an intent to phase out phone mirroring in favor of proprietary software. By integrating CarPlay and Android Auto into a split-screen format, the automaker aims to balance customer demand for familiar mobile interfaces with its goal of maintaining control over the vehicle's native digital ecosystem.
