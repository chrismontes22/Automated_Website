Galaxy S27 Ultra might take eye strain seriously amid rumors of DC dimming
============================================================

Samsung is reportedly planning to integrate DC dimming technology into the upcoming Galaxy S27 Pro and S27 Ultra to help reduce user eye strain and headaches.

### Key Points
- Tipster Walle claims Samsung is collaborating with safety firms to improve eye comfort on future flagship smartphones.
- The proposed DC dimming technology aims to replace or supplement current Pulse Width Modulation (PWM) dimming methods.
- PWM dimming functions by rapidly flickering the display, which can cause discomfort for some users during extended screen time.
- DC dimming maintains consistent brightness by adjusting voltage rather than rapidly cycling the display on and off.
- These potential display upgrades are expected to be integrated into the existing Eye Comfort Shield feature within Samsung's display settings.

### Why it Matters
Integrating DC dimming could significantly improve the user experience for individuals sensitive to screen flicker and light-induced headaches. This shift suggests Samsung is prioritizing long-term health and accessibility features to remain competitive in the premium smartphone market.
