Bitcoin, copper, stocks, yields all fall; gold rises
============================================================

Bitcoin and other major cryptocurrencies are declining as global investors shift capital toward safe-haven assets like gold and Treasury bonds amid growing concerns over economic stagnation.

### Key Points
- Bitcoin dropped over 2% in 24 hours, with ether, XRP, and solana experiencing even steeper losses.
- Copper futures fell nearly 1%, reflecting broader market anxiety regarding global economic health and growth-sensitive assets.
- The 10-year Treasury yield dropped eight basis points to 5.08%, while gold prices rose by 1% as investors sought safety.
- CME fed funds futures indicate a 29% probability of a Federal Reserve interest rate hike later this month.
- Analysts are monitoring the producer price index and the ongoing unwind of artificial intelligence-related stock positions for further market signals.

### Why it Matters
This broad-based exit from risk assets suggests that investors are increasingly worried about a stagflationary environment. The shift toward gold and bonds highlights a defensive market posture as participants weigh persistent inflation concerns against the potential for an economic slowdown.
