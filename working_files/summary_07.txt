Meta is making its AI chatbot more like an assistant
============================================================

Meta is upgrading its AI chatbot with new productivity features, including calendar integration and autonomous task management, to compete directly with rivals like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude.

### Key Points
- Meta AI now integrates with user calendars to generate daily briefings and schedule events automatically.
- The update is powered by the new Muse Spark 1.1 model, enabling more complex research and task execution.
- Users can now provide real-time feedback to steer the AI's output during the generation process.
- The assistant can perform ongoing tasks, such as weekly meal planning or monitoring product restocks, without requiring repeated prompts.
- The features are rolling out immediately in select markets via the Meta AI app and web interface, with broader platform support coming soon.

### Why it Matters
This update signals a strategic shift for Meta as it pivots from entertainment-focused AI toward the productivity-driven model popularized by OpenAI and Google. By embedding these tools into daily workflows, Meta aims to establish its chatbot as a central personal assistant rather than just a creative or social utility.
