Didn't Pre-Order iPhone 18 Pro Yet? Here's How Long the Wait is Now
============================================================

Apple has officially opened pre-orders for the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max, with initial delivery dates beginning September 18 for early customers in the United States.

### Key Points
- Pre-orders for the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max launched on September 12 at 5 a.m. Pacific Time.
- Early orders are scheduled for delivery on September 18, while newer orders for specific configurations are now delayed until October.
- iPhone 18 Pro Max models with 256GB and 512GB storage capacities are currently showing the longest wait times, with estimates reaching October 6–13.
- Apple Store pickup remains a potential alternative for customers seeking launch-day availability despite tightening online delivery windows.

### Why it Matters
- High demand for the latest iPhone models is already impacting supply chain availability and extending shipping timelines for many configurations. This trend highlights the importance of early ordering for consumers who want to secure devices during the initial launch window.
