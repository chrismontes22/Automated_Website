Google Pixel 11 Series, Pixel Watch 5, Pixel Tag: Specs, Features, Price, Release Date
============================================================

Google has unveiled its new Pixel 11 smartphone lineup, the Pixel Watch 5, and the Pixel Tag tracker, all featuring upgraded Tensor G6 processors and advanced generative AI capabilities.

### Key Points
- The Pixel 11 series includes four models ranging from $899 to $1,900, all featuring 256 GB base storage and the new Tensor G6 chipset.
- Pixel 11 Pro and Pro XL models introduce "Instant Night Sight" and 120X Pro Zoom, while the Pro Fold features a more durable hinge and glass-fiber design.
- The Pixel Watch 5 debuts with a Snapdragon W5 Gen 2 chip, 3,000-nit peak brightness, and new health-tracking features for blood pressure and sleep breathing.
- Google’s $29 Pixel Tag utilizes ultra-wideband technology for precise item tracking and integrates with the Android Find Hub network starting November 11.
- All new mobile devices will receive seven years of software updates and feature deep integration with Gemini for proactive task automation and real-time media translation.

### Why it Matters
These hardware updates signal Google's aggressive push to integrate generative AI directly into the user experience through proactive assistance and advanced camera processing. By standardizing high-end features like the Tensor G6 chip and expanding its ecosystem with the Pixel Tag, Google is positioning its hardware to compete more directly with the premium offerings from Apple and Samsung.
