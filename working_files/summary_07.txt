Nothing OS 5.0 drops with Android 17 and on-device app building
============================================================

Nothing has officially announced Nothing OS 5.0, a major software update based on Android 17 that introduces enhanced AI tools, a redesigned interface, and improved system-wide customization options.

### Key Points
- Nothing OS 5.0 features the new Geist typeface, transparent widgets, and wallpaper-adaptive icon colors for a more cohesive visual experience.
- The update integrates Essential AI tools, including real-time transcription, Smart Collections, and a feature that builds custom apps from plain-language prompts.
- A dedicated Glyph Interface app consolidates lighting controls and introduces new widgets for tracking digital habits and timers.
- Built on Android 17, the software adds split notification panels, enhanced Quick Share functionality, and improved privacy indicators.
- The Open Beta program is currently available for the Phone (3), with a stable public release scheduled for mid-October.

### Why it Matters
This update represents a significant shift toward deeper AI integration and user-driven customization within the Nothing ecosystem. By leveraging Android 17, the company aims to improve device performance and cross-platform connectivity while refining its distinct hardware-software aesthetic.
