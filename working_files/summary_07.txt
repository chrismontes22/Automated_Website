These $40 flat-head earbuds reminded me of the '90s in the best possible way
============================================================

Sivga has released the M260, a pair of retro-styled, flat-head earbuds featuring a premium machined aluminum build and high-quality audio performance for an affordable price of $40.

### Key Points
- The Sivga M260 earbuds are available in 3.5mm and USB-C versions, with the latter including a built-in DAC for direct device compatibility.
- Constructed from machined aluminum with gold accents, the earbuds offer a durable, premium build quality rarely seen in the budget audio category.
- The flat-head design sits in the outer ear rather than the canal, providing high comfort for all-day use without the fatigue associated with traditional IEMs.
- Each unit features a custom 14.2mm dynamic driver that delivers tight bass, clear mid-range vocals, and smooth treble.
- The detachable cable uses a standard MMCX connector and includes a nylon-braided sheath, inline volume controls, and a built-in microphone.

### Why it Matters
The M260 offers a high-value alternative for users seeking comfortable, wired audio solutions that bypass the need for external dongles or charging. By combining premium materials with a retro form factor, Sivga provides a durable and convenient option for listeners who prefer traditional earbud designs over modern in-ear monitors.
