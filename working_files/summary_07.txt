New York City Public Schools banning AI use through middle school this year
============================================================

New York City Public Schools will implement a moratorium on generative artificial intelligence for students in pre-K through eighth grade for the upcoming 2026-2027 academic year.

### Key Points
- The ban prohibits student-facing AI software and companion chatbots across the nation's largest school district for younger students.
- High school students remain exempt from the moratorium and will instead participate in twice-yearly AI literacy courses.
- Mayor Zohran Mamdani stated the district will spend the year studying the technology's impacts before determining future integration.
- Schools Chancellor Kamar Samuels emphasized that the policy aims to prioritize critical thinking and human connection over reliance on automated tools.
- The decision follows the district's previous implementation of a bell-to-bell cell phone ban during the instructional day.

### Why it Matters
This policy represents a significant divergence from federal guidance that encourages the responsible integration of AI tools in classrooms. By prioritizing traditional instruction over generative technology, the district is setting a national precedent for how large school systems manage the potential risks of early-age digital reliance.
