These MagSafe power banks are made for the iPhone Duo
============================================================

The emergence of 25W Qi 2.2 charging technology and specialized magnetic power banks is expanding portable charging options for standard smartphones and new wide-format foldable devices.

### Key Points
- New Qi 2.2 power banks from brands like UGREEN and Baseus offer improved charging efficiency for iPhones and recent Pixel devices.
- The BMX SolidSafe Duo features a 5,000mAh semi-solid-state battery and a square design specifically tailored for wide foldables like the iPhone Duo.
- The SolidSafe Duo includes a 360-degree kickstand and a TFT display for battery monitoring, retailing at $79 with 15W wireless and 20W wired charging.
- Baseus plans to release the PicoGo Air AM72 magnetic power bank in November to address the growing demand for foldable-compatible accessories.
- Accessory manufacturers are rapidly developing specialized hardware to accommodate the unique dimensions of emerging foldable smartphone form factors.

### Why it Matters
These specialized accessories address a critical hardware gap for wide-format foldable phones that are often incompatible with standard, elongated magnetic power banks. As foldable adoption grows, the market is shifting toward custom-fit charging solutions that prioritize both ergonomic utility and device-specific form factors.
