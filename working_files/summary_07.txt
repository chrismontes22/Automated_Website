Cherry MX built the mechanical keyboard, but Hall Effect, optical, and Topre switches are changing the game — Here's how to pick the right one
============================================================

Understanding the differences between mechanical, optical, and Hall Effect keyboard switches is essential for selecting the right hardware for gaming, professional typing, or general daily computer use.

### Key Points
- Mechanical switches use physical metal contacts and are categorized into linear, tactile, and clicky varieties based on their feel and sound.
- Optical switches utilize infrared light beams to register keystrokes, offering increased durability and faster response times compared to traditional mechanical designs.
- Hall Effect switches employ magnets to measure precise travel distance, allowing for highly customizable actuation points favored by competitive gamers.
- Topre switches combine rubber domes with capacitive sensors to provide a softer, fatigue-reducing typing experience often preferred by professional writers.
- Major industry players include Cherry MX, Razer, Wooting, SteelSeries, and Gateron, each competing through proprietary switch technology and performance-focused features.

### Why it Matters
Choosing the correct switch technology directly impacts typing comfort, gaming performance, and the long-term durability of your hardware. Understanding these distinctions helps consumers avoid unnecessary costs while ensuring their keyboard meets specific needs for precision, speed, or ergonomic support.
