AI giant Anthropic publicly files for listing on the U.S. stock market
============================================================

AI developer Anthropic has filed its S-1 prospectus for an initial public offering, revealing significant revenue growth alongside massive infrastructure spending and substantial net losses for the company.

### Key Points
- Anthropic reported $16.3 billion in revenue for the first half of 2026, surpassing its total 2025 revenue of $4.6 billion.
- The company recorded a net loss of $80.7 billion through June, largely driven by accounting charges and $9 billion in infrastructure spending.
- Anthropic has committed to future cloud computing contracts worth up to $637 billion with partners including Amazon and Google.
- The firm’s enterprise portfolio includes the Claude chatbot, Claude Code, and Claude Cowork tools.
- The S-1 filing highlights potential risks, including the possibility of AI models developing unanticipated capabilities that could cause real-world harm.

### Why it Matters
This IPO serves as a critical test for investor confidence in high-valuation AI firms following the successful public debut of SpaceX earlier this year. The filing underscores the immense capital requirements of the industry while highlighting the ongoing tension between rapid technological scaling and the safety concerns raised by leadership.
