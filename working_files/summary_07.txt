I Tested the Three Big Voice Assistants to See Who’s Best at Predicting Weather
============================================================

A recent performance test of Amazon’s Alexa Plus, Google Gemini for Home, and Apple’s Siri reveals significant differences in weather reporting accuracy and detail across smart voice assistants.

### Key Points
- Google Gemini for Home proved the most accurate, providing precise neighborhood-level forecasts and clear hourly visual data on Nest Hub devices.
- Amazon’s Alexa Plus offers the most comprehensive weather details, including wind and air quality, though its reports are occasionally inconsistent or overly chatty.
- Apple’s Siri performed as a moderate option but suffered from recurring bugs that caused temperature estimates to deviate by five degrees from actual conditions.
- All three assistants maintained reasonable accuracy for current temperature readings, generally staying within a few degrees of verified local monitors.
- Users can improve efficiency by syncing weather, traffic, and news updates into a single "Good morning" routine across these platforms.

### Why it Matters
Choosing the right voice assistant for daily planning depends on whether a user prioritizes granular data or high-precision accuracy. These performance variations highlight how different underlying algorithms and data sources impact the reliability of smart home devices for essential daily tasks.
