Sony Is Killing PlayStation Game Discs Exactly How Apple Killed the Headphone Jack
============================================================

Sony has announced plans to end physical disc production for its gaming consoles by 2028, signaling a complete transition toward a fully digital, proprietary ecosystem for all users.

### Key Points
- Sony will cease physical disc production for its gaming hardware by 2028, citing shifting consumer preferences toward digital downloads.
- The move eliminates the secondary market for games, preventing users from reselling, lending, or purchasing used physical copies.
- All game purchases will be funneled through the PlayStation Store, granting Sony total control over pricing and distribution.
- Digital game purchases function as revocable licenses rather than ownership, meaning content can be removed from user libraries if licensing agreements expire.
- The transition mirrors previous industry shifts, such as the removal of the 3.5mm headphone jack, to prioritize proprietary hardware and service-based revenue.

### Why it Matters
This shift fundamentally redefines consumer ownership by transforming gaming consoles from personal property into terminals for rented digital services. By removing physical media, companies gain absolute authority over content access, leaving users vulnerable to library deletions and the loss of resale rights.
