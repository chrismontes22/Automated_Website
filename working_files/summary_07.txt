Goodbye, Fire Tablet: Amazon’s New Alexa Tablet Is a Flashy Portable Entertainment Hub
============================================================

Amazon is replacing its Fire Tablet lineup with a new series of Alexa Tablets running Android 16, featuring full Google Play Store access and advanced generative AI integration.

### Key Points
- The new lineup includes the 12-inch Pro, 11-inch, and 8-inch models, with prices starting at $230, $330, and $500 respectively.
- Devices transition from Fire OS to Android 16 at launch, with an Android 17 update scheduled for early 2027.
- All models feature Alexa Plus AI, which provides on-screen intelligence to analyze browser content and manage personal tasks.
- The 12-inch Pro model supports a keyboard and USI 2.0 pen, offering up to 16GB of RAM and a 2.8K 120Hz display.
- Amazon will wind down Fire Tablet production, though it has committed to four years of software support for existing devices.
- Preorders are open now, with official shipping across the United States beginning October 14.

### Why it Matters
This shift to Android 16 and the Google Play Store marks a significant strategic pivot for Amazon, moving away from its closed ecosystem to compete more directly with premium tablets. By integrating generative AI and high-end hardware, Amazon aims to position these devices as versatile productivity and entertainment hubs rather than just budget-friendly media players.
