5 things I learned after a month of daily-driving the Pixel 11 Pro XL
============================================================

The Google Pixel 11 Pro XL offers improved durability and battery efficiency, though it continues to face challenges with cellular connectivity and high-end camera performance against competitors.

### Key Points
- The device features a 5,115mAh battery that supports full-day usage and includes native Qi 2.2 wireless charging integration.
- Build quality has improved, with the phone surviving multiple drops and featuring a stable, wobble-free camera bar design.
- Camera performance remains reliable for general use but struggles with product photography compared to Chinese flagship rivals.
- Users may experience inconsistent cellular connectivity, including intermittent signal drops and issues switching between 5G and LTE networks.
- The software experience remains a highlight, featuring a clean, bloatware-free interface and useful tools like the Rambler feature in Gboard.

### Why it Matters
The Pixel 11 Pro XL represents a significant hardware refinement for Google, yet it highlights a growing gap between standard flagships and specialized ultra-premium devices. For consumers, this underscores the trade-off between Google's superior software ecosystem and the advanced camera hardware found in competing high-end smartphones.
