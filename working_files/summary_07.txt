Google Pixel 11 vs. Motorola Edge 2026: There's an obvious winner
============================================================

The Google Pixel 11 outperforms the Motorola Edge 2026 by offering superior software longevity, higher storage capacity, and better camera performance despite a higher initial retail price point.

### Key Points
- The Google Pixel 11 provides seven years of Android OS updates, significantly exceeding the two-year support window offered by the Motorola Edge 2026.
- Google’s device features 256GB of base storage and the Tensor G6 chipset, while the Motorola model is limited to 128GB and an aging MediaTek Dimensity 7450 processor.
- The Pixel 11 includes Qi 2 wireless charging magnets and a more durable glass-and-metal build compared to the plastic chassis of the Edge 2026.
- Motorola’s Edge 2026 offers superior IP69K dust and water resistance and a lighter 160g design, but it lacks the long-term software value of the Pixel.
- The Pixel 11 retails for $899, whereas the Motorola Edge 2026 is priced at $600, though the Pixel is frequently subject to post-launch discounts.

### Why it Matters
The disparity in software support and storage options makes the Pixel 11 a more sustainable long-term investment for consumers despite the $300 price gap. Choosing the Motorola Edge 2026 may result in premature obsolescence, highlighting the importance of evaluating update policies and hardware longevity before purchasing a mid-range smartphone.
