I replaced the Jellyfin client with Moonfin on all my devices, and the themes sold me
============================================================

The Moonfin media client offers Jellyfin and Emby users an enhanced, highly customizable alternative to official software, featuring extensive theme stores, cross-server support, and diverse media library integration.

### Key Points
- Moonfin provides advanced UI customization, including a theme store with styles mimicking major streaming services like Netflix and Disney+.
- The client supports unified playback by connecting to both Jellyfin and Emby servers simultaneously within a single interface.
- It includes built-in support for diverse media types, such as retro game ROMs, comics, audiobooks, and eBook readers.
- Users can integrate Jellyseerr or Seerr and manage live TV and DVR browsing directly through the application.
- Performance may vary on older hardware, with some devices experiencing lag due to high-resolution background effects and UI scaling.
- The Moonfin web server plugin enables theme syncing and management across various platforms, including Apple TV, Roku, and mobile devices.

### Why it Matters
Moonfin represents a growing trend in the self-hosted community toward modular, user-centric software that prioritizes aesthetic personalization and cross-platform compatibility. By consolidating multiple media servers into one interface, it simplifies the user experience for enthusiasts managing fragmented digital libraries.
