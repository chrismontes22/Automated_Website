Why Meta is charging to post more hyperlinks
============================================================

Meta has introduced a tiered subscription model for Facebook pages, limiting free users to two hyperlinks per month while charging up to $499 monthly for unlimited link sharing.

### Key Points
- Facebook pages are now restricted to two free hyperlinks per month.
- The "Meta One Advanced" plan costs $49.99 monthly for eight hyperlinks.
- The "Max" plan costs $499 monthly for unlimited hyperlink access.
- Social media platforms increasingly restrict external links to keep users within their proprietary ecosystems.
- Industry analysts note that these policies aim to maximize time spent on-platform to increase advertising revenue.

### Why it Matters
Social media platforms are aggressively prioritizing internal engagement by creating friction for users attempting to navigate to external websites. This shift forces businesses and content creators to pay for traffic, fundamentally altering the economics of digital marketing and audience distribution.
