Laptop catches fire on US flight, pilot reports
============================================================

A passenger’s laptop caught fire on an American Airlines flight from Atlanta to Dallas, prompting an emergency response and medical treatment for one individual on board.

### Key Points
- American Airlines Flight 2398 landed safely at Dallas Fort Worth International Airport on Friday following reports of smoke from a passenger's device.
- Flight attendants contained the fire mid-flight, and emergency personnel met the aircraft at the gate upon arrival.
- The Federal Aviation Administration has recorded 58 verified lithium battery incidents involving smoke or fire on aircraft so far this year.
- FAA regulations mandate that lithium batteries must be stowed in carry-on baggage rather than checked luggage to mitigate fire risks.
- Data shows 84 laptop-related battery incidents have been reported to the FAA over the last two decades.

### Why it Matters
Lithium battery fires represent a persistent safety challenge for the aviation industry as the use of portable electronics and power banks continues to rise. These incidents highlight the critical importance of strict adherence to battery storage regulations to prevent mid-air emergencies and protect passenger safety.
