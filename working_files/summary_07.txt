VAIO’s Multi-Flip laptop is back after a decade, and it’s not cheap
============================================================

VAIO has launched the VAIO T, a 13.3-inch convertible laptop featuring a unique center-pivot display mechanism that hides the keyboard when the device is used in tablet mode.

### Key Points
- The VAIO T utilizes a "Multi-Flip Mechanism" with urethane sheet hinges and magnetic retention to allow the screen to rotate 180 degrees.
- This is the first product released under the "VAIO Next" range following Nojima’s acquisition of the company in January 2025.
- Hardware options include Intel Core Ultra 5 or 7 processors, up to 64GB of RAM, and a 2TB PCIe 5.0 SSD.
- The device is currently exclusive to the Japanese market with a starting price of 374,000 yen, approximately $2,400.
- A "Kachi-iro" Special Edition features a signature indigo colorway and the high-performance Intel Core Ultra X7 358H processor.

### Why it Matters
The VAIO T revives a niche hinge design that lost the broader market to Lenovo’s 360-degree standard, signaling a shift in how manufacturers approach convertible ergonomics. By prioritizing a keyboard-free tablet experience, VAIO is challenging current industry design norms to address long-standing user complaints about traditional hybrid laptops.
