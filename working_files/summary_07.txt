A New Thermal Add-On to My Phone Changed How I See My Home Forever
============================================================

The Thermal Master P3 is a compact, smartphone-compatible thermal imaging camera that allows users to detect hidden cameras, insulation gaps, and water leaks with professional-grade diagnostic capabilities.

### Key Points
- The device costs $279 and is compatible with both iPhone and Android smartphones.
- It features a 25Hz refresh rate and measures temperatures ranging from -4 to 1,112 degrees Fahrenheit.
- Users can identify hidden cameras, electrical hubs, and thermal leaks by scanning surfaces from a distance of one to two feet.
- The accompanying free app provides advanced customization, including magnification, color palettes, and specialized modes like birdwatching.
- Performance may be limited by reflective or metallic surfaces, which can cause inaccurate temperature readings.

### Why it Matters
This device democratizes access to thermal imaging technology, offering homeowners and travelers a portable tool for DIY inspections and security checks. By identifying energy inefficiencies or hidden surveillance devices, users can potentially save on professional inspection costs and improve their personal privacy.
