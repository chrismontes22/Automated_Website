Not ready to give up Google Assistant? There's apparently a way to bring it back
============================================================

A Reddit user has discovered a workaround to restore Google Assistant on Android devices by uninstalling recent Google app updates and disabling automatic updates in the Play Store.

### Key Points
- Google officially replaced Google Assistant with Gemini as the default Android digital assistant on September 4, 2026.
- The workaround requires users to uninstall Google app updates and decline the prompt to switch to Gemini upon relaunching the assistant.
- Users must disable automatic updates for the Google app in the Play Store to prevent the system from reverting to Gemini.
- This method relies on older app versions and may be patched by Google through future software updates or server-side changes.

### Why it Matters
This discovery highlights a significant user preference for the legacy Google Assistant experience over the newer Gemini AI integration. It underscores the tension between Google's push for generative AI adoption and the desire of long-term users to maintain familiar, established software functionality on their mobile devices.
