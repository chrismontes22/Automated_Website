Govee’s portable smart lamp is down to one of its best prices to date
============================================================

Govee’s portable Table Lamp Classic is currently available on Amazon for $59, offering a $21 discount on the smart lighting device that features Matter support and long-lasting battery life.

### Key Points
- The Govee Table Lamp Classic is priced at $59, marking its second-lowest price point since the product's launch.
- The device includes a 4,800mAh battery providing up to 30 hours of runtime on color settings or five hours on bright white light.
- Integrated Matter support allows users to control the lamp via various smart home platforms without relying solely on the Govee app.
- Users can access 72 preset lighting scenes, music synchronization, and AI-generated custom effects through the companion mobile application.
- The cordless, portable design is intended for versatile use across different indoor environments, though the unit lacks weather resistance for outdoor conditions.

### Why it Matters
This discount highlights the growing market trend toward portable, interoperable smart home lighting that reduces the need for multiple fixed fixtures. By supporting the Matter standard, Govee provides consumers with greater flexibility and easier integration into existing smart home ecosystems.
