Gemini gets better memory: smart home update refines automations and more
============================================================

Google is rolling out significant updates to its smart home ecosystem, including expanded 15-minute conversational memory for Gemini and Gemini Live support for first-generation Nest and Home devices.

### Key Points
- Gemini Live is now available on first-generation Google Home Mini and Nest Hub devices for Google Home Premium subscribers.
- Gemini’s conversational memory has been extended to 15 minutes, allowing users to follow up on previous requests without restarting the interaction.
- The Google Home app now provides clearer guidance for saving automations and includes improved controls for smart blinds and window coverings.
- New security features alert users to update or remove passcodes when access is revoked for smart locks.
- Expanded hardware support now includes Walmart’s Onn Outdoor Plug-In and Outdoor Floodlight Hardwired cameras.
- A specific firmware update is rolling out for third-generation wired Nest Cams and the third-generation wired Nest Doorbell.

### Why it Matters
These updates aim to reduce user frustration by making AI interactions more fluid and context-aware through extended memory capabilities. By refining automation guidance and security protocols, Google is attempting to create a more intuitive and reliable smart home experience for its growing user base.
