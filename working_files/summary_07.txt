5 Video Doorbells With Local Storage That Won't Spy On You
============================================================

Video doorbells featuring local storage options from brands like Eufy, Aqara, TP-Link, and Wyze offer homeowners enhanced privacy and security by eliminating the need for recurring cloud subscription fees.

### Key Points
- The TP-Link Tapo D225 supports up to 512 GB of local storage via microSD and features 128-bit encryption for transmitted data.
- Eufy’s E340 uses a dual-camera system with 8GB of built-in storage, expandable through the HomeBase S380 smart hub.
- The Aqara G400 stands out for its Apple HomeKit compatibility and support for Power over Ethernet (PoE) installations.
- Wyze’s Duo Cam Video Doorbell provides dual-lens coverage and a removable 6,200 mAh battery for flexible power management.
- The Eufy C31 offers an affordable entry point at $85, utilizing a microSD slot and optional HomeBase 3 integration for local AI processing.

### Why it Matters
Choosing a doorbell with local storage allows users to maintain full control over their personal data while avoiding the ongoing monthly costs associated with manufacturer cloud subscriptions. This approach ensures that sensitive home security footage remains physically on the device or a private hub rather than being stored on external servers.
