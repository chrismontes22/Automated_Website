Ford’s first ultra-cheap EV is called Fathom, a full-featured truck for $28,350
============================================================

Ford has unveiled the Fathom, a new midsize electric truck built on a streamlined assembly platform, set to launch with a competitive starting price of $28,350.

### Key Points
- The Fathom features a high-resolution touchscreen, Apple CarPlay, Android Auto, and sensors for Ford’s Blue Cruise hands-free driving system.
- Ford utilizes a new Universal Electric Vehicle (UEV) platform that assembles vehicle sections in parallel to significantly reduce labor and component costs.
- The truck offers more interior volume than a Toyota RAV4 and includes both an open cargo bed and a front trunk.
- Production is scheduled for the Louisville Assembly Plant, with pre-orders opening early next year and initial deliveries expected before the end of 2027.
- The Fathom enters the market to compete against the minimalist Slate Truck, which currently retails for $24,950.

### Why it Matters
This aggressive pricing strategy represents a major pivot for Ford as it attempts to recover from significant losses and low consumer interest in previous electric vehicle models. By prioritizing high-volume manufacturing and feature-rich designs, the company aims to capture the budget-conscious segment of the electric truck market.
